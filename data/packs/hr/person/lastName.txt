description: Croatian surname, ONE list for both sexes. Croatian surnames do NOT inflect for gender: Ana Horvat and her brother Marko Horvat carry the identical form, and so do the -ić patronymics (Ana Kovačević / Marko Kovačević) and the rare adjectival -ski names, which stay -ski for a woman rather than becoming -ska the way Polish Kowalski becomes Kowalska or Czech Novák becomes Nováková. That is why this pack has no person/female/lastName.txt: its absence is the design, not an omission. The colloquial derivations (Horvatica, Kovačevićka, "the Horvat woman") are possessive nicknames, never a registered surname, so they are deliberately not here. What does differ between the sexes is declension, not form: a man's surname declines through the seven cases (vidio sam Marka Horvata) while a woman's stays in the nominative (vidio sam Anu Horvat). The order is the real one — Horvat leads the Croatian register at roughly 20.000 bearers, about 0,5% of the country, followed by Kovačević, Babić and Marić — and -ić is the dominant ending the way -son is in Sweden. Weights are the pack default rank-decay curve rather than measured counts, because Croatian surnames are genuinely spread: the curve's top share of about 0,35% is close enough to Horvat's real 0,5% that it does not misrepresent the country.
