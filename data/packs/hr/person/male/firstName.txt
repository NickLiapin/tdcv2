Weights are the pack default rank-decay curve rather than measured shares, because Croatian given names really are spread: Ivan, the commonest, is only a few percent of men. Almost every name here is unambiguously male; the two genuine exceptions, Saša and Vanja, appear in the female list as well and are the only overlap between the two.
