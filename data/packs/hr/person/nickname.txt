---
description: Common Croatian hypocoristic. Croatian shortens a name and then adds -o, -e, -a or -ica, which is why a man's nickname often ends in a vowel — Ivo from Ivan, Joško from Josip, Mate from Matej. The -ica ending is the ordinary female one (Ivica is the exception: it is a MAN'S name in Croatia, not a diminutive of Iva).
locale: hr
