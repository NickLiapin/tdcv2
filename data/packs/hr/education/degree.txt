description: Academic degree or level of education (Croatian). These are the titles the Croatian system awards after the Bologna reform — univ. bacc. and mag. from a university programme, bacc. and struč. spec. from a professional one, dr. sc. from a doctoral one. SSS, VŠS and VSS are the older shorthand for secondary, higher and high qualification and still appear on Croatian job adverts.
