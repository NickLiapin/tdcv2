description: Employee benefit (Croatian). Božićnica, uskrsnica and regres are Croatian institutions with no English equivalent: a Christmas bonus, an Easter bonus and a holiday allowance, all three tax-free up to a statutory ceiling and expected rather than exceptional. NOTE ON THE CATEGORY NAME: this directory used to be called hr, for human resources, and every file in it was unreachable in every locale — the address resolver reads a locale-shaped first segment as a locale, so hr.benefit meant "the Croatian pack" and never "the hr category". It was renamed to workplace. This pack is the locale that name collided with.
locale: hr
