description: Dishes of the Croatian kitchen. Croatia has TWO cuisines that share almost no vocabulary: the Adriatic one — brudet, gregada, crni rižot, buzara, soparnik, pašticada, rožata — built on fish, olive oil and the peka bell, and the Pannonian interior one — sarma, čobanac, kulen, štrukli, purica s mlincima, kotlovina, kremšnite — built on pork, paprika and lard. Istria is a third register again (maneštra, fuži, tartufi).
