description: Street names that recur across Croatian towns (rulers and bans, writers, scientists, regions, rivers, trades, dates). Two grammatical shapes are mixed and a config must compose them differently: the GENITIVE phrases (kralja Tomislava, Nikole Tesle) follow the thoroughfare type — Ulica kralja Tomislava — and are already inflected, so must not be inflected again; the ADJECTIVAL names (Cvjetna, Zagrebačka) are feminine to agree with ulica and stand before it (Cvjetna ulica) or alone with the number (Cvjetna 15). Streets unique to one city belong in the Croatia country pack, not here.
locale: hr
