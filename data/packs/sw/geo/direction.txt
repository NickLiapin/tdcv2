description: Compass direction (Swahili). Worth knowing where these words come from: kaskazini (north) and kusini (south) are built on kaskazi and kusi, the names of the two monsoon winds that governed Indian Ocean sailing. The Swahili compass is a wind compass before it is a map one, and kaskazi and kusi are also seasons — they appear in date/season.txt.
