description: Country and territory names (Swahili). Swahili gives many countries a U- prefix, which is a real noun class and not an ornament: Ufaransa, Ujerumani, Uingereza, Uchina, Ureno, Uholanzi, Uswisi. Marekani (the United States) and Misri (Egypt) do not take it. A pack that spelled these the English way would be in the script but not in the language.
locale: sw
