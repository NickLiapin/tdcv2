Swahili has NO generational suffix — no Jr., no Sr., no III — because a Tanzanian child does not take the father's full name; the father's given name simply becomes the child's second name. What does follow a Tanzanian name is an English-language academic or professional post-nominal, because degrees are awarded and written in English.
