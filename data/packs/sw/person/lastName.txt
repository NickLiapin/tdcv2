description: Tanzanian family name. ONE list serves both sexes: Swahili has no grammatical gender and family names never inflect, which is why there is no person/female/lastName.txt — its absence is the design. Two things a config cannot infer: many Tanzanians carry Given + FATHER'S GIVEN NAME rather than an inherited surname, so this list deliberately overlaps the given-name lists (Juma, Hamisi, Said, Bakari, Rajabu are all both); and the Mwa- and Mw- names are the southern-highlands pattern while Mushi, Swai, Kimaro, Massawe and Shirima are the Kilimanjaro one, so a Tanzanian reads a region off a surname. Weights are rank-derived on a gentle Zipf curve, not counts from a registry.
