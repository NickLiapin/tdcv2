description: Marital status in Swahili. Note that the VERB is gendered even though the language is not: a man kuoa (marries), a woman kuolewa (is married), so "Ameoa" and "Ameolewa" both mean married and a config must pick the one matching the row's sex. Ndoa ya kimila (customary), ya kidini (religious) and ya kiserikali (civil) are the three legally recognised forms of marriage in Tanzania.
