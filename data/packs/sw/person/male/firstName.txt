description: Tanzanian male given name, in three real blocks: the Arabic-derived Muslim names that dominate the coast and Zanzibar, the Christian names that dominate upcountry, and the Swahili meaning-names at the end. This is TANZANIA's mix, not an average of the Swahili-speaking countries — coastal Tanzania, upcountry Kenya and the eastern DRC name their children very differently, and blending them would produce a population that exists nowhere. Mosi, Pili, Tatu, Nne, Sita, Saba, Nane and Kumi are birth-order names; Juma, Hamisi, Jumanne and Alhamisi are day-of-birth names. Nine names are UNISEX and appear in the female list too, deliberately: Baraka, Amani, Tumaini, Imani, Zawadi, Nuru, Bahati, Heri and Tatu. Weights are rank-derived on a gentle Zipf curve, not counts from a registry: no Tanzanian name registry is public, and no Swahili name is sharply concentrated the way Vietnamese Nguyen or Korean Kim is.
