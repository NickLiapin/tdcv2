description: Colour name (Irish). Irish does not cut the spectrum where English does: glas is the green of grass and of living things and also the grey of a sheep, while uaine is the green of paint and cloth; gorm is blue and is also the word used of a black person's skin; rua is the red of hair, fur and rust, dearg the red of blood and paint. A translation that maps one English colour word to one Irish one will be wrong about half the time. Radical form — a colour adjective lenites after a feminine noun (bró ghlas).
locale: ga
