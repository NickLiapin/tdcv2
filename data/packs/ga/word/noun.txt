description: Common Irish noun in the RADICAL FORM — nominative singular, no article, no séimhiú and no urú. This matters more in Irish than in most languages: the first letter of a word changes according to what stands before it. bó becomes an bhó (the cow), Corcaigh becomes i gCorcaigh (in Cork), bád becomes ár mbád (our boat). A value drawn from this list therefore CANNOT be dropped into a sentence unchanged — the config has to apply the mutation the grammar calls for. Everything in this pack ships the radical form unless its own description says otherwise.
