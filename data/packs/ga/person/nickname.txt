description: Irish pet form or byname. Two things are going on. The productive diminutive is the suffix -ín, added to a shortened stem: Seán -> Seáinín, Máire -> Máirín, Nóra -> Nóirín, Pádraig -> Páidín; several of these (Máirín, Róisín, Bláithín, Gearóidín, Pádraigín, Seosaimhín) have become given names in their own right. The other is the BYNAME — Irish distinguishes two people of the same name by adding Óg, Mór, Bán or Rua after it (Pádraig Óg, Máire Rua, Donncha Bán), the way a townland actually tells them apart. Only attested forms are listed; the -ín suffix is regular, so a config may form more.
