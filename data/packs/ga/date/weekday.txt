description: Weekday (Irish), in the Dé form a date uses — Dé Luain means both Monday and on Monday. Dé takes the genitive after it and prefixes h to a vowel: the bare nouns are Luan, Máirt, Céadaoin, Déardaoin, Aoine, Satharn, Domhnach, and the joined forms here are Dé Luain, Dé hAoine, Dé Domhnaigh. Use these as they stand.
