description: Fruit (Danish). Denmark has an apple culture of its own — Gravensten, Ingrid Marie, Filippa, Guldborg and Belle de Boskoop are named varieties that a Dane will ask for by name — and the wild berries in the middle are picked in hedgerows and plantations rather than bought. Multebær is a northern berry that grows in Denmark barely at all and belongs mostly to Greenland and the Nordic neighbours.
