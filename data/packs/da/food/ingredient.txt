description: Cooking ingredient (Danish). Four of these have no equivalent in a neighbouring list. Rugbrød is the dense sourdough rye that is the base of every smørrebrød and is eaten daily. Remoulade is a Danish condiment closer to a mild tartare sauce than to French rémoulade and goes on chips, fish and roast beef. Ymer and A38 are Danish soured milk products eaten at breakfast with ymerdrys. Karry here means the sweet mild Danish curry powder used in boller i karry and karrysild, not an Indian spice blend.
