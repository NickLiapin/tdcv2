description: School subject (Danish), as the folkeskole names them. Three have no English counterpart. Natur/teknologi and fysik/kemi are single subjects written with a slash, not pairs. Kristendomskundskab is a knowledge subject about religion rather than religious instruction, and it is compulsory. Uddannelse og job and færdselslære are obligatory cross-cutting topics with no exam. Håndværk og design replaced the older sløjd and håndarbejde in 2014.
locale: da
