description: Academic degree / qualification level as awarded in Denmark. The Danish upper secondary school is four parallel tracks, not one: stx is the general gymnasium, hf a two-year course, hhx the commercial and htx the technical one, and all four give access to university. Eux combines a vocational apprenticeship with the general entrance qualification. Two things a European default gets wrong here: professionsbachelor from a university college is a distinct qualification from a university bachelorgrad and is not a lesser version of it, and doktorgrad (dr.phil., dr.med., dr.scient.) is a HIGHER degree awarded above the ph.d. for a substantial published work, not a Danish word for a ph.d.
