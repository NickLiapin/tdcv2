---
description: Complete Danish street names of the kind found in almost every Danish town — trades, nature, national figures, infrastructure. They are complete because Danish binds the thoroughfare type onto the name (Storegade is one word), so a name and a suffix cannot be drawn separately and joined with a space. Note the pattern in the endings: some names carry the definite article as a suffix (Strandvejen, Hovedgaden, Bakkedraget) and some do not (Storegade, Skovvej, Rosenvænget); this is fixed per street and is why whole names are shipped rather than parts. City-specific streets belong in a country pack.
locale: da
