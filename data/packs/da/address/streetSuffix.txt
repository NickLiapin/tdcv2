description: Danish thoroughfare element. Like Swedish and unlike English or Polish, it is NOT a separate word: Danish writes the type joined onto the name as one compound — Storegade, Strandvejen, Rådhuspladsen — never "Store gade". The list is given in both forms on purpose, because Danish marks the definite article as a SUFFIX rather than with a separate word: -gade is the indefinite "street" and -gaden "the street", -vej and -vejen, -park and -parken. Danish street names use both, and which one a given street takes is fixed and not predictable, so a generator should draw a whole name from address/streetName where it can. Allé is one of the very few elements written as a separate word (Roskilde Allé).
locale: da
