description: Trailing element of a Danish place name (Nakskov, Herlev, Rudkøbing, Gentofte). These are the productive Danish toponym endings: -by and -torp/-rup/-strup mark Viking-age farm settlements, -lev an early land grant, -inge and -løse are among the oldest layers, -sted and -um older still, and -købing marks a chartered market town. -gaard is written -gård in modern spelling but survives in the older form in many place and family names. Compose after a cityPrefix.
