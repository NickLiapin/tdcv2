description: Leading element of a Danish place name (Nørresundby, Storeheddinge, Nykøbing, Gammelby). Danish binds it onto the name as one word. Note the cardinal points: a Danish place name uses Nørre-, Sønder-, Øster- and Vester-, not the Swedish Norr-/Söder-/Öster-/Väster- and not the bare Nord-/Syd-. Compose with a citySuffix; it does not by itself name a real place.
locale: da
