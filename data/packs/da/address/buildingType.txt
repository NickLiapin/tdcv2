description: Building type (Danish). Villa, rækkehus, dobbelthus and klyngehus are the four Danish detached/terraced categories used in property records. An andelsboligforening is a housing co-operative that owns the building while the residents own shares in it, and it is one of the three normal ways a Dane holds a home, beside owning and renting. Højskole and forsamlingshus have no equivalent elsewhere: the first is a residential folk high school with no exams, the second the village assembly hall that the association movement built across the country in the 1800s.
