description: Human-readable colour name (Danish, lowercase — Danish does not capitalise colour names). One thing a translator gets wrong every time: lyserød is PINK, not "light red". Danish builds the everyday word for pink out of lys + rød, so lyserød and rosa are the pink shades while a genuinely pale red would be called svag rød or lys rød written apart. Danish colour adjectives also agree with the noun: et rødt hus takes the neuter -t, den røde bil the definite -e, so a config that puts a colour in front of a noun has to know the noun's gender.
locale: da
