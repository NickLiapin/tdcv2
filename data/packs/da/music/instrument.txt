description: Musical instrument (Danish). Two are Danish and not shared with the neighbours. Luren is the bronze-age curved horn dug out of Danish bogs, the oldest playable instrument in the country and the emblem on the butter tin; the Lurblæserne statue in Copenhagen is named for it. Langeleg is the Danish drone zither of the folk tradition, played flat on a table.
