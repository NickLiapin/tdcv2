description: Music genre (Danish). Four are Danish institutions rather than international categories. Dansktop is the Danish equivalent of the Swedish dansband — melodic, singable, with its own charts and its own touring circuit. Fællessang is communal singing from Højskolesangbogen, done standing up at meetings, schools, funerals and on television, and it is closer to a national practice than to a genre. Salmer means the Danish hymn tradition of Kingo, Brorson and Grundtvig, whose texts are sung far outside church. Spillemandsmusik is the fiddle repertoire of the villages.
