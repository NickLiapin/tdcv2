Grammatical gender varies between the two Danish genders, common and neuter — en stol but et bord, en lampe but et spejl — and Danish marks the definite article as a suffix on the noun itself, so the shelf is hylden and the table bordet. A config that composes an adjective or an article in front of these values has to know which gender each noun is; the list gives the indefinite singular base form throughout.
