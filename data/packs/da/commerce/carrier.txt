description: Parcel carriers used in Denmark. PostNord is the merged Danish-Swedish post office and the successor to Post Danmark; DAO grew out of the newspaper distribution network and delivers overnight to the door, which is why it now carries a large share of Danish letters and parcels. GLS runs the dense pakkeshop network — a parcel picked up at a kiosk or a supermarket is the default Danish delivery, not the exception. Burd, Coolrunner and Shipmondo are Danish last-mile and shipping-platform firms, and nemlig.com is the Danish online grocer that delivers its own orders.
