description: Order return reason (Danish). Fortrydelsesret is the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal under forbrugeraftaleloven and covers every distance sale. Bytteret is a separate and purely voluntary exchange period that Danish shops grant on top of it — Danish law gives no right to return a purchase made in a physical shop at all, so a Danish "bytteret 30 dage" sign is a promise from the retailer, not the law.
