Danish adjectives agree with the noun's gender and definiteness, so a neuter noun takes the -t form (et sammenklappeligt bord) and a definite or plural phrase the -e form (den sammenklappelige stol). Svanemærket is the Nordic ecolabel, shared with Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland, and is a stronger selling word on a Danish listing than any adjective about the product itself.
