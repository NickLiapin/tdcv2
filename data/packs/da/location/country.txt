description: Country and territory names (Danish). Danish keeps its own forms for many of them — Tyskland, Frankrig, Storbritannien, Nederlandene, Elfenbenskysten, Ækvatorialguinea — and writes them without an article. Færøerne and Grønland are listed as separate entries and not as parts of Denmark, which is the correct and legally accurate treatment: the three of them form rigsfællesskabet, each of the two has its own parliament, government, language and country code, and neither is in the EU although Denmark is.
