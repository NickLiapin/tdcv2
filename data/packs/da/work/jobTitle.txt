description: Danish job titles across sectors (tech, healthcare, trades, transport, business, service, public). Danish job titles are written in lower case. Several of the public-sector ones have no direct English equivalent and should not be translated away: pædagog is a three-and-a-half-year professional degree covering daycare, after-school care and social pedagogy; social- og sundhedsassistent and social- og sundhedshjælper are the two graded SOSU care qualifications; sundhedsplejerske is the nurse who visits every newborn at home; dagplejer minds up to four children in her own house as a municipal service; fuldmægtig, specialkonsulent and chefkonsulent are graded civil service ranks; sagsbehandler and socialrådgiver are the caseworker and the qualified social worker and are different jobs; kordegn is the parish clerk who also keeps the civil register of births and deaths, because in Denmark the church does that.
locale: da
