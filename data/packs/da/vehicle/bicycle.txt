description: Bicycle type (Danish). In Denmark the bicycle is transport rather than sport: there are more bicycles than people, and about a quarter of all trips in Copenhagen are made on one. Ladcykel is the load-carrying front-box bike used as a family car substitute; christianiacykel is the three-wheeled original, built in the Copenhagen free town of Christiania since 1984, and Danes use the brand name as the generic word for the type.
