description: Vehicle body type (Danish). Stationcar is the Danish word for an estate car — a loan from English that means something else in English, so it is a false friend rather than a borrowing. Varevogn is worth a note of its own: a van on yellow plates is taxed at a fraction of a car's registration tax provided it is used for business, which is why Danish roads carry so many of them and why "papegøjeplader", the half-yellow half-white plates for mixed use, exist at all. Knallert is the moped, which a Dane may ride from fifteen.
