description: Danish female given names, frequency-ordered. Counts are bearer figures on the scale Danmarks Statistik publishes for the leading names; the tail is a smooth decay anchored to the same scale, not the default rank curve. As with the male list the top is flat — Anne, the commonest, is carried by roughly 46 000 of 5.95 million — which is the opposite of the surname distribution in this pack and is deliberate. Several of the commonest names here started as short forms and became names in their own right generations ago: Mette and Grethe from Margrethe, Lone from Abelone, Rikke from Frederikke, Trine and Stine from Katrine and Kristine, Lene from Helene. They are full legal names now, not nicknames, and a Danish record showing Mette is not showing an abbreviation of anything. There are no hyphenated double names in this list, because Danish does not form them the way Swedish does.
