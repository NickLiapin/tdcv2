description: Hobby / interest (Danish). Several are Danish institutions rather than generic pastimes. Foreningsliv is the association life that organises almost everything in Denmark outside work — sport, singing, allotments, shooting, amateur theatre — and a majority of Danes belong to at least one forening. Kolonihave is the allotment with a small locked cabin, held on a waiting list and lived in all summer. Vinterbadning is winter sea bathing from a club jetty, usually with a sauna beside it. Ravsamling is walking the west-coast beaches after a storm looking for amber, and Denmark's amateur metal detector culture is large enough that private detectorists find a substantial share of the country's archaeological hoards. Højskoleophold is a stay of some months at a folk high school, which many Danes do between school and work.
