The first three are the languages of the rigsfællesskab: dansk, and then færøsk and grønlandsk (kalaallisut), each the sole official language of its own country within the realm — Greenlandic is a Inuit language unrelated to any of the others and has been Greenland's only official language since 2009. Tysk comes fourth for a reason: the German minority in Sønderjylland is a recognised national minority with its own schools and its own party, guaranteed by the Bonn-Copenhagen declarations of 1955, and Danish holds the mirror-image status south of the border in Schleswig-Holstein. Dansk tegnsprog was given legal recognition in 2014.
