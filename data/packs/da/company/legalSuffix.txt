description: Company legal / name suffix used in Denmark. A/S is the public limited company and ApS the private one; I/S, K/S and P/S are the three partnership forms. Two are specifically Danish. A.m.b.a. — andelsselskab med begrænset ansvar — is the co-operative form that owns Danish agriculture outright: Arla and Danish Crown are farmer-owned a.m.b.a. Erhvervsdrivende fond is the industrial foundation, and it is the single most distinctive fact about Danish corporate ownership: Novo Nordisk, Carlsberg, Maersk, LEGO's holding structure, Lundbeck and Grundfos are all controlled by foundations rather than by shareholders, which is why Danish company registers are full of them.
