description: Common generic drug names as spelled in Danish. Danish keeps the classical -ph- and -c- that Swedish converts to -f- and -k-: Danish writes phenoxymethylpenicillin, doxycyclin, clindamycin and zopiclon where Swedish writes fenoximetylpenicillin, doxycyklin, klindamycin and zopiklon, so a list copied from the Swedish pack is visibly wrong. Dicloxacillin sits high in this list for a Danish reason: Denmark runs one of the narrowest antibiotic policies in Europe and reaches for a narrow-spectrum penicillin where most countries reach for a broad one.
