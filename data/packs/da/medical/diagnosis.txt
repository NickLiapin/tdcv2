description: Medical diagnosis in Danish that can occur in any patient — no sex or ancestry restriction. Danish everyday clinical language is plainer than the Latinate English default and a translator should not "correct" it: a heart attack is a blodprop i hjertet and a stroke a blodprop i hjernen, literally a blood clot in the heart or in the brain; multiple sclerosis is dissemineret sklerose; rheumatoid arthritis is leddegigt and osteoarthritis slidgigt; cataract is grå stær and glaucoma grøn stær. Roskildesyge is the Danish name for norovirus gastroenteritis, after an outbreak at a Roskilde wedding party in 1952. One deliberate absence: Denmark has NO burnout diagnosis of its own — where Sweden codes utmattningssyndrom as F43.8A, Danish clinicians record a belastningsreaktion or simply arbejdsrelateret stress, so no equivalent has been invented here. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale; ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry.
locale: da
