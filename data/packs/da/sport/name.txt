description: Sport (Danish). Håndbold and badminton are the two Denmark is disproportionately good at — Denmark is the only nation outside Asia ever to have won the Thomas Cup, and the men's handball team has won the Olympic, world and European titles. Speedway is a genuine Danish specialism with a run of world champions. Two are local and have no English equivalent: krolf is croquet crossed with golf, played on village greens mostly by pensioners, and rundbold is the schoolyard bat-and-run game every Danish child plays, the cousin of Swedish brännboll.
