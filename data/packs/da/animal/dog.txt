description: Dog breed (Danish). The first four are the Danish native breeds recognised by Dansk Kennel Klub — broholmeren is the old Danish mastiff, bred back from near-extinction in the 1970s, gammel dansk hønsehund is the Old Danish Pointer, and dansk-svensk gårdhund is the shared Danish-Swedish farm dog. Grønlandshund belongs to the rigsfællesskab: it is a sled dog, kept in Greenland north and east of the Arctic Circle under rules that forbid any other breed there. Denmark also has a hundelov that bans thirteen named breeds outright, so those are absent from this list on purpose rather than by oversight.
