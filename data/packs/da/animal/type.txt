description: Animal, general (Danish). One trap for a translator: marsvin means BOTH the guinea pig and the harbour porpoise, and the porpoise is the common cetacean in Danish waters, so a Danish record naming a marsvin is genuinely ambiguous without context. The last block is the rigsfællesskab — isbjørn, moskusokse, rensdyr, hvalros and slædehund belong to Greenland, not to Denmark proper.
