description: Court type (Thai judiciary). Thailand runs THREE separate court systems side by side, not the single hierarchy a European config assumes — ศาลยุติธรรม (the courts of justice, ศาลชั้นต้น to ศาลอุทธรณ์ to ศาลฎีกา), ศาลปกครอง (the administrative courts, with their own supreme court ศาลปกครองสูงสุด) and ศาลรัฐธรรมนูญ (the constitutional court), and a case never moves between them
locale: th
