description: Thai post-nominal. Thai has NO generational suffix — no Jr., Sr., II or III — because the 1913 Surname Act gave every family its own surname and children are told apart by their nickname, not by a number; a config that appends 'Jr.' to a Thai name is wrong. What Thai does put after a name is the degree abbreviation and the abbreviation of a royal decoration (ม.ป.ช., ป.ช., ท.ช. and the rest), which appear on formal name lists and business cards.
