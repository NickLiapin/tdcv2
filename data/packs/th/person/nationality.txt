description: Nationality in Thai. Thai has no separate adjectival form the way European languages do — a nationality is the country name with ชาว prefixed to it (ชาวไทย, ชาวญี่ปุ่น, ชาวอเมริกัน), and this file holds the bare country stem so a config can prefix ชาว or use it as-is after เชื้อชาติ / สัญชาติ. Note also that Thai paperwork distinguishes สัญชาติ (citizenship) from เชื้อชาติ (ethnicity) and asks for both on the same form, which no European identity document does.
locale: th
