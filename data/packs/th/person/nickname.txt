description: Thai nickname (ชื่อเล่น). This is the single most distinctive fact about Thai naming and a config cannot infer it: virtually every Thai is given a one- or two-syllable nickname in infancy and is called by it for life — by family, by friends, by colleagues, by newsreaders and on the credits of a film. The legal given name may go unused for years outside paperwork. Crucially the nickname is NOT derived from the legal name the way Janek comes from Jan or Kasia from Katarzyna, so this list has no relationship to person/male/firstName.txt or person/female/firstName.txt and must be drawn independently. Nicknames are as often an English loanword (เบียร์ Beer, กอล์ฟ Golf, ไอซ์ Ice, เค้ก Cake, บอส Boss) as an ordinary Thai word (นก bird, ปลา fish, ส้ม orange, หมู pig, เล็ก small), and most are unisex.
