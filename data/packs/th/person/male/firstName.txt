description: Thai male given name (ชื่อจริง). Thai writes the given name FIRST, like English and unlike Chinese or Japanese — but a Thai is addressed BY the given name, never by the surname: สมชาย ชินวัตร is คุณสมชาย, and 'Mr Shinawatra' is not something a Thai would say. This is the opposite of the European default and a config that formats honorific + surname will be wrong. Given names are long by European standards because they are built from Pali and Sanskrit roots, and many are chosen by a monk from the day of the week the child was born. In daily life the given name is largely unused anyway — see person/nickname.txt, which is what people are actually called. The weights are the pack's gentle default curve; Thai given names are not sharply concentrated.
locale: th
