description: Thai honorific / title. Thai puts the title before the GIVEN name, never before the surname: คุณสมชาย is Khun Somchai, and there is no Thai equivalent of 'Mr Smith'. นาย / นาง / นางสาว are the legal คำนำหน้านาม printed on the national ID card; เด็กชาย and เด็กหญิง replace them for children under fifteen. คุณ is the everyday polite address and is not gendered. The military, police, monastic and academic titles below are used as part of the written name in Thailand, not only in office.
