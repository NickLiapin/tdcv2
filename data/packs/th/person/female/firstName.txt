Note that Thai given names are frequently UNISEX: สมพร, สมจิตร, ธนพร, กมล and the ศิริ- and สุ- compounds are carried by men and women alike, so this list deliberately OVERLAPS with person/male/firstName.txt and gender cannot be recovered from a given name alone. What does mark gender in Thai is the title in front (นาย / นาง / นางสาว), not the name. The weights are the pack's gentle default curve; Thai given names are not sharply concentrated.
