description: Thai family name (นามสกุล). Thai surnames do NOT inflect for gender — a man and a woman in the same family carry the identical string — which is why there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack; its absence is the design, not an omission. The weights are the pack's gentle default curve rather than a steep one, and that is deliberate: the 1913 Surname Act (พระราชบัญญัติขนานนามสกุล) required every family to register a surname AND forbade duplicating one already registered, so Thai surnames are long Pali-Sanskrit compounds that are near-unique to one family. There is no Smith, no Kim, no Nguyen head to the distribution — the flat end of the curve is the true shape. For the same reason this list is much shorter than the European ones: it holds attested Thai family names rather than a frequency-ranked top-N, because no meaningful top-N exists. Note also that Thai people are addressed by GIVEN name, not surname — คุณสมชาย, never คุณชินวัตร — so a config that formats 'Mr <surname>' produces something no Thai would say.
