description: Academic degree / qualification level as awarded in Thailand. NOTE: the vocational certificates ประกาศนียบัตรวิชาชีพ (ปวช.) and ประกาศนียบัตรวิชาชีพชั้นสูง (ปวส.) sit between school and university and have no clean European equivalent — ปวช. runs alongside upper secondary and ปวส. above it, below a bachelor's. Thai bachelor's degrees are named by faculty (…บัณฑิต), master's …มหาบัณฑิต, doctorate …ดุษฎีบัณฑิต.
