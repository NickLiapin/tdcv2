Thailand has three seasons and no winter in the temperate sense, so the real seasonal events are the two monsoons — มรสุมตะวันตกเฉียงใต้ brings the rains from June, มรสุมตะวันออกเฉียงเหนือ the cool dry air from November — plus tropical storms rated by strength (ดีเปรสชัน, โซนร้อน, ไต้ฝุ่น), flooding, drought and the ฝุ่น PM2.5 haze season of the cool months. No blizzard, ice storm or cold snap appears here because none of them happen.
