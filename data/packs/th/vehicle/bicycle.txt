description: Bicycle type (Thai). Thai names the two sport bikes after animals — จักรยานเสือหมอบ (road, "crouching tiger") and จักรยานเสือภูเขา (mountain, "mountain tiger") — จักรยานแม่บ้าน is the everyday step-through bike most households own, and สามล้อถีบ (the pedal samlor) is still a working passenger vehicle in provincial towns rather than a museum piece.
locale: th
