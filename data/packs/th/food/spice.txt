description: Spice / herb (Thai). Note that กะเพรา (holy basil, the sharp peppery one in ผัดกะเพรา) and โหระพา (Thai sweet basil, the aniseed one in green curry) are DIFFERENT herbs used in different dishes, as is ใบแมงลัก (lemon basil) — English collapses all three to "basil", and a config that treats them as interchangeable will produce nonsense. ข่า (galangal) is likewise not ขิง (ginger).
locale: th
