description: Cooking ingredient (Thai). น้ำปลา (fish sauce) and กะปิ (fermented shrimp paste) are the base seasonings of Thai cooking the way salt and butter are in Europe — they are in almost everything, and ปลาร้า (fermented fish) plays the same role in the Northeast. The four sweet-sour-salty balancers น้ำตาลปี๊บ (palm sugar), น้ำมะขามเปียก (tamarind water), มะนาว and น้ำปลา are what a Thai cook reaches for instead of stock and cream.
