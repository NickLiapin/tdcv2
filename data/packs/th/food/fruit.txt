description: Fruit (Thai). The list is tropical because Thailand is — durian, mangosteen, rambutan and longan are the everyday market fruit, while apples, pears and cherries are imported specialities and are deliberately absent. สตรอว์เบอร์รี is the one temperate fruit kept, because it is genuinely grown in the northern highlands. กล้วยน้ำว้า and กล้วยหอม are different bananas used for different things and are both listed.
