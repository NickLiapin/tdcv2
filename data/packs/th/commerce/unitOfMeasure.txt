description: Unit of measure for products (Thai). Thailand is metric, but the traditional units are alive: ขีด is 100 g and is how markets sell meat, and land is measured in ไร่ (1600 m2) - งาน (400 m2) - ตารางวา (4 m2), never in hectares. Thai also counts with classifiers — ชิ้น, อัน, ตัว, ใบ, เส้น — which vary by the kind of object.
