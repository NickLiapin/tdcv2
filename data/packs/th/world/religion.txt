description: Religion (Thai). Thai Buddhism is THERAVADA (roughly 93% of the population), not the Mahayana of East Asia, and the Muslim minority (~5%, mostly Sunni) is concentrated in the deep south. Religion is not exclusive here: การนับถือผี — spirit belief, the ศาลพระภูมิ spirit house standing outside almost every Thai building — coexists with Buddhism inside the same household rather than competing with it, so a config that treats one religion per person as the whole picture will be wrong about Thailand.
