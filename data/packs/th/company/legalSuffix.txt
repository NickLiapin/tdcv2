description: Company legal form / name tail used in Thailand. WARNING for configs: the Thai form BRACKETS the company name rather than trailing it — บริษัท <name> จำกัด (Co., Ltd.) and บริษัท <name> จำกัด (มหาชน) (PLC), with ห้างหุ้นส่วนจำกัด leading a partnership — so this value cannot simply be appended to a name. The Latin Co., Ltd. / PCL forms are the ones Thai firms print on their own signage; the rest of the list is the descriptive tail words Thai company names use.
locale: th
