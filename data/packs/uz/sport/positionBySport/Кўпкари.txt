description: Roles in кўпкари, the mounted game played across Central Asia in which riders compete for a goat carcass. Чавандоз is the rider; оқсоқол the elder who presides; жарчи the crier who announces the prizes; соврин берувчи the person who puts up a prize, which at a large game can be a car. От боқувчи is the groom who keeps the horses.
locale: uz
