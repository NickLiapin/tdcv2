description: The tail of an Uzbek settlement name. Обод (settled, prosperous) and кент (town, from Sogdian) are the two workhorses and between them account for a large share of the real map. Several suffixes name water — булоқ, қудуқ, сой, ариқ — which is how oasis settlements were named in the first place. Note қалъа, whose тутуқ белгиси (ъ) marks a genuine glottal break, not a soft sign.
locale: uz
