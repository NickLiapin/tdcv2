description: Words that open an Uzbek place name. Compose one with a citySuffix and you get a plausible settlement: Янги + обод, Оқ + тепа, Чуқур + кўл. Янги (new) and Эски (old) are the two commonest, and the colour words Оқ, Қора, Қизил, Кўк are older than either. Three of these prefixes show the letters Cyrillic has that Latin has to spell with two characters: Ўрта, Ғарбий, Чўл.
locale: uz
