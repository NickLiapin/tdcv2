description: What follows the name on an Uzbek street sign, already carrying the possessive suffix: Алишер Навоий кўчаси, not кўча. Кўчаси is the everyday word; проспекти and булвари are Russian-era borrowings for the wide roads; маҳалласи and гузари name the neighbourhood and its little square rather than a road. The abbreviated forms кўч. and пр. are what actually fits on a plate.
locale: uz
