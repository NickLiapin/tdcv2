description: Street names found across Uzbekistan: poets and scholars first, then civic virtues, trades and landscape. The scholarly block at the head is national rather than local — Ал-Хоразмий, whose name became the word algorithm, Ибн Сино, Абу Райҳон Беруний, Имом Бухорий, Мирзо Улуғбек. Compose one before a streetSuffix: Алишер Навоий кўчаси. Watch Ал-Хоразмий and Абу Райҳон side by side: the х and the ҳ are different letters and different sounds, and Latin spells them x and h.
locale: uz
