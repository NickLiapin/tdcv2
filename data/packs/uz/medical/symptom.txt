description: Symptoms as a patient reports them. Uzbek names most pains as a possessive compound: бош оғриғи is head-its-pain, кўкрак оғриғи chest pain, бел оғриғи lower back pain. Жиғилдон қайнаши — heartburn — is literally the boiling of the жиғилдон, the crop or gullet.
locale: uz
