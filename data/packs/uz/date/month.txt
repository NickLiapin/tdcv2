description: Month names, lower case, as Uzbek writes them. THEY ARE RUSSIAN-DERIVED, not Turkish: январ, феврал, март, апрел, not Ocak or Subat. They arrived in the Soviet period and were not replaced after 1991. In a date the day takes a hyphen before the month — 5-март — because the day number is an ordinal and the hyphen is how Uzbek attaches an ordinal suffix to a numeral.
