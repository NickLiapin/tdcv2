description: The sixteen points of the compass in Uzbek, capitalised. Uzbek builds the intermediate points by hyphenating the two cardinals with a linking -и: Шимоли-шарқ, Жануби-ғарб. The three-part points repeat the first element — Шимоли-шимоли-шарқ. Ғарб and Шарқ show two of the letters Cyrillic writes with one character and Latin with two.
locale: uz
