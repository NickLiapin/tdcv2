description: Flowers. Лола — the tulip — is first for good reason: the wild tulips of the Chatkal and Nurata ranges are the ancestors of every garden tulip, and лолақизғалдоқ is the red steppe poppy that covers the foothills in April. Бойчечак is the snowdrop that marks the end of winter and has its own children's song.
