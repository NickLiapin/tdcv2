description: Legal forms of an Uzbek company, abbreviations first and then the full names they stand for. МЧЖ is масъулияти чекланган жамият, the limited liability company that most private firms use; АЖ is the joint-stock company; ЯТТ is the individual entrepreneur. The abbreviations are built from Cyrillic initials, so МЧЖ in Cyrillic is MChJ in Latin — three letters becoming four.
