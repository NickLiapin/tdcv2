description: Corporate verbs in the -моқ dictionary form, which is how Uzbek verbs are cited. Uzbek builds most of these from a noun plus -лаштирмоқ: рақам gives рақамлаштирмоқ (to digitise), марказ gives марказлаштирмоқ (to centralise). Stack the suffixes and you get интеграциялаштирмоқ, which is as long as this kind of word gets.
locale: uz
