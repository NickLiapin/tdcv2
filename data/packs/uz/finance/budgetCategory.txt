description: Household budget lines. Three of them are specific to how money moves here: тўй харажатлари (wedding costs, which can be a household's largest single outlay), қариндошларга ёрдам (support sent to relatives) and садақа ва хайрия (alms and charity). Коммунал тўловлар covers the utilities as one line, the way a bill arrives.
locale: uz
