UZBEKISTAN USES THE POST-SOVIET NUMERIC SCALE, not European 34-46 and not S/M/L: the number is roughly half the chest measurement in centimetres, so 48 is a medium man's shirt. Imported and online clothing carries S/M/L too, so a real dataset holds a mixture; the numeric run is what a home-made label shows.
