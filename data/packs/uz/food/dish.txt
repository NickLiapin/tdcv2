description: Uzbek dishes. Палов leads, and тўй оши is the wedding version cooked in a cauldron for hundreds. Then the dumplings and soups — чучвара, манти, лағмон, шўрва, мастава — the breads, and the sweets. Сумалак is made once a year from sprouted wheat for Наврўз and is cooked overnight by a group of women taking turns at the cauldron.
