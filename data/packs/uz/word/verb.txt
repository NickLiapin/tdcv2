description: Core verbs in the -моқ dictionary form. Бўлмоқ (to be) and қилмоқ (to do) head the list and between them build a large part of the language: Uzbek forms hundreds of compound verbs by putting a noun in front of қилмоқ. The -моқ ending is the citation form only; a real sentence never uses it bare.
locale: uz
