description: Titles and forms of address, informal first. Ака and опа — older brother and older sister — are attached to a first name to address almost any adult; домла and устоз are the teacher's titles. Ҳожи is used of someone who has made the pilgrimage. The military and professional ranks at the end are capitalised.
locale: uz
