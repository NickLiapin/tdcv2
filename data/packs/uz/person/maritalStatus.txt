description: Marital status. Uzbek distinguishes the man's and the woman's side of the same event: уйланган is said of a man who has married, турмушга чиққан of a woman, and бева аёл and бева эркак are the widow and the widower. Расмий никоҳда and диний никоҳда separate the civil registration from the religious ceremony, which do not always coincide.
