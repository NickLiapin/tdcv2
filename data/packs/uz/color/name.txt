description: Colour names. The base terms оқ, қора, қизил, кўк, яшил, сариқ come first, then the modified shades. Uzbek forms a colour from a thing with -ранг: қаҳваранг from coffee, ғиштранг from brick, зумрадранг from emerald. Кўк covers both blue and the green of growing things, which is why яшил is listed separately.
locale: uz
