description: Employee benefits. Ўн учинчи ойлик — the thirteenth salary — is an annual bonus paid as an extra month, inherited from the Soviet system and still offered. Санаторий йўлланмаси is a voucher for a stay at a sanatorium, another survival, and болалар боғчаси means the employer provides a nursery place.
