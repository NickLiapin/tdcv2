description: Technical nouns. Most came through Russian and are unchanged — сервер, протокол, индекс, интерфейс — but the data words are native: маълумотлар базаси for database, жадвал for table, устун for column, қатор for row. Объект needs a hard sign in Cyrillic, which is why Latin writes it obyekt with a bare y.
