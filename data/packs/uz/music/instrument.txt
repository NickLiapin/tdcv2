description: Musical instruments, national ones first. Дутор is the two-stringed lute almost every household once had, танбур the long-necked one that carries мақом, ғижжак the spike fiddle, and доира the frame drum. Карнай is the long straight trumpet blown at weddings — if you hear it in a street, there is a тўй.
