description: Healthcare jobs. Фелдшер and акушерка are the mid-level clinical roles inherited from the Soviet system, which has no direct western equivalent: a фелдшер runs a rural health post and treats patients. Бош врач is the chief physician of a hospital, and it keeps the Russian word rather than using шифокор.
