description: Forms of employment contract. Муддатли and муддатсиз шартнома are the fixed-term and open-ended contracts; ўриндошлик is holding a second post alongside a main job, a formal category in Uzbek labour law. ЯТТ is the registered sole trader, and it appears here as a way of being engaged rather than employed.
