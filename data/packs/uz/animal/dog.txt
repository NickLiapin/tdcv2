description: Dog breeds in Uzbek. The first four are Central Asian and belong here rather than in any European list: the алабай shepherd dog, the този and тайғон sighthounds bred for coursing, and the ўзбек овчаркаси. Ovcharka, from the Russian for shepherd, has become the ordinary Uzbek word for a guard dog of any size.
