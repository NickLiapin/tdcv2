description: Delivery companies and the generic Uzbek terms for them. The international couriers keep their Latin trademarks — a Cyrillic pack does not repaint a logo. Everything after Cainiao is an ordinary Uzbek noun phrase: почта хизмати, курьер хизмати, шаҳар ичи курьери. Курьер needs a soft sign in Cyrillic, which Latin cannot show, so it is written kuryer there.
