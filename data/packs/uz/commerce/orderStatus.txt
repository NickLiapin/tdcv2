description: The states an online order passes through, in order. Uzbek marks most of them with the past-tense passive -ди ending: қабул қилинди, тасдиқланди, жўнатилди, етказилди. The two that break the pattern are progressive — тўлов кутилмоқда and йиғилмоқда — because they describe something still happening.
