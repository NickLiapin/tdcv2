description: Departments of a shop or market, ordered the way you would walk one: food first, then household chemicals, cosmetics, clothing, electronics, hardware. Гўшт растаси and балиқ растаси use раста, the word for a market row rather than a shop aisle, because that is still where most of this trade happens.
