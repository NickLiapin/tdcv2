description: Units of measure, metric plus the local land measures. Сотих and таноб are the ones to notice: a сотих is one hundred square metres and is how house plots are advertised, while таноб is the older unit still heard in farming talk. Центнер, a hundred kilograms, is standard in grain and cotton reporting.
