description: How a purchase is paid for in Uzbekistan. Нақд пул still leads by volume, but the domestic card and QR systems have moved fast, and бўлиб тўлаш — instalments — is offered on almost any consumer good. Насия is the older word for buying on trust from a neighbourhood shop, with no paperwork behind it.
