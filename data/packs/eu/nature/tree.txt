description: Tree (Basque). HARITZA, the oak, is not a neutral entry: the oak of Gernika under which the Bizkaian charters were sworn is the emblem of Basque self-government, and "Gernikako Arbola" is the anthem. Basque distinguishes the tree from its fruit with -ondo: sagar is an apple, sagarrondo the apple tree.
