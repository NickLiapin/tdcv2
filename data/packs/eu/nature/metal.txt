description: Metal (Basque). BURDINA, iron, is the one with history behind it: the Basque hills held the ore that fed hundreds of water-powered forges, and the Basque word for such a forge, OLA, is the second element of a whole family of surnames — Olabarria, Mendiola, Loiola, Olaizola. Reading those names is reading an industrial map.
