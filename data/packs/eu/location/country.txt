description: Country and territory names as Basque writes them, following Euskaltzaindia's recommended forms. Translated, not transliterated: Germany is Alemania, the Netherlands Herbehereak ("the low lands"), the United Kingdom Erresuma Batua, the United States Ameriketako Estatu Batuak. Compound names are joined with "eta" (Trinidad eta Tobago, Sao Tome eta Printzipe, Antigua eta Barbuda) and a final -y becomes -i in Basque spelling (Paraguai, Uruguai). Names that keep a c, q, v, w or y do so because Basque orthography keeps those five letters for foreign proper names only.
