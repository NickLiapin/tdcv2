Euskara, the first value, is a LANGUAGE ISOLATE: after two centuries of searching nobody has demonstrated a relative for it anywhere on earth — not Iberian, not Berber, not Georgian, not any of the dozens of proposals — and it is older in place than every Indo-European language that now surrounds it, Spanish, French, Latin and Celtic all having arrived after it was already there. It is also the only surviving pre-Indo-European language of western Europe. Every other name in this list belongs to a family; the first one does not.
