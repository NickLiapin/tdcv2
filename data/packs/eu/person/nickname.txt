description: Basque hypocoristic. The diminutive is made by PALATALISING a consonant rather than by adding a syllable: s and z become x, t becomes tt, l becomes ll, n becomes ñ. So Jose is Joxe, Ines is Iñes, polit ("pretty") becomes pollit, and the affection is carried by the sound itself. On top of that sit the endings -txo, -txu and -txa. Several of these hypocoristics have hardened into full legal given names — Patxi, Txomin, Koldo and Iñaki all began here.
