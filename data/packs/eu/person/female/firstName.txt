description: Basque female given names, ordered by rough real-world commonness. Many are Basque nature words (Haizea "the wind", Izar "star", Lorea "the flower", Enara "swallow", Ihintza "dew", Elorri "hawthorn"), many others are the sanctuary that the Virgin is venerated at (Arantzazu, Itziar, Begoña, Uxue, Izaskun, Arrate, Larraitz) used directly as a given name, and a third group are Basque coinages for Spanish devotional names (Edurne for Nieves, Nekane for Dolores, Garbiñe for Purísima, Sorkunde for Concepción). A few — Amets "dream", Alaitz, Iraitz — are used for either sex, so this list and the male one may overlap. Weights are the project default decay curve, not measured counts.
