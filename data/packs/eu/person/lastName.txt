description: Basque surnames. They are overwhelmingly TOPONYMIC: each one names a farmstead (baserri) or a landscape feature, which is why they are long and transparent — Etxeberria is "the new house" (etxe + berri + -a), Zubizarreta "the place of old bridges", Goikoetxea "the upper house", Mendizabal "broad mountain", Iturriaga "the place of the spring". They DO NOT inflect for gender: a Basque surname is a place, and a place has no sex, so there is no feminine list here and its absence is the design. Spelling follows the Euskaltzaindia standard; the Spanish and French civil registries often hold a Romanised variant of the same name (Etxeberria as Echeverría, Agirre as Aguirre, Muxika as Múgica) — that variant is registry data and belongs to the Spain and France country packs, not here. Ordered by rough real-world commonness; the weights are the project default decay curve, not measured counts, and that flatness is close to the truth for once, because each Basque surname names a separate farmstead and none reaches the share a Kim or a Nguyen holds.
