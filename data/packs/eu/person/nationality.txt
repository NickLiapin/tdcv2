description: Nationality / demonym (Basque), formed with the suffix -ar or -tar on the country name. The first value is the one that matters: EUSKALDUN does not mean "born in the Basque Country" — it is built from "euskara" plus the possessive -dun, so it means literally "one who has Basque", a speaker. Basque identity is stated in the language as a linguistic fact rather than a territorial or ancestral one, and "euskal herritar" (Basque-Country-dweller) is the separate word for the territorial sense.
locale: eu
