description: Colour name (Basque, with the definite article, lower case). Two things a config cannot infer. URDINA covers a stretch of the spectrum that no single English or Spanish word covers: it is blue, but grey hair is ile urdina ("blue hair") and so is a grey-blue sea or sky, so a colour swatch mapped from #0000FF is only part of what the word does. And GORRIA ("red") doubles as "bare" — larrugorri, "red-skinned", means naked — which is why red turns up in Basque idiom far more often than a colour term normally would. The -xka ending on gorrixka, horixka and berdexka is the productive "-ish" suffix.
