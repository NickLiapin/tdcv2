description: Sport (Basque). About a third of this list has no equivalent anywhere else. PILOTA is the Basque ball game and it is a family, not one game — esku-pilota is played with the bare hand, zesta-punta with a curved basket, and pala, errebotea, laxoa and xarea are separate codes with separate courts. HERRI KIROLAK, the rural sports, are competitive versions of farm and quarry work: aizkolaritza is log-chopping, harri-jasotzea stone-lifting, segalaritza scything, txinga-eramatea carrying weights, idi-probak dragging a block with oxen. ESTROPADAK are the traineru rowing regattas raced along the coast every summer.
