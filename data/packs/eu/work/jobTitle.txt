description: Basque job titles across sectors. Basque builds an agent noun with -ARI / -LARI / -TZAILE / -GILE, which is why so many of these end alike: irakasle (teacher), arrantzale (fisher), sukaldari (cook), itzultzaile (translator), okin (baker). Four of them exist only where Basque is spoken: a BERTSOLARI improvises sung verse in competition, a PILOTARI plays the Basque ball game professionally, an AIZKOLARI competes at log-chopping, a TXISTULARI plays the txistu at civic ceremonies — all four are paid occupations, not hobbies. Police, tax and civil-service job titles that name one state's institutions are country data and are not here.
locale: eu
