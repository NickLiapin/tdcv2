description: Writing system (Basque). Basque itself is written in the Latin alphabet and always has been — there is no Basque script. What its alphabet does have is Ñ and a set of DIGRAPHS that stand for one sound each: tx is English "ch", tz and ts are two distinct affricates that Spanish does not distinguish, tt and dd are palatal stops, ll and rr are their own letters. X on its own is "sh", never "ks". C, Q, V, W and Y are in the alphabet but are used only in foreign words and names, so a sorting or validation routine written for Spanish will mis-handle Basque in both directions.
