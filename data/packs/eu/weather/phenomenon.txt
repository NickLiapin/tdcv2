description: Weather phenomenon (Basque). The rain words are not synonyms and a translator that flattens them loses the point: ZIRIMIRIA is the fine persistent drizzle that hangs over the Atlantic side for days, LANGARRA a lighter one still, ZAPARRADA a sudden downpour and ERAUNTSIA a violent squall. HEGO HAIZEA is the föhn off the Pyrenees — hot, dry and blamed locally for headaches.
