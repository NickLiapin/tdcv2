description: Trailing element of a Basque place name. These are the productive toponymic suffixes and second members of the compound: -aga and -eta both mean "place of, abundance of" (Gorostiaga "place of hollies", Zubieta "place of bridges"), -tegi "shelter, dwelling", -di "grove", -pe "under", -gain "on top of", -alde "beside", -ondo "next to", -barren "the lower end", -goiti "upper", -beiti "lower". Joined to a cityPrefix they make a name of the right Basque shape; they are word-forming elements, not municipalities.
locale: eu
