description: Street names of the kind found across the whole Basque-speaking area, in two real shapes: a bare noun compounded with the generic (Iturri kalea, Merkatu plaza, Geltoki bidea) and an adjective carrying the article (Nagusia, Zaharra, Berria). The personal names are Basque writers, artists and navigators whose streets exist on both sides of the border — Etxepare printed the first book ever published in Basque in 1545, Axular wrote its greatest prose, Mitxelena built the modern standard, Elkano completed the first circumnavigation. Streets belonging to one particular town, and the Spanish- or French-language street names that share the same signs, are country data and are not here.
