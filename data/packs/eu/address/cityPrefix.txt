description: Leading element of a Basque place name. Basque toponymy is built from a small stock of landscape words that combine freely, which is exactly why Basque surnames are long and readable — the surname IS the farmstead name. Combine one of these with a citySuffix (Etxe + berri = Etxeberri, Mendi + zabal = Mendizabal, Zubi + eta = Zubieta) and you get a name of the right shape. It does not by itself name a real municipality; real Basque towns belong to the Spain and France country packs.
locale: eu
