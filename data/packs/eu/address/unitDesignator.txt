description: Basque address unit / interior designator. The Iberian pattern applies on both sides of the border and it is not the Polish or German one: a flat is identified by FLOOR plus SIDE, written after the street and number — Zubieta kalea 12, 3. ezk. means number 12, third floor, left-hand door. "ezk." is ezkerra (left), "esk." eskuina (right).
