description: Basque thoroughfare type — it goes AFTER the street name, not before it: Zubieta kalea, Askatasunaren etorbidea, Foruen enparantza. Spanish and French put the generic first (calle Mayor, rue de la Paix) and a config that copies that order produces something no Basque sign shows. The word also carries the definite article. KARRIKA is the northern (French-side) word for a street where the south says KALEA, and ENPARANTZA the Bizkaian word for a square where Gipuzkoa says PLAZA — both are standard, and both are in this list on purpose. BIDEGORRIA, "the red road", is the modern Basque coinage for a cycle path.
