description: Compass direction (Basque). IPAR and HEGO are not only compass points: ipar(haize) is the north wind and HEGOA is the hot dry föhn that comes down off the Pyrenees onto the Basque coast, so "hegoa dabil" is a weather report, not a bearing. The same two words also name the French and the Spanish Basque Country (Iparraldea, Hegoaldea).
