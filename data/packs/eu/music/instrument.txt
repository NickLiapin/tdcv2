description: Musical instrument (Basque). The first six are Basque instruments and have no equivalent elsewhere: the TXISTU is a three-hole flute played one-handed while the same player beats the DANBOLIN drum with the other; the TXALAPARTA is a plank struck by two players in interlocking rhythm, descended from cider-apple pressing; the ALBOKA is a hornpipe with two cane tubes and a cow-horn bell; the TRIKITIXA is the diatonic accordion, always paired with the PANDEROA; the TTUN-TTUNA is the string drum of the northern valleys.
