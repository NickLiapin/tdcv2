description: Common adjective (Basque, bare form). Basque adjectives FOLLOW their noun and are not inflected for gender, because the language has none: etxe handia, etxe handiak, mendi handia — one form throughout. The article and every case ending attach to the LAST word of the noun phrase, which is the adjective when there is one, so it is handi + a, not etxea handi.
locale: eu
