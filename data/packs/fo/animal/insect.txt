description: Insect and small invertebrate in Faroese. The list is SHORT on purpose. The Faroes are treeless, windswept and cool, so the insect fauna is among the poorest in Europe - no native wasp fauna to speak of, hardly any butterflies, no beetle fauna worth the name - and the language accordingly has few native insect words. Padding this out with borrowed names would misrepresent both the islands and the vocabulary.
