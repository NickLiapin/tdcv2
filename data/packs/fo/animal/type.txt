description: Animal in Faroese. Worth knowing before generating anything: the Faroes have NO native land mammals at all. Every four-legged animal on this list that actually lives there - seyður, neyt, ross, hara, mús, rotta, ketta, hundur - was carried in by people. The native fauna is birds, fish and sea mammals, which is why grind (pilot whale), kópur (seal) and nísa (porpoise) are everyday words while bjørn, úlvur and fílur are words for animals in books.
