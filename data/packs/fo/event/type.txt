ÓLAVSØKA is the national holiday on 28-29 July, when the Løgting opens and the whole country goes to Tórshavn; KAPPRÓÐUR, the rowing race between village crews, is the sporting event of the summer and is held at Ólavsøka and at every regatta before it. Neither has an equivalent elsewhere.
