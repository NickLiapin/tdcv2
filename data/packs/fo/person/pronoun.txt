description: Faroese personal pronoun, given as nominative/dative because Faroese has four cases and a pronoun changes shape in each (hann / hann / honum / hansara). Faroese has THREE third-person plural pronouns and they are chosen by gender: TEIR for a group of men, TÆR for a group of women, TEY for a mixed group or for neuter - and tey is also what is used for one person of unstated gender. English and Danish have one word here and Faroese has three, which no config can guess.
locale: fo
