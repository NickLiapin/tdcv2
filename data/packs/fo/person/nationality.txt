description: Nationality in Faroese. FØROYINGUR is the demonym and is not a synonym for dani: Faroese people hold Danish citizenship but the Faroe Islands are outside the European Union, and a Faroese passport says Føroyar on it. Faroese builds a demonym with -ingur, -ari, -i or -maður and there is no single rule.
locale: fo
