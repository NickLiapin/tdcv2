---
description: Evocative noun for a company name, to compose with a legal suffix (Varðin P/F, Sp/f Bára). The stock is sea, rock, weather and birds, which is what Faroese companies are actually named after - the fishing company Varðin, the salmon farmer Bakkafrost, the power utility SEV. There is no tradition of naming a company after its founder here the way there is in Denmark.
locale: fo
