description: Company legal form in Faroese. P/F (partafelag) and Sp/f (smápartafelag) are the FAROESE forms and are what a Faroese company registration actually produces - Bakkafrost is P/F Bakkafrost. They correspond to Danish A/S and ApS, which also appear because Danish-registered companies trade here, but a Faroese company is not an A/S. Note the slash: P/F and Sp/f are written with it and are not abbreviated any other way.
locale: fo
