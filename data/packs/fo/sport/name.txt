description: Sport in Faroese. KAPPRÓÐUR - village crews racing wooden boats - is the national sport and the centrepiece of every summer regatta, and it is not a curiosity: the boats are the traditional Faroese ones and the crews are from real villages. Fótbóltur is the mass participation sport, and the Faroe Islands field their own national team because they are a full UEFA and FIFA member in their own right, separately from Denmark.
