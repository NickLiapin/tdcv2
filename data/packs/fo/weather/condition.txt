description: Weather in Faroese, and the vocabulary is unusually fine-grained because the weather is the country's main subject. MJØRKI is fog and is a daily condition rather than an event; SÚLD is the fine wet drizzle that is not quite rain; ÆL is a sudden squall of rain, hail or snow that passes in minutes; ROK is a gale strong enough to stop the ferries; ÓDN is a full storm. English collapses most of these into rain and wind.
locale: fo
