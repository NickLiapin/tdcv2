description: Academic or vocational qualification in Faroese. SVEINAPRÓGV is the journeyman's certificate at the end of a trade apprenticeship and ranks as a full qualification here, not as a lesser one. Degrees above it are taken at Fróðskaparsetur Føroya or, for most subjects, in Denmark - the islands have one university and it does not cover every field.
