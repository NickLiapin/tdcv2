description: Adjective for a product listing, in the masculine singular citation form. VATNTÆTTUR and VINDTÆTTUR sit at the top rather than the bottom because in the Faroes they are the first thing a buyer looks for. BURÐARDYGGUR is the standard Faroese word for sustainable and UMHVØRVISVINARLIGUR for environmentally friendly - both are native compounds, not borrowings.
locale: fo
