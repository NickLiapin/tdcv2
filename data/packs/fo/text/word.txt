description: Common Faroese words, for slugs, usernames and tags. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Faroese book at all. The search that other locales use to find a public-domain corpus (query l.fo) returns "No records found", while the same search returns books for Icelandic and Danish, so there was nothing to build from and this file was written by hand rather than harvested. It is not the output of data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs and is much smaller than a corpus-backed locale's. The vocabulary is weighted towards sea, weather, land and work because that is what the language talks about most.
