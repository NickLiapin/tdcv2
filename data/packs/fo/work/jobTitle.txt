description: Job title in Faroese across sectors. The order is not the European default and that is the point: the sea comes first, because fishing, fish processing and salmon farming are what the Faroese economy is. NETABØTARI (net mender) and ALIFISKAARBEIÐARI (salmon farm hand) are ordinary jobs here. TYRLUFØRARI (helicopter pilot) is on the list because the helicopter is scheduled public transport in the Faroes, not an emergency service only.
