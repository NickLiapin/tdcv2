description: The generic element that ends a Faroese settlement name, glued on with no space: Klaks+vík, Fugla+fjørður, Sør+vágur, Tórs+havn, Viðar+eiði. Almost every Faroese village is named for the landform it stands on, which is why so many of these are a bay, a fjord, a headland or a gorge. GJÓGV, a cleft in a sea cliff, is the extreme case: it is a common landform here, the village Gjógv is named for one, and no Danish word covers it.
locale: fo
