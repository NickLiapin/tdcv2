description: Directional qualifier used with a Faroese street, district or place name. Faroese says EYSTUR for east where Icelandic says austur and Danish oest - the same word, but the Faroese spelling is the one that matters when you are checking a pack is really Faroese. Faroese also keeps a separate set of comparatives (norðari, sunnari, eystari, vestari) for telling two neighbouring places apart.
