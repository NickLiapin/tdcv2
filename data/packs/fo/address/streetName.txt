description: Faroese street names on the ordinary compound pattern: a specific element welded to a generic one, written as one word, with the house number after a space and no comma (Skúlavegur 12). These are the recurring names found in town after town rather than one town's register - a full street list for Tórshavn or Klaksvík is tied to that town's FO postcode and belongs in a Faroe Islands country pack. The last ten are the preposition type (Yviri við Strond, Undir Bryggjubakka): a real Faroese address written as several words, where the preposition is part of the name and is capitalised with it.
