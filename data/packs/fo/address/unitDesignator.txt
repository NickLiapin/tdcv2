description: Secondary address unit in Faroese. HÆDD is a storey, and Faroese counts the ground floor as the first (1. hædd), the continental way rather than the British way. Faroese addresses do not use the Danish th./tv. door markers; the side is written out (høgru megin, vinstru megin) or the flat is numbered.
locale: fo
