---
description: The generic element that ends a Faroese street name. As in Icelandic and unlike Danish or English it is NOT a separate word: it is welded onto the specific element and the compound is written as one word (Landa+vegur = Landavegur, Skúla+gøta = Skúlagøta). GØTA is the ordinary Faroese word for a street - Icelandic writes the same word gata, and the oe is the Faroese signature. Each element is a noun with its own gender.
locale: fo
