description: Leading qualifier of a Faroese place name. Faroese has TWO patterns and they are written differently. The glued kind takes no space and no hyphen (Norðragøta, Syðrugøta, Miðvágur, Norðskáli) - note that Icelandic hyphenates the same thing and Faroese does not. The second kind is a PREPOSITION standing as its own word before the name (Við Áir, Undir Gøtueiði, Á Hjalla, Í Botni); it is a settled Faroese address type with no Icelandic or Danish parallel. Neither kind names a real place on its own - combine it with a toponym.
locale: fo
