description: Country and territory name in Faroese. Faroese naturalises a foreign place name rather than borrowing its spelling, and the results differ from Danish as often as not: Týskland, Bretland, Frakland, Grikkaland, Turkaland, Sveis, Niðurlond, Svøríki, Kekkia, Hvítarussland. The list is sorted in FAROESE alphabetical order, which is not the Latin one: the accented vowels á í ó ú ý sort immediately after their base letters (so Ísland follows Iran), and æ and ø close the alphabet. Faroese has no c, q, w, x or z of its own - the handful here appear only inside foreign names.
