SÓLJA is the national flower of the Faroe Islands, the marsh marigold that fills the wet ground in spring; FÍFA is cotton grass and LYNGUR heather, and between them they are most of what actually flowers on a Faroese hillside. The garden flowers further down the list survive here only with shelter.
