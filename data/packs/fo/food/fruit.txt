description: Fruit in Faroese. Two things a config cannot infer. First, almost none of this grows here: the only fruit the Faroes produce in any quantity is rabarba, and krækiber is the wild berry of the heath. Everything else arrives by ship or plane. Second, and more important for anyone joining these words to others - EPLI IN FAROESE IS A POTATO, not an apple. The apple is SÚREPLI. See food/vegetable.
locale: fo
