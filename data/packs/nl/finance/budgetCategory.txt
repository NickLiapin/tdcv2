description: Household budget or spending category (Dutch). Amounts are in EUR in both the Netherlands and Belgium, and Dutch writes them with a full stop for thousands and a comma for decimals (45.000,50 EUR). Zorgverzekering is a separate fixed line here because Dutch basic health insurance is compulsory for every resident and bought from a private insurer as a monthly premium, so it is a household bill rather than a tax. Gemeentelijke heffingen bundles the municipal and water-board levies that arrive as one annual bill.
