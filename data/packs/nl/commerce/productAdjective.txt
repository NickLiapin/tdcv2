Dutch attributive adjectives take an -e ending in almost every position (de handige lamp, het handige apparaat, een handige lamp) and drop it only before a singular indefinite neuter noun (een handig apparaat), so a config that glues these straight onto a noun needs to add the -e itself. Adjectives precede the noun.
