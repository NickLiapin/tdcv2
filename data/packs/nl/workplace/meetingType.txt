description: Type of workplace meeting (Dutch). Functioneringsgesprek and beoordelingsgesprek are two distinct annual conversations under Dutch practice: the first is a two-way review of how the job is going, the second is the employer's one-way appraisal. The ondernemingsraad (works council) is statutory in Dutch firms above fifty staff, so its meeting is a normal calendar item.
locale: nl
