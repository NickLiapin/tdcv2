description: Employment benefit as offered in the Netherlands. Vakantiegeld is statutory rather than optional: every Dutch employee is owed at least 8 percent of annual salary as holiday allowance, normally paid out in May. Reiskostenvergoeding, thuiswerkvergoeding and the fietsplan are tax-facilitated Dutch schemes. Belgian employers offer a partly different set (maaltijdcheques, ecocheques, bedrijfswagen), so this list is Netherlands-first.
