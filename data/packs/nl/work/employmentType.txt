description: Employment / contract type under Dutch labour law. The zzp'er, a self-employed person working without staff, is a very large and specifically Dutch category rather than a fringe case. Oproepcontract, nulurencontract and min-maxcontract are the three named on-call forms, payroll and uitzendkracht are two legally distinct kinds of hired-in labour, and a leerwerkplek is the paid workplace half of a BBL vocational track. Part-time work is the Dutch norm rather than the exception. Belgian contract law names its categories differently.
