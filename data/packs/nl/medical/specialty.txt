description: Medical specialties as the Dutch specialist register names them. Several names are not calques of the English: gastroenterology is Maag-darm-leverziekten (abbreviated MDL), general surgery is simply Heelkunde, ENT is Keel-neus-oorheelkunde (KNO) and paediatrics is Kindergeneeskunde. Ouderengeneeskunde (elderly care medicine) is a registered specialty that exists in the Netherlands and almost nowhere else. Flanders uses Gastro-enterologie, Pediatrie and Urgentiegeneeskunde where the Netherlands uses Maag-darm-leverziekten, Kindergeneeskunde and Spoedeisende geneeskunde; the Netherlands form is given here. Dutch primary care is built around the huisarts, who is the gatekeeper to every hospital specialty on this list.
