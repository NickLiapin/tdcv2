description: Common generic drug names (INN) spelled as the Dutch Farmacotherapeutisch Kompas spells them. Dutch adds a final -e to many INN stems (amoxicilline, not amoxicillin; metformine, not metformin; gabapentine, not gabapentin) and dutchifies a few whole words (hydrochloorthiazide). Acenocoumarol is the vitamin-K antagonist of the Netherlands and Flanders, where German-speaking practice uses phenprocoumon, and nitrofurantoïne is the Dutch first choice for a simple urinary tract infection.
locale: nl
