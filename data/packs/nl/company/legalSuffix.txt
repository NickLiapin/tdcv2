description: Company legal form and name suffix in Dutch, covering both countries. The Netherlands writes them with points - B.V., N.V., V.O.F., C.V. - alongside Stichting, Vereniging, Coöperatie, Maatschap and Eenmanszaak. Belgium writes them without points and reformed them in 2019: BVBA was abolished and renamed BV, CVBA became CV, Comm.V became CommV, and NV kept its name; BVBA is kept here because it is on every Belgian document written before 2019. VZW is the Belgian non-profit, the Dutch counterpart is Stichting. Koninklijke is not a legal form but a royal designation the monarch grants to a company of a hundred years standing and unimpeachable reputation, and it is written in front of the name.
