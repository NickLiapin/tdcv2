description: Vehicle body type (Dutch). The two-wheeler entries at the end are Dutch legal vehicle classes, not body styles: a snorfiets is limited to 25 km/h and wears a blue plate, a bromfiets to 45 km/h on a yellow plate, and a brommobiel is a 45 km/h microcar drivable on a moped licence. These distinctions govern insurance, helmets and whether the vehicle belongs on the cycle path, and they have no equivalent in most locales.
