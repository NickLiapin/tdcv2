description: Property listing status (Dutch). Verkocht onder voorbehoud is a distinct and very common Dutch state, not a synonym for sold: the deal is agreed but still conditional, normally on the buyer securing a mortgage or on a building survey, and the property often returns to the market. A Dutch private buyer also has three days of statutory bedenktijd after signing in which the sale can be undone without penalty.
