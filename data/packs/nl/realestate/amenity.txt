description: Property amenity as written in a Dutch listing. Berging (a lockable private storage room, usually on the ground floor or in the basement) and Fietsenstalling are near-universal features of Dutch flats and are always named in an advertisement. Dubbel glas and Zonnepanelen appear because a Dutch listing must state an energy label, so insulation and solar are selling points rather than extras.
