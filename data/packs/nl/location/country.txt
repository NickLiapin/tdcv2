description: Country and territory names (Dutch). Dutch country names diverge from English far more than a reader expects - Duitsland, Frankrijk, Oostenrijk, Zwitserland, Zweden, Griekenland, Hongarije, Oekraine, Oezbekistan - so these are genuine translations, not respellings. Two notes on the Kingdom itself: 'Nederland' is the country and 'Holland' is only two of its twelve provinces, so Holland is not listed as a country name; and the Kingdom of the Netherlands is four countries, which is why Curacao, Aruba and Sint Maarten appear here in their own right while 'Nederlandse Antillen' is retained for records predating its dissolution in 2010.
