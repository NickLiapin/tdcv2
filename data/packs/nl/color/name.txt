Dutch does not capitalise colour names - unlike German next door, where they are nouns and always capitalised - so do not carry the de pack's convention over. Oranje is the Dutch national colour, after the House of Orange-Nassau: it is the shirt of every national team ('Oranje' is the team), the colour of Koningsdag, and it carries a cultural weight no other name in this list has.
