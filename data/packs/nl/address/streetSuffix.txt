description: Dutch thoroughfare element - normally FUSED onto the end of the street name as one word (Kerkstraat, Julianalaan, Molenweg), not written separately. This is the opposite of English and Spanish, where the type is a separate word. Several of these are specific to a country shaped by water: gracht is a canal in a town, singel a moat-turned-ring-road, kade and wal a quay, dijk a dyke, polder reclaimed land, and they name real streets everywhere in the Netherlands.
locale: nl
