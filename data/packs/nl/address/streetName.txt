description: Street names common across the Netherlands and Flanders. A Dutch street name is normally ONE fused word carrying its own thoroughfare type (Kerkstraat, Julianalaan, Molenweg), not a name plus a separate suffix - so these are complete street names, and address/streetSuffix.txt is the element already fused onto them rather than something to append. The Dutch address puts the street FIRST and the number AFTER it: 'Kerkstraat 12', never '12 Kerkstraat'. City-specific streets belong in a country pack.
