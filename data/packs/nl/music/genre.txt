description: Music genre (Dutch). Hardstyle and gabber are Dutch-born electronic genres - gabber came out of Rotterdam - and Dutch trance is an export of its own. Nederpop and Nederhop are pop and hip hop sung in Dutch. Levenslied and its tearjerker form smartlap, and kleinkunst (the sung-cabaret tradition), have no clean English equivalent. Carnavalsmuziek belongs to the Catholic south of the Netherlands and to Flanders, not to the whole country. Loanwords keep their real spelling.
