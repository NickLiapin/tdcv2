description: Sport (Dutch). The Dutch sporting landscape is genuinely unlike its neighbours'. Korfbal is a mixed-gender team sport invented in the Netherlands and played almost nowhere else. Speed skating has national-sport status, which is why schaatsen, langebaanschaatsen, marathonschaatsen and shorttrack are separate entries rather than one. Kaatsen (Frisian handball) and fierljeppen (pole-vaulting across a canal) are Frisian sports with no equivalent anywhere. TRAP: unqualified 'hockey' in Dutch means FIELD hockey, in which the Netherlands is a leading nation - ice hockey is always 'ijshockey', never bare 'hockey'. Baseball has its own Dutch word, 'honkbal', and a real domestic league. Veldrijden (cyclo-cross) is followed nationwide but is overwhelmingly a Flemish sport rather than a Dutch one.
