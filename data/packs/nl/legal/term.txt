description: Legal term (Dutch). Dutch splits what English calls a judgment three ways and the words are not interchangeable: a vonnis is a first-instance decision of a rechtbank, an arrest is a decision of a gerechtshof or the Hoge Raad, and a beschikking is a decision in proceedings that have no opposing party. Cassatie is review on points of law only, so the Hoge Raad does not re-examine the facts. Griffierecht is the court fee every claimant pays up front.
locale: nl
