description: Common noun (Dutch), lowercase. Dutch does NOT capitalise nouns - the neighbouring de pack does the opposite, and that convention does not carry over; capitalising these is a visible spelling error. Two pairs where Dutch splits what English joins: gezin is the household family and familie the wider relatives; mens is a human being while man and vrouw are the sexes. Fiets, dijk and polder are in the list as ordinary high-frequency nouns, not local colour.
locale: nl
