description: Common adjective (Dutch), lowercase, in the uninflected base form. Dutch adjectives take an -e ending before most nouns (een groot huis but het grote huis), so a config that glues these straight onto a noun will sometimes produce the wrong form. Gezellig and lekker are here because they are among the highest-frequency adjectives in the language and neither translates cleanly - gezellig is the warm-companionable quality of a place or an evening, lekker covers tasty, pleasant and feeling-good alike.
