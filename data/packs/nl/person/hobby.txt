description: Hobby / interest (Dutch). Note what is NOT here as a hobby: ordinary cycling. In the Netherlands a bicycle is transport, not recreation, so 'fietsen' as a pastime means the recreational day-trip (fietstocht) and the sport is named separately as wielrennen. Schaatsen, zeilen, volkstuinieren and klaverjassen are Dutch pastimes with no clean English equivalent.
