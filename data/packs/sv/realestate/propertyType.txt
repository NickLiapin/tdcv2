description: Property type (Swedish). Bostadsrätt is the normal way Swedes own a flat — you buy a share in the co-operative that owns the building, not the flat itself — while hyresrätt is a rental contract with regulated rent that is itself traded. Ägarlägenhet, true freehold apartment ownership, has only existed since 2009 and is rare.
