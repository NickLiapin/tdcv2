description: Swedish surnames, frequency-ordered, with real bearer counts rather than a rank curve — the concentration is the whole point. Sweden's -sson patronymics are extraordinarily dominant: Andersson and Johansson together are carried by roughly 460 000 of about 10.5 million people, and the top ten names cover close to a sixth of the country, so a flat curve would misrepresent Sweden badly. Counts for the leading names are Statistics Sweden (SCB) bearer figures rounded to the nearest thousand; the tail is a smooth decay anchored to the same scale. Swedish surnames DO NOT inflect for gender — Andersson is Andersson for a woman — so there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack and its absence is deliberate. The -dotter forms (Andersdotter, Larsdotter) are historical: they died out when patronymics froze into inherited family names in the late 1800s and are not current surnames, so they are not in this list. A Swedish full name carries a single surname.
locale: sv
weighted: true
