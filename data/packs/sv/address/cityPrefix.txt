description: Leading element of a Swedish place name (Norrköping, Storvik, Gammelstad, Nyköping). Swedish binds it onto the name as one word, so these are stems without the final -a of the free-standing adjective: Stor-, not Stora-. Compose with a citySuffix; it does not by itself name a real place.
