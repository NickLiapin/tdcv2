description: Swedish thoroughfare element. Unlike English or Polish this is NOT a separate word: in Swedish the type is written joined onto the name as one compound word, after it — Storgatan, Sveavägen, Kungsholmstorg — never "Stor gatan". Most take the definite article as part of the word (-gatan, -vägen, -torget); a few are conventionally indefinite (-gränd, -plan, -väg in some regions). Use address/streetName for complete names; this list is for composing new ones.
