description: Sport in Mongolian. THE FIRST THREE ARE ЭРИЙН ГУРВАН НААДАМ, "the three games of men" — wrestling, archery and horse racing — which are not three sports that happen to be popular but a single institution: they are what Наадам is, they are held together every July, and they are the frame Mongolians understand sport through. Wrestling in particular is the national sport by a distance. After them come the Olympic disciplines Mongolia actually competes in — freestyle wrestling, judo, boxing, shooting — where a country of three and a half million has taken medals well out of proportion to its size, and then the ordinary international list.
