description: Mongolian familiar or pet name. MONGOLIAN MAKES A PET NAME BY A RULE, NOT FROM A SEPARATE VOCABULARY: clip the name to its first element and add a long vowel, and VOWEL HARMONY DECIDES WHICH VOWEL. A back-vowel stem takes -аа (Бат to Батаа, Ганбат to Ганаа, Наранцэцэг to Нараа), a front-vowel stem takes -ээ (Сүх to Сүхээ, Энхбаяр to Энхээ, Мишээл to Мишээ), a rounded back stem takes -оо (Дорж to Доржоо, Болормаа to Болороо) and a rounded front stem takes -өө (Мөнхбат to Мөнхөө, Өлзий to Өлзийгөө). Those four shapes of one suffix are the clearest everyday demonstration of Mongolian vowel harmony there is, and a config that appends a single fixed suffix will produce a form no Mongolian would write. The rule applies to both sexes; there is no separate male and female nickname stock. Because the pet name is DERIVED from the given name rather than chosen independently, a row whose nickname shares no stem with its given name is incoherent — worth knowing before joining this list to a name at random.
