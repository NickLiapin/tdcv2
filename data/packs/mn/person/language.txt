description: Language spoken, named in Mongolian. The head of the list is what is actually spoken in Mongolia: монгол is the state language, казах is the language of the Kazakh minority in Баян-Өлгий in the far west and is the country's only other language with schools and a press of its own, and буриад, ойрад, дархад and урианхай are Mongolic varieties spoken inside the country. орос was the compulsory foreign language until 1990 and англи replaced it afterwards, which is why those two are the commonest second languages by a wide margin and split neatly by generation.
