description: Mongolian female given name. The given name is the real name here as it is for men — the element before it is the father's name, not a surname. WHAT MARKS A NAME FEMALE IS THE SECOND ELEMENT, and the set is small, systematic and easy to read once known: -маа (Tibetan ma, the feminine particle: Дэлгэрмаа, Болормаа, Жаргалмаа), -цэцэг flower (Алтанцэцэг, Наранцэцэг), -туяа ray of light (Оюунтуяа, Энхтуяа), -чимэг ornament (Оюунчимэг, Уранчимэг), -гэрэл light (Гэрэлмаа, Оюунгэрэл), -зул butter-lamp (Мөнхзул, Хонгорзул), -лхам (Tibetan lhamo, goddess: Цэрэнлхам) and -хорол (Tibetan khorlo, wheel: Ичинхорлоо). The FIRST elements are shared with men and carry no gender at all: оюун intellect, мөнх eternal, алтан golden, наран sun, энх peaceful, болор crystal, ариун pure, уран skilled. A SUBSTANTIAL SET OF NAMES IS GENUINELY UNISEX and this list deliberately overlaps person/male/firstName.txt for them — Энхжаргал, Мөнхбаяр, Ганбаяр, Золжаргал, Батжаргал, Оюун-Эрдэнэ, Од-Эрдэнэ, Нэргүй, Билгүүн, Пүрэвсүрэн, Лхагвасүрэн and the seven weekday names are all worn by both sexes, because Mongolian has no grammatical gender and nothing in the morphology forces a choice. A config that infers sex from a Mongolian given name will be wrong for a real share of the population. Weights are RANK-DERIVED Zipf decay rather than measured counts; no Mongolian name frequency table is published.
