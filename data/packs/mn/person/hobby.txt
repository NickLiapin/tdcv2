description: Hobby or interest in Mongolian. The first block is specific to Mongolia and has no European counterpart: шагай наадах is playing with sheep anklebones, of which there are several distinct games; сурын харваа is Mongolian archery, one of the three games of Наадам; бөх барилдах is Mongolian wrestling, another; and морь унах with морь уяах, training a racehorse for the summer races, is the third. хөөмийлөх is throat singing and морин хуур is the horsehead fiddle. Note that Mongolian expresses a hobby as a VERB PHRASE — ном унших, "to read books", rather than the noun "reading" — so most values here are two or three words with the verb LAST, and a config that treats them as nouns and puts them in front of a verb will produce a sentence with two verbs and no object.
