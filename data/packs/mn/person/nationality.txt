description: Nationality or ethnic name in Mongolian. The head of the list is Mongolia's own composition rather than an alphabetical world tour: халх is about four fifths of the population and is the group the standard language is named for, казах is the largest minority, and дөрвөд, буриад, баяд, урианхай, дархад, захчин, өөлд, торгууд, мянгад, хотон and үзэмчин are recognised ястан. халимаг (Kalmyk, in Russia) and өвөр монгол (Inner Mongolian, in China) are Mongols outside the state, which matters because "Mongolian" as a nationality and "Mongolian" as an ethnicity are not the same set — roughly twice as many Mongols live outside Mongolia as in it. The rest are the ordinary world nationalities.
