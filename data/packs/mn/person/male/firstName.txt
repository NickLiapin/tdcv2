description: Mongolian male given name — and in Mongolia the given name IS the name: it is how a person is addressed, introduced, listed, alphabetised and printed on a team sheet, because the element in front of it is the father's name rather than an inherited surname (see person/lastName.txt). Four layers show through and all four are live. The NATIVE layer is built from transparent words and is by far the most productive: бат firm, мөнх eternal, энх peaceful, эрдэнэ jewel, ган steel, баяр joy, жаргал happiness, баатар hero, болд steel, зориг courage, наран sun, алтан golden, чулуун stone, төмөр iron, хүрэл bronze — and they combine freely, which is why Батбаяр, Мөнхбат, Энхжаргал and Ганбаатар all exist side by side. The TIBETAN-BUDDHIST layer came with the Gelug church and is enormous: the seven weekday names Даваа, Мягмар, Лхагва, Пүрэв, Баасан, Бямба and Ням are themselves Tibetan planet names given to a child born on that day, and Дорж (vajra), Цэрэн (long life), Сүрэн, Лувсан, Гомбо, Жамбал, Данзан, Пунцаг, Содном and Дугар are all Tibetan. The SANSKRIT layer arrives through Tibetan: Занабазар, Мижид, Очир. And there is a small AVOIDANCE layer that is genuinely Mongolian and worth knowing before a config treats it as corrupt data — Нэргүй means "no name", Хүнбиш means "not a person", Энэбиш means "not this one", given in families that had lost infants to make the child uninteresting to whatever was taking them. They are real registered names and Нэргүй is common. VOWEL HARMONY is live and visible: a name is either all-back (Батбаяр, Ганбаатар, Наранбаатар) or all-front (Мөнхбат, Энхтүвшин, Түвшинбилэг), never mixed, and any suffix a config appends must take its vowel from the stem. Weights are RANK-DERIVED Zipf decay rather than measured counts — Mongolia publishes birth registrations but not a name frequency table, and inventing shares would be worse than saying so.
