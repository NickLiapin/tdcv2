description: Government body in Mongolia. Mongolian is the official language of one country, so these are that country's actual ministries and general agencies rather than a generic set — and they are named as they are because they are the words. What deliberately is NOT here belongs to the Mongolia country pack: the register number (регистрийн дугаар) and its check letter, the provinces and their codes, the phone plan and the banks. Note that a Mongolian ministry name is a chain of genitives ending in ЯАМ, ministry, and a general agency ends in ЕРӨНХИЙ ГАЗАР — the head noun comes last, the reverse of "Department of Health".
