description: Official document type in Mongolia. Two entries are the country's own and a config should know what they are without this pack pretending to generate them: ИРГЭНИЙ ҮНЭМЛЭХ is the national identity card, carried by every citizen from sixteen, and МАЛЫН ГЭРЧИЛГЭЭ is the livestock movement and ownership certificate, which is a real document a herding household deals with. THE NUMBERS ON THESE DOCUMENTS — the register number with its two Cyrillic prefix letters and its check digit above all — ARE NOT IN THIS PACK AND MUST NOT BE: they belong to the Mongolia country pack, which owns identifiers, formats and check digits. This file holds the NAMES of kinds of document, which is language.
