description: Country name in Mongolian. THIS LIST IS SHORTER THAN THE 233-ENTRY REFERENCE ON PURPOSE: it holds the countries with a Mongolian name form I could stand behind, and stops rather than inventing Cyrillic spellings for the rest. Two things about the naming. FIRST, MONGOLIAN DROPS THE RUSSIAN -ИЯ ENDING that most of these names arrived through: Италия becomes Итали, Испания Испани, Германия Герман, Австралия Австрали, Норвегия Норвеги, Турция Турк. A form that keeps the -ия is a Russian name, not a Mongolian one. SECOND, a handful of countries have GENUINELY MONGOLIAN names owing nothing to Russian, and they are the ones that matter most: ХЯТАД for China (from the Khitan), ОРОС for Russia, ЭНЭТХЭГ for India, СОЛОНГОС for Korea, БАЛБА for Nepal, ГҮРЖ for Georgia, МЯСЫР and ЕГИПЕТ both current for Egypt. Those are translations rather than transliterations and a config must not respell them from English.
