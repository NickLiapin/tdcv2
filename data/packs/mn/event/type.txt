НААДАМ heads the list and is not a festival among festivals: the national games of wrestling, archery and horse racing are a public holiday of several days in July, and a very large share of everything a Mongolian events dataset contains happens either at Наадам or at ЦАГААН САР, the lunar new year. Both move against the Gregorian calendar and both stop the country.
