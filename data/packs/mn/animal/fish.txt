ТУЛ heads the list: the Mongolian taimen is the largest salmonid in the world, lives in the northern rivers, can exceed a metre and a half, and is catch-and-release only by law. Mongolia is landlocked and its fish vocabulary is river vocabulary — зэвэг lenok, хадран grayling, цурхай pike, гутаарь burbot, хилэм sturgeon — and there is no native sea-fish stock in the language at all; the sea names at the end of the list are borrowings that arrived with imported food.
