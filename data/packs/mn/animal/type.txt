description: Animal in Mongolian. The first five are ТАВАН ХОШУУ МАЛ, the five snouts — horse, cattle, camel, sheep and goat — which is not a list but a category of Mongolian: the five livestock species a herding household counts its wealth in, named together in one fixed phrase, and the reason Mongolia has more livestock than people by a factor of about twenty. The block after them is the wild fauna of the country and much of it exists nowhere else: тахь is the Przewalski horse, the only never-domesticated horse left alive, extinct in the wild by 1969 and reintroduced from zoos; мазаалай is the Gobi bear, of which a few dozen survive; ирвэс is the snow leopard; хулан the Asiatic wild ass; аргаль the largest wild sheep in the world.
locale: mn
