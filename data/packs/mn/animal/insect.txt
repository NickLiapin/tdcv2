description: Insect and other small crawling creature in Mongolian. царцаа, the locust, is an agricultural event rather than a curiosity — swarms damage pasture in dry years. Note that Mongolian хорхой is a general word for any grub, worm or creeping thing and is not specific to insects; it is also the second half of олгой хорхой, the Gobi "death worm" of folklore.
