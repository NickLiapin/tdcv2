ИДЛЭГ ШОНХОР, the saker falcon, is the national bird and the centre of a real economy — Mongolia is the world's main source of sakers for Gulf falconry — and бүргэд, the golden eagle, is the bird the Kazakh eagle hunters of Bayan-Ölgii fly at foxes and wolves from horseback. АНГИР, the ruddy shelduck, is treated as sacred and is not hunted, because its colour is the colour of a lama's robe. ТООДОГ, the great bustard, is the heaviest flying bird in the country and is a protected species.
