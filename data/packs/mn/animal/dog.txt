БАНХАР COMES FIRST BECAUSE IT IS THE ONLY NATIVE ONE: the Mongolian bankhar is the livestock-guarding dog that has worked with herding camps for millennia, big, black-and-tan, four-eyed (the tan spots above the eyes), and it is a landrace rather than a registered breed — there is no Mongolian kennel club standard, which is exactly why it belongs at the head of a Mongolian list and not in a table of breeds. хотошо is its Buryat counterpart. Everything after them is an imported breed, named in Mongolian as Mongolian names them, which is by Russian-mediated transcription.
