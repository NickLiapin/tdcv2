description: Medical specialty in Mongolian. The names are the Greek-derived international terms in Cyrillic, which is what a Mongolian hospital signboard carries, arriving through Russian medical education — so it is Кардиологи and not a Mongolian compound. Two entries are the country's own: УЛАМЖЛАЛТ АНАГААХ УХААН is traditional Mongolian medicine, which has a state-licensed profession, its own hospitals and its own faculty at the medical university, and ЗҮҮ ТӨӨНӨ is the moxibustion and acupuncture practised within it.
locale: mn
