A note on the fit to this country: Mongolians sit in the ЗҮҮН АЗИЙН group and share its principal markers — the ALDH2 variant that causes the alcohol flush reaction, high hepatitis B carriage, elevated stomach and liver cancer — but they also carry a distinct North and Central Asian component that none of the twelve names captures, and the dairy question cuts across the whole scheme: Mongolians largely lack the European lactase-persistence allele while eating a heavily dairy diet, because цагаан идээ is fermented. A config that treats "East Asian" as a complete description of a Mongolian cohort will misattribute risk in both directions. Each value is the filename of a file in medical/diagnosisByAncestry/.
