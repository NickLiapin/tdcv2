description: Leading element of a Mongolian place name. TWO OF THESE ARE A GENUINE MONGOLIAN ORIENTATION SYSTEM AND NOT COMPASS WORDS AT ALL: ар means the shaded northern side of a mountain, behind it, and өвөр the sunny southern side, in front of it — which is why the provinces are Архангай and Өвөрхангай, "behind the Khangai" and "in front of the Khangai", and why the Mongol homeland of Inner Mongolia is Өвөр Монгол. The colour words carry fixed landscape senses too: улаан marks iron-red earth, цагаан chalk or salt ground, хар bare ground where the snow does not lie or a spring-fed source, and хөх the blue-grey of distant hills. баян, rich, means good pasture and is the commonest first element in the country. Combine with a real toponym; on its own an entry here does not name a settlement.
