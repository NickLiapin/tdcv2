description: Name element found on Mongolian streets — khans and historical figures, writers and composers, civic values, and landscape words. Compose it BEFORE a streetSuffix, and note the construction: THE NAME TAKES THE GENITIVE AND THE THOROUGHFARE TYPE FOLLOWS IT — Их сургуулийн гудамж, Энх тайваны өргөн чөлөө — which is the reverse of "Main Street" and of "ulica Długa" alike. The street list of one particular city belongs in a country pack, not here. Three groups are worth naming. The imperial figures — Чингис хаан, Хубилай, Өгэдэй, Мандухай, Занабазар, Богд хаан — were unusable as street names for most of the twentieth century and came back after 1990. The revolutionary layer — Сүхбаатар, Чойбалсан — largely survived that turn, unlike in Eastern Europe. And a large share of Mongolian street names are simply the words for landscape and weather, because that is what the naming stock of the language is made of.
