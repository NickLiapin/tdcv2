description: Trailing element of a Mongolian place name, and almost every one is a LANDSCAPE WORD, because that is what steppe and desert toponymy is made of: уул a mountain, гол a river, нуур a lake, булаг a spring, худаг a well, даваа a pass, толгой a hill, хөндий a valley, ам a valley mouth, говь the gravel desert, элс sand, нуруу a range, сайр a dry watercourse, жалга a gully, бүрд an oasis. Three are not terrain and are worth separating out: овоо is the stone cairn at a pass where travellers add a stone and walk round it three times, so a place named -овоо is named for a shrine; хийд and сүм are a monastery and a temple, and a striking number of Mongolian settlements exist because a monastery was there first; and -баатар, as in Улаанбаатар, is "hero" and belongs to the twentieth century rather than to the landscape.
locale: mn
