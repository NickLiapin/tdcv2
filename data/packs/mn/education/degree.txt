description: Academic qualification in Mongolia. The ladder is бүрэн бус дунд (basic education, nine years), бүрэн дунд (complete secondary, twelve years), then дипломын боловсрол, бакалавр, магистр and доктор. Ph.D and Sc.D are written in Latin on Mongolian diplomas.
