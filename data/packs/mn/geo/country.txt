description: The world's countries as MONGOLIAN names them, weighted by population in millions. Two rules run through the list. FIRST, MONGOLIAN DROPS THE RUSSIAN -ИЯ ENDING that most of these names arrived through: Италия becomes Итали, Испания Испани, Германия Герман, Норвегия Норвеги, Австралия Австрали, Турция Турк. Keeping the -ия produces a Russian name, not a Mongolian one, and that is the commonest way this list gets faked. SECOND, a set of countries has GENUINELY MONGOLIAN names owing nothing to Russian or English, and those are translations rather than transliterations: ХЯТАД for China (from the Khitan people who ruled north China before the Mongols), ОРОС for Russia, ЭНЭТХЭГ for India, СОЛОНГОС for Korea, БАЛБА for Nepal, ГҮРЖ for Georgia. A config must not respell those from the English name. Each value is the filename of a file in geo/capitalByCountry/, geo/currencyByCountry/ and geo/currencyCodeByCountry/.
