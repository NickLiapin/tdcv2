description: Household budget category in Mongolian. ТҮЛШ, fuel, is a category of its own here rather than part of utilities, because in a ger district household the winter coal and wood bill is a large separate line and is what the air-quality debate in Ulaanbaatar is about. МАЛЫН ТЭЖЭЭЛ, livestock fodder, is a household budget line for a herding family and rises sharply in a dzud winter.
