description: Colour name in Mongolian. Two things a config should know. FIRST, MONGOLIAN SPLITS BLUE WHERE ENGLISH DOES NOT: хөх is the dark blue and цэнхэр the light blue, and they are separate basic colour words, not shades of one — the flag is described with хөх, the sky with цэнхэр. SECOND, a large part of the colour vocabulary of the language is HORSE COLOUR and is not usable of anything else: халиун, хээр, зээрд, хул, шарга, буурал and соохор all name coat colours and a Mongolian would not use them of a car or a shirt, so they live in animal/horse.txt and not here. The remaining entries are the ordinary colour words plus the mineral names — оюу turquoise, номин lapis, маргад emerald — which do double duty as colours the way "turquoise" does in English.
