description: Music genre in Mongolian. The first four are Mongolian genres, not styles within a world category: УРТЫН ДУУ, the long song, stretches a handful of words over minutes of ornamented melody and is on the UNESCO list; БОГИНО ДУУ is its short, metrical counterpart; ХӨӨМИЙ is throat singing; and ТУУЛЬ is the sung heroic epic. Modern Mongolian popular music is a real industry and Mongolian hip hop in particular has been a major domestic genre since the 1990s.
