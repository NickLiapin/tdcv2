VEGETABLES ARE A RECENT ARRIVAL: Mongolia's growing season is about ninety frost-free days, the traditional diet had essentially no plant food in it, and most of the words here are twentieth-century. The four that hold up in the climate — төмс potato, лууван carrot, байцаа cabbage, манжин beet — are what is actually grown and stored, and they are what a Mongolian shop has in March. The wild alliums gathered on the steppe are in food/spice.txt, where they belong, because that is how they are used.
