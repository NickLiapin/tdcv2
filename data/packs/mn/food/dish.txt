description: Dish in Mongolian. THE MONGOLIAN KITCHEN IS BUILT ON MEAT, WHEAT AND MILK AND ALMOST NOTHING ELSE, which is a fact about a country where crops barely grow: бууз and хуушуур and банш are all the same filling of minced mutton in different wrappings, цуйван is fried noodles with meat, хорхог and боодог are cooked with fire-heated stones inside a milk churn or inside the skinned animal itself, and борц is air-dried meat that keeps through a winter. ЦАГААН ИДЭЭ, "white food", is the whole dairy half of the cuisine — ааруул, аарц, хурууд, өрөм, тараг, ээзгий, айраг — and it is seasonal, made in summer when the animals are in milk and dried to last. There is no vegetarian tradition and very little vegetable in the traditional repertoire; the vegetables that do appear arrived in the twentieth century.
