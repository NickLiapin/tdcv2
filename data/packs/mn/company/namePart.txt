Mongolian company naming leans hard on a small stock of auspicious words, and the pattern is worth knowing: МӨНХ eternal, ЭРДЭНЭ jewel, АЛТАН golden, ХИШИГ blessing, БУЯН merit, ӨЛЗИЙ good fortune, ТҮМЭН ten thousand, ГЭГЭЭ radiance — which is why a Mongolian business register has hundreds of firms whose names differ only in which auspicious word came first. The second block is landscape and empire: ХАНГАЙ, ГОВЬ, АЛТАЙ, ХҮННҮ, ЧИНГИС, СОЁМБО. The third is the borrowed international vocabulary that a technology or mining company reaches for.
