description: Company legal form and name suffix in Mongolian. IT GOES AFTER THE NAME, like the English "Ltd" and unlike the French "Société" — Эрдэнэт ХХК, not ХХК Эрдэнэт. ХХК is Хязгаарлагдмал хариуцлагатай компани, the limited liability company, and is by a long way the commonest legal form in the country; ХК is the joint stock company; ТӨХК a state-owned company; ТББ a non-governmental organisation; ХЗХ a savings and credit cooperative; ХОРШОО a cooperative, which in the countryside is the usual legal wrapper for a group of herding households. Because Mongolian is the language of one country, these are Mongolian legal forms and not a generic set.
