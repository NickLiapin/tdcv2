description: Book genre (Maori). PURAKAU, at the head, is not "mythology" in the dismissive sense the English word carries: purakau are the accounts of atua and ancestors and they carry history, law and instruction inside a narrative frame. WHAKAPAPA books are genealogical records. WAIATA and MOTEATEA — composed song and traditional chant — are published as literature here, because chant IS the classical literature of te reo.
