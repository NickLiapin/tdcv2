description: Marketing verb (Maori). WHAKA- IS THE CAUSATIVE PREFIX AND IT BUILDS ALMOST THIS WHOLE LIST: prefix it to a stative and you get "to make it so". Ora (alive) gives WHAKAORA, to heal; kaha (strong) WHAKAKAHA, to strengthen; tipu (grow) WHAKATIPU, to grow something; mana (authority) WHAKAMANA, to authorise or empower; hou (new) WHAKAHOU, to renew. This is the single most productive process in te reo and it means a config can build far more of these safely than the list holds.
locale: mi
