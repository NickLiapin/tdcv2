description: Marketing adjective (Maori). Note a grammatical fact behind the whole list: TE REO MAORI HAS NO SEPARATE ADJECTIVE CLASS. Words like nui (big), auaha (innovative) and toitu (sustainable) are stative verbs — they can head a sentence on their own, without any verb "to be" — so what English calls an adjective is here a verb that happens to follow a noun. TOITU is the word to watch commercially: it means enduring and unbroken, and it has become the standard New Zealand term for sustainable, to the point of naming a national carbon-certification programme.
