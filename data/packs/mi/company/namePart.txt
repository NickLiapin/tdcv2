description: Element of a company or organisation name (Maori). New Zealand organisation names are built from exactly this stock, usually as TE + noun + O + noun ("the X of Y"), which is why so many begin with Te. The values divide into virtues (aroha, kotahitanga, mana, tumanako), natural features (awa, maunga, moana, puna, ngahere), native species (kauri, totara, tui, kea) and light and growth words (rama, marama, tipu, puawai, rito, kakano). RITO, the centre shoot of a flax bush that must never be cut, is a favourite for anything to do with children or renewal, and comes from the whakatauki about the heart of the harakeke.
locale: mi
