description: Marketing noun (Maori). RAUTAKI (strategy), PUNAHA (system), RARAUNGA (data), RATONGA (service), HOTAKA (programme), TUAPAPA (foundation) and WHAKAKITENGA (vision) are the standard modern business vocabulary and were coined, not borrowed — te reo builds a term out of its own roots where English would take a Latin one. KAUPAPA is the word that does the most work and is hardest to translate: it is at once the purpose, the topic, the plan and the principle underlying a piece of work, and a New Zealand organisation will use it in English sentences because nothing in English covers it. KETE, a woven basket, is the standard metaphor for a body of knowledge — the three kete of knowledge Tane brought back from the heavens — and so serves as "toolkit".
