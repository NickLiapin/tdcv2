description: Fish and seafood (Maori). WATCH TUNA. In te reo Maori TUNA IS THE FRESHWATER EEL, not the ocean fish English calls tuna — the same five letters, an entirely different animal, and one of the most reliable traps in any Maori dataset. Longfin eel, tuna kuwharuwharu, is endemic, can live eighty years and is a serious traditional food; the English tuna would be kahu or its own borrowed name. Note also that KOURA is crayfish and also gold, and that PAUA, KINA, KUTAI, PIPI, TUATUA and TIO are shellfish rather than fish — the organising category in Maori is KAIMOANA, food from the sea, which cuts across the biological one. INANGA is whitebait, the juvenile of five galaxiid species, and is also the name of a pale variety of pounamu.
