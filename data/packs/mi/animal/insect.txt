description: Insect and other small crawling creature (Maori). The category is folk-taxonomic rather than entomological: NGARARA covers insects, spiders and lizards alike — anything small and crawling — and PUNGAWEREWERE (spider) sits in this list for that reason. WETA is the one every reader will have heard of: a flightless cricket the size of a mouse, of which the giant wetapunga is among the heaviest insects on earth, and the name means "god of ugly things". HUHU is the fat grub of a longhorn beetle and was eaten. KIHIKIHI, the cicada, is named for the sound it makes, as several Maori animal names are.
