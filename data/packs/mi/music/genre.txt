description: Music genre (Maori). THE FIRST FIVE ARE MAORI FORMS AND ARE NOT "FOLK MUSIC". MOTEATEA is the classical chant repertoire — laments, love songs and songs of complaint, composed by named authors and transmitted exactly. WAIATA-A-RINGA is the action song, developed in the twentieth century and now the heart of kapa haka. HAKA is a posture dance, not always a war dance, and there are haka for welcome and for funerals. KARANGA is the ceremonial call and PATERE a fast rhythmic recitation, often listing whakapapa. The rest are international genres and keep their names.
