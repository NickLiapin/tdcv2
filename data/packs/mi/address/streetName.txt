description: The name part of a street, to be placed AFTER a streetSuffix (Ara + Kauri = Ara Kauri). This is the te reo word stock that New Zealand streets are actually named from — native trees, native birds, landforms, weather and virtues — and not a list of real streets, which are country data in new_zealand.geo.streetName. Note the macrons carefully: they distinguish words outright rather than decorating them, so KAKA (a forest parrot) and KAKA (clothing) and KAKA (a stalk) are three different words separated only by which vowels are long, and a street sign that drops the macron has changed what it says.
locale: mi
