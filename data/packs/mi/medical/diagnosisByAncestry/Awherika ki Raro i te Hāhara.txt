description: Conditions seen at higher prevalence in people of Sub-Saharan African descent (Maori). THIS LIST IS A PREVALENCE SIGNAL, NOT AN EXCLUSIVE ONE — every condition here occurs in every population, and ancestry shifts the odds rather than deciding the case. HOW THIS LIST IS BUILT, because a medical list must say. Where te reo Maori has an ESTABLISHED term it is used: HUANGO is asthma, REWHAREWHA influenza, MATE HUKA diabetes ("sugar sickness"), MATE PUKUPUKU cancer, HUKIHUKI epilepsy, MATE KOHI tuberculosis, MATE WAREWARE dementia, PAPOURI depression, AWANGAWANGA anxiety, PAIPAI a sexually transmitted infection. Where it does not, the value uses the transparent MATE + organ or MATE + cause pattern te reo actually builds diagnoses with — mate whatukuhu is kidney disease, mate pukahukahu lung disease, pehanga toto teitei high blood pressure. NO SINGLE-WORD TERM HAS BEEN COINED HERE: an invented medical word is undetectable later and would be the worst kind of fabrication in a data pack. A clinical system must take its vocabulary from Te Reo Hapai, the standardised Maori glossary for the health and disability sector, and not from this file.
