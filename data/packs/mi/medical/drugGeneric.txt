THESE ARE INTERNATIONAL NONPROPRIETARY NAMES and are the same in every language on earth — that is the entire point of the INN system — so none of them is translated and all of them use letters outside the Maori alphabet. One local note: New Zealand says PARACETAMOL, not acetaminophen, and its medicines are funded through PHARMAC, which keeps a narrower published range than most countries, so this list is shorter than an American one for a real reason. RONGOA RAKAU, Maori plant medicine, is a separate practice with its own materia medica (kawakawa, koromiko, harakeke, manuka) and is not a list of generic drugs.
