---
description: Kind of legal case (Maori). WHAKAWA is a trial or hearing and TAKE a matter or claim. TAKE TIRITI, a Treaty of Waitangi claim, has no equivalent anywhere else: it is a claim that the Crown has breached the guarantees of Te Tiriti o Waitangi, heard by the Waitangi Tribunal, and it may concern events of a hundred and eighty years ago. TAKE WHENUA MAORI, Maori land matters, go to the Maori Land Court, a court that exists nowhere else and that has run since 1865.
locale: mi
