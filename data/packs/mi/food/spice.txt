description: Spice and seasoning (Maori). THE NATIVE PART OF THIS LIST IS DELIBERATELY TINY AND THAT IS THE HONEST PICTURE: pre-contact Maori cooking seasoned with HOROPITO, whose leaf is genuinely peppery and is still used commercially, with KAWAKAWA, and with the smoke and steam of the hangi — Aotearoa is far outside the spice trade and there was nothing else to reach for. PEPA (pepper) and TOTE (salt) are loans; KARIKA is garlic. Everything below them keeps its international name because te reo has no established form for it, and coining twenty Maori spice words would be exactly the fabrication a data pack must not commit. Those values are why part of this file uses letters outside the Maori alphabet.
locale: mi
