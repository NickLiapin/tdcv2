description: Dish (Maori). The Maori dishes here are the same ones food/dishByCuisine/Maori.txt holds; the loanwords beside them show how te reo takes an English food word and rebuilds it so that every syllable ends in a vowel — biscuit becomes PIHIKETE, chocolate TIAKARETE, soup HUPA, pie PAI, sausage TOTITI, pudding PUTINI, jelly TIERE. THE MACRON IS DOING REAL WORK IN THIS LIST. KEKE is cake, a nineteenth-century loan. KEKE with both vowels long is an armpit. Nothing but the two macrons separates them, they are not the same word in any sense, and a pack that dropped the macron as "an accent" would be serving one for the other.
