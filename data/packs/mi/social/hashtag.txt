description: Hashtag word, to be prefixed with # in a template (Maori). SPACES CANNOT SURVIVE A HASHTAG, so several of these are run together the way they actually appear online. The most-used te reo hashtags are real campaigns: TEREOMAORI and KIAKAHATEREOMAORI ("be strong, the Maori language") around Maori Language Week, MATARIKI at the new year, and TOITUTETIRITI, the slogan of the 2024 Treaty marches. Note that a hashtag strips macrons in some clients, which turns MAORI into a different word — one of the practical reasons the macron matters.
locale: mi
