description: Common verb (Maori). TE REO MARKS ASPECT, NOT TENSE. A bare verb here is not a present tense: the particle in front of it says whether the action is under way (KEI TE), completed (KUA), habitual or beginning (KA), or past and finished (I). So "kai" alone is neither "eat" nor "ate" — it is the action, and the timing lives in a separate word. That is why every status list in this pack reads as a particle plus a verb.
locale: mi
