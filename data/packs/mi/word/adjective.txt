description: Descriptive word (Maori). THERE IS NO ADJECTIVE CLASS IN TE REO. Every word here is a STATIVE VERB: it can stand as the whole predicate of a sentence with no copula anywhere — "he nui te whare" and "ka nui" are complete — and when it follows a noun it is not agreeing with anything, because te reo has no gender, no case and no number agreement to agree with. Nothing in the word changes for the noun it describes, ever.
locale: mi
