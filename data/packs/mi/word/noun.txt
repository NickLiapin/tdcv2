description: Common noun (Maori). Note what does NOT appear: an article. Te reo marks singular and plural on the ARTICLE rather than on the noun — TE tangata is the person, NGA tangata the people, and the noun itself is unchanged. A few nouns do lengthen a vowel in the plural instead (tangata becomes TANGATA with the first a long, wahine becomes WAHINE with the first a long, matua becomes MATUA), and THAT IS A MACRON DOING GRAMMAR: the only difference between one person and several is the length of a vowel. Drop the macron and you have deleted the plural.
locale: mi
