description: Product noun (Maori). Two things this list shows. FIRST, the pattern behind PUREHUA-style compounds: te reo names an unfamiliar object by describing it, so a computer is RONOHIKO ("electric brain"), a television POUAKA WHAKAATA ("picture box"), a telephone WAEA ("wire") and a camera WHAKAAHUA ("image-maker") — coinage, not borrowing. SECOND, where it DOES borrow, the loan is rebuilt: table TEPU, chair TURU, book PUKAPUKA (from pukapuka, a leaf, extended), bicycle PAIHIKARA, motor MOTUKA, radio REO IRIRANGI. Both strategies are alive at once and neither is more correct than the other.
locale: mi
