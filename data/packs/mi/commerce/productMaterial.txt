description: Material a product is made of (Maori). The native materials at the head are the ones Maori craft actually uses and several have no English word: HARAKEKE is New Zealand flax and MUKA the prepared fibre stripped from it, which was rope, line, cloak and basket; KIEKIE and PINGAO are the pale and gold weaving materials that make the patterns in a tukutuku panel; POUNAMU is nephrite jade; HUKAHUKA are the twisted tassels on a cloak. RAUPO thatched houses. Below them are the introduced materials, borrowed and rebuilt — glass KARAEHE, plastic KIRIHOU, rubber RAPA, wool WURU, cotton MIRO? no, cotton is MIRO-less: WURU is wool and KAKANO MIRO is not cotton, so cotton is left in its borrowed form.
