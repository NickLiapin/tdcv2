description: Product review headline (Maori). Note how a Maori sentence starts: KA PAI is the everyday "well done, good", TINO PAI "very good", KAORE I PAI "not good". HE + noun forms an exclamation — "he rawe!" is "brilliant!". The negative KAORE and KORE come BEFORE the thing negated and take the whole clause with them, which is why every negative headline here begins with them.
locale: mi
