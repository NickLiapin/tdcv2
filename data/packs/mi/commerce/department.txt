description: Shop department (Maori). Ordinary retail vocabulary, much of it loanwords rebuilt to Maori shape — KAKAHU is clothing (native), HU shoes, TAPUTAPU tools and equipment, MIHINI machines, PUKAPUKA books, TAKARO toys, RONGOA medicines. TAONGA MAORI, at the foot, is a real department in New Zealand shops and covers carving, weaving, pounamu and taonga puoro.
