description: Adjective used on a product listing (Maori). Remember these are STATIVE VERBS, not adjectives — te reo has no adjective class — so "hou" can head a sentence by itself ("He hou tenei", this is new) with no verb "to be" anywhere in it. They follow the noun, so a listing reads TARU HOU rather than "new item". TUTURU means genuine or authentic and is what a Maori craft listing says instead of "handmade"; MAORI itself is the ordinary word for normal or natural, which is where the name of the people comes from — tangata maori, ordinary people, as against the strangers who arrived.
locale: mi
