description: Technical adjective (Maori). MATIHIKO, digital, is a coinage from matihiko meaning a finger — the digit — which is the same metaphor Latin used and te reo arrived at independently. TUIHONO is online, TUKUTUKU is the network (and also the woven lattice panels on a wharenui wall, which is where the word comes from).
