description: Technical noun (Maori). Almost all coined rather than borrowed: RONOHIKO is a computer ("electric brain"), IPURANGI the internet ("stream of data"), RARAUNGA data, PUMANAWA software (literally "the talents of the heart", the same word used for a person's gifts), PURERE hardware, PUNAHA a system, KONAE a file, KOPAKI a folder, MUKA a fibre. PAPAPATUHI is a keyboard and KIORE the mouse — kiore is the Pacific rat, and te reo made the same joke English did.
locale: mi
