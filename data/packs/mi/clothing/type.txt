description: Garment (Maori). The first six are Maori garments and none of them is a "type of coat". KOROWAI is a cloak with hanging tassels, worn at graduations and weddings and lent within a whanau rather than bought; KAHU HURUHURU is a feather cloak, the most prestigious; KAITAKA a fine flax cloak with woven borders; PIUPIU the flax skirt worn in kapa haka; PARE a headband and TIPARE the same worn across the forehead. These are taonga: a cloak has a name and a history and is placed on someone as an act, not put on as clothing. The rest are introduced garments with loaned names — shirt HATE, trousers TARAU, coat KOTI, hat POTAE, sock TOKENA — each rebuilt to end in a vowel.
