description: Kind of government body (Maori). New Zealand government departments carry official te reo names beside their English ones and several are now known chiefly by the Maori one — the transport agency is Waka Kotahi, the health ministry Manatu Hauora, the education ministry Te Tahuhu o te Matauranga. MANATU is a ministry and TE PUNI a directorate; TARA and RUNANGA are used for offices and councils. This file holds the KINDS of body; the actual named New Zealand departments are country data and belong to the new_zealand pack.
