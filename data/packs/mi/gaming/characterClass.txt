description: Character class (Maori). Built from real Maori roles: TOA is a warrior, TOHUNGA an expert and a priest, KAIHAUTU the one who calls the stroke in a canoe and so a leader, KAITIAKI a guardian, KAIWHAKAORA a healer, MATAKITE a seer, KAIRUKU a diver, KAIWHAIWHAI a tracker or pursuer. RANGATIRA is a chief. These are occupations te reo already has words for, so no invention was needed.
