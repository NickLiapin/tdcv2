description: Tree (Maori). Almost every tree in this list is ENDEMIC TO AOTEAROA and has no English name other than the Maori one — kauri, rimu, totara, kahikatea, pohutukawa, manuka, nikau and kowhai are what English-speaking New Zealanders call them too, because English never coined an alternative. Trees are not scenery in Maori thought: the forest is the domain of TANE MAHUTA, and a totara felled for a canoe was taken with karakia and with the permission of the hapu. TOTARA is the canoe and carving timber; KAURI, which can live two thousand years, gave the largest hulls; HARAKEKE, at the foot of the list, is not a tree but is here because it is the plant everything was made from — rope, baskets, cloaks, fishing line.
