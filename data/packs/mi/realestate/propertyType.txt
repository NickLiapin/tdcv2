description: Kind of property (Maori). PAPAKAINGA is the one with no overseas counterpart: housing built by a whanau or hapu on their own ancestral Maori land, a live category in New Zealand district plans and one that ordinary residential zoning rules had to be rewritten to allow. WHARE is a house, WHARE MOTUHAKE a standalone one, WHARE PIRI a semi-detached, PAPA WHARE an apartment block. PAAMU is a farm, borrowed from "farm".
