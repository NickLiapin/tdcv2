description: Field of tertiary study (Maori). Built on the MATAI pattern — matai means to examine or inspect, so matai hinengaro is psychology, matai pori sociology, matai whetu astronomy — which lets te reo name a discipline out of its own roots. MATAURANGA MAORI and TIKANGA MAORI are degree majors at New Zealand universities and wananga, not electives.
