description: School subject (Maori). THESE ARE THE OFFICIAL LEARNING AREAS OF THE NEW ZEALAND CURRICULUM IN TE REO and they were coined from Maori roots rather than borrowed: PUTAIAO is science, PANGARAU mathematics, HANGARAU technology, TIKANGA-A-IWI social sciences, TOI the arts, HAUORA health and wellbeing. Two have no counterpart in a European curriculum: TE REO MAORI as a subject in its own right, and MATAURANGA MAORI, the Maori knowledge system, which is taught as a body of knowledge and not as cultural studies.
