description: Kind of vehicle (Maori). WAKA IS THE HEAD OF THIS LIST AND IT IS NOT MERELY "VEHICLE". A waka is the canoe, and specifically the ocean-going canoe that brought each iwi's ancestors here — every iwi names its founding waka, and "ko Tainui te waka" is a line of a formal introduction. The word then extended to any conveyance, so a car is a WAKA and New Zealand's transport agency is called Waka Kotahi. WAKA TAUA is a war canoe, WAKA AMA an outrigger and WAKA HOURUA the double-hulled voyaging canoe that crossed the Pacific by star navigation. The modern vehicles are loans rebuilt to Maori shape — motor car MOTUKA, bus PAHI, train TEREINA, bicycle PAIHIKARA, aeroplane WAKA RERERANGI (a coinage, "sky-travelling vehicle", rather than a loan).
