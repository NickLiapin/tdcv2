description: Language spoken, as Maori names it. The naming pattern is TE REO X — "the language of X" — with the country or people name unchanged, because te reo has no adjective-forming suffix for this. TE REO MAORI is an official language of New Zealand under the Maori Language Act; TE REO ROTAROTA is New Zealand Sign Language, likewise official, and rotarota is a genuine te reo word for signing rather than a transliteration. The Polynesian languages are grouped first on purpose: Maori is an East Polynesian language and Cook Islands Maori, Tahitian and Hawaiian are close enough that a speaker recognises a great deal of them — te reo Kuki Airani and te reo Maori are mutually intelligible in part.
