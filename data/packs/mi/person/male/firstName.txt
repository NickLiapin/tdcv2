description: Given name most often borne by a man (Maori). Two facts a config cannot infer. FIRST, MAORI HAS NO GRAMMATICAL GENDER and a large share of these names are genuinely unisex — Aroha, Whetu, Moana, Maia, Manaia, Nikau, Kahu, Koa, Rangimarie, Tumanako, Kahurangi and Ariki appear in BOTH this file and person/female/firstName.txt on purpose, and the overlap is the design rather than a duplication bug. The split into male and female files exists because the engine expects it, not because te reo makes it. SECOND, roughly half of these are nineteenth-century mission transliterations of Biblical and English names, and they are the clearest evidence in this pack of Maori phonotactics: every syllable must end in a vowel and no two consonants may touch, so William becomes WIREMU, James HEMI, Joseph HOHEPA, David RAWIRI, Stephen TIPENE, Nathaniel NATANAHIRA, Zachariah HAKARAIA. Sounds te reo does not have are mapped onto the nearest one it does (l to r, b to p, s to h, j to h, g to k), and a vowel is inserted or appended wherever the English form would leave a consonant exposed. They are reshapings, not letter-for-letter transliterations. The TE- prefix on Te Ariki and Te Rangi is the definite article fused into the name and is written as part of it. Weights are this project's default decay curve ordered by prominence, NOT measured frequency: New Zealand's Births Deaths and Marriages registry publishes top-name lists but no frequency table for Maori given names, so nothing better exists to encode.
locale: mi
