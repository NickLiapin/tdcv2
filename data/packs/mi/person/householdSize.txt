description: Number of people usually living in the dwelling. The numbers are universal but the concept behind them is not: WHANAU is not a household. A whanau is the extended family — grandparents, siblings' children, cousins, and people connected by whangai (customary adoption, in which a child is raised by relatives without the birth link being severed or hidden). Maori households in New Zealand are on average larger than the national figure and far more often contain three generations, so a size distribution copied from a European locale will under-count the upper end of this list.
