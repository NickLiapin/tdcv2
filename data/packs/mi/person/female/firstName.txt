description: Given name most often borne by a woman (Maori). MAORI HAS NO GRAMMATICAL GENDER, so a large share of this list is genuinely unisex and appears in person/male/firstName.txt as well — Aroha, Whetu, Moana, Maia, Manaia, Nikau, Kahu, Koa, Rangimarie, Tumanako, Kahurangi, Aria and Ataahua are on both lists deliberately. The two files differ in what they LEAN toward, not in what te reo permits. HINE- is the one genuinely sex-marking element: hine is a girl or young woman, so Hinemoa, Hinewai, Hinerangi and Hinemoana are female-forming compounds; PARE- and MAREIKURA are likewise female-associated. The TE- prefix on Te Aroha and Te Ata is the definite article fused into the name. The mission transliterations here work exactly as the male ones do — Mary becomes MERE or MERI, Rebecca RIPEKA, Rachel RAHERA, Sarah HERA, Elizabeth ERIHAPETI, Margaret MAKARETA, Catherine KATARAINA — reshaped so that every syllable ends in a vowel and no consonants cluster. Weights are the project default decay curve ordered by prominence, not measured frequency; no per-name frequency table for Maori given names is published.
