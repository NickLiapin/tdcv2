description: Weather condition (Maori). PAKI is fine settled weather and is a word with weight — it is the opposite of awha and is what a fishing or planting decision waits for. AWHA is a storm, UA rain, HAU wind, KOHU fog, HUKARERE snow, WHATU hail, HUKA frost and also foam and also sugar, three unrelated senses te reo keeps in one word. Note the hemisphere: the cold, wet months in Aotearoa are Pipiri to Here-turi-koka, June to August, so a config that pairs snow with December is half a year out.
locale: mi
