description: Kind of venue (Maori). MARAE is the first value and the most important: it is the whole complex — the open ground, the wharenui, the wharekai — and it is where a very large share of Maori gatherings actually happen, from funerals to conferences to vaccination clinics. It is not a hall for hire; it belongs to a hapu, and using it means being welcomed onto it.
