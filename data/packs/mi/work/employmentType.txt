description: Type of employment (Maori). MAHI TUAO, volunteer work, sits differently here than a European list would place it: work done for the marae, the kohanga reo or the hapu is unpaid and is nonetheless a genuine and expected occupation, so a dataset that treats it as "not employed" is losing real information. MAHI A-KAINGA is working from home and MAHI RANGI casual work taken day by day.
