Names are te reo: HANGARAU technology, HAUORA health, PUTEA finance, HANGA construction, MATAURANGA education, MANAAKITANGA hospitality — and manaakitanga is worth pausing on, because it is not a synonym for the service industry. It is the obligation to care for, feed and shelter a guest, one of the load-bearing values of Maori society, and a hotel chain using it as a job category is borrowing a moral term. WAKA, for the transport sector, is the same word as canoe and is what New Zealand's transport agency is actually named (Waka Kotahi).
