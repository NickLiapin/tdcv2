description: Workplace skill (Maori). The first block are skills that are Maori and are genuinely listed on New Zealand job descriptions: WHAIKORERO (formal oratory), KARANGA (the call that opens a powhiri, performed by women), TE REO MAORI itself, TIKANGA (protocol), WHAKAPAPA (genealogy), MATAURANGA MAORI (the Maori knowledge system), KAITIAKITANGA (environmental guardianship, now written into resource-management law) and WHAKAWHANAUNGATANGA — building relationship before business, which in Maori settings is not a soft skill but a precondition for anything else happening.
