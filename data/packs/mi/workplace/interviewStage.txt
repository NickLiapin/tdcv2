description: Stage of a hiring process (Maori). WHAKAWHANAUNGATANGA at the head is not small talk and not a formality — in a Maori workplace the first meeting establishes who you are and who you are connected to, and a candidate who cannot place themselves is at a disadvantage no CV corrects. POWHIRI is the formal welcome onto a marae or into an organisation and may itself be part of the process.
