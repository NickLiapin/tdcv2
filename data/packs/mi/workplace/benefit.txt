description: Employment benefit (Maori). TWO OF THESE HAVE NO EUROPEAN EQUIVALENT AND ARE ORDINARY IN NEW ZEALAND. TANGIHANGA LEAVE is bereavement leave written for a funeral that lasts three days and is attended by the whole extended whanau, not for an afternoon off — many collective agreements name it explicitly. AKO TE REO MAORI, paid time to learn the language, is a benefit an increasing number of New Zealand employers offer, because a century of suppression means most Maori employees did not grow up speaking it. KIWISAVER is New Zealand's workplace retirement scheme and keeps its name.
