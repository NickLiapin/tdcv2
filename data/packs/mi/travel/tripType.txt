description: Kind of trip (Maori). HAERENGA is a journey. Several of these are specifically Maori reasons to travel: HAERENGA MARAE, going to a marae for a hui or tangi; HOKI KI TE KAINGA, returning to the ancestral home; and HIKOI, which is a walk but also a protest march, the most famous being the 1975 Land March the length of the North Island.
locale: mi
