description: Sport (Maori). THE FIRST FIVE ARE MAORI AND ARE NOT FOLK GAMES. KI-O-RAHI is a traditional ball game with a circular field, two teams with opposite objectives, and a story attached to it; it is now played in New Zealand schools and has been demonstrated internationally. WAKA AMA, outrigger canoe racing, is one of the fastest-growing sports in the country with tens of thousands of paddlers and a national championship on Lake Karapiro. MAU RAKAU is weapons training with the taiaha. The introduced codes below show the usual reshaping — rugby WHUTUPORO, netball POITARAWHITI, basketball POITUKOHU, cricket KIRIKITI, soccer POIKIRI — and note that in New Zealand rugby union, netball and cricket are the mass-participation sports, not soccer.
