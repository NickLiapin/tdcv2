description: Job titles in Kyrgyz. THE AGENT SUFFIX IS A FOUR-WAY ROUNDING-HARMONY SET: -чы / -чи / -чу / -чү, one shape per vowel class, and it is what turns a thing into the person who works with it — мал (livestock) gives малчы, темир (iron) gives темирчи, жумуш (work) gives жумушчу, окуу (study) gives окуучу and сүрөт (picture) gives сүрөтчү. Kazakh forms the same word with a TWO-way -шы / -ші, so a Kazakh table cannot produce жумушчу or сүрөтчү at all. Half the native titles below are built with this suffix, which is why they end in four different ways.
locale: ky
