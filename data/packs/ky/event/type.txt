description: Kinds of event in Kyrgyz. THE LIFE-CYCLE FEASTS ARE NOT DECORATION: той is the general word for a celebration with guests, and Kyrgyz names each occasion separately — бешик той when a baby is first put in the cradle, тушоо кесүү when the tie is cut from a toddler's legs so it can walk, кыз узатуу when a daughter is seen off to marry, and аш, the large memorial feast held a year after a death. All four are ordinary calendar entries here.
locale: ky
