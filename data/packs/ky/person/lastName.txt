description: Kyrgyz family names — and this field holds TWO DIFFERENT SYSTEMS at once, which is the single most important thing about Kyrgyz naming. (1) The Soviet-Russian family name in -ов/-ев, inherited from the 1920s and still the commonest form on documents: Асанов, Жумабеков, Осмонов. (2) The native patronymic, the father's given name followed by УУЛУ, 'son of' — Бакыт уулу, Асан уулу — which is official, appears in passports, and is written as TWO SEPARATE WORDS, not as a suffix. So a config joining person.male.firstName with person.lastName produces either 'Азамат Осмонов' or 'Азамат Бакыт уулу', and both are correct full names of the same kind; the second is three words long and that is not an error. Weighted with the default decay curve because no Kyrgyz surname frequency table is published; the -ов names are ranked first because they still dominate the record, with the уулу forms behind them and growing.
