description: Languages as Kyrgyz names them. THIS IS THE CLEANEST PLACE TO SEE KYRGYZ ROUNDING HARMONY: the 'in language X' suffix has FOUR shapes, -ча / -че / -чо / -чө, picked by the last vowel of the stem for backness AND for roundness — кыргызча (ы, back unrounded), өзбекче (е, front unrounded), монголчо (о, back rounded), түркчө (ү, front rounded). Kazakh, the nearest big relative, has only two shapes (-ша / -ше) because Kazakh does not carry rounding into its suffixes; a Kazakh-shaped table would give түркше and монголша, which are wrong in Kyrgyz. This is the harmony rule that separates the two languages, and it is visible in almost every value below.
