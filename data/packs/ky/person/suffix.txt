description: What can follow a Kyrgyz personal name. KYRGYZ HAS NO Jr./Sr./II/III SYSTEM AT ALL, so this list does not contain one — inventing 'Кичи' and 'Улуу' to fill the shape would have produced name suffixes no Kyrgyz document carries. What genuinely follows a name is the patronymic word (уулу / кызы) and the postposed kin honorifics, plus the academic abbreviations.
