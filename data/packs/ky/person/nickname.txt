description: Kyrgyz short and affectionate name forms. THE LIST IS DELIBERATELY SHORT. Kyrgyz makes them with a productive rule rather than from a fixed stock: take the first syllable of the name and add -ке or -кө (Чыңгыз -> Чыке, Асан -> Асаке, Сүйүнбек -> Сүке), which is respectful-affectionate and used for elders as much as for children; or add -жан to the whole name. I have listed only the forms I can vouch for as actually used, rather than mechanically running the rule over every name in the pack and shipping the output as if it were attested.
