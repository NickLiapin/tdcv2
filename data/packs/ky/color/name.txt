description: Colour words in Kyrgyz. Two things a European colour list does not have. FIRST, the intensive forms: Kyrgyz reduplicates the first syllable with a closing consonant to mean "utterly this colour" — аппак, капкара, кыпкызыл, көпкөк, сапсары, жапжашыл — and these are everyday words, not poetry. SECOND, the horse-coat terms тору, жээрде, кула, буурул, ала and чаар are ordinary colour vocabulary in a herding language and are used of other things too.
