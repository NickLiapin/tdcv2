description: Common noun in Kyrgyz. THIS IS WHERE THE PLURAL SHOWS ROUNDING HARMONY: Kyrgyz has TWELVE forms of the plural suffix — -лар/-лер/-лор/-лөр, -дар/-дер/-дор/-дөр, -тар/-тер/-тор/-төр — four vowel shapes (back unrounded, front unrounded, back rounded, front rounded) times three consonant shapes chosen by what the stem ends in. So адам gives адамдар, үй gives үйлөр, көз gives көздөр, and сөз gives сөздөр. Kazakh has only SIX of these, because Kazakh rounding does not reach the suffix: Kazakh writes көздер where Kyrgyz writes көздөр. If you want one test that tells a Kyrgyz text from a Kazakh one, it is whether the plural after a rounded stem is -дөр or -дер.
