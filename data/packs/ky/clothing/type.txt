description: Garments in Kyrgyz. Калпак (the white felt hat), тебетей (the fur-trimmed winter hat), элечек (the married woman's wound headdress) and чепкен (the felt coat) are worn, sold and given as gifts today — the калпак has its own national day on 5 March — so they belong in a clothing list rather than in a costume museum.
locale: ky
