THE MANAS ENTRY IS THE POINT OF THIS LIST: "Манас" is the longest epic ever recorded — around half a million lines, some twenty times the Odyssey and the Iliad together — and it is not a museum text. It is still recited from memory by манасчы (Manas reciters), broadcast, taught in school and quoted in ordinary argument, so эпос, дастан and санжыра (oral genealogy) are live publishing categories here and not historical curiosities.
