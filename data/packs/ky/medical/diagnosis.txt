description: Diagnoses in Kyrgyz. Where Kyrgyz has its own word for a condition it is used rather than the Russian one — кургак учук for tuberculosis, кант диабети for diabetes, сары оору for hepatitis/jaundice, сасык тумоо for influenza, дем кысма for asthma — and the Latin-Russian term is used only where the language really has no other.
locale: ky
