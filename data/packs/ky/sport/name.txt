---
description: Sports in Kyrgyz. КӨК БӨРҮ IS THE NATIONAL SPORT: two teams of riders carry a goat carcass to the opponents' clay goal, it has a federation, a league and televised finals. Эр эңиш is mounted wrestling, кыз куумай a chase on horseback, and тогуз коргоол the two-row sowing board game that is taught in schools and played at championship level. Эркин күрөш (freestyle wrestling) is where Kyrgyzstan's Olympic medals come from.
locale: ky
