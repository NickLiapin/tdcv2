description: Music genres in Kyrgyz. Күү is a solo instrumental piece for komuz with a story behind it — a named genre, not "folk music"; айтыш is a sung improvised duel between two akyns and is still broadcast on television with prizes; кошок is the funeral lament; and Манас айтуу, reciting the epic, is a performance genre with its own professionals.
