description: Musical instruments in Kyrgyz. Комуз, the three-string fretless lute, is the national instrument and is played by amateurs everywhere; кыл кыяк is the bowed horsehair fiddle; ооз комуз is the metal jaw harp, usually played by women; чоор and сыбызгы are shepherd's pipes. These are current instruments with living repertoire, not museum pieces.
locale: ky
