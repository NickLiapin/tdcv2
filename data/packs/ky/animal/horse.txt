description: Horse breeds in Kyrgyz. Kyrgyz also classifies a horse by AGE AND GAIT rather than by breed — кулун (foal), тай (yearling), кунан (three-year-old), бышты (four-year-old), айгыр (stallion), бээ (mare), жорго (ambler) — and those words are far commoner in speech than any breed name, so the two ambler entries are here on purpose.
