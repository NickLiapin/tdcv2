description: Dishes in Kyrgyz, weighted toward what is actually eaten. Бешбармак — boiled meat over hand-cut noodle sheets, eaten with the hands, which is what the name says — is the feast dish; лагман and ашлям-фуу came from the Dungan and Uyghur kitchens and are now everywhere; кымыз (fermented mare's milk) and максым (fermented barley drink) are drunk as food, not as novelties.
locale: ky
