description: Employee benefit (Hebrew). Half of this list has no European counterpart. קרן השתלמות is a tax-advantaged savings fund an employer co-pays into and the employee may withdraw tax-free after six years — it is the benefit Israelis negotiate hardest for. דמי הבראה is a 'recuperation payment', a legally mandated annual sum per year of service. החזר נסיעות, reimbursement of the cost of getting to work, is likewise a legal entitlement rather than a perk. תלושי שי לחג are the vouchers handed out before Rosh Hashanah and Passover, and סיבוס / תן ביס are the prepaid lunch cards that most Israeli offices run on.
locale: he
