description: Leave type (Hebrew). Two Israeli specifics: מילואים is reserve military service, a legally protected absence that an employer must hold the job open for and that recurs every year for a large share of the workforce; שבעה is the seven days of mourning after the death of a close relative, and Israeli labour practice treats it as leave in its own right. חל״ת is the standard acronym for unpaid leave.
locale: he
