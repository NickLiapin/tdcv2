description: Common adjective (Hebrew, masculine singular). Hebrew adjectives FOLLOW the noun and agree with it in gender, number and definiteness — בית גדול, דירה גדולה, בתים גדולים, הבית הגדול (the article is repeated on both words). Placing one of these before a noun, English- or Polish-fashion, produces something ungrammatical.
