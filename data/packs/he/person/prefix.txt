description: Israeli honorific / form of address, in the abbreviated form used before a name. Two notes. Hebrew acronyms are written with a gershayim ״ (U+05F4) before the last letter — ד״ר, עו״ד, רו״ח, ח״כ — and single-letter abbreviations with a geresh ׳ (U+05F3); everyday typing substitutes ASCII " and '. And military rank is a normal civilian form of address in Israel in a way it is not in Europe: reserve and retired officers are routinely introduced by rank.
