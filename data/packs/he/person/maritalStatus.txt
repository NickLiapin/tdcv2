description: Marital status in Hebrew. Every form is GENDERED — נשוי for a man, נשואה for a woman — so a row's status must agree with its row's gender, and both members of each pair are listed here. ידוע בציבור deserves its own note: Israel has no civil marriage, only religious marriage through the rabbinic, sharia or church courts, so couples who cannot or will not marry that way register as ידועים בציבור, a status with most of the legal effects of marriage. It is common and it is not a European 'cohabiting' equivalent.
