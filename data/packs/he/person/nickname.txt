description: Common Israeli hypocoristic / informal nickname. Hebrew makes these by three routes, all visible here: adding ־י to a shortened stem (יוסף → יוסי, רפאל → רפי, מרים → מירי), the Slavic-Yiddish ־יק and ־קה endings that came with the Ashkenazi immigration (שמואל → שמוליק, צבי → צביקה, יצחק → איציק), and reduplicating the stem (דוד → דודו). The Russian forms סשה, מישה and גרישה are used unchanged by the post-1990 immigrant community.
