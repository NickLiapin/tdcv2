description: Music genre (Persian). موسیقی دستگاهی is Iranian classical music, organised into twelve دستگاه modal systems rather than into keys, and is a different thing from موسیقی سنتی as a broad label. بندری، آذری، کردی، لری and بلوچی are Iran's own regional musics. نوحه، مداحی and تعزیه are religious forms performed in Muharram — تعزیه is a passion play with music, the only indigenous Iranian theatre.
locale: fa
