description: Unit of measure (Persian). The last four are traditional Iranian weights still heard in a bazaar: مثقال is 4.608 g and is the unit gold is priced in, سیر is 16 مثقال, and من is a larger bulk measure whose size varied by city and trade. دست counts a matched set — a دست of dishes, a دست of clothes.
locale: fa
