description: Loan / credit facility (Persian). Iranian banking is interest-free in law, so a loan is written as one of a fixed set of Islamic contracts rather than as a rate: قرض‌الحسنه is the benevolent no-profit loan, مرابحه a cost-plus sale, مضاربه a profit-sharing venture, جعاله payment for a job done, and اجاره به شرط تملیک a lease that ends in ownership. وام ازدواج is a state-mandated marriage loan every newly married couple may claim.
