description: Persian family-name ending. Persian has NO generational suffix — there is no Jr., no Senior, no II or III after an Iranian name, and academic titles precede the name instead of trailing it (see person/prefix). What does sit at the end of a Persian name is the element that BUILT the surname: زاده born-of, پور son-of, نژاد lineage, فر glory, نیا descent, وند and لو tribal, یان plural/Armenian. Use these to compose a family name, not to append a generation to one.
locale: fa
