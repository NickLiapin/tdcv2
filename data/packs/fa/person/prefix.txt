description: Persian honorific / form of address, placed BEFORE the name. آقا and خانم are the everyday pair; جناب آقای and سرکار خانم are their formal written forms; دکتر and مهندس are used as social titles far more freely in Iran than in Europe. حاج آقا / حاجیه خانم mark someone who has made the hajj, سید / سیده marks claimed descent from the Prophet, and امیر is a general-officer rank in the Iranian armed forces, not a given name in this position.
locale: fa
