description: Persian family name. Iranian surnames are RECENT — they were made compulsory only by the Civil Registration Act of 1919, so almost every one is transparently derived: from a place (تهرانی، شیرازی، اصفهانی، نجفی), a trade (نجار carpenter, زرگر goldsmith, صراف money-changer, کشاورز farmer), or an ancestor's given name with an ending (محمدی، حسینی، علیزاده، ولی‌زاده). Persian surnames do NOT inflect for gender — a man and a woman in the same family carry the identical string — so this pack ships no person/female/lastName.txt, and that absence is the design, not an omission. Weights use the default decay curve: Iranian surnames are not concentrated, and even محمدی is only about one percent of the country.
