description: Company legal form and name element used in Iran. سهامی خاص (private joint stock) and با مسئولیت محدود (limited liability) are the two ordinary forms; سهامی عام is the public one required for a Tehran Stock Exchange listing. Unlike German or Polish practice the form usually PRECEDES nothing and follows the name: شرکت X (سهامی خاص).
locale: fa
