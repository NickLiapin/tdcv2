description: Element an Iranian company name is built from. Iranian firms name themselves after the country and its past (پارس، ایران، آریا، پاسارگاد، هخامنش، تخت جمشید), its mountains and rivers (البرز، دماوند، زاگرس، کارون، اروند), and its myths — هما, the phoenix-like bird whose shadow makes a king, is the name of the national airline; سیمرغ is the bird of the Shahnameh and the statuette of Iran's main film award.
locale: fa
