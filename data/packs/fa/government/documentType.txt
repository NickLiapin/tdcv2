description: Official document type (Persian). Three have no European equivalent. شناسنامه is a bound birth-certificate booklet that follows a person for life — marriages, children and death are all written into it. کارت ملی carries the ten-digit کد ملی that every transaction in Iran asks for. کارت پایان خدمت proves military service is completed or exempted, and without it an Iranian man cannot be hired, get a passport or leave the country.
