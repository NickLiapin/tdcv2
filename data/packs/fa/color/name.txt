description: Colour name (Persian). Persian names a great many of its colours after food: عنابی jujube, زرشکی barberry, آلبالویی sour cherry, بادمجانی aubergine, پسته‌ای pistachio, نخودی chickpea, گندمی wheat, حنایی henna, جگری liver. فیروزه‌ای, turquoise, is named after the stone mined at Neyshabur — the word turquoise reached Europe through this trade.
locale: fa
