description: Writing system (Persian). Three are Iranian: خط میخی is Old Persian cuneiform, cut at Persepolis and Bisotun; خط پهلوی wrote Middle Persian under the Sasanians; خط اوستایی was invented purely to record the Avesta and marks every vowel, which the others do not. خط فارسی itself is the Arabic alphabet plus the four letters پ چ ژ گ that Arabic does not have.
