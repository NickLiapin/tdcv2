description: Trailing element of an Iranian place name. آباد (settled, cultivated) is by far the most productive — hundreds of Iranian towns are X-آباد — and ستان marks a region (لرستان, گلستان). چای, سو, بلاغ and لو are Turkic endings, ordinary in Azerbaijani-speaking north-western Iran; کلا and محله are Caspian, common in Mazandaran and Gilan.
