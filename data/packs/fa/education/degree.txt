description: Academic qualification as awarded in Iran. The Persian everyday names differ from the official ones — a کارشناسی is universally called a لیسانس, a کارشناسی ارشد a فوق‌لیسانس, and a کاردانی a فوق‌دیپلم. The last four are seminary ranks from the حوزه علمیه, a parallel system to the universities whose سطح چهار is treated as equivalent to a doctorate and whose اجتهاد is the licence to issue independent religious rulings.
