description: Property listing status (Persian). Iranian renting works on رهن: the tenant hands the landlord a large lump sum, refundable in full at the end, and pays reduced or no monthly rent — رهن کامل is the all-deposit-no-rent version and رهن و اجاره the mixed one. مشارکت در ساخت is the standard Iranian development deal, land swapped for finished flats.
