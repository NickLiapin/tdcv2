description: Event type in Urdu. The first block is the life-cycle events an Urdu-speaking family actually holds, and they are not one event with stages: مہندی, بارات and ولیمہ are three separate functions on three separate days, held by different sides of the family, and a wedding record that collapses them into "wedding" loses the structure the whole industry is organised around. عقیقہ is the naming ceremony held for a newborn, ختم قرآن a completion reading, and قل and چہلم the third-day and fortieth-day gatherings after a death.
