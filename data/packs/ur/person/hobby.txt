description: Hobbies and interests in Urdu. Four have no European equivalent and are among the most widely practised things on this list. پتنگ بازی is kite flying, which is a competitive sport with cut lines and a season, not a children's pastime. کبوتر بازی is pigeon fancying and racing, a serious hobby with its own market. نعت خوانی and قوالی are devotional singing, practised at gatherings across the Urdu-speaking world and counted as an accomplishment rather than as worship. خطاطی, Urdu calligraphy in the Nastaʿlīq hand, is an art form in its own right because the script itself is one.
locale: ur
