description: Language names in Urdu. The first two lines carry the fact that defines this whole pack. اردو and ہندی are the SAME SPOKEN LANGUAGE in two scripts — one grammar, one core vocabulary, one everyday sentence — and a speaker of either understands the other without study. They separate in two places only. Script: Urdu is written right-to-left in the Perso-Arabic alphabet in the sloping Nastaʿlīq hand, Hindi left-to-right in Devanagari, and neither reader can read the other's page. Register: as the vocabulary climbs away from the kitchen and the street towards law, literature, science and administration, Urdu reaches for Persian and Arabic (مطالعہ, انتظامیہ, معاشرہ, تعلیم) where Hindi reaches for Sanskrit (adhyayan, prashasan, samaj, shiksha). A data pack lives almost entirely in that upper register — job titles, diagnoses, legal terms, government departments — which is exactly where the two diverge, so an Urdu pack transliterated out of a Hindi one would be in the wrong language while looking correct in the right script. The rest of the list is the languages of Pakistan (پنجابی has by far the most speakers there and no official status; اردو is the national language and is the mother tongue of only a small minority), the other large South Asian languages, and the world languages an Urdu form offers.
