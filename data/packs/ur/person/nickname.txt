description: Familiar and household names in Urdu. Three different things are on this list and all three are how a person is actually addressed. First, the clipped given name plus ی — عمران becomes عمی, نعمان becomes نومی, کامران becomes کامی, عائشہ becomes ایشی, فاطمہ becomes فاطی. Second, the گھر کا نام, the "house name", a completely separate name given in childhood that often bears no relation at all to the legal one: منا, منی, گڑیا, گڈو, پپو, بلو, ٹیپو, سونو. A great many people in the Urdu-speaking world are known by such a name for life inside the family and by their registered name nowhere else, so a nickname column here is NOT a diminutive of the first-name column and must not be derived from it. Third, kinship words used as address to people who are not relatives — بھائی, باجی, آپا, چاچا, ماموں — and the honorific-plus-title forms (خان صاحب, شاہ جی) that stand in for a name entirely.
locale: ur
