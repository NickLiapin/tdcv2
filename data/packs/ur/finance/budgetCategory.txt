description: Household budget category in Urdu. Three of these carry weight that a European budget list has no line for. زکوٰۃ is the obligatory annual alms of 2.5% on savings and is budgeted as a fixed outgoing, not as discretionary charity; قربانی is the animal bought at Eid al-Adha and is a large once-a-year expense; and جہیز, the goods a family accumulates for a daughter's marriage, is saved towards for years and is one of the biggest single line items a household here will ever plan for. بجلی کا بل sits separately from گیس کا بل because they are billed by different utilities.
locale: ur
