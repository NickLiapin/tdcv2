description: Bird names in Urdu. Several are words English borrowed on the way out of South Asia — مینا became "myna" and بلبل "bulbul" — while others are the ordinary Urdu names of birds an Urdu speaker actually sees: کوا, چڑیا, فاختہ, ہدہد. شاہین and باز are not synonyms and the difference matters to any Urdu reader, because Iqbal built a whole national symbolism on شاہین, the mountain falcon, as against the trained hunting باز. چکور, the chukar partridge that in Urdu poetry gazes at the moon, is a stock literary image rather than merely a bird.
