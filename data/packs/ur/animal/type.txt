description: Animals in Urdu, general list. Six of these mark a distinction Urdu makes and English does not: گائے is a cow and بھینس a water buffalo, which are different animals kept for different milk and are never one word in Urdu; بکری is a goat and بھیڑ a sheep, but دنبہ is specifically the fat-tailed sheep of the north; شیر in ordinary Urdu means the tiger and ببر شیر the lion, so a careless translation of "lion" as شیر renames the animal. مارخور is the screw-horned wild goat of the northern mountains and is Pakistan's national animal.
