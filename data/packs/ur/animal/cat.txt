description: Cat breeds in Urdu. Breed names are mostly the country of origin plus بلی, which is how Urdu names any imported animal type. دیسی بلی is the last line for a reason: "desi" means locally-bred and unpedigreed, it is what the overwhelming majority of cats in the Urdu-speaking world are, and a data set of Pakistani or Indian pets that contains only pedigree breeds is wrong about the population it claims to describe.
locale: ur
