description: Dog breeds in Urdu. The first block is the imported pedigree breeds, named as Urdu writes them. The second is the region's own, which no European list contains: بلی کتا (the "Bully Kutta", a large Punjabi and Sindhi mastiff), گل ٹیر and گل ڈونگ, the Afghan کوچی shepherd, and the تازی, the sighthound the Arabic-speaking world calls a saluki and Urdu has always called by this name. آوارہ کتا closes the list — the free-roaming street dog, which in most South Asian cities outnumbers every pedigree on this list put together.
