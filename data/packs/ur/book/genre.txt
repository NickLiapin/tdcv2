description: Book genre in Urdu. The first nine are verse FORMS, not subjects, and that is the defining difference between an Urdu catalogue and a European one: غزل, نظم, مرثیہ, قصیدہ, مثنوی and رباعی are shelved and reviewed as distinct genres, an Urdu bookshop has a section for each, and a reader asks for a غزل collection the way a Western reader asks for a novel. Prose forms follow — افسانہ is the short story as a literary institution, داستان the long oral romance, سفرنامہ the travel account, and خود نوشت the autobiography as against سوانح عمری, a life written by someone else.
