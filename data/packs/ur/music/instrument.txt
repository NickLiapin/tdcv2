description: Musical instruments in Urdu. رباب is the Pashtun lute and the national instrument of that music; الغوزہ is the twin flute of Sindh and Punjab, played two pipes at once with circular breathing; چمٹا is a pair of iron tongs with jingles, played in qawwali and folk. ہارمونیم is a European instrument that South Asia adopted, redesigned to be played sitting on the floor, and then made the backbone of qawwali and ghazal — it is more central here than in the place it came from. طبلہ is a PAIR of drums played with both hands and is not a single drum.
