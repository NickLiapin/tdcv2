description: Country and territory names in Urdu. Names are translated rather than transliterated where Urdu has its own form — Germany is جرمنی, Greece is یونان, Egypt is مصر, Britain is برطانیہ and the United States is امریکہ — and only where Urdu has no inherited name is the local one respelled. India is given as بھارت, which is the name Pakistani Urdu uses for the modern state; ہندوستان is the older and broader word for the subcontinent and is not the same word doing the same job. See geo/country.txt for the weighted subset used with capitals and currencies.
