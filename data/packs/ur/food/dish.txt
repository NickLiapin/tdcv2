description: Dishes in Urdu. The list is what is actually eaten across the Urdu-speaking world rather than a restaurant menu: the biryanis and pulaos, the slow-cooked نہاری and حلیم that are eaten at breakfast rather than at dinner, the kebab family (which is six different things and not one), the everyday dal-and-roti meals, and the sweets. سری پائے is head and trotters simmered overnight and is a Sunday breakfast, not an oddity. حلوہ پوری and نان چنے are breakfasts. قلفی and فالودہ are frozen desserts with no European equivalent.
locale: ur
