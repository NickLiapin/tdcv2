description: Vegetables in Urdu. Several are gourds that English lumps together and Urdu does not: توری, گھیا, کدو, ٹینڈا and کریلا are five distinct vegetables cooked five different ways. ساگ is a dish as much as a plant — the mustard greens of a Punjabi winter — and میتھی is fenugreek eaten as a leaf, not the seed sold as a spice. اروی and کچالو are taro corms.
