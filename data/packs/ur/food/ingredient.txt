description: Cooking ingredients in Urdu. The four flours are separate ingredients and not grades of one: آٹا is wholemeal wheat for roti, میدہ refined white flour, سوجی semolina, بیسن gram flour. The pulses likewise — دال چنا, دال مسور, دال ماش, دال مونگ and دال ارہر are five different lentils with five different cooking times. گھی and بناسپتی are clarified butter and its hydrogenated vegetable substitute, which is what most cooking here actually uses. گڑ is unrefined cane jaggery.
locale: ur
