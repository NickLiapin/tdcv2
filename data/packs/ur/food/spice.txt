description: Spices in Urdu. گرم مصالحہ is a BLEND and not a single spice — the proportions differ from household to household and the list is not fixed — and the same is true of چاٹ مصالحہ and بریانی مصالحہ, so a recipe field holding one of those three is naming a mixture. الائچی and بڑی الائچی are green and black cardamom and are not interchangeable. کالا نمک is black salt, a sulphurous rock salt with a distinct smell, not salt that is black.
locale: ur
