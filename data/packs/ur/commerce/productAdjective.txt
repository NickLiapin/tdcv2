These are the words a Pakistani or Indian listing actually uses: اصلی ("genuine") and امپورٹڈ carry more selling weight in this market than any quality claim, because the counterfeit trade is large enough that authenticity is the headline feature. ہاتھ سے بنا (handmade) and دیسی (locally made) are not downmarket words here.
