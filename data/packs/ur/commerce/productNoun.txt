description: Product nouns in Urdu — the things sold. The household block is worth reading as data rather than as vocabulary: چارپائی (the woven rope bed), دیگ and دیگچی (the two sizes of cooking pot), توا (the flat griddle a roti is made on), چمٹا, ہانڈی, لوٹا, صراحی, مٹکا and سلور (the stone mortar and pestle) are objects in daily use across the Urdu-speaking world that a European product list has no entry for at all. Electronics and appliances follow in the Urdu spellings a shop sign uses.
