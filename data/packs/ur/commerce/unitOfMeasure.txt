description: Unit of measure in Urdu. The metric units are official in both Pakistan and India, but the traditional ones below them are what a shop actually weighs with and a config that omits them is describing a market that does not exist: سیر is about 933 g and still used for grain and vegetables, تولہ is 11.66 g and is THE unit gold is priced in across South Asia, ماشہ and رتی are its subdivisions, and من (the "maund", 40 seers, about 37 kg) is the unit a wheat or cotton crop is bought in. گز is the yard cloth is cut by, and مرلہ and کنال are the land units every property advertisement uses — 1 کنال is 20 مرلہ, about 505 square metres.
locale: ur
