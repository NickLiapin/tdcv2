description: Parcel carriers as Urdu writes them. The first block is what a package in the Urdu-speaking world is actually carried by: ٹی سی ایس and لیوپرڈ are the two big Pakistani couriers, ڈاک خانہ the state post, ڈی ایکس is a domestic courier and بلیو ایکس, ٹریکس and پوسٹ ایکس handle e-commerce; ڈیلیوری, ڈیلہیوری and بلیو ڈارٹ serve the Indian side. The international integrators follow. Cash on delivery is the dominant payment mode across this whole market, so a carrier here is usually also collecting the money — see commerce/paymentMethod.
locale: ur
