description: Store department or product category in Urdu. کریانہ is the neighbourhood dry-goods shop and the word covers the whole grocery category; درزی and کپڑا are separate departments because unstitched cloth and tailoring are bought separately from ready-made clothes across this market. عطر و خوشبو and جہیز کا سامان have no European counterpart on a department list — the first is the attar and fragrance counter, the second the household goods bought for a dowry, which is a real retail category with its own shops.
locale: ur
