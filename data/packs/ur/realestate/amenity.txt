description: Property amenities in Urdu, as a listing lists them. Four of these are advertised here and nowhere in Europe, and all four exist because of infrastructure that cannot be assumed. بورنگ is the property's own water bore, which matters where the mains supply is intermittent. جنریٹر, یو پی ایس and سولر are three separate answers to load shedding — scheduled power cuts — and a listing names which one it has. سرونٹ کوارٹر, separate staff accommodation, is an ordinary feature of a middle-class house here. چوکیدار is a live-in watchman.
locale: ur
