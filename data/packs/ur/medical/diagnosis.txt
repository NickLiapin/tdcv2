Five of these are regional facts rather than entries on a list. تپ دق (tuberculosis) is not historical here — Pakistan carries one of the five largest TB burdens in the world. ہیپاٹائٹس سی is at among the highest prevalence anywhere. تھیلیسیمیا is common because first-cousin marriage is common, which concentrates recessive carriers. ڈینگی returns as a seasonal epidemic after every monsoon. پولیو is on the list because Pakistan and Afghanistan are the last two countries where wild poliovirus still circulates.
