description: Secondary address unit in Urdu. Most of these are the word نمبر ("number") after a noun, because an Urdu address numbers the thing rather than abbreviating it: مکان نمبر ۱۲, گلی نمبر ۴. پورشن, اپر پورشن and لوئر پورشن have no European equivalent and are the commonest rental unit in urban Pakistan — one floor of a house let separately with its own entrance, so the same مکان نمبر can be two households. کوارٹر is government or railway staff housing and marks the address as institutional.
locale: ur
