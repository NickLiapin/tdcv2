description: Leading element of an Urdu place name (نیا شہر, بالا کوٹ, ڈیرہ اسماعیل خان). Combine with a real toponym; on its own it does not name a real city. Two of these behave differently from a European prefix. ڈیرہ means the encampment of the man who founded the settlement and is always followed by his name, so ڈیرہ غازی خان and ڈیرہ اسماعیل خان are "Ghazi Khan's camp" and "Ismail Khan's camp" rather than places called Dera. چک plus a number is a whole class of Punjab village name created by the canal colonies of the 1890s, where surveyed squares were numbered rather than named, and thousands of villages still carry that number as their legal name.
locale: ur
