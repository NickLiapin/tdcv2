description: Trailing element of an Urdu place name (اسلام آباد, بہاولپور, سیالکوٹ, گوجرانوالہ). آباد is Persian for "settled/populated" and is the single most productive place-name ending in the Urdu world — a ruler's or saint's name plus آباد founds a city. پور is its Sanskrit-descended counterpart, inherited from the same soil, so پور and آباد mark the two layers of the region's history sitting side by side in the same list. شریف ("noble") attaches only to towns holding a major shrine. خیل is Pashto and marks the clan a settlement belongs to, so a name ending in it is almost always in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. کلاں and خورد are Persian "greater" and "lesser" and pair two villages of the same name.
locale: ur
