description: Kind of building, in Urdu. The list carries the housing vocabulary of the Urdu-speaking city, where the words are not interchangeable the way "house" and "home" are in English: مکان is the physical dwelling, گھر is the household living in it, کوٹھی a detached villa on its own plot, حویلی an old walled family compound built around a courtyard, and بنگلہ the colonial-era single-storey house whose name English borrowed back as "bungalow". پورشن is the Pakistani rental unit with no European equivalent — one floor of a house let separately, with its own door and stair, so a two-storey مکان is routinely two addresses. Places of worship for four communities are listed because Pakistani and Indian address data contains all of them.
