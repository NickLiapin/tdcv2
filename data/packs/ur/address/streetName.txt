description: Street and road names as Urdu forms them. Pakistani and Indian Urdu streets are named from four wells, and all four are here: gardens and light (گلشن, گلستان, بہار, نور, سحر, کہکشاں), civic virtues (آزادی, اتحاد, ترقی, انصاف, امن), poets and reformers whose names belong to the whole Urdu-reading world rather than to one country (اقبال, غالب, میر, انیس, حالی, سرسید, فیض, فراز), and flowers, trees and gemstones. Compose with address/streetSuffix — روڈ, سڑک, گلی or شاہراہ — since an Urdu street name is the qualifier and the suffix carries the noun. Names of specific national figures, cities and cantonment roads are deliberately absent: those belong to a country pack, not to the language.
locale: ur
