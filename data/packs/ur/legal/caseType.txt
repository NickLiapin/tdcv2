description: Kind of legal case in Urdu. خلع and طلاق are both divorce and are two different actions: طلاق is pronounced by the husband, خلع is sought by the wife through a court, and an Urdu family-law record distinguishes them. جرگہ and پنچایت are informal tribal and village dispute councils rather than courts — they have no statutory power in either Pakistan or India and they nevertheless settle an enormous share of disputes, so a case-type field that omits them is describing only the formal system.
locale: ur
