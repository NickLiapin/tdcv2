description: Legal terminology in Urdu. Much of this is Arabic through Islamic jurisprudence rather than through English: وکیل is an advocate, گواہ a witness, حلف an oath, وراثت inheritance, وصیت a will, and عدت the waiting period a woman observes after divorce or widowhood, which is a legal fact with financial consequences in family law here.
