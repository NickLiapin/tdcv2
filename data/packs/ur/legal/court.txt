description: Kinds of court, in Urdu. This holds the WORDS the language uses for a tier of court, not one country's judiciary — Urdu serves two legal systems, and Pakistan and India build different hierarchies out of largely the same terms. Two entries have no European equivalent: وفاقی شرعی عدالت is a court that tests whether laws conform to Islamic injunctions, and دار القضا is an informal Islamic arbitration forum. عدالت عالیہ is a High Court and عدالت عظمیٰ a Supreme Court.
locale: ur
