description: Kinds of official document in Urdu. Four have no European counterpart and are ordinary paperwork here. نکاح نامہ is the marriage contract — a signed civil document with financial terms, not a certificate issued after the fact. ڈومیسائل certifies which district a person belongs to and gates university admission and government jobs. فرد ملکیت is the extract from the land revenue register that proves who holds a piece of land. حلف نامہ is a sworn statement written on judicial stamp paper, which is how a great many routine declarations are made across the region. کردار کا سرٹیفکیٹ, a character certificate from a school or the police, is required for jobs and admissions.
locale: ur
