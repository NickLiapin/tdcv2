description: Sports in Urdu. The order says something true about the region. کرکٹ is not one sport among many here — it is the sport, and everything else competes for what is left. ہاکی comes second by history rather than by present popularity: Pakistan won three Olympic golds and four World Cups at field hockey and it is still the official national sport. اسکواش third for the same reason — Pakistani players held the world championship almost continuously from 1950 to 1997. کبڈی and پہلوانی (traditional wrestling on earth) are the village sports with the deepest roots, نیزہ بازی is tent pegging on horseback and Pakistan is a leading nation in it, and گلی ڈنڈا is the stick-and-peg street game most children here learn before they learn cricket.
