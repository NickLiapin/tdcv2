description: Field hockey positions in Urdu. Pakistan's hockey vocabulary keeps the older English names (اِن سائیڈ رائٹ, سنٹر ہاف) that the modern game has largely dropped, because that is the era the country's hockey was built in. پنالٹی کارنر اسپیشلسٹ is a real squad role — the drag-flicker who takes short corners.
