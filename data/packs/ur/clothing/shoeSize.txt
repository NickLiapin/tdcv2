description: Shoe size as it is printed on a shoebox in the Urdu-speaking world: the UK scale, not the European one. Pakistan and India both inherited British sizing and never changed it, so a shoe labelled 8 is a UK 8 — roughly EU 42 — and a config that reads these as European sizes will be four numbers out on every row. Half sizes appear mostly on imported sports footwear; locally-made خُسہ and chappal are sold in whole sizes only. Children's sizes restart at 1 on a separate scale and are not mixed in here.
