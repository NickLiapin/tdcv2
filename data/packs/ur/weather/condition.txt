description: Weather in Urdu. The vocabulary is monsoon vocabulary: مون سون, موسلا دھار بارش (torrential rain) and حبس (oppressive still humidity before rain) name states of weather that a temperate list has no words for. سموگ is the last line and is recent — since the mid-2010s the Punjab plain has had a winter smog season severe enough that schools close for it, so it is now a routine weather condition rather than pollution.
