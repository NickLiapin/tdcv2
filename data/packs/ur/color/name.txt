description: Colour names in Urdu. The list is mostly Persian and Sanskrit-descended vocabulary rather than the loanwords a modern paint chart uses, because these are the words Urdu prose and Urdu speech actually contain. Several name a colour by naming the thing that has it and cannot be translated by one English word: گندمی is wheat-coloured skin, سانولا is dark-complexioned, موتیا is the off-white of the jasmine bud, دھانی the pale green of unripe rice, فیروزی turquoise after the stone, and مورپنکھی the blue-green of a peacock's neck. کاسنی is the mauve of the chicory flower. سرخ and لال are both red and are not interchangeable in register — لال is the everyday word, سرخ the literary one.
locale: ur
