description: Academic qualification in Urdu. The ladder is not the European one. میٹرک is the tenth-class school certificate and انٹرمیڈیٹ the twelfth, and the twelfth splits by stream — ایف اے arts, ایف ایس سی pre-medical or pre-engineering, آئی کام commerce — so an Urdu CV states which. The بی اے and بی ایس سی that follow were two-year "pass" degrees until Pakistan replaced them with the four-year بی ایس, so both shapes are in circulation. The last four lines are the دینی مدارس ladder, which is a genuine parallel system rather than a religious footnote: درس نظامی is its eight-year curriculum, and Pakistan's Higher Education Commission recognises شہادۃ العالمیہ as equivalent to an MA in Arabic and Islamic Studies, so it is a degree that appears in real employment records.
