description: Business buzz adjective in Urdu, for generating corporate catch-phrases. Pakistani and Indian corporate Urdu code-switches heavily, so about a third of these are English loans in Urdu script (ڈیجیٹل, اسمارٹ, گلوبل) sitting beside the Persianate formal register (جامع, مربوط, مستعد) — that mixture is what an actual company brochure in Urdu reads like, and a list written entirely in one register would not.
