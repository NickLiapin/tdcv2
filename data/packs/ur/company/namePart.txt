description: Evocative noun for a company name in Urdu — compose with company/legalSuffix. Pakistani and Indian firm names are drawn from a narrow and recognisable set of wells, and all of them are here: light and stars (نور, ضیاء, شمس, ستارہ, کہکشاں), heights and strength (شاہین, عقاب, پہاڑ, فولاد), gardens (گلشن, چمن, بہار), auspicious abstractions (برکت, رحمت, کامیاب, ترقی), the compounds built on ال- (الکریم, الرحمٰن, الفلاح), and the geography of the region (سندھ, راوی, چناب, ہمالیہ, خیبر). The ال- forms are the single most productive pattern for a Muslim-owned firm in this market.
