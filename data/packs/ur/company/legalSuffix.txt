description: The element that closes a company name in Urdu. The first block is the statutory forms — پرائیویٹ لمیٹڈ, لمیٹڈ, and the two partnership words — written in Urdu script as a company's own Urdu letterhead prints them. The rest are trade-name endings rather than legal forms, and several are specific to this business culture: برادران is "brothers" and is the commonest family-firm ending in Urdu (شیخ برادران), اینڈ سنز its English-borrowed twin, تاجران merchants, ٹریڈرز and انٹرپرائزز the small-trader endings a shop sign carries, and کارخانہ a workshop. A config joining a namePart to one of these should not also add a European suffix.
locale: ur
