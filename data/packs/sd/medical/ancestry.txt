description: Genetic ancestry group in Sindhi, the parent of medical/diagnosisByAncestry. These are descent populations as clinical genetics uses them, not social race categories. The compass words are Sindhi's own — اتر north, ڏکڻ south, اوڀر east — rather than the Persian شمالي / جنوبي / مشرقي an Urdu list would use. ڏکڻ ايشيائي is the group most speakers of this pack belong to, and it carries specific clinical weight in Sindh: marriage between first cousins is ordinary rather than exceptional here, with survey estimates putting consanguinity above half of all marriages, and cousin marriage concentrates recessive carriers inside a single biradari. That is why beta thalassaemia runs far above the frequency a continental average would predict.
