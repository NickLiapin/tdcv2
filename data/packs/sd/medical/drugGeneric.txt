description: Generic drug names in Sindhi script. Pakistan is a very large generic manufacturer and a prescription written in Sindh names the molecule far more often than a brand, so this is the form that actually reaches a pharmacy counter. Names are transliterated rather than translated, and the transliteration itself is diagnostic: English k becomes ڪ, English hard t becomes the retroflex ٽ and English d becomes ڊ, so the same molecule is spelt visibly differently from its Urdu rendering.
locale: sd
