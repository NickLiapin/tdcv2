description: Nephrology diagnoses in Sindhi. Kidney stones are markedly more common across this belt than in Europe — heat, chronic dehydration and diet — and diabetic kidney disease is the main road into dialysis here. Sindh is also unusual in how that dialysis is paid for: a large share of the province's patients are kept alive by charitable and government-subsidised units rather than by insurance, which is why ڊائيلاسز تي مريض is a recorded status in its own right.
