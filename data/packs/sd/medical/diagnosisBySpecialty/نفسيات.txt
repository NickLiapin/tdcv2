description: Psychiatric diagnoses in Sindhi. Mental illness carries heavy stigma across Sindh and is very substantially under-recorded; complaints arrive as bodily symptoms — گهٻراهٽ, سڄي بدن جو سور, ٿڪاوٽ — far more often than as psychological ones, so any psychiatric column drawn from real records understates prevalence. تماڪ جي لت is separated out because chewed tobacco, not smoked, is the dominant form here.
