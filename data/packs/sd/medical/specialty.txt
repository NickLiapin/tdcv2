description: Medical specialties in Sindhi. The last two lines are not folklore: حڪمت (Unani, the Greco-Arabic tradition) and هوميو پيٿي are licensed and separately registered systems of practice in Pakistan, with their own councils and their own qualified practitioners, and a Sindhi patient record genuinely names them. Note نڪ ڪن نڙي for ENT: three plain body words rather than a Latinate compound, and both نڪ (nose) and ڪن (ear) take ڪ, the plain-k letter, while a word like کنگهه (cough) takes ک, the aspirated one.
