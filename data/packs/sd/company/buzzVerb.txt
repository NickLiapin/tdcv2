description: Business buzz verb in Sindhi, given in the infinitive, which in this language ends in -ڻ: ڪرڻ to do, ڏيڻ to give, بڻائڻ to make. Most entries are compounds of a noun with one of those three, so the same endings recur down the list — that repetition is how the grammar builds verbs, not a shortage of vocabulary, and it is also the clearest way to tell the language apart from Urdu, whose infinitive ends in a long -a instead. The simple verbs are the ones carrying consonants Urdu has no letter for: پکيڙڻ to spread, ڳنڍڻ to join, کٽڻ to win, پرکڻ to test, گڏ ڪرڻ to bring together.
