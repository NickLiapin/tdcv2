description: The element that closes a company name in Sindhi. The first three are statutory: a firm incorporated in Pakistan is a پرائيويٽ لميٽيڊ or a لميٽيڊ, and a ڪوآپريٽو سوسائٽي is the third registered form, used by farming and housing societies. Sindhi letterheads borrow all three from English rather than translating them. Everything after that is a trade name rather than a legal form, and several belong to this business culture in particular: برادران is brothers and is the commonest family-firm ending in the market, اينڊ سنز is its English-borrowed twin, واپاري and ٽريڊرز are the merchant endings a shop sign carries, انٽرپرائزز is the small-trader ending, and ڪارخانو is a workshop. A config that joins a namePart to one of these should not add a European suffix as well.
locale: sd
