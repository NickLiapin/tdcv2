description: Business buzz adjective in Sindhi, for generating corporate catch-phrases. About a third are English loans, and the spelling of a loan is what identifies the language rather than the word: the t of اسمارٽ and the d of ڊجيٽل are written ٽ and ڊ, letters Sindhi uses and Urdu does not, so the same two borrowings come out visibly different in the two scripts. The rest is the Persianate formal register a Sindhi prospectus shares with Urdu — جامع, مربوط, مستعد — plus a few adjectives that belong to Sindhi alone: رٿابند is planned or laid out, ڀروسي جوڳو is worthy of trust, and پيشيوراڻو carries the Sindhi adjectival ending -اڻو, which Urdu has no form of.
locale: sd
