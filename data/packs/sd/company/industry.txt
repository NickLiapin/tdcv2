description: Industry sector in Sindhi, as a company register or a job advertisement names it. This is not the Punjab list respelled. Sindh grows cotton, rice and sugarcane, and the first processing step of each is a separately registered trade rather than a branch of farming: ڪپهه جننگ is ginning, چانورن جي ڇڙائي is rice husking, کنڊ سازي is sugar milling, and کجور جي پيداوار is the date crop of Khairpur, one of the largest date-growing districts anywhere. The Thar coalfield gives ڪوئلي جي کوٽائي, and Karachi puts بندرگاهي خدمتون and جهاز راني on a provincial list where an inland province would have neither. اسلامي بينڪاري and تڪافل are deliberately kept apart from مالياتي خدمتون and بيمو, because both hold their own licence in Pakistan: تڪافل is mutual cover in which the participants underwrite one another out of a shared fund, so no premium is paid to an insurer and no interest is earned on it. اجرڪ ۽ سوسي, رلي سازي, جنڊي ڪم and ڪاشي گري are the block-printed cloth, the patchwork quilts, the lacquered woodwork and the blue tilework that Hala and Bhit Shah live on.
