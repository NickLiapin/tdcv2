description: Product nouns in Sindhi - the things sold. The household block is worth reading as data rather than as vocabulary: کٽ the woven rope bed, رلي the patchwork quilt stitched from scraps that is the signature textile of a Sindhi house, ديڳ and ديڳڙي the two sizes of cooking pot, توو the flat griddle a roti is made on, مٽ the porous earthen jar that keeps water cold without electricity, لوٽو the water pot carried to the washroom, and سل ۽ لوڙهو the stone slab and roller that spices are ground on. None of them has an entry on a European product list. The file also shows the pair the Sindhi alphabet turns on: ڪتاب a book takes ڪ, plain k, while کٽ a bed takes ک, aspirated kh, and the two letters are not interchangeable.
