description: Colour as it appears on a Sindhi product listing, a shorter and more commercial set than color/name. The basic colour words are Sindhi rather than Persian loans - اڇو white, ڪارو black, ڳاڙهو red, سائو green, نيرو blue, ڀورو brown - and a catalogue makes new shades productively by putting ڳوڙهو (deep) or هلڪو (light) in front of them, which is why those pairs recur down the list. ڌاڻي is the pale green of young shoots and has no single-word English equivalent.
locale: sd
