Two of the cloth names are Sindh's own and appear on no other locale's list: اجرڪ is the resist-block-printed cotton in madder red and indigo that is worn as a shawl, spread as a bedsheet and given at weddings and funerals across the province, and سوسي is the narrow striped handloom cloth woven at Hala and Nasarpur. ٽالهي is sheesham, the timber Sindhi furniture is actually built from, and a listing names the wood rather than saying wooden. پتل, ٽامو and ڪانسي - brass, copper and bronze - are distinct materials on a kitchenware listing, never synonyms for metal.
