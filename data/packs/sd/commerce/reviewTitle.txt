description: Product review headlines in Sindhi, as a buyer writes them. The mix is deliberately uneven the way real reviews are - enthusiastic, flat and disappointed - and several are the stock phrases of Pakistani marketplaces: پئسا پورا وصول (full value for the money), اصلي مال (genuine goods) and تصوير کان مختلف (different from the photo), which is the standard complaint where a listing is photographed once and stocked loosely afterwards. دٻو ڀڳل مليو appears because a packaging complaint is about the journey rather than about the seller.
locale: sd
