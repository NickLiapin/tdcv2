description: Adjectives a Sindhi product listing uses. اصلي (genuine) and امپورٽيڊ carry more selling weight in this market than any quality claim, because the counterfeit trade is large enough that authenticity is the headline feature rather than a footnote, and هٿ سان ٺهيل and ديسي are not downmarket words here. The core adjectives are Sindhi rather than borrowed - وڏو big, ننڍو small, ڳرو heavy, سهڻو beautiful - and they are spelt with the implosive and retroflex letters that give Sindhi the largest alphabet of any Arabic-derived script, fifty-two letters where Urdu has under forty.
