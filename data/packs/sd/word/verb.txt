description: Common verbs, given in the infinitive, which in Sindhi ends in -ڻ: ڪرڻ to do, وڃڻ to go, کائڻ to eat. That ending is one of the clearest markers of the language — Urdu's infinitive ends in -نا, so ڪرڻ and کائڻ could not be mistaken for کرنا and کھانا. Eight of these carry the aspirated ک: رکڻ, کڻڻ, کائڻ, لکڻ, سکڻ, سيکارڻ, کولڻ and کلڻ.
