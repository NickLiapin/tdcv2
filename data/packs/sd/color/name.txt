description: Colour names in Sindhi. The first block is native, and it is what separates this list from an Urdu one: اڇو white, ڪارو black, ڳاڙهو red, پيلو yellow, سائو green, نيرو blue, ناسي brown and ڦڪو pale. Sindhi colour adjectives agree with their noun in gender, so the -و ending running down the list is the masculine singular and ڪارو becomes ڪاري before a feminine noun. ڪارو (black) and کارو (salty) differ only in the first letter, plain ڪ against aspirated ک, and are not the same word. The Persian and Arabic layer follows, and after it the shades named for the thing that carries them: ڌاڻي is the pale green of young shoots, موتئي the off-white of the jasmine bud, ڪٿئي the brown of catechu, گلناري the red of the pomegranate flower, and ڪڻڪ رنگو the colour of wheat, which is said of skin.
