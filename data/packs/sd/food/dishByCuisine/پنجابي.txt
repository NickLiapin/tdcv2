description: Punjabi dishes in Sindhi. سرنهن جو ساگ eaten with مڪئي جي ماني is a winter dish and only a winter dish, because mustard greens are a cold-season crop - a Punjabi menu offering it in June is describing a restaurant rather than a kitchen. دال مکڻي is named for butter (مکڻ) and is cooked overnight with cream until the black lentils turn almost purple; note the aspirated ک in مکڻي against the plain ڪ of مڪئي and ڪڙهي in the same list.
locale: sd
