description: Chinese dishes as they are eaten and named in Sindh. This is Pakistani-Chinese cooking, which is its own hybrid rather than a copy: چڪن مانچورين and هاٽ اينڊ سار سوپ are South Asian inventions that no kitchen in China serves, and چائنيز چانور means fried rice with a particular local seasoning. Karachi has had Chinese restaurants since the 1930s and this is what they cook.
