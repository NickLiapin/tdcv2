description: Dishes as they are eaten and named in Sindh. The list leads with سنڌي برياني because it is the dish the province is known for and it is not simply a spicier biryani: it carries potato, sour dried plum (آلو بخارو) and yoghurt, which is why a Sindhi cook can tell one from a Karachi or a Bombay biryani at a glance. Four entries are found almost nowhere else. ڪوڪي is a thick griddle bread kneaded with onion, green chilli and pomegranate seed and cooked slowly so it keeps for a journey. سائي ڀاڄي is spinach, dill and lentils cooked to a purée and eaten with rice. پلو مڇي is the hilsa of the Indus, a bony, oily river fish whose season is watched the way a mango season is watched. تڙيل دال with دال پڪوان is the Sindhi breakfast: a crisp fried disc of flour and a bowl of split-pea dal. ٿڌل closes the list, a cold almond and poppy-seed drink of the hot months.
locale: sd
