description: Job titles in Sindhi, across sectors. Two features of this list belong to Sindh and to nowhere else in Pakistan. First, the revenue service keeps its own titles: the man who holds the village record of rights is the تپيدار and the officer set over a taluka is the مختيارڪار, where Punjab says patwari and tehsildar — the same work under different names, and the entries they make still decide who owns what. Second, the countryside runs on tenancy: the هاري works land held by a زميندار for a share of the crop under the Sindh Tenancy Act, and both words belong in an employment record here exactly as an engineer does. مهاڻو is the fisherman of the Indus delta and of Manchhar Lake, a hereditary calling rather than a trade one takes up. کير فروش, the milk seller, shows why the two kaf letters have to be kept apart: ک is an aspirated kh and ڪ is plain k, so ڪير is who and کير is milk.
locale: sd
