description: Hospitality jobs in Sindhi. باورچي is the cook, نانبائي specifically the tandoor baker and a separate trade with its own premises, and چانهه وارو keeps the tea stall. On a Sindh highway the word hotel covers all three: the roadside hotel is first a place to stop for tea and a charpoy, and only afterwards a restaurant.
