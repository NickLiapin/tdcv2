description: Trailing element of a Sindhi place name. آباد and پور are the two great productive endings and they come from different layers of the same soil — آباد is Persian for "settled" and takes a founder's or a saint's name, پور is its Sanskrit-descended equivalent doing the identical job a thousand years earlier. پتڻ is a river ferry crossing, and it ends place names because the Indus carried no bridges for most of its history: a town grew wherever the crossing was. واهه is an irrigation canal and بند an embankment, and both name settlements that did not exist before the Sukkur Barrage laid its canal system across upper Sindh in 1932. کوهه is a well, the thing a desert village in Thar is founded on and named for. ڀٽ is a sand dune — Bhit Shah, the dune where Shah Abdul Latif is buried, is the one everybody knows.
locale: sd
