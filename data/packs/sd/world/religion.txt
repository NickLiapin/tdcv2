description: Religions. Sindh holds the largest Hindu population of any Pakistani province — several million people, concentrated in Tharparkar and Umerkot — so هندو ڌرم is not a foreign entry on this list. The Muslim majority is overwhelmingly Sufi in practice, organised around shrines rather than seminaries, which is why the province's religious calendar is a calendar of عرس festivals. Note that Sindhi carries three words for a religion — Sanskrit-derived ڌرم and مت beside Arabic-derived مذهب — and uses them across the board rather than splitting them by origin: the dictionary glosses ڌرم as مذهب outright and writes ٻڌ مذهب for Buddhism.
