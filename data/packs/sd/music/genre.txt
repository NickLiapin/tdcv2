description: Musical genres, and وائي, سُر and ڳجهارت belong to Sindhi and to no other language — ڪافي is shared with Punjabi and Saraiki, and ڀڳت is pan-Indic outside its Sindhi sense of a folk dance-drama. ڪافي and وائي are the two forms Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai wrote in, and سُر is a chapter of his Shah jo Risalo — thirty of them, each named for a mode and a story, and still sung nightly at his shrine at Bhit Shah by singers who hold the post by inheritance. ڳجهارت is the Sindhi riddle-song, a competitive form in which the answer is hidden in the verse. لاڏو and سهرو are both the groom's wedding songs and the dictionary lists each under the other — لاڏو the one women sing dancing in a ring, سهرو the one sung as the garland is tied.
