description: Instruments. The first six are Sindh's own. يڪتارو is the single-string drone a wandering faqir carries; دنبورو is the five-string lute of Shah Latif's shrine, said to have been designed by him — the Latif Award is a model of it in gold; سرندو is the bowed folk fiddle; نڙ is a long reed flute played with circular breathing; and بورينڊو is a clay ocarina, three or four finger holes in a fired sphere, a village and children's instrument whose shape answers to clay figures from Mohenjo-daro — the same thing, in the same place, across some five thousand years. The shepherd's instrument is the الغوزو, the paired flute at the end of the Sindhi block.
