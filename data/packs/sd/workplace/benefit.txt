description: Employee benefits. گريجويٽي is the statutory one — thirty days' wages for every completed year — and پراويڊنٽ فنڊ is a voluntary company scheme beside it; both are lump sums paid on leaving rather than a pension stream. عيدي — the bonus paid before Eid — is expected rather than discretionary, and حج جي سهولت, an employer paying for the pilgrimage, is a real benefit offered by large Pakistani firms and appears on no Western list.
