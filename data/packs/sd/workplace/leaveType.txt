description: Leave types. موڪل is the Sindhi word for leave, where Urdu says چھٹی. Three entries have no Western counterpart: حج جي موڪل and عمري جي موڪل for the pilgrimages, which large employers grant once in a career, and عدت جي موڪل — the waiting period a widowed or divorced woman observes, which employers accommodate in practice rather than by statute.
locale: sd
