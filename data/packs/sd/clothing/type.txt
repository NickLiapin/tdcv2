description: Garments, and the two that matter most are Sindh's own. اجرڪ is the block-printed shawl in madder red and indigo whose trefoil motif is the one carved on the Mohenjo-daro priest-king's shawl; it is given as a mark of honour and worn by men and women alike. سنڌي ٽوپي is the mirrored, cut-out cap worn with it, and the two together are the provincial dress — Sindhi Culture Day, the first Sunday of December, fills whole cities with them. Note سٿڻ for the loose trousers where Urdu says شلوار, and جتي for the flat leather shoe.
