description: Academic qualification in Sindhi. The ladder is not the European one. مئٽرڪ is the certificate for class ten and انٽرميڊيئٽ for class twelve, and neither is awarded by the school: both are set and marked by a Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, of which Sindh has several — Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas and Shaheed Benazirabad — so a CV here names the board along with the year and the grade. The twelfth year splits by stream and the CV says which: ايف اي arts, ايف ايس سي pre-medical or pre-engineering, آءِ ڪام commerce. بي اي and بي ايس سي below were two-year pass degrees until Pakistan moved to the four-year بي ايس, so both shapes are in circulation and both turn up in real employment records. The last four lines are the madrasa ladder, which is a genuine parallel system rather than a religious footnote: درس نظامي is its curriculum, and the Higher Education Commission recognises شهادت العالميه as equivalent to a master's degree in Arabic and Islamic Studies.
