description: Occasions. ڄڃ is the wedding procession that brings the groom's party to the bride's village — Urdu says بارات — and it opens with ڄ, one of Sindhi's four implosive consonants — ٻ ڏ ڄ ڳ — which Urdu and Punjabi lack, though neighbouring Saraiki has the same four. عرس is the death anniversary of a saint, kept as a festival at the shrine: Shah Abdul Latif's at Bhit Shah and Lal Shahbaz Qalandar's at Sehwan draw hundreds of thousands over several days and are the largest gatherings in the province's year. چاليهو is the fortieth day after a death.
