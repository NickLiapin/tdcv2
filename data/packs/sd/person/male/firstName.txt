description: Male given names in Sindhi. محمد leads for a structural reason rather than a statistical one: across the region it is very commonly the FIRST element of a two-part name whose SECOND element is what the man is actually called - Muhammad Iqbal is addressed as Iqbal, Muhammad Bachal as Bachal - so محمد works as a prefix as much as a given name and stands at the head of a very large share of male records. Then come the names that make this a Sindhi list and not a generic Pakistani one. عبداللطيف is for Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai, the eighteenth-century poet whose Shah Jo Risalo is the central book of the language and whose shrine at Bhit Shah draws crowds every year; سچل is for Sachal Sarmast, the other great Sufi poet, whose name means truthful; شهباز is for Lal Shahbaz Qalandar of Sehwan. Naming a son after a saint or a poet is ordinary here in a way it is not further north. Two further patterns belong to Sindh alone. The ڏنو names - الله ڏنو, and the same idea in الله وسايو and مولا بخش - mean given by God or settled by God and are vowed names, promised at a shrine before the child was born. And the weekday names: a boy born on Monday may simply be called سومار, on Wednesday ٻڌو, on Thursday خميسو, on Friday جمعو. The compound families are the other shape a European list has no room for: عبد + a divine attribute (عبدالرحمٰن, عبدالقادر, عبدالستار) is ONE name and not two words; غلام + a holy figure (غلام رسول, غلام حسين) is another; and ...الله (ثنا الله, عطا الله) a third. الله رکيو belongs to the same family and shows the letter that catches people out: its ک is the aspirated kh of رکڻ, to keep, so the name means kept by God, and it is a different letter from the plain ڪ that carries every Arabic k in this file (ڪريم, اڪرم, شڪيل, ڪاظم). Both letters are Sindhi and neither substitutes for the other. Every final long i is ي, never ی. THE WEIGHTS ARE AN ORDERING, NOT MEASURED SHARES. Pakistan's census publishes no name frequencies and NADRA releases no counts, so the list is ordered by observed commonness and carries the project's default gentle decay, floor(1000000 * rank ** -0.1), which spreads only about 1.7 to 1 from head to tail. That is the right shape for a diffuse naming pool, but note that it does NOT encode محمد's real prominence as a leading element, which is far larger than 1.7 to 1 - a config needing a realistic share must set that weight itself.
locale: sd
weighted: true
