description: Nationality or demonym in Sindhi, adjective form. Led by Pakistan, then ڀارتي, because India comes second in a Sindhi list in a way it would not in a Balochi or Pashto one: Partition split the Sindhi people almost in half, several million Sindhi speakers live in India, and a Sindhi household on either side of the border is likely to have relatives on the other. After that come the Gulf states, where large numbers of Sindhis work, and Afghanistan, which appears constantly in Pakistani records after four decades of refugee movement.
