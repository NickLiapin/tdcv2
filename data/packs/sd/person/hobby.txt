description: Hobbies and interests in Sindhi. Several here are specific to Sindh and are among the most widely practised things on the list. ڀرت ڀرڻ is Sindhi embroidery, a mirror-work and chain-stitch tradition that is a household skill rather than a craft revival, and اجرڪ سازي is the block-printing of the ajrak, the madder-and-indigo shawl that is the province's emblem and is given as a gift at every significant occasion. ڪوڏي is kabaddi under its Sindhi name. پتنگ بازي is competitive kite flying with cut lines and a season, not a children's pastime. ڪبوتر بازي is pigeon fancying and racing, with its own market and its own vocabulary. Note the verbs: Sindhi forms them with a final ڻ - پڙهڻ to read, ٻڌڻ to listen, ڳائڻ to sing, پالڻ to keep an animal - where Urdu uses نا, so a Sindhi hobby phrase does not look like an Urdu one even when it means the same thing.
