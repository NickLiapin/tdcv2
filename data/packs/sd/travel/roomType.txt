description: Hotel room types in Sindhi. فيملي روم is the ordinary booking in this market rather than a special request, because families travel together and in numbers, and a hotel that cannot put six people in one room loses the business. عورتن لاءِ الڳ ماڙ, a women-only floor, is a real room category in Pakistan and the Gulf rather than a courtesy, and it is what a family booking for unaccompanied women will actually ask for. ماڙ is the Sindhi word for a storey, and the same root gives ماڙي, a multi-storey house, which turns up as a village name element as well.
