description: Filler words in Sindhi, the lorem-ipsum vocabulary of this pack. Written by hand: data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs assembles these three files from public-domain books and has no Sindhi title to draw on, so the list was compiled rather than extracted. It is deliberately ordinary — kin, the body, the house, food, the land, animals, the town, the state, abstractions, adjectives, verbs, numbers and function words — so that a random draw reads as Sindhi rather than as a glossary of one subject. Where Sindhi has a word of its own it stands here in place of the Perso-Arabic one an Urdu list would carry: ماڻهو for a person, ڳوٺ for a village, وڻ for a tree, ڏينهن for a day, اک for an eye. Every verb is an infinitive and every Sindhi infinitive ends in ڻ — ڪرڻ to do, وڃڻ to go, کائڻ to eat — where Urdu ends in -نا. Two of the function words are single characters with no Urdu counterpart: ۾ (in) and ۽ (and).
