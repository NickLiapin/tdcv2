description: Playing positions in Sindhi, mixed across sports. Cricket positions dominate the list because cricket dominates the province, and several of them - سلپ, گلي, پوائنٽ, لانگ آن, ڊيپ فائن ليگ - name a patch of ground rather than a role in a team, a kind of position that no other sport here has. کيڏاري is the Sindhi word for a player, from کيڏڻ to play, which is why the twelfth man is ٻارهون کيڏاري rather than a borrowed English phrase.
locale: sd
