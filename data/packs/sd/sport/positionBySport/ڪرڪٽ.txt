Batting order, bowling type and fielding place are three different kinds of position and all three sit on the same list, which is exactly how a team sheet reads. ٻارهون کيڏاري, the twelfth man, may field but neither bat nor bowl. Karachi club cricket has fed the national side for seventy years - Hanif Mohammad and Javed Miandad both came up through it - and the province plays as Sindh in the domestic first-class season.
