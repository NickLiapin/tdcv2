A ريڊر crosses alone into the opposing half and has to get back before his breath runs out; the defenders are named for where they stand along the back line, ڪارنر at the two ends, ڪور beside them and اِن at the centre, and they work as a chain rather than as individuals. Kabaddi is played from village grounds up to national level in Pakistan and its position names have no English originals to borrow.
