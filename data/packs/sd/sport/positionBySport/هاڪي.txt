description: Field hockey positions in Sindhi. Pakistani hockey keeps the older English names - اِن سائيڊ رائيٽ, سينٽر هاف - that the modern game has largely dropped, because that is the era the country's hockey was built in: Olympic gold in 1960, 1968 and 1984, and four World Cups. پينلٽي ڪارنر اسپيشلسٽ is a real squad role rather than a nickname, the drag-flicker who takes the short corners and is often the highest scorer in the side.
locale: sd
