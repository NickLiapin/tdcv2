description: Terms of procedure. Four near the end come from Islamic family law and are used in a Pakistani court exactly as written: مهر, the dower a husband owes his wife and which is stated in the marriage contract; نان نفقو, maintenance; عدت, the waiting period after divorce or widowhood; and وصيت, the bequest, which under Islamic inheritance rules may cover only a third of an estate because the rest is already assigned.
