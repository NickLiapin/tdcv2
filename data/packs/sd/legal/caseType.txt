description: Case types. The last two are not courts at all and that is the point: فيصلو is the ordinary word for a decision or verdict and also names the Sindhi tribal settlement, convened by elders and binding by custom rather than statute — Sindh says faislo where Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa say jirga — and پنچائت is its village council counterpart. A very large share of disputes in rural Sindh is settled through them and never reaches a courtroom, so a dataset of Pakistani legal matters that omits them describes only the part the state can see.
