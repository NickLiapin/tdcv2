description: Technical verb in Sindhi, plain infinitive. The Sindhi infinitive ends in ڻ — ڳنڍڻ, پڙهڻ, لکڻ, روڪڻ — and that final ڻ is a retroflex n, made with the tongue curled back, which Arabic does not have and which Sindhi added a letter for. Where the base is an Arabic or English noun rather than a Sindhi verb root, Sindhi makes a verb of it with ڪرڻ, "to do", which is why roughly half this list is a two-word compound: تجزيو ڪرڻ is literally "to do analysis".
locale: sd
