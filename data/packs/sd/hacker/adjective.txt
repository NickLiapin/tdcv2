description: Technical adjective in Sindhi, for generating mock system chatter. Sindhi reaches for its own word wherever it has one rather than borrowing wholesale: ڳجهو is secret, ورهايل is distributed, ڳرو and هلڪو are heavy and light. Adjectives of this shape agree with the noun they qualify — the final و is masculine singular and becomes ي in the feminine — so a config that pastes one of these in front of an arbitrary noun will sometimes produce a mismatch that a Sindhi reader notices immediately.
