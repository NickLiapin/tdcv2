description: Technical verb in the continuous aspect, in Sindhi — the form a progress message takes. Sindhi builds it from the verb stem plus رهيو آهي, so ڳنڍي رهيو آهي is "is connecting" and لکي رهيو آهي is "is writing". The auxiliary carries gender: رهيو is masculine and رهي feminine, and the masculine form used throughout here is what an unnamed machine subject takes by default, which is why every line in this file is internally consistent and can be pasted after a system name without further agreement work.
locale: sd
