description: Gemstone (Slovak). Dubnicky opal - the precious opal of Dubnik near Presov - came from what was for centuries the world's only significant opal mine, and the Slovak crown jewels and several European royal collections are set with it; Slovak obsidian from the Zemplin hills is likewise a named local stone. These are the Slovak counterparts of the Czech granat and vltavin.
