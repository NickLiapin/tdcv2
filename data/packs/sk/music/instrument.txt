description: Musical instrument (Slovak). Fujara - the two-metre shepherd overtone flute of Podpolanie - is on the UNESCO list of intangible heritage, and so is the Slovak gajdy tradition; koncovka is its stopless little sister, drumbla the jew's harp, ozembuch the stamping stick of a folk band and heligonka the diatonic button accordion. None of these is a Czech instrument.
