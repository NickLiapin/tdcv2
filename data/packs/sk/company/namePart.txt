description: Evocative noun for a Slovak company name (compose with a legalSuffix or an adjective). Draws on Slovak rivers, mountains, regions, minerals and old trades - the naming stock Slovak firms have actually used since the nineteenth century. Drotar (the itinerant Kysuce wire-worker), baca and valach (the head shepherd and his hands on a salas) and bryndziaren (a sheep-cheese dairy) are trades with no Czech counterpart.
locale: sk
