description: Parcel carriers commonly used in Slovakia. Zásielkovňa is the Slovak brand of Packeta; Z-Box, BalíkoBOX and AlzaBox are parcel-locker networks, and SPS (Slovak Parcel Service) is the long-established domestic courier. As in Czechia most Slovak e-commerce parcels move through pickup points rather than to the door.
