description: Payment method (Slovak). Several entries are Slovak-only: CardPay and TatraPay are Tatra banka's e-commerce buttons, SporoPay belongs to Slovenská sporiteľňa and ePlatby to VÚB, Pay by square is the national QR-payment standard, Quatro is the standard instalment product, and a gastrolístok is the employer-issued meal voucher. Dobierka (cash on delivery) is still a mainstream option rather than a fringe one. Slovakia is in the eurozone, so all of these settle in euro.
