description: Court type in the Slovak judiciary. Slovakia has NO vrchny sud - the Czech high-court tier between the regional and supreme courts simply does not exist here, which is the sharpest structural difference between the two systems. It has instead a Specializovany trestny sud for corruption and organised crime, a Najvyssi spravny sud created in 2021, and since the 2023 reform mestske sudy in Bratislava in place of the old okresne sudy.
