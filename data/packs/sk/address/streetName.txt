description: Street names common across Slovakia (nature, trades, national figures, dates). A Slovak address writes the street FIRST and the number after it (Dlhá 12), and the type word — ulica — is normally left out unless it is námestie or trieda. Note the group beginning with a preposition (Na hôrke, Pri potoku, Pod lesom, Za humnami): these are ordinary Slovak street names, not fragments. The national-figure group is Slovak rather than Czech — Štúr, Hurban, Hodža, Hviezdoslav, Bernolák, Sládkovič, Kukučín — and 29. augusta is the anniversary of the Slovak National Uprising. City-specific streets belong in a country pack.
