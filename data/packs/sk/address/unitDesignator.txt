description: Slovak address unit / interior designator, abbreviated as it is written before a number. A Slovak house carries TWO numbers, exactly as in Czechia: the súpisné číslo (s. č.), a permanent registration number unique within the municipality, and the orientačné číslo (o. č.), the sequential number along the street. Both are written together as súpisné/orientačné — Dlhá 1234/12 — and only the second one counts up the street.
locale: sk
