description: Slovak thoroughfare type — goes BEFORE the street name (námestie Slobody, trieda SNP, nábrežie Dunaja). Like Czech and unlike Polish or German, Slovak normally OMITS the commonest one, ulica, when writing an address: the street line reads Dlhá 12, not ulica Dlhá 12. Korzo is the Košice pedestrian promenade and is used as a street type there.
locale: sk
