description: Dishes of Slovak cuisine (Slovak). Bryndzove halusky - potato dumplings with sheep cheese and bacon - is the national dish, and the bryndza / ostiepok / zincica group all come off a salas, the mountain sheep dairy. Lokse, strapacky and demikat are Slovak and have no Czech counterpart; the Bratislavsky rozok and the Skalica trdelnik are both protected designations.
