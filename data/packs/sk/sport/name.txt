description: Sport (Slovak). Vodny slalom is the Slovak national sport in medal terms - Michal Martikan and the Hochschorner twins made Slovakia the most decorated canoe-slalom nation per head in the world - and chodza (race walking) brought Slovakia an Olympic title. Ladovy hokej is the game the country follows. Nohejbal is played here as in Czechia, but it was invented in Bohemia, not Slovakia.
