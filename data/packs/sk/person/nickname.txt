description: Common Slovak hypocoristic / informal name (Jan -> Jano, Juraj -> Duro, Pavol -> Palo, Katarina -> Katka). Slovak short forms are their own: where Czech turns Jan into Honza and Josef into Pepa, Slovak says Jano and Jozo, and Duro, Palo, Stevo and Feri have no Czech counterpart at all. The pattern is not derivable by rule, so this is a list rather than a suffix.
