Several are specifically Slovak pastimes: jaskyniarstvo (caving - Slovakia has some seven thousand catalogued caves and an organised amateur speleological society), drotarstvo (the Kysuce wire craft, now a folk art), chalupárenie and chatárčenie (weekending at a village cottage or a mountain cabin), hubárčenie (going to the woods for wild mushrooms) and turistika, which in Slovak means marked-trail hill walking on the densest trail network in Europe rather than tourism.
