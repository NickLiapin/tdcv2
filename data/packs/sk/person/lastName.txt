description: Slovak surname in the MASCULINE form. Slovak surnames inflect for gender: nearly every one takes -ova in the feminine, including the Hungarian ones (Kovac -> Kovacova, Horvath -> Horvathova, Nagy -> Nagyova), and adjectival surnames swap -y for -a (Cierny -> Cierna). The feminine list is person/female/lastName, derived line for line by data/scripts/build-sk-surnames.mjs - draw the same row index from both for one family. Slovak differs from Czech here in a way that looks small and is not: a surname in -ek KEEPS its e (Bartek -> Bartekova, Marek -> Marekova), where Czech drops it (Marek -> Markova). About a fifth of these are Hungarian, Rusyn or Roma in origin - Horvath, Toth, Nagy, Szabo, Olah, Lakatos, Rusnak, Sabol - because that is who lives in Slovakia, not because the list is contaminated. A Slovak full name carries a single surname, so draw once. Weights are a rank-decay curve, not measured shares: Slovak surnames are spread gently (Horvath is well under 1% of the population), so no name dominates.
weighted: true
locale: sk
