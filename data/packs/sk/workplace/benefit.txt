description: Employee benefit (Slovak). Rekreacny poukaz is Slovak law and has no counterpart anywhere else: an employer with more than 49 staff must contribute up to 275 EUR a year towards an employee's holiday INSIDE Slovakia. Gastrolistky are the meal vouchers, and since 2021 an employee may take the finance prispevok na stravovanie in cash instead - the Slovak reform Czechia made differently. Slovak law guarantees four weeks of paid leave, so a fifth week is offered as a benefit.
