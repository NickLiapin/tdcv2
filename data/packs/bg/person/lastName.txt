description: Bulgarian surnames in the MASCULINE form, roughly frequency-ordered. Bulgarian surnames inflect for gender and there is no neutral form: the -ов / -ев patronymic type takes -ова / -ева (Иванов → Иванова, Георгиев → Георгиева) and the -ски adjectival type takes -ска (Раковски → Раковска). Practically every Bulgarian surname is one of those three shapes, so practically every one changes — the opposite of Serbian, where none of them do. The feminine list is therefore a real file, person/female/lastName.txt, and it is DERIVED line for line by data/scripts/build-bg-surnames.mjs rather than maintained by hand, because the two must stay aligned row for row or a generated household ends up with a husband and wife from different families. A Bulgarian also carries a MIDDLE name, and it is not a second given name: it is the father's given name plus the same -ов / -ев suffix, so Иван Петров Георгиев is Ivan, son of Petar, of the Georgiev family — and a daughter of the same father is Мария Петрова Георгиева, patronymic and surname both feminine. The weights are a fitted rank-decay curve (rank^-0.35), not measured bearer counts; the exponent puts the top name near its real share of roughly 1.5%, where the pack default would flatten it to 0.5%.
