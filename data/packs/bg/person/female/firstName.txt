description: Bulgarian female given names, frequency-ordered. Two patterns are worth knowing. First, most male names have a female counterpart formed with -а or -ка and both are in everyday use — Иван/Иванка, Тодор/Тодорка, Стоян/Стоянка, Петър/Петя — so a Bulgarian household routinely contains the same name twice in two genders. Second, Bulgarian draws female names from nature and abstractions more freely than from saints: Калина and Малина are shrubs, Иглика a primrose, Ягода a strawberry, Зорница the morning star, Невена a marigold, Ралица a wildflower, Снежана from snow, Надежда is "hope" and Свобода is "freedom". The weights are a fitted rank-decay curve (rank^-0.6), not measured bearer counts — see person/male/firstName for why the default curve was not used.
