description: Common Bulgarian hypocoristic (Иван → Ванко, Георги → Гошо, Димитър → Митко, Мария → Мими). Bulgarian shortens a name by cutting it back to one syllable and adding -о, -чо, -ко or -и, so the nickname is often shorter than the name and frequently unrecognisable from it — Христо becomes Ицо, Александър becomes Сашо.
locale: bg
