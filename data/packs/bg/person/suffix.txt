description: Name suffix (Bulgarian). Bulgarian academic titles PRECEDE the name — проф. д-р Иван Петров — so what trails a name is only a doctoral-science abbreviation or a regnal numeral. And the numeral is the point: Bulgarian has no Jr / Sr convention at all, so I, II, III number MONARCHS (Борис III, Симеон II) and never a son who shares his father's name. A Bulgarian son who shares his father's name is distinguished by the patronymic middle name instead, which every Bulgarian already carries.
