description: Bulgarian male given names, frequency-ordered. Note what the head of the list is made of: Bulgarian first names are heavily saint-derived (Георги, Иван, Димитър, Никола, Петър) because a child is traditionally named after a grandparent, which recycles the same pool every second generation and keeps it small. Alongside them sits a second, entirely native layer built out of ordinary words — Светослав "holy glory", Владимир "rules the world", Здравко from "health", Първан "first-born", Калоян from the medieval tsar — and a Bulgar layer older than the Slavic one: Аспарух, Кубрат, Крум, Тервел, Пресиян. The weights are a FITTED rank-decay curve (rank^-0.6), not measured bearer counts: the exponent was chosen so the top name lands near its real share of about 5%, because the pack's default rank^-0.1 curve would put it near 0.5% and make Bulgarian given names look evenly spread, which they are emphatically not. This pack holds names of the Bulgarian LANGUAGE; the Turkish and Roma given names carried by Bulgarian citizens are not Bulgarian-language names and are deliberately not mixed in here.
