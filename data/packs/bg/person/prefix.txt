description: Bulgarian honorific or title, in the abbreviated form written before a name. Two have no European counterpart: бай is a warm familiar address to an older man (бай Иван) with no feminine equivalent, and хаджи once marked someone who had made the pilgrimage — it fossilised into a whole family of surnames (Хаджиев, Хаджидимитров) and is still occasionally used as a title.
