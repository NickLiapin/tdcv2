description: Property type (Bulgarian). The room count works differently from the English one and gets mistranslated constantly: Bulgarian counts LIVING ROOMS, the sitting room included and the kitchen excluded, so двустаен is a one-bedroom flat and тристаен a two-bedroom one. Гарсониера is a flat with a kitchen niche rather than a separate kitchen.
locale: bg
