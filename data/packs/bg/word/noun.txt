description: Common noun (Bulgarian). This is the BASE form, and unlike every other Slavic language that is the only form there is: Bulgarian lost the noun case system entirely, so град is град in every position in a sentence and word order plus prepositions do the work that Russian's six cases do. What Bulgarian adds instead is a definite article stuck on the END of the word — град, градът, града.
