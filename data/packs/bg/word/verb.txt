description: Common verb (Bulgarian), cited in the FIRST PERSON SINGULAR PRESENT — чета, "I read". This is not a stylistic choice: Bulgarian has NO INFINITIVE. Alone among the Slavic languages it lost the form outright (a Balkan Sprachbund trait it shares with Greek, Romanian and Albanian) and replaced it with да + present, so "to read" is да чета. A Bulgarian dictionary heads its verb entries with the 1sg present, and so does this file. Every other Slavic pack in this repo lists infinitives; comparing them line to line will look wrong, and is not.
