description: Mark on the Bulgarian school and university scale. Bulgaria grades from 2 to 6 with SIX as the top mark and 2 as failing — the inverse of the German and Polish scales, where 1 or 2 is best. Marks carry a name as well as a number, and both are written (Отличен 6.00); intermediate values to two decimals are normal, so a 5.50 is a real mark, not a rounding artefact.
locale: bg
