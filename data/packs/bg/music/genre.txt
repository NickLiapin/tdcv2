description: Music genre (Bulgarian), including the domestic ones. Народна музика is traditional village music; поп-фолк, universally called чалга, is its commercial descendant crossed with Balkan and Turkish pop and is the most-listened-to genre in the country. Both are listed because they are not the same thing and Bulgarians never confuse them.
