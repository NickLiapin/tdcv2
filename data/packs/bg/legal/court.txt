Bulgaria is the only country where Bulgarian is the official language, so this list names a real court system rather than a generic set of court words — compare the Spanish and Portuguese packs, which have to stay country-neutral. Bulgaria splits its apex court in two: Върховен касационен съд for civil and criminal matters, Върховен административен съд for administrative ones, and neither outranks the other.
