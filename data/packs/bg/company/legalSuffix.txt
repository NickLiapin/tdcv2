description: Company legal form used in Bulgaria, written AFTER the name (Софарма АД, Каолин ЕООД). ЕООД — a limited company with a single owner — is by far the commonest form for a small Bulgarian business and has no direct English equivalent; ЕТ is a sole trader, whose registered name legally includes the owner's own name. Bulgaria is the only country where Bulgarian is the official language, so these are the only forms the language needs.
