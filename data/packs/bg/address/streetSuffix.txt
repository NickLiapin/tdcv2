description: Bulgarian thoroughfare type — it goes BEFORE the street name (ул. Христо Ботев, бул. Витоша, пл. Свобода), the opposite of the English order. Both the abbreviation and the spelled-out word are in use; the abbreviated form is what appears on an envelope. ж.к. (жилищен комплекс) is a Bulgarian peculiarity: in a panel-block estate the address has no street at all and reads ж.к. + block + entrance + floor + flat.
locale: bg
