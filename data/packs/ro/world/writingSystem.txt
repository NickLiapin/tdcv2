description: Writing system (Romanian). Romanian itself has used two: it was written in a Romanian Cyrillic alphabet until the 1860s, and the same language was written in a Soviet-designed Cyrillic orthography in the Moldavian SSR until 1989 — Transnistria still prints it that way. The modern Latin orthography needs five letters English does not have: a-breve and i-circumflex/a-circumflex, plus s and t with a COMMA below, not a cedilla.
