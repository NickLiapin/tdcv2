description: Common Romanian hypocoristic — the everyday short form of a given name. Romanian builds them with -el, -ica, -uta, -ita and by clipping (Ion -> Nelu / Ionel / Nicu, Gheorghe -> Gigi / Ghita, Constantin -> Costel / Titi, Dumitru -> Mitica, Elena -> Lenuta, Maria -> Marioara). Several here are now given in their own right on birth certificates.
locale: ro
