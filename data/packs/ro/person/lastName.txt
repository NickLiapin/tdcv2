description: Romanian surnames, roughly frequency-ordered. ONE list, for both sexes: Romanian surnames do NOT inflect for gender — Popescu is Popescu whether the bearer is Ion or Maria — so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack, unlike pl, ru or cs. That absence is the design, not an oversight. The list mixes what the language mixes: Latin-formed patronymics in -escu (Popescu, Ionescu) beside Slavic-origin names that are just as Romanian (Rusu, Novac, Popovici, Cazacu, Leahu), occupational names (Ciobanu the shepherd, Croitoru the tailor, Fieraru the smith), regional ones (Ardeleanu, Moldovan, Ungureanu, Olteanu) and, at the tail, the Hungarian and German surnames borne in Transylvania by hundreds of thousands of Romanian citizens. The counts are the pack's default rank-decay curve, NOT measured counts: Popa, the commonest Romanian surname, is only about one percent of the population, so no sharp concentration is being flattened here.
locale: ro
