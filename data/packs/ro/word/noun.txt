description: Common noun (Romanian) in the INDEFINITE NOMINATIVE SINGULAR, with no article on it — the dictionary form. Two things a config cannot guess. First, Romanian marks the definite article as a SUFFIX, not a separate word: om -> omul, casa -> casa, prieten -> prietenul, floare -> floarea, so writing an article in FRONT of any of these produces something no Romanian would write. Second, the plural is not derivable from the singular: Romanian forms it with six competing endings (-i, -e, -uri, -ele, -le, and no change at all) chosen by gender and by history, and some are suppletive — om -> oameni, cap -> capete, while ochi is the same in both numbers. The list also shows the shape of the language: Latin bones (apa, casa, om, floare) carrying a thick Slavic layer of everyday words (prieten, ceas, nevasta, munca, poveste), plus dor, which Romanian claims has no equivalent anywhere.
