description: Leading element of a Romanian place name (Baia Mare, Targu Jiu, Valea Lupului). Most of these already carry the definite article fused on and clipped in the toponym — Dealu is Dealul, Vadu is Vadul, Podu is Podul — which is why they never take an article in front. Combine with a real toponym; the element alone does not name a real place.
locale: ro
