description: The 149 Bhutanese postcodes that appear in the published register, running 11001 to 46102. BHUTAN DOES HAVE A POSTCODE SYSTEM, and this file exists because that is worth stating plainly against the common belief that it does not — the Universal Postal Union publishes Bhutan's addressing specification, Bhutan Post operates the codes, and international carriers route on them. Five digits, no separator, and the digits are a hierarchy rather than a serial: the 1st is the postal region (dzongdey) — 1 western, 2 southern, 3 central, 4 eastern; the 2nd is the dzongkhag within that region; the 3rd is the dungkhag (sub-district) or 0 where the dzongkhag has none; the last two are the delivery area. So 11001 is Thimphu GPO and 21101 is a Phuentsholing dungkhag code, and the 3rd digit is why 15102 (Sombaykha) and 11103 (Lingzhi) look like outliers and are not. THE POSTCODE'S DISTRICT NUMBERING IS NOT THE DZONGKHAG CODE. There are twenty dzongkhags but only four regions and at most nine dzongkhag slots inside each, so the postcode's second digit and the CID's dzongkhag code in bhutan.geo.dzongkhagCode are unrelated numbering systems over the same districts: Thimphu is postal 11 and dzongkhag code 14, Zhemgang is postal 36 and dzongkhag code 20. THE CODE IS ALSO NOT THE DELIVERY MECHANISM most of the time. The UPU's own specimens show a Bhutanese address carrying a building or house number, a Thimphu zone, a village and a gewog, with the postcode appended after the locality name — and outside the thromdes there are no street names or house numbers to carry, so the postcode narrows delivery to a post office and a person's name does the rest. Written after the locality: "Thimphu 11001". One caution about the source: the Parcelforce extract of the register places 21006 Chapchha and 32005 Barshong in Thimphu, when Chapcha is a Chhukha gewog and Barshong a Tsirang one — the codes themselves are right and the district column of that file is not, which is why only the codes are carried here. Sources: Universal Postal Union, Bhutan addressing specification (postcode structure); the Bhutan Post register as republished by Parcelforce Worldwide, October 2019 (the codes).
