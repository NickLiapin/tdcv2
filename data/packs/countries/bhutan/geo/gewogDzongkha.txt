description: The same 205 gewogs as bhutan.geo.gewog in Dzongkha script, INDEX-ALIGNED LINE FOR LINE with it. The Election Commission of Bhutan's delimitation notifications print both forms side by side, which is why this file exists and why it is trustworthy: it is not a transliteration produced here, it is the pairing the Commission published. Read alongside bhutan.geo.gewog it is also the clearest demonstration in this pack of why Roman Dzongkha cannot be reversed — the UNGEGN report says so outright, and the reason is visible in the data: ཁ་སྨད་ is Khamaed and ཁ་སྟོད་ is Khatoe, ནུ་སྦིས་ is Nubi and སྦིས་གདུང་ is Bidung, and the prefixed and superscript consonants that distinguish them are simply not romanised. Two spellings that look the same in Latin are different words in Dzongkha, and a Latin gewog name is therefore not a safe join key between two Bhutanese datasets — the Dzongkha string or the dzongkhag-plus-code pair is. Every entry ends in U+0F0B TSHEG, the syllable dot, exactly as printed; Zhemgang's eight gewogs were published with a terminal ། U+0F0D SHAD instead and that sentence punctuation has been stripped, and ONE entry, Shenga Bjemi in Punakha, reached this file as ཤེལ་རྔ་_སྦྱེ་མི་ with a stray ASCII underscore after the tsheg and has had that underscore deleted — those are the only two alterations made to the published strings.
