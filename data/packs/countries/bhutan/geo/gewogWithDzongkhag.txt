description: The same 205 gewogs as bhutan.geo.gewog, each followed by its dzongkhag — "Chhoekhor, Bumthang" — because a bare gewog name does not identify a place in Bhutan. Two gewogs are called Norbugang (Pemagatshel and Samtse) and two are called Gakiling (Haa and Sarpang), so a config that draws a gewog and a dzongkhag independently will produce impossible addresses roughly nineteen times in twenty; this file is the pair drawn together. Index-aligned with bhutan.geo.gewog and bhutan.geo.gewogDzongkha.
