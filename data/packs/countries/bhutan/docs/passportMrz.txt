address: bhutan.docs.passportMrz
description: The second line of the machine-readable zone of a Bhutanese passport — 44 characters of ICAO TD3, WITH ALL FIVE CHECK DIGITS COMPUTED FOR REAL. This is the only verifiable arithmetic in the Bhutan pack, and the reason deserves to be stated once, here: Bhutan publishes no checksum for the Citizenship Identity Card (tested and disproved against 11 380 real numbers — see bhutan.docs.cid), none for the taxpayer number, none for the vehicle registration and none for the postcode, and Bhutan is not in the IBAN registry, so there is no modulus-97 to compute either. What IS published, and applies to every Bhutanese passport, is ICAO Doc 9303: give each character its value — 0-9 for digits, 10 to 35 for A to Z, 0 for the filler '<' — multiply by the repeating weights 7, 3, 1 from the left, sum, take the remainder modulo 10. The implementation here was verified against the specimen printed in Doc 9303 itself: document number L898902C3 check 6, date of birth 740812 check 2, expiry 120415 check 9, optional-data check 1, composite check 0 — and against that specimen with one digit altered, which the check rejects. THE LAYOUT, position by position: 1-9 the document number left-aligned and padded with '<'; 10 its check digit; 11-13 the nationality, BTN — Bhutan's ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code, not BT and not BHU; 14-19 the date of birth as YYMMDD; 20 its check; 21 the sex, M, F or filler; 22-27 the expiry as YYMMDD; 28 its check; 29-42 optional data, left blank; 43 its check; 44 the composite over positions 1-10, 14-20 and 22-43 together. NOTE THE TWO-DIGIT YEAR: an MRZ carries no century, so a birth year of 30 is 1930 on an old holder's book and 2030 on a child's, and the reader disambiguates from context. Not modelled: line 1, which carries the name in Latin letters. Bhutanese names transliterate inconsistently and are structurally awkward for the field — most Bhutanese have two given names and NO family name at all, so the surname field of a Bhutanese MRZ is frequently a repetition, a father's name or the filler, and line 1 does not round-trip back to a person.
generator: tdc
