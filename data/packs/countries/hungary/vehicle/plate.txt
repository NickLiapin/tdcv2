description: Hungarian number plate. BOTH formats are shipped because both are on the road - the pre-2022 AAA-123 (drawn 70%) and the format introduced in July 2022, AA BB-123 (drawn 30%, roughly its share of the fleet by 2026). Neither format encodes a region - unlike Poland or Germany, the letters in a Hungarian plate carry no geography, they are simply issued in sequence. I, O and Q are left out of the letter set, as they are on the real plates, to keep them apart from 1 and 0.
generator: tdc
locale: hu
