description: Adószám — the 11-digit tax number of a Hungarian business, written 12345678-1-23. Three parts - an 8-digit core whose last digit is a REAL check digit (weights 9,7,3,1,9,7,3 over the first seven; check = (10 - sum mod 10) mod 10), then a 1-digit VAT status code, then a 2-digit tax-office code. The VAT digit is drawn 2 six times out of ten because a plain domestic VAT payer is by far the common case. The office codes are the real ones - 02 through 20 are the 19 counties in alphabetical order, 41-44 are the Budapest directorates and 51 is the large-taxpayer directorate. The first 8 digits are the same number as hungary.tax.vat carries after the HU prefix. Check digit verified against three published company numbers - MOL 10625790, OTP Bank 10537914, Richter Gedeon 10484878.
