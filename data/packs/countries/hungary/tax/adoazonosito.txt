description: Adóazonosító jel — the 10-digit tax identifier NAV issues to a private person. Every digit means something - the 1st is the constant 8 (a natural person, not a company), digits 2-6 are the number of days between 1867-01-01 and the person's birth date, digits 7-9 are a same-day serial, and the 10th is the check digit. REAL ARITHMETIC - each of the first nine digits is multiplied by its position (1st by 1 ... 9th by 9), the products are summed, and the check digit is that sum modulo 11; a remainder of 10 is never issued and is rejected here. The day range 23010..53325 is exactly birth dates 1930-01-01 through 2012-12-31, so every number generated decodes to a real date. Rule and the 1867 epoch as published in the 2023 Digitális kultúra school-leaving exam (2311, 2023-05-22).
