description: Hungarian telephone number in E.164 form. Three real branches of the +36 plan - mobile (20 Yettel, 30 Telekom, 31 and 50 Digi, 70 One/Vodafone) with a 7-digit subscriber number, Budapest which is the single-digit area code 1 with 7 digits, and every other landline which is a 2-digit area code with 6 digits.
