description: Bosnian vehicle plate, the series in force since 28 September 2009 — one letter, two digits, a dash, one letter, a dash, three digits (A12-B-345), black on white with a blue band at the left carrying BIH. THIS IS THE MOST DELIBERATELY DESIGNED OBJECT IN THE COUNTRY and every part of it is a decision. First, it carries NO PLACE: no town code, no canton, no entity, no republic, nothing that says where the car was registered, which is the opposite of every neighbour — Croatia stamps ZG or ST, Serbia BG or NS, Slovenia LJ or MB. Bosnian plates before 1998 did carry the town, and a car registered in one entity driving through the other was a target for it; removing the code was the point, and it was done under international administration precisely so that a vehicle would be unreadable as belonging to anybody. Second, it carries NO HERALDRY: no coat of arms, no flag, no entity emblem, in a country whose two entities have separate arms. Third, and this is the piece that solves the digraphia problem in one move, THE ONLY LETTERS ALLOWED ARE THE SEVEN THAT LOOK THE SAME IN LATIN AND CYRILLIC — A, E, J, K, M, O, T. There is no B, no S, no P, no C, because each of those is either absent from one script or reads as a different sound in the other, and no Š, Č, Ć, Ž or Đ. So a Bosnian plate is a string that a Sarajevo reader and a Banja Luka reader see identically and that neither has to translate; the country did not choose a script for its plates, it chose the intersection of both. Seven characters, two letters and five digits, giving 7 x 100 x 7 x 1000 = 4,900,000 combinations, which is why the earlier 1998 series (three digits, a letter, three digits, on the same seven-letter rule) was replaced rather than extended. Compare bosnia_and_herzegovina.geo.postalCode, which reached neutrality by inheriting a plan nobody re-cut, and bosnia_and_herzegovina.phone, which never reached it at all. Plates are issued by IDDEEA, the state identity-documents agency, for the whole country. Diplomatic, military, temporary and personalised series are out of scope.
