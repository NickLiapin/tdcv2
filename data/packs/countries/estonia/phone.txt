address: estonia.phone
description: An Estonian mobile number in E.164 form — +372 and then a subscriber number beginning with 5. Estonia has NO area codes at all: the plan went flat and closed on 1 February 2003, so there is nothing between the +372 and the subscriber number, no trunk prefix to add and no leading zero to strip, and a "city code" column has nothing to put in it. The plan is also MIXED-LENGTH, which is the second thing foreign schemas get wrong: mobile numbers are seven OR eight digits and both are current, fixed lines are seven, so a validator that pins Estonia to one length rejects real numbers. The 5-series generated here is the everyday mobile range; 81 through 87 are mobile too and eight digits, and are listed in estonia.phoneMobilePrefix rather than drawn here, because a regex alternation would give them half the draws when they hold a small minority of the numbers. For the same reason the seven- and eight-digit forms come out roughly evenly here, which is not the live proportion — eight digits has been the default for new numbers for years and seven-digit mobiles are a shrinking legacy. Fixed lines are estonia.phoneLandline and the spaced national form is estonia.phoneNational. This generator emits the unspaced E.164 form so it survives a CSV round-trip.
generator: tdc
locale: et
