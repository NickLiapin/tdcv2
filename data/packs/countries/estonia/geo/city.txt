address: estonia.geo.city
description: All 47 Estonian towns (linnad), weighted by the population of the town as a SETTLEMENT. The weights matter more here than in most countries because Estonia is extraordinarily top-heavy: Tallinn alone holds roughly a third of the population and Tallinn plus Tartu more than two fifths, so an unweighted draw over 47 names puts about 96% of its rows in places that between them hold a quarter of the country. Note that a town's population as a settlement is NOT its population as a municipality, and the two differ by tens of thousands for the towns that absorbed countryside in the 2017 reform — Pärnu the town has about 41 500 people, Pärnu linn the local-government unit about 52 000, because the unit swallowed Audru, Paikuse and Tõstamaa and kept the town's name. Only fifteen of these towns are still municipalities in their own right; the rest were folded into a rural municipality and kept the STATUS of a town without keeping the powers, which is why estonia.geo.city and estonia.geo.municipality are different lists that share many names and must not be joined on the name. Estonia has no separate "city" tier above these: linn is the only word, and Loksa with 2 568 residents holds it on the same terms as Tallinn.
