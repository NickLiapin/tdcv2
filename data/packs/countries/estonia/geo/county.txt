description: The 15 counties (maakonnad) of Estonia — the first-level division, in the official Estonian alphabetical order (õ, ä, ö, ü sort after z, so Põlva precedes Pärnu). The 2017 reform redrew eleven of the county boundaries but abolished none of the counties; what it did abolish was the county government, so a maakond is now a statistical and address unit with no administration of its own.
