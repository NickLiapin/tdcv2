description: The 15 counties (maakonnad) of Estonia — the first-level division — in the official Estonian alphabetical order, in which õ, ä, ö and ü sort after z, so Põlva precedes Pärnu and Jõgeva precedes Järva. That collation is the thing to get right about this list: sort it with a plain byte or English comparator and four of the fifteen move. The 2017 reform redrew eleven of the county boundaries but abolished none of the counties; what it abolished was the county GOVERNMENT (maavalitsus), wound up at the end of 2017 with its duties split between ministries and municipalities, so a maakond today is a statistical and postal unit with no administration, no budget and no elected body of its own. This is why Estonia has no middle tier: the state sits directly on 78 municipalities, and a dataset expecting a regional authority to own anything will find nobody at this level. The word maakond is part of the name in formal use ("Harju maakond"), though the short forms Harjumaa, Ida-Virumaa and so on are what people say and what appears in most addresses. Codes in estonia.geo.countyCode line up with this list row for row.
