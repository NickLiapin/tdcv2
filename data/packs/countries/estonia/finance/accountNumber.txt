---
address: estonia.finance.accountNumber
description: An Estonian domestic bank account number (kontonumber) — digits only, minimum 4 and maximum 14 of them, each bank choosing its own length inside that range. The first two digits are the bank identifier and the last is a check digit computed by the Estonian Banking Association's 7-3-1 method: weights 7, 3, 1 repeating from the RIGHT over every digit before the check, summed, and the check digit is whatever brings that sum up to the next multiple of ten. Twelve digits is generated here, which is Swedbank's and LHV's real length. The domestic number is NOT a shortened IBAN and the IBAN is not a decorated account number: the IBAN carries the bank identifier a second time in positions 5-6 and pads this number out to fourteen digits with leading zeros, so account 221013264447 becomes EE522200221013264447 and the "00" in the middle is padding, not part of the account. Since the SEPA migration in 2014 the domestic form is no longer accepted for transfers and Estonians quote the IBAN, but it is still what older records, invoices and accounting systems hold, which is why converting one to the other is a real operation and not a reformat. See estonia.finance.iban for the IBAN and estonia.finance.referenceNumber for the payment reference, which carries the same 7-3-1 check.
generator: tdc
locale: et
---

<sequence name="serial"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{9}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="account"><compute>
  <let name="body"><concat><field name="bank"/><field name="serial"/></concat></let>
  <!-- 11 digits before the check, so 7,3,1 from the right reads
       3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7 from the left. -->
  <let name="sum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="body"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><mod><subtract><int v="10"/><mod><use name="sum"/><int v="10"/></mod></subtract><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <result><concat><use name="body"/><use name="check"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
