---
address: estonia.finance.iban
description: Estonian IBAN — 20 characters: EE, two ISO 7064 check digits, a two-digit bank identifier and a 14-digit field holding the domestic account number left-padded with zeros. TWO checksums are computed here, not one. The domestic account number ends in its OWN check digit under the Estonian Banking Association's 7-3-1 method (weights 7, 3, 1 repeating from the RIGHT over the digits before it, the sum taken up to the next multiple of ten), and the ISO 7064 MOD-97 pair is then computed on top of the finished BBAN. The structural fact that most generators miss is that the bank identifier appears TWICE: once in IBAN positions 5-6, and again as the first two digits of the domestic account number itself, because the Banking Association's domestic standard defines the account number as bank code plus account identifier plus check digit. That is why every real Estonian IBAN reads EE kk bb 00…bb…, with the same two digits before and after the padding zeros — EE52 22 00 22 1013264447 at Swedbank, EE95 77 00 77 1001523585 at LHV. A generator that fills the 14-digit field with free digits produces an IBAN that passes MOD-97 and is still visibly not Estonian. Bank identifiers are the real ones issued through Finantsinspektsioon and published by the Estonian Banking Association (see estonia.finance.bankCode); the central bank's own code 16 and the payment institutions are excluded from the draw because customer accounts are not opened there. The account body is structural, not an issued account.
generator: tdc
locale: et

<sequence name="bank"><gen type="text" value="10,12,17,22,42,51,75,77,83,96"/></sequence>
<sequence name="serial"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{9}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="iban"><compute>
  <!-- The 14-digit domestic field minus its check digit: 13 digits, and the
       domestic account number itself opens with the bank identifier. -->
  <let name="body"><concat><str v="00"/><field name="bank"/><field name="serial"/></concat></let>
  <!-- 7-3-1: weights repeat from the RIGHT, which over 13 digits reads
       7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7 from the left. Leading zeros add nothing. -->
  <let name="nsum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="body"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="ncheck"><mod><subtract><int v="10"/><mod><use name="nsum"/><int v="10"/></mod></subtract><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <let name="bban"><concat><field name="bank"/><use name="body"/><use name="ncheck"/></concat></let>
  <!-- ISO 7064 MOD-97: move EE00 to the back, letters to numbers, fold mod 97. -->
  <let name="rearranged"><concat><use name="bban"/><str v="EE"/><str v="00"/></concat></let>
  <let name="digits"><join><each>
    <over><use name="rearranged"/></over>
    <do><encode as="base36"><current/></encode></do>
  </each></join></let>
  <let name="rem"><reduce>
    <over><use name="digits"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><mod><add><multiply><acc/><int v="10"/></multiply><current/></add><int v="97"/></mod></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><pad width="2" fill="0"><subtract><int v="98"/><use name="rem"/></subtract></pad></let>
  <result><concat><str v="EE"/><use name="check"/><use name="bban"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{iban}}</data>
