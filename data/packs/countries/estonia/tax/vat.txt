address: estonia.tax.vat
description: The Estonian VAT number (KMKR-number, käibemaksukohustuslasena registreerimise number) — EE and nine digits. It is NOT the registry code with a prefix, and that is the mistake worth naming: the registrikood (estonia.docs.registrikood) exists from the day a company is founded and the KMKR number only from the day it registers for VAT, they are different lengths, they are issued by different bodies — the register by the courts, the VAT number by Maksu- ja Tolliamet — and a company below the registration threshold has the first and not the second. It does carry a check digit, though neither Wikipedia's VAT page nor the usual VAT references state the algorithm: weights 3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7 over the first eight digits, and the ninth digit is whatever brings the weighted sum up to the next multiple of ten. That is the same 7-3-1 family as the Estonian bank account and reference number, one of the few places where a country reuses one checksum across banking and tax. Verified against published numbers: EE100207415 is accepted (weighted sum 35, next ten 40, check 5) and rejected with one digit changed, and the worked example 723456782 (sum 168, next ten 170, check 2) agrees. Issued numbers run upward from 100000000, so the generated ones start 100 or 101.
generator: tdc
locale: et
