description: North Korean universities. Kim Il Sung University (1946) is the country's first and its graduates fill the senior administration; Kim Chaek University of Technology is the engineering counterpart. Pyongyang University of Science and Technology is the outlier and worth knowing about: founded in 2010 and funded by evangelical Korean-American donors, it taught in English and was the only DPRK institution with a substantial foreign faculty until the 2020 border closure sent them home.
