description: Liberian universities. The University of Liberia was chartered in 1862 and is one of the oldest in Africa; Cuttington, founded 1889 by the Episcopal Church, is the oldest private one in the region. Both closed during the civil wars and reopened afterwards, so a Liberian degree dated between 1990 and 2003 needs looking at — many were awarded in exile or after long interruptions.
