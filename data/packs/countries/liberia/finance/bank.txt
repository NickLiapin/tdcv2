description: Liberian banks. The Central Bank of Liberia issues the Liberian dollar, but the country runs on two currencies at once: the US dollar is legal tender alongside it and has been since the founding — Liberia used the US dollar as its only currency from 1943 to 1982 — so prices, wages and bank balances all exist in both, and any Liberian financial record has to say which. The Nigerian banks hold much of the commercial sector.
