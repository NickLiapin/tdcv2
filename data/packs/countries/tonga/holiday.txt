description: Tongan public holidays. The calendar is a monarchy's: the birthdays and coronation of the reigning king and of the crown prince are all public holidays, and Constitution Day marks the 1875 constitution that made Tonga a constitutional monarchy. Sunday observance is written into that constitution — commerce is legally prohibited on Sundays, which is unusual enough to affect any dataset of Tongan trading hours.
