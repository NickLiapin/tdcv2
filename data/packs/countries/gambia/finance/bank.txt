description: Gambian banks. The Central Bank of The Gambia issues the dalasi. Trust Bank and the Nigerian entrants — GTBank, Access, Zenith — make up most of the commercial sector, and the Islamic bank AGIB serves a country that is over ninety percent Muslim. Remittances from Gambians abroad are a larger share of national income than in almost any other country, so the money-transfer operators matter here as much as the banks.
