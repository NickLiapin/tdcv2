description: Gambian public holidays. Independence from Britain is 18 February 1965. Revolution Day on 22 July marks the 1994 coup that brought Yahya Jammeh to power and was kept as a holiday through his twenty-two years; it survived his departure in 2017. The Islamic calendar supplies most of the rest — Koriteh and Tobaski are the local names for Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, the Wolof and Mandinka forms rather than the Arabic ones, and they are what a Gambian document will say.
