description: The 150 largest Norwegian towns and urban settlements (tettsteder), weighted by their population at 1 January 2025. A tettsted is Statistics Norway's definition of a built-up area — at least 200 residents and no more than 50 metres between buildings — and it is drawn on the ground, not on the administrative map, so it crosses municipal borders freely: the Oslo tettsted counted here (1 110 887) is half again the population of Oslo municipality (724 290), because it runs on into Bærum, Lørenskog and Oppegård without a break in the houses. Three of the ten largest entries carry a SLASH, and that is the register speaking, not a typo: Stavanger and Sandnes, Fredrikstad and Sarpsborg, Porsgrunn and Skien have grown into single continuous settlements that SSB counts and names as one. There is no published figure that splits them, so splitting them here would mean inventing one. Nine of the 150 have such a name. If a config needs a plain single place name for an address line, draw norway.geo.municipality instead. Source: SSB table 04859, Bosatte, 1 January 2025.
