description: All 357 municipalities (kommuner) of Norway, weighted by population at 1 January 2025. The count is the fact to hold on to: 422 municipalities became 356 in the 2020 reform, and exactly ONE of those mergers has since been undone — Haram left Ålesund again on 1 January 2024, taking the total to 357. So a list of 356 is one year out of date and a list of 422 is pre-2020 (426 takes you back before 2018). The municipality is the unit a Norwegian actually lives in and the unit almost every public register keys on; it is NOT the same thing as a town, which is why Bærum (132k) and Asker (100k) sit high in this list without being towns at all, and why the town of Jessheim appears here as Ullensaker. Twenty-six municipalities carry official parallel Sámi or Kven names in the register (Karasjok is "Kárášjohka – Karasjok"); the Norwegian form is used here. Sources: Statistics Norway KLASS classification 131 valid 1 January 2024 for the names, SSB table 06913 for the populations at 1 January 2025.
