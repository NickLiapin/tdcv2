address: norway.vehicle.plateLetters
description: The 398 two-letter combinations that an ordinary Norwegian five-digit registration plate can carry. Norway drops seven letters from the alphabet — I, M, O and Q because they read as 1, 0 or each other, and Æ, Ø and Å because a plate has to be legible abroad — and holds back G and W for special series (G for gas vehicles and test plates, W for four-digit plates and test plates), leaving the twenty letters A B C D E F H J K L N P R S T U V X Y Z. From the 400 pairs those twenty make, two are struck out for what they spell rather than how they look: NS, the wartime collaborationist party Nasjonal Samling, and SS on five-digit plates. Doubled pairs (AA, BB, ZZ) are ordinary and issued. Nothing in the pair encodes geography — the county-letter system ended in 1971 and plates come from one national pool — so any lookup from letter to fylke is reading a map that was withdrawn half a century ago.
