description: Every bank that holds a bank identifier in the Norwegian payment system, from the "Norwegian BIC Table" that Bits AS — the banks' own infrastructure company — publishes and keeps current. Two things about this list are Norwegian rather than generic. First, the savings banks: sixty-nine of the ninety-nine names here carry the word Sparebank, they are self-owned foundations with no shareholders, and a country of 5.6 million supports that many of them, so a plausible-looking Norwegian bank list made of five big names is wrong about the shape of the market. Second, the list moves: the 2025 mergers are already in it, so Sparebanken Vest and Sparebanken Sør appear as "Sparebanken Norge" and SpareBank 1 SR-Bank as "SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge ASA", and Sbanken — a name in every older Norwegian test fixture — is gone, absorbed into DNB. The foreign banks that appear (Nordea Bank Abp NUF, Svenska Handelsbanken AB NUF, Danske Bank) are branches, which is what NUF means and why they are Norwegian for payment purposes. Names are as Bits registers them. Source: Bits AS, Norwegian BIC Table.
