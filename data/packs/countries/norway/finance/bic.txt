address: norway.finance.bic
description: The 8-character BIC (SWIFT) codes of banks operating in Norway, as registered by Bits AS. All end in NO plus a two-character location code; DNBANOKK is DNB, NDEANOKK Nordea, SPRONO22 and the SP-prefixed ones the savings banks. An 8-character BIC addresses the bank's head office; payment systems that want 11 characters pad it with XXX, which is why the University of Bergen publishes its BIC as DNBANOKKXXX while the register holds DNBANOKK. Source: Bits AS, Norwegian BIC Table.
