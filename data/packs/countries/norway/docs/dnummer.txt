address: norway.docs.dnummer
description: The Norwegian D-number — the identity number given to people who are not registered residents but still have business with the Norwegian state: seasonal workers, offshore crews, foreign directors of Norwegian companies, students, anyone who needs a bank account or a tax card without living here. It is a fødselsnummer in every respect but one: THE DAY IS OFFSET BY 40. Someone born on 1 January 1980 gets 410180, someone born on the 31st gets 710180, so the first two digits of a D-number are always in 41–71 and never in 01–31. That single rule is what a validator must know, because a D-number passes every fødselsnummer check — same individnummer ranges, same two modulus-11 check digits over the same weight vectors — and a system that only accepts day 01–31 rejects several hundred thousand people who legitimately have one. It also means a naive date parser reads a D-number's birth date as a month-51 nonsense date rather than failing loudly. The offset is the ONLY difference; do not confuse it with the H-number, a temporary hospital number that offsets the MONTH by 40 instead, or with the FH-number, which encodes no date at all. Check digits are computed over the offset day, not the real one, so a D-number's check digits do not match the corresponding fødselsnummer. Draws that produce a check digit of 10 are unissuable and are redrawn.
generator: tdc
locale: nb
