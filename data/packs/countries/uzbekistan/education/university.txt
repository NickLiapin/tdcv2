description: 177 higher education institutions of Uzbekistan, in Uzbek. The list is worth reading for its shape rather than its length. FILIAL IS THE UNIT: a large share of these entries are named "X institutining Y filiali" — a branch of a Tashkent institute in a provincial city — because Uzbekistan expanded higher education after 2017 by opening branches of existing institutes rather than founding new universities, and a branch has its own admissions, its own building and its own line in the register. So an Uzbek education field of "Toshkent axborot texnologiyalari universiteti" is genuinely ambiguous between the Tashkent campus and its branches in Nukus, Samarqand, Urganch and Fargʻona. The second block is FOREIGN BRANCH CAMPUSES, and there are more than thirty of them, which is unusual anywhere: Westminster, Turin Polytechnic, Inha, Webster, Amity, MISiS, Lomonosov Moscow State, Gubkin, MGIMO and Pisa all run degree-granting campuses in Uzbekistan. Third is the military and security block — the Armed Forces Academy, the Interior Ministry Academy, the State Security Service Academy, the tank command school at Chirchiq — which are higher education institutions in Uzbek law and sit in the same register as the universities. The vocabulary distinguishes universitet, institut, akademiya and oliy harbiy bilim yurti and the words are not interchangeable; an institut is not a lower rank of university but a single-subject one. Names carry the correct U+02BB in oʻ and gʻ and U+02BC in taʼlim; the Russian-era transliterations (Tashkent, Fergana, Bukhara) that Western records use are different strings from these.
