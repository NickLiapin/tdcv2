description: All 35 commercial banks of Uzbekistan, named exactly as the Central Bank registers them in its own directory of head offices — which is why the legal form is welded on and the brand is in quotation marks. The Uzbek legal forms are the thing to read: AJ (aksiyadorlik jamiyati) is a joint-stock company, ATB (aksiyadorlik tijorat banki) a joint-stock commercial bank, XATB a private joint-stock commercial bank, AITB a joint-stock innovation commercial bank. The shape of this market is unusual and a plausible-looking list of five big names would be wrong about it in both directions. The state still owns the top of it — the National Bank for Foreign Economic Activity, Oʻzsanoatqurilishbank, Asakabank, Agrobank, Xalq banki, Mikrokreditbank, Turonbank, Aloqabank and the Business Development Bank between them held the large majority of banking assets through the 2020s, an unusually state-heavy sector for a country that has been privatising hard — and the bottom of it is a burst of new digital banks that did not exist five years ago: TBC (2020), Anorbank, Uzum Bank, AVO Bank, Apex Bank, Hayot Bank, Open Bank, Poytaxt bank. The foreign entrants are Korean (KDB), Turkish (Ziraat), Iranian (SODEROT), Georgian (TBC) and Kazakh (Tenge, a Halyk subsidiary). Names move: Ravnaq-bank became Octobank, Ipoteka-bank was sold to Hungary's OTP in 2023, and the register still shows the old names on older documents. Source: Markaziy bank, directory of commercial-bank head offices.
