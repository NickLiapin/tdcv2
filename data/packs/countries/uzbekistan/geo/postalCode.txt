address: uzbekistan.geo.postalCode
description: 186 real Uzbek postal codes (pochta indeksi), harvested from the addresses of bank branches in the published Central Bank MFO register rather than generated from the format. Six digits, and STRUCTURED: the first two are the region (see uzbekistan.geo.regionCode), the next two the district or district-centre, the last two the post office — so 230100 is post office 00 in district 01 of Qoraqalpogʻiston and 100047 is a Tashkent office. THE COUNTRY RENUMBERED and both series are still in circulation on paper: the six-digit codes here were brought in by an order of the Communications Agency of 28 October 2005, replacing a Soviet series that began 70 and 71, and the same register shows the same Tashkent street under 700084 and under 100084. So an Uzbek address file assembled from mixed-vintage sources contains two incompatible schemes that are both six digits, both numeric, and both pass any format check; a code starting 7 is pre-2005 and dead. The 4th and 5th columns of the register also caught two codes whose region prefix contradicts the town beside them (a Namangan office filed under 11 and a Navoiy office under 20); both were dropped rather than carried, because a postcode that points at the wrong region is worse than a missing one. This is a sample of the register, not the whole national list, which UZPOST does not publish as a file.
