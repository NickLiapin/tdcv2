description: All 163 tumans of Uzbekistan — the second level of administration, the one that actually appears on a document. THE TUMAN IS THE UNIT THAT MATTERS: it is the third group of the PINFL (the "code of district, city" in the 2022 decree), it is what a passport office is attached to, and an Uzbek address is written mahalla, tuman, viloyat rather than street, city, region. Three things a reader coming from Europe gets wrong. (1) 163 is not 175: the official figure counts the twelve districts of Toshkent city as well, and those are a different animal, so they live in uzbekistan.geo.tashkentDistrict instead of being mixed in here. (2) The name is written BARE, without the word tuman after it, which is how Uzbek writes it in a list; in running text it takes the possessive, Zangiota tumani. (3) The district name is NOT a key — Buxoro, Samarqand, Andijon, Namangan, Fargʻona, Qarshi, Termiz and Guliston are each both a city and the rural district wrapped around that city, and Nukus is a district in Qoraqalpogʻiston whose seat is Oqmangʻit rather than Nukus itself. The list moves: Taxiatosh was carved out of Xoʻjayli in 2017 and Boʻzatov out of Kegeyli and Chimboy in 2019, so a 2016 dataset of Qoraqalpogʻiston has 14 districts where this has 16. Names in Uzbek Latin with the correct U+02BB in oʻ and gʻ. Source: the Uzbek statistics agency SOATO/MHOBT classifier as reproduced on Wikipedia.
locale: uz-latn
