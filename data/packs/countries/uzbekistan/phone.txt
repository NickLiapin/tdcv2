description: An Uzbek mobile number in E.164 form — +998, a two-digit operator prefix and seven subscriber digits, nine digits of national number in all. The plan is CLOSED and there is NO TRUNK PREFIX: the number you dial across the street is the number you dial from abroad without the +998, there is no leading 0 to strip and no 8 to dial first, and an "area code" column has nothing to put in it for a mobile because the 17 mobile prefixes are non-geographic. Two Uzbek specifics. The prefix is the OPERATOR, and 90/91/92 (Beeline), 93/94/50 (Ucell), 97/87/88 (Mobiuz), 99/95/70/77 (Uzmobile), 33 (Humans), 20 (OQ) and 98/80 (Perfectum) are recognised on sight by anyone in the country — number portability exists, so the prefix is where the number STARTED rather than where it is now, and an operator column derived from the prefix is right about most rows and wrong about the ported ones. And 95, 98 and 80 are not ordinary GSM: 95 and 98 were CDMA allocations and 80 is a 5G standalone block, so a device model inferred from those prefixes is not the same population as the rest. The international access prefix from inside Uzbekistan is 00, having replaced the Soviet 8~10. This file emits the unspaced E.164 form so it survives a CSV round trip; uzbekistan.phoneNational carries the spaced local form. Source: the ITU national numbering plan for Uzbekistan.
