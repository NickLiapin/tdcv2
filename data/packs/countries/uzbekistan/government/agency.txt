description: The central bodies of the Uzbek state — ministries, committees, agencies — under the short form used in speech; the full form prefixes "Oʻzbekiston Respublikasi". THE SET IS REBUILT EVERY FEW YEARS AND THAT IS THE POINT OF THIS DESCRIPTION: Uzbekistan reorganised its executive in 2017, 2019, 2022 and again in 2023, merging, splitting and renaming ministries each time, so a name is a DATE STAMP. The Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Economy merged into Iqtisodiyot va moliya vazirligi; the old Davlat soliq qoʻmitasi and Davlat bojxona qoʻmitasi lost the Davlat from their names; the State Committee on Statistics became an agency and then a committee again; the ITC ministry has been renamed towards Raqamli texnologiyalar. So an Uzbek record naming a ministry can usually be dated to within two years by the name alone, and a join between a 2019 dataset and a 2025 one on ministry name will silently drop most rows. Two bodies here are not ministries and are frequently mistaken for them: Davlat xavfsizlik xizmati is the successor to the Soviet-era SNB and reports to the President, and Milliy gvardiya is a separate armed service under the President rather than a police branch of the Interior Ministry. The Markaziy bank is constitutionally independent of the Cabinet and is included because it is the counterparty in most financial records. 30 entries; this is a snapshot, not a register.
