description: South Sudanese towns. Juba is the capital and by far the largest; the others are state capitals and river ports. Malakal and Bentiu sit in the oil-producing north and both were largely emptied and partly destroyed in the 2013-18 war, so population figures for them differ by an order of magnitude depending on the year and the source. Wau and Aweil anchor the Bahr el Ghazal region in the west.
