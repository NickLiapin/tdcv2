description: THE SOURCE OF TRUTH for Philippine banks. Each line is the bank's 9-digit BRSTN, one space, then its name — "010530667 BDO Unibank". philippines.finance.bank and philippines.finance.bankCode are both sliced out of this list, so a name and its routing number cannot drift apart. The BRSTN (Bank Routing Symbol Transit Number) is assigned by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and is what routes a domestic transfer through PESONet or InstaPay — the nearest Philippine equivalent of a sort code, and NOT a SWIFT/BIC, which is a separate identifier the same banks also hold for inbound international payments. EVERY CODE HERE CARRIES THE AMERICAN ABA CHECK DIGIT, WHICH IS NOT A COINCIDENCE: the Philippines took its clearing apparatus from the United States, and the BRSTN is nine digits validated by the same modulo-10 3-7-1 rule as a US routing number. All 42 codes below were verified against it — see philippines.finance.brstn, which computes it. The list is current banks only: Allied Bank, iBank, Prudential Bank, Export & Industry Bank, Sterling Bank of Asia, UCPB and Robinsons Bank all appear in circulating BRSTN tables and were dropped because they have since merged or closed, and PSBank was dropped because the published tables give it Metrobank's code, which cannot be right and could not be resolved. Foreign banks' Manila branches are included, because they hold BRSTNs and clear domestically on the same terms. GCash also holds a BRSTN (018040010) and is deliberately absent: it is an electronic money issuer rather than a bank, and it moves more retail transfers in the Philippines than most of the banks here.
