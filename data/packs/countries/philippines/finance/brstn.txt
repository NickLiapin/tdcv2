address: philippines.finance.brstn
description: Philippine bank routing number (BRSTN) with a REAL, COMPUTED CHECK DIGIT — the only verified checksum in this pack, and it is an American one. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas took the United States routing-number format whole: nine digits, and the ninth is the ABA modulo-10 check, weights 3-7-1 repeating, chosen so the weighted sum of all nine digits is divisible by 10. This generator draws the first six digits — clearing region and institution — from a real bank's published BRSTN (philippines.finance.bankCode), draws a 2-digit clearing branch, and computes the ninth digit properly, so the institution half always names a bank the BSP actually issued a code to and the check digit always holds. VERIFIED BOTH DIRECTIONS against real published BRSTNs: the rule accepts 010530667 (BDO Unibank), 010040018 (Bank of the Philippine Islands), 010269996 (Metropolitan Bank and Trust), 010350025 (Land Bank of the Philippines), 010080010 (Philippine National Bank), 010590018 (DBP), 010140015 (Security Bank), 010280014 (RCBC), 010419995 (UnionBank) and 011020011 (Asia United Bank) — 42 of 42 in philippines.finance.bankWithCode pass — and rejects each of them with a single digit altered. THE BRANCH IS NOT GUARANTEED TO EXIST: the drawn 2-digit branch may be a clearing office that bank never opened, so use philippines.finance.bankCode when the number has to resolve to a real branch and this when you need many distinct, structurally valid, checksum-correct routing numbers. Note the consequence of the shared format: a US ABA validator cannot tell a Philippine BRSTN from a US routing number, so a system that infers country from a passing routing number will get the Philippines wrong.
generator: tdc
locale: en

<sequence name="real"><gen type="template" value="philippines.finance.bankCode"/></sequence>
<sequence name="branch"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="brstn"><compute>
  <!-- First six digits: clearing region + institution, taken from a real BRSTN. -->
  <let name="base"><concat>
    <slice from="0" to="6"><field name="real"/></slice>
    <field name="branch"/>
  </concat></let>
  <!-- ABA modulo-10: weights 3,7,1 repeating over the eight base digits; the
       check digit is what makes the nine-digit weighted sum divisible by 10. -->
  <let name="sum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="base"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="3,7,1,3,7,1,3,7"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><mod><subtract><int v="10"/><mod><use name="sum"/><int v="10"/></mod></subtract><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <result><concat><use name="base"/><use name="check"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{brstn}}</data>
