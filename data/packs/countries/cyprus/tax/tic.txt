address: cyprus.tax.tic
description: Cypriot Tax Identification Code (ΑΦΜ / TIC) — 8 digits plus a check LETTER. The 1-indexed odd digits are first mapped through the table 1,0,5,7,9,13,15,17,19,21; the even digits count as themselves; the total mod 26 selects A-Z. The base is drawn from the ranges the published rule is known to cover (leading 0 or 9 for individuals, 1 for companies) and never starts 12, which is reserved and never issued. TICs issued since March 2023 start at 60000000 under a check rule the Tax Department has not published, so this file does not generate them.
generator: tdc
