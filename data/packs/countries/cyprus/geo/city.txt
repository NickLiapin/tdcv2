description: Towns and municipalities of the Republic of Cyprus, roughly by population. GREEK forms only — this is a deliberate choice, not an oversight. Cyprus has two official languages and every place here also has a Turkish name (Λευκωσία / Lefkoşa, Λεμεσός / Limasol), but a country pack in this project carries one language, and Greek is the form the Republic's own postal, land-registry and municipal records use. The list is also limited to the area the Republic effectively administers, so Αμμόχωστος, Κερύνεια and Μόρφου are absent: they are Cypriot towns in law, but an address there is not deliverable under the Republic's postcode system. Their districts are still listed in cyprus.geo.district.
