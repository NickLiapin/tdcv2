address: iran.vehicle.plate
description: Iranian registration plate, in the four-part layout used since 2005 — two digits, a Persian letter, three digits, then the province code under the word ایران, written here as "12 ب 345 ایران 68". The letter is a category, not a serial: the seventeen letters on ORDINARY private cars are ب ج د س ص ط ق ل م ن و ه ی (thirteen of them), and every other letter marks a different kind of vehicle — ت taxi on a yellow plate, ع public transport, الف government on red, پ police on green, ژ disabled and war-veteran, ک agricultural, ث IRGC, ش army, ز Ministry of Defence, and the Latin letters D and S for diplomatic and service-of-mission plates. A dataset of "car plates" containing ت or الف is not a dataset of private cars. This file emits the private set only. The two-digit province code is many-to-one and grows as a province runs out of series: Tehran holds 10, 11, 20, 21, 22, 30, 33, 40, 44, 55, 66, 77, 88 and 99 (it was given 11 first and then every multiple of it), خراسان رضوی holds 12, 32, 36, 42 and 74, اصفهان 13, 23 and 43, and single anchors include 14 خوزستان, 15 آذربایجان شرقی, 16 قم, 17 آذربایجان غربی, 18 همدان, 19 کرمانشاه, 21 البرز/کرج. Because the mapping is many-to-one and only partly published, this file does NOT tie the code to iran.geo.province — the code emitted is a code in use, but the plate is not made to agree with any city or province elsewhere in the record. ON DIGITS: a real plate is painted in PERSIAN digits ۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹, which are not the Arabic-Indic digits used on Arab plates — ۴ ۵ ۶ against ٤ ٥ ٦, different characters at U+06F4-U+06F6 rather than U+0664-U+0666. ASCII digits are emitted here because that is what Iranian plate-recognition and registry systems store; rendering them in Persian is the config's job.
generator: tdc
locale: fa
