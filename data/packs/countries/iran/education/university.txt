description: Universities of Iran, Persian names. The system splits three ways and the split matters for any record about a student: the state universities (دانشگاه تهران، شریف، امیرکبیر and the provincial ones), دانشگاه آزاد اسلامی — a single fee-paying institution with hundreds of branches and over a million students, so "دانشگاه آزاد اسلامی" in a record names a network rather than a campus — and دانشگاه پیام نور, the state distance-learning university, likewise nationwide. Separately, every medical faculty in Iran was moved out of the universities in 1985 and put under the Ministry of Health as its own دانشگاه علوم پزشکی, so دانشگاه تهران and دانشگاه علوم پزشکی تهران are two different institutions with different rectors on different campuses, and a medical student in Iran does not attend the university that shares the city's name. Names beginning دانشگاه صنعتی are the technical universities; the شریف، امیرکبیر (the former Tehran Polytechnic) and علم و صنعت trio are the ones an Iranian CV expects to be recognised.
