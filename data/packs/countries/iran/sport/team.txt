description: Football clubs of Iran, Persian names — the Persian Gulf Pro League (لیگ برتر خلیج فارس) and the best-known clubs below it. پرسپولیس and استقلال are both from Tehran, both state-linked, and between them hold most of the country's support; their derby (داربی پایتخت) is the fixture the calendar is built around. Iranian club names often carry the industry that owns them rather than a place — ذوب‌آهن is a steelworks, مس رفسنجان a copper mine, صنعت نفت آبادان and پارس جنوبی جم the oil and gas industry, ماشین‌سازی a machine-tool plant — so a club name is not a city name and cannot be parsed as one. Several famous clubs also carry a place that is NOT where the club plays: استقلال خوزستان is in Ahvaz. Names are given in the short form Iranian media use, without the "باشگاه فرهنگی ورزشی" prefix that appears in official registrations.
