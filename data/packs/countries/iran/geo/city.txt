description: Cities of Iran, Persian names, roughly ordered by population. Tehran and its ring dominate the list — کرج، اسلامشهر، قرچک، ملارد، ورامین، پاکدشت، شهریار and بهارستان are separate legal cities, not districts of Tehran, and several are larger than provincial capitals. Names written with a نیم‌فاصله (the zero-width non-joiner, U+200C) keep it: خرم‌آباد، قائم‌شهر، نجف‌آباد، نسیم‌شهر are one word each, and replacing the ZWNJ with a space or deleting it both give a spelling no Iranian registry uses — this is the character most often lost when Persian text passes through a system that normalises whitespace. Some cities carry an Arabic-script name that is written differently in Persian than in Arabic (خرمشهر، آبادان، اهواز sit in Arabic-speaking Khuzestan); the spellings here are the Persian ones used by Iranian registries, which is what an Iranian record contains. The list is national and does not pair a city with the province in iran.geo.province — nothing here makes a generated city and province agree.
