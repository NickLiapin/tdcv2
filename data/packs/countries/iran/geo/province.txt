description: The 31 provinces (استان) of Iran, the country's first-level division, in the Persian alphabetical order the government's own tables use. The count is 31 and has been since 2010, when البرز was split out of تهران — a list of 30 is pre-2010 data and a list of 28 is pre-2004, before خراسان was divided into رضوی، شمالی and جنوبی, so the number of provinces dates a dataset. Six names are two-part and the second part is not a suffix that can be stripped: آذربایجان شرقی and آذربایجان غربی are different provinces, as are خراسان رضوی، خراسان شمالی and خراسان جنوبی, and چهارمحال و بختیاری، سیستان و بلوچستان and کهگیلویه و بویراحمد each join two historic regions with و. Several provinces share a name with their capital (اصفهان، کرمان، همدان، بوشهر، زنجان، سمنان، قم، ایلام، اردبیل، یزد، مرکزی is the exception), so a column holding "اصفهان" is ambiguous between the province and the city unless the schema says which.
