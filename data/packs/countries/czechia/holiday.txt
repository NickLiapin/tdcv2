description: The 13 public holidays of Czechia, in calendar order. Two of them are the reason a Czech calendar cannot be copied from a Catholic neighbour: 5 July for Cyril and Methodius and 6 July for the burning of Jan Hus, a Catholic-condemned reformer commemorated by the state. Czechia is one of the least religious countries in Europe and its calendar shows it — Assumption and All Saints, which are days off in Poland and Austria, are ordinary working days here.
