description: The public universities of Czechia, official Czech names, oldest and largest first. Univerzita Karlova was founded in 1348 and is the oldest university north of the Alps and east of Paris, which is why a Czech CV can carry a date no other country's can. Note the vocabulary: a vysoká škola is a university too, not a college — ČVUT, VŠCHT and VŠE are full universities whose names simply predate the word univerzita.
