address: czechia.finance.iban
description: Czech IBAN — 24 chars: CZ + 2 ISO 7064 mod-97 check digits + a 4-digit ČNB bank code + the 6-digit account prefix + the 10-digit account number. Three checks hold at once here, not one: the mod-97 IBAN check and BOTH of the domestic weighted mod-11 checks (prefix under 10,5,8,4,2,1 and main part under 6,3,7,9,10,5,8,4,2,1), so the BBAN is still a valid Czech account number after the IBAN is taken apart. Verified against the published CZ6508000000192000145399, which is the account 19-2000145399/0800.
