description: Streets and squares that really exist in Czech cities — Prague first, then Brno, Ostrava and Plzeň. The generic Czech street vocabulary (Lipová, Na Příkopě-style preposition names) lives in the language pack at cs.address.streetName; this file is the named ones you could point at on a map. Note the shape of a Czech address: the street comes first and the number after it (Vodičkova 12), and the word ulice is left out unless the name itself is a náměstí or a třída.
