description: The 14 first-level units of Czechia — 13 kraje plus the capital, which is a kraj in its own right and is never called one (it is Hlavní město Praha, not "Pražský kraj"). Two more break the pattern: Kraj Vysočina puts the word first because Vysočina is a highland, not a town, and Moravskoslezský kraj names two historic lands rather than a city. Given in the nominative, as on forms; the address form inflects (v Jihomoravském kraji).
