description: The 76 okresy of Czechia plus the capital, ordered by kraj. The okres was abolished as a tier of government in 2002 and still has not gone away — it names the court, the land registry, the social-security office and the vehicle register, so it keeps appearing on documents long after it stopped governing anything. Four are city halves (Brno-město and Brno-venkov, Praha-východ and Praha-západ) where the surrounding district is administered separately from the city it surrounds.
