description: Czech company registration number (IČO) — 8 digits issued by the statistical office to every legal person and every sole trader. The last digit is a real mod-11 check over the first seven under weights 8,7,6,5,4,3,2: remainder 0 gives 1, remainder 1 gives 0, otherwise 11 minus the remainder. Leading zeros are part of the number and are significant — Škoda Auto is 00177041, not 177041.
generator: tdc
