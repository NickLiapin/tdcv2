description: The fourteen public and national holidays of Malta (Maltese names) — the most of any EU member state, and since 2020 one that falls on a weekend is added to the worker's leave rather than lost. Five are national days marking political events (Jum il-Ħelsien, Sette Giugno, Jum il-Vitorja, Jum l-Indipendenza, Jum ir-Repubblika); the other nine are Catholic feasts, which is why a Maltese calendar carries three saints' days that a Northern European one does not. Sette Giugno keeps its Italian name — it commemorates the riots of 7 June 1919, from the period when Italian, not Maltese, was the language of law and letters in Malta.
