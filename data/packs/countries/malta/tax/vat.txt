description: Maltese VAT number — MT plus eight digits, where the last two are a real check pair. Rewritten from the shipped stub, which drew eight free digits and said "no checksum"; Malta does have one. The first six digits are weighted 3,4,6,7,8,9, summed, and the check pair is 37 minus that sum modulo 37 — so it lands in 01..37 and never in 38..99, which is worth knowing if you are eyeballing output. The leading digit is never 0. Verified against the published example MT 1167-9112 (python-stdnum doctest): 3+4+36+49+72+9 = 173, 173 mod 37 = 25, 37-25 = 12.
generator: tdc
