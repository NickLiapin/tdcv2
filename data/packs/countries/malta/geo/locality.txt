address: malta.geo.locality
description: The 68 local councils of Malta in their official Maltese form. These are the ONLY elected sub-national bodies in the country — there is no county, province or district layer above them, so nothing here corresponds to poland.geo.voivodeship or spain.geo.province. Maltese fuses the definite article onto the place name (Il-Mosta, Tas-Sliema, Ħaż-Żebbuġ, Ir-Rabat), which is why these strings do not look like the postal forms in malta.geo.city; the two files are aligned line for line.
locale: mt
