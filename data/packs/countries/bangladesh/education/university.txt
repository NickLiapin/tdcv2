description: Universities in Bangladesh that exist, public first and then private, in English — which is the language a Bangladeshi degree certificate, transcript and university website are actually written in, even though teaching is largely in Bengali. Note the spelling: most of these institutions kept the PRE-2018 romanisation of their city after the districts were renamed, so it is still the University of Chittagong and Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology in a country whose district is now Chattogram, while a few newer ones such as Chattogram Veterinary and Animal Sciences University did adopt the new spelling — a config must not normalise one into the other. Two entries are not campuses at all and are the largest in the country by enrolment: National University affiliates roughly two thousand colleges and awards their degrees, so a very large share of Bangladeshi graduates hold a National University degree and never attended anything called a university, and Bangladesh Open University is the distance-learning body. The engineering group (BUET and the four regional ETs, plus IUT) is the most competitive admission in the country, and the ten-odd "Science and Technology University" entries are a deliberate government programme of one per region rather than a naming coincidence. The list stops short of the full set — there are around 55 public and over 110 private universities — and it leaves out institutions whose names were changed in the political renamings after 2024, because a name that is being litigated is not a name a test set should assert.
