---
address: bangladesh.finance.routingNumber
description: Bangladesh Bank routing number — NINE digits, one per bank branch, and the number a BEFTN, RTGS or cheque-clearing transfer is addressed to. Layout, all of it real geography — 3-digit bank code (bangladesh.finance.bankCode), 2-digit district code (bangladesh.finance.districtCode), 3-digit branch serial, and a CHECK DIGIT that this file actually computes. The check is a weighted mod-10 over the eight body digits with the weights 5, 3, 9 repeating (5,3,9,5,3,9,5,3), summed and taken mod 10 — no subtraction from 10, the remainder IS the digit. An equivalent published-looking form is weights 5,7,1 repeating with the digit taken as (10 - sum mod 10) mod 10, which gives the same answer for every input and is the form the folklore garbles into the American ABA 3,7,1; the ABA weights are NOT what Bangladesh uses and pass only about one number in five. The algorithm was recovered by fitting a weight vector to 620 routing numbers published by nine banks across 47 districts and it reproduces all 620. It has a real weakness worth knowing before trusting it — the weight 5 falls on body positions 1, 4 and 7, and 5 times a digit is only ever 0 or 5 mod 10, so changing a digit in those three places by an even amount does not change the check at all. Over the 620 real numbers, 44,640 single-digit mutations were tested and 37,200 were rejected while 7,440 slipped through, every one of them at position 1, 4 or 7. So this check catches most typos and cannot catch that class, which is a property of the scheme and not of the implementation. Bangladesh has NO IBAN — it is not in the IBAN registry and never joined the scheme — so there is deliberately no bangladesh.finance.iban in this pack. A domestic transfer is addressed by routing number plus account number, an international one by SWIFT/BIC plus account number, and a config that generates a BD-prefixed IBAN is generating something that does not exist. The pack does not model which branch serials a bank has actually issued — bank code and district code are real, the branch part is well-formed.
generator: tdc
---
<sequence name="bank"><gen type="template" value="bangladesh.finance.bankCode"/></sequence>
<sequence name="district"><gen type="template" value="bangladesh.finance.districtCode"/></sequence>
<sequence name="branch"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{3}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="routing"><compute>
  <let name="body"><concat><field name="bank"/><field name="district"/><field name="branch"/></concat></let>
  <!-- Weights 5,3,9 repeating over the eight body digits; the remainder mod 10 is the check. -->
  <let name="sum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="body"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="5,3,9,5,3,9,5,3"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><mod><use name="sum"/><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <result><concat><use name="body"/><use name="check"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{routing}}</data>
