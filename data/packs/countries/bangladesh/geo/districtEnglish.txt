description: All 64 districts of Bangladesh in their current official English spelling, alphabetically, index-aligned line for line with bangladesh.geo.district. Five of these were respelled by government order in 2018 and the new spelling is the legal one — Chittagong became Chattogram, Comilla became Cumilla, Barisal became Barishal, Jessore became Jashore and Bogra became Bogura. A test set that still says Chittagong or Comilla is a test set from before 2018, and the renaming is the single most common way an English-language Bangladesh dataset dates itself. Chapai Nawabganj is also written Chapainawabganj and Nawabganj; the district was renamed from plain Nawabganj to distinguish it from the Nawabganj upazila in Dhaka, and the spaced form is used here. Cox's Bazar keeps its apostrophe, which is the only punctuation in the list and the thing a naive CSV export breaks on.
