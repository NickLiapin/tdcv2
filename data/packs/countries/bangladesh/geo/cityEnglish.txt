description: The same places as bangladesh.geo.city, in their official English romanisation, index-aligned line for line with it. The 2018 respellings apply here too — Chattogram, Cumilla, Barishal, Jashore, Bogura — and so do a handful of older ones that are easy to miss: Saidpur is the town in Nilphamari and is not Saidpur in Sylhet, Mongla is spelled without the extra n that the Bengali মোংলা suggests, and Sreemangal is also written Srimangal and Sreemongol in older sources. This file exists because a Bangladeshi record is written in English about as often as in Bengali — a bank statement, an airline booking and a passport are English, a utility bill and a land record are Bengali — and a config should pick whichever agrees with the rest of the row rather than transliterating one into the other at generation time.
