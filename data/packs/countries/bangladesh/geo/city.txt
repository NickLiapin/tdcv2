description: Cities and towns of Bangladesh in Bengali, roughly by population and then by administrative weight, index-aligned line for line with bangladesh.geo.cityEnglish. The head of the list is the 12 city corporations (ঢাকা is split into two of them, উত্তর and দক্ষিণ, and is written once here because the name on an address is just ঢাকা); after them come the district towns, and then the places that are not district headquarters but are large or nationally known anyway — সাভার and টঙ্গী, which are industrial and effectively part of greater Dhaka; মোংলা, the second seaport; বেনাপোল, the land port that carries most of the India trade; ভৈরব and ঈশ্বরদী, the two big rail junctions; শ্রীমঙ্গল, the tea capital; টেকনাফ, the southern end of the country. Bangladesh is one of the most densely populated countries on earth and the boundary between town and countryside is not sharp: a very large share of the population lives in places that have a bazar and a bus stand but no municipal status, and no city list can represent them, so a config generating rural Bangladeshi addresses should reach for bangladesh.geo.district plus the bn pack's address elements rather than for this file.
