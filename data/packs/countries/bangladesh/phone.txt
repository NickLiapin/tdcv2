address: bangladesh.phone
description: Bangladeshi mobile number in E.164 form — +880 then 1, then the operator digit, then eight subscriber digits, eleven digits in all when written nationally as 01XXXXXXXXX. The operator digit is NOT free: BTRC's national numbering plan allots 013 and 017 to Grameenphone, 014 and 019 to Banglalink, 015 to Teletalk (the state operator), and 016 and 018 to Robi, which absorbed Airtel's 016 block when the two merged in 2016. That is the whole mobile market — four operators, seven prefixes — so 010, 011 and 012 are not mobile numbers at all and a generator that emits them is emitting something no Bangladeshi SIM can be. The 013 and 014 blocks were opened in 2018 when the older ones ran short, so a dataset that only has 017/018/019 numbers is a dataset from before then. Landlines are a different shape and are not generated here: a 1-2 digit area code (02 Dhaka, 031 Chattogram, 041 Khulna, 0721 Rajshahi, 0821 Sylhet) plus a 6-8 digit subscriber number, and they are vanishingly rare in personal records because Bangladesh went mobile almost without a fixed-line stage. Mobile numbers matter here more than in most countries for a reason beyond telephony: the number IS the account for bKash, Nagad and Rocket (see bangladesh.finance.mobileFinancialService), so in a Bangladeshi payment record the phone column and the wallet column are frequently the same value.
generator: tdc
