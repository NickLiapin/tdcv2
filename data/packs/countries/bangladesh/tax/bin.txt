description: BIN — Business Identification Number, the VAT registration number issued by Bangladesh's National Board of Revenue through its online VAT portal, and THIRTEEN digits, not twelve. The twelve-digit number in this pack is the income-tax e-TIN (bangladesh.tax.etin) and the two are routinely confused — a TIN is a taxpayer, a BIN is a VAT-registered business unit, a business has both, and NBR will not issue the BIN until the TIN exists. The 13-digit form is often called the e-BIN and it is the ONLY valid one: NBR invalidated the older 9-digit and 11-digit BINs on 30 November 2019, so a Bangladeshi VAT record holding a 9- or 11-digit BIN is a record from before that date and would be rejected today. NBR describes the thirteen as a 9-digit registration followed by a 4-digit identifier for the registered unit, which is why a BIN is commonly PRINTED with a hyphen between the ninth and tenth digit; this file emits the thirteen digits unpunctuated so the value drops into a key column, and a config that wants the printed form should insert the hyphen itself. NO CHECK DIGIT is published for the BIN — none is computed here, and none is invented. The digits are drawn uniformly, including a possible leading zero, because real BINs do carry leading zeros and stripping them is how a BIN gets destroyed by a spreadsheet.
