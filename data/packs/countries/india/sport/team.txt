description: Indian professional club teams. Cricket comes first because in India it is not one sport among several — the ten Indian Premier League franchises are the country's most recognised team names by a wide margin, and unlike European clubs they are city or state franchises created in 2008 rather than clubs with a century behind them. Then the Indian Super League football clubs, where the two Kolkata giants (Mohun Bagan, founded 1889, and East Bengal, 1920) really are older than the league they now play in. Then Pro Kabaddi League teams: kabaddi is a genuinely Indian professional sport with no counterpart in a European pack, and leaving it out would misdescribe what Indian sport is.
