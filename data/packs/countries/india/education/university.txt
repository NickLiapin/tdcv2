description: Indian universities and degree-granting institutes, in their official English names. The list mixes three kinds that Indian records keep apart — the Institutes of National Importance created by their own Acts of Parliament (the IITs, IIMs, AIIMS, NITs, IISc), the central and state universities, and the private and deemed-to-be universities — because a student record cites whichever one issued the degree. An IIT is not a university in Indian law and does not call itself one, which is why the names below say Institute rather than University where that is what they are.
