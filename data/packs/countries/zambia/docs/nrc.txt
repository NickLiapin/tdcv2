description: National Registration Card number — the Zambian NRC, and the format is unusual enough to trip most validators. It is three parts: a six-digit serial, a two-digit district code for the district that issued it, and a single check-like suffix digit, written 123456/78/1. The middle pair is geography, so two NRCs issued in the same district share it. The NRC is required to vote, to open a bank account and to register a SIM, and it is issued at 16.
