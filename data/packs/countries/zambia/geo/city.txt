description: Zambian cities and towns. The shape of this list is the Copperbelt: Kitwe, Ndola, Chingola, Mufulira, Luanshya and Kalulushi are all mining towns within an hour of each other, and together they hold a share of the urban population that rivals Lusaka. Livingstone is the tourist town at Victoria Falls and was the capital until 1935. Kabwe, once Broken Hill, gave its name to a famous hominin skull and to one of the worst lead-contamination sites in the world.
