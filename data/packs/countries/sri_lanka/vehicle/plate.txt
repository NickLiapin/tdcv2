address: sri_lanka.vehicle.plate
description: A Sri Lankan number plate in the CURRENT format, introduced on 13 July 2013 — three Latin letters, a hyphen and four digits, e.g. CAB-1234, running from AAA-0001 upward. This is the third scheme in living memory and the pack models it because it is the one every vehicle registered since 2013 carries; the other two are named below and one of them is generated separately. THE FIRST LETTER IS THE VEHICLE CLASS, not a serial and not a place. C is the motor-car block, which advances CAA, CAB … CAZ and then CBA; B is the bus and coach block; K covers vans and lorries; Q is the tricycle and three-wheeler block. That is what can be attested from published sources, and it is where this file stops: the Department of Motor Traffic has never issued the full block table as one document, the public reconstructions of it disagree with each other about which letters carry motorcycles, tractors and trailers, and the pack will not pick between them — so the leading letter here is drawn from the four attested class blocks and the two after it are drawn freely, which makes every value well-formed and its class truthful, without claiming the pack knows the whole allocation. WHAT CHANGED IN 2000 AND WHY IT MATTERS. Before 2000 a plate was a number, the Sinhala letter ශ්‍රී (Sri) and another number, e.g. 1 Sri 1234 — a scheme running since 1956, and one whose Sinhala character made the plate itself an object in the language politics of the period. In 2000 that was replaced by a two-letter PROVINCE code plus a two-letter class series plus four digits (WP KA-1234); that form is sri_lanka.vehicle.plateProvincial and is still on a very large share of the fleet, because a Sri Lankan car stays registered for decades and plates are not reissued. In 2013 the province code stopped being part of the registration NUMBER — the number became the three letters and four digits alone — while the province emblem and code remained printed on the plate itself. So a 2013-onward plate identifies a class and a serial, and a 2000-2013 plate identifies a province and a class, and a dataset that assumes one shape for the whole fleet will mis-parse roughly half of it.
