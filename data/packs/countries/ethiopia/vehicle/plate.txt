address: ethiopia.vehicle.plate
description: Ethiopian civilian licence plate. The leading digit is the REGION the vehicle is registered in and the letter is the plate class — A private, B commercial, C government — followed by a five-digit serial. The region codes are worth a note: they were assigned to the region set as it stood before 2020, so Sidama, South West Ethiopia, Central Ethiopia and South Ethiopia — all created between 2020 and 2023 — have no code of their own and their vehicles still carry the code of the region they left. A plate is therefore evidence of an administrative map that no longer exists, which is exactly the kind of lag a test dataset should reproduce rather than tidy away.
