description: Ethiopian cities and principal towns, roughly by population. Addis Ababa is far larger than anything else and holds the African Union, so it appears in datasets with a weight no other Ethiopian city has. Several entries carry two names in ordinary use because Amharic and Oromo name the same place differently — Adama is Nazret, Bishoftu is Debre Zeit, Hawassa is Awasa in older spellings — and which name a document uses often says more about when it was written than about where it points. Harar is walled and is the only Ethiopian city that is also a region.
