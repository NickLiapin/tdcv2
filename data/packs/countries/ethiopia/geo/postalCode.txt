description: Ethiopian postal codes. Delivery in Ethiopia is overwhelmingly to a PO box rather than to a street address — Ethiopian Postal Service boxes are the normal business address, and buildings outside central Addis frequently have no number at all — so a postal code here identifies a post office rather than a delivery walk. 1000 is central Addis Ababa. The codes below are the principal offices; a full Ethiopian address in practice reads as a sub-city, a kebele number and a PO box.
