description: Ethiopian public universities. Addis Ababa University (1950, as the University College of Addis Ababa) and Haramaya (1954, founded as an agricultural college with Oklahoma State) are the two old ones; almost everything else on this list was built in two waves of expansion after 2000, which took the country from two universities to more than forty in twenty years. The naming rule is geographic — a university is called after the town it stands in — so the list doubles as a list of regional centres.
