description: Afghan banks. Da Afghanistan Bank is the central bank and issues the afghani; the commercial list is short because the sector is. Azizi and Afghanistan International Bank are the two largest private banks, Bank-e-Millie Afghan (1933) is the oldest bank in the country and Pashtany Bank the other state-owned one. Kabul Bank appears in no list here on purpose: it collapsed in 2010 in what was then one of the largest banking frauds in the world relative to a country's economy, and its successor New Kabul Bank was later merged away. Most Afghans move money through hawala rather than through any of these.
