description: Tazkira number — the Afghan national identity document. The paper tazkira is a booklet issued by the district civil registry and its number is a volume-page-record triple rather than one flat serial: it identifies where in the register the entry sits, which is why two Afghans can hold visually similar numbers issued in different districts. The electronic tazkira (e-tazkira), rolled out from 2018, replaced this with a single national number and a biometric chip, but enrolment is partial and the paper form is still in wide circulation, so an Afghan dataset holds both shapes at once. This file models the paper triple.
