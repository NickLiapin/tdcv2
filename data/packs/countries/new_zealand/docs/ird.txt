Primary weights 3,2,7,6,5,4,3,2; when the primary check resolves to 10 the secondary weights 7,4,3,2,5,2,7,6 are used; the rare base that fails both is nudged to the next valid one. Every emitted number passes IRD's own validation.

       stage below. -->

    <when><test><equals><var name="r1"/><int v="0"/></equals></test><then><int v="0"/></then></when>

  <!-- Residual case (c1 == 10 and c2 == 10): this base has no valid check

      <otherwise><concat><field name="base"/><var name="c1"/></concat></otherwise>
    </choose>
  </result>
