address: tanzania.finance.account
description: Tanzanian bank account number — commonly 10 to 16 digits, and STRUCTURAL ONLY, for two separate reasons. First, TANZANIA HAS NO IBAN. It is not in the IBAN registry, has never adopted the scheme, and there is no TZ.. account string over which a MOD-97 could be computed. Anyone reaching for tanzania.finance.iban is looking for something that does not exist, and this pack deliberately does not ship one; a plausible-looking TZ IBAN would be worse than none, because it would pass every generic IBAN validator and be rejected by every actual bank. A domestic transfer is addressed by bank plus account number and settles over TISS, the Tanzania Interbank Settlement System, for large values or over TIPS, the Tanzania Instant Payment System live since 2022, for retail; an inbound international transfer is addressed by SWIFT/BIC plus account number, which is why tanzania.finance.swift exists in this directory and an iban file does not. Second, there is no national account-number standard: each bank sets its own length and its own internal rule and publishes neither — CRDB and NMB numbers are commonly 16 and 14 digits respectively, others are shorter — so nothing generated here can be checked against anything, and this file says so rather than picking one bank's shape and calling it the country's. Balances are in Tanzanian shillings, TZS. The shilling is divided into 100 cents in law and into nothing at all in practice; the smallest circulating coin is 50 shillings and prices are quoted in whole shillings, so a Tanzanian money column with two decimal places is modelling a currency nobody uses.
