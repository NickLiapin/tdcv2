description: Banks licensed by the Bank of Tanzania, as at the January 2025 register — 34 commercial banks first, then the 3 community banks, the 4 microfinance banks and the 2 development banks, 43 in all. Ordered by category and then alphabetically, which is the register's own order and not size: CRDB and NMB are the two largest by assets and both sit in the middle of the list. Four entries carry the country's structure rather than just a name. PEOPLE'S BANK OF ZANZIBAR is owned by the Revolutionary Government of Zanzibar, the only bank on a national register anywhere in this pack collection owned by a government that is not the national one — the union again, this time on a balance sheet. Tanzania Commercial Bank is the former Tanzania Postal Bank, renamed in 2021 after absorbing Twiga Bancorp and Tanzania Women's Bank. Mwalimu Commercial Bank was capitalised by the teachers' union and Mkombozi by the Catholic church, which is a kind of ownership a European register does not produce. Names follow the current brand, and several banks outlived a rebrand: Absa Bank Tanzania was Barclays until 2020, NCBA is the merged CBA and NIC, Mwanga Hakika is the merged Mwanga Community and Hakika Microfinance, Letshego Faidika was Advans. Banks that appear in older Tanzanian bank lists and are deliberately absent because they no longer trade: FBME (licence revoked 2017), Bank M (absorbed into Azania, 2018), Twiga Bancorp and Tanzania Women's Bank (both into TPB/TCB), Covenant Bank for Women, Efatha Bank. The Bank of Tanzania itself is the regulator and the issuer of the shilling (TZS) and is not a retail bank, so it is not here.
