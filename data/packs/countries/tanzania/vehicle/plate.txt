address: tanzania.vehicle.plate
description: Tanzanian mainland number plate — the letter T, three digits, then three letters, written T 123 ABC. THE LETTERS CARRY NO REGIONAL MEANING. Registration is national and sequential through what is now TRA's Motor Vehicle Registration system, so T 123 ABC tells you roughly WHEN a vehicle was registered and nothing at all about where it lives; a reader arriving from Poland, Germany or Spain will look for a region code and there is none, and a reader arriving from Kenya will find the same absence for a different reason. The T is fixed and is not a simplification — every mainland civilian plate in the current generation begins with it, and the format has been stable since the early 2000s, when it replaced the 1990s three-letter-plus-four-digit series and the 1980s TXX 9999. Plates are 520 x 110 mm; front is black on white, rear black on yellow. Unlike Kenya, NO LETTER EXCLUSION IS DOCUMENTED — Kenya skips I and O so they are not read as 1 and 0, and nothing published says Tanzania does, so this file draws all 26 rather than inventing a restriction to make the two countries match. THERE IS NO CHECK CHARACTER. What this file does not generate, because each is its own series outside the civilian format: Z plates for Zanzibar, which are in tanzania.vehicle.plateZanzibar; STK for Serikali Kuu Tanzania, the central government, and SM for local government; SMZ for the Revolutionary Government of Zanzibar, so the union has two separate government plate prefixes; police plates in yellow on black; Tanzania People's Defence Force plates in the form 9999 XX 99; yellow plates for the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister, Speaker and ministers; and diplomatic plates, yellow on green, which are T followed by a three-digit mission code and then CD. The spacing here follows the way plates are read and printed; some references render the same registration closed up as T 123ABC.
generator: tdc
locale: en
