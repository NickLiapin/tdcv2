address: tanzania.docs.nationalId
description: Tanzanian National Identification Number (NIN), the number on the NIDA kitambulisho — TWENTY digits, the longest national ID number in this pack collection, and printed in four groups: 19120527-14109-00002-18. The blocks are real. The first eight are the holder's date of birth as YYYYMMDD, which is why a NIN leaks an exact birthday to anyone who reads it. The next five are the POSTCODE of the ward where the holder registered (see tanzania.geo.postalCode): first digit the zone, second the region, third the district, last two the ward — so the middle block is a place, not a serial, and two people born the same day in the same ward share ten of the twenty digits. The next five are the serial within that ward. The last two are the only part NIDA has never explained in any published document. THERE IS NO PUBLISHED CHECK DIGIT and this file computes none. That was checked rather than assumed: NIDA's own site, its application form 2A, its two ID4Africa conference papers and the third-party API wrappers that talk to the NIDA verification service all state one rule and only one — "a 20 digit customer NIN" — with no checksum, no regex beyond twenty digits, and no account of the trailing pair. Deriving one empirically the way tanzania.tax.tin's was derived is not possible here and would not be right if it were: a corpus of real TINs is a public register of businesses, while a corpus of real NINs is a pile of living people's birthdays. So this file generates well-formed NINs that no NIDA lookup will recognise, and says so. Two notes for anyone modelling Tanzanian identity records. The NIN has no expiry and cannot be changed — from November 2025 citizens may correct other NIDA particulars, but not the number. And the NIN is now the spine of the whole system: SIM registration, bank onboarding, the TIN application and voter registration all key off it, so a Tanzanian record that carries a TIN almost certainly carries a NIN too. Birth dates here are drawn 1940-2008 because registration is for those 18 and over.
generator: tdc

<sequence name="dob"><gen type="date" from="1940-01-01" to="2008-12-31" format="YYYYMMDD"/></sequence>

<sequence name="ward"><gen type="number" value="101..999" length="3" first_zero="true"/></sequence>
<sequence name="serial"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{5}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="tail"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
