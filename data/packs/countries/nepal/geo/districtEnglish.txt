address: nepal.geo.districtEnglish
description: The same 77 districts as nepal.geo.district, in their usual English romanisation, index-aligned line for line with it. Nepal has no single official transliteration standard and several of these names are written more than one way in genuine government English documents — Kavrepalanchok is also Kavre and Kabhrepalanchok, Sindhupalchok also Sindhupalchowk, Dhanusha also Dhanusa, Achham also Accham, Terhathum also Tehrathum, Bardiya also Bardia, Kapilvastu also Kapilbastu. One value per district is chosen here rather than a spread, so that a generated column joins cleanly. The two split districts are given their SHORT English names, which is what English-language Nepali sources actually use: Nawalpur and Parasi for the two halves of Nawalparasi, Rukum East and Rukum West for the two halves of Rukum. This file exists because a Nepali record is written in English about as often as in Nepali — a passport, a bank KYC form, an airline booking and a university transcript are English; a citizenship certificate, a land record and a municipal document are Nepali — so a config should pick the file that agrees with the rest of the row rather than transliterate at generation time.
locale: en
