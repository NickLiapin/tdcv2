address: nepal.finance.accountNumber
description: A Nepali bank account number as it is written on a cheque and a statement — a run of digits, in practice thirteen to sixteen of them, with no letters, no country prefix and no separator. NEPAL HAS NO IBAN. It is not in the IBAN registry, its banks issue none, and nothing in this pack generates one: a value beginning "NP" followed by digits is not a Nepali account identifier of any kind, and code that manufactures one by taking a country code and running MOD-97 over it has produced a number no Nepali bank will accept and no MOD-97 check can be verified against. That refusal is the point of this file. There is also NO NATIONAL ACCOUNT-NUMBER FORMAT. Each bank numbers its own accounts, so length and internal layout differ across the twenty banks in nepal.finance.bank — some encode a branch and a product code in the leading digits, some do not — and neither the banks nor Nepal Rastra Bank publish a checksum for any of them. That is why a Nepali domestic transfer is addressed by BANK plus ACCOUNT NUMBER plus ACCOUNT NAME together, cleared through NCHL's connectIPS or the interbank system, and why a Nepali payment record carrying an account number alone is under-specified. This address emits a plausible run of digits and makes no claim about which bank could have issued it; a config wanting a coherent record should draw the bank from nepal.finance.bank into its own column. Cross-border, Nepal uses SWIFT/BIC plus the account number in the ordinary way of countries outside the IBAN area — and a BIC has no check digit either; it is an allocated code, not a computed one.
generator: tdc
