address: nepal.tax.vat
description: The number that fills the "VAT number" field on a Nepali invoice. Nepal DOES levy VAT — मूल्य अभिवृद्धि कर, introduced by the Value Added Tax Act 2052 (1996), a single rate of 13%, registration compulsory above a turnover threshold — but it issues NO SEPARATE VAT IDENTIFIER. A Nepali VAT-registered business is identified by its 9-digit PAN and nothing else, so this address resolves to nepal.tax.pan and returns the same nine digits rather than a second number that does not exist. There is no country prefix: nothing in Nepal is ever written NP123456789, and a validator built for the EU shape of two letters plus digits will reject every genuine Nepali VAT registration. The sequence matters in a Nepali business record: a business gets its PAN first from the Inland Revenue Department and then, if its turnover crosses the threshold or it is in a compulsorily-registered trade, registers that same PAN for VAT — so PAN-registered and VAT-registered are two STATES of one number rather than two numbers, and a Nepali company record properly carries the PAN plus a VAT-registered flag, not a second identifier column. That also means the tax number on a Nepali invoice does not tell you whether VAT was charged; only the invoice's own तिरो/मूल्य अभिवृद्धि कर line does. A config that gives every generated Nepali company a VAT registration is describing a more formalised economy than the real one — a large share of Nepali businesses are below the threshold or pay presumptive and turnover tax instead, and hold a PAN without ever being VAT-registered.
