description: A year in Bikram Sambat (विक्रम सम्बत्, abbreviated वि.सं.), WHICH IS THE CIVIL CALENDAR OF NEPAL. This is the single most important fact in this pack. BS is not a religious or ceremonial alternative to the Gregorian calendar — it is the calendar the government of Nepal dates its acts, gazettes, budgets, court judgments, tax years, school terms, citizenship certificates, passports, land deeds, contracts and newspapers in. A Nepali record whose date column is Gregorian is a record written for a foreigner. THE OFFSET IS 56 YEARS AND 8 MONTHS, NOT A FLAT NUMBER OF YEARS, and that is the thing that catches everyone: BS 2082 began in mid-April 2025 and ends in mid-April 2026, so from mid-April to 31 December the BS year is CE + 57 and from 1 January to mid-April it is CE + 56. A converter that adds a constant is wrong for a third of every year. Compare Thailand, where the Buddhist Era is a flat +543 because its new year was moved to 1 January in 1941; Nepal never moved its, and this is why the two look similar and behave completely differently. The year begins on 1 बैशाख, which falls on 13, 14 or 15 April depending on the year. WHAT THIS PACK DOES NOT MODEL, and cannot: THE MONTH LENGTHS. Bikram Sambat as Nepal keeps it is a SIDEREAL SOLAR calendar — a month runs from one solar ingress (संक्रान्ति) into a zodiac sign to the next, so its length is set by the actual apparent motion of the sun and varies from 29 to 32 days, differently in different years. There is no formula. The lengths are computed astronomically and PUBLISHED ANNUALLY by the नेपाल पञ्चाङ्ग निर्णायक समिति, the government's Calendar Determination Committee, which also has to approve every पात्रो and wall calendar printed in the country; every working BS-to-Gregorian converter in existence is therefore a hard-coded LOOKUP TABLE of month lengths, typically covering about BS 1970 to 2100, and outside that range it simply stops. So: NOTHING IN THIS PACK CONVERTS BETWEEN THE TWO CALENDARS, no file here shifts the engine's Gregorian dates, and a config needing a real BS date must bring its own table. This address emits the YEAR ONLY, BS 2040 to 2090 — that is roughly 1983 to 2033 CE — because a year number is the one part of the calendar that can be stated without knowing how long its months were. One more trap: a four-digit Nepali year cannot be told from a Western one by shape. 2082 is a BS year, 2025 is its CE twin, both are four digits, and only a label distinguishes them.
