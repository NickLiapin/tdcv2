description: The 64 municipalities (მუნიციპალიტეტი) of Georgia — the country's only level of ELECTED local government, each with a directly elected mayor and assembly, which is what makes this and not the region the unit an administrative record is filed against. Five of the 64 are self-governing cities (თვითმმართველი ქალაქი — თბილისი, ბათუმი, ქუთაისი, რუსთავი, ფოთი) and 59 are self-governing communities (თვითმმართველი თემი) named after their seat. Written bare, without the word მუნიციპალიტეტი: the official form is genitive-plus-noun (გორის მუნიციპალიტეტი), and the noun is ordinary Georgian vocabulary that belongs in the language pack, exactly as Poland's list carries dolnośląskie without województwo. Two traps. A municipality name is USUALLY but not always its seat's name — the seat of ყაზბეგი is the town of სტეფანწმინდა, and ხელვაჩაური and ჩხოროწყუ are municipalities whose seats are villages, so joining this list to georgia.geo.city on the string will silently drop rows. And the count excludes the five occupied municipalities of the Tskhinvali region (ახალგორი, ერედვი, თიღვი, ქურთა, აჟარა), which are constituted in law but have no functioning Georgian administration; the Abkhazian districts are likewise absent. Georgian has no capital letters.
