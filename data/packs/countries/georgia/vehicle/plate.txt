description: Georgian vehicle registration plate in the series introduced in 2014 — two letters, three digits, two letters, hyphenated AB-123-CD, black on white with a blue EU-style band on the left carrying GE. THE LETTERS ARE LATIN, NOT GEORGIAN, which is the one thing about this format that surprises people: Georgia writes everything else on the vehicle's papers in Mkhedruli, but the plate has to be readable at a border, so it uses the Latin alphabet and Arabic numerals — a Georgian plate is one of the few Georgian strings in this whole pack that carries no Georgian script at all. The format is NOT geographic: unlike the Polish or the old Soviet plates, nothing in a Georgian plate encodes the region, so a plate cannot be joined to georgia.geo.region and a data set that pretends otherwise is inventing a relationship. The series is issued sequentially and nationally. Special forms exist and are not generated here: SP-xxx-SP is reserved for the Security Police, embassies carry white-on-red diplomatic plates with a country code, motorcycles use a two-line AA/1234, trailers AA-123-A, and temporary transit plates since 1 April 2024 are 1234 AA. The published description of the series does not state which letters are excluded, so none are excluded here.
