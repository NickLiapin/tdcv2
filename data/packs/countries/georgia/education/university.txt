description: Higher education institutions of Georgia, in Georgian, state universities first. Two conventions in the names are worth reading before splitting on them. Georgian institutions are very often named "სახელობის" — "named after" — and the honorand's name comes FIRST, in the genitive, so the university's own name is buried in the middle of the string: ივანე ჯავახიშვილის სახელობის თბილისის სახელმწიფო უნივერსიტეტი is Tbilisi State University, and truncating it at 30 characters leaves only the man it is named after. And სახელმწიფო means state-owned; its absence marks a private institution, of which Georgia has an unusually large number for its size — the tail of this list is mostly private, and თბილისის თავისუფალი უნივერსიტეტი, კავკასიის უნივერსიტეტი and ბიზნესისა და ტექნოლოგიების უნივერსიტეტი are among the country's better-known ones. სოხუმის სახელმწიფო უნივერსიტეტი is on the list and is not in Sokhumi: it has operated in exile in Tbilisi since 1993, which is a live example of why an institution's name does not locate it. ქუთაისის საერთაშორისო უნივერსიტეტი is the newest of the large ones, opened in 2020. The generic word უნივერსიტეტი belongs to the language pack; the institutions are Georgia's, and would be the same if the country changed its language.
