description: Geographic area codes of Georgia's national numbering plan. THE CODES ARE NOT ALL THE SAME LENGTH, which is the thing to design around: თბილისი is the two-digit 32 and everywhere else is three digits, and because the plan is closed at nine national digits the subscriber block absorbs the difference — seven digits behind 32, six behind everything else. A fixed-width parse of Georgian landlines therefore mis-splits every Tbilisi number or every provincial one, depending on which width it picked. The east of the country is numbered from 3 (341 რუსთავი, 350 თელავი, 370 გორი) and the west from 4 (422 ბათუმი, 431 ქუთაისი, 415 ზუგდიდი, 493 ფოთი), a division that follows the old trunk regions rather than today's მხარე boundaries. Codes allocated to Abkhazia and the Tskhinvali region (342, 344, 347, 374 and 442-448) are constituted in the plan but are omitted here for the same reason those places are omitted from georgia.geo.city: no Georgian operator can complete a call to them. 33 is reserved for Tbilisi expansion and unassigned, so it is absent too.
