description: The Georgian personal number (პირადი ნომერი) — eleven digits, printed on the ID card and the passport, and the single identifier a Georgian is known by to every state system. It is ALSO the individual's taxpayer number: Georgia issues no separate TIN to a natural person, so the number on a tax return, a bank account, a title deed and an employment contract is this one, and a schema with distinct personalNumber and taxId columns for an individual has invented a distinction Georgia does not make. NO CHECK DIGIT IS GENERATED HERE, AND THAT IS A DECISION RATHER THAN AN OMISSION. Georgia publishes no checksum specification for the personal number; the Public Service Development Agency and the Revenue Service validate it by looking it up in the register, not by arithmetic, and there is no entry for Georgia in python-stdnum, the usual home for national-identifier algorithms. Reverse-engineered mod-11 rules for it circulate, and any of them would make this file LOOK more rigorous while quietly rejecting real numbers and accepting fake ones — worse than no checksum, because it looks verified. Two further things a generator should not assume: the number encodes NO date of birth and NO sex (unlike a PESEL, an ИИН or a codice fiscale), so it cannot be joined to an age or a gender column, and the first digits are historically a registration-office code rather than a birth region, so they do not locate the holder today. Digits only — the leading digit may be zero, so store it as text.
