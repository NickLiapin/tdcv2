---
address: slovakia.finance.iban
description: Slovak IBAN — 24 chars: SK + 2 ISO 7064 mod-97 check digits + a 4-digit NBS bank code + the 6-digit account prefix + the 10-digit account number. Three checks hold at once here, not one: the mod-97 IBAN check and BOTH of the domestic weighted mod-11 checks (prefix under 10,5,8,4,2,1 and main part under 6,3,7,9,10,5,8,4,2,1), so the BBAN is still a valid Slovak account number after the IBAN is taken apart. Verified against the published SK3112000000198742637541, whose prefix 000019 and base 8742637541 both come out as exact multiples of 11. The length matches Czechia's 24 because the underlying account format is the shared Czechoslovak one, so length alone will not tell CZ and SK apart — the country code and the bank register will. Slovakia is in the eurozone, so a SK IBAN settles in EUR and is reachable for SEPA euro credit transfers as a domestic instrument, which a CZ IBAN is not.
generator: tdc
