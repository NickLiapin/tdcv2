description: Banks and building societies operating in Slovakia, trading names, roughly by size. Line for line this list matches slovakia.finance.bankCode, so drawing the same row index from both gives a bank and its real four-digit NBS code. Stavebné sporiteľne are included because they hold ordinary retail accounts here; the last entry is the central bank. Ownership is largely Austrian, Italian, Belgian and Czech — Slovenská sporiteľňa belongs to Erste, VÚB to Intesa Sanpaolo, Tatra banka to Raiffeisen and ČSOB to KBC — so the names on the high street are Slovak while the parents are not. Defunct registrations (Sberbank Slovensko 3100, OTP Banka Slovensko 5200, ZUNO 8410) are deliberately left out.
