description: The public universities of Slovakia, official Slovak names, largest and oldest first. Univerzita Komenského was founded in 1919, immediately after Czechoslovakia came into being, and the date is the point: Slovakia had no university of its own under Hungarian rule, so the whole system is a twentieth-century creation and no Slovak CV can carry a mediaeval alma mater the way a Czech one can. Note the vocabulary: a vysoká škola is a full university too, not a college — the arts academies are degree-granting institutions whose names simply predate the word univerzita. Univerzita J. Selyeho in Komárno teaches in Hungarian, for the Hungarian minority of the southern border strip.
