address: slovakia.geo.street
description: Streets and squares that really exist in Slovak towns — Bratislava first, then Košice, Banská Bystrica, Nitra, Žilina, Trnava and Prešov. The generic Slovak street vocabulary (Lipová, Záhradná and the rest) lives in the language pack at sk.address.streetName; this file is the named ones you could point at on a map. Note the shape of a Slovak address: the street comes first and the number after it (Obchodná 12), and the word ulica is left out unless the name itself is a námestie or a trieda. Hlavná — simply "Main" — is the commonest street name in the country and is the central street of both Košice and Prešov.
locale: sk
