description: Towns and cities of Slovakia, roughly by population. The distribution is unusually top-heavy: Bratislava is about twice Košice and Košice about three times Prešov, after which the list flattens into a long tail of towns between ten and fifty thousand, so a Slovak address column drawn uniformly will look wrong unless the order is respected. Bratislava sits in the far south-west corner on the Austrian and Hungarian borders — the only capital in the world bordering two other states — while Košice, the second city, is at the opposite end near Hungary and Ukraine, and the two are further apart in character than a country this size suggests.
