description: The 79 okresy of Slovakia, ordered by kraj from west to east. Unlike Czechia, where the okres was abolished as a tier of government in 2002 and survives only as a label on courts and registers, the Slovak okres is still a live unit of state administration with its own okresný úrad. It also still governs the vehicle register, which is why the first two letters of a pre-2023 number plate name the okres. Nine of them are city districts numbered with Roman numerals — Bratislava I to V and Košice I to IV — and Košice-okolie is the ring administered separately from the city it surrounds.
