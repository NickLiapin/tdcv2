address: slovakia.tax.ico
description: Slovak company registration number (IČO) — 8 digits issued by the Statistical Office to every legal person and every sole trader. The last digit is a real mod-11 check over the first seven under weights 8,7,6,5,4,3,2: remainder 0 gives 1, remainder 1 gives 0, otherwise 11 minus the remainder. Leading zeros are part of the number and are significant — Univerzita Komenského is 00397865, not 397865. This is one identifier where Slovakia and Czechia really do share the arithmetic, both having inherited it from the Czechoslovak statistical register, and an IČO alone cannot tell you which of the two countries issued it. The tax numbers built on top of it can: a Czech DIČ is CZ plus the IČO, whereas a Slovak DIČ is an unrelated number the tax office assigns.
generator: tdc
