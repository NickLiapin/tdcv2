description: Slovak tax identification number (DIČ) — ten digits assigned by the tax office (Finančná správa) to every taxpayer, natural or legal. It carries a real check: the whole ten-digit number is divisible by 11, which makes the last digit the first nine modulo 11 (because 10 = -1 mod 11). Legal persons are overwhelmingly issued 20........, and that is the band drawn here; the third digit is constrained to 2,3,4,6,7,8,9 so the output also satisfies the stricter published validation regex used by VIES front-ends as well as the plain divisibility rule. This number is NOT the birth number. Czechia derives a natural person's DIČ from their rodné číslo, so a Czech DIČ leaks a date of birth; Slovakia does not, and slovakia.docs.rodneCislo must never be substituted here.
