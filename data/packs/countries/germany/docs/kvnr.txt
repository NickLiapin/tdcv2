description: German health insurance number (Krankenversichertennummer, KVNR) — the 10-character number printed on the electronic health card (eGK), the part that stays with a person for life whichever insurer they move to. A capital letter A-Z, eight digits and a check digit. For the check the letter becomes its place in the alphabet as two digits (A = 01, Z = 26), the resulting ten digits are weighted 1-2-1-2-…, each product is reduced to its digit sum, and the check digit is the total modulo 10 (guideline of the GKV-Spitzenverband under § 290 SGB V). Verified against the worked example A123456780 and an independent open-source validator. The 9-digit insurer code (IK) that a full 20-character number appends is not part of this value.
generator: tdc
locale: de
