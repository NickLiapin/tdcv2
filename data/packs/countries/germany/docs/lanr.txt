description: German lifelong doctor number (lebenslange Arztnummer, LANR) — the 9-digit number every doctor and psychotherapist in statutory health care bills under, issued by the regional Kassenärztliche Vereinigung. Six digits that identify the doctor for life, a check digit, and a two-digit specialty group (Fachgruppe) that changes when the doctor's field does. The check digit weights the first six digits 4-9-4-9-4-9 and is 10 minus the sum modulo 10, with 10 written as 0. The specialty is drawn from the assigned groups 01-69 — 01-03 family doctors, 28 cardiology, 34 and 40 paediatrics, 62 radiology, 68 psychological psychotherapists and so on; pin one with specialty="28". The pseudo numbers used for an unknown or non-contract doctor (3333333, 4444444, 555555x, 9999999) are never drawn. Verified against those pseudo numbers, which are built to pass the same check, and an independent open-source implementation.
