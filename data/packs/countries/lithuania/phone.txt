description: Lithuanian mobile number in E.164 form (+370 6XXXXXXX). The national significant number is always 8 digits; the leading digit sorts the type, and 6 is the whole mobile range. The domestic trunk prefix changed from 8 to 0 during 2024-2025 but never appears in E.164.
