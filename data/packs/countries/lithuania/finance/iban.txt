address: lithuania.finance.iban
description: Lithuanian IBAN — 20 chars, the shortest in the Baltics: LT + 2 ISO 7064 check digits + a 5-digit bank identifier + an 11-digit account number. The bank identifiers are real, taken from the Bank of Lithuania's published register, so a generated IBAN routes to a bank that exists. Lithuania joined the euro on 1 January 2015, last of the three Baltic states, so an IBAN generated here is a euro account.
