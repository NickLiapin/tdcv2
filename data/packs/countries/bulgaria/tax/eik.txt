description: Bulgarian ЕИК (единен идентификационен код) — the 9-digit company number issued by the Търговски регистър, formerly БУЛСТАТ. The ninth digit is a two-stage weighted check over the first eight: weights 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8 modulo 11, and only when that yields 10 a second pass with weights 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10 modulo 11, with a second 10 collapsing to 0. Both stages are implemented here.
