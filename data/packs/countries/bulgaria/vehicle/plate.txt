description: Bulgarian number plate — a one- or two-letter област code, four digits, then a two-letter series. Since 1992 only the twelve Cyrillic letters that are also Latin glyphs are used (А В Е К М Н О Р С Т У Х), so a Bulgarian plate reads identically to a driver who knows neither alphabet — a deliberate design decision, not a coincidence. The област codes are not always the first letter of the province: Р is Русе while Пловдив is РВ, ЕН is Плевен, М is Монтана from its old name Михайловград, and ТХ is Добрич from Толбухин. София-град has outgrown one code and now uses С, СА and СВ.
