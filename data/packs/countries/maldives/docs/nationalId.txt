address: maldives.docs.nationalId
description: The Maldivian national identity number — one Latin letter followed by six digits, A123456, printed on the national identity card issued by the Department of National Registration under the Civil Registration and Issuance of Birth Certificates and National Identity Cards Act (Law 22/2022). It is the key everything else in the country hangs off: a bank account, a SIM, a passport application, a school enrolment, a vote, a pension record and an eFaas digital identity are all keyed on it. THERE IS NO PUBLISHED CHECK DIGIT AND THIS GENERATOR DOES NOT INVENT ONE. Seven characters, one of them a letter: there is no room for a modulus-11 remainder and the Department has never published a validation rule, so the six digits are drawn and that is the whole of it. Third-party tax-data sites describe the Maldivian identity number as "seven digits, the last a control digit" — that description is not of this number at all, it is of the Maldives Inland Revenue Authority's seven-digit business partner number (see maldives.tax.tin), and applying it here rejects every real Maldivian ID because a real one starts with a letter. Inventing a plausible checksum would have made this generator look rigorous while making it disagree with every card in the country, so nothing is computed. The letter is a SERIES marker, not an initial and not a sex or region code: every identity number in circulation begins with A, which gives a million numbers against a citizen population of about 383,000, so the A series has not been exhausted and no second series letter has been observed. This generator therefore emits A. Note the number carries NO date of birth and NO sex — unlike its neighbours, Sri Lanka's NIC and India's older schemes, a Maldivian ID number is opaque, so it discloses nothing on its own and a leaked ID number is not a leaked birthday. Written as one unbroken token, no space and no hyphen after the letter.
generator: tdc
