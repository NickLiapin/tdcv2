This is the form on an island council sign, in the Dhivehi census tables and on a national identity card, and it is the form that settles the romanisation arguments: the island the census romanises Dhihdhoo and the council romanises Dhidhdhoo is one spelling in Thaana. Thaana is right-to-left and lives in Unicode U+0780-U+07BF; it is neither Arabic nor Sinhala, and none of these values contain a codepoint from either of those blocks. Note that a Dhivehi vowel is a diacritic (fili) over or under the consonant, so a name that looks four characters wide can be eight codepoints, and truncating a Thaana string by codepoint count cuts vowels off their consonants. Source: Maldives Bureau of Statistics, Census 2022.
