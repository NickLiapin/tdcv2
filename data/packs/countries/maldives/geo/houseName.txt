address: maldives.geo.houseName
description: Real house names of Malé City, every one of them taken from the Malé postcode register. THIS IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT FACT ABOUT A MALDIVIAN ADDRESS: buildings have names, not numbers. There is no house number, no building number and no number on the street; a Malé address is the house name, the ward and the postcode, and the street is often left out altogether because the house name is what the post office indexes. The names are Dhivehi words and compounds — Alimasge (ruby house), Aanooraanee (radiant), Aazamaan (the age) — and a great many end in -ge, which simply means house, so Fenfiyaazuge is "the house Fenfiyaazu". Names with English or Arabic words in them (Villa, Manzil, Palace, Manzilge) are common in newer buildings and are equally real. Three consequences for anyone modelling Maldivian addresses. The house name is a free text field of unbounded shape, not a number, so a schema with an integer house_number cannot hold a Maldivian address at all. House names are NOT unique across the country — they are unique only within a ward — so the key is house name plus ward plus postcode. And the same name recurs with a suffix on the parts of a subdivided plot (Irumatheebai, the west side; Hulhangubai, the south side), which is a Maldivian inheritance practice showing through in the address data.
locale: dv-latn
