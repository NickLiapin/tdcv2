address: maldives.geo.houseName
description: Real house names of Malé City. THIS IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT FACT ABOUT A MALDIVIAN ADDRESS: buildings have names, not numbers. There is no house number, no building number and no number on the street; a Malé address is the house name, the ward and the postcode, and the street is often left out altogether because the house name is the identifier the post office indexes. The names are Dhivehi words and compounds — Alimasge (ruby house), Aanooraanee (radiant), Aazamaan (the age) — and a great many end in -ge, which simply means house, so Fenfiyaazuge is "the house Fenfiyaazu". Names with English words in them (Villa, Manzil, Palace) are common in newer buildings and are equally real. Two consequences for anyone modelling Maldivian addresses. First, the house name is a free text field of unbounded shape, not a number, and a schema with an integer house_number cannot hold a Maldivian address at all. Second, house names are NOT unique across the country — they are unique only within a ward — so the key is house name plus ward plus postcode. Values here are a sample taken from the Malé postcode register, spread across the alphabet of streets.
