address: maldives.geo.atollIso
description: The ISO 3166-2:MV subdivision codes of the twenty administrative atolls. They are NOT in geographic order and they are not in code-letter order: ISO assigned them from an older numbering and then patched it, so Seenu in the far south is MV-01 and Haa Alif in the far north is MV-07, and Alif Dhaalu is MV-00 — a zero code, which is legal in ISO 3166-2 and which trips over every parser that strips leading zeros or treats the numeric part as an integer. MV-00 is the same "the southern half of Ari is the odd one out" fact that the postcode zones record, and the opposite of what the boat letter codes say. Malé City is MV-MLE, alphabetic rather than numeric, and is not in this list because it is not an atoll. Order matches maldives.geo.atoll.
