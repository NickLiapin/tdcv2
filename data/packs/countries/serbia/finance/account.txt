address: serbia.finance.account
description: Serbian domestic account number (текући рачун) — 18 digits, written with two dashes as 160-0000000123456-78: a 3-digit bank code, a 13-digit account and 2 control digits. This is the number Serbs actually quote to each other; the IBAN is that same BBAN with RS and two more check digits in front of it (serbia.finance.iban). The control digits are ISO 7064 MOD 97-10 over the 16 preceding digits, so the whole 18-digit string leaves remainder 1 mod 97 — the arithmetic is verifiable without touching the IBAN layer at all, and this generator computes it. In everyday use the middle block is written without its leading zeros — 160-123456-78 for the same account — and banks fill them back in; the electronic form is always the full eighteen digits, which is the form generated here. Verified against the National Bank of Serbia's published register of bank giro accounts: all 21 of them pass MOD-97, among them Banca Intesa 908-16001-87, NLB Komercijalna banka 908-20501-70 and UniCredit Bank Srbija 908-17001-94.
generator: tdc
