description: Serbian EBS (евиденцијски број странца), the number a foreign resident carries where a citizen carries a JMBG — 13 digits with the JMBG's own structure and the JMBG's own mod-11 check. Article 7 of the Zakon o strancima is explicit about both halves of it: "Struktura evidencijskog broja stranca se određuje shodno odredbama Zakona o jedinstvenom matičnom broju građana, na način da se stalno nastanjenom strancu i strancu kome je odobren azil u Republici Srbiji određuje broj registra 06, a ostalim kategorijama stranaca broj registra 66." So the two register codes are the whole difference: 06 for a permanently settled foreigner or an asylum holder, 66 for every other category — a residence permit, a work permit, a temporary stay. Everything else is the JMBG: DDMMGGG, the same three-digit serial with the same sex split at 500, the same weights 2-7 from the right, the same rule that remainder 1 is never issued. THIS is the number that is current for foreigners and the JMBG that is legacy for them; for Serbian citizens it is the other way round and the JMBG is untouched — see serbia.docs.jmbg. The two blocks are not arbitrary: they sit inside the Yugoslav federal ranges the JMBG reserved for people without a republican citizenship (00-09) and for temporary residence (60-69), which is why an EBS is well-formed as a JMBG and still cannot collide with one. It is issued by the regional police administration of the Ministry of the Interior, not by the Tax Administration, and it is what a foreigner quotes to open a bank account or sign an employment contract.
