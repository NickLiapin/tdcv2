address: south_korea.tax.businessNumber
description: South Korean business registration number (사업자등록번호) — 10 digits laid out XXX-XX-XXXXX with its own check digit, and the one place where the arithmetic has a genuine quirk. The first three digits are the issuing tax office, the middle two the taxpayer class (01-79 individual general taxpayer, 80 apartment management office, 81 and 86-88 for-profit corporation head office, 82 non-profit, 83 state or local authority, 84 foreign corporation, 85 for-profit corporation branch, 89 unincorporated religious body, 90-99 individual tax-exempt), then a five-digit serial whose last digit is the check. Digits 1-9 carry weights 1,3,7,1,3,7,1,3,5 — but the ninth weight is applied twice over: the product d9 x 5 is added to the running sum AND its tens digit, floor(d9 x 5 / 10), is added again. Then check = (10 - total mod 10) mod 10. Miss the second term and eight numbers in ten still validate, which is exactly why it has to be written down. Emitted as bare digits; Korea always prints the two hyphens. The tax-office block is structural — real codes run roughly 101 to 621 and are grouped by region, but the roster is not modelled.
generator: tdc
