address: south_korea.vehicle.plate
description: South Korean number plate in the current form — three digits, one Hangul syllable, four digits (123가4567). The leading block went from two digits to three in September 2019 because the two-digit space was exhausted; both are legal and on the road, but only new registrations get three, so that is what is generated. The Hangul syllable is the use class, not a random letter — the 32 syllables from 가 to 주 are private vehicles, 아 바 사 자 are commercial, and 하 허 호 are rental cars, which is how a hire car is spotted at a glance in Korea. Regional plates carrying a province name in front (서울 12가 3456) were abolished for private cars in 2004.
