description: South Korean fixed line in E.164 form (+82 + area code without its 0 + subscriber number). Unlike most numbering plans the Korean national significant number is not a fixed length — Seoul's one-digit code 2 carries an 8-digit subscriber number, and the two-digit provincial codes normally carry 7. Eight-digit subscriber numbers do occur behind two-digit codes as well, particularly on newer business ranges; only the common form is generated.
