address: south_korea.geo.roadName
description: Real South Korean road names (도로명). These model the ROAD-NAME address system, which became the sole legal address on 1 January 2014 and replaced the older 지번 lot-number system — an address is now 도로명 + building number (세종대로 110), not 동 + lot number. The old lot numbers still exist in the land registry and are still what people say out loud, but they are not what the post office routes on, and nothing here models them. The suffix carries the road's rank: 대로 for a road of eight lanes or more, 로 for two to seven, 길 for anything smaller.
locale: ko
