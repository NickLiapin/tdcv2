description: South Korean driving licence number — 12 digits laid out AA-BB-CCCCCC-DE, where AA is the region that first issued it (11 to 26 and 28), BB the year of first issue, CCCCCC a serial, D a check digit and E the reissue count. STRUCTURAL ONLY. D is a real check digit on a real licence, but the algorithm behind it has never been published, so it is drawn here rather than computed and a generated number will not survive a genuine check. Licences issued before May 2014 printed the region as a Korean place name instead of the two digits, and a shorter 10-digit form without the region block is still in circulation.
