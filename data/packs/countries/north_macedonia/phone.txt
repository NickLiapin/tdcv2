address: north_macedonia.phone
description: North Macedonian mobile number in E.164 form — +389 then a two-digit network code beginning 7 and six subscriber digits, eight national digits in all. The whole mobile plan sits under 7 with no exceptions, so a Macedonian mobile is 07X nationally and +3897X internationally, and the trunk zero is dropped in the international form exactly as it is in Serbia and Croatia. The blocks: 070, 071 and 072 are Македонски Телеком (the old MobiMak, then T-Mobile, still the largest); 075, 076, 077 and 078 are А1 Македонија, the merged one.Vip network; 073 and 079 are MTEL/GreenMobile and 074 is Телекабел, all three small resellers. This generator draws the seven codes of the two real networks and leaves out 073, 074 and 079, which together hold a very small share — including them at one-tenth each would put a third of the country's numbers on operators that do not have a third of the country's subscribers. Number portability has been in force since 2008, so the block no longer proves the operator, but unlike Britain or the Netherlands the block still tells you which network the number was ISSUED on, which is what most Macedonian data actually records. LANDLINES ARE NOT GENERATED HERE and their plan is worth knowing anyway, because it is unusually lopsided: Скопје is 02, the ONLY single-digit area code in the country, and everywhere else is three digits — 031 Куманово, 032 Штип, 033 Кочани, 034 Струмица, 042 Гостивар, 043 Велес, 044 Тетово, 045 Кичево, 046 Охрид, 047 Битола, 048 Прилеп — so a Macedonian geographic number is 02 plus seven digits in the capital and 0XX plus six anywhere else, and code-plus-fixed-length parsing breaks on it. See north_macedonia.phoneAreaCode for the geographic codes as a drawable list. The emergency numbers are not the European 112 alone: 192 police, 193 fire, 194 ambulance and 195 crisis management all still answer beside it.
