address: singapore.geo.streetName
description: Real Singapore road names, written the way the signs write them. Two things a schema should know before using this list. First, a large share of Singapore's road names are NUMBERED rather than named - Ang Mo Kio Avenue 3, Tampines Street 11, Jurong West Street 41, Toa Payoh Lorong 4 - because the HDB towns were laid out as grids and the roads inside them are numbered off the town name, so the "street" of an address often carries the district name inside it. Second, the generic word is not always English: "Jalan" is road and "Lorong" is lane (Malay), "Kampong" is village, and these are part of the proper name, not a translatable suffix. Do not run a Singapore street name through a "detect the street-type word and strip it" step - it will take Jalan Besar down to Besar, which is not a place. The names themselves stay put whatever language you write the rest of the record in, which is why they are country data and not language data; the WORDS for street, road and avenue live in the language packs.
locale: en
