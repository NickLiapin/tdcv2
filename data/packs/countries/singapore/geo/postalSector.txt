description: Every two-digit postal SECTOR Singapore uses — the first two digits of a six-digit postal code. 01 to 82 with one hole: 74 is not assigned. The sector is the only geographic part of a Singapore postal code, and it maps onto the 28 postal districts property listings still quote as D1, D9, D15 and so on: D1 is sectors 01-06 (Raffles Place, Marina, Downtown), D9 is 22-23 (Orchard, River Valley), D10 is 24-27 (Tanglin, Holland, Bukit Timah), D15 is 42-45 (Katong, Joo Chiat), D19 is 53-55 and 82 (Serangoon Garden, Hougang, Punggol), D28 is 79-80 (Seletar). Note D17 (49-50 and 81) and D19 (53-55 and 82) are not contiguous - 81 and 82 were added later and bolted onto existing districts, which is why a "sector range per district" rule breaks on those two. Feeds singapore.geo.postalCode.
