---
address: turkey.finance.bankCode
description: The 5-digit bank code that occupies positions 5-9 of a Turkish IBAN, index-aligned with turkey.finance.bank. It is the bank's 4-digit EFT code with a leading zero — Ziraat's EFT code 0010 appears in the IBAN as 00010 — so an IBAN starting TR__00064 is an İş Bankası account and TR__00062 a Garanti BBVA one. Codes in the 002xx block belong to the katılım (participation) banks, which were licensed later than the deposit banks and were given their own range.
