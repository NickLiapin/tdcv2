address: turkey.tax.vkn
description: Vergi Kimlik No — the 10-digit Turkish tax number issued by the Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı to legal persons (companies, partnerships, associations). Natural persons do not get one: since 1 July 2006 their tax number IS their T.C. Kimlik No, so a Turkish invoice carries a 10-digit VKN for a company and an 11-digit TCKN for a sole trader, and any field that must hold both is 11 wide. The check digit is unusual — it is not a weighted sum of the digits themselves. Each of the first nine digits is first TRANSFORMED, v_i = (d_i + (10 − i)) mod 10, and only then weighted by 2^(10−i) mod 9; a transformed digit of 0 contributes 0, while one of 9 contributes 9 rather than the 0 the modulo would give. The check is (10 − sum mod 10) mod 10.
generator: tdc
