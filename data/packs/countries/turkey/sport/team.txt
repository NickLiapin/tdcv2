description: Turkish football clubs — Süper Lig and 1. Lig, plus long-established clubs currently in the lower divisions. The naming convention is its own: most clubs are "place + spor" written as one word (Trabzonspor, Konyaspor, Antalyaspor), so the club name is not a phrase and must not be split on a space the way a Polish or Spanish club name can be. The big four Istanbul and Trabzon clubs are the exception, and the newer franchises take a sponsor prefix instead (Çaykur Rizespor, MKE Ankaragücü). Turkish case: Fenerbahçe, Beşiktaş, Şanlıurfaspor and İstanbulspor all lose letters to an ASCII upper().
