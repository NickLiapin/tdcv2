address: turkey.geo.provinceCode
description: The two-digit province code (il kodu), 01–81, index-aligned with turkey.geo.province. In Türkiye this one number does three jobs, which is why it is worth shipping as an aligned pair rather than three unrelated generators — it is the registration-plate prefix (34 is İstanbul on every plate in the city), the first two digits of every postal code in the province, and the province's statistical code. Keep the leading zero: the value is a string, and 06 is Ankara while 6 is nothing.
