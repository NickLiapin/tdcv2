address: turkey.geo.region
description: The seven geographical regions (coğrafi bölge) of Türkiye, fixed at the 1941 Geography Congress and taught in every school. They are NOT an administrative tier — nothing is governed at this level and no province code, postal code or plate derives from them; the only first-level administrative division is the province (turkey.geo.province). A record that needs a real statistical grouping wants the 12 NUTS-1 İBBS regions instead, which are not the same seven and are not in this pack. Turkish case: İç Anadolu upper-cases to İÇ ANADOLU, not IC ANADOLU.
locale: tr
