---
address: turkey.geo.district
description: Districts (ilçe) of Türkiye, the second-level division and the part of an address that sits between the street and the province — a Turkish address reads mahalle, sokak, ilçe, il, so a record without an ilçe is not a Turkish address. These are drawn from across the whole country and are NOT tied to the province drawn beside them: pairing this address with turkey.geo.province in the same record will produce combinations such as "Kadıköy / Erzurum" that do not exist. There is no aligned district-to-province file in this pack, so a config that needs the pair to be true has to fix the province and choose the district itself. Turkish case: Şişli, Çankaya and İnegöl all lose a letter to an ASCII upper().
locale: tr
