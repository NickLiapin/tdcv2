address: turkey.geo.province
description: The 81 provinces (il) of Türkiye, the country's only first-level administrative division, listed in province-code order and index-aligned with turkey.geo.provinceCode — the two files must stay in the same order or the code stops naming the province. The order is alphabetical for the 67 provinces that had the codes in 1962 (Adana 01 … Zonguldak 67), then chronological for the 14 created since (Aksaray 68 … Düzce 81), so the tail does not sort. Three entries look out of alphabetical place because the province was renamed after the codes were fixed: 33 was İçel until 2002 (now Mersin), 46 was Maraş until 1973 (now Kahramanmaraş), 63 was Urfa until 1984 (now Şanlıurfa). Turkish case is not the ASCII rule — i uppercases to İ and I lowercases to ı — so an invariant upper() turns İstanbul into ISTANBUL and Isparta into isparta, both wrong; sort and compare these values with a Turkish collator.
locale: tr
