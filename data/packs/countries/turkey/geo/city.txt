address: turkey.geo.city
description: Cities of Türkiye, roughly ordered by population. The 81 province capitals come first, then large cities that are not capitals (İskenderun, Tarsus, Gebze, Alanya…), which matter here because Türkiye's metropolitan-municipality law makes the province and its capital coextensive in 30 provinces and a naive "city = province" mapping then loses them. Three capitals do not carry their province's name and are listed under the city name, not the province's: Kocaeli's capital is İzmit, Hatay's is Antakya, Sakarya's is Adapazarı. Turkish case: i uppercases to İ and I lowercases to ı, so an invariant upper() gives ISTANBUL for İstanbul and an invariant lower() gives isparta for Isparta — both wrong, and both silently.
locale: tr
