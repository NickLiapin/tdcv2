address: turkey.docs.tckn
description: T.C. Kimlik No — the 11-digit Turkish national identity number, issued once per citizen by the MERNİS registry and never reused. Two of the eleven digits are checks, and the rule is not a Luhn or a mod-11: the 10th digit is ((d1+d3+d5+d7+d9) × 7 − (d2+d4+d6+d8)) mod 10, an odd/even split weighted 7 against −1, and the 11th is simply (d1+…+d10) mod 10. The first digit is never 0, so the number is a string and not an integer. Unlike a PESEL or a CNP the body carries nothing — no birth date, no sex, no province — it is a pure serial, so nothing about the holder can be read back out of it. Since 1 July 2006 this number is also the tax number of every Turkish natural person (see turkey.tax.vkn).
generator: tdc
