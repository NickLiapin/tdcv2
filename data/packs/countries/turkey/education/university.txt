Two things a neighbour's pack would not warn about: Boğaziçi, ODTÜ, İTÜ and Bilkent teach in English while carrying Turkish names, so the name and the language of instruction do not track each other; and a Turkish university can be closed by decree, as İstanbul Şehir Üniversitesi was in 2020 — it is deliberately absent here. Turkish case: an ASCII upper() breaks İstanbul, İzmir, Işık and Bilgi's peers alike.
