address: kenya.phone
description: Kenyan mobile number in E.164 form — +254 followed by nine digits, of which the first three are the operator prefix. Written nationally the same number starts 0 instead of +254, so +254722123456 is dialled 0722123456; the leading 0 is a trunk prefix and is dropped, never both. The prefixes here are the real allocation and not a range: Safaricom holds 070x, 071x, 072x, 0740-0746, 0748, 0757-0759, 0768, 0769, 079x and the newer 0110-0115; Airtel holds 0730-0739, 0750-0756, 0762, 0785-0789 and 0100-0102; Telkom holds 0770-0779; Equitel (Finserve, Equity Bank's MVNO) holds 0763-0766; Jamii Telecom (Faiba) holds 0747. The 01xx block was opened by the Communications Authority because the 07xx block ran out. Drawing uniformly over these hundred prefixes puts about 59% of numbers on Safaricom, which lands close to its real subscriber share — and that matters more here than in most countries, because a Kenyan phone number is also a payment address: the number IS the M-Pesa account.
