description: KRA PIN — the Kenya Revenue Authority Personal Identification Number, 11 characters — one leading letter, nine digits, one trailing letter. The leading letter is the taxpayer class and is real information, not decoration: A for a natural person, P for a non-individual (company, partnership, society, trust). The nine digits are issued by iTax. THE TRAILING LETTER IS A CHECK CHARACTER, BUT KRA HAS NEVER PUBLISHED HOW IT IS COMPUTED, so this file draws it uniformly from A-Z instead of computing it, and a PIN generated here will not survive the iTax PIN Checker. That is a deliberate refusal, not an omission. It was tested rather than assumed: 25 real company PINs published in Kenyan business directories (P000619379O, P000609147B, P000595101M, P051126254X, P051129820X and twenty more) were fitted to the model letter_index = (c + sum of w_i * digit_i) mod 26 by Gaussian elimination over GF(2) and GF(13), the two prime factors of 26. NO consistent solution exists modulo either factor, so the check letter is not a linear mod-26 function of the nine digits under the natural A=0..Z=25 mapping — which rules out the whole weighted-sum family that PESEL, NIP, the ABA routing number and the Steuer-ID belong to. The three rules most often asserted online were tested directly and all fail: the 9-digit value mod 26 leaves 17 different offsets across those 25 PINs, the digit sum mod 26 leaves 15, and mod 23 leaves 17; a working rule would leave exactly one. Digit blocks are real: legacy PINs sit in 000xxxxxx and iTax-era ones in 05xxxxxxx, so the digits are drawn from those two blocks rather than uniformly. The KRA PIN is also Kenya's VAT number — see kenya.tax.vat.
