description: Clubs of the Kenya Cup, the national fifteen-a-side rugby championship. Rugby matters more in Kenya than a glance at the football table suggests — the Kenya Sevens side has been a World Series regular and the Safari Sevens is a fixture on the African calendar — and the club game is concentrated in Nairobi, western Kenya and Nakuru rather than spread nationally. As in football, several sides are institutional: Kabras Sugar is a sugar miller's team, KCB a bank's, Menengai Oilers an oil refiner's, Blak Blad is Kenyatta University and Strathmore Leos is Strathmore University.
