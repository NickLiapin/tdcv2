description: The county headquarters (the seat of each county government), index-aligned with kenya.geo.county and kenya.geo.countyCode. Several are not the largest town in the county and several are not named after it — Tana River is governed from Hola, Makueni from Wote, Tharaka-Nithi from Kathwana, Nyandarua from Ol Kalou, Kirinyaga from Kerugoya, Turkana from Lodwar, West Pokot from Kapenguria, Samburu from Maralal, Trans-Nzoia from Kitale, Uasin Gishu from Eldoret, Elgeyo-Marakwet from Iten, Nandi from Kapsabet, Baringo from Kabarnet, Laikipia from Rumuruti, Vihiga from Mbale and Taita-Taveta from Mwatate. Guessing the headquarters from the county name is therefore wrong about a third of the time, which is why this is a list and not a rule.
