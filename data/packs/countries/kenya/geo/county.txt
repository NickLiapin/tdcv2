description: The 47 counties of Kenya, in official county-code order 001-047. Counties are the first-level division and the ONLY one — they were created by the 2010 Constitution and took office after the March 2013 general election, replacing the eight colonial-era provinces (Central, Coast, Eastern, Nairobi, North Eastern, Nyanza, Rift Valley, Western) which no longer exist as units of government. A Kenyan address, a voter roll or a devolved budget is organised by county, never by province, so a pack that shipped the eight provinces would be modelling a country that ended in 2013. Index-aligned with kenya.geo.countyCode and kenya.geo.countyCapital. Nairobi is formally Nairobi City County.
