address: kenya.geo.countyCode
description: Official Kenyan county codes, 001-047, zero-padded to three digits. Index-aligned with kenya.geo.county — 001 is Mombasa, 047 is Nairobi. The codes were assigned by the 2010 Constitution's First Schedule and run roughly coast-inland then west, so they are a fixed enumeration and not derivable from the county name. They appear on vehicle logbooks, IEBC results sheets and Treasury revenue-allocation tables. They are NOT the code on a number plate — Kenyan plates carry no regional code at all, see kenya.vehicle.plate.
