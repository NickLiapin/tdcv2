description: Kenyan cities and principal towns, roughly ordered by population. Only five places hold city charters — Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru (2021) and Eldoret (2024); everything below them is a municipality or town, so "city" here is the pack's ordinary sense of a populated place, not the Kenyan legal status. The list is Nairobi-heavy at the top because the country is: greater Nairobi and its satellite towns (Ruiru, Kikuyu, Kitengela, Ongata Rongai, Juja, Ruaka) hold a large share of the urban population.
