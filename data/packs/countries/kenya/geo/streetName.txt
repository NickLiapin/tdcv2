address: kenya.geo.streetName
description: Real Kenyan road names, mostly Nairobi and Mombasa, given in full because a Kenyan road name is not a name plus a separable type word — "Ngong Road", "Uhuru Highway" and "Waiyaki Way" each carry their own generic and are not interchangeable, so slicing them the way usa.geo.streetNamed does would produce roads nobody could direct you to. The set mixes English generics (Avenue, Road, Street, Way, Drive, Highway, Lane) with Swahili and Kenyan proper nouns (Uhuru = freedom, Harambee = pulling together, Biashara = business, Jogoo = cockerel), which is exactly how Nairobi street signs read. Note that a Kenyan postal address is normally a P.O. Box and a postal code, not a street and a house number, so a street name here is a locator rather than a delivery address.
locale: en
