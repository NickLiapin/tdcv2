description: Chartered universities in Kenya — the 36 public ones first, in charter order, then the private chartered ones. All are accredited by the Commission for University Education; "chartered" is the operative word, since Kenya also has a tier of university colleges and institutions holding letters of interim authority that are not full universities and are not listed. The public list is heavily devolved by design: after 2013 most counties acquired a university, which is why Garissa, Taita Taveta, Tharaka, Bomet, Alupe and Tom Mboya sit alongside Nairobi and Kenyatta. Several private ones are church foundations (Baraton is Adventist, the Catholic University of Eastern Africa, Scott Christian, St Paul's, Tangaza) and Aga Khan and Amref are health-sciences institutions.
