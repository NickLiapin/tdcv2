description: Kenyan bank account number — commonly 10 to 13 digits, some banks longer. STRUCTURAL ONLY, and for two separate reasons. First, KENYA HAS NO IBAN: it is not in the IBAN registry, has never adopted the scheme, and there is no KE.. account string to compute a MOD-97 over. Anyone reaching for kenya.finance.iban is looking for something that does not exist, and this pack deliberately does not ship one. A domestic transfer is addressed by bank code + branch code + account number (see kenya.finance.sortCode), an international one by SWIFT/BIC + account number. Second, there is no national account-number standard: each bank sets its own length and its own internal check rule and publishes neither, so nothing generated here can be validated against anything. Balances are in Kenyan shillings (KES); the currency has no subdivision in practical use, cents having been withdrawn.
