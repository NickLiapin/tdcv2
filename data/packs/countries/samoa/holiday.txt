description: Samoan public holidays. Independence from New Zealand administration is 1 January 1962 — Samoa was the first Pacific island country to become independent, and it celebrates on 1 June rather than on the anniversary itself. White Sunday in October is a children's holiday of a kind found nowhere else: children lead the church service and are served first at the family meal.
