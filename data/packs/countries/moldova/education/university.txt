description: Universities and academies of Moldova in their official Romanian names. Nearly all of them are in Chișinău; Bălți, Comrat, Cahul and Taraclia hold the only ones outside it, and that concentration is real rather than an omission. Comrat State University teaches in Russian, Gagauz and Romanian and is the university of the Gagauz autonomy; the Taraclia branch of the Bulgarian University of Ruse serves the Bulgarian minority in the south. The Academy of Sciences of Moldova is not listed because it is a research academy, not a degree-granting university. Institutions on the left bank operate under authorities Moldova does not recognise and are not listed.
