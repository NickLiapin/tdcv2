address: moldova.vehicle.plate
description: Moldovan registration plate in the format introduced in 2015 — three letters and three digits, written ABC 123, on a white plate with a blue band carrying the Moldovan flag and the code MD rather than the EU circle of stars. THE 2015 CHANGE IS THE THING TO GET RIGHT: the format before it carried the place of registration in its leading letters — C Chișinău, BL Bălți, CH Cahul, CT Cantemir, AN Anenii Noi, in the shape C DL 974 or CH AW 352 — and the 2015 series threw that away. A plate issued today tells you nothing about where its keeper lives, and any code that maps Moldovan plate prefixes to raions is reading a system that stopped in 2015. Pre-2015 plates remain valid until the vehicle is re-registered, so both formats are on the road; only the current one is generated. Letters are Latin across the full alphabet (a real example is VYH 698) and there is no reserved or excluded letter I could source, so none is excluded here. Vehicles registered on the left bank carry plates issued by the authorities there, which Moldova does not recognise and which are not generated by this file; left-bank keepers who want to drive abroad take a neutral Moldovan plate in this same format.
