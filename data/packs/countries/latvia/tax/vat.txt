description: Latvian PVN (pievienotās vērtības nodokļa) registration number — LV plus the payer's 11-digit number. Rewritten from the stub that shipped here, which declared "no checksum" and drew eleven free digits; that is not true of Latvia. For a legal entity the eleven digits are its vienotais reģistrācijas numurs and carry a real check — weights 9,1,4,8,3,10,2,5,7,6,1 over all eleven, weighted sum 3 modulo 11 — so a random eleven-digit LV VAT number is rejected by any validator about ten times out of eleven. Verified against LV40003521600, and LV40003521601 is refused. A sole trader registers under their own personas kods instead, which is a different arithmetic; latvia.docs.personasKods produces that shape.
generator: tdc
