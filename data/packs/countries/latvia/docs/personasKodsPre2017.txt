---
address: latvia.docs.personasKodsPre2017
description: Latvian personas kods, the date-bearing form issued up to 30 June 2017 — DDMMYY-CNNNZ. C is the century (0 = 1800s, 1 = 1900s, 2 = 2000s), NNN a birth-order serial, Z the check digit. Use this address when the code must agree with a birth date elsewhere in the record; latvia.docs.personasKods mixes this form with the post-2017 one. Nobody was reissued in 2017, so this remains the form most living Latvians hold. It encodes no sex — that is where Latvia parts company with Estonia's isikukood and Lithuania's asmens kodas, whose leading digit is a sex-and-century marker. Dates run 1900-01-01..2017-06-30, so the 1800s marker 0 never fires. Check digit verified against the published number 161175-19997.
generator: tdc
locale: lv
---
<sequence name="dob"><gen type="date" from="1900-01-01" to="2017-06-30" format="YYYYMMDD"/></sequence>
<sequence name="serial"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{3}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="kods"><compute>
  <let name="year"><to_number><slice from="0" to="4"><field name="dob"/></slice></to_number></let>
  <let name="ddmmyy"><concat>
    <slice from="6" to="8"><field name="dob"/></slice>
    <slice from="4" to="6"><field name="dob"/></slice>
    <slice from="2" to="4"><field name="dob"/></slice>
  </concat></let>
  <let name="century">
    <choose>
      <when><test><greater_than><use name="year"/><int v="1999"/></greater_than></test><then><str v="2"/></then></when>
      <otherwise><str v="1"/></otherwise>
    </choose>
  </let>
  <let name="base"><concat><use name="ddmmyy"/><use name="century"/><field name="serial"/></concat></let>
  <!-- Z = (1101 - SUM(w*d)) mod 11 mod 10, w = 1,6,3,7,9,10,5,8,4,2. -->
  <let name="sum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="base"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="1,6,3,7,9,10,5,8,4,2"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><mod><mod><subtract><int v="1101"/><use name="sum"/></subtract><int v="11"/></mod><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <result><concat>
    <use name="ddmmyy"/>
    <str v="-"/>
    <use name="century"/>
    <field name="serial"/>
    <use name="check"/>
  </concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
