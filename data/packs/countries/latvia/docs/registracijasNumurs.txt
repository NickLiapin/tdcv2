description: Latvian vienotais reģistrācijas numurs — the 11-digit number the Uzņēmumu reģistrs gives a legal entity, and the same number the Valsts ieņēmumu dienests uses as the taxpayer number, so Latvia has no separate company number and tax number to reconcile. It carries a real check: with weights 9,1,4,8,3,10,2,5,7,6,1 over ALL eleven digits the weighted sum must be 3 modulo 11, and the roughly one draw in eleven that has no single-digit solution is refused by the predicate below rather than fudged into a zero. Only the leading digit is modelled beyond that. 4, 5 and 9 are what real numbers start with — 4 and 5 for commercial entities, 9 for state bodies, associations and foundations — and this is not guesswork: all ten banks and all five foreign-bank branches in Latvijas Banka's register lead with 4 or 5, while Latvijas Banka itself is 90000158236 and Latvijas Biozinātņu un tehnoloģiju universitāte is 90000041898. No normative act publishes how the interior digits are formed, so they are drawn flat rather than invented. Verified against seventeen real published numbers, every one accepted by the rule above: the fifteen credit institutions in Latvijas Banka's Finanšu tirgus dalībnieku reģistrs (40003551060 Citadele's peer BluOr, 40103303559 Citadele, 40203448611 INDEXO, 40003194988 Industra, 50103189561 Magnetiq, 40003563375 Reģionālā investīciju banka, 40003074497 Rietumu, 40003151743 SEB, 40003043232 Signet, 40003074764 Swedbank, 40203752520 SEB Pank branch, 40103200513 Bigbank branch, 40203154352 Luminor branch, 40103212230 OP Corporate branch, 50203334311 TF Bank branch), plus the two 9-numbers above; and all 187 single-digit mutations of those seventeen were refused. A natural person trading in their own name uses their personas kods here instead, which is different arithmetic entirely.
