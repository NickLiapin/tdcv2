address: latvia.docs.personasKods32
description: Latvian personas kods, the dateless form issued since 1 July 2017 — 32NNNN-NNNNZ. Latvia stopped putting the birth date in the number and marked the break by opening every new code with 32, a day of the month that cannot exist, so old and new can never be confused by a reader or by a parser. Everything after the 32 is random: PMLP's own description is that the first digits are "32" and the rest are system-generated digits from 0 to 9 with no human involvement — no birth date, no sex, no region, no region-of-registration, nothing. Everyone entered in the Fizisko personu reģistrs from that date holds this form, and a holder of an old code may swap to one once in a lifetime, free, on request. Use this address when a record must NOT leak an age: that is what the form was created for, after years of argument that a birth-date-bearing identifier invited discrimination in hiring and in shops. Use latvia.docs.personasKodsPre2017 when the code must agree with a birth date elsewhere in the record, and latvia.docs.personasKods for a realistic mix of the two. The check digit rule is UNCHANGED from the pre-2017 form; the claim is not inferred here but taken from python-stdnum, whose validate() branches on the "32" prefix only to skip the birth-date check and then applies the identical calc_check_digit_pers(). Verified against its published vectors: 328673-00679 accepted, 328673-00677 refused, and every single-digit mutation of the valid one refused.
generator: tdc
locale: lv
