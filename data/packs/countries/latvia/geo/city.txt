description: Cities (pilsētas) of Latvia, roughly ordered by population. The first seven are the valstspilsētas that are local governments in their own right and so appear in no novads. Latvia is extremely top-heavy — Rīga alone holds about a third of the country, and the drop from Rīga to Daugavpils is nearly eightfold — so a uniform draw over this list badly over-represents small towns. The tail is genuinely tiny: Durbe and Piltene have a few hundred residents each and are still legally cities.
