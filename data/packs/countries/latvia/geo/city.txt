description: All 81 cities (pilsētas) of Latvia, weighted by the population the Central Statistical Bureau records at 1 January 2026. The weighting is not decoration here — Latvia is the most top-heavy country in the Baltics: Rīga alone is 588 911 of the 1 288 367 people living in a Latvian city, 45.7 % of the whole urban population, and the drop from Rīga to Daugavpils is nearly eightfold. A uniform draw over this list puts a third of its output in towns of under 2 000 people, which is not what a Latvian address column looks like. The tail is genuinely tiny and genuinely legal: Durbe, Subate and Piltene have a few hundred residents each and have held city rights since the 17th, 19th and 14th centuries. Ten of these are valstspilsētas (latvia.geo.valstspilseta) but only SEVEN of the ten are local governments in their own right — Jēkabpils, Ogre and Valmiera are valstspilsētas INSIDE a novads, which is the detail every foreign summary of Latvia's 2021 reform gets wrong. Five entries are recent: Koknese and Iecava became cities on 1 July 2021, Ādaži, Mārupe and Ķekava on 1 July 2022. One is imminent and deliberately NOT here — the Saeima passed the law making Carnikava (5 305 residents in April 2026) Latvia's 82nd city with effect from 22 August 2026, six days after this file was written, so a reader finding 81 where they expected 82 is looking at a file that is right for its date. Sources: the city list and its city-rights dates from Latvijas pilsētu uzskaitījums; populations from Wikidata P1082, every one of the 81 qualified to 1 January 2026, which sums with the 35 novadi to 1 845 096 — the published national total of 1,845 million.
