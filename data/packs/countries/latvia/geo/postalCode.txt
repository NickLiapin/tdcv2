address: latvia.geo.postalCode
description: Latvian pasta indekss. Latvia is the odd one out among its neighbours — the country prefix is PART OF THE CODE, not decoration, so the written form is LV-NNNN and the LV is never dropped the way Lithuania drops its LT- and Estonia has no prefix at all. Store it as a five-character string with the hyphen; storing four digits and prepending "LV-" at render time is what turns a Latvian address into a Lithuanian one the first time a country column is wrong. The first two digits are the routing district: 10-13 Rīga, 20-21 Pierīga and Jūrmala, 30-38 Kurzeme and Zemgale, 40-48 Vidzeme and northern Latgale, 50-58 the Daugava valley and southern Latgale — the same broad geography as the five historical lands in latvia.geo.region, and NOT the same as the 35 novadi, which the postal map predates. The four digits are drawn flat across the issued span 1001..5871, which is honest about the range and wrong about the density: real Latvian post clusters hard on Rīga's 10xx, because Rīga holds 45.7 % of everyone living in a Latvian city (see latvia.geo.city). A flat draw therefore under-represents Rīga addresses by roughly the same factor a flat draw over the city list does, and the span also contains unissued gaps, so a code from this generator is well-formed rather than guaranteed deliverable. The index is owned by VAS "Latvijas Pasts".
generator: tdc
locale: lv
