description: The 35 novadi of Latvia, weighted by population at 1 January 2026. The novads is Latvia's ONLY first-level local government; the historical lands in latvia.geo.region are a cultural and legal classification with no council. Two dates matter and a stale list gets both wrong. The reform of 1 July 2021 cut 119 local governments (110 novadi and 9 republican cities) to 43, so any list of 110 names is not merely long but wrong — most of those novadi no longer exist. Then on 1 July 2025, when the council elected on 7 June 2025 first sat, Varakļānu novads was absorbed into Madonas novads under the 30 June 2024 amendment, taking the count from 43 to 42: these 35 novadi plus the seven valstspilsētas that are separate local governments. Varakļānu novads is therefore NOT in this file, and a pack that still lists 36 is a 2021-2024 pack — it survived that long only because the Constitutional Court had ruled on 28 May 2021 that folding it into Rēzeknes novads was unconstitutional, and the 2024 law moved it to Madona instead, which is where its residents had said they wanted to go. Note also that Jelgavas novads is the one novads containing no city at all: the city of Jelgava is a separate valstspilsēta and the novads is the rural ring around it. Sources: Latvijas administratīvais iedalījums ("Kopš 2025. gada 7. jūnija Latvijā ir 35 novadi un 7 novados neietilpstošas valstspilsētas"); populations from Wikidata P1082 at 1 January 2026.
