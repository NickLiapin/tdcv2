---
address: latvia.finance.amount
generator: tdc
description: A sum of money written the way Latvia writes it: groups of three separated by a NO-BREAK SPACE, a COMMA for the decimal point, and the euro sign AFTER the figure with another no-break space before it — 1 234 567,89 €. That is the lv-LV currency pattern in CLDR and it disagrees with the English 1,234,567.89 on every count, which is how Latvian amounts get silently corrupted: a comma read as a thousands separator turns 1 234,56 into 123456, a factor of a hundred, and both readings are valid numbers so nothing downstream complains. THE SEPARATORS HERE ARE U+00A0, NOT ORDINARY SPACES — invisible in an editor, and the reason a naive split on " " gives the wrong field count and a regex of \d+(\.\d\d)? matches nothing at all. The sign goes after the number in Latvian, unlike English; "€ 12,50" is not a Latvian amount. Latvia has used the euro since 1 January 2014 and the cent is a genuine hundredth, so two decimals are always carried and never rounded away — there is no Norwegian-style cash rounding here. The magnitude is spread across five decades rather than drawn uniformly, because a uniform draw over a seven-digit range puts nearly every value in the millions and a Latvian price list is mostly two, three and four figures. Nothing here is a number the engine can do arithmetic on: it is the PRINTED form. For a value to compute with, generate a plain number and format it at the point of output.
locale: lv
---
<sequence name="raw"><gen type="number" value="1..9999999"/></sequence>
<sequence name="shift"><gen type="number" value="0..4"/></sequence>
<sequence name="cents"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="amount"><compute>
  <!-- Spread the magnitude: divide the 7-digit draw by 1, 10, 100, 1000 or 10000. -->
  <let name="scale"><at>
    <in><list v="1,10,100,1000,10000"/></in>
    <index><to_number><field name="shift"/></to_number></index>
  </at></let>
  <let name="whole"><divide><to_number><field name="raw"/></to_number><use name="scale"/></divide></let>
  <!-- CLDR lv-LV: group separator U+00A0, decimal comma, pattern "#,##0.00 ¤". -->
  <let name="grouped"><group size="3" sep=" "><use name="whole"/></group></let>
  <result><concat><use name="grouped"/><str v=","/><field name="cents"/><str v=" €"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{amount}}</data>
