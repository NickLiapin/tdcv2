---
address: latvia.finance.iban
description: The Latvian IBAN — 21 characters: LV, two ISO 7064 MOD-97-10 check digits, a four-LETTER bank code drawn from latvia.finance.bankCode, and a 13-character account. The letter bank code is the visible difference from Poland, Lithuania and Estonia, whose BBANs are numeric throughout, and it means the MOD-97 fold has to transliterate letters to numbers before it can run; a digits-only implementation gets the wrong remainder rather than an error, which is the worst way to fail. The registry declares those 13 account characters as `13!c`, alphanumeric, and most Latvian accounts are in fact all digits — so most implementations quietly assume digits and most of the time get away with it. They do not always: Latvijas Banka publishes its own account as LV35LACB0EUR175050100, where the account part begins 0EUR, and that IBAN is valid. Digits are what this generator draws, because that is what a customer account looks like; the counter-example is recorded here so nobody writes `[0-9]{13}` into a validator. Verified by MOD-97 against seven published numbers, all accepted: LV35LACB0EUR175050100 (Latvijas Banka, own account-details page), LV08HABA0551041541190, LV58HABA0551004772731, LV95PARX0002242990005 and LV26TREL9160032000000 (Latvijas Biozinātņu un tehnoloģiju universitāte, own rekvizīti page), LV51NDEA0000082414423 (LU Botanical Garden), and the IBAN Registry example LV80BANK0000435195001; each is rejected with one digit changed. Printed in groups of four for humans, unspaced in files, which is what is emitted here.
generator: tdc
locale: lv
---
<sequence name="bank"><gen type="template" value="latvia.finance.bankCode"/></sequence>
<sequence name="account"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{13}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="iban"><compute>
  <let name="bban"><concat><field name="bank"/><field name="account"/></concat></let>
  <!-- ISO 13616: move the country code and "00" to the end, letters become 10..35, take mod 97. -->
  <let name="rearranged"><concat><use name="bban"/><str v="LV"/><str v="00"/></concat></let>
  <let name="digits"><join><each>
    <over><use name="rearranged"/></over>
    <do><encode as="base36"><current/></encode></do>
  </each></join></let>
  <let name="rem"><reduce>
    <over><use name="digits"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><mod><add><multiply><acc/><int v="10"/></multiply><current/></add><int v="97"/></mod></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><pad width="2" fill="0"><subtract><int v="98"/><use name="rem"/></subtract></pad></let>
  <result><concat><str v="LV"/><use name="check"/><use name="bban"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{iban}}</data>
