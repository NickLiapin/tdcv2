description: A Latvian mobile number in E.164 form — +371 and then eight digits beginning with 2. Latvia's plan has been CLOSED and eight digits flat since 2008, when a 6 was prepended to every landline and a 2 to every mobile in one move: there are no area codes, no trunk prefix, no leading zero to strip, and the number dialled from the next street is the number dialled from Australia minus the +371. That is the fact a foreign address schema gets wrong about Latvia, because a "city code" column has nothing to put in it and a national format that inserts a 0 produces a number that does not exist. The leading digit is the whole of the classification: 2 is mobile, 6 is a fixed line (latvia.phoneLandline), 7 was payphones, 5 is reserved and unissued, and 80/81/88/89/90 are toll-free, shared-cost and premium-rate. The second digit of a mobile number carries NO operator meaning — number portability has been in force for years and all seven of the 2x blocks are shared. Written nationally in the same eight digits with no prefix at all (latvia.phoneNational); this generator emits the unspaced E.164 form so it survives a CSV round-trip. Emergency numbers are three digits and outside the plan: 112 general, 110 police, 113 ambulance, 114 gas, 115 coast guard. Source: the ITU-T E.164 national numbering plan for country code +371, administered by VAS "Elektroniskie sakari" and regulated by the Sabiedrisko pakalpojumu regulēšanas komisija.
