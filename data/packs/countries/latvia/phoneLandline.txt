The blocks are named after the RAJONI, the districts abolished in 2009, so the telephone map is two administrative reforms out of date and matches neither the 35 novadi nor the five judicial regions. They are not area codes either: all eight digits are dialled from anywhere, number portability applies, and a line can be carried to another region, so the leading block is a hint about where a line was first sold and not a reliable location. The pattern below draws 63x through 67x, the ranges actually in service; 60x, 61x, 62x are reserved and unissued, 68x is reserved or given to trunked radio and private networks, and 689 held the payphones until 31 December 2011.
