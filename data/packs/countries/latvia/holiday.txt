description: Public holidays of Latvia (svētku dienas, official Latvian names). Two dates carry the country's own history rather than the European calendar: 4 May 1990, when the Supreme Soviet declared the restoration of independence, and 18 November 1918, the proclamation of the Republic. Jāņi on 23-24 June is the midsummer holiday and the only two consecutive non-religious days off in the year.
