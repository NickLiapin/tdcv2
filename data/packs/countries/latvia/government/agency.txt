description: State institutions of Latvia — the direct administration bodies (tiešās pārvaldes iestādes) under the Cabinet, plus the constitutional bodies and the two state companies that a Latvian record is most likely to name. The Cabinet counts 144 direct administration institutions; this is the working subset a dataset actually meets. Five are worth knowing before reading any Latvian data. Valsts ieņēmumu dienests (VID) collects tax and customs and is the authority behind latvia.tax.vat. Pilsonības un migrācijas lietu pārvalde (PMLP) runs the Fizisko personu reģistru and issues the personas kods — it is the body that stopped putting birth dates in the number on 1 July 2017. Uzņēmumu reģistrs issues the vienotais reģistrācijas numurs in latvia.docs.registracijasNumurs. Centrālā statistikas pārvalde publishes the population figures the geography files here are weighted by. And Latvijas Banka is both the central bank and, since the Finanšu un kapitāla tirgus komisija was merged into it on 1 January 2023, the single financial supervisor — so a Latvian dataset dated before 2023 will name FKTK for work Latvijas Banka does now, and FKTK no longer exists. Two entries carry the prefix VAS and are NOT ministries or agencies but state joint-stock companies performing delegated public functions: Ceļu satiksmes drošības direkcija (CSDD), which registers every vehicle and issues both the plate and the driving licence, and Latvijas Pasts, which owns the LV-#### postal index. Names are in normal Latvian case with their diacritics, which is how the institutions write themselves and not how a register upper-cases them. Source: Valsts pārvaldes iestādes Latvijā, grouped by ministry.
