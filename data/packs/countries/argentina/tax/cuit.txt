description: Argentine CUIT (Clave Única de Identificación Tributaria) — 11 digits: a 2-digit type prefix (20/23/24/27 personas físicas, 30/33/34 personas jurídicas), the 8-digit DNI or AFIP serial, and a mod-11 check digit over the weights 5,4,3,2,7,6,5,4,3,2. Remainder 0 yields 0 and remainder 1 yields 9; AFIP itself sidesteps the remainder-1 case by re-issuing under prefix 23/33, so those values satisfy the published check-digit rule without matching AFIP's assignment convention. Pass prefix="30" to pin a taxpayer type.
