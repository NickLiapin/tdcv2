description: Argentine national holiday (feriado nacional, ley 27.399). Six of them move — Carnaval and the Easter days follow the liturgical calendar, and Güemes, San Martín, Diversidad Cultural and Soberanía Nacional are trasladables, shifted to a Monday when their fixed date (17 June, 17 August, 12 October, 20 November) falls midweek. Jueves Santo is a día no laborable rather than a full feriado.
