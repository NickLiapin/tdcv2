address: thailand.finance.bankWithCode
description: The paired table behind thailand.finance.bank and thailand.finance.bankCode — the THREE-DIGIT bank code, a space, then the bank's Thai name. Thai bank codes are three digits and always written with their leading zero (002, not 2); they are the Bank of Thailand / SMART member codes, they are what a domestic transfer form and every PromptPay integration ask for, and they are NOT SWIFT codes and NOT part of an IBAN, because Thailand has none. Kept as one file so a name and its code cannot drift apart. The list is the banks trading today: 065 ธนาคารธนชาต is deliberately absent because Thanachart merged into ธนาคารทหารไทยธนชาต (ttb) in 2021 and the code is retired, and 079 ANZ (Thai) is absent for the same reason. Codes are not reissued when a bank is renamed — 011 was TMB before the merger and 070 was ACL before ICBC bought it — so the code outlives the name and a code that no longer matches a bank you know is usually history rather than an error.
locale: th
