address: thailand.sport.team
description: Thai football clubs — Thai League 1 and Thai League 2 sides, Thai names. Thai club names are written the way Thai writes any borrowed word: the English part is transliterated into Thai script and a space IS used between the transliterated words, so บุรีรัมย์ ยูไนเต็ด is "Buriram United" spelled out phonetically rather than translated. That makes club names one of the few Thai lists with internal spaces, which is the opposite of the problem the rest of this pack has. Most clubs are named for a province or a city, so a value here often contains a value from thailand.geo.province. Sponsor names get bolted onto club names and come off again when the sponsor leaves, so this list ages faster than any other file in the pack.
locale: th
