address: thailand.docs.nationalId
description: Thai national identification number (เลขประจำตัวประชาชน) — 13 digits carrying a REAL mod-11 check digit, printed on the บัตรประชาชน as 1-2345-67890-12-3 and emitted here bare. The check is the published rule: multiply the first twelve digits by the descending weights 13,12,11,10,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2, sum, and the thirteenth digit is (11 - sum mod 11) mod 10 — the outer mod 10 is what turns a remainder of 0 or 1 into a printable 1 or 0 instead of 11 or 10, and dropping it is the usual way this algorithm is got wrong. THE RULE HAS A BLIND SPOT and it is worth knowing before you rely on it: digit 3 is multiplied by 11, and 11 times anything is 0 modulo 11, so the third digit contributes NOTHING to the check and any error in it passes silently. Digit 3 is the second digit of the province code, so a Thai ID that names the wrong province validates perfectly. Every other single-digit error, and every transposition of two adjacent digits, is caught. Structure, all of it modelled except where said: digit 1 is the person's registration category (1 Thai born from 1984 registered on time, 2 Thai born from 1984 registered late, 3 Thai already in the household register before the 1984 reform, 4 added at the 1984 census, 5 a Thai the census missed, 6 and 7 foreigners resident temporarily and their children, 8 naturalised citizens and permanent residents); digits 2-3 are the ADMINISTRATIVE province code of the district office that issued it, drawn from the real set in thailand.geo.provinceCode and NOT the postcode prefix; digits 4-5 are that province's district (อำเภอ) and are drawn from 01 to 30 as a plausible range rather than a real อำเภอ table, so the province half of the number is real and the district half is not; digits 6-10 are the household registration book and the entry in it; digits 11-12 are a serial; digit 13 is the check. The number is a Thai person's primary key: it is the tax ID (thailand.tax.tin), it is the PromptPay handle, it is what a hospital, a bank and a SIM registration all ask for, and it is protected personal data under the PDPA — which is why no real citizen's number appears anywhere in this pack. The check rule was verified against published 13-digit numbers of the same scheme rather than against anyone's ID card; see thailand.tax.corporateTin.
