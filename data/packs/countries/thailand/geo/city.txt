address: thailand.geo.city
description: Cities and towns of Thailand in Thai, roughly by size. Read the first line before using this as a plain city list: กรุงเทพมหานคร is not one city among many but a metropolitan area holding something like a tenth of the country's population and more than thirty times the next largest municipality — Thailand has no second city in the sense that most countries do, and a uniform draw over this list is a much flatter distribution than the real one. Most of these names are ALSO province names, because a Thai provincial capital normally takes its province's name (เชียงใหม่ the city sits in เชียงใหม่ the province), so a value drawn here will frequently equal a value drawn from thailand.geo.province and that is correct rather than a duplication bug. The exceptions are worth knowing: หาดใหญ่ is bigger than สงขลา which is its provincial capital, พัทยา and แหลมฉบัง are self-governing municipalities that are not capitals of anything, and นนทบุรี, ปากเกร็ด and รังสิต are continuous with Bangkok on the ground while being separate administratively. Thai script: no spaces between words, no capital letters.
locale: th
