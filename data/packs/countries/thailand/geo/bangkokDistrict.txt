description: The 50 districts of Bangkok (เขต, khet). This list exists because Bangkok is not a province and does not divide like one — a จังหวัด is divided into อำเภอ (amphoe) and those into ตำบล (tambon), while กรุงเทพมหานคร is divided into เขต and those into แขวง (khwaeng). The words are different, the law is different, and an address form that offers "อำเภอ" to someone in Bangkok has got it wrong. These are the values that go in the district field of a Bangkok address, and the first eight (พระนคร, ดุสิต, หนองจอก, บางรัก, บางเขน, บางกะปิ, ปทุมวัน, ป้อมปราบศัตรูพ่าย) are the original 1972 set. Thai script: no spaces, no capitals.
locale: th
