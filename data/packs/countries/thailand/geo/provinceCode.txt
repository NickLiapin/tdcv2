address: thailand.geo.provinceCode
description: The two-digit ADMINISTRATIVE province code of Thailand, the one the Department of Provincial Administration assigns and the one that is embedded in identity numbers — digits 2 and 3 of a 13-digit national ID (thailand.docs.nationalId) and digits 2 and 3 of a juristic person registration number (thailand.tax.corporateTin). It is also the numeric part of the ISO 3166-2:TH code. 77 values, one per province plus Bangkok (10). DO NOT CONFUSE IT WITH THE POSTCODE PREFIX. The two schemes agree from 20 upwards and disagree across the whole central block, because postcodes give Bangkok and สมุทรปราการ one shared prefix while the administrative scheme gives them 10 and 11 separately: นนทบุรี is administrative 12 but postal 11, พระนครศรีอยุธยา is 14 but postal 13, สระบุรี is 19 but postal 18, and administrative 19 has no postal counterpart at all. Deriving one from the other is wrong for ten provinces. 28, 29, 59, 68, 69, 78, 79, 87, 88, 89 and 97 to 99 are unassigned in both schemes.
