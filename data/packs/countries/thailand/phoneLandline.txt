address: thailand.phoneLandline
description: Thai fixed-line number in E.164 form. Thailand's landline area codes are ONE digit in the Bangkok metropolitan area (2, giving +66 2 nnnnnnn with a seven-digit subscriber number) and TWO digits everywhere else (3x, 4x, 5x, 7x, giving +66 44 nnnnnn with six digits) — so a Thai landline is eight or nine digits after the country code depending on where it is, and a fixed-length check will reject half the country. The trunk 0 is dropped internationally, exactly as for mobiles. The area code is compulsory even for a local call: Thailand went to closed dialling in 1997, so a Bangkok number dialled from Bangkok still starts 02. Roughly, 3x is the east and the near south-west, 4x the northeast, 5x the north, 7x the south. This file draws Bangkok about a third of the time, which is close to the real share of fixed lines.
