description: Jamaican universities. The University of the West Indies is the one to understand: it is a single regional university with campuses in Jamaica, Trinidad and Barbados and centres across the English-speaking Caribbean, founded 1948, and it is jointly owned by seventeen governments — so a UWI degree is Caribbean rather than national, and a Jamaican graduate may have studied in Trinidad. UTech was the College of Arts, Science and Technology and became a university in 1995.
