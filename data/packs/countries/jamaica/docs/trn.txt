description: Taxpayer Registration Number — nine digits, issued by Tax Administration Jamaica and used far beyond tax. The TRN is the closest thing Jamaica has to a universal identifier: it is required to open a bank account, register a vehicle, be employed, or receive a government payment, and it is quoted on most official forms. There is no separate national ID number in general use, so a Jamaican person record is keyed on the TRN.
