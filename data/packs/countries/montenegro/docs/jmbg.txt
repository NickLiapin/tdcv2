description: Unique Master Citizen Number (JMBG) — the thirteen-digit identifier inherited from Yugoslavia and still used across most of its successor states. The structure is unusually revealing: DDMMYYY where the year is the last three digits, then a two-digit register code for the region of birth, a three-digit serial in which the range separates men from women, and a mod-11 check digit. It therefore leaks date of birth, sex and birth region from the number alone, which is why several successor states have moved to replace it.
