description: Montenegrin public holidays. Independence Day on 21 May marks the 2006 referendum; Statehood Day on 13 July marks two things at once — the 1878 recognition of Montenegrin independence at the Congress of Berlin and the 1941 uprising against the Axis occupation. Orthodox Christmas and Easter follow the Julian calendar, so Christmas is 7 January, and the Catholic and Muslim holidays are observed as well for the Croat and Bosniak communities.
