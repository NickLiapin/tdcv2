description: The sixteen districts of Sierra Leone, grouped into four provinces and the Western Area. Five districts are recent: Falaba, Karene, Kambia and Moyamba's neighbours were reorganised in 2017 when the country went from twelve districts to sixteen, so a pre-2017 dataset is missing four names entirely. Western Area Urban is Freetown and Western Area Rural the peninsula around it — the two are administratively separate and are frequently conflated in data.
