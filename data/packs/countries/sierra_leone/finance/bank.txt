description: Sierra Leonean banks. The Bank of Sierra Leone is the central bank; Sierra Leone Commercial Bank and Rokel Commercial Bank are the two state-owned commercial banks and between them hold most retail accounts. The Nigerian banks — GTBank, Zenith, UBA, Access — arrived in the 2000s and are a significant part of the sector, which is characteristic of West Africa: Nigerian banking capital follows the region.
