description: Central agencies, authorities, directorates and independent institutions of the Albanian state, taken from the official 2025 register of public authorities published by the Commissioner for the Right to Information and Protection of Personal Data. Names are the official Albanian ones, with the abbreviation appended where the institution itself uses it — Albanian administration is spoken in initials (AKSHI, ASHK, QKB, DPT, KLSH, SPAK), and a form that only carries the long name is not the form a real record holds. Four are worth knowing before reading any Albanian dataset: the QENDRA KOMBËTARE E BIZNESIT issues the NIPT and is the company register (see albania.tax.nipt); the DREJTORIA E PËRGJITHSHME E TATIMEVE is the tax administration; the AGJENCIA SHTETËRORE E KADASTRËS holds land and property, an unusually loaded institution in a country that spent thirty years untangling communist-era expropriation; and AKSHI runs e-Albania, the single portal through which almost every state service is now delivered — Albania closed most physical counters in 2022, so an Albanian's dealings with the state run through one login.
