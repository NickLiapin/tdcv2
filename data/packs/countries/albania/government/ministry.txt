description: The ministries of the Albanian government, plus the two institutions that stand beside them in any list of central bodies — the Kryeministria and the Kuvend. Names are as the official 2025 register of public authorities carries them. Albanian cabinets are re-cut often and portfolios travel between ministries, so a ministry list is a snapshot, not a constant; what does not move is the shape — the foreign ministry is MINISTRIA PËR EVROPËN DHE PUNËT E JASHTME, with EU accession named in the title rather than handed to a separate ministry, which is how a candidate country that opened negotiations in 2022 organises itself.
