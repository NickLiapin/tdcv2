description: Albanian postal code (kodi postar) — four digits, introduced by Posta Shqiptare on 11 October 2006. The first two digits are the postal branch at DISTRICT level (the pre-2014 rreth, which the post office kept as its own geography and which is therefore NOT the same map as the 12 counties or the 61 municipalities); the last two identify the office serving one administrative unit inside it. The first pair is drawn here from the published district table, so 1042 is a Tirana code and 9401 a Vlorë one, and codes like 4700 (Kurbin) or 8700 (Tropojë) fall where the post office really put them. Two of the pairs are not places at all: 17 is transit and 18 is the EMS express office, both in Tirana. Codes with 00 as the last pair are the district centre itself.
