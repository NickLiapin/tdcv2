description: An Armenian mobile number in E.164 form — +374, a two-digit mobile network code, six subscriber digits. The national significant number is EIGHT digits for every kind of Armenian number, mobile or fixed, so the international form is always twelve characters after the plus sign; there is no long/short split of the kind Russia or Turkey have. Written internationally without the trunk zero: an Armenian dialling at home types 091 234567, but the 0 is a national prefix and is not part of the number. The codes drawn here are the ones allocated to the three operators — 33, 43, 91, 96, 99 to Team Telecom Armenia (the old ArmenTel/Beeline); 77, 93, 94, 98 to Viva (K-Telecom, VivaCell-MTS until the 2023 rebrand); 41, 44, 50, 55, 66, 95 to Ucom, which absorbed Orange Armenia's 55 and 95 when it bought the business in 2015. NUMBER PORTABILITY HAS BEEN LIVE SINCE 1 APRIL 2014, so the code no longer tells you the operator: a 093 number can be on Team's network and a 091 on Viva's, and any routing logic that maps prefix to carrier is reading a 2013 world. 97 IS DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED. It was the mobile code of Nagorno-Karabakh, reached as +374 97 through Karabakh Telecom while +374 47 carried its fixed lines; both stopped after the territory was dissolved on 1 January 2024. Some tables now show 97 reassigned to Team, which I could not corroborate, so this file does not draw it — a number in a range that may or may not be live is worse than one fewer prefix. Fixed lines are a different shape entirely and live in armenia.phoneLandline. Emergency numbers are 112 and 911 together, plus 101 fire, 102 police, 103 ambulance, 104 gas.
