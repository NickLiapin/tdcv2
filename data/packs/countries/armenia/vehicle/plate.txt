address: armenia.vehicle.plate
description: An Armenian registration plate in the current private-vehicle format — two digits, two letters, three digits, written 34 SL 123. The plate is black on white with the flag and the code AM on a band down the left; the design in force since 6 August 2014 adds a hologram and a Data Matrix code and sets the characters in the German FE-Schrift, which is why an Armenian plate photographs like a German one. THE DIGIT-GROUP LENGTHS ARE THE VEHICLE CLASS. NN LL NNN is a private vehicle; NNN LL NN — three digits first, two last — is a state vehicle, and the trailing pair is a fixed code for the body that owns it: 01 after ՕՏ is the Constitutional Court, after ՍՍ the Presidential Administration, ՕՕ 01 to ՕՕ 11 the National Assembly, ՈՍ 01 to ՈՍ 14 the Police, ՏՏ 01 the Court of Cassation, ՏՏ 02 the National Security Service, GG 01 the Prosecutor General, ՏL 01 to ՏL 33 the banks. So the same eight characters in a different rhythm mean a completely different thing, and a regex that accepts both without distinguishing them will label a ministry car as a private one. THE LETTERS ARE A MIXED ALPHABET IN PRACTICE. Armenia started in 1996 with four letters chosen to look like Armenian ones — Latin L for Լ, and the Armenian letters Ս, Տ and Օ, which read to a foreign eye as U, S and O — and Latin letters were added a handful at a time and at extra cost: D, N, T, V in 2010, A, M, Q, R in 2012, C, F, Z in 2013, X in 2016, B and H in 2017, J in 2018, W, Y, K and E in 2019. All 26 Latin letters are now in ordinary use, so this file draws A-Z, but plates issued before those dates carry Armenian Ս, Տ, Օ and Ո, and G and I appear only doubled (GG, II) on Police and Prosecutor vehicles. The leading two digits are a regional code and are enumerated in armenia.vehicle.plateRegionCode. Not generated here: diplomatic plates (white on red, NN L NNN LL), bus plates (black on yellow, NNNN L, S for city and L for coach), military plates (black, ՊՆ NNNN L, in Armenian letters only), UN plates (white on blue, UN NNN AM), and the single-letter trailer plates.
