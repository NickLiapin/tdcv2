description: 新臺幣的貨幣單位。The currency is the 新臺幣 (New Taiwan dollar, ISO 4217 TWD), nominally divided into 10 角 and 100 分. THE SUBDIVISIONS ARE DEAD AND THAT IS A MODELLING FACT: the smallest coin in circulation is the NT$1, no Taiwanese price, salary, invoice, receipt or bank statement carries a fractional dollar, and cash totals are rounded to the whole dollar at the till — yet ISO 4217 records TWD with two decimal places, so every currency library will format 1000 as 1,000.00 and a schema will size the column with two decimals that are always zero. 角 survives only in speech (「一塊五」 for NT$1.50 is said, not paid) and in the petrol pump and stock prices, which quote to two and to one decimal respectively. THE WORD 元 AND THE WORD 塊 ARE BOTH "DOLLAR": 元 is written, 塊 is spoken, and a price read aloud will not match the string it was read from. THE NAME CARRIES 新 FOR A REASON: the 新臺幣 replaced the 舊臺幣 on 1949-06-15 at 40,000 old to 1 new, at the end of the hyperinflation that followed the war, and the 新 has never been dropped in seventy-five years of official use. It was legally a provincial currency issued by 臺灣銀行 rather than a national one until 2000, when the 中央銀行 finally took over issuance — the notes still say 中央銀行 and are still printed by 中央印製廠. THE SYMBOL IS AMBIGUOUS AND THAT MATTERS FOR PARSING: inside Taiwan the sign is a bare $, so $100 on a Taiwanese menu is NT$100 and not one hundred US dollars; NT$ is the disambiguated form used for export and in international price lists. Formal and legal documents do not use Arabic numerals at all — a contract, a cheque and a receipt write the amount in the anti-tampering numerals 壹貳參肆伍陸柒捌玖拾佰仟萬 and close it with 整, giving 新臺幣壹萬貳仟參佰肆拾伍元整 — so a Taiwanese financial document may hold the same amount twice in two entirely different character sets. And Chinese counts in ten-thousands: 萬 is 10,000 and 億 is 100,000,000, so a Taiwanese speaker says 三十萬 for 300,000 and 一億 for 100 million while the written figure is grouped by threes in the Western way, and a spoken-to-written conversion has to regroup rather than transliterate.
