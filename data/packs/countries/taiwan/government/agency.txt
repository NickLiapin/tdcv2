description: 中華民國的憲政機關與常設行政機關 — the standing public bodies a citizen or a company actually transacts with, as distinct from the cabinet ministries at taiwan.government.ministry, which are renamed at almost every reorganisation. THE FIRST SIX ENTRIES ARE THE REASON THIS FILE CANNOT BE MODELLED ON ANY OTHER COUNTRY'S: THE REPUBLIC OF CHINA HAS FIVE BRANCHES, NOT THREE. Beside 行政院 (executive), 立法院 (legislature) and 司法院 (judiciary) stand 考試院, which runs the civil-service examinations and owns personnel policy for the whole state, and 監察院, which audits the government and can impeach officials — powers that in a three-branch state are split between a parliament and an auditor. Both are constitutional organs with their own presidents, their own budgets and their own subordinate ministries: 考選部 and 銓敘部 answer to 考試院, 審計部 to 監察院, and none of the three appears in the cabinet. A schema that maps Taiwanese government onto a Montesquieu three-branch tree has two whole branches with nowhere to go. 總統府 is the office of the head of state and is not the executive: the 總統 is directly elected and appoints the 行政院院長 without the 立法院's consent, but the premier and not the president heads the government, so "head of state" and "head of government" are two people and only one of them faces the legislature. 中央研究院 and 國史館 report to 總統府 rather than to any ministry, which is why the national academy is not under the science council. THE DELIVERY LAYER IS WHAT THIS PACK ACTUALLY DEPENDS ON. 內政部戶政司 issues taiwan.docs.nationalId and publishes the population figures that weight taiwan.geo.city; 經濟部商業發展署 registers every company and publishes taiwan.tax.ubn against name and address at an open API; 財政部財政資訊中心 owns the check-digit specification behind that number and runs the 統一發票 invoice lottery; 中華郵政 maintains the postal register behind taiwan.geo.postalCode3; 內政部移民署 issues taiwan.docs.uiNumber; 交通部公路局 issues taiwan.vehicle.plate; 國家通訊傳播委員會 allocates taiwan.phoneMobilePrefix. Seven files in this pack trace to a body on this list. 中央選舉委員會, 公平交易委員會 and 國家通訊傳播委員會 are 獨立機關 and also appear under ministries, because they hold cabinet rank while being outside cabinet control. 衛生福利部中央健康保險署 runs the single-payer health system that covers 99.9 per cent of the population on one card keyed to the national ID number, which is the reason that number is the master key to Taiwanese personal data.
