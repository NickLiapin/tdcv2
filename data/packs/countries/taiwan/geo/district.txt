description: 每一個「區」的名字，也就是六個直轄市與三個市底下的第二級行政區，共 170 個，取自中華郵政的鄉鎮市區郵遞區號名冊。THE WORD 區 IS NOT A GENERIC LABEL HERE: a 區 exists only inside a 直轄市 or a 市, and a 縣 has no 區 at all — its second level is the 鄉, 鎮 and 縣轄市 at taiwan.geo.township — so drawing this file beside a county from taiwan.geo.city produces an address that cannot exist. 新北市 has 29 entries and 高雄市 38, which are the two largest sets, and 嘉義市 has only 2 (東區, 西區) — the whole city divides into east and west and nothing else. Six of the entries are 直轄市山地原住民區, a category created by the 2014 amendment to the 地方制度法 and unique to Taiwan: 烏來區 in 新北市, 復興區 in 桃園市, 和平區 in 臺中市, and 那瑪夏區, 桃源區, 茂林區 in 高雄市 elect their own district chief and council while every other 區 has an appointed 區長, so "district" is two different legal objects in one column. Several names repeat across cities and are NOT unique keys — 中正區 exists in 臺北市, 基隆市 and 臺南市, 東區 in 臺南市, 嘉義市, 新竹市 and 臺中市, 北區 in four places, 信義區 in 臺北市 and 基隆市 — so a district name alone never identifies a place in Taiwan and must be joined with the city. Below the 區 sit the 里 and below that the 鄰, which is where a Taiwanese household registration record actually lands; neither is modelled here. 東沙群島 and 南沙群島 carry postal codes under 高雄市 but are uninhabited garrisons administered from 旗津區, and 釣魚臺 is claimed rather than administered; all three are deliberately absent.
