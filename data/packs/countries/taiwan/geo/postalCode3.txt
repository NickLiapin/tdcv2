description: The 3-digit part of a Taiwanese postal code — 365 codes covering the 368 鄉鎮市區, every one of them issued, taken from the 中華郵政 register. THIS IS A LIST OF REAL CODES, NOT A THREE-DIGIT PATTERN: only these 365 of the 1000 arrangements are allocated, so a [0-9]{3} generator emits a code that addresses nowhere about two times in three. The first digit is a postal region running roughly north to south — 1 臺北市, 2 基隆/新北/宜蘭/馬祖, 3 桃竹苗, 4 臺中, 5 彰化/南投, 6 雲嘉, 7 臺南, 8 高雄/澎湖, 9 屏東/臺東/花蓮/金門 — so the code and the city agree only if they are drawn together; see taiwan.geo.postalCode for the full 3+2 form and for why the last two digits are a different kind of thing. The numbering is not contiguous and the gaps are not accidents: 新北市 runs 207-253 with 205, 209 and many others unused, because the register was laid out along postal delivery routes rather than by administrative order, and 連江縣 (209-212) is interleaved inside the 新北市 range for the same historical reason. Codes are printed in front of the address in Taiwan, not after it.
