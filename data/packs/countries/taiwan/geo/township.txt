description: 縣底下的第二級行政區：鄉、鎮與縣轄市，共 198 個，取自中華郵政的鄉鎮市區郵遞區號名冊。THE THREE KINDS ARE ONE LEVEL, NOT THREE LEVELS, and that is the thing a foreign schema gets wrong: 鄉, 鎮 and 縣轄市 sit side by side under a 縣, all three elect a chief and a representative council, and the only difference between them is population and how urban they are — a 鄉 becomes a 鎮 and a 鎮 becomes a 縣轄市 by crossing a threshold in the 地方制度法, currently 100,000 for a 縣轄市 since the 2015 amendment lowered it back from 150,000. A 縣 never contains a 區. Names of the 鄉 include the 山地鄉, which are indigenous townships where the chief must by law be an indigenous person — 仁愛鄉, 信義鄉, 尖石鄉, 泰武鄉 and about thirty others — a legal category with no counterpart outside Taiwan. As with the districts the names are not unique keys: 大同鄉 is in 宜蘭縣 and 信義鄉 in 南投縣, but 東勢 names both a 鄉 in 雲林縣 and a 區 in 臺中市, 大同 names a 鄉 in 宜蘭縣 and a 區 in 臺北市, and 中山, 仁愛, 信義 and 和平 recur all over the island because they are political rather than local names — so a second-level name must always be joined with its county or city. 烏坵鄉 belongs to 金門縣 on paper but lies 100 km away off 莆田 and is garrisoned rather than governed; it keeps its own telephone code 0826 and its own postal code and is in this list because the register has it.
