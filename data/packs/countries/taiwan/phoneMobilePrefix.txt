address: taiwan.phoneMobilePrefix
description: The four-digit opening blocks of Taiwanese mobile numbers, as allocated by the 國家通訊傳播委員會 and published in its 行動通信網路業務用戶號碼核配現況. A Taiwanese mobile number is TEN DIGITS INCLUDING THE LEADING ZERO — 0912-345-678 — of which these four are the first, and six subscriber digits follow. The numbering plan reserves the whole 090 to 098 range for mobile service; 099 was the 隨身碼 personal-number service and was shut down on 2026-02-28, and this list is the union of the blocks actually assigned to operators inside 090-098, which is 72 of the 90 possible. THE PREFIX IS NOT A CARRIER KEY AND HAS NOT BEEN SINCE 2005, when 號碼可攜 (number portability) came in: a 0912 number was issued by 中華電信 but the person holding it today may be on any network, and Taiwanese porting rates are high because the three operators compete hard on price. Worse, the operator set itself changed: 亞太電信 merged into 遠傳電信 and 台灣之星 into 台灣大哥大 in December 2023, so the blocks 0906, 0907, 0977, 0980, 0982, 0985 (亞太) and 0902, 0908, 0973, 0986 (台灣之星) now belong to carriers whose names do not appear in any allocation table older than 2024. Taiwan has THREE mobile operators, not five, and had five two years ago. See taiwan.telecom.operator. Two facts a foreign model gets wrong. There is no fixed-mobile length ambiguity to worry about — a Taiwanese mobile is always ten digits national and a landline is nine or ten depending on the area code, but the leading 09 is unambiguous. And the trunk zero is REAL and must be dropped for international use: the number a Taiwanese person writes on a business card is 0912-345-678 and the same number from abroad is +886-912-345-678, so a naive concatenation of +886 and the printed number produces +8860912345678, which reaches nothing. See taiwan.phone for the E.164 form.
