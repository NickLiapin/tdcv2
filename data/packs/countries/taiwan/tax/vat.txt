description: TAIWAN ISSUES NO SEPARATE VAT NUMBER, and this file exists to say so rather than to invent one. A business registered for 營業稅 in Taiwan is identified by the eight-digit 統一編號 it already has — no VAT-specific serial, no extra check digit, no "TW" prefix, because Taiwan is not in the EU, is not in VIES, and is not in the ISO country-code registries those schemes key on. The value drawn here is therefore deliberately identical in shape to taiwan.tax.ubn: that IS the VAT number of a Taiwanese company, and a schema carrying taxId and vatId separately will find them equal in every row, which is correct. Four consequences worth designing around. THE TAX IS CALLED 加值型營業稅 and the headline rate is 5 per cent, the lowest general consumption-tax rate in Asia after Japan's early years and less than a third of the EU average, so a European default of 19-25 per cent applied to Taiwanese rows overstates the tax by a factor of four or five. IT IS ALWAYS INCLUDED IN THE DISPLAYED PRICE by law, so a Taiwanese price tag, menu, receipt and web checkout total is gross, and a system that adds tax at checkout has charged 5 per cent twice. THE INVOICE IS A LOTTERY TICKET, which has no counterpart anywhere else and changes what an invoice record has to carry: every sale must issue a 統一發票 (Government Uniform Invoice) bearing an eight-character number in the form AB-12345678, and every two months the Ministry of Finance draws numbers and pays prizes from NT$200 to NT$10,000,000 against the last three to eight digits — so the invoice number is a bearer instrument, is drawn from a centrally allocated block rather than from the seller's own sequence, and cannot be modelled as an incrementing per-seller counter. Buyers who want the invoice attributed to their company give the seller their 統一編號 at the point of sale, which is the everyday reason a Taiwanese person knows their employer's UBN by heart. And a business below the small-scale threshold pays 1 per cent 營業稅 assessed by the tax office rather than 5 per cent on its own returns, so "registered for business tax" is not one status and the rate cannot be read off the identifier.
