description: 中華民國汽車號牌 in the series in force since 2012 — three capital letters, a hyphen, four digits, black on white for a private car, 38 x 16 cm. THE LETTERS I AND O ARE NEVER USED and that is a documented commitment rather than a guess: the 交通部公路局 kept them out to avoid confusion with 1 and 0, and although the fixed three-letters-then-four-digits layout makes confusion impossible, the promise was made and has been kept, so the alphabet is the 24 letters A-Z less I and O. A generator using the full alphabet emits plates that cannot exist, at a rate of about one in five. THE PLATE ENCODES NOTHING ABOUT THE PLACE. Unlike a Mongolian, a Russian or an Italian plate, a Taiwanese one carries no region code: the letters are a series (字軌) issued sequentially by whichever 監理站 happens to be handing them out, so a plate says roughly WHEN the car was registered and nothing about where. The published ranges for a private car are AJF-DZZ, FAA-GZZ and UAA-VZZ in the current 8代2版 series and AAA-DZZ, FAA-QZZ, SAA-VZZ and XAA-ZZZ in the 8代1版 that ran alongside it, so a real plate falls in those bands; this file draws from the whole 24-letter alphabet and is therefore well-formed rather than in-series. THE PLATE GOES WITH THE CAR AND DIES WITH IT — a number is issued once, follows the vehicle rather than the owner, and is never recycled after the vehicle is scrapped, so a plate is a permanent unique key for a vehicle in a way a British or an American plate is not. Three older layouts are still legal on the road and a Taiwanese vehicle dataset contains all of them: the 7代 (1992式) two-character forms AB-0001, M9-0001, 2R-0001 and the reversed 0001-DA and 0001-A2, which put the digits first. Motorcycles are a separate and much larger population; see taiwan.vehicle.plateMotorcycle. Special plates are not generated: 使 and 外 for diplomatic vehicles, plus temporary, test, agricultural and heavy-machinery plates, all use different layouts. Colour carries meaning — white is private, yellow is commercial (a 計程車 taxi is a yellow plate), red is a heavy truck or trailer, green is a light motorcycle — so plate colour is a field in Taiwanese vehicle data and is not decoration.
