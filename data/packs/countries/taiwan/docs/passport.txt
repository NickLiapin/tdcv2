description: 中華民國護照號碼 — nine digits, no letter, no check digit published by 外交部領事事務局. The ROC passport is the identity document with the most political weight of anything in this pack and the one whose data model has changed most recently: the cover was restyled on 2021-01-11 to shrink 中華民國 and REPUBLIC OF CHINA almost to invisibility and enlarge TAIWAN, precisely because holders were being mistaken for PRC citizens at borders during the pandemic. The document code on the machine-readable zone is P and the issuing-state code is TWN, which is not an ISO 3166-1 country code assigned to a UN member but is nevertheless what every border system reads. NO CHECKSUM IS COMPUTED HERE because none is published: the passport number's own check digit lives in the MRZ, computed over the number by the ICAO 9303 7-3-1 rule like every other passport in the world, and that is a property of the machine-readable line rather than of the number, so a nine-digit passport number standing alone in a column has nothing to validate against. Two things a schema should hold. The number changes at every renewal — it is not a stable person key, unlike taiwan.docs.nationalId which is — so a passenger record keyed on passport number will not join to the same person ten years later. And the ROC issues a second passport type, 無戶籍國民護照, to nationals without household registration in Taiwan (mostly overseas Chinese communities): it looks identical, carries no 身分證字號, and does NOT confer the right to live in Taiwan, so "has an ROC passport" and "is a resident of Taiwan" are different facts about different sets of people.
