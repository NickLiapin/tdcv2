---
address: taiwan.docs.uiNumber
description: 新式外來人口統一證號 — the Uniform ID Number carried by every foreign national resident in Taiwan since 2021-01-02, and the reason this pack has two identity-number files instead of one. It is one letter and nine digits, it uses EXACTLY the arithmetic of taiwan.docs.nationalId — same letter-to-two-digits table, same weights 1,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1,1, same mod 10 — and it differs in one character: the second digit is 8 for a man and 9 for a woman, where a citizen's number has 1 or 2. That is the whole change, and it was the point of the reform. THE OLD FORMAT WAS A DIFFERENT SHAPE AND BROKE EVERYTHING IT TOUCHED: until 2021 a foreign resident got TWO letters and eight digits (AC00000014), which no Taiwanese form, bank system, ticketing site, hospital registration desk or government portal was built to accept, because all of them validated against the one-letter nine-digit citizen pattern. Foreign residents in Taiwan spent twenty years unable to buy a high-speed rail ticket online or open an account without a manual override. The 移民署 published the change as 新式外來人口統一證號 and issued the first new numbers on 2021-01-02; the official worked example in the leaflets is A800000014, whose old-format twin was AC00000014. Holders of an old number keep it until they renew, so BOTH FORMATS ARE IN CIRCULATION and a Taiwanese identity column in the wild contains one-letter-nine-digit citizen numbers, one-letter-nine-digit resident numbers, and two-letter-eight-digit legacy resident numbers — three patterns, one column, and only the first two share a checksum. The old two-letter form used a different conversion for the second letter and is NOT generated here. A number here is well-formed, not issued.
generator: tdc

<sequence name="area"><gen type="template" value="taiwan.docs.idAreaLetter"/></sequence>
<!-- 8 male, 9 female. This is the only structural difference from a citizen's number. -->
<sequence name="sex"><gen type="text" value="8,9"/></sequence>
<sequence name="serial"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{7}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="uin"><compute>
  <let name="idx"><subtract>
    <to_number><encode as="base36"><field name="area"/></encode></to_number>
    <int v="10"/>
  </subtract></let>
  <let name="code"><at>
    <in><list v="10,11,12,13,14,15,16,17,34,18,19,20,21,22,35,23,24,25,26,27,28,29,32,30,31,33"/></in>
    <index><use name="idx"/></index>
  </at></let>
  <let name="letterPart"><add>
    <divide><use name="code"/><int v="10"/></divide>
    <multiply><mod><use name="code"/><int v="10"/></mod><int v="9"/></multiply>
  </add></let>
  <let name="base"><concat><field name="sex"/><field name="serial"/></concat></let>
  <let name="sum"><reduce>
    <over><use name="base"/></over>
    <init><use name="letterPart"/></init>
    <do><add><acc/><multiply><current/>
      <at><in><list v="8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1"/></in><index><current_index/></index></at>
    </multiply></add></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><mod><subtract><int v="10"/><mod><use name="sum"/><int v="10"/></mod></subtract><int v="10"/></mod></let>
  <result><concat><field name="area"/><use name="base"/><use name="check"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{uin}}</data>
