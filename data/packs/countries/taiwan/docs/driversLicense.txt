description: 中華民國汽車駕駛執照號碼 — AND IT IS THE HOLDER'S NATIONAL ID NUMBER, not a separate series. This file exists to say that rather than to invent a format. The 交通部公路局 prints 統一編號 on the licence and uses it as the licence number, so for a citizen the driving licence number IS taiwan.docs.nationalId, character for character, and for a foreign resident it is taiwan.docs.uiNumber. A schema that carries idNumber and licenceNumber as two columns for a Taiwanese driver will find them equal in every row, and THAT IS CORRECT rather than a data-quality defect. The consequence worth designing around is the reverse one: a Taiwanese driving licence is not an anonymous credential. Handing it over at a car rental desk hands over the number that keys the holder's household registration, tax file, health insurance and bank identity, which is why Taiwanese privacy guidance treats a photographed licence as a leaked ID. Two further facts a foreign model misses. The licence has NO EXPIRY for holders under 75 since the 2013 reform abolished the six-year renewal — the 有效日期 field on an ordinary Taiwanese car licence reads 無期限 — so an expiry column will be empty for most of the population and then suddenly populated, at two-year intervals, for everyone over 75. And car and motorcycle licences are separate documents with separate tests, both bearing the same number, so one person legitimately holds two licences that cannot be told apart by their number.
