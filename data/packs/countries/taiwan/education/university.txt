description: 臺灣現存的大學，公立與私立、普通大學與科技大學都在內。TAIWAN HAS AROUND 145 HIGHER-EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS (大專院校) FOR 23 MILLION PEOPLE, of which the 112 listed here are the ones carrying the title 大學 — roughly one university per 200,000 people, among the highest densities in the world, and the fact that shapes this list: the 1994 教改 opened the sector, almost every 專科學校 was upgraded to a 技術學院 and then to a 科技大學, and the result is that more than half the names here end in 科技大學 rather than 大學. THE TWO TRACKS ARE SEPARATE SYSTEMS, NOT A PRESTIGE GRADIENT: 普通大學 recruit through the 學測/分科 examinations and 科技大學 through the 統測 from vocational high schools, the curricula are different, and a student normally cannot cross between them — so "university" in Taiwanese data is two populations with different entry routes, and a schema that ranks them on one scale has mismodelled the system. THE SECTOR IS SHRINKING FAST and the list goes stale quickly. Taiwan's birth rate is the lowest in the world; the cohort that entered university in 2016 was the first of the collapse, and universities have been closing or merging since — 明道大學 and several others have shut, and the Ministry of Education projects more. Two mergers already in this list are traps for a historical join: 國立陽明交通大學 is the 2021 merger of 國立交通大學 and 國立陽明大學, so records before 2021 name two institutions that are now one, and 國立高雄科技大學 is the 2018 merger of three. 國立清華大學 absorbed 國立新竹教育大學 in 2016. THE NAME PREFIX IS A LEGAL STATUS: 國立 means national, funded by the 教育部; 市立 means municipal, of which there are exactly two (臺北市立大學 and 高雄市立空中大學); anything with no prefix is private. 國防大學 answers to the 國防部 rather than the 教育部. 國立空中大學 is an open university that teaches by broadcast and has more students than most of the campuses on this list. The old shorthand 台清交成 — 臺大, 清大, 交大, 成大 — is what a Taiwanese employer means by a top university, and 臺大 alone occupies a position in Taiwanese life that has no single-institution equivalent in most countries.
