description: Bahamian banks. The Central Bank of the Bahamas issues the Bahamian dollar and pegs it one-to-one to the US dollar, and both circulate interchangeably — a shopper in Nassau routinely receives change in a mixture. The offshore sector is far larger than the domestic one: the Bahamas has been an international financial centre since the 1930s, and the number of licensed banks exceeds anything the resident population would need.
