description: Central African public holidays. Two dates carry the country's founding: Boganda Day on 29 March for Barthélemy Boganda, who led the country to autonomy and died in a plane crash a year before independence, and National Day on 1 December for the 1958 proclamation of the republic. Independence from France is 13 August 1960. The Catholic calendar supplies the rest — the CAR is majority Christian — and Ramadan's end is observed for the Muslim minority in the north.
