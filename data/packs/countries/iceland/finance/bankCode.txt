address: iceland.finance.bankCode
description: The four-digit bank-and-branch number (bankanúmer) that opens every Icelandic account number and every Icelandic IBAN. It is really two fields glued together: the first two digits are the INSTITUTION and the last two are one of its branches. The institution blocks are the part this file asserts as real, and they are few, because Icelandic retail banking is a three-firm market. 01 is Landsbankinn, 03 is Arion banki, 05 is Íslandsbanki, 07 is Kvika banki, 11 is the savings banks (sparisjóðir) collectively, and 22 is indó, the first new Icelandic deposit-taker in a generation. That list is short for a reason worth stating: the 2008 collapse took the entire commercial banking sector with it, all three big banks were rebuilt inside eleven days in October 2008 (their founding dates are legible in their kennitölur — see iceland.docs.kennitalaCompany), and the sparisjóður movement that once had dozens of local banks has been reduced to three institutions trading under one Sparisjóðurinn brand plus indó. So an Icelandic bank code beginning 02, 04 or 06 belongs to an institution that no longer exists. THE BRANCH HALF IS DRAWN, NOT ASSERTED: Iceland publishes no open register of branch numbers, so this generator picks the last two digits at random within the institution's block and a code it produces may name a branch that has closed or never opened. That is stated rather than papered over, because a fabricated branch list dressed as a real one is worse than a random pair of digits. The institution weights are approximately the retail market: Landsbankinn, Íslandsbanki and Arion held 36.1%, 31% and 30% of Icelandic current accounts respectively at the last published measurement, with a few points moved here to the three small houses.
generator: tdc
locale: is
