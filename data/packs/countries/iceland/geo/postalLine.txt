address: iceland.geo.postalLine
description: The last line of an Icelandic address, exactly as Iceland Post prints it: the three-digit postal code, a space, and the delivery place IN THE DATIVE CASE. THE CASE IS THE POINT AND IT IS THE THING A FOREIGN ADDRESS MODEL GETS WRONG. Icelandic marks grammatical case with endings, and the postal line answers the question "where?", so the town is not named but located: the town is Kópavogur and the address line is 200 Kópavogi; Hafnarfjörður becomes 220 Hafnarfirði, Akranes becomes 300 Akranesi, Selfoss becomes 800 Selfossi, Vestmannaeyjar becomes 900 Vestmannaeyjum, Ísafjörður becomes 400 Ísafirði, Egilsstaðir becomes 700 Egilsstöðum. Some names do not change (101 Reykjavík, 810 Hveragerði, 240 Grindavík) and that is not an inconsistency, it is what those declensions do. So joining iceland.geo.city to iceland.geo.postalCode yields "200 Kópavogur", which is a string no Icelandic envelope has ever carried; this file is the string it does carry. Values are the register at postur.is/gogn/Gotuskra/postnumer.txt, restricted to delivery codes with residents, and weighted by the population of the code on 1 January 2026 (Statistics Iceland, MAN03200). Note that the place name here is the DELIVERY place and not always the settlement: 230 and 260 are both Reykjanesbæ although the towns are Keflavík and Njarðvík, 225 is Garðabæ although the place is Álftanes, and 116 is Reykjavík although the district is Kjalarnes.
locale: is
weighted: true
