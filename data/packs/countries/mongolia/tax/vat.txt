description: MONGOLIA ISSUES NO SEPARATE VAT NUMBER, and this file exists to say so rather than to invent one. A person registered for нэмэгдсэн өртгийн албан татвар (VAT) in Mongolia is identified on the invoice by the number they already have — the seven-digit taxpayer number for a company, the ten-character registration number for an individual — with no VAT-specific serial, no additional check digit and no "MN" country prefix, because Mongolia is not in the EU and its numbers are not part of the VIES scheme. The value drawn here is therefore deliberately identical in shape to mongolia.tax.tin: that IS the VAT number of a Mongolian company, and a schema carrying taxId and vatId as separate columns for a Mongolian party will find them equal in every row, which is correct. Three consequences worth designing around. Registration is a threshold rather than a status you can read off the number — it is compulsory once taxable turnover passes 50 million tögrög and voluntary from 10 million — so whether a given Mongolian company charges VAT cannot be derived from its identifier and has to be modelled as its own flag. The rate is a single 10 per cent, the lowest headline VAT rate in the region and lower than any EU member's, so a European default of 19-25 per cent applied to Mongolian rows will overstate tax by a factor of two. And VAT compliance in Mongolia runs through ebarimt, the electronic receipt system, in which every retail sale generates a numbered receipt tied to the seller's taxpayer number and, optionally, the buyer's — so a Mongolian sales record generally carries a receipt identifier as well as a VAT number, and a model with only the latter is missing the field the tax authority actually reconciles on.
