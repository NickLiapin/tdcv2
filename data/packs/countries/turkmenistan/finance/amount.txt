---
address: turkmenistan.finance.amount
description: A sum of money written the way Turkmenistan writes it: groups of three separated by a NO-BREAK SPACE, a COMMA for the decimal point, and the currency AFTER the figure behind another no-break space — 1 234 567,89 TMT. That is the tk-TM pattern in CLDR ("#,##0.00 ¤", group U+00A0, decimal ","), and it disagrees with the English 1,234,567.89 on every count, which is how a Turkmen amount gets silently corrupted: a comma read as a thousands separator turns 1 234,56 into 123456, a factor of a hundred, and both readings are valid numbers so nothing downstream complains. THE SEPARATORS HERE ARE U+00A0, NOT ORDINARY SPACES — invisible in an editor, and the reason a naive split on " " gives the wrong field count. The unit printed is the ISO code TMT because CLDR supplies tk-TM with no local currency symbol; Turkmen everyday writing uses "m" after the figure, and the banknotes and the Central Bank use the word manat spelled out, so all three forms occur and none of them is the only right one. Two decimals are kept and they are real money: teňňe coins are still minted down to 1 teňňe, so a Turkmen price genuinely can end in ,07 rather than rounding to the manat. Note that a figure written this way says nothing about what it is worth: the official peg has been 3.5 manats to the dollar since January 2015 while cash exchange is illegal and the street rate is many times that, so the same printed amount converts to two very different dollar figures. The magnitude is spread across five decades rather than drawn uniformly, because a uniform draw over a seven-digit range puts nearly every value in the millions and a Turkmen price list is mostly two, three and four figures. Nothing here is a number the engine can do arithmetic on: it is the PRINTED form.
generator: tdc
locale: tk
---
<sequence name="raw"><gen type="number" value="1..9999999"/></sequence>
<sequence name="shift"><gen type="number" value="0..4"/></sequence>
<sequence name="tenne"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="amount"><compute>
  <!-- Spread the magnitude: divide the 7-digit draw by 1, 10, 100, 1000 or 10000. -->
  <let name="scale"><at>
    <in><list v="1,10,100,1000,10000"/></in>
    <index><to_number><field name="shift"/></to_number></index>
  </at></let>
  <let name="whole"><divide><to_number><field name="raw"/></to_number><use name="scale"/></divide></let>
  <!-- CLDR tk-TM: group separator U+00A0, decimal comma, pattern "#,##0.00 ¤". -->
  <let name="grouped"><group size="3" sep=" "><use name="whole"/></group></let>
  <result><concat><use name="grouped"/><str v=","/><field name="tenne"/><str v=" TMT"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{amount}}</data>
