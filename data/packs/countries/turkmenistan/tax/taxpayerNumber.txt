description: The Turkmen individual taxpayer number (indiwidual salgyt belgisi, ИНН in the Russian-language paperwork that most Turkmen commerce still runs on) — twelve digits, issued to a person and to a company alike, and quoted on every declaration, invoice and application. THE ISSUER HAS MOVED and English sources have not caught up: what they still call the Main State Tax Service now runs as the Salgyt müdiriýeti, a directorate inside the Ministry of Finance and Economy, which is what tax.gov.tm titles itself — so the number is assigned by a department of a ministry rather than by a standalone revenue body. It is ONE number for both, which is the first thing to know about it: Turkmenistan has no separate company registration number in the sense of a Polish REGON or a Norwegian organisasjonsnummer, so the taxpayer number is the join key for a Turkmen legal entity as well as for a Turkmen individual. THERE IS NO PUBLISHED CHECK DIGIT AND THIS GENERATOR COMPUTES NONE. The Tax Code of Turkmenistan (25 October 2005, as amended) says only that "the procedure and conditions for the assignment, application and change of the individual taxpayer number are determined by the Main State Tax Service of Turkmenistan" — it delegates the format and does not state it, and the Service publishes nothing. A twelve-digit number that carried a plausible mod-11 tail would look verified and would not be, which is worse than an honestly unchecked one. THE LENGTH ITSELF IS SECOND-HAND and is flagged here rather than buried: twelve digits is what commercial guides to working in Turkmenistan state (and two independent ones agree), not what a Turkmen source states, and the one aggregator that claims a range says "7 to 20 characters", which is a way of saying it does not know either. Turkmenistan does levy value-added tax at 15% (goşulan baha üçin salgyt), but it issues no separate VAT identifier the way the EU does — a registered business quotes this number — so there is no turkmenistan.tax.vat address in this pack and a routine that expects one should not be pointed at Turkmenistan.
