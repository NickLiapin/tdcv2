description: The number of a Turkmen internal passport (içerki pasport, formally raýatlyk pasporty) — a Roman numeral, a hyphen, the two-letter code of the issuing province, then six digits: I-AŞ 235123. This is the document that identifies a person INSIDE Turkmenistan, and it is a different book from the biometric passport used to leave the country; the two were separated in 2008 and a Turkmen citizen normally holds only the internal one. THERE IS NO CHECK DIGIT. That is not an omission in this pack: Turkmenistan publishes no checksum for this number, none for the biometric passport number, none for the taxpayer number, and there is no Turkmen IBAN to compute a mod-97 over, so nothing anywhere in this pack computes a check digit and nothing here should be taken as validating one. What IS published is the shape. The province code comes from turkmenistan.docs.passportSeries and is the register's own list. The pattern "Roman numeral - two letters - six digits" and the six-digit length come from the specimen the Turkmenistan consular service in Moscow publishes, which reads I-AŞ 235123, issued by the police department of Azatlyk district of Aşgabat in 1998. THE ROMAN NUMERAL IS FIXED AT I HERE, deliberately: I is the only numeral attested in a published specimen, later series numerals are the ordinary Soviet-inherited convention and would be a guess, and a guess that looks like a fact is the thing this pack most wants to avoid. Note the district on that specimen — Azatlyk etrap of Aşgabat was abolished in the January 2018 reorganisation that reduced the capital to four boroughs, so a valid, current, lifelong passport can name a district that no longer exists, and a system that validates the issuing office against a current list will reject its own citizens.
