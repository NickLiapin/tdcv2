description: The geographic dialling codes of Turkmenistan. The national significant number is EIGHT digits, so the code and the subscriber number share a fixed budget and the split moves: Aşgabat's code is the two-digit 12 and its subscribers carry six digits, the provincial codes are three digits and their subscribers carry five, and the four four-digit codes here (1354, 1392, 2444) leave four. That is the single most important fact in this file, because the secondary sites that describe Turkmenistan as "+993 XX XXXXXX" produce nine digits for anything outside the capital, and nine-digit Turkmen numbers do not exist. Two published numbers pin both halves down: Türkmenpoçta's head office in Aşgabat is (+993 12) 27 01 06, six digits after 12; the Demirbetonönümleri works in Türkmenabat is listed by the Ministry of Industry and Construction Production as (+993 422) 7-98-21, five digits after 422. The first digit of a geographic code is the province — 1 Ahal and the capital, 2 Balkan, 3 Daşoguz, 4 Lebap, 5 Mary — and the provincial capitals are 222 Balkanabat, 322 Daşoguz, 422 Türkmenabat, 522 Mary. THE PLACE NAMES ATTACHED TO THESE CODES IN CIRCULATING TABLES ARE OFTEN STALE and the codes are not: 246 is still the code for the town that was called Serdar from 2002 and is called Gyzylarbat again since 2022, and the two codes listed against "Nyýazow" (348 and 432) belong to districts renamed in the 2022 reorganisation. Trunk prefix is 8 and international access is 8~10, both inherited from the Soviet plan. Source: the national numbering plan as tabulated on en.wikipedia; Turkmenistan does not publish its plan in a form this pack could reach, and no ITU numbering-plan sheet for TM was found.
