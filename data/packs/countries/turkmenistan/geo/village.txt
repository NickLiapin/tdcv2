description: The 446 villages (oba) that are the SEAT of a rural council (geňeşlik) in Turkmenistan, weighted by their 2022 census population. Turkmenistan counts 1 692 villages in all; these are the ones that head a geňeşlik, which is the unit a rural address is actually filed under and the reason the UPU's Turkmen address example reads "oba Ýokary-şor, ş-çe Beşir" — village inside town inside district inside province. They are included because more than half the country is rural (the 2022 census puts the rural share at 52.9%), so a Turkmen address drawn only from cities is wrong about where most people live. The tail of this list is small: the smallest seats hold a few hundred people, and the plain villages that are not seats, which are not listed here, go down to single figures. Source: the 2022 population census of Turkmenistan (17 December 2022), the only national count published since 1995, as tabulated per municipality on en.wikipedia from the State Statistics Committee release. THE COUNT IS DISPUTED: independent observers, RFE/RL among them, hold that the published total of 7 057 841 overstates the real population, which emigration has cut hard since 2010; no alternative figure has been published either, so these are the only numbers there are and they are used here as RELATIVE weights, which survives a uniform inflation of the whole table.
weighted: true
locale: tk
