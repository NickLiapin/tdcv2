---
address: east_timor.finance.iban
description: Timor-Leste IBAN — 23 chars: TL + 2 ISO 7064 mod-97-10 check digits + a 19-digit BBAN (3-digit bank code + 14-digit account + 2-digit national check). The IBAN check digits are computed exactly; the 2-digit national check inside the BBAN is generated as plain digits because its algorithm is not published.
generator: tdc
locale: pt
---
<sequence name="bank"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{3}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="account"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{14}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="nacional"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="iban"><compute>
  <!-- ISO 13616: move "TL00" to the end, expand letters to their base-36 value
       (T -> 29, L -> 21), Horner-fold mod 97, check = 98 - remainder. -->
  <let name="bban"><concat><field name="bank"/><field name="account"/><field name="nacional"/></concat></let>
  <let name="rearranged"><concat><use name="bban"/><str v="TL"/><str v="00"/></concat></let>
  <let name="digits"><join><each>
    <over><use name="rearranged"/></over>
    <do><encode as="base36"><current/></encode></do>
  </each></join></let>
  <let name="rem"><reduce>
    <over><use name="digits"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><mod><add><multiply><acc/><int v="10"/></multiply><current/></add><int v="97"/></mod></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><pad width="2" fill="0"><subtract><int v="98"/><use name="rem"/></subtract></pad></let>
  <result><concat><str v="TL"/><use name="check"/><field name="bank"/><field name="account"/><field name="nacional"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{iban}}</data>
