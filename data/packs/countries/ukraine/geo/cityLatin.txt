description: The ukraine.geo.city list under Ukraine's own official romanisation — Cabinet of Ministers resolution No. 55 of 27 January 2010, the table used on passports, road signs and in the state's dealings with foreign registers. It is not a transliteration of Russian and it is not BGN/PCGN, which is why the capital is Kyiv and not Kiev, and why the Dnipro city is Dnipro and not Dnepr. The rules that bite: г = h and ґ = g (so Uzhhorod, Luhansk); и = y and і = i (Kryvyi Rih); х = kh, ц = ts, щ = shch; є ї й ю я take ye yi y yu ya at the start of a word and ie i i iu ia inside it (Yevpatoriia, Sievierodonetsk, Zaporizhzhia); the soft sign and the apostrophe are simply dropped (Sloviansk, Kamianets-Podilskyi); зг becomes zgh so it cannot be read back as ж. Every line here was produced mechanically from the Cyrillic list by that table, line for line, so the two files stay in the same order and can be drawn as a pair. No `locale:` is declared: these are Ukrainian place names in Latin script, not English words, and calling them English would file Ukraine's own romanisation under somebody else's language.
