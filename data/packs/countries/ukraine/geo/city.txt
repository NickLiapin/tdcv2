description: Cities of Ukraine in their official Ukrainian names, roughly ordered by population before 2022. The names are the current register forms, which for many cities are not the ones a decade-old dataset carries — Дніпро was Дніпропетровськ until 2016, Кропивницький was Кіровоград, Кам'янське was Дніпродзержинськ. Renaming has not stopped, so a name here can be superseded by a later act of parliament. Cities in areas the government does not currently control are kept, because Ukrainian registers keep them. See ukraine.geo.cityLatin for the same list under the state's official 2010 romanisation.
