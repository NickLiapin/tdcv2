address: ukraine.tax.edrpou
description: ЄДРПОУ — the code every legal entity in Ukraine gets from the Unified State Register of Enterprises and Organisations. Eight digits, the last a check digit, and leading zeros are part of the code, not decoration: Ощадбанк is 00032129 and writing it 32129 makes it a different company. The check is a weighted mod 11 with a twist no neighbour shares — the weight vector depends on the value of the code itself. Codes in the band 30000000-60000000 use 7,1,2,3,4,5,6 over the first seven digits; everything below the band and everything above it uses 1,2,3,4,5,6,7. That split is a fossil of the register's own history, the low band holding Soviet-era and early-1990s registrations. If the remainder is 10 the whole sum is recomputed with every weight raised by 2 (so 3,4,5,6,7,8,9 or 9,3,4,5,6,7,8) and the result taken mod 11 then mod 10, which is why a second pass appears below. The boundary case is written here as "below 6000000 in the first seven digits", because the published rule states the band on the full eight-digit code and the check digit is exactly what is not yet known when the band has to be chosen; the ambiguity covers one prefix in nine million.
generator: tdc
