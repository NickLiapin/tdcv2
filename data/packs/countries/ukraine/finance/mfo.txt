address: ukraine.finance.mfo
description: МФО — the six-digit code the National Bank of Ukraine gives every licensed bank, and the field that sits at positions 5-10 of a Ukrainian IBAN. These are the real codes of banks holding a licence: 305299 ПриватБанк, 300465 Ощадбанк, 322313 Укрексімбанк, 322001 Універсал Банк (monobank), 300335 Райффайзен Банк, 334851 ПУМБ. The code carries no check digit and is not derived from the bank's name or its ЄДРПОУ; it is simply assigned, and it is reassigned to nobody when a bank is wound up. The list is NOT positionally paired with ukraine.finance.bank — drawing both in one row will not give you a bank and its own code. ukraine.finance.iban draws its bank code from this file, so every IBAN generated carries a real МФО.
