description: Banks holding a licence from the National Bank of Ukraine, in the short names Ukrainians actually use rather than the registered "АТ КБ ..." forms. The sector is unusually state-heavy for Europe: Ощадбанк and Укрексімбанк have always been state banks, ПриватБанк was nationalised in 2016 and Сенс Банк in 2023, and those four alone hold most of the retail deposits. Універсал Банк is the licence behind monobank, which has no branches at all. Not positionally paired with ukraine.finance.mfo — that file lists real codes, this one real names, but line N of one is not the bank on line N of the other.
