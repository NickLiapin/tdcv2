description: УНЗР — унікальний номер запису в Єдиному державному демографічному реєстрі, the lifetime record number printed on the ID card and in the biometric passport. Written РРРРММДД-ННННК: the birth date in full ISO order, a hyphen, a four-digit serial whose parity carries sex (odd male, even female, so the first codes issued for a given day are 0001 and 0002), and one check digit. It is assigned once, at the first biometric document, and never changes — not on marriage, not on a change of name, not when the card itself is reissued with a new number. Note that Ukraine therefore has TWO lifetime personal numbers, the tax РНОКПП and this one, and they encode the same birth date in two different ways: РНОКПП as a day count from 1899, УНЗР as plain calendar digits. CAVEAT, and it is the one soft spot in this pack: the check digit here is the ICAO 9303 weighted mod-10 (weights 7,3,1 repeating, sum mod 10) over the twelve preceding digits, which is the method the state's own explanations name, but unlike the РНОКПП and ЄДРПОУ in this pack I could not find a single published УНЗР to test it against in either direction. Treat this digit as documented-but-unverified.
