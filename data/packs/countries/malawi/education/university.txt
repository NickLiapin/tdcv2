description: Malawian universities. The University of Malawi was for decades the only one and was federal — a single university with constituent colleges in different towns — until 2021, when it was broken up: Chancellor College in Zomba kept the University of Malawi name, the Polytechnic in Blantyre became MUBAS, and the medical school and college of nursing became KUHeS. A dataset of Malawian graduates therefore holds institution names that no longer exist for anyone who studied before 2021, and the mapping is not one-to-one.
