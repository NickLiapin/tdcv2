description: The 28 districts of Malawi, the country's first-level administrative division, grouped into three regions. The district is the unit that matters in a Malawian address and in almost every government dataset; the region above it is used for statistics and for the football league but has no administration of its own. Likoma is the smallest by a wide margin — it is an island in Lake Malawi, closer to the Mozambican shore than to the Malawian one, and it is a district all the same.
