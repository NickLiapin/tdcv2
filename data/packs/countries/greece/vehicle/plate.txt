description: Greek vehicle plate — three letters, a hyphen and four digits (e.g. ABE-1234). Only the 14 letters whose shape is common to the Greek and Latin alphabets (ABEZHIKMNOPTYX) are ever issued, so a plate reads the same either way. The letter trigram encodes the issuing prefecture, but that mapping is not modelled here.
