address: uganda.geo.districtRegion
description: The region each district in uganda.geo.district belongs to, index-aligned with it — Central for the first 25 entries, Eastern for the next 37, Northern for the next 38, Western for the last 35. Kept as a parallel list rather than folded into the district name because a Ugandan district is never written "Mbale, Eastern" the way an American city is written "Austin, TX": the region is a statistical grouping used by the Bureau of Statistics and by donors, not part of an address and not a level of government, so it belongs beside the district and not inside it. See uganda.geo.region for what the four regions are and are not. The split is the census one: 25 Central, 37 Eastern, 38 Northern, 35 Western districts, which with the 11 cities makes the 146 administrative units of the 2024 census.
