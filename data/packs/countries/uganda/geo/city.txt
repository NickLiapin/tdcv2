description: Ugandan cities and principal towns ordered by their 9 May 2024 census populations. "City" here is the pack's ordinary sense of a populated place, not the Ugandan legal status — only 11 places hold a city charter (uganda.geo.charteredCity) and five of the ten biggest entries in this list are not among them. The shape of the list is the point: after Kampala at 1.8 million the next four are Nansana, Kira, Makindye-Ssabagabo and Kyengera, none of them a city, all of them inside Wakiso District, all of them effectively continuous with Kampala. Greater Kampala is where Uganda's urban population is, and a generator that draws Ugandan towns uniformly will badly under-weight it. Arua at 384,656 is the largest urban place outside the Kampala orbit, which is itself worth knowing — it sits in the far north-west near the DR Congo and South Sudan borders, not on the Kenyan side where the road and rail money went. FOUR OF UGANDA'S LARGEST SETTLEMENTS ARE DELIBERATELY ABSENT. Kyaka II (90,021), Kyangwali (72,098), Rwamwanja (43,456) and Nakivale are refugee settlements, not towns; the census counts them because roughly 1.8 million refugees live in Uganda, more than in any other African country, under a settlement policy that gives plots and freedom of movement rather than camps. They are real, populous and would rank above Entebbe, and they are still not places a person is "from" or addresses a letter to, so they are held out of this list rather than silently mixed into it. Bweyale IS included: it is a trading town in Kiryandongo that grew up beside a settlement but is a town in its own right. Note the spellings that differ between the town and the district it sits in — the town is Luweero and the district is Luwero.
