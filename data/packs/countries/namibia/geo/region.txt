Several were renamed in 2013 to drop colonial-era and apartheid-era names: Caprivi became Zambezi, Karas became ǁKaras with the lateral click written, and Kavango was split into East and West. The Zambezi Region is the strip in the northeast — the Caprivi Strip, a corridor drawn by Germany in 1890 to reach the Zambezi and never useful for it, and one of the most obviously artificial borders on the continent.
