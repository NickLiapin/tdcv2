description: Namibian towns. Windhoek is the capital and roughly a fifth of the population; the north — Oshakati, Ondangwa, Rundu — holds most of the rest, which is the opposite of what the country's colonial-era infrastructure was built for. Walvis Bay is the deep-water port and was held by South Africa for four years after Namibian independence, not transferring until 1994. Lüderitz and Swakopmund keep their German names from the 1884-1915 colony.
