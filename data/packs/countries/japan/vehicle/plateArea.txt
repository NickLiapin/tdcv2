address: japan.vehicle.plateArea
description: All 138 place names (地名) that appear on the top line of a Japanese registration plate, in the north-to-south order of the 運輸支局 and 自動車検査登録事務所 that issue them. The name is the office that registered the vehicle, not the owner's town, and it must be changed when the vehicle's home base moves to another office's area. Most are kanji, one to four characters; three are hiragana, and each for its own reason - なにわ for Osaka city because the office is the なにわ branch, とちぎ to keep it apart from the 栃木 that Utsunomiya used to carry, いわき as the first hiragana plate in Japan. 尾張小牧 and 伊勢志摩 are the only four-character names, both the result of two towns refusing to lose. Roughly half are ご当地ナンバー, local names added since 2006 on top of the office's own.
locale: ja
