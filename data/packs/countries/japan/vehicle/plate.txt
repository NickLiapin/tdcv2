address: japan.vehicle.plate
description: A Japanese registration plate as it reads, e.g. 品川 300 あ 12-34. Four parts, all meaningful. The place name (japan.vehicle.plateArea) is the registering office. The three-digit 分類番号 encodes the vehicle class in its FIRST digit - 1 large goods, 2 bus, 3 ordinary passenger, 4 and 6 small goods, 5 and 7 small passenger, 8 special purpose, 9 and 0 large special - and this file emits the passenger and goods classes a civilian fleet is made of. The single hiragana encodes use: あいうえかきくけこ and を are commercial (green plates), the rest are private, and わ and れ mark a rental car, which is why tourists notice them. Four kana are never used - お because it reads as あ at speed, し and へ for what they say, ん because it cannot start a word. The serial runs ・・・1 to 99-99 with a middle hyphen at four digits and ・ filling the empty places, so 0000 does not exist. Not modelled: the letters (A, C, F, H and others) that have appeared in the second and third position of the 分類番号 since 1999 once a popular number ran out, and the E H K M T Y よ series reserved for US forces personnel.
