description: Japanese My Number (個人番号) — 12 digits, an 11-digit base plus a trailing check digit. The check follows 平成26年総務省令第85号 第五条 exactly - 11 minus (sum of Pn x Qn) mod 11, where Pn is the nth digit counted from the RIGHT of the base and Qn is n+1 for n=1..6 and n-5 for n=7..11; when that remainder is 0 or 1 the check digit is 0. Read left to right the weights are 6,5,4,3,2,7,6,5,4,3,2. Unlike the corporate number, My Number is NOT public: the Number Act forbids collecting or holding it outside the tax, social-security and disaster-relief uses it lists, which is why no real number appears anywhere in this pack.
generator: tdc
