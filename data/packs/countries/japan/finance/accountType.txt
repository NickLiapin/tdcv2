description: Japanese deposit-account types (預金種目) as printed on a transfer slip, most common first. 普通 is the ordinary account nearly every individual holds; 当座 is the current account a business needs to draw cheques; 貯蓄 and 定期 are savings and fixed-term. The word is written bare on the slip, without 預金, and a transfer form requires it alongside the bank code, branch code and account number.
