description: The four-digit 金融機関コード (statutory bank code), index-aligned with japan.finance.bank, from the Zengin-Net clearing-participant list of 2026-08-03. Japan has NO IBAN and is not in the IBAN registry: a domestic transfer is addressed by this four-digit bank code plus a three-digit branch code plus a seven-digit account number plus the holder's name in katakana, and an inbound international transfer uses a SWIFT BIC instead. Nothing in this pack invents an IBAN for Japan, and a JP-prefixed IBAN found anywhere is fabricated. The blocks are meaningful: 0001-0999 banks, 1000-1999 shinkin, 2000-2999 credit unions and government-affiliated lenders, 3000-3999 agricultural co-operatives, 9900 ゆうちょ銀行 alone.
