description: The 47 prefectures of Japan in JIS X 0401 order, index-aligned with japan.geo.prefectureCode. The suffix is part of the name and carries a distinct legal history - 都 belongs to Tokyo alone (a metropolis that absorbed a city and now has 23 special wards instead), 道 to Hokkaido alone (a "circuit", never a prefecture in the old sense), 府 to the two former imperial capitals Kyoto and Osaka, and 県 to the other 43. All four are legally equal today, but the name is 東京都 and never 東京県, so the suffix cannot be swapped or dropped in formal use.
