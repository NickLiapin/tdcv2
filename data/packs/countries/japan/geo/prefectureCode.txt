address: japan.geo.prefectureCode
description: JIS X 0401 prefecture codes 01-47, index-aligned with japan.geo.prefecture. These are the first two digits of every 全国地方公共団体コード and of the municipality code in Japan Post's postal-code file, so they are the join key between a prefecture and its municipalities. They are NOT the driving-licence 公安委員会 code and NOT the postal-code prefix; those are three unrelated numbering schemes.
