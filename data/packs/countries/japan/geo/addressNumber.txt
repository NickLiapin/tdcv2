address: japan.geo.addressNumber
description: The 丁目・番・号 block that ends a Japanese address — the part a foreign form would call a street number, and is not one. A Japanese address is written large to small (prefecture, municipality, district, then this), and Japan has almost no named streets: 丁目 is a numbered sub-district, 番 a numbered land block within it, 号 a numbered building within the block, so the three numbers walk you inward through nested areas rather than along a line of doors. Two written forms are in use and both appear here: the spelled-out 1丁目9番1号 and the contracted 1-9-1. Kyoto and parts of Sapporo are the exception that proves the rule - they were laid out on a grid and do use street names - and neither is modelled here.
