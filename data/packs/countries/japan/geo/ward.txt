address: japan.geo.ward
description: The 23 special wards (特別区) of Tokyo, in the statutory order used by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. These are not sub-units of a city - Tokyo City was abolished in 1943 and each ward is a municipality with its own mayor, assembly and residence register, which is why an address in central Tokyo reads 東京都 + ward and never names a city. The 20 designated cities elsewhere (Yokohama, Osaka, Nagoya and so on) also have wards, but those are 行政区: administrative divisions of the city with no council of their own, a different thing under the same character 区.
