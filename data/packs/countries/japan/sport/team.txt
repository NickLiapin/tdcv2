description: Japanese professional clubs — the twelve baseball clubs of Nippon Professional Baseball first, then association-football clubs of the J.League. No division is asserted for the football clubs: promotion and relegation move them between J1, J2 and J3 every season, and the J.League switched to an autumn-to-spring calendar in 2026, so any J1 list here would be stale within a year. A club's name in Japan carries its corporate sponsor for baseball (福岡ソフトバンク, 東北楽天) and its city or region for football, which is a legacy of the J.League's founding rule that a club be named for a place rather than a company.
