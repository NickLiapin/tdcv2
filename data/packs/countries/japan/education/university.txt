description: Japanese universities that exist, national first, then public, then private. Names are the current legal ones, which in Japan move more than one would expect - 東京科学大学 is the 2024 merger of 東京工業大学 and 東京医科歯科大学, 大阪公立大学 the 2022 merger of the city and prefectural universities of Osaka, and 東京都立大学 reverted from 首都大学東京 in 2020. The 大学 suffix is part of the name; a Japanese form asks for the whole string, not an abbreviation.
