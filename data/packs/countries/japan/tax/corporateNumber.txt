---
address: japan.tax.corporateNumber
description: Japanese corporate number (法人番号) — 13 digits assigned by the National Tax Agency. The check digit is the LEADING digit, not the trailing one, computed per 財務省令第七十号 第二条 as 9 minus (sum of Pn x Qn) mod 9, where Pn counts from the right of the 12-digit base and Qn is 1 for odd n and 2 for even n; left to right the weights are 2,1,2,1,... Because the remainder is 0..8 the check digit is always 1..9 and never 0. Unlike My Number this number is PUBLIC - the NTA publishes every corporate number with its name and address on 法人番号公表サイト - so a generated one may collide with a real company. The 12-digit base is the 会社法人等番号 from the commercial register for incorporated bodies; its internal layout (registry-office code, entity kind, serial) is not modelled here.
generator: tdc
