description: Lesotho's universities. The National University of Lesotho at Roma is the descendant of Pius XII College, founded 1945, which became the shared university of Basutoland, Bechuanaland and Swaziland — so it is the parent institution of the universities of Botswana and Eswatini as well, and the three countries' higher education all traces back to this one campus in the Lesotho highlands.
