description: All 79 cities (şəhərlər) of Azerbaijan, weighted by the population of the city itself at 1 January 2025. "The city itself" is the load-bearing phrase, because Azerbaijani statistics publish two numbers for the same name and they differ by a factor of two. Sumqayıt the administrative unit holds 427 600 people; Sumqayıt the town holds 379 400, the rest living in Corat and Hacı Zeynalabdin, which are separate named settlements under the same city administration. Baku is the extreme case: the city administration counts 2 351 300, but 1 164 100 of those live in the 48 qəsəbələr — Mərdəkan, Buzovna, Maştağa, Lökbatan and the rest — that keep their own names in a postal address, leaving 1 187 200 in Baku proper, which is the figure used here. The same subtraction gives Gəncə 281 200 rather than 331 000. That this is the right reading is confirmed by the SSC's own grouping table 1.2, which counts exactly one city over 500 000, four between 100 000 and 500 000, five between 50 000 and 100 000, 28 between 20 000 and 50 000, 22 between 10 000 and 20 000, 14 between 5 000 and 10 000, two between 3 000 and 5 000 and three under 3 000 — and the 79 weights below fall into those nine bands in exactly those numbers. Four entries deserve a warning. XANKƏNDİ (2 100) and AĞDƏRƏ (200) are not small towns but towns being repopulated: Xankəndi held 55 100 people before 1991 and Ağdərə is at the very beginning of resettlement after September 2023, so these figures will be stale within a year in a way the others will not. XIRDALAN, at 195 900 the country's fourth city, is not a city of republic subordination and appears on no list of "major Azerbaijani cities" — it is the centre of Abşeron district and it grew fivefold in twenty years as Baku spilled over its boundary. And ŞUŞA (19 300) is counted as inhabited again for the first time since 1992. Cities of republic subordination are Bakı, Gəncə, Xankəndi, Lənkəran, Mingəçevir, Naftalan, Naxçıvan, Sumqayıt, Şəki, Şirvan and Yevlax; the other 68 are subordinate to a district. Source: State Statistical Committee, table 1.19, population by economic region, administrative unit and settlement at 1 January 2025.
