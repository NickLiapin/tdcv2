address: azerbaijan.finance.amount
description: A sum of money written the way Azerbaijan writes it — groups of three separated by a FULL STOP, a COMMA for the decimal point, and the manat sign AFTER the figure with a no-break space before it: 1.234.567,89 ₼. That is the az-AZ currency pattern in CLDR, and it is the OPPOSITE of English on both separators, which is how Azerbaijani amounts get destroyed in transit: 1.234 read as an English decimal is 1.234, a thousandth of its real value, and both readings are valid numbers so nothing downstream complains. THIS ALSO DIFFERS FROM THE NEIGHBOURS, which is the trap for anyone copying a Caucasus pack. Georgia and Armenia group with a NO-BREAK SPACE (1 234 567,89 ₾), Russia does too, and Azerbaijani, despite the Soviet century, follows the Turkish full-stop convention instead — so the separator that is invisible in a Georgian amount is a visible dot in an Azerbaijani one, and code that strips U+00A0 to "clean" the figure fixes Tbilisi and does nothing for Baku. The space before the symbol IS U+00A0, though, so a naive split on " " still gives the wrong field count here. The symbol is ₼, U+20BC, which no font older than 2014 carries; azerbaijan.finance.currency explains the fallback. Two decimals are always written even though the 1, 3 and 5 qəpik coins have effectively left circulation, because bank and invoice amounts keep them. The magnitude is spread across five decades rather than drawn uniformly, because a uniform draw over a seven-digit range puts nearly every value in the millions and an Azerbaijani price list is mostly two, three and four figures — a monthly minimum wage is 400 ₼ and a metro fare 0,50 ₼. Nothing here is a number the engine can do arithmetic on: it is the PRINTED form. For a value to compute with, generate a plain number and format it at the point of output.
generator: tdc
locale: az
---
<sequence name="raw"><gen type="number" value="1..9999999"/></sequence>
<sequence name="shift"><gen type="number" value="0..4"/></sequence>
<sequence name="qepik"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{2}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="amount"><compute>
  <!-- Spread the magnitude: divide the 7-digit draw by 1, 10, 100, 1000 or 10000. -->
  <let name="scale"><at>
    <in><list v="1,10,100,1000,10000"/></in>
    <index><to_number><field name="shift"/></to_number></index>
  </at></let>
  <let name="whole"><divide><to_number><field name="raw"/></to_number><use name="scale"/></divide></let>
  <!-- CLDR az-AZ: group separator FULL STOP, decimal comma, pattern "#,##0.00 ¤" with ¤ = U+20BC. -->
  <let name="grouped"><group size="3" sep="."><use name="whole"/></group></let>
  <result><concat><use name="grouped"/><str v=","/><field name="qepik"/><str v=" ₼"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{amount}}</data>
