address: azerbaijan.phone
description: An Azerbaijani mobile number in E.164 form — +994, a two-digit operator code and seven digits, nine significant digits in all. The Azerbaijani plan is CLOSED and uniform: every number, mobile or fixed, is a two-digit code plus seven digits, dialled nationally with a leading 0 that is NOT part of the number and must be stripped before the +994. That single rule is what a foreign schema gets wrong, in both directions: storing "0501234567" and dialling "+9940501234567" produces a number with an impossible ten-digit national part, and storing "+994501234567" and printing it nationally without restoring the 0 produces one an Azerbaijani cannot dial. The mobile codes are 050 and 051 and 010 (Azercell), 055 and 099 (Bakcell), 070 and 077 (Nar), and 060 (Naxtel, which serves the Nakhchivan exclave and only the exclave — see azerbaijan.phoneMobilePrefix). They are NOT reliable operator labels: Azerbaijan has had mobile number portability since 2016, so a 050 number may sit on any network, and the only inference the code supports is mobile-versus-fixed. Written nationally in the grouping 050 123 45 67 and internationally as +994 50 123 45 67; this generator emits the unspaced E.164 form so it survives a CSV round-trip, and azerbaijan.phoneNational carries the spaced national form with its leading zero. Emergency numbers are 101 fire, 102 police, 103 ambulance, 112 the unified line, and none of them fit this pattern. Source: the national numbering plan administered by the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport.
