description: The Azerbaijani personal identification number, FİN (Fərdi İdentifikasiya Nömrəsi) — SEVEN characters of mixed letters and digits, printed as "Personal No" on the new identity card and passport and in the lower right corner of the old ones, and never changed for the life of the holder. It is the key every Azerbaijani system joins on: the ASAN service centres, the e-government gateway, the compulsory medical insurance register and the banks all identify a person by FİN, and the State Tax Service says explicitly that where a natural person has no taxpayer number the FİN substitutes for it. Three properties are worth encoding. First, it is ALPHANUMERIC, not numeric — a column typed as an integer loses it, and a leading digit is not a guarantee that the rest are digits. Second, THE LETTER O IS NEVER USED: the tax service's own published note says "there is never 'O' letter in FIN, only '0' (zero)", so the alphabet is the 25 letters A-Z minus O plus the ten digits, and an OCR or transcription step that turns 0 into O has produced a FİN that cannot exist. Third, it carries NO STRUCTURE: no birth date, no sex, no region, no issue year, and — this is the part that separates it from a PESEL, an ИИН or a fødselsnummer — NO PUBLISHED CHECK DIGIT. The State Tax Service and the State Migration Service describe the format and say nothing about a checksum, and nothing in the published material distinguishes a valid FİN from any other seven-character string over that alphabet. So this generator DRAWS the field rather than computing it, and no validator can do better than a format check: to know whether a FİN is real you have to ask the register. This file therefore makes no correctness claim beyond shape, deliberately, and 34 raised to the seventh power is about 52 billion, so a drawn value colliding with a living person's is possible; nothing published lets a generator avoid that, which is the honest state of affairs and not a gap in this pack. Source: State Tax Service of Azerbaijan, TIN description filed with the OECD (Section II, Personal Identification Number).
