address: azerbaijan.tax.voen
description: The Azerbaijani taxpayer identification number, VÖEN (Vergi Ödəyicisinin Eyniləşdirmə Nömrəsi) — ten digits, issued by the State Tax Service under the Ministry of Economy, and the number printed on every Azerbaijani invoice, contract and set of bank details. Its structure is published and this generator follows it exactly: THE FIRST TWO DIGITS are the code of the territorial administrative unit that registered the taxpayer, the NEXT SIX are a serial, THE NINTH is a check digit, and THE TENTH IS A LEGAL-STATUS FLAG — 1 for a legal person, 2 for a natural person. That last digit is the useful one and the one most often thrown away: 9900003611 ends in 1 and is Kapital Bank, and a number ending in 2 belongs to an individual trader, so a schema that stores VÖEN as a number and drops nothing still tells you which kind of taxpayer you are looking at. THE CHECK DIGIT IS NOT IMPLEMENTED HERE, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. The State Tax Service's own description, filed with the OECD, says only that "the software determines the ninth digit via special algorithm" and stops there; the algorithm is not published, and the weight vectors circulating on third-party validator sites are not traceable to any Azerbaijani source. Inferring one from numbers this generator produced would be circular, and inferring one from a handful of real numbers would be a guess dressed as arithmetic — worth less than no checksum, because it would look verified. So the ninth digit is DRAWN, and a value from this file is a correctly-shaped VÖEN that the tax service's own checker would reject about nine times in ten. If a config needs numbers that pass, it must use real ones. The two-digit prefixes below are the ones observed on VÖENs that Azerbaijani institutions publish for themselves — 02 (Naxçıvanbank 0200432771), 13 (Unibank 1300017201, Turanbank 1300016391), 14 (AccessBank 1400057421), 15 (Expressbank 1500031691), 17 (PAŞA Bank 1700767721, Bank of Baku 1700038881), 20 (Xalq Bank 2000296061, TIF-AGMA 2007333601) and 99, which is not a territory at all but the large-taxpayer block (Azərbaycan Beynəlxalq Bankı 9900001881, Kapital Bank 9900003611, Azərpoçt 9900037711). The tax service does not publish the territory-to-prefix table, so no claim is made about which prefix means which district. Written bare, ten digits, no separators and no country letters — unlike an EU VAT number, a VÖEN never carries a country prefix. See azerbaijan.tax.vat for what happens when VAT enters the picture.
