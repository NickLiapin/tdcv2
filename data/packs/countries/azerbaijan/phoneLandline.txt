description: An Azerbaijani fixed-line number in E.164 form — +994, a two-digit area code and seven digits. The area codes are 12 for Baku, 18 for Sumqayıt, 36 for the whole Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic, and 20 to 26 for seven multi-district regions: 20 covers the Baku hinterland and central Aran (Ağdaş, Ağsu, Bərdə, Göyçay, İsmayıllı, Kürdəmir, Qobustan, Şamaxı, Ucar, Zərdab), 21 the Şirvan-Salyan group, 22 Gəncə and the whole north-west, 23 the Quba-Xaçmaz coast, 24 Şəki-Zaqatala and Mingəçevir, 25 Lənkəran-Astara, and 26 Karabakh. THIS IS A 2011 REFORM AND THE MOST DESTRUCTIVE FACT IN AZERBAIJANI CONTACT DATA. Before 1 August 2011 every district had its own three-digit code — Ağdaş was 193, Quba 169, Şəki 177, Tovuz 231, Gəncə 22 — and the reform collapsed some sixty of them into these ten, moving the discarded digits into the front of the seven-digit subscriber number. So a pre-2011 Azerbaijani landline is not merely formatted differently, it is a DIFFERENT NUMBER, and a records system that carries phone numbers from before and after that date carries two incompatible schemes in one column with nothing in the string to tell them apart. That is also why an old Azerbaijani number often has fewer than seven digits after its code. Since the reform, the area code no longer identifies a district — only Baku, Sumqayıt and Nakhchivan are single-place codes, and the rest cover between five and thirteen districts each, so mapping an area code to a city is right in three cases out of ten. Written nationally with the trunk zero in the same 3-3-2-2 grouping as a mobile: 012 493 56 00. Source: the national numbering plan; the district-code table before and after 1 August 2011.
