address: indonesia.vehicle.plateCode
description: The regional letter prefix on an Indonesian number plate. It does NOT correspond to the 38 provinces one-for-one and never has — the codes descend from the Dutch colonial residency map, so Jawa Barat alone carries D, E, F, T and Z while a single DA covers the whole of Kalimantan Selatan, and B covers Jakarta plus Depok, Bekasi and Tangerang across three provinces. The Papuan codes are the recent ones: PA stayed with Papua and PB with Papua Barat, while Papua Barat Daya took PY in May 2024, Papua Selatan took PS in July 2024, Papua Tengah took PT and Papua Pegunungan launched PG in Wamena in October 2024. A pack that derived plate codes from the province list would get all six of those wrong.
