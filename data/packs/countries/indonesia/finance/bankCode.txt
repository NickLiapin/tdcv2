---
address: indonesia.finance.bankCode
description: The three-digit sandi bank (bank code) assigned by Bank Indonesia, index-aligned with indonesia.finance.bank. Indonesia has NO IBAN — it is not in the IBAN registry, so there is no indonesia.finance.iban file and any 20-odd-character ID-prefixed string presented as an Indonesian IBAN is fabricated. A domestic transfer is addressed by this three-digit code plus the account number, and an international one by SWIFT/BIC. Account numbers themselves are set per bank, 10 to 16 digits with no national format and no check digit, which is why this pack ships no account-number generator either. Every code here was checked against the Bank Indonesia sandi list.
