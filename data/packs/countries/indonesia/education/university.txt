description: Universities of Indonesia, official Indonesian names, state institutions first and then the large Islamic, Christian and private ones. The naming is worth reading rather than guessing at: an institut is a specialist technical or agricultural institute rather than a lesser university (Institut Teknologi Bandung and Institut Pertanian Bogor are two of the country's strongest), a universitas negeri is a former teachers' college and a different institution from the plain universitas in the same city, and UIN is a state Islamic university. IPB deliberately styles itself IPB University in English while remaining Institut Pertanian Bogor in Indonesian.
