description: Indonesian football clubs — the eighteen of the 2025-26 top flight first, then well-known clubs of the second tier and the recent past. Most are Perse- or PS- contractions of Persatuan Sepak Bola plus the town, which is why so many look alike: Persib is Bandung, Persija Jakarta, Persik Kediri, Persis Surakarta, Persita Tangerang.
