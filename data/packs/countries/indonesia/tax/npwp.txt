address: indonesia.tax.npwp
description: Indonesian NPWP (Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak) in its classic 15-digit written form, NN.NNN.NNN.N-NNN.NNN. This is the form that carries a REAL check digit, which is why it is the one generated here: the ninth digit is a Luhn (mod-10 double-add-double) check over the first eight, exactly as on a payment card. The leading pair is a registration serial plus a taxpayer-type code (0 treasury, 1-3 corporate, 4 and 6 sole trader, 5 civil servant, 7-9 employee); the next six digits are the registration serial, the three after the hyphen are the KPP tax-office code and the last three are the branch indicator, 000 for a head office. Indonesia is migrating away from this number: since PMK 112/2022 an individual's NPWP is simply their 16-digit NIK and an entity's is a 0 prepended to these 15 digits, with the 15-digit form retired from DJP services on 1 July 2024. The 16-digit form is at indonesia.tax.npwp16 — but note that it inherits the NIK's total absence of a checksum, so the migration traded a verifiable number for an unverifiable one. Check digit verified in both directions against three published corporate NPWPs: PT Telkom Indonesia 01.000.013.1-093.000, PT Pertamina (Persero) 01.001.664.0-051.000 and PT Bank Tabungan Negara 01.001.609.5-093.000 all compute their printed ninth digit, and every single-digit alteration of them fails.
generator: tdc
