description: Towns of Slovenia (mesta), roughly ordered by 2025 population. Slovenia calls a settlement a mesto only above about 3,000 inhabitants under the Zakon o lokalni samoupravi, which is why the list runs out fast and ends in places most countries would call villages — Kostanjevica na Krki has around 700 people and is a town. Ljubljana at roughly 290,000 is the only settlement over 100,000 and Maribor the only other over 50,000, so the whole urban system below the capital is small; Slovenia has no second city in the sense Croatia has Split.
