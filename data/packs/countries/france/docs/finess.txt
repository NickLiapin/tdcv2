description: French health establishment number (numéro FINESS, Fichier national des établissements sanitaires et sociaux) — the 9-digit number of a hospital, clinic, care home, pharmacy or the legal body that runs them; nothing in France's health and social sector is funded without one. Two digits for the département, a 0, five digits and a Luhn check digit. The Agence du numérique en santé describes the 3rd and 4th digits as always 0, but real numbers vary in the 4th (the Pitié-Salpêtrière in Paris is 750100125), so only the 3rd is held at 0 here. Départements 01-95 are drawn; Corsica (2A, 2B) and the overseas départements are not. Verified against the ANS example 150001899 and the published numbers of several Paris and Lyon hospitals.
generator: tdc
locale: fr
