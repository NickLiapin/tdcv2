description: Pakistani universities and degree-awarding institutions in their official English names, which is the form that appears on the degree itself. The list mixes the three kinds a student record actually cites — federal and provincial public universities chartered by an act of the National Assembly or a provincial assembly, the specialised public institutes (PIEAS, the Institute of Space Technology, NUST, GIK), and the chartered private universities (LUMS, Aga Khan, Habib, Riphah, Hamdard) — because a transcript names whichever body issued the degree. A few are worth knowing apart from their names: Allama Iqbal Open University is a distance university with one of the largest enrolments in the world, the University of the Punjab was founded in 1882 and predates the country by 65 years, Forman Christian College and Gordon College are missionary foundations older still that were nationalised in 1972 and returned to their trusts decades later, and the Aga Khan University is a private medical university whose teaching hospital is the reference laboratory for much of the region. Universities of Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are included; the Higher Education Commission recognises their degrees on the same footing.
