---
address: chile.tax.rut
description: Chilean RUT (Rol Único Tributario) — the same number as the RUN and the same check character: a 7-8 digit body plus the módulo 11 digit with cyclic weights 2,3,4,5,6,7 right to left, where a remainder of 1 prints as K (real example: 60.803.000-K, Servicio de Impuestos Internos). Companies are issued bodies in the 60-99 million range
generator: tdc
---
<sequence name="body"><gen type="regex" value="[1-9][0-9]{6,7}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="rut"><compute>
  <let name="len"><length><field name="body"/></length></let>
  <!-- Módulo 11: walking right to left the weights cycle 2,3,4,5,6,7,2,3,...
       Index i from the left sits (len-1-i) places from the right, so its
       weight is 2 + ((len-1-i) mod 6). -->
  <let name="sum">
    <reduce>
      <over><field name="body"/></over>
      <init><int v="0"/></init>
      <do><add><acc/>
        <multiply><current/>
          <add><int v="2"/>
            <mod>
              <subtract><use name="len"/><int v="1"/><current_index/></subtract>
              <int v="6"/>
            </mod>
          </add>
        </multiply>
      </add></do>
    </reduce>
  </let>
  <!-- 11 - (sum mod 11), folded so 11 becomes 0; a remainder of 1 leaves 10,
       which prints as K. Roughly one RUT in eleven ends in K. -->
  <let name="c"><mod><subtract><int v="11"/><mod><use name="sum"/><int v="11"/></mod></subtract><int v="11"/></mod></let>
  <result>
    <choose>
      <when><test><equals><use name="c"/><int v="10"/></equals></test><then><str v="K"/></then></when>
      <otherwise><use name="c"/></otherwise>
    </choose>
  </result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{body}}${{rut}}</data>
