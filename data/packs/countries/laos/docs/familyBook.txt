description: The number of a ປຶ້ມສຳມະໂນຄົວ — the family census book, the household registration document that in Laos does more identity work than the identity card does. NO CHECK DIGIT, for the same reason and on the same evidence as laos.docs.nationalId: nothing is published, Laos has no python-stdnum module, and verification is a lookup in the register held by the ກະຊວງປ້ອງກັນຄວາມສະຫງົບ. WHAT IT IS. One book per HOUSEHOLD, not per person. It carries the book number, the head of household, the address, the registration date and then a line per resident with sex, date of birth, relation to the head, identity-card number, ethnicity, nationality, occupation, workplace, where they moved from and when — so the family book is the join table between people and places in Lao administration, and a Lao person proves who they are, where they live and who they are related to by producing it. School enrolment, land transactions, marriage registration and most bank account openings ask for the book, not the card. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR A SCHEMA, and it is the reason this address exists as a separate field: a Lao person record has a HOUSEHOLD key as well as a personal one, several rows will legitimately share one family-book number, and a data set that models Lao people as independent rows with no household has thrown away the structure the country is administered by. IT IS BEING ABOLISHED. The Ministry of Public Security has stopped issuing paper books and is replacing them with an electronic ໃບຢັ້ງຢືນສຳມະໂນຄົວ (census data certificate); books already issued stay valid until the ministry formally cancels them. So a Lao record from before that transition holds a book number and a record after it holds a certificate, and the two are not the same document. Digits only, store as text.
