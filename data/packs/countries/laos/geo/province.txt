description: The first-level division of Laos in Lao script. NOT a hand-kept list — it is sliced out of laos.geo.provinceWithCode, so it can never fall out of step with the province number and the postcode prefix that belong to it. 18 values: 17 ແຂວງ plus ນະຄອນຫຼວງວຽງຈັນ. Names are written BARE, without the ແຂວງ in front, exactly as Poland carries dolnośląskie without województwo — ແຂວງ is ordinary Lao vocabulary and belongs in the language pack. The capital is the ONE exception and it is deliberate: ນະຄອນຫຼວງວຽງຈັນ keeps its title because the province next door is also called ວຽງຈັນ, and stripped of the title the two are the same string. WRITING SYSTEM, and it applies to every Lao-valued file in this pack. LAO HAS NO SPACES BETWEEN WORDS and no capital letters, so ສະຫວັນນະເຂດ is one unbroken run of syllables and anything that splits on whitespace, title-cases, or counts words gets the wrong answer; a Lao sentence is space-delimited at the PHRASE level only, which is why a few values in this pack (bank names, ໄກສອນ ພົມວິຫານ) do contain spaces while the geography does not. Lao also writes vowels around the consonant rather than after it — ໄຊ is stored ໄ then ຊ but read ຊ-ໄ, so the first codepoint of a Lao string is frequently not the first sound of the word, and sorting Lao by codepoint puts every ໄ- and ເ- word together in a block that means nothing. MEASURED ON THIS PACK: ສະຫວັນນະເຂດ is 11 codepoints and 33 UTF-8 bytes; ນະຄອນຫຼວງວຽງຈັນ is 15 codepoints and 45 bytes; the longest bank name here runs past 120 bytes. Every Lao character is three bytes in UTF-8 and combining vowels and tone marks are codepoints that occupy no width, so bytes are three times codepoints and codepoints overcount what the reader sees. Size storage at three to four times a Latin equivalent, count grapheme clusters for display width, and never truncate mid-cluster — a cut between ນ and ້ leaves a dotted-circle box.
