address: laos.geo.districtWithCode
description: The paired table behind laos.geo.district and laos.geo.districtCode — the four-digit ເມືອງ code, a space, then the district in Lao. All 148 of them, which is the whole second level of Lao local government and not a sample. THE CODE IS HIERARCHICAL: the first two digits are the province number from laos.geo.provinceCode and the last two number the district inside it, so 0101 is ຈັນທະບູລີ in ນະຄອນຫຼວງວຽງຈັນ and 1301 is ໄກສອນ ພົມວິຫານ in ສະຫວັນນະເຂດ, and truncating any code to two digits rolls it up to its province correctly. District numbers run 01 upward with no gaps, and the count per province varies from 4 (ເຊກອງ) to 15 (ສະຫວັນນະເຂດ) — so a generator that draws a province and then a district number from a fixed range invents districts that do not exist, which is exactly why this file is a table and not two independent draws. THE ເມືອງ IS THE UNIT THAT MATTERS IN LAOS and a reader coming from Europe will get this wrong. Laos has THREE levels of administration and no more: ແຂວງ (province), ເມືອງ (district, also called ເທສະບານ), ບ້ານ (village). There is no city, no municipality, no county and no commune — the ຕາແສງ that once sat between ເມືອງ and ບ້ານ was abolished. So a Lao postal address is village, district, province, and the ບ້ານ is what actually locates a house; "city" is a category Lao administration does not have, which is why laos.geo.city in this pack is the URBAN PART of a ເມືອງ rather than a municipality (it says so itself). Names are written BARE, without the ເມືອງ in front, which is ordinary Lao vocabulary. Three districts are named identically to a district in another province — ວຽງທອງ is 0703 in ຫົວພັນ and 1106 in ບໍລິຄຳໄຊ, ໂພນທອງ is 0612 in ຫຼວງພະບາງ and 1606 in ຈຳປາສັກ, ໄຊເສດຖາ is 0103 in the capital while ອັດຕະປື spells its own ໄຊເຊດຖາ (1701) with a different sibilant — so the district NAME is not a key and the four-digit code is.
