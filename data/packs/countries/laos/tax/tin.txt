description: The Lao taxpayer identification number issued by the ກົມສ່ວຍສາອາກອນ (Tax Department) of the ກະຊວງການເງິນ — twelve digits, printed in groups of three (123-456-789-012) and emitted here in that punctuated form because that is how it is printed on a Lao tax invoice and an enterprise registration certificate. NO CHECK DIGIT, and the reason is worth stating exactly because one commercial validation guide claims otherwise. TaxDo's Laos page says the TIN ends in "a checksum or validation number" but publishes no algorithm; the Taxpayer Identification Number Decree 354/PM of 2007 that creates the number does not describe one; the Tax Department verifies a TIN through its own online lookup, which returns registration status rather than doing arithmetic; and Laos has NO python-stdnum module, the usual home for national-identifier algorithms, which carries th and vn on either side of it. An unpublished checksum is not a checksum you can implement — a plausible mod-11 would reject real numbers and accept fake ones while LOOKING verified, which is strictly worse than none. So this file emits a correctly shaped number that no arithmetic can confirm, and it claims nothing more. THE LENGTH IS NOT UNANIMOUS in the published sources and you should check your target system before matching on it: TaxDo's prose says eleven digits while its own worked examples are twelve, and the Lao Trade Portal describes the enterprise registration certificate number as twelve. Twelve is generated here. ONE NUMBER, NOT THREE. Since the one-stop registration reform a Lao enterprise receives its TIN together with its Enterprise Registration Certificate, generated by the TaxRIS system, so a Lao company does not hold a separate company number and tax number the way a German or a Polish one does — and it does not get a third number for VAT either; see laos.tax.vat. INDIVIDUALS HAVE ONE TOO, which is newer than the business number: a 2021 Ministry of Finance notification extended TIN registration to individuals, so a Lao person appearing in tax data before then does so through an employer's number and after it may have one of their own.
