description: The four states of the Federated States of Micronesia — Yap, Chuuk, Pohnpei and Kosrae — spread across 2,700 kilometres of the western Pacific. The federation is loose: each state has its own constitution, its own language and considerable autonomy, and Chuuk has voted on independence more than once. Yap is where stone money is still socially current: rai discs up to four metres across, whose ownership transfers without the stone moving.
