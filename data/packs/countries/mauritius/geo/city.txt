description: Mauritian towns. Port Louis is the capital and the port; the conurbation running inland through Beau Bassin, Quatre Bornes, Vacoas and Curepipe holds more people than the capital does and is effectively one continuous city climbing to cooler altitude — the colonial pattern of moving uphill from a malarial port. Names come from three colonial layers at once: Dutch, French and British.
