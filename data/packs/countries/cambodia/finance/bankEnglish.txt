description: The same banks as cambodia.finance.bank in their Latin trading names, IN THE SAME ORDER, so the two files line up row for row and a Khmer name can be read against its English one. Cambodian banks operate bilingually — the statement, the branch sign and the app are in Khmer, the SWIFT record, the annual report and the AFC-facing paperwork are in Latin — so both forms are in daily use and neither is a translation of the other. Where a bank's Latin name is an initialism its Khmer name is a phonetic spelling of that initialism rather than a translation (ABA is ធនាគារ វឌ្ឍនៈអាស៊ី, from Advanced Bank of Asia), and where a bank's name is a Khmer word the Latin form is a romanisation of the Khmer (ស្ថាបនា is Sathapana, "to establish"). Two warnings. This file and cambodia.finance.bank are two separate addresses: drawing both gives you two independent banks, not one bank in two scripts — for a coherent pair, draw cambodia.finance.bankWithBic once and slice it. And this order is maintained by hand against the sliced files, so it is the one place in this pack where a row could silently fall out of step; the BIC pairing in cambodia.finance.bankWithBic is the one the engine enforces.
