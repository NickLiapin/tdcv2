CAMBODIA HAS NO IBAN, so if you are looking for one, stop: the routing identifier is the BIC at cambodia.finance.swiftBic. VALUES ARE KHMER SCRIPT. Khmer normally puts no space between words, but these names DO contain spaces, because the space in Khmer marks a phrase boundary and a transliterated foreign name is a phrase — ធនាគារ ជេ ត្រាស់ រ៉ូយ៉ាល់ is "J Trust Royal" spelled out phonetically with a space between each borrowed word. That makes bank names one of the few Khmer lists with internal spaces, the opposite of the problem the geography files have. The names are written without the legal-form suffix that appears in the register (ភីអិលស៊ី, ម.ក, ក.អ — plc, limited company, public limited); those are ordinary Khmer commercial vocabulary and live at km.company.legalSuffix.
