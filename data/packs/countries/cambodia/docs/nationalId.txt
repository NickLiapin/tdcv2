description: The number printed on the អត្តសញ្ញាណប័ណ្ណសញ្ជាតិខ្មែរ, the Khmer nationality identity card issued by the Ministry of Interior to every Cambodian from the age of 15 and valid for ten years. NO CHECK DIGIT IS GENERATED HERE AND THAT IS A DECISION, NOT AN OMISSION. Cambodia publishes no checksum specification for this number anywhere: not in the sub-decree that fixes the card's format, not by the អគ្គនាយកដ្ឋានអត្តសញ្ញាណកម្ម that issues it, and there is no entry for Cambodia in python-stdnum, the usual home for national-identifier algorithms, which carries modules for Thailand and Vietnam either side of it. Validation in Cambodia is a register lookup, not arithmetic. A reverse-engineered mod-11 rule would make this file LOOK more rigorous while quietly rejecting real cards and accepting fake ones, which is worse than no checksum because it looks verified. THE NUMBER ENCODES NOTHING. The sub-decree describes it as issued to each citizen តាមលំដាប់ — in sequence — at first registration or at replacement for damage, loss, expiry or correction, so unlike a PESEL, a Thai national ID or an Italian codice fiscale it carries no date of birth, no sex and no province, and it cannot be joined to an age or a gender column or used to locate the holder. It is also NOT permanent: because a replacement card can draw a new number, the card number is a property of the card and the thing that follows the person for life is the separate លេខសម្គាល់បុគ្គល at cambodia.docs.personalIdentificationCode. A schema that stores one Cambodian identity number and calls it both has merged two fields the sub-decree lists separately. LENGTH: nine digits, which is what the cards in circulation carry and what a Cambodian bank or SIM-registration form asks for; published sources are not unanimous, and a widely repeated 2017 government announcement of a ten-digit number describes the central register's personal code rather than the card number — secondary sources conflate the two, so if your data has to match a specific Cambodian system, check which of the two that system stores. Digits only, leading zero possible, so store as text. This is protected personal data under Cambodia's 2015 identification law and no real citizen's number appears anywhere in this pack.
