description: The លេខសម្គាល់បុគ្គល — the personal identification code Cambodia's civil-registration law and the sub-decree on the identity card list as a field DISTINCT from the card number, assigned to an individual at first registration and permanent until death. This is the half of Cambodia's identity numbering that behaves like a national identity number in the sense other countries use the phrase: the card number at cambodia.docs.nationalId belongs to a piece of plastic and can change when the plastic is replaced, this one belongs to the person and does not. Ten digits, the length the Ministry of Interior announced in 2017 when it described a single code under which records from government departments and the private sector would be collected in one central database — the announcement that most secondary write-ups have since mis-reported as the length of the card number. NO CHECK DIGIT, for the same reason and with the same evidence as the card number: nothing is published, and validation is a lookup against the register rather than arithmetic. It encodes nothing — no birth date, no sex, no province — so it cannot be joined to any other column. WHAT TO BE CAREFUL OF: the two numbers are not interchangeable and are not derivable from one another, they are nine and ten digits and so are easy to tell apart on sight but easy to merge in a schema, and the rollout of the biometric eID card from 2025 is what finally puts both on one document with a QR code. If you are modelling records from before that rollout, most Cambodian systems held the card number only.
