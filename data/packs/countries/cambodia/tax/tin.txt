address: cambodia.tax.tin
description: The Cambodian taxpayer identification number issued by the អគ្គនាយកដ្ឋានពន្ធដារ (General Department of Taxation) — a Latin letter, three digits, a hyphen and nine digits, written K001-901234567 or L001-901234567 and printed on every tax invoice, customs declaration and filing. THE LEADING LETTER IS THE TAXPAYER'S CLASS, not a checksum and not a region: Cambodia's self-assessment regime sorts registrants into small, medium and large taxpayers under Prakas 009 of 12 January 2021 on the basis of turnover, assets, sector and legal form, and the letter follows that classification and the registration channel — so a Cambodian company's TIN can CHANGE ITS LETTER when the company grows out of one class into another, which makes it a poor primary key for a taxpayer over time and a common source of duplicate rows in Cambodian data. NO CHECK DIGIT. Nothing published by the GDT, by org-id.guide's KH-TIN entry, or by any of the commercial validation services describes a checksum for this number, and Cambodia is absent from python-stdnum; verification is done by looking the number up in the GDT's own online checker, which returns registration status rather than doing arithmetic. So this file emits a correctly shaped number that no arithmetic can confirm, and it claims nothing more. LENGTH IS NOT UNANIMOUS in the published sources — org-id.guide and TaxDo both give one letter plus twelve digits, which is the shape generated here; one commercial validator diagrams a ten-digit tail instead. If you must match a specific system, check its column width first. THIS IS A BUSINESS NUMBER. A Cambodian individual normally has no TIN at all: salary tax is withheld and declared by the employer, so a natural person appears in Cambodian tax data through their employer's number and their identity card, not through one of these. One entity, one number — the registration number issued by the Ministry of Commerce carries straight into the GDT record, so a Cambodian company does not hold a separate company number and tax number the way a German or a Polish one does, and it does not get a third number when it registers for VAT: see cambodia.tax.vat.
