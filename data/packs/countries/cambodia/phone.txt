---
address: cambodia.phone
description: Cambodian mobile number in E.164 form. Country code is 855. The number is +855 followed by EIGHT OR NINE national digits, and which one you get is decided by the prefix rather than at random: the generator draws a prefix and its subscriber length together from cambodia.phoneMobilePrefixWithLength, drops the national trunk 0, and emits six or seven digits behind it accordingly. So +855 12 345 678 and +855 97 1234 567 are both correct and a fixed-length validator rejects one or the other. THE TRUNK 0 IS DROPPED INTERNATIONALLY. A Cambodian writes 012 345 678 at home; the same number is +85512345678 abroad, never +855012345678, and that extra zero is the single commonest defect in Cambodian phone columns — it survives round-tripping through most spreadsheets because it still looks like a phone number. The prefixes and lengths follow Google's libphonenumber metadata for KH, which is the published spec most validators enforce; see the paired file for the two ranges it admits that are not generated here and why. A prefix says nothing about who serves the number (portability since 2017) and nothing about where the subscriber is (there are no geographic mobile codes). Mobile is what "phone number" means in Cambodia: fixed lines are a small minority of connections, mobile penetration exceeds one SIM per person, and since the 2023-25 SIM-registration campaign every SIM is tied to an identity card, which is why a Cambodian person record joins a phone number to cambodia.docs.nationalId far more often than a European one would.
generator: tdc

  </choose></let>
  <result><concat><str v="+855"/><use name="natPrefix"/><use name="tail"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
