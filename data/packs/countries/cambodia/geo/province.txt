description: The first-level division of Cambodia written in Khmer. NOT a hand-kept list — it is sliced out of cambodia.geo.provinceWithCode, so it can never fall out of step with the province code and the postcode prefix that belong to it. 25 values: 24 ខេត្ត plus រាជធានីភ្នំពេញ, which is the capital and not a province; see cambodia.geo.provinceWithCode for why it is in the list anyway and cambodia.geo.phnomPenhKhan for how it divides differently. Names are written BARE, without the ខេត្ត or រាជធានី in front: those two words are ordinary Khmer vocabulary and live in the language pack at km.address.streetSuffix, exactly as Poland's list carries dolnośląskie without województwo. WRITING SYSTEM, and it applies to every Khmer-valued file in this pack. Khmer has NO SPACES BETWEEN WORDS and NO CAPITAL LETTERS, so បន្ទាយមានជ័យ is one unbroken run of three words and any code that splits on whitespace, title-cases, or checks for an initial capital gets the wrong answer. Worse, Khmer STACKS consonants: the invisible COENG sign U+17D2 tells the renderer to tuck the next consonant under the previous one, so ក + U+17D2 + ដ + ា + ល in កណ្ដាល is five codepoints drawn as three visible clusters. MEASURED ON THE VALUES IN THIS PACK: កណ្ដាល is 6 codepoints, 18 UTF-8 bytes and 4 rendered clusters; ត្បូងឃ្មុំ is 10 codepoints, 30 bytes and 5 clusters; the longest court name here, សាលាដំបូងខេត្តព្រះសីហនុ, is 23 codepoints, 69 bytes and 13 clusters. Every Khmer character is three bytes in UTF-8 and roughly every second codepoint disappears into a stack or a mark, so THREE DIFFERENT LENGTHS disagree on every value — bytes are about three times codepoints, and codepoints are about 1.6 times what the reader actually sees. A VARCHAR sized by eye, a fixed-width column and a "truncate at 20 characters" rule will all cut a Khmer name in the middle of a stack and leave a dotted-circle box on screen. Count grapheme clusters for display width, size storage in bytes at three to four times a Latin equivalent, and never truncate at a codepoint boundary.
