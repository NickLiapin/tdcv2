description: Languages of Papua New Guinea — the three official ones and a handful of the largest indigenous languages. The number behind this file is the point: PNG has roughly 840 living languages, more than any other country on earth and about an eighth of the world's total, in a population of ten million. Tok Pisin is the creole that lets them talk to each other and is the true national language by usage; Hiri Motu is a second lingua franca in the south, now declining; English is the language of government and schooling. Any dataset that models a PNG person's language as a single value is compressing something enormous.
