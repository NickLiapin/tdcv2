description: Papua New Guinean towns. Port Moresby is the capital and is not connected by road to any other major centre — the highway network is fragmented by mountains and rivers, so domestic travel is largely by air, and that shapes everything from freight costs to how a national dataset is collected. Lae is the industrial port and the start of the Highlands Highway; Mount Hagen and Goroka anchor the densely populated highlands where most of the country actually lives.
