description: Botswanan towns. Gaborone was built from nothing after independence in 1966 — it was chosen as the capital because the colonial administration had run Bechuanaland from Mafeking, in South Africa, and the new country needed a capital inside its own borders. Jwaneng and Orapa are diamond towns owned by Debswana and are closed to visitors without a permit; the mines there are among the richest in the world and are the reason Botswana went from one of the poorest countries at independence to a middle-income one.
