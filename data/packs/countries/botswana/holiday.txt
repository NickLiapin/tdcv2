description: Botswanan public holidays. Independence from Britain is 30 September 1966 and is marked over two days. Sir Seretse Khama Day on 1 July honours the first president, who married a British woman in 1948 and was exiled for it by a British government under pressure from apartheid South Africa — the affair is a founding story of the country. President's Day in July gets two days as well, and Boxing Day and the day after Christmas make the December holidays four.
