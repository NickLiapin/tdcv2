address: ghana.docs.ssnit
description: The SSNIT number — the member number of the Social Security and National Insurance Trust, Ghana's statutory pension scheme, which every formally employed Ghanaian has and which appears on payslips, employer returns and pension statements. THIRTEEN ALPHANUMERIC CHARACTERS is the whole of what SSNIT publishes about it, and this file emits exactly that and no more. SSNIT's own public answer is that the number is "a unique 13-alphanumeric" generated for each registered member; it has never published which positions are letters, which are digits, what any of them encode, or whether there is a check character. So the pattern below is deliberately unstructured — an invented "letter plus twelve digits" would be a guess dressed as a specification, and this pack does not do that. THE OLD FORM WAS EIGHT DIGITS, plain, and was replaced during the biometric re-registration exercise; members who registered before it still have eight-digit numbers in historical payroll data, so a system reading Ghanaian pension records must accept both lengths, and the eight-digit form is not a truncation bug. WHAT HAS ACTUALLY HAPPENED SINCE: SSNIT now transacts on the Ghana Card. The National Identity Register Regulations make the Ghana Card the identification for pension business, and SSNIT's own message to members was that the number on the Ghana Card is now their SSNIT number — so for anything current, ghana.docs.ghanaCard is the number in play and this one is a legacy key. That consolidation is the pattern to expect from Ghanaian systems generally: the tax number went the same way in 2021, see ghana.tax.taxId.
