address: ghana.geo.postalCode
description: The Ghanaian postal code — GA-183 — two characters of region-and-district from ghana.geo.postcodeDistrict, a hyphen, then three or four digits naming a 500-metre area inside that district. SAY THE HONEST THING FIRST: GHANA HAD NO POSTAL CODES AT ALL BEFORE 2017. Not sparse ones, not ignored ones — none, which is why an older Ghanaian address ends at the town name and why every legacy form and database from the country has an empty postcode column. What exists now exists because GhanaPostGPS was built, and the postcode is simply the front half of the digital address in ghana.geo.digitalAddress; there was never a separate postal scheme for it to replace. That also means the postcode is not what a Ghanaian quotes. The full digital address is: it is what the Ghana Card application asks for, what a delivery app wants, and what people screenshot to each other. This bare form appears on the UPU's own Ghana address sheet, in P.O. Box and poste restante addresses where a precise point would be meaningless — "Accra-Central GA-183", "Sakumono GT-372" — and it is the reason the file exists. FORM: written with hyphens by GhanaPostGPS itself and often run together in the wild (GA183, EO1070, N503074 are all real), so a validator must accept both. The area part is three digits in most districts and four in the largest, and both are generated here — in roughly equal numbers, which is NOT the real ratio: of the published examples that can be checked, three digits outnumber four about three to one, and no distribution has ever been published to draw from, so an even split is the honest thing to do rather than a guessed weighting. One further disagreement worth knowing, since it decides what a validator should accept: the UPU sheet says three or four digits, and Wikipedia gives a five-digit example (N503074, which is district N5 and area 03074). This file follows the postal authority's own registered form and generates three or four only. NOT A UK POSTCODE despite the two-letter opening: there is no inward/outward split, no space, and the letters are region and district rather than a sorting office.
generator: tdc
