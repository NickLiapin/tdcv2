description: THE GHANAPOSTGPS DIGITAL ADDRESS — GA-543-0125 — and the most interesting thing in this pack, because it is a national addressing system built to replace street addresses rather than to index them. Launched 18 October 2017, it assigns a permanent code to EVERY FIVE-METRE SQUARE OF GHANA, whether or not anything stands there and whether or not the road it stands on has a name. That is the design premise, and it is the honest description of the problem: most of Ghana has no street address. Roads in unplanned areas are unnamed, houses are unnumbered or carry a plot number that means nothing to a stranger, and directions are given by landmark — the junction, the blue kiosk, behind the church. A digital address is the first thing in Ghanaian history that lets an ambulance, a courier or a bank be told WHERE somewhere is in one field. It is now required to get a Ghana Card, and Ghanaians read it off a phone app rather than knowing it by heart. THE STRUCTURE, per the Universal Postal Union's own Ghana addressing sheet: two characters of postcode district (region letter + district character, from ghana.geo.postcodeDistrict), a hyphen, three or four digits of postcode AREA — a 500-metre cell inside the district — a hyphen, and four digits of UNIQUE ADDRESS naming the 5x5-metre square inside that cell. The first two parts together are the postcode and stand alone as one: see ghana.geo.postalCode. Real published examples: GA-183-8164 (Accra Central, from the UPU sheet), AK-010-1295 (Kumasi Central Police Station), GD-110-6313 (Legon Post Office), NT-0148-3057 (Sagnarigu, Northern Region). NOT A GEOHASH AND NOT DERIVED FROM COORDINATES IN ANY PUBLISHED WAY: the area numbers spiral outward from each district centre, so nearby addresses have nearby numbers, but no published rule converts a digital address to a latitude and longitude — that is a lookup against GhanaPost, and the digits below are drawn, not computed from a point. Compare ghana.geo.poBox, the addressing this replaced and did not abolish.
