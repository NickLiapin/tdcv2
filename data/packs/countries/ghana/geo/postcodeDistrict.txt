address: ghana.geo.postcodeDistrict
description: THE SOURCE OF TRUTH for the two characters that open every Ghanaian postcode and every GhanaPostGPS digital address. ghana.geo.postalCode and ghana.geo.digitalAddress both draw from here, so the two cannot drift apart. One line is one DISTRICT — there are 215 lines and Ghana had 216 districts when the scheme was cut, which is the reason to believe this is the real assignment rather than a plausible one. HOW TO READ A LINE: the first character is the REGION and the second is the district within it. G is Greater Accra, A is Ashanti, B is Brong-Ahafo, C is Central, E is Eastern, N is Northern, U is Upper East, V is Volta, W is Western, and X — not U — is Upper West, because U was already taken. THAT IS TEN REGION LETTERS, NOT SIXTEEN, AND IT IS NOT AN ERROR IN THIS FILE. GhanaPostGPS was built in 2017 and the six new regions were created in 2019, so the postcode still speaks the old geography: an address in Ahafo, Bono or Bono East is still a B, an address in Savannah or North East is still an N, Oti is still a V, and Western North is still a W. A system that maps the first letter to one of ghana.geo.region's sixteen names will fail on six of them. The second character is a mnemonic where one was available — GA is Accra Metropolitan, GT is Tema, AK is Kumasi, CC is Cape Coast, XW is Wa — and an arbitrary letter or a DIGIT where the region ran out of sensible ones, which is why A2, A9, B2, E3, N4 and N5 are here and are correct. PROVENANCE, since it decides how far to trust the tail of this list: GhanaPostGPS publishes a "Regions and District Codes" page that renders no table, so the full assignment below is taken from a published Ghanaian compilation and then corroborated — every prefix independently attested elsewhere (GA and GT from the UPU's own Ghana address sheet, AK and GD and GL and CC and CE and NT and XW from published landmark addresses, EO and N5 from Wikipedia's examples) appears in it, and the line count lands one short of the 216 districts that existed when the scheme was cut. Grouped by region, alphabetical within each.
