description: Streets that actually exist in Hà Nội and Hồ Chí Minh City, named ones you could walk down. This is the country half of a deliberate split: vi/address/streetName.txt holds the generic national stock (Lê Lợi, Trần Hưng Đạo, Nguyễn Huệ — the figures and dates every Vietnamese town names a street after, which stay true wherever Vietnamese is spoken), while these are place-bound and would mean nothing in another country. The Hàng- names are the Old Quarter of Hà Nội, where each street was once a single trade's guild street — Hàng Bạc the silversmiths, Hàng Đào the silk dyers — and the name survived the trade. Vietnamese street names take no suffix of their own: the type word (Đường, Phố, Ngõ) goes in FRONT, so it is "Đường Đồng Khởi", never "Đồng Khởi Đường". Values carry stacked diacritics (a vowel modifier plus a tone on one letter).
locale: vi
