description: Cities of Vietnam, roughly ordered by population. Hồ Chí Minh is written here in its official form; in speech and in plenty of records it is still Sài Gòn, and "TP. HCM" and "TP. Hồ Chí Minh" are both common in written data. Huế was a provincial city until 2025, when it became Vietnam's sixth centrally-governed city. Values are Vietnamese: Latin letters that carry up to two marks at once — a vowel modifier (ă â ê ô ơ ư) plus a tone (à á ả ã ạ) — so "Đà Nẵng", "Quy Nhơn" and "Buôn Ma Thuột" hold precomposed code points (NFC) that naive ASCII-folding, NFD round-trips and byte-length checks all get wrong: "Nguyễn" is six characters but eight bytes.
