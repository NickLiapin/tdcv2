description: Universities of Vietnam (Vietnamese names). Vietnamese distinguishes three words that all come out as "university" in English and the distinction is real: "Đại học Quốc gia" and the regional "Đại học" are umbrella institutions holding member schools; a "Trường Đại học" is a single school, and is a member of one of those umbrellas when its name carries a suffix like "- ĐHQG TP.HCM"; a "Học viện" is an academy, usually attached to a ministry. So "Trường Đại học Bách khoa - ĐHQG TP.HCM" and "Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội" are not the same kind of thing despite reading alike. Values are Vietnamese with stacked diacritics on single vowels, which breaks naive ASCII-folding and byte-length checks.
locale: vi
