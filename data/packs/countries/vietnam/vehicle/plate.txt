description: Vietnamese vehicle plate — a 2-digit province code, a series letter, then five digits punctuated 000.00, e.g. 30A-123.45. THE PROVINCE CODE HERE IS NOT THE ONE IN A CCCD: plates use their own two-digit table (Hà Nội 29-33 and 40, Hồ Chí Minh 50-59, Hải Phòng 15-16, Đà Nẵng 43, Cần Thơ 65), while vietnam.geo.provinceCode carries the three-digit administrative code (Hà Nội 001, Hồ Chí Minh 079) and postcodes use a third two-digit prefix again. Three schemes, same 63 provinces, no agreement between them — do not derive one from another. The busiest provinces hold several codes because one ran out. The series letter is the issue batch; I, J, O, Q, R and W are avoided because they read as digits or as each other. Cars registered before the five-digit series still carry four digits (30A-1234) and both forms are on the road; two-letter series exist for special categories (LD joint venture, NN foreign, NG diplomatic, KT economic) and are not generated here. From August 2023 a plate follows its owner rather than the vehicle (biển số định danh), which changed who keeps the number on resale but not the format. Motorbike plates use a letter-plus-digit series (29-K1) and are a different shape from this.
