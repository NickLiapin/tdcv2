description: UK postcode — a real postcode area + district number, a space, then the inward code (one digit + two letters). Like the Mexican zip, the geographic head (the area) is real while the district and inward parts are plausible fill; the inward pair excludes C,I,K,M,O,V per Royal Mail rules. The central-London sub-district-letter forms (SW1A, EC1A) are not modelled.
generator: tdc

<!-- District 1-99: no leading zero, matching real outward codes. -->

<!-- Inward code: a digit then two letters, none from the excluded set C,I,K,M,O,V. -->
<sequence name="inward"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9][ABD-HJLNP-UW-Z]{2}"/></sequence>
<data>${{area}}${{district}} ${{inward}}</data>
