address: luxembourg.phoneLandlinePrefix
description: The two-digit blocks a Luxembourg fixed line is built on. They were geographic once and the map is still readable in them — 22, 25, 28 and 29 are Luxembourg City, 42 and 43 the Kirchberg plateau and the airport, 53 to 57 the Esch-sur-Alzette basin, 72 Echternach, 75 Grevenmacher, 80 Diekirch, 81 Ettelbruck, 92 Clervaux, 95 Wiltz, 99 Troisvierges — but they stopped being reliable in 1999, when number portability arrived and a subscriber could move house and keep the number, so an inference from prefix to town is a guess about where someone was living when the line was installed. They are not dialling codes and are never dialled on their own: Luxembourg's plan is closed, there is no trunk prefix, and the whole eight-digit number is dialled from everywhere including abroad. 20 is deliberately absent — the ILR reserves it for VoIP rather than for a place — and so are 4 on its own (an old Luxembourg City block from the six-digit era) and the 04 and 05 blocks the operators keep for internal routing. Source: ILR, règlement 14/174/ILR of 14 July 2014, and the historic prefix allocation published with it.
