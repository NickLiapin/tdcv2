address: luxembourg.finance.bank
description: Every credit institution and payment institution that holds an IBAN bank code in the Luxembourg payment system, from the register the ABBL — the banks' own association — publishes and keeps current. The list is the shape of the Luxembourg market and it is not the shape of a normal country's: 132 codes for 694,000 residents, and the names tell you why. Almost none of these banks are retail banks. Four are — Spuerkeess (the state savings bank, "Banque et Caisse d'Epargne de l'Etat"), BIL, BGL BNP Paribas and Raiffeisen, plus POST Luxembourg for the CCP accounts — and the rest are private banks, fund custodians, and the Luxembourg branches of banks headquartered somewhere else, because Luxembourg is where a European fund keeps its depositary and its transfer agent. Six Chinese banks hold Luxembourg codes; so do Brown Brothers Harriman, Clearstream, State Street, the EIB and the Banque centrale du Luxembourg. Since 2020 the register has also opened to payment institutions and e-money houses — PayPal, MANGOPAY, Satispay, Vivid Money, Rakuten — which is why the codes above 600 are mostly not banks in the old sense at all. Names are as the ABBL registers them, including the branch suffixes that make an entity Luxembourgish for payment purposes. Source: ABBL, Luxembourg Register of IBAN-BIC Codes, version of 31 July 2026.
