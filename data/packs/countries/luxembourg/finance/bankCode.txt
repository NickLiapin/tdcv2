---
address: luxembourg.finance.bankCode
description: The three-digit bank identification codes of the Luxembourg IBAN — the first three characters of the BBAN, sitting between the two check digits and the thirteen-digit account number. They are the whole of the Luxembourg BBAN's structure: unlike the German BLZ or the British sort code there is no branch part, no clearing part and no national check digit inside them, so LU + check + 3 + 13 is the entire scheme. The low numbers are the country's history in order of arrival: 001 is Spuerkeess, 002 BIL, 003 BGL BNP Paribas, 009 Raiffeisen, 014 ING, 111 POST for the CCP accounts that predate IBAN altogether, 999 the Banque centrale du Luxembourg and 998 the European Investment Bank, which is headquartered in Luxembourg and therefore has one. A code is not the same thing as a bank: several banks hold two or three (Société Générale has 060 and 061, Banque Havilland 284 and 318, PayPal 351 and 751), so this file has 132 codes for 120 distinct institution names. Draw this together with luxembourg.finance.iban rather than inventing three digits — a Luxembourg IBAN whose first three BBAN digits are not in this list has a correct mod-97 and a bank that does not exist. Source: ABBL, Luxembourg Register of IBAN-BIC Codes, version of 31 July 2026.
