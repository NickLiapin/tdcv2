address: luxembourg.finance.bic
description: The BIC (SWIFT) codes of the institutions in the Luxembourg IBAN register, one per bank code. Every one has LU in characters 5 and 6 — that is the ISO 3166 country field of the BIC and it is the only part of the code a validator can check against Luxembourg — and characters 7 and 8 are the location, most often LL for Luxembourg City. BCEELULL is Spuerkeess, BILLLULL is BIL, BGLLLULL is BGL BNP Paribas, CCPLLULL is POST, CEDELULL is Clearstream, BCLXLULL the central bank. 123 of the 132 entries are the 8-character head-office form; payment systems that want 11 pad those with XXX, and the other 9 are already 11 characters because the institution registered a branch code (BOFALULLVAM, SAFRLULLCCY, FUNDLU2LXXX), so a fixed-width 8 or 11 assumption breaks on this list in both directions. Note the LOCATION code in characters 7 and 8 is not always letters: SGABLU2S, BMARLU2M and MOLULU22 carry a digit there, which is legal in a BIC and trips a naive eight-letter regex. 128 codes are distinct — a bank with two IBAN codes may point both at one BIC. Source: ABBL, Luxembourg Register of IBAN-BIC Codes, version of 31 July 2026.
