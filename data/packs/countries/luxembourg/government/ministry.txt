description: The twenty-two ministerial portfolios of the Luxembourg government. The count against the population is the fact: twenty-two portfolios for 694,000 residents, held by a cabinet of around fifteen people, which means most Luxembourgish ministers hold two or three at once — the same person is routinely Minister of Finance and something else, and a Luxembourgish record naming "the minister" is naming a portfolio, not a person. The portfolios are also renamed at almost every change of government, far more freely than the administrations under them, so a list of ministries dates a document within a legislature: the Ministère des Affaires intérieures was the Ministère de l'Intérieur before 2023, and the Ministère de la Digitalisation only exists at all because Luxembourg made it a portfolio of its own in 2018. Note that there is no ministry of the interior in the policing sense — the police report to the Ministère des Affaires intérieures but the courts and the prosecution service belong to the Ministère de la Justice — and that Défense and Affaires étrangères are separate portfolios in a country whose army numbers under a thousand. Written the way the government writes them, "Ministère de/des" plus the portfolio. Source: gouvernement.lu, the composition of the government.
