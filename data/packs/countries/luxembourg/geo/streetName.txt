address: luxembourg.geo.streetName
description: Real Luxembourg street names out of the national address register, weighted by the number of localities in which the register records a street of that name. Two things here are Luxembourg and not France or Germany. First, THE MOST COMMON STREET NAME IN THE COUNTRY IS "Maison" — 268 localities use it — because a village too small to name its streets numbers its houses instead, and the address is literally "Maison 12, L-9645 Derenbach". A validator that requires a street type in front of the number rejects several thousand perfectly good Luxembourgish addresses. Second, the register mixes languages inside one column and always has: of the 6,455 distinct names it holds, about 3,600 are French in form (Rue de l'Eglise 67, Rue des Champs 65, Rue Principale 56, Rue de la Gare 52) and about 1,500 are Luxembourgish or German (Haaptstrooss 49, Duerfstrooss 35, Millewee 28, Am Eck 22), with the rest bare toponyms belonging to neither. Nothing sorts them by region: a French name and a Luxembourgish one sit on facing sides of the same village. That is why this file declares no locale — the values are genuinely two languages, and tagging it "fr" would be a lie about half of them. Which names are here: every name the register records in two or more localities, plus every street of Luxembourg City and Esch-sur-Alzette. The 5,466 names that occur exactly once in one small village are left out to keep the file to the size the other packs run to; one obvious test entry in the register ("10ième machine SEO") is dropped. Source: Administration du cadastre et de la topographie, Registre national des localités et des rues, table RUE, extract of 10 August 2026.
weighted: true
