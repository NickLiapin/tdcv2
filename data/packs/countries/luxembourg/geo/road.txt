address: luxembourg.geo.road
description: The numbered road network of Luxembourg outside the motorways — 99 routes nationales (N) and 325 chemins repris (CR), as the national address register holds them. The CR is the Luxembourgish oddity: a "chemin repris" is literally a road TAKEN BACK OVER, a former communal road the state resumed responsibility for, so the letters record who pays for the tarmac rather than how big the road is, and a CR can be anything from a two-lane link between villages to a lane over a hill. Numbers carry a letter suffix when a road has been split or realigned (CR152B, CR152C, CR152D, N 5D), which is why a plain integer field loses information here. The motorways are not in this list: there are six of them, lettered A, 165 km in all, held on a different scheme — and at 57 km of motorway per 1,000 km² that is the densest motorway network in Europe, against 837 km of routes nationales and 1,891 km of chemins repris that this file covers. Written as they appear on signs and maps — N5D, N50, CR101A — from the register's two-character type field and four-character number field. Source: Administration du cadastre et de la topographie, Registre national des localités et des rues, table CPTCH, extract of 10 August 2026.
