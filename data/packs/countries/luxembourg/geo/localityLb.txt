description: The only Luxembourgish place names the Luxembourg state itself writes down. The national address register (CACLR), kept by the Administration du cadastre et de la topographie under the loi du 25 juillet 2002, holds ONE official name per locality and that name is French — 590 of them. Alongside it the register keeps an alias table of 144 entries, and exactly 80 of those carry the flag "L" for Lëtzebuergesch: Bieles for Belvaux, Suessem for Sanem, Zolver for Soleuvre, Stroossen for Strassen, Bartreng for Bertrange, Beetebuerg for Bettembourg. Those 80 are this file. What is NOT in it is the interesting part: the register has no Luxembourgish alias for Luxembourg, for Esch-sur-Alzette or for Dudelange, even though everybody says Lëtzebuerg, Esch-Uelzecht and Diddeleng. The Luxembourgish name of a place is therefore a real thing with no complete official list — which is exactly why this pack keeps French as the default and ships the Luxembourgish forms separately, here for localities and in luxembourg.geo.communeLb for communes, rather than pretending one file can carry a trilingual country. Written as the register writes them, including the entries it stores in capitals. Source: CACLR, table ALIAS.LOCALITE, extract of 10 August 2026.
