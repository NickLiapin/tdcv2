address: luxembourg.geo.commune
description: All 100 communes of Luxembourg, weighted by their population at 1 January 2026. The count is the fact worth holding, because it has moved four times in fifteen years: 118 communes in 2006, 106 after the mergers of 1 January 2012, 105 after 1 January 2015, 102 after 1 January 2018, and 100 since 1 September 2023, when Bous and Waldbredimus became Bous-Waldbredimus and Grosbous and Wahl became Groussbus-Wal. So a list of 102 is pre-September-2023, a list of 105 is pre-2018 and a list of 118 is pre-2012, and the round number is the government's own headline — 100 communes is the fewest the country has had since the communes were created in the 1840s. The commune is the only level of local government Luxembourg has: the three DISTRICTS (Luxembourg, Diekirch, Grevenmacher) were abolished on 3 October 2015 and no longer exist, and the twelve CANTONS in luxembourg.geo.canton are statistical and judicial areas with no council and no budget. So a Luxembourg address has a commune above it and nothing else administrative in between. Names are the official FRENCH forms used by the law, the postal address and the Syvicol register; luxembourg.geo.communeLb carries the Luxembourgish forms of the same 100. Note that a commune is not a locality: the commune of Sanem has 19,611 residents spread over four villages — Belvaux 9,370, Soleuvre 6,443, Sanem 2,874 and Ehlerange 1,001 — so the village the commune is named after is only its third largest, and none of the four is called by the commune's name on an envelope. For the place that goes on an envelope, draw luxembourg.geo.city. Sources: Syvicol, Annuaire des communes, for the names; STATEC, Population par commune au 1er janvier 2026, for the counts.
