address: luxembourg.phoneNational
description: A Luxembourg mobile number written the way people in Luxembourg write it — the three-digit block, then three, then three: 621 123 456. There is no leading zero and no area code to put in front, because the plan is closed and non-geographic, so the national form is the international one with the +352 removed and nothing added back. Use this when a column is meant to look like something a person typed; use luxembourg.phone when it has to be machine-dialable. Fixed lines are grouped differently — in twos, 26 12 34 56 — which is luxembourg.phoneLandline's business, and the two groupings are a reliable way to tell at a glance which kind of number you are looking at even though both belong to the same closed plan. Businesses often print a line with a hyphenated extension after it (47 59 81-1), and that hyphen and what follows are NOT part of the number.
