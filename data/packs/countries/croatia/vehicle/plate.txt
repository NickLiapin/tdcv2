description: Croatian vehicle plate — a two-letter code for the town of registration, then three or four digits, a dash and one or two letters (ZG 123-AB). On the physical plate the Croatian chequy coat of arms sits between the town code and the digits; there is no character for it, so this generator writes a space. All 34 issued town codes are here, diacritics included — ZG Zagreb, ST Split, RI Rijeka, OS Osijek, ČK Čakovec, KR Krapina, KŽ Križevci, ŠI Šibenik, VŽ Varaždin, ŽU Županja and the rest. Velika Gorica has no code of its own; it registers as ZG.
generator: tdc
locale: hr
