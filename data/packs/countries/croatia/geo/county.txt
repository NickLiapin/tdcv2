description: The twenty counties (županije) of Croatia plus the City of Zagreb, in the official numbering order. The županija is Croatia's FIRST-level division — unlike the county in the USA pack, which is a second-level unit under a state. Grad Zagreb is not a županija but holds the powers of one, which is why the country is usually described as "twenty counties and the City of Zagreb" rather than twenty-one counties.
