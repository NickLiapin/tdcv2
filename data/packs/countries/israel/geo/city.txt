description: Cities and larger local-council towns of Israel, Hebrew names, roughly ordered by population. Names that are officially double-barrelled keep their hyphen (תל אביב-יפו, מודיעין-מכבים-רעות, מעלות-תרשיחא) because that hyphen is part of the legal name, not a line break. Arab-majority localities are listed under the Hebrew spelling used by Israeli registries (אום אל-פחם, סחנין, שפרעם); the Arabic form of the same names belongs to the ar language pack, not here. Localities beyond the 1949 armistice line are not listed — see the pack report.
