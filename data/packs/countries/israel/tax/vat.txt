address: israel.tax.vat
description: Israeli VAT registration number (מספר עוסק מורשה). Israel issues no separate VAT identifier and no letter country prefix — unlike an EU member state, where a VAT number is the national tax number with "PL"/"DE"/"FR" welded on the front. What a business quotes as its VAT number IS its existing 9-digit number: the owner's Teudat Zehut for a sole trader (עוסק מורשה), the company number (ח.פ.) for a company. So the only check the number carries is its own — the same weights-1,2 fold used by the Teudat Zehut — and a validator written for an EU-shaped "two letters plus digits" will reject every genuine Israeli VAT number. This file draws across the whole 9-digit space; a value beginning 51 or 52 reads as a company, anything else as a sole trader. VAT itself has been levied at 18% since January 2025 (17% before that); the rate is not part of the number.
generator: tdc
