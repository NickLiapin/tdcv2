description: Israeli football clubs, Hebrew names — Ligat ha'Al and Liga Leumit. Israeli club names are built from one of three historic sports-federation prefixes (מכבי, הפועל, בית"ר) plus a place, so the prefix is part of the name and never dropped, and two different clubs can share a town: מכבי תל אביב and הפועל תל אביב are rivals, not variants of one another. Israel plays in UEFA rather than the Asian confederation, which is why its clubs appear in European competition.
