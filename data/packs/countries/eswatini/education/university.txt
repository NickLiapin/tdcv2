description: Eswatini's universities. The University of Eswatini descends from the shared university of Basutoland, Bechuanaland and Swaziland — the same institution that produced the universities of Lesotho and Botswana — and became national in 1982. It was the University of Swaziland until the 2018 renaming, so degrees issued before then carry the old name.
