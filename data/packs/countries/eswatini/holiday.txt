description: Eswatini's public holidays. Two are ceremonies rather than dates: Incwala, the kingship ritual held around the December solstice, and the Umhlanga reed dance in late August-September — both move with the lunar and agricultural calendar and both are central to the monarchy's authority, which here is executive rather than symbolic. Independence from Britain is 6 September 1968 and is called Somhlolo Day after King Sobhuza I.
