description: Bruneian public holidays. National Day on 23 February marks full independence from Britain in 1984 — Brunei was the last British protectorate in Asia. The Sultan's birthday is a national holiday and the Sultan has ruled since 1967, one of the longest reigns in the world. The calendar is largely Islamic: Ramadan, both Eids, Isra Mikraj, the Islamic New Year and the Prophet's birthday are all observed, and Chinese New Year is kept for the Chinese minority.
