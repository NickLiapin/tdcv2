description: Universities of Myanmar, Burmese names. NEARLY ALL OF THEM ARE STATE INSTITUTIONS AND EACH ANSWERS TO A DIFFERENT MINISTRY, which is the structural fact a foreign reader misses: the medical schools belong to the Ministry of Health, the technological universities to Science and Technology, the education colleges to Education, ကာကွယ်ရေးတက္ကသိုလ် to Defence, and ကူးသန်းရောင်းဝယ်ရေးတက္ကသိုလ် to Commerce — so "the university system" is not one system and admission to each runs on a separate national ranking. Admission is decided almost entirely by the matriculation exam score, and the score decides the SUBJECT as much as the place: medicine takes the highest marks, then dentistry and engineering, and a student does not choose a faculty so much as receive one. NOTE THE NUMBERED MEDICAL SCHOOLS — ဆေးတက္ကသိုလ်(၁)ရန်ကုန်, ဆေးတက္ကသိုလ်(၂)ရန်ကုန် — where the number in Burmese digits is part of the institution's name and not a campus index, and any romanisation that drops it leaves two different universities with the same name. ရန်ကုန်တက္ကသိုလ်, founded 1920, is the country's oldest and was the centre of the independence movement and of 1962, 1974 and 1988, which is why the military governments spent decades breaking it up: undergraduate teaching was moved out to ဒဂုံတက္ကသိုလ်, အရှေ့ရန်ကုန်တက္ကသိုလ် and အနောက်ရန်ကုန်တက္ကသိုလ် on the city's edge specifically to keep students off the central campus and away from each other, and several of the universities in this list exist for that reason rather than an academic one. Since the 2021 coup a large part of the sector has been in a Civil Disobedience Movement strike, with many academics dismissed and many students refusing to enrol, so current enrolment in these institutions is nothing like their capacity — a Myanmar education dataset drawn as if all of them are operating normally is describing 2019.
