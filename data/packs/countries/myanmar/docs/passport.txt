description: Myanmar passport number as printed by the နိုင်ငံကူးလက်မှတ်ထုတ်ပေးရေးဌာန — two Latin letters followed by six digits, e.g. MB544664. Latin, not Burmese: the passport is an ICAO 9303 travel document with a machine-readable zone, so it is the one Myanmar identity document whose number is written in the Latin alphabet, and it is NOT derived from and does not contain the holder's NRC. There is no check digit inside the number, and nothing about the number itself is verifiable: the check digits that exist are the ICAO 9303 ones computed over the machine-readable zone, which are a property of the printed MRZ and not of the number — and those ARE computed here, for real and against the published Doc 9303 specimen, at myanmar.docs.passportMrz. It is the only arithmetic in this pack that verifies anything, because it is the only published one Myanmar has. The two-letter prefix on current books is drawn from the ranges actually seen in circulation rather than from a published allocation table, because none is published. DO NOT CONFUSE THE NUMBER WITH THE PASSPORT TYPE. Myanmar issues nine types and stamps the type on the book — PV visit, PJ job, PS seaman, PB business, PE student, PT dependent, PR religious, PD diplomatic, PO official — and PV, PS and PJ are the common ones. The type governs what the holder may do abroad: a Myanmar citizen cannot simply take a job overseas on a PV, and the PJ is issued against a labour-ministry overseas-employment record. That two-letter type code is a separate field from the two letters at the front of the number, and a data model that folds them together will produce passports whose number claims a type the book does not have. Not modelled: the type code, and the pre-2021 non-biometric books whose numbers are still valid on unexpired documents.
