address: myanmar.docs.nrcPrefix
description: The first half of a Myanmar NRC number — the STATE NUMBER and the TOWNSHIP CODE, kept together in one value so the pair can never be wrong. An NRC reads 12/ဗဟန(နိုင်)123456: the 12 is Yangon, ဗဟန is Bahan township, and the two are allocated together by the township immigration office that issued the card. THE NRC STATE NUMBERS ARE NOT THE POSTAL ONES AND NOT THE ISO ONES, and this is the single most common way Myanmar identity data is got wrong. NRC counts 1 Kachin, 2 Kayah, 3 Kayin, 4 Chin, 5 Sagaing, 6 Tanintharyi, 7 Bago, 8 Magway, 9 Mandalay, 10 Mon, 11 Rakhine, 12 Yangon, 13 Shan, 14 Ayeyarwady — alphabetical-ish in Burmese and fixed since the 1950s. The POSTCODE counts 01 Kachin, 02 Sagaing, 03 Chin, 04 Magway, 05 Mandalay, 06 Shan, 07 Rakhine, 08 Bago, 09 Kayah, 10 Ayeyarwady, 11 Yangon, 12 Mon, 13 Kayin, 14 Tanintharyi, 15 Nay Pyi Taw. Only Kachin agrees. So 12 means Yangon on an ID card and Mon State on an envelope, and a join between the two schemes silently relocates most of the country — see myanmar.geo.postalPrefix. THERE IS NO 15 HERE AND NEVER WILL BE: Nay Pyi Taw was carved out of Mandalay Region in 2005, thirty years after the NRC numbering was fixed, so a person registered in ဇဗသ (Zabuthiri) or ပဗသ (Pubbathiri) or ပျဉ်းမနား (Pyinmana) carries a 9 — the union capital has no NRC number of its own and its residents are numbered as if Mandalay Region still contained them. The township code is three Burmese consonants abbreviating the township name, one letter per syllable: ဗဟန from ဗဟန်း, ဒဂန from ဒဂုံ, ဥကတ from တောင်ဥက္ကလာပ. It is a BURMESE-SCRIPT code and there is no agreed Latin transliteration of it — the same ဥကတ is written OuKaTa, OUKATA, OKT and 3/OKT by different systems, which is why no romanised form is shipped in this pack. Values here are the codes as published in the openly available NRC township table (github.com/htetoozin/Myanmar-NRC); duplicates within a state and any code carrying a Zawgyi-only codepoint were dropped, and the township NAMES from that source are deliberately not shipped because several of them are misspelt (ချမ်းအေးသာစည် for ချမ်းအေးသာစံ) — see myanmar.geo.township for hand-checked names. This is most of the country's township codes but not provably all of them: townships have been created, merged and renamed since, and no government list of the codes is published.
locale: my
