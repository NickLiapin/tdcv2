description: NAMED bodies of the Myanmar state — the particular departments a Myanmar record actually refers to, as against the generic ministry-and-office words in the Burmese language pack at my.government.agency. THE ONE TO UNDERSTAND IS အထွေထွေအုပ်ချုပ်ရေးဦးစီးဌာန, the General Administration Department, and it has no counterpart in most countries: it is the single department that runs the country's territory at every level below the union, from the region down to the ward, and it employs the officer who chairs the township committee, keeps the household registration list, signs the recommendation letters without which a citizen can do almost nothing, and collects the land tax. Which ministry it sits under is therefore one of the most consequential facts in Myanmar governance — it was moved from Home Affairs, which the army controls under the constitution, to the civilian Office of the Union Government in 2018, and moved straight back in 2021. Below it, ရပ်ကွက်အုပ်ချုပ်ရေးမှူးရုံး, the ward administrator's office, is where ordinary life meets the state: the overnight-guest list, the household list, the letter you need to open a bank account. လူဝင်မှုကြီးကြပ်ရေးနှင့်ပြည်သူ့အင်အားဝန်ကြီးဌာန issues the NRC and the passport and keeps the population register; ပြည်တွင်းအခွန်များဦးစီးဌာန issues the TIN; ရင်းနှီးမြှုပ်နှံမှုနှင့်ကုမ္ပဏီများညွှန်ကြားမှုဦးစီးဌာန (DICA) registers every company through the MyCO system; ကုန်းလမ်းပို့ဆောင်ရေးညွှန်ကြားမှုဦးစီးဌာန registers vehicles and issues plates; မြန်မာနိုင်ငံတော်ဗဟိုဘဏ် is the central bank. ရန်ကုန်မြို့တော်စည်ပင်သာယာရေးကော်မတီ and its Mandalay twin are city governments with their own taxes and their own by-laws, not branches of a ministry. Named institutions rather than ministries, because Myanmar reorganises and renames its ministries often — the number of them changed three times between 2011 and 2021 — while these departments persist under whatever ministry currently owns them.
