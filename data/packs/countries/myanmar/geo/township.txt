description: မြို့နယ် — the township, which is THE address unit of Myanmar and the one a foreign address model has no slot for. A Myanmar address is written from the small end to the large: house number, street or ward (ရပ်ကွက်), then township, then town or region — and it is the township, not the street and not the postcode, that routes post, identifies a school catchment, names the court and the General Administration Department office you deal with, and appears in the NRC number on your identity card. Two different streets with the same name in two townships are two different addresses and nothing else distinguishes them. This list covers the three urban areas whose townships appear in most addresses: the 33 townships of the ရန်ကုန် city area plus the outer Yangon Region townships, the six of မန္တလေး city with အမရပူရ and ပုသိမ်ကြီး beside them, and the eight of နေပြည်တော်. IT IS NOT THE NATIONAL LIST — Myanmar has around 330 townships and the rest are not shipped here, because the openly available township tables that would supply them carry misspelt names (ချမ်းအေးသာစည် where the township is ချမ်းအေးသာစံ) and a name that is nearly right is worse in an address than a name that is absent. The township CODES for the whole country, which are the part an NRC needs, are shipped in full at myanmar.docs.nrcPrefix. Note the four ဒဂုံမြို့သစ် entries: they are separate townships, not compass variants of one, created in the late 1980s when the government moved several hundred thousand people out of central Yangon onto empty land, and their names carry the direction as part of the name. Note also that the ROMANISATION of these is unstable — သင်္ဃန်းကျွန်း is Thingangyun, Thingankyun and Thin Gan Kyun in three government documents — so a Latin township column will not join cleanly to anything.
