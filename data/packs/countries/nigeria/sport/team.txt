description: Football clubs of the Nigeria Premier Football League — the twenty of the 2025/26 season. Football means football here; Nigeria has no rival domestic sport with a professional league of this size, and the national team, the Super Eagles, carries more public attention than every club combined. THE CLUB NAMES ARE STATE NAMES for a reason worth knowing before treating them as ordinary brands: most NPFL clubs are owned and funded by their state governments rather than by private owners, which is why so many are simply a state or city plus a word — Rivers United, Nasarawa United, Bayelsa United, Kwara United, Katsina United, Plateau United. That also makes the league table unstable between seasons, since a change of governor can change a club's budget. The exceptions are the old independents and the newer private clubs: Enyimba of Aba, twice African champions and the most decorated Nigerian side; Enugu Rangers, the oldest and the 2025/26 champions; Shooting Stars of Ibadan; and Remo Stars, privately run, who won their first title in 2024/25.
