address: nigeria.geo.streetName
description: A Nigerian street — a name from nigeria.geo.streetNamed plus a designation. The designations are the English ones, because Nigerian street signage is in English, but the MIX is Nigerian rather than British: Close and Crescent are far commoner in Nigeria than in England, especially across Abuja's planned districts where whole neighbourhoods are Closes off a Crescent, and Way carries major urban arteries that a British city would call a Road. Expressway is included because Nigerian addresses genuinely sit on them (Lekki-Epe Expressway, Lagos-Ibadan Expressway). Note the Nigerian house-number habit this file does not encode: an address is very often "Plot 740" or "No. 15" rather than a bare number, and in unplanned areas there may be no number at all and the address is a landmark — "opposite the filling station" — which is a real property of Nigerian addressing and not something a generator should pretend away.
generator: tdc
locale: en
