description: Capitals of the 36 states, index-aligned with nigeria.geo.stateOrFct, with Abuja last as the seat of the Federal Capital Territory. Two lines are the ones people get wrong. LAGOS STATE'S CAPITAL IS IKEJA, NOT LAGOS — Lagos city is the commercial capital of the country and the state government sits at Ikeja, so a record pairing "Lagos" with "Lagos" is wrong even though it looks obviously right. And ABUJA IS NOT A STATE CAPITAL: it is the federal capital and the seat of the FCT, which is why it appears only on the last line, opposite the territory rather than opposite a state. Several capitals share their state's name (Bauchi, Enugu, Gombe, Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Sokoto), which is correct and not a copy error. Kebbi's capital is Birnin Kebbi, Ekiti's is Ado Ekiti, Osun's is Osogbo and Edo's is Benin City — the Nigerian one, no relation to the Republic of Benin next door.
