address: nigeria.geo.stateCode
description: ISO 3166-2:NG subdivision codes, index-aligned with nigeria.geo.stateOrFct — all 37 lines, the 36 states in alphabetical order then NG-FC for the Federal Capital Territory last. Because the first 36 lines match nigeria.geo.state exactly, the alignment holds against either file. ISO itself keeps the distinction this pack keeps: the 36 carry the category "state" and NG-FC alone carries "capital territory", and ISO's name for it is "Abuja Federal Capital Territory". The codes are NOT simply the first two letters of the name — Katsina is NG-KT and not NG-KA, Kaduna is NG-KD, Kebbi is NG-KE, Bayelsa is NG-BY against Bauchi's NG-BA, and Zamfara is NG-ZA — so they have to be enumerated rather than derived. The two-letter part is also not what a vehicle plate or a postal code uses; see nigeria.vehicle.plateCode and nigeria.geo.postalCode.
