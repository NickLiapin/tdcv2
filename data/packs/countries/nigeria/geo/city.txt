description: Cities of Nigeria, roughly by population. Africa's most populous country, past 200 million people, and the ranking below is rough on purpose — Nigeria has not completed a census since 2006 and every figure in circulation is a projection or a dispute, so treat the order as indicative and the membership as solid. Lagos is far larger than anything else and is one of the largest cities on earth; Abuja is the federal capital but not the largest city, a split Nigeria shares with Brazil and Turkey. Some names carry a second form in ordinary use: Ife is also Ile-Ife, Osogbo is also Oshogbo, Zaria's Hausa name is Zazzau, and Port Harcourt is universally shortened to PH.
