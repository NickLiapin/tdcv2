---
address: romania.finance.iban
description: Romanian IBAN — 24 chars: RO + 2 ISO 7064 mod-97-10 check digits + a four-LETTER bank code + a 16-character account number. Romania is in the EU but not the euro area, so the account behind it is denominated in lei, not euro.
generator: tdc
---
<!-- Unlike the Polish or Spanish BBAN, the Romanian one is not all digits: the
     bank is identified by four letters, which the mod-97 fold turns into their
     base-36 values before folding. -->
<sequence name="bank"><gen type="template" value="romania.finance.bankCode"/></sequence>
<sequence name="account"><gen type="regex" value="[0-9]{16}"/></sequence>
<sequence name="iban"><compute>
  <let name="bban"><concat><field name="bank"/><field name="account"/></concat></let>
  <!-- ISO 13616: move the country code and a "00" placeholder to the end. -->
  <let name="rearranged"><concat><use name="bban"/><str v="RO"/><str v="00"/></concat></let>
  <let name="digits"><join><each>
    <over><use name="rearranged"/></over>
    <do><encode as="base36"><current/></encode></do>
  </each></join></let>
  <!-- Horner fold, so the 30-digit value never has to exist as one number. -->
  <let name="rem"><reduce>
    <over><use name="digits"/></over>
    <init><int v="0"/></init>
    <do><mod><add><multiply><acc/><int v="10"/></multiply><current/></add><int v="97"/></mod></do>
  </reduce></let>
  <let name="check"><pad width="2" fill="0"><subtract><int v="98"/><use name="rem"/></subtract></pad></let>
  <result><concat><str v="RO"/><use name="check"/><use name="bban"/></concat></result>
</compute></sequence>
<data>${{iban}}</data>
