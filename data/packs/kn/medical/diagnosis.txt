description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient — no sex or ancestry restriction. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. Kannada clinical writing mixes inherited terms (ಮಧುಮೇಹ, ಕ್ಷಯರೋಗ, ಕಾಮಾಲೆ) with transliterated English for anything that entered medicine recently, and that is how a Kannada discharge summary actually reads. ಕ್ಯಾಸನೂರು ಅರಣ್ಯ ಕಾಯಿಲೆ, known locally as ಮಂಗನ ಕಾಯಿಲೆ, is on the list because it is endemic to the Western Ghats of Karnataka and essentially nowhere else.
locale: kn
