description: Kannada family names: community, caste-occupational and regional names actually inherited in Karnataka (ಗೌಡ, ಶೆಟ್ಟಿ, ಹೆಗಡೆ, ಕುಲಕರ್ಣಿ, coastal Tulu ones like ರೈ and ಆಳ್ವ, Kodava ones like ಪೂವಯ್ಯ). TWO THINGS A CONFIG CANNOT INFER. (1) An inherited surname exists in Kannada but is NOT universal: a very large share of Kannadigas instead write initials before the given name, where the initials stand for the ancestral village and the father's given name — ಎಚ್.ಡಿ. ದೇವೇಗೌಡ, ಬಿ.ಎಸ್. ಯಡಿಯೂರಪ್ಪ, ಡಿ.ಕೆ. ಶಿವಕುಮಾರ. So a Kannada surname column is legitimately empty for many real people; do not treat every row as needing one. (2) Kannada surnames do NOT inflect for gender — ಸುಧಾ ಮೂರ್ತಿ and ನಾರಾಯಣ ಮೂರ್ತಿ carry the same ಮೂರ್ತಿ — so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack. Its absence is the design. Weights are the project default rank decay, not measured frequencies; no surname census exists for Karnataka.
