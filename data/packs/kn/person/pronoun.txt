description: Kannada third-person pronoun, nominative/dative. Kannada marks gender only in the third person, and ಅವರು is both the plural and the respectful singular used for any adult one is not intimate with — so ಅವರು is the safe default, not a plural-only form. Kannada has no neopronoun tradition; nothing equivalent to ze/xe was invented for this list.
locale: kn
