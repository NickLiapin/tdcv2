description: Event type. The first block is the Karnataka life-cycle set: ಗೃಹಪ್ರವೇಶ is the housewarming that consecrates a new house, ನಾಮಕರಣ the naming on the eleventh day, ಉಪನಯನ the sacred-thread rite, ಸೀಮಂತ the pre-birth blessing and ಷಷ್ಟ್ಯಬ್ದಿ the sixtieth-birthday re-marriage. ಜಾತ್ರೆ is a village temple fair, not a trade fair.
