description: Kannada thoroughfare type. It goes AFTER the name (ಎಂ.ಜಿ. ರಸ್ತೆ, ಜಯನಗರ ಬಡಾವಣೆ), the opposite of Polish or Spanish. ಮುಖ್ಯ ರಸ್ತೆ and ಅಡ್ಡ ರಸ್ತೆ are numbered rather than named across most of urban Karnataka: 5ನೇ ಮುಖ್ಯ ರಸ್ತೆ, 3ನೇ ಅಡ್ಡ ರಸ್ತೆ. ಓಣಿ is the north Karnataka word for a lane and ಗಲ್ಲಿ the south one.
locale: kn
