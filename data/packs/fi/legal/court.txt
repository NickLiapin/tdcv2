description: Court (Finnish judiciary). Finland runs two parallel ladders: the general courts (käräjäoikeus, hovioikeus, korkein oikeus) and the administrative courts (hallinto-oikeus, korkein hallinto-oikeus) that hear disputes with public authorities. Valtakunnanoikeus is the rarest of them — the High Court of Impeachment, convened only to try a minister or a supreme-court justice, and it has sat four times in a century.
