description: Academic degree / qualification level as awarded in Finland. Finland has two parallel higher-education ladders: the universities (kandidaatti, maisteri, tohtori) and the ammattikorkeakoulut, whose AMK and YAMK degrees are professionally oriented and not junior versions of the university ones. Diplomi-insinööri is the five-year engineering master's and is used as a title. Dosentti is a qualification above the doctorate rather than a post.
