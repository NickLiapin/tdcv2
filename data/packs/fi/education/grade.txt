description: Grade / mark in the Finnish school system. Comprehensive school and upper secondary use the numeric scale 4-10, where 4 is a fail and 10 the top mark — there is no 1, 2 or 3. The matriculation examination is different and famously so: it is graded in LATIN, from laudatur down to improbatur, abbreviated L, E, M, C, B, A and I, and a Finn will say "I got an L in maths" for the rest of their life.
