Three are Finnish and have no international equivalent in this list: Suomi24 is the country's large anonymous discussion forum, Vauva.fi began as a parenting site and became a national gossip board in its own right, and Tori.fi is the classifieds site every Finn uses for second-hand goods. Jodel, though German, took hold in Finland far more strongly than almost anywhere else.
