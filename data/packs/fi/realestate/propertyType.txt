description: Property type (Finnish). The Finnish system needs explaining: you almost never own a flat, you own SHARES in an asunto-osakeyhtiö, a housing company that owns the building, and those shares entitle you to a named flat. That is what kerrostaloasunto and rivitaloasunto mean in a Finnish deed. Asumisoikeusasunto is a third tenure between owning and renting — you pay a capital share of about 15 % and then a monthly charge, and you can never sell it at a profit. Only a detached house on its own plot is owned outright.
locale: fi
