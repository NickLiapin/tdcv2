description: Property amenity (Finnish). Three are Finnish and unremarkable here while being unheard-of elsewhere. Sauna: nearly every Finnish flat block has one, many flats have their own, and a listing without a sauna says so as a drawback. Lämmitystolppa: the post beside a parking space that a car's block heater is plugged into, without which a Finnish car does not reliably start in February. Väestönsuoja: the civil defence shelter that Finnish law requires in larger buildings, used the rest of the time as a storage cage — Finland has shelter places for some 4.8 million people.
