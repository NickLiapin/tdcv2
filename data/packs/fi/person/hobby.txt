description: Hobby / interest (Finnish). Note what is NOT in this list: saunominen. Going to the sauna is not a Finnish hobby, it is infrastructure — there are roughly three million saunas for 5.6 million people, every flat block has one, most workplaces have one, Parliament has one, and a Finn heats it the way other countries run a bath. It therefore appears in this pack as a building type, a property amenity, a hotel facility and a kind of work event, and not here. Avantouinti — cutting a hole in the lake ice and swimming in it, usually straight out of that sauna — IS a hobby and is here. Several others are Finnish institutions rather than generic pastimes: marjastus and sienestys (berry and mushroom picking, made possible for everyone by jokamiehenoikeudet, the right of public access, which lets anyone walk and pick on land they do not own), mökkeily (going to the summer cabin, which is what August is for), pilkkiminen (ice fishing through a hole), suunnistus and the mass amateur choirs.
locale: fi
