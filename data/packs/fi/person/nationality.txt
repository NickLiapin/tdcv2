description: Nationality / demonym (Finnish). Finnish forms these with -lainen / -läinen on the country stem — Suomi gives suomalainen, Ruotsi gives ruotsalainen — and the result serves as both the adjective and the noun, with no separate masculine and feminine form, because Finnish has no gender. Note the vowel harmony: a word with back vowels takes -lainen and one with front vowels takes -läinen (venäläinen, virolainen but englantilainen), and the choice is not free.
locale: fi
