description: Finnish female given names, frequency-ordered. Counts for the leading thirty are bearer figures on the scale the Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV) publishes for names in FIRST position, rounded; the tail is a smooth decay anchored to the same scale, not the default rank curve. As with the male list, Finns carry two or three given names and are called by the first. The traditional second names for women — Kaarina, Helena, Anneli, Marjatta, Hannele, Tuulikki, Sinikka, Kyllikki, Annikki, Orvokki, Tellervo, Mielikki — are far commoner counted in any position than in first position, and several of the -kki names come straight out of the Kalevala. They sit in the middle of this list for that reason rather than at the top. Note also the Finnish naming calendar: every day of the year carries names, a nimipäivä is genuinely celebrated, and the almanac is what keeps names like Vappu, Vuokko, Sylvi and Impi in circulation. Several names here are ordinary Finnish nouns used as names — Kielo is a lily of the valley, Kanerva heather, Lumi snow, Vieno gentle — which Finnish allows freely because a name needs no gender marking to work.
