description: Language name (Finnish). The order at the top is constitutional, not editorial. Finland has TWO national languages, suomi and ruotsi, with equal status — every citizen may deal with the state in either, laws are published in both, and Swedish-speakers are about 5.2 % of the population. Below them sit the languages the constitution and the saamen kielilaki name separately: three Sámi languages (pohjoissaame, inarinsaame and koltansaame, the last two spoken by only a few hundred people each and only in Finland), romanikieli, karjala and suomalainen viittomakieli — Finnish Sign Language is a language in its own right and is protected by name in the constitution, alongside a separate Finland-Swedish sign language.
