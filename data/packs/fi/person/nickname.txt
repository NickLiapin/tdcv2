description: Finnish hypocoristic / everyday short name. Finnish forms these by cutting the name to one or two syllables and adding a heavy consonant plus -e, -a or -u: Matti becomes Masa, Lauri becomes Late, Timo becomes Timppa, Sami becomes Samppa, Antti becomes Antsa, Anne becomes Anski, Sanna becomes Sansku. The result is used as an ordinary everyday name, not only within a family. This list is deliberately shorter than the Polish or Swedish one — only forms I could confirm are in genuine current use are here, and nothing was coined to reach a target length.
locale: fi
