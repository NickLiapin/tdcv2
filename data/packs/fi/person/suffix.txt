description: Finnish post-nominal / name suffix. Finnish has no Jr / Sr — the equivalents are vanhempi and nuorempi, abbreviated vanh. and nuor. and written after the name, and they are rare. Everything else here is a Finnish degree abbreviation, and Finns use them after the name far more readily than Swedes or Britons do: FM is filosofian maisteri, DI diplomi-insinööri, VTM valtiotieteiden maisteri, LL lääketieteen lisensiaatti. The doctorate abbreviations end in -T and the licentiate ones in -L.
