description: Complete Finnish street names of the kind found in almost every Finnish town — nature, trades, infrastructure, national figures. They are complete because Finnish binds the thoroughfare type onto the name as one word (Koulukatu is a single word), so a name and a suffix cannot be drawn separately and joined with a space. Two Finnish facts show here. First, consonant gradation: the genitive that binds the parts weakens the stem, so Helsinki gives Helsinginkatu and Turku gives Turuntie. Second, when the honoured person is named in full, Finnish writes the genitive as a separate word instead of compounding — Aleksis Kiven katu, Minna Canthin katu — while a single name binds: Runeberginkatu. City-specific streets belong in a country pack.
locale: fi
