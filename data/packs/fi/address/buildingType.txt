description: Building type (Finnish). Rivitalo (a terrace), paritalo (a semi-detached pair) and luhtitalo (walk-up flats reached from an open external gallery) are the categories a Finnish building register actually uses, and saunarakennus — a free-standing sauna building — is an ordinary line in that register rather than a luxury: Finland has roughly three million saunas for 5.6 million people. Väestönsuoja, the civil defence shelter, is required by law in larger buildings.
