description: Trailing element of a Finnish place name (Rovaniemi, Seinäjoki, Valkeakoski, Lappeenranta). Almost every Finnish town name ends in a landscape word, because the names describe the terrain: -järvi a lake, -joki a river, -koski a rapid, -lahti a bay, -niemi a headland, -mäki a hill, -vaara a fell-side hill in the east and -tunturi a fell in Lapland. Compose after a cityPrefix. Note that the first part goes into the genitive and can gradate — Helsinki becomes Helsingin-, Turku becomes Turun-.
locale: fi
