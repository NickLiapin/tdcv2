description: Finnish thoroughfare element. Like Swedish and unlike English or Polish this is NOT a separate word: it is written joined onto the name as one compound, after it — Koulukatu, Rantatie, Mäntykuja — never "Koulu katu". The part in front goes into the genitive when it is a name, and the genitive triggers consonant gradation: Helsinki gives Helsinginkatu, Turku gives Turuntie, Aleksanteri gives Aleksanterinkatu. Use address/streetName for complete names; this list is for composing new ones.
