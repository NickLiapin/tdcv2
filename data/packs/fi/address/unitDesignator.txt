description: Finnish address unit designator. A Finnish flat address is street, number, stairwell letter, flat number — Mannerheimintie 12 A 5 — so the bare capital letter IS the designator for the stairwell (rappu) and needs no word in front of it. "as." abbreviates asunto and "krs" kerros; the ground floor is katutaso and is counted as floor 1, unlike Sweden. PL is postilokero, a PO box.
locale: fi
