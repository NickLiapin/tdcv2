description: Directional qualifier used with a Finnish district, estate or road name (Itäkeskus, Pohjois-Haaga, Länsi-Pasila). Finnish has single native words for the intermediate compass points — koillinen, luode, kaakko, lounas — instead of compounding north with east the way Germanic languages do. The hyphenated forms are the bound stems that go in front of a place name.
