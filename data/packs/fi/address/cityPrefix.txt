description: Leading element of a Finnish place name (Isojoki, Vähäkyrö, Ylöjärvi, Pohjois-Karjala). Finnish binds it onto the name as one word — Isojoki, never "Iso joki" — except in front of a proper name, where a hyphen is used: Pohjois-Karjala, Keski-Suomi, Suur-Savo. Compose with a citySuffix; it does not by itself name a real place. The binding can reshape the stem (Ylä + järvi gives the real town name Ylöjärvi), which is ordinary Finnish compounding rather than a typo.
locale: fi
