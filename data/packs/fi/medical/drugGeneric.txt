description: Common generic drug names as spelled in Finnish. Finnish writes the INN in Finnish orthography, which changes the look of many of them: c becomes k, ph becomes f, x becomes ks, and a final -e is dropped — so English ciprofloxacin is siprofloksasiini, clindamycin klindamysiini, oxycodone oksikodoni and paracetamol parasetamoli.
locale: fi
