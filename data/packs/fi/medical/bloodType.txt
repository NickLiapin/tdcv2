description: ABO / Rh blood type as written in Finland. The fourth group is the DIGIT ZERO, 0, not the letter O — Finnish (like Swedish, German and most of continental Europe) writes veriryhmä 0, and a Finnish record showing "O+" is following the English convention rather than the Finnish one. Finland has an unusually high share of A: roughly 44 % of Finns are group A and only about 6 % are AB.
