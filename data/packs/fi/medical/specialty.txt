description: Medical specialities (Finnish, as Valvira registers them). Finnish reaches for a plain native compound where English reaches for Greek: keuhkosairaudet instead of pulmonology, munuaistaudit instead of nephrology, ihotaudit, silmätaudit, korva-, nenä- ja kurkkutaudit. Yleislääketiede is the GP speciality, and työterveyshuolto is a speciality of its own here because Finnish law obliges every employer to provide occupational health care.
