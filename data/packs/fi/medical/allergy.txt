description: Common allergies (Finnish). Koivun siitepöly is the dominant spring allergen in Finland and cross-reacts with apple, carrot and stone fruit — the oral allergy syndrome that a very large share of allergic Finns have. Punkinpurema is here because of alfa-gal, the red-meat allergy a tick bite can trigger, and ticks are widespread in southern Finland.
