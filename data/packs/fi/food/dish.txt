description: Dish (Finnish). This is what Finns actually eat. Karjalanpiirakka — a rye crust around rice porridge, eaten with munavoi — is the everyday pastry sold in every shop; ruisleipä is sour rye bread and Finland eats more of it per head than anywhere; lohikeitto is the creamy salmon soup; kaalilaatikko, lanttulaatikko and perunalaatikko are the baked casseroles that make up a Christmas table. Mämmi is a black baked rye-malt pudding eaten only at Easter and is the single most divisive food in the country. Korvapuusti is the cardamom cinnamon bun, salmiakki the ammonium-chloride liquorice that visitors reliably spit out, and leipäjuusto a squeaky baked cheese served warm with cloudberry jam.
