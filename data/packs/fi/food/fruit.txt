description: Fruit (Finnish). The wild berries in the middle are ordinary groceries here, not exotica: mustikka, puolukka, vadelma, karpalo and variksenmarja are picked in the forest by anyone under jokamiehenoikeudet, the right of public access, which lets you pick berries on land you do not own. Note lakka and hilla — the cloudberry has two names, hilla in the north and lakka in the south, and Finns argue about which is correct.
