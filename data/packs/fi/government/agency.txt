description: Government body (Finnish agencies and public offices). Kela pays every social benefit from student aid to pensions and is the single agency a Finn deals with most. Digi- ja väestötietovirasto keeps the population register and issues the personal identity code. Hyvinvointialue is new and important: since 2023 healthcare and social services moved from the 300-odd municipalities to 21 wellbeing services counties, so a Finnish health record after that date belongs to a hyvinvointialue and not to a kunta.
