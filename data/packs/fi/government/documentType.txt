description: Official document type (Finnish). Verokortti is the one with no European equivalent: the Tax Administration issues every wage-earner a tax card carrying their personal withholding rate, and an employer without it must withhold at 60 %. Kela-kortti is the health insurance card used at every pharmacy. Virkatodistus is the certificate of civil status drawn from the population register — Finnish life asks for it constantly.
locale: fi
