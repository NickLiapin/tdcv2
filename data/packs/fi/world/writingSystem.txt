description: Writing system (Finnish). Note what the Finnish alphabet itself does: it adds å, ä and ö after z, and ä and ö are LETTERS IN THEIR OWN RIGHT rather than accented a and o — Finnish sorts them at the end of the alphabet, so Ähtäri comes after Zürich, and a system that folds them into a and o will sort a Finnish list wrongly. Å exists only in names of Swedish origin.
locale: fi
