description: Mobile carrier (Finnish operators and international brands). Elisa, Telia Finland and DNA own the three Finnish networks; Saunalahti is Elisa's budget brand and its name means "sauna bay", Moi Mobiili and Tele Finland are the other domestic budget brands. Worth knowing for any Finnish telecom dataset: unlimited-data subscriptions priced by speed rather than by gigabyte are the Finnish norm, and Finns use more mobile data per head than almost anyone in the world because of it.
