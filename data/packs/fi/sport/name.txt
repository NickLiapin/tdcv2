description: Sport (Finnish). Pesäpallo leads because it is the national sport — a Finnish reworking of baseball from the 1920s in which the ball is pitched straight up and the bases zigzag, played nowhere else. Keihäänheitto, the javelin, is close to a national obsession and Finland has won it more often than any other country. The last three are not jokes: eukonkanto (wife-carrying, world championships at Sonkajärvi), suopotkupallo (swamp football, at Hyrynsalmi) and saappaanheitto (boot throwing) are real competitive sports with real Finnish championships, and any Finn will confirm it.
