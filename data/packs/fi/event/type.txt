description: Event type (Finnish). Most of these are fixed points in the Finnish year rather than generic occasions: vappu on 1 May is a students' and workers' carnival, juhannus is the midsummer weekend when the cities empty for the lakes, pikkujoulut is the whole December season of workplace Christmas parties, and itsenäisyyspäivä on 6 December is spent watching the President shake 1 800 hands on television. Two are school rituals with no equivalent abroad: penkkarit, when school-leavers ride through town on decorated lorries throwing sweets, and vanhojen tanssit, the formal ballroom dance the year below them performs the next day. Nimipäivä matters here — Finnish name days are printed in every calendar and genuinely celebrated.
