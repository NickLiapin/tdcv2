Every Finnish month name is a transparent compound ending in -kuu, "moon": tammikuu is the oak month, heinäkuu the hay month, marraskuu the month of the dead. These are the NOMINATIVE forms. Inside a date Finnish puts the month in the partitive — 5. tammikuuta 2026 — and those forms are in DATE_LOCALE.json under monthsInDate.
