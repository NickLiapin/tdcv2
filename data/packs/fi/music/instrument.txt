description: Musical instrument (Finnish). Kantele is the national instrument — the plucked box zither that Väinämöinen builds from a pike's jawbone in the Kalevala — and jouhikko is the older bowed lyre it partly replaced. Haitari, the accordion, carries humppa and the dance-pavilion repertoire, and paimenhuilu and tuohitorvi are the herding instruments of the forest.
locale: fi
