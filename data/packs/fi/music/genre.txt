description: Music genre (Finnish). Four of these are Finnish institutions. Iskelmä is the domestic popular song; humppa is a fast two-beat dance music with its own bands; suomalainen tango is a genre of its own, in a minor key and far more melancholy than the Argentine kind, with an annual festival in Seinäjoki that crowns a Tango King and Queen; and rautalanka is the instrumental guitar rock of the early 1960s. Metal is not a joke entry either — Finland has more metal bands per head of population than any other country. Joiku is the Sámi vocal tradition and is not Finnish music but Sámi.
