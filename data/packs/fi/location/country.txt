description: Country and territory names (Finnish). Finnish keeps its own forms for a great many of them — Saksa, Ranska, Britannia, Alankomaat, Venäjä, Ruotsi, Viro, Norsunluurannikko — and uses no article. Note that Finnish country names take case endings like any other noun: Suomessa is "in Finland", Ruotsiin "to Sweden", so a config that puts one in a sentence must inflect it.
