description: Company legal / name suffix used in Finland. Oy is the limited company and Oyj the listed one; Ky and Ay are the two partnership forms and tmi the sole trader. Osk — osuuskunta, the co-operative — is not a curiosity here: Finland has the highest co-operative membership per head in the world, and the largest grocery chain (S-ryhmä) and the largest bank group (OP) are both co-operatives owned by their customers. Note that Finnish writes the suffix before or after the name with equal frequency: Oy Tekniikka Ab or Tekniikka Oy.
locale: fi
