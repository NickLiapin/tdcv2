description: Gemstones in Sinhala. THIS IS NOT AN ORNAMENTAL LIST FOR SRI LANKA — the island was called Ratnadeepa, the Island of Gems, for two thousand years, Ratnapura means "city of gems", and the gravels there still produce most of the world's fine blue sapphire and effectively all of its පද්මරාග, the pink-orange padparadscha whose very name is Sinhala for "lotus colour". The first nine entries are the නවරත්න, the nine gems of the classical set worn as a single ring against the nine planets, and a Sinhala speaker names them in that order.
