description: Flowers in Sinhala. THE LIST IS SHAPED BY THE TEMPLE, NOT BY THE FLORIST: අරලිය, පිච්ච, දෑසමන්, ඉද්ද, නෙළුම් and ඕලු are the flowers laid on a Buddhist shrine, which is why an ordinary Sinhala speaker can name them and not a florist's catalogue. නිල් මානෙල්, the blue water lily, is the national flower, and ඇහැළ, the golden shower, is the flower of the April new year.
