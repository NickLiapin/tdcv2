description: Colour names in Sinhala. THE NATIVE PALETTE IS SMALL AND MADE OF OBJECTS: කොළ (green) is literally "leaf", තැඹිලි (orange) is the king coconut, රන්වන් is "gold-coloured", දුඹුරු, කළු, සුදු, රතු, නිල්, කහ and දම් round out the basic terms, and shades are built with තද- "dark" and ලා- "light" rather than with separate words. Everything beyond that basic set is an English loan written in Sinhala script — which is not sloppiness but how the modern language names paint chips and product colours.
locale: si
