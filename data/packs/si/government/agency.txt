description: Government bodies as Sinhala names them. NAMED HERE RATHER THAN GENERICISED BECAUSE SINHALA HAS ONE COUNTRY — these are Sri Lanka's, and a reader should not have to guess whose. ප්‍රාදේශීය ලේකම් කාර්යාලය (Divisional Secretariat) and the ග්‍රාම නිලධාරී කාර්යාලය (village officer's office) below it are the two layers a citizen actually deals with, and they have no European counterpart: the village officer certifies who you are and where you live before almost any other transaction can proceed.
