description: Sinhala male given names. Most are transparent Sanskrit or Pali compounds and are still read as words, not as opaque labels — ඉසුරු is "wealth", ජය- "victory", සිරි- "splendour", ධම්ම-/ධර්ම- "the Dhamma", -පාල "protector", -දාස "servant of". THE LIST IS TWO GENERATIONS AT ONCE and that is deliberate: the -පාල / -දාස / -සිරි names (සෝමපාල, ජිනදාස, පද්මසිරි) belong to people born before about 1970 and are almost never given to children now, while the short vowel-final names (කසුන්, ලහිරු, තරිඳු, සදුන්) are post-1990. Drawing a birth year and a given name independently will therefore produce anachronistic pairs. A SMALL SET IS UNISEX — කුමුදු, තුෂාර, නයනා, මංජුල and සමන් are all given to both sexes — so an overlap between this file and person/female/firstName.txt is correct rather than a duplication bug. THE COUNTS ARE A RANKED APPROXIMATION, NOT MEASURED DATA: Sri Lanka publishes no given-name frequency table.
