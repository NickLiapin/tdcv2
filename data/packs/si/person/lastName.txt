NO FEMININE FORM EXISTS — a Sinhala surname does not inflect for gender, so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack; a woman carries her surname unchanged. TWO STOCKS COEXIST IN ONE POPULATION. The native Sinhala compounds (Jayawardena, Ratnayake, Dissanayake, Wickramasinghe) are Sanskrit/Pali-derived and freely productive; the Portuguese-era names (Perera, Fernando, Silva, de Silva, Peiris, Mendis, Dias, Fonseka, Rodrigo, Soysa, Corea, Almeida) arrived with the 16th-century Portuguese and later Dutch coastal administrations and are carried today by Sinhala Buddhists as readily as by Catholics — a Sri Lankan named ප්‍රනාන්දු is not thereby Portuguese or Christian. These are SINHALESE surnames only; the Sri Lankan Tamil name stock is entirely separate and lives in the ta pack. THE COUNTS ARE A RANKED APPROXIMATION, NOT MEASURED DATA: Sri Lanka publishes no surname frequency table, so the order is best-evidence and the decay is a gentle Zipf tuned so the commonest three take roughly 3-5% each.
