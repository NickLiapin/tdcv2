THE EVERYDAY SINHALA HONORIFIC IS A SUFFIX, NOT A PREFIX: නිමල් පෙරේරා මහතා, never මහතා නිමල් පෙරේරා. මහතා is Mr, මහත්මිය a married woman, මෙනවිය an unmarried one, and මහත්මයා/මැතිනිය are the raised-register variants used in speeches and citations. A config that concatenates person.prefix + name will produce correct Sinhala only for the doctorate and clergy titles in that file; for the ordinary courtesy title it must append from this one.
