description: Pastimes as a Sinhala speaker would name them. Four are locally specific rather than translations: භාවනාව (meditation) is a mainstream lay practice and not a niche one; කැරම් (carrom) is the board game of Sri Lankan households and clubs; දැල් පන්දු (netball) is the women's national sport; and බතික් is the island's own textile craft. ක්‍රිකට් leads the list because it does in life.
locale: si
