description: GE NAMA (ge name, house name) — the element a Sinhala full name carries BEFORE the given name, and the piece a European-shaped name field has nowhere to put. It names the house or lineage, not the individual: හේරත් මුදියන්සේලාගේ නිමල් බණ්ඩාර is "Nimal Bandara of the Herath Mudiyanse house". It is inherited patrilineally, it is NOT the surname (the surname still follows the given name), and it is often longer than either. The Kandyan -මුදියන්සේලාගේ / -ආරච්චිගේ / -අප්පුහාමිලාගේ forms end in the genitive -ගේ, literally "of the house of"; the -ගෙදර forms (ඉහළගෙදර "upper house", පහළගෙදර "lower house") locate the house in the village instead. IN EVERYDAY WRITING THE GE NAME IS ABBREVIATED TO INITIALS and only the given name and surname are spelled out, which is why Sri Lankan records are full of names like H.M. Nimal Bandara — the initials are the ge name, not middle names, and expanding them from the initials alone is impossible. Legal documents spell it in full. දොන් is the Portuguese honorific Dom, absorbed into the same slot on the low country.
