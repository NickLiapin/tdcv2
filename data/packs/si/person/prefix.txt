description: Honorifics that PRECEDE a Sinhala name. This is only half the system and the half that fits the European template — see person/suffix.txt for the other half, which follows the name and is the commoner one. පූජ්‍ය is the address for a Buddhist monk and is not optional politeness: a bhikkhu is named පූජ්‍ය + ordination name + village of origin, never Mr. ආචාර්ය is a doctorate and වෛද්‍ය a physician; Sinhala keeps them apart where English writes Dr for both.
locale: si
