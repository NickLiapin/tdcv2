TWO ENTRIES ARE STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN DECORATIVE. පාලි is the liturgical language of Theravada Buddhism: every Sri Lankan chant, precept and sutta is Pali, so it is a language millions of people recite daily without speaking. දිවෙහි (Dhivehi, of the Maldives) is Sinhala's NEAREST LIVING RELATIVE — both are Indo-Aryan islands off a Dravidian coast, and Sinhala is far closer to Dhivehi than to දෙමළ (Tamil), which it has sat beside for two thousand years without becoming related to.
