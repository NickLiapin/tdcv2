description: Garments in Sinhala. THE FIRST FIVE ARE SRI LANKAN AND HAVE NO EUROPEAN EQUIVALENT. සරම, the sarong, is what a very large share of Sri Lankan men actually wear at home and in the village — it is ordinary clothing, not costume. රෙද්ද හැට්ට is the cloth-and-jacket the older village woman wears; ඔසරිය is the Kandyan drape of the saree, cut and worn differently from the Indian සාරිය beside it; ආර්ය සිංහල ඇඳුම is the men's national dress worn to weddings and to parliament. The rest are the shirt-and-trousers vocabulary, mostly English loans in Sinhala script.
