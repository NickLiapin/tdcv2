description: Trailing element of a Sinhala place name, and the productive half of Sri Lankan toponymy — nearly every town on the island ends in one of these. -ගම and -ගමුව are village, -පුර and -නුවර city, -ගොඩ high ground, -පිටිය a flat, -වත්ත an estate, -වෙල and -කුඹුර paddy land, -හේන slash-and-burn plot, -වැව a tank, -තොට a ford or port, -මුල්ල a corner, -දෙණිය a hollow, -කන්ද a hill, -ඇල a canal, -දූව an island. Read together they are a land-use map: the names record what was there before the town was.
