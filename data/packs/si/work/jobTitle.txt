FOUR OCCUPATIONS HERE HAVE NO EUROPEAN COUNTERPART AND ARE NOT DECORATION. ත්‍රී වීලර් රියදුරු, the three-wheeler driver, is one of the largest single occupations in the country. තේ දළු නෙළන්නා and රබර් කිරි කපන්නා, the tea plucker and the rubber tapper, are the plantation workforce and are overwhelmingly women and men respectively. ආයුර්වේද වෛද්‍යවරයා is a REGISTERED medical profession in Sri Lanka with its own council and its own degree, not an alternative-therapy job title. ජ්‍යෝතිෂවේදියා, astrologer, is a paid profession consulted for marriages and business openings. Note also that Sinhala job nouns take a personal ending — -වරයා, -යා, -කරු, -න්නා — so the title names a person and not a post.
