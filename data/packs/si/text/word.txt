description: Filler words in Sinhala, for lorem-ipsum style text. THE REGISTER IS WRITTEN SINHALA (ලිඛිත සිංහල), NOT SPOKEN. Sinhala is a textbook diglossia: the written language and the spoken language differ in the verb system, not only in vocabulary. Written Sinhala conjugates the verb to agree with its subject in person, number and gender (ළමයා යයි / ළමයෝ යති) and SPOKEN SINHALA DOES NOT AGREE AT ALL — one form, යනවා, serves every subject. Nobody speaks the written form and nothing is printed in the spoken one, so a Sri Lankan reads one language and talks another, and a model trained on this text will produce prose no one says out loud. That is correct for filler in a book, a form or a news column, and wrong for dialogue or chat messages. Note that word/verb.txt holds the SPOKEN citation forms, so its verbs deliberately do not match these. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Sinhala book at all, so this list was written by hand rather than counted out of a corpus, and it is a spread across the everyday semantic fields rather than a frequency list.
