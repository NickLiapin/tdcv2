description: Filler sentences in Sinhala, one per line. THE REGISTER IS WRITTEN SINHALA (ලිඛිත සිංහල), NOT SPOKEN. Sinhala is a textbook diglossia: the written language and the spoken language differ in the verb system, not only in vocabulary. Written Sinhala conjugates the verb to agree with its subject in person, number and gender (ළමයා යයි / ළමයෝ යති) and SPOKEN SINHALA DOES NOT AGREE AT ALL — one form, යනවා, serves every subject. Nobody speaks the written form and nothing is printed in the spoken one, so a Sri Lankan reads one language and talks another, and a model trained on this text will produce prose no one says out loud. That is correct for filler in a book, a form or a news column, and wrong for dialogue or chat messages. Note that word/verb.txt holds the SPOKEN citation forms, so its verbs deliberately do not match these. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Sinhala book at all, so these were written by hand; the reader should be able to tell a handwritten filler set from a corpus-derived one, and this is the honest label for it.
