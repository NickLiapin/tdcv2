description: Units of measure in Sinhala. THE LAND UNITS ARE THE SRI LANKAN PART. පර්චස් (perch) and අක්කර (acre) are how land is bought, sold and advertised in Sri Lanka to this day — a Colombo property listing is priced per perch, one perch being about 25.3 square metres, and nobody quotes square metres. අඟල් and අඩි (inches and feet) survive alongside the metric units for the same imperial-inheritance reason.
locale: si
