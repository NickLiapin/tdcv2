description: Instruments in Sinhala. THE DRUMS ARE THE POINT AND THEY ARE REGIONAL, NOT GENERIC. ගැටබෙරය is the Kandyan drum of the up-country dance, යක්බෙරය the two-headed low-country drum of the devil-dance rituals, දවුල the processional drum of a perahera, තම්මැට්ටම the paired kettle drums played with sticks, උඩැක්කිය the hourglass hand drum, and රබාන the flat frame drum a family plays together at new year. A single word for "drum" throws all of that away. හොරණෑව is the shawm that plays with them; තාලම්පොට the small cymbals that keep time.
