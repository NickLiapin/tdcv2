description: Employee benefits in Sinhala. TWO OF THESE ARE COMPULSORY BY LAW rather than offered: අර්ථසාධක අරමුදල and භාර අරමුදල are the provident and trust funds every private-sector employer in Sri Lanka must pay into, so they appear on every payslip and are not a perk. The named funds and their contribution rates are institutions and belong to a country pack; these are the words for them.
