THE LEGAL FORMS ARE WRITTEN IN ENGLISH EVEN ON SINHALA SIGNAGE — (Pvt) Ltd and PLC are what the Registrar of Companies issues and what goes on the letterhead, so they are kept in Latin here rather than transliterated. The trade words below them (Traders, Stores, Enterprises, Agencies, Industries, Holdings) are how a great many Sri Lankan firms are actually named, and the Sinhala forms are used by cooperatives, societies and state bodies.
