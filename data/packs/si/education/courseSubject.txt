description: School subjects in Sinhala. RELIGION IS A COMPULSORY EXAMINED SUBJECT in Sri Lankan schools and every pupil sits the one matching their own faith — බුද්ධ ධර්මය, හින්දු ධර්මය, ඉස්ලාම් or ක්‍රිස්තියානි ධර්මය — which is why four of them are on this list and why a pupil record here carries a religion in its subject column whether or not the schema expected one. සිංහල and දෙමළ are both first-language subjects because both are national languages.
locale: si
