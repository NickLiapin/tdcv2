description: Qualifications in Sinhala. THE FIRST TWO ARE NOT DEGREES AND ARE THE MOST IMPORTANT ENTRIES HERE: සාමාන්‍ය පෙළ (GCE Ordinary Level, sat at about 16) and උසස් පෙළ (Advanced Level, at about 18) are the two national examinations that decide a Sri Lankan life. University places are allocated almost entirely on the A/L z-score, the entry is fiercely competitive, and "highest qualification" for a large share of the adult population is one of these two rather than any degree. A schema that starts at bachelor level cannot hold most Sri Lankan education records.
