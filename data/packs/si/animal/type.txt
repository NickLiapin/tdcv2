description: Animals in Sinhala, weighted towards what actually lives on the island. අලියා (the Sri Lankan elephant), දිවියා (the Sri Lankan leopard, the island's apex predator — there are no tigers and no lions here despite the name සිංහල meaning "lion people"), වළහා (sloth bear), ගෝනා (sambar), මී හරකා and කබරගොයා (water monitor, a normal sight in a Colombo canal) are everyday words. Note that Sinhala animal nouns carry an animate ending -ා in the citation form, which is why every one of these ends that way.
locale: si
