description: Sports in Sinhala. ක්‍රිකට් is first because it is what "sport" means in Sri Lanka in practice, but වොලිබෝල් is the sport the state declared national in 1991 — the law and the life disagree and this list keeps both at the top. THREE ARE INDIGENOUS AND EXIST NOWHERE ELSE: එල්ලේ, a bat-and-ball village game; අංගම්පොර, a Sri Lankan martial art of unarmed and bladed combat that was banned under British rule and revived in the twentieth century; and පොර පොල් ගැසීම, the ritual coconut-smashing contest of the new year season. දැල් පන්දු (netball) is the women's national game and Sri Lanka has won the Asian championship at it.
locale: si
