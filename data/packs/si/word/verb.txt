description: Common Sinhala verbs in the -නවා citation form, which is the SPOKEN register. THIS IS THE DIGLOSSIA IN ONE FILE: the written form of the same verb is different — spoken කනවා "eats" is written කයි, spoken යනවා is written යයි — and written Sinhala also makes the verb agree with its subject in person, number and gender, which spoken Sinhala does not do at all. A dictionary lists the -නවා form, so that is what is here; text/sentence.txt is in the written register and its verbs will therefore not match these forms.
locale: si
