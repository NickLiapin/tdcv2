description: Common Sinhala nouns. Note that Sinhala marks ANIMACY rather than gender: a noun is animate or inanimate and that decides its plural and its case endings, so ගස (tree) and මිනිසා (man) behave differently in a way no European grammar predicts. The citation form given here is the definite singular, which is how a Sinhala dictionary lists a noun.
locale: si
