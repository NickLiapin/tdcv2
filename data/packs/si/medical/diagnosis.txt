THE TOP OF THIS LIST IS SRI LANKAN EPIDEMIOLOGY, NOT A TRANSLATED WESTERN ONE. ඩෙංගු උණ returns in epidemic waves every year with the monsoon and fills the wards. මී උණ (leptospirosis) is an occupational disease of paddy farmers wading in flooded fields. සර්ප දෂ්ට, snakebite, is a leading cause of hospital admission — Sri Lanka has one of the highest snakebite burdens on earth. නොදන්නා හේතුවක් සහිත නිදන්ගත වකුගඩු රෝගය (CKDu) is a dry-zone farming epidemic with no agreed cause.
