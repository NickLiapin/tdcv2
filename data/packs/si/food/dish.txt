description: Dishes eaten in Sri Lanka, named in Sinhala. බත් සහ කරි — rice with several curries at once — is the meal, eaten at midday and often at night too, and everything else is around it. FOUR THINGS A NON-LOCAL LIST WOULD GET WRONG. කිරිබත්, milk rice, is not a dish but a marker: it is cooked for the first of the month, for a birthday and for the new year, so its presence in a record is a date signal. ආප්ප and ඉඳිආප්ප are breakfast and dinner, not lunch. කොත්තු රොටී is street food made by chopping roti on a griddle and is identified by the noise of it. And තෝසෙ, වඩේ and ඉඩ්ලි sit in this list without being marked foreign, because South Indian food was absorbed here centuries ago.
