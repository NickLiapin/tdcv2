description: Vegetables in Sinhala. කරවිල (bitter gourd) heads the list because bitterness is a wanted flavour here rather than a tolerated one. The leafy greens ගොටුකොළ, මුකුණුවැන්න and කංකුං are eaten raw as මල්ලුං, shredded with coconut, which is why they are named individually rather than as "greens". මඤ්ඤොක්කා (cassava), බතල (sweet potato) and කිරිඅල are starch staples, not side dishes.
locale: si
