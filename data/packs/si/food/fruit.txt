description: Fruit in Sinhala. Most of these do not exist in a European list and are ordinary here: රඹුටන් and මැංගුස්ටින් are roadside fruit in season, දිවුල් (wood apple) is drunk as a juice, බෙලි is medicinal, නෙල්ලි sour enough to be eaten with salt, and අම්බරැල්ලා and බිලිං go into curry rather than into a fruit bowl. තැඹිලි, the king coconut, is a drink sold by the roadside and is listed apart from පොල් because the two are not interchangeable.
locale: si
