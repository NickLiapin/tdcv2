description: Metal. IRIN, iron, is not a neutral material in Yoruba: it belongs to ÒGÚN, the òrìṣà of iron and of everyone whose work touches it — blacksmiths first, then hunters and soldiers, and now drivers, mechanics and surgeons, who still swear oaths on a piece of iron in a Nigerian court. Idẹ is brass and bronze, the metal of Ọ̀ṣun and of the Ifẹ̀ heads; ọjẹ is lead, fàdákà silver, wúrà gold. The rest are modern and are named as they are written in Nigeria.
