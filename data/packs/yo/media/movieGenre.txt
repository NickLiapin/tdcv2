Yoruba-language cinema is an INDUSTRY, not a niche: the Yoruba film market predates and runs alongside English-language Nollywood, descends directly from the Yoruba travelling theatre of Hubert Ogunde and Duro Ladipo, and releases straight to disc and streaming in Yoruba. Two of its genres have no Western counterpart. ÌTÀN ÌBÍLẸ̀ is the historical epic set in the era of the ọba and the babaláwo, with masquerades and incantation, and it is the flagship genre. ÌWÀSÙ is the evangelical film, made and distributed by churches, with a sermon as its plot.
