description: Loan type. The two that matter most here are not from a bank: ÈSÚSÚ (or àjọ) is the rotating savings club, where the pot comes to each member in turn and no interest is charged; and àwìn ọjà, supplier credit, is how most small traders stock a stall. ÌDÓGÓ is a pawn of land or a crop against a loan, an old and still-used Yoruba instrument.
