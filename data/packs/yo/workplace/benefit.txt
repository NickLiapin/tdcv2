description: Employee benefit. Two entries have no European counterpart. ÌRÀNLỌ́WỌ́ ÌSÌNKÚ, burial assistance, is a normal and expected part of a Nigerian employment package, because a funeral is a large family expense and an employer is expected to contribute. And ÌFÚNNI NÍ IṢẸ́ ỌWỌ́ / ÀJỌ, the workplace savings club — èsúsú or àjọ, where a group contributes monthly and each member takes the pot in turn — is how a great deal of saving actually happens, at work as elsewhere.
