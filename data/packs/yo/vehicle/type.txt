description: Vehicle body type. FOUR of these have no European equivalent and they carry most people in Yorubaland. DÀNFÓ is the yellow minibus of Lagos, MỌ́LÚẸ̀ the big square long-distance bus, Ọ̀KADÀ the motorcycle taxi, and KẸ̀KẸ́ MÁRÙN-ÚN the three-wheeler (also called kẹ̀kẹ́ NAPEP after the government scheme that introduced it). None of them is a car type: each is a whole mode of public transport with its own routes, fares and rules, and a Nigerian mobility dataset that only has "car / bus / motorcycle" has lost the distinctions that matter.
