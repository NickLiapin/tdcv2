description: Cuisine, as Yoruba names it. Yorùbá, Hausa and Ìgbò are on the list as cuisines in their own right, which is how a Nigerian talks about food — the three are as different from each other as three European national cuisines, and "Nigerian" as a separate entry means the pan-Nigerian dishes (jollof rice, suya, puff-puff) that belong to no one region. The foreign cuisines keep their international names because that is what Yoruba writing does with them.
