description: Vegetable. The Ẹ̀FỌ́ block is the heart of it: ẹ̀fọ́ is a leafy vegetable, and Yoruba names each one — tẹ̀tẹ̀ (amaranth), ṣọ̀kọ̀ (Celosia), yánrin, gbúre, ọ̀dú, ewúro (bitter leaf) — because the choice of leaf is what makes one ẹ̀fọ́ rírò different from another. EWÉDÚ (jute mallow) and ILÁ (okra) are drawn separately because they are cooked to a deliberate slipperiness that is the point of the dish, not a defect. ÌṢU, yam, is the staple and the crop with a festival of its own.
