description: Dishes of Yoruba cuisine. The staples come in pairs and the pairing is not decorative: a SWALLOW (iyán pounded yam, àmàlà from yam flour, ẹ̀bà from garri) is never eaten alone but always with a soup, and which soup goes with which swallow is a real convention — àmàlà with ewédú and gbẹ̀gìrì, iyán with ẹ̀gúsí. A config that draws a swallow and a soup independently will produce combinations no Yoruba kitchen serves.
locale: yo
