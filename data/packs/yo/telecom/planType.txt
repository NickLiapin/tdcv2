ÈTÒ ALẸ́ — the night plan — is a real and distinctively Nigerian product: a cheap block of data usable only between about midnight and five in the morning, bought by students and anyone downloading anything large. Prepaid ("owó tẹ́lẹ̀") is overwhelmingly the norm; postpaid contracts are a minority.
