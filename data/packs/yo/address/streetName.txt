description: Street names of the kind found across Yoruba towns: the virtues (Ìfẹ́, Àlàáfíà, Ìṣọ̀kan, Òmìnira, Ìtẹ̀síwájú), the natural world, the crafts, and above all the CHIEFTAINCY TITLES, which is the group a non-Yoruba reader will not expect. Aláàfin, Ọọ̀ni, Awùjalẹ̀, Olúbàdàn, Aláké, Déjì and Ọ̀ràngún are the crowned rulers of Ọ̀yọ́, Ifẹ̀, Ìjẹ̀bú, Ìbàdàn, Aké, Àkúrẹ́ and Ìlá; Balógun, Ààrẹ, Ìyálọ́de, Ọ̀tún and Òsì are graded chieftaincies. Yoruba towns name streets after these titles constantly, and the title outlives whoever holds it. Compose AFTER a streetSuffix (Títì Ìfẹ́, Ọ̀nà Balógun); the real named streets of any particular city belong to a country pack.
locale: yo
