description: Trailing qualifier of a Yoruba place name. The compass words are the ones a Nigerian will recognise immediately, because local-government areas are named this way and printed on every official form: Ìbàdàn Àríwá, Ìbàdàn Àríwá Ìlà Oòrùn, Ògbómọ̀ṣọ́ Gúúsù, Àkúrẹ́ Àríwá. Òkè / Ìsàlẹ̀ (upper / lower) and Tuntun / Àtijọ́ (new / old) split a town the same way. Ìjẹ̀bú-Òde and Ìjẹ̀bú-Igbó show the other pattern, where the qualifier says what kind of place it is rather than where it sits.
locale: yo
