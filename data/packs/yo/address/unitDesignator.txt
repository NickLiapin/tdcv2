description: Address unit / interior designator in Yoruba. Nọ́mbà is the house number, yàrá a room, àjà a storey (àjà kìíní = first floor), bílọ́ọ̀kù a block. The post-box line is the one to know about: street numbering is patchy outside city centres, so most Nigerian business addresses carry a P.O. Box or P.M.B. instead of a street address, and those two are written in English on the envelope even by Yoruba speakers.
locale: yo
