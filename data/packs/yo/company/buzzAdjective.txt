Be clear about the register: THE NIGERIAN BOARDROOM RUNS IN ENGLISH, and there is no Yoruba corporate-jargon vocabulary to translate "scalable" or "synergistic" into. What Yoruba does have is an ADVERTISING register — market-crier and radio-jingle language — and these are its words: gbéṣẹ́ (it gets the job done), tí ó lágbára (strong), tí ó péye (complete), tí ó ṣeé gbẹ́kẹ̀lé (trustworthy). Nothing here is a coinage; these are ordinary Yoruba adjectives used the way an advert uses them.
