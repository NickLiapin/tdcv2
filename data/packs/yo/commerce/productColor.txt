description: Colour of a product, as a Yoruba listing would give it. Read color/name.txt first: Yoruba is a THREE-TERM colour language — funfun, dúdú and pupa are the only basic colour words, and they cover the light band, the dark band and the warm band respectively, not white, black and red. So a product colour in Yoruba is normally one of those three, or a comparison built with àwọ̀ ("colour of") plus a thing: àwọ̀ ewé leaf-green, àwọ̀ ojú ọ̀run sky-blue, àwọ̀ ilẹ̀ earth-brown. The English loans at the end are what a young urban shopper on an e-commerce site actually types, so both registers are here; a config that treats them as thirty independent hues is over-reading the language.
locale: yo
