description: Unit of measure. The first block is what a Yoruba MARKET actually counts in, and none of it is metric: DÉRÍCÀ is the milk tin used as a grain measure, MÚDÙ the larger bowl (about four dérícà), ÒSÙWỌ̀N a measure generally, ÌDÌ a bundle, ÒGÒ a heap laid out on the stall. Grain, garri, beans, pepper and tomatoes are sold by these, not by the kilogram, and a Yoruba shopping list would use them. The metric units follow, because that is what a packaged product carries.
