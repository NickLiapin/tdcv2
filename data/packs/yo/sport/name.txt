description: Sport. BỌ́Ọ̀LÙ AFẸSẸ̀GBÁ is football, and the word is a real Yoruba coinage — "the ball struck with the foot" — used by Yoruba broadcasters rather than a borrowing. ÌJÀKADÌ (wrestling) and GÍDÍGBÓ are the indigenous combat sports, contested at festivals to drumming, with the drummer as much a part of the match as the referee. AYÒ ỌLỌ́PỌ́N is the sown-seed board game played across West Africa, and ỌKỌ̀TỌ́ the spinning-top game children play. The rest are borrowed sports under borrowed names, which is honest: Nigeria plays them, Yoruba has not renamed them.
locale: yo
