description: Kind of official document. ÌWÉ Ẹ̀RÍ ÌBÍLẸ̀ — the certificate of indigeneity — has no European counterpart and is worth understanding: it certifies which local government area you are INDIGENOUS to, is issued by that local government, and is demanded for school places, scholarships and public jobs. It follows descent, not residence, so a person born and raised in Lagos may hold one for a town they have never lived in.
