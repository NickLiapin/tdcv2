description: Symptom or complaint, as a patient reports it. Yoruba describes a symptom with the body part plus what it is doing, and the verb is often reduplicated: orí fífọ́ "head splitting", inú rírun "belly hurting", ara gbígbóná "body hot". A Yoruba patient names the PART first, which is the opposite order from English.
locale: yo
