description: Genetic ancestry group, used as the parent of diagnosisByAncestry. These are ancestry populations as clinical genetics defines them — GEOGRAPHIC DESCENT, not social race categories. Yoruba names a group of people with ỌMỌ ("child of") plus the place, the same construction it uses for nationality, so the labels read as "child of sub-Saharan Africa", "child of the Pacific islands". Yoruba speakers themselves belong to the first group, which for a Yoruba dataset makes it the ordinary case rather than a minority one.
locale: yo
