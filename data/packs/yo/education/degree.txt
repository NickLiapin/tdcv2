description: Academic qualification. Nigerian degrees are awarded and written in ENGLISH, so those entries keep their English abbreviations — that is what appears on the certificate. The Yoruba entries name the levels of schooling themselves, which is what people say: ilé ẹ̀kọ́ alákọ̀ọ́bẹ̀rẹ̀ is primary school, girama secondary, and the two Nigerian sub-degree awards, the OND and HND from a polytechnic, have no European equivalent and are a distinct track, not a lesser degree.
locale: yo
