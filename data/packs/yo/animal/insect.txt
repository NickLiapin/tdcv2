description: Insect and other small crawling creature — Yoruba groups them all as KÒKÒRÒ, which covers insects, spiders, worms and germs alike, so "kòkòrò àrùn" is a disease germ. Ikán the termite eats a house from the inside and is a stock Yoruba image for hidden ruin; oyin the honeybee gives the word for honey and for sweetness; àáyán the cockroach and ẹ̀fọn the mosquito need no introduction in a wet-season Lagos kitchen.
locale: yo
