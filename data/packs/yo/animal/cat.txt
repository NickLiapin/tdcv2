description: Cat breed. As with dogs, Yoruba has one word — ológbò (also ológìní) — and no breed vocabulary; pedigree cats are rare in Nigeria and the household cat is a landrace. The first entry is the honest Yoruba answer; the rest are international breed names, not translated, because there is nothing to translate them into.
