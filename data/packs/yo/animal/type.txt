description: Animal. Several of these are characters before they are species: ÌJÀPÁ the tortoise is the trickster of Yoruba folktale and turns up in more àlọ́ than any other creature, ẸKÙN the leopard and KÌNNÌÚN the lion are praise-words applied to people, and AJÁ, ÀGÙNTÀN, EWÚRẸ́ and ÀKÙKỌ are the sacrificial animals, each òrìṣà taking its own. Yoruba marks the sex of an animal with akọ (male) and abo (female) in front of the noun, not by a separate word.
