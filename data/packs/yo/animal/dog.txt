description: Dog breed. Yoruba has ONE word for a dog, ajá, and no native breed vocabulary at all, because dogs were not bred to type here — the ordinary Nigerian dog is a landrace and is called simply ajá ìbílẹ̀, "the local dog". Everything below that first entry is an imported breed under its international name, which is what a Nigerian breeder, vet or newspaper advert writes; Boerboel, Caucasian Shepherd and Bullmastiff are the ones actually common in Nigeria, kept as guard dogs rather than pets. Nothing here has been invented in Yoruba to fill the list out.
locale: yo
