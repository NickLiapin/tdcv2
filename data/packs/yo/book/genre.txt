description: Book genre. ÌTÀN ÀRÒSỌ ÌJÌNLẸ̀ is where the Yoruba novel actually lives: D. O. Fágúnwà's books, and Amos Tutuola's after them, are hunter's-journey tales through a spirit forest, a genre with no English name that Yoruba readers know instantly. ÀLỌ́ is the folktale, ÒWE the proverb collection, ORÍKÌ praise-poetry — all three are printed as books and taught in school. Ìwé mímọ́ is scripture.
