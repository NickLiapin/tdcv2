Amounts are in Náírà (₦), the Nigerian currency, and the bands are set at Nigerian levels — the national minimum wage is a monthly figure in the tens of thousands of naira, so a European band structure converted at the exchange rate would put every household in the bottom bucket. Yoruba names the currency Náírà and its hundredth Kọ́bọ̀.
