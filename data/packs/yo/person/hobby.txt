description: Hobby or pastime, in Yoruba. Yoruba names an activity with a VERBAL NOUN built by reduplicating the verb's first consonant with an i: kà (read) gives kíkà, kọ (write) kíkọ, gbá (kick) gbígbá — so "reading" is ìwé kíkà, "book reading", object first. Ayò ọlọ́pọ́n is the sown-seed board game played across the whole region; àlọ́ pípa is telling the folktales that begin "àlọ́ o!" and expect the listeners to answer; oríkì is reciting a person's or lineage's praise-poetry from memory, which is a real and prized skill, not a curiosity.
locale: yo
