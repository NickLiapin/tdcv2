description: Common Yoruba descriptive word. Most of these are VERBS, not adjectives: "ó dára" is literally "it is-good", with no copula anywhere, and the true attributive adjectives (ńlá, kékeré, tuntun, rere) are a small closed class that follows its noun — ilé ńlá, "house big". So a config that puts one of these in front of a noun the English way produces something ungrammatical.
locale: yo
