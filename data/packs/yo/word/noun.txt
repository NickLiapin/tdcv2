description: Common Yoruba noun. Two structural things a config should know. Yoruba nouns have NO plural marker — ọmọ is child or children, and number comes from àwọn in front or from the context, never from the noun — and NO grammatical gender or noun class. And a great many nouns are built from a verb with a prefix (ì-, à-, ìrìn from rìn "to walk"), so the noun and verb lists here overlap in root more than an English pair would.
