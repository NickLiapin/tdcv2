description: Common Yoruba verb, given in its bare form. Yoruba verbs DO NOT INFLECT — not for person, not for number, not for tense: lọ is go, went and will go alike, and tense comes from a separate particle in front (ó ti lọ, ó ń lọ, ó máa lọ). So a config can join a subject to any verb in this list without agreement, which is not true of any European locale.
