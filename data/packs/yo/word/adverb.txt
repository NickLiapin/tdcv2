description: Common Yoruba adverb. The distinctive group is the IDEOPHONES — kíákíá, pátápátá, wéréwéré, gbágbáá, rọra — reduplicated words that describe the manner of an action directly rather than by derivation, and which Yoruba has hundreds of. There is no -ly suffix to derive an adverb from an adjective; the manner word is its own lexical item, and it goes after the verb.
locale: yo
