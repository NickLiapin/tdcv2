description: Technology noun. Yoruba HAS coined for this domain and the coinages are in daily use on BBC Yorùbá and in Yoruba broadcasting: Ẹ̀RỌ AYÁRABÍÀṢÁ is the computer, "the machine that is as fast as magic"; ÀYÉLUJÁRA is the internet, "that which pierces through the world"; ẹ̀rọ amóhùnmáwòrán is the television, "the machine that captures sound and picture"; ẹ̀rọ agbóhùnsáfẹ́fẹ́ the radio. These are real words, not this file's inventions. Where no coinage exists the English term is borrowed into Yoruba phonology, which is also what speakers do.
