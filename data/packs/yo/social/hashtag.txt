description: Hashtag word, in Yoruba. ỌWÀMBẸ̀ is the one to know: the Yoruba society party — a wedding, a naming, a chieftaincy, a funeral — with hired canopies in the street, live Fújì or Jùjú, aṣọ ẹbí (the family cloth everyone wears to match), and money sprayed over the dancers. It is a whole social institution in one word, and it is a live hashtag. AṢỌ ẸBÍ, GÈLÈ and AṢỌ ÒKÈ come with it. ỌMỌLÚÀBÍ is the Yoruba ideal of a well-brought-up person and is used as a hashtag the way a value is.
