description: Dog breed (Catalan). Four of these are native breeds of the Catalan-speaking lands and exist under no other name: the gos d'atura catala (Catalan sheepdog), the ca de bestiar and ca de bou of Mallorca, and the ca eivissenc or podenc eivissenc of Ibiza — the last is what the rest of the world calls the Ibizan hound. The gos d'aigua espanyol is the Catalan water dog of the Ebre delta.
