description: Music genre (Catalan). Five of these are genres of the Catalan lands and are not translations: havaneres, the sailors' songs brought back from Cuba and still sung with burnt rum on the Costa Brava; sardana, the cobla music of the circle dance; rumba catalana, the Barcelona Roma guitar style of Peret and Gato Perez; nova canco, the singer-songwriter movement that carried the language through the Franco years; and cant d'estil, the improvised sung verse of the Valencian country.
