description: Weather phenomenon (Catalan). Most of this list is untranslatable and locally specific: tramuntana is the dry north wind that scours the Emporda and the Roussillon, mestral the north-west wind, garbi and marinada the sea breezes, llevantada the destructive easterly storm off the Mediterranean, and gota freda the autumn cut-off low that produces the flash floods of the Valencian country. Torb is the Pyrenean ground-blizzard, the word Andorra and the Cerdanya use for wind-driven snow. Arc de Sant Marti is a rainbow, literally Saint Martin's arc. Cap de fiblo is a waterspout or small tornado.
