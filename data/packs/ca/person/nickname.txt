description: Catalan hypocoristic — the short form a person is actually called by. These are formed differently from the Castilian ones and that difference is the point: Catalan clips the END of the name and keeps the tail (Eulalia > Laia, Gabriel > Biel, Bartomeu > Tomeu, Francesc > Cesc, Meritxell > Txell, Concepcio > Cio, Elisabet > Bet, Manel > Nel, Sebastia > Tia, Jacint > Cinto), where Castilian keeps the head or rebuilds the whole word (Francisco > Paco, Jose > Pepe, Concepcion > Concha). The other Catalan device is the diminutive in -et / -eta (Pepet, Jaumet, Marieta, Tereseta, Carmeta), which is productive on any name. Some of these have become full registered names in their own right: Laia, Biel, Quim, Manel and Toni are all on birth certificates now.
locale: ca
