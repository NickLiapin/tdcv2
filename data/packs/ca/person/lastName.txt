description: Catalan surnames. ONE list for both sexes: Catalan surnames do NOT inflect for gender — Montserrat Ferrer and her brother Jordi Ferrer carry the identical form — so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack, unlike pl, cs or ru. That absence is the design. A Catalan carries TWO surnames, paternal then maternal, as in Spain and Portugal, so a full name draws from this list TWICE; in the formal Catalan style the two are joined by the connector "i" (Jordi Pujol i Soley, Josep Tarradellas i Joan, Antoni Gaudi i Cornet), which the Spanish civil registry omits, so both written forms are current — see person/male/fullName and person/female/fullName. The stock is overwhelmingly toponymic: Puig is a hill, Serra a ridge, Vila a town, Riera a stream, Font a spring, Coll a pass, Roca a rock, Bosch a wood, Camps fields, Prat a meadow, Mas a farmstead, Soler a homestead, Pi a pine — a Catalan surname usually names the piece of land the family held, where a Castilian one usually names the father (-ez: Perez, Gonzalez, Rodriguez). There is no -ez patronymic layer here at all, and that is the single sharpest test separating this list from the es pack. The first thirty or so are in the real order of frequency; past that the order carries NO frequency claim, and this list is deliberately NOT weighted because no published count of Catalan surnames by bearer exists to weight it with — the Spanish and French registries count surnames by state, not by language, so any curve here would be an invention. Catalan surnames are in any case genuinely spread, with nothing like the concentration of Vietnamese Nguyen or Korean Kim.
locale: ca
