description: Title or article placed before a Catalan name. The first four are the PERSONAL ARTICLE, a feature Catalan has and Castilian does not: everyday Catalan says en Jordi, la Maria, n'Anna before a given name, and the Balearic Islands use the salat forms es Tomeu, sa Catalina. It is part of ordinary speech, not a courtesy title. The honorifics Excel-lentissim and Il-lustrissim carry the interpunct (U+00B7). Mn. is Mossen, the address for a secular priest, and is how Jacint Verdaguer is still universally named (Mossen Cinto).
locale: ca
