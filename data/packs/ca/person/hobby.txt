description: Pastime (Catalan). Several of these exist only in the Catalan lands and are not translations of anything: castells are the human towers of the colles castelleres, sardanes the circle dance, correfoc and diables the fire-running, gegants the giant processional figures, havaneres the sung sea-shanties of the Costa Brava, caramelles the Easter door-to-door singing, and botifarra and truc are the two card games actually played in a Catalan bar. Buscar bolets — going out for wild mushrooms in autumn — is a national pastime with its own vocabulary. Col-leccionisme carries the interpunct.
