description: School mark (Catalan). Two scales are in force at once and both are here: primary and secondary report the four assoliment bands (NA no assoliment, AS satisfactori, AN notable, AE excel-lent), while the numeric 0-10 scale with its named bands — suspens, aprovat, be, notable, excel-lent, matricula d'honor — is what a batxillerat and a university transcript carry. Excel-lent carries the interpunct (U+00B7).
