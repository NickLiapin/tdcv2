description: Job title (Catalan). Where a trade has a distinct Catalan word it is used rather than a borrowing: paleta is a bricklayer, lampista a plumber, manya and serraller a locksmith, guixaire a plasterer, xarcuter a delicatessen butcher, llevadora a midwife. Il-lustrador and instal-lador carry the interpunct (U+00B7). Titles are given in the masculine citation form except where the feminine is the ordinary one; Catalan inflects almost every job title for gender (cambrer/cambrera, mestre/mestra, dependent/dependenta, infermer/infermera, advocat/advocada) and a config building realistic records should pick the form that agrees with the person.
locale: ca
