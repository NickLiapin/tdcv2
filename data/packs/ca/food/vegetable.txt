description: Vegetable (Catalan). Calcot is the winter spring-onion grown by earthing up a second-year onion, eaten grilled over vine cuttings at a calcotada, and the word exists in no other language; rovello is the saffron milk cap that Catalonia goes to the woods for every autumn. Enciam is lettuce, bledes chard, carbasso courgette, alberginia aubergine, mongeta tendra green bean — none of these is the Castilian word with an accent moved.
