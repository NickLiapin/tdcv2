description: The NAME half of a Catalan street, to be composed after a streetSuffix — Catalan puts the thoroughfare word first (carrer Major, avinguda del Mar, placa de la Font), the opposite of English. Three stocks are mixed, as the real street map mixes them: common nouns with the article (de la Font, del Mar, dels Til-lers), saints and Marian names, and pan-Catalan cultural figures who have a street in every town from Perpinya to Alacant rather than in one city only. City-specific streets — Diagonal, Rambla de Catalunya, Gran Via — are NOT here: they belong to a country pack.
locale: ca
