description: Leading element of a Catalan place name (Sant Cugat, Vilanova, Castellar, la Pobla, Puig-reig, Sa Pobla). Catalan place names keep the definite article and Catalan keeps it LOWERCASE inside a sentence — l'Hospitalet, la Seu, el Prat, els Hostalets — which is why the articles are listed in lower case here. Sa, ses and es are the salat article of the Balearic Islands and of part of the Costa Brava, a form that exists nowhere in Castilian. The element alone does not name a real place; combine it with a toponym.
locale: ca
