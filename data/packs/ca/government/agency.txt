GENERIC vocabulary spanning four states, not one country's organisation chart: an ajuntament is a town council everywhere, a consell comarcal the county-level body of Catalonia, a diputacio the provincial one, and a conselleria a ministry of the Valencian and Balearic governments. Sindicatura de greuges is the ombudsman. The individually NAMED bodies — the Generalitat, the Consell General d'Andorra, the Govern balear, a prefecture in Northern Catalonia — belong in a country pack.
