description: Hindi honorific or form of address, placed before a name. श्री / श्रीमती / सुश्री are the everyday equivalents of Mr / Mrs / Ms, and कुमारी marks an unmarried woman. The religious and caste titles (पंडित, स्वामी, महंत, मौलाना, हाजी, सरदार, ठाकुर) are in ordinary administrative use in India, not archaisms.
