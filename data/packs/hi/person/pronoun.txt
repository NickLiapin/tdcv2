description: Personal pronoun (Hindi). Note that Hindi third-person pronouns are NOT gendered — वह covers he, she and it alike, and it is the VERB, not the pronoun, that agrees with the subject's gender. There is therefore no he/she pair to draw from here, unlike the European locales. The pairs below are the singular/plural and near/far distinctions Hindi actually makes, plus the honorific plural आप used for a single respected person.
