Two things a config cannot infer. First, the Punjabi names ending in -प्रीत and -दीप (गुरप्रीत, मनप्रीत, जसप्रीत, संदीप, कुलदीप, मनदीप) are genuinely UNISEX, so they appear in the female list as well — that overlap is intentional. Second, कुमार and सिंह are not given names: they sit between the given name and the surname (राजेश कुमार शर्मा) and live in person/lastName.txt, so do not compose them from here. Names are drawn on a gentle frequency curve; Indian given names are far less concentrated than surnames.
