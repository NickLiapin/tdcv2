description: Hindi hypocoristic or pet name — the घर का नाम, the name a family actually uses at home. Unlike a European diminutive these are usually NOT derived from the given name at all (a Rajesh may be गोलू to his family all his life), so a config must not try to compute one from person/male/firstName.txt. Several are gendered pairs — मुन्ना/मुन्नी, बबलू/बबली, गुड्डू/गुड्डी, बंटी/बबली.
locale: hi
