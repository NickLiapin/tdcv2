description: Indian surnames written in Devanagari, roughly frequency-ordered and covering the real community spread of Indian data — Brahmin, Kayastha, Bania/Jain, Rajput, agrarian OBC, Punjabi and Sikh, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Odia, Assamese, the four southern states, Muslim, Christian, Parsi, Scheduled Caste and Adivasi names. Indian surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this one list serves male and female rows alike and there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt — its absence is the design, not an oversight. Two cautions a config cannot infer. First, कुमार and सिंह very often occupy the MIDDLE slot of a full name rather than the family slot (राजेश कुमार शर्मा), and across the Hindi belt they are also millions of people's entire surname of record; a full-name template that always joins first + last will therefore be right, but a template that assumes three parts must not treat them as given names. Second, the strongly sex-marked surname-slot words — देवी and कुमारी, and कौर in the Sikh convention — are deliberately EXCLUDED, because a shared list containing देवी would hand that name to male rows; a config that wants them must supply them per gender itself. सिंह IS kept, because outside that Sikh convention it is an ordinary inherited family name borne by men and women alike across North India. The weights are a realistic long tail rather than a sharp concentration: India has tens of thousands of surnames and no single one approaches the share Nguyen holds in Vietnam.
