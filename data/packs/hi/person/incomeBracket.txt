description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Hindi. Amounts are in INR (₹), the currency of India. The bands are written in the INDIAN NUMBERING SYSTEM, which groups by लाख (1 lakh = 100,000) and करोड़ (1 crore = 10,000,000 = 100 lakh) rather than by thousands and millions — an Indian salary is quoted as ₹12 लाख, never as ₹1.2 million. A config that formats a raw number for an Indian reader must also use the 2,2,3 digit grouping (₹12,00,000), not the Western 3,3,3.
