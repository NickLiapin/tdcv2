description: Hindi thoroughfare type. Unlike the European locales this element goes AFTER the name, not before it — महात्मा गांधी मार्ग, स्टेशन रोड, चांदनी चौक, नेहरू नगर — which is the reverse of Polish ulica Długa. Note also that Indian addresses locate a building by area rather than by street as often as not, which is why नगर, कॉलोनी, विहार, सेक्टर and मोहल्ला (settlement and neighbourhood units, not roads) belong in the same list as मार्ग and रोड.
