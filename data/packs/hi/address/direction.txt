description: Directional qualifier used with an Indian locality, sector or colony name (दक्षिण दिल्ली, पश्चिम विहार). The last four are the intercardinal directions of वास्तु, the Indian architectural tradition — ईशान is north-east, आग्नेय south-east, नैऋत्य south-west and वायव्य north-west. They are not archaic; Indian property listings advertise a plot's वास्तु orientation as a selling point.
