description: Spice (Hindi). This is the working shelf of an Indian kitchen rather than a list of exotic flavourings — हल्दी, जीरा and धनिया go into almost every savoury dish made in the country. गरम मसाला is a blend, not a single spice, and its composition differs by region and by household. अमचूर is dried green-mango powder and हींग is asafoetida; neither has a Western equivalent.
