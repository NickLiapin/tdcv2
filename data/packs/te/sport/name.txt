description: Sport (Telugu). The traditional games are not decoration: కబడ్డీ, ఖోఖో and కుస్తీ are played competitively at state level, and కోడిపందేలు (cockfighting) and ఎద్దుల పందేలు are Sankranti practices in coastal Andhra that are technically illegal and universally held anyway. బ్యాడ్మింటన్ and షూటింగ్ are where the Telugu states actually win internationally.
