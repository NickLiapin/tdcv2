description: Trip type (Telugu). తీర్థయాత్ర (pilgrimage) is not a niche category here: Tirumala alone receives tens of millions of visitors a year and pilgrimage is the single commonest reason a Telugu family travels, ahead of holidays. పుణ్యక్షేత్ర దర్శనం and పండుగ ప్రయాణం (going home for a festival, which empties the cities at Sankranti) are likewise ordinary trip types that a European list has no reason to carry.
locale: te
