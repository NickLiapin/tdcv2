description: Employment or contract type (Telugu). Two Indian categories have no European counterpart and are in constant use here: ఔట్‌సోర్సింగ్ describes a worker employed through an agency but working full time in a government office, and దినసరి కూలీ is the daily-wage worker paid by the day with no contract at all — together they are a very large share of the real labour market in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
