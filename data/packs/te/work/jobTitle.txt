description: Telugu job titles across sectors — agriculture, aquaculture, technology, healthcare, government, the trades, transport, business and services. Three things a config cannot infer. First, government titles are disproportionately represented on purpose, because a government post is a far larger share of the aspirational job market here than in a European locale and the titles are precise, ranked and used in ordinary conversation (వి.ఆర్.ఓ., ఎం.ఆర్.ఓ., తహసీల్దార్, ఆర్.డి.ఓ., కలెక్టర్ are steps on one ladder). Second, ఆశా కార్యకర్త and అంగన్‌వాడీ కార్యకర్త are real, paid, overwhelmingly female frontline health and childcare posts under national schemes and are among the commonest job titles a rural Telugu woman holds. Third, several trade titles are the ordinary Telugu words rather than English loans — మేస్త్రి is the mason-foreman, రజకుడు the washerman, కుమ్మరి the potter — and they name occupations that in this society still correlate with community, which a config generating social data should be aware of.
