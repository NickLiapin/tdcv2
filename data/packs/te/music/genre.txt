description: Music genre (Telugu). కర్ణాటక సంగీతం leads for a reason worth stating: Carnatic music is South India's classical tradition, and TELUGU is the language most of its repertoire is actually sung in — Tyagaraja, Annamacharya and Ramadasu composed in Telugu, and Tamil and Kannada singers learn Telugu texts to perform them. The folk forms are living performance traditions with named practitioners, not archive categories: బుర్రకథ and ఒగ్గు కథ are narrative ballad forms, హరికథ is sung religious storytelling, and ధూం ధాం is the political song-and-dance form that carried the Telangana statehood movement through the 2000s.
