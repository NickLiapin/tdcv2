description: Field of study or major (Telugu). The Intermediate stream names (ఎం.పి.సి., బై.పి.సి., సి.ఇ.సి., ఎం.ఇ.సి.) are a genuinely Telugu-states institution: a student picks one at sixteen and it determines every course open to them afterwards, so it functions as a field of study long before university does.
