description: Marital status (Telugu). Several of these inflect for the subject's gender and the list carries both forms where they differ — వితంతువు is a widow and భార్యను కోల్పోయినవాడు a widower, అవివాహితుడు is masculine and అవివాహిత feminine. A config that already knows the row's gender should filter rather than draw blind. నిశ్చితార్థమైన (formally betrothed) is a status that carries real social and legal weight here and is not the same as a Western "engaged".
