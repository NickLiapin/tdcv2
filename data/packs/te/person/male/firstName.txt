description: Telugu male given name, roughly frequency-ordered. REMEMBER THE ORDER: this is the SECOND element of a Telugu name, not the first — the ఇంటిపేరు (person/lastName.txt) precedes it. Three things a config cannot infer. First, the long Sanskritic compounds (వెంకటేశ్వరరావు, సూర్యనారాయణ, లక్ష్మీనారాయణ, హనుమంతరావు) belong to the older generations and the -రావు / -య్య endings mark them as such; the short modern names (రవి, మహేష్, తేజ, చరణ్) belong to people born after roughly 1980, so a config generating a birth date alongside a name should be aware that the pairing is not random in real data. Second, వెంకట, సాయి, శ్రీ and లక్ష్మీ act as first halves of compound given names as much as names in their own right, which is why a real Telugu given-name field often holds two words. Third, the Muslim names here (అబ్దుల్, ఖాదర్, మస్తాన్, మహబూబ్) and the Christian ones (ప్రభుదాస్, ఆశీర్వాదం, జ్ఞానప్రకాశం) are Telugu-speaking people's names written in Telugu script, not foreign imports — Andhra Pradesh and Telangana have large Telugu-speaking Muslim and Christian populations and a pack without them is not describing the states.
