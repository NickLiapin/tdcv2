description: Telugu female given name, roughly frequency-ordered. As with the male list this is the SECOND element of the name — the ఇంటిపేరు comes first (అక్కినేని సావిత్రి, ఘంటసాల సావిత్రి). Two facts a config cannot infer. First, the -అమ్మ ending (సుబ్బమ్మ, పార్వతమ్మ, లక్ష్మమ్మ, ఈశ్వరమ్మ, నర్సమ్మ) is a genuinely Telugu female-name marker and is strongly generational: it is ordinary among women born before about 1970 and almost absent below that age, so a config pairing a name with a birth year should not draw it flat. -అమ్మ is also a respect particle in its own right — పార్వతమ్మ is పార్వతి with respect attached — which is why the same woman may appear as both in different records. Second, this list deliberately does NOT double as a surname list: Telugu family names do not inflect for gender, there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack, and its absence is the design, not an oversight — a female row draws its family name from person/lastName.txt exactly as a male row does.
locale: te
