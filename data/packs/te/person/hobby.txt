description: Hobby or pastime (Telugu). The list is Telugu rather than translated: కర్ణాటక సంగీతం (Carnatic music, whose composers Tyagaraja and Annamayya wrote in Telugu, which is why Telugu is the language most Carnatic songs are actually sung in), కూచిపూడి (the classical dance form of a village of that name in Krishna district), అవధానం (a public feat of literary improvisation that is a genuinely Telugu institution and has no counterpart elsewhere), ముగ్గు (the rice-flour floor drawing done at dawn, at its most elaborate during సంక్రాంతి), and కోడిపందేలు (cockfighting, illegal but a very live Sankranti practice in coastal Andhra) are all specific to this culture. Cricket and film-watching lead because they honestly do.
locale: te
