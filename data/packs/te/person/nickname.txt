description: Telugu pet name — the ముద్దుపేరు, the name a family actually uses at home. Like the Hindi house-name equivalent it is usually NOT derived from the given name at all: a మహేష్ may be బుజ్జి to his family for sixty years, so a config must not try to compute one from person/male/firstName.txt. Several are affectionate common nouns rather than names — బంగారం is "gold", ముద్దు is "a kiss", పండు is "fruit" — and several are gendered pairs, బుజ్జి/బుజ్జమ్మ, చిన్నా/చిన్ని, బాబు/పాప. The cinema nicknames at the end (బన్నీ, చెర్రీ, తారక్, చిరు, బాలయ్య, వెంకీ) are real public bynames of Telugu film actors and are used in print, not invented here.
locale: te
