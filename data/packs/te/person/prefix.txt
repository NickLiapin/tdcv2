description: Telugu honorific or form of address placed BEFORE a name — and since the family name already comes first, these stand at the very head of the line (శ్రీ నందమూరి తారక రామారావు). చిరంజీవి and సౌభాగ్యవతి have no European counterpart: they are the address forms used to a younger man and to a married younger woman in a letter or on a wedding invitation, and they are still live usage rather than archaism. బ్రహ్మశ్రీ and వేదమూర్తి address a Vedic scholar; జనాబ్, హాజీ and మౌలానా are the Muslim forms in ordinary Telugu use. Note that the everyday Telugu marker of respect is not a prefix at all but the suffix గారు — see person/suffix.txt.
locale: te
