description: Garment type (Telugu). The list is what a Telugu wardrobe actually contains, not a translated Western one. చీర is the six-yard sari and లంగా ఓణి the half-sari a girl wears between childhood and marriage — a distinct garment with its own coming-of-age ceremony. పంచె is the men's unstitched lower cloth, worn కట్టు (tucked) for temple and formal occasions. The wedding-specific items (పట్టు చీర, లాంఛనాలు) are separate goods, and the modern Western garments at the end are equally real in urban wardrobes.
