The four intercardinals — ఈశాన్యం, ఆగ్నేయం, నైరుతి, వాయవ్యం — are the Sanskrit corner names, and they are not merely compass points here: they are the corners of వాస్తు, so a Telugu property listing states which corner a plot faces and an east-facing (తూర్పు ముఖం) plot commands a real price premium over a south-facing one. A config generating property data for this locale that treats the four directions as interchangeable is throwing away the single most-quoted attribute of a Telugu land advertisement.
