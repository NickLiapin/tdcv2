description: Leading element of a Telugu place name (పెద కాకాని, కొత్తపేట, ఉత్తర రాజుపాలెం). Three strands are visible in the list and they map onto history: the pure Telugu qualifiers (పెద big, చిన small, కొత్త new, పాత old, ఎర్ర red, నల్ల black, తెల్ల white) are the oldest layer; the Sanskritic and devotional ones (శ్రీ, వెంకట, రామ, కృష్ణ, భద్ర) come with temple settlement; and the Persian and Arabic ones (ఇబ్రహీం, హైదర్, మహబూబ్, నిజాం, జహీర్) are the Qutb Shahi and Asaf Jahi layer and mark a TELANGANA toponym far more often than a coastal Andhra one. Combine with a real toponym and a citySuffix; on its own this does not name a real place, and a generated combination must never be presented as one.
locale: te
