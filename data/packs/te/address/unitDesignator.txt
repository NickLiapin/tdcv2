description: Secondary address unit designator (Telugu). Two facts a config cannot infer. First, the door number LEADS a Telugu address rather than trailing it: an envelope starts డోర్ నం. 8-3-214/A and the street, colony and town follow, so a config that appends the unit in the European position produces something no Telugu postman would read comfortably. Second, those door numbers are genuinely hierarchical in the older Hyderabad system — 8-3-214/A means ward, block, plot and sub-plot — which is why slashes and multiple hyphens are normal and not a formatting error. పోర్షన్ and వాటా again have no Western equivalent: they identify one separately-let part of an ordinary house.
locale: te
