description: Building type (Telugu). Two entries have no European counterpart and are the commonest rental units in Hyderabad, Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam: a పోర్షన్ (also called a వాటా) is one floor or one wing of an ordinary family house let separately, with its own door number, and it is what most urban Telugu tenants actually rent — not a purpose-built flat. పెంకుటిల్లు (tiled roof), డాబా ఇల్లు (flat concrete roof) and రేకుల షెడ్డు (corrugated sheet) name a house by its ROOF, which is how rural Telugu classifies housing and how the census counted it for decades.
