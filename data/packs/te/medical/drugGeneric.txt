description: Common generic drug name (Telugu script). Indian prescribing is generic-name based by regulation and the National List of Essential Medicines is what a government hospital dispenses, so these are the names that actually appear on a Telugu prescription slip — written, in practice, in Latin script even on an otherwise Telugu form, which is worth knowing before rendering them. పారాసిటమాల్ (not "acetaminophen"), ఒ.ఆర్.ఎస్. and యాంటీ-స్నేక్ వెనమ్ reflect Indian rather than American usage.
locale: te
