description: Genetic ancestry group in Telugu, used as the parent of diagnosisByAncestry. These are ancestry populations as clinical genetics defines them — geographic descent, not social race categories. దక్షిణ ఆసియా is the group that describes Telugu speakers themselves, and it is not a small consideration here: South Asian populations carry a markedly higher risk of type 2 diabetes and of coronary artery disease at a lower body-mass index than European ones, which is why a Telugu clinical dataset that applies European BMI thresholds will under-count both.
locale: te
