description: Official document type named in Telugu. Telugu is spoken in essentially one country, so these are the documents an INDIAN citizen holds. Two things a config cannot infer. First, India has no single identity document: ఆధార్ is the biometric number, పాన్ the tax number, ఓటరు గుర్తింపు కార్డు the electoral ID and రేషన్ కార్డు the household food-subsidy card, and a person routinely produces two or three of them together because none alone proves everything. Second, కుల ధ్రువీకరణ పత్రం, ఆదాయ ధ్రువీకరణ పత్రం and నివాస ధ్రువీకరణ పత్రం — caste, income and residence certificates issued by the tahsildar — are among the most-requested documents in the Telugu states because admissions, scholarships and reservations depend on them, and they have no European counterpart at all. The India COUNTRY pack holds the number formats; this list holds only the names.
