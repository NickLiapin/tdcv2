Telugu is spoken in essentially one country, so these are INDIA's institutions, and every entry is a real, currently existing body. Note the two-level structure a Telugu form assumes: a citizen deals with a కేంద్ర (Union) body and a రాష్ట్ర (state) one for the same matter, and the state entries here are the generic office names (రెవెన్యూ శాఖ, రవాణా శాఖ, గ్రామ సచివాలయం) rather than either state's specific department, because Telugu is the official language of two separate states. The India COUNTRY pack holds the identifier formats these bodies issue; this list holds only their names.
