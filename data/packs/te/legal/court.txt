description: Court or tribunal in the Indian judicial hierarchy, named in Telugu. Telugu is spoken in essentially one country, so this is India's judiciary — but note that it is TWO high courts, not one: Andhra Pradesh and Telangana were a single state until 2014 and shared a High Court until 2019, and they now have separate ones, so a config generating a court for a Telugu row cannot assume a single jurisdiction. లోక్ అదాలత్ (a settlement forum whose award is binding as a civil decree) and గ్రామ న్యాయాలయం (a mobile village court) are statutory Indian institutions with no European counterpart.
