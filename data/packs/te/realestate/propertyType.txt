description: Property type (Telugu). Two categories dominate Telugu property advertising and have no clean European equivalent: ఓపెన్ ప్లాట్ is a bare residential plot bought as a long-term investment and is probably the single most-traded property class in the Telugu states, and వ్యవసాయ భూమి is quoted and sold in ఎకరాలు and సెంట్లు rather than square metres. వెంచర్ names a newly laid-out plotted development, which is how most peri-urban land here reaches the market.
