description: Event type (Telugu). The life-cycle ceremonies at the head are the events a Telugu family actually organises and each is a distinct occasion with its own guest list: బారసాల is the eleventh-day naming, అన్నప్రాశన the first solid food, అక్షరాభ్యాసం the first writing lesson, ఉపనయనం the thread ceremony, and పెళ్లిచూపులు the formal meeting that precedes a match. The festival entries are here as event types because a Telugu venue or catering dataset is dominated by them: ఉగాది opens the Telugu year, సంక్రాంతి is the three-day harvest festival that empties the cities of coastal Andhra as everyone goes home, and బతుకమ్మ is the nine-day flower festival of Telangana, danced by women around a stacked floral arrangement, which is the state's own festival and has no equivalent in Andhra.
