description: Horse breed (Telugu). The Indian breeds lead: మార్వాడీ and కథియావాడీ are the desert horses of the north-west, and దక్కనీ is the Deccan horse of this region. Horses are not a working animal in Telugu agriculture — the ox and the buffalo are — so the vocabulary here is small and largely borrowed.
locale: te
