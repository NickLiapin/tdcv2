description: Animal (Telugu). The domestic and field animals come first because that is their weight in Telugu life and language — the buffalo (గేదె) is the main dairy animal here rather than the cow, and the ox (ఎద్దు) still names a festival day at Sankranti. ఏనుగు, పులి and చిరుత are the large wild animals of the Eastern Ghats and Nallamala forests, and కృష్ణజింక (blackbuck) is the state animal of Andhra Pradesh.
