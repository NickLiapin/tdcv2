description: Fish (Telugu). Andhra Pradesh produces the largest share of India's inland fish and farmed shrimp, so this is a working commercial list rather than a natural-history one: బొచ్చె (rohu), కొర్రమేను (murrel) and పండుగప్ప (barramundi) are what a Telugu fish market sells by name, and వనామి (Pacific white shrimp) is the single species behind the aquaculture boom of the Krishna and Godavari districts.
