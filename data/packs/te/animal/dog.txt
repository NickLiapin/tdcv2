description: Dog breed (Telugu). The first three are INDIAN breeds and belong at the head of a Telugu list: రాజపాళయం and చిప్పిపరై are southern sighthounds, and నాటు కుక్క — the Indian pariah dog — is by a wide margin the commonest dog in the Telugu states and is a genuine landrace, not a stray without a type.
