description: Bird (Telugu). నెమలి is the national bird and a live presence in Rayalaseema fields, రామచిలుక (rose-ringed parakeet) is the parrot of every Telugu proverb, and కొంగ, పెద్ద కొంగ and కంకణాలు are the waterbirds of the Kolleru and Pulicat wetlands. పాలపిట్ట, the Indian roller, is the state bird of both Telugu states and is deliberately sighted on Dasara day for luck.
locale: te
