description: Book genre (Telugu). Several are Telugu literary categories rather than translations: పద్య కావ్యం is the classical verse epic that Telugu literature is founded on, శతకం is a hundred-verse moral poem addressed to a deity and is the form every Telugu schoolchild memorises, అవధాన సాహిత్యం records the improvisation contests, and దిన సీరియల్ names the serialised novel that Telugu weeklies carried for decades and that shaped modern popular fiction here.
locale: te
