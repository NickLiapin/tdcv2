description: Budget or spending category (Telugu). Two entries would not appear in a European budget and are ordinary here: చిట్టీ is the chit fund, a regulated rotating savings-and-credit scheme that a very large number of Telugu households contribute to monthly, and పెళ్లి ఖర్చులు (wedding expenses) is treated as its own long-running savings line rather than a one-off, because a Telugu wedding is commonly the largest single expenditure of a family's life.
locale: te
