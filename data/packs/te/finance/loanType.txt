description: Loan type (Telugu). బంగారంపై రుణం — the gold loan — is listed high on purpose: pledging household gold for a short-term loan is a mainstream, regulated and very large credit market in the Telugu states, offered by banks and by dedicated gold-loan companies, and it has no European counterpart. పంట రుణం (crop loan) and స్వయం సహాయక సంఘ రుణం (self-help group loan) are likewise ordinary rural credit products here; the SHG movement in Andhra Pradesh is one of the largest in the world.
