description: Vegetable (Telugu). The greens matter more here than in a European list and several have no everyday English name: గోంగూర is roselle or sorrel leaf and is the defining Andhra green, తోటకూర is amaranth, గోకర కాయ? — no; the gourds (బీరకాయ, పొట్లకాయ, సొరకాయ, కాకరకాయ, దోసకాయ) are a whole family that European cooking barely distinguishes, and each is a different dish.
