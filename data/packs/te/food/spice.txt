description: Spice or herb (Telugu). Guntur is the chilli capital of India and its market is the largest chilli yard in Asia, so మిరపకాయలు are not one spice among many here but the base of the cooking; ఆవాలు (mustard), మెంతులు (fenugreek) and ఇంగువ (asafoetida) are the tempering trio that starts almost every dish; and the whole-spice list runs long because a Telugu kitchen grinds its own కారం పొడి and సాంబార్ పొడి rather than buying blends.
