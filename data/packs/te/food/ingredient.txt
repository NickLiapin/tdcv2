description: Cooking ingredient (Telugu). The pulses are listed individually because a Telugu kitchen treats them as different foods with different dishes — కందిపప్పు for everyday పప్పు, మినపప్పు for the dosa and vada batter, పెసరపప్పు for pongal — and lumping them as "lentils" loses the distinction. బెల్లం (jaggery) and చింతపండు (tamarind) are the two ingredients most responsible for the sweet-sour axis of Andhra cooking, and నెయ్యి (ghee) is both a cooking fat and a ritual substance.
locale: te
