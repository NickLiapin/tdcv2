description: Dish (Telugu) — what is actually eaten in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Two structural facts a config cannot infer. First, a Telugu meal is a SEQUENCE served onto rice in order — పప్పు, then కూర, then పులుసు or చారు, then పెరుగు — so several of these are courses rather than standalone dishes, and a "favourite dish" field means something slightly different here. Second, పచ్చడి and ఊరగాయ are not condiments in the European sense: ఆవకాయ, the mustard-and-chilli mango pickle, is made once a year in bulk by the household and is a defining Telugu food, and గోంగూర, the sorrel leaf, is the single item most identified with Andhra cooking anywhere in India.
