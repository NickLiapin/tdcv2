description: Store department or product category (Telugu). The list is a Telugu shop's own division of goods rather than a translated Western one: కిరాణా is the dry-goods grocery that is still the default shop, పప్పు ధాన్యాలు (pulses) and నూనెలు (oils) are counted as separate departments because they are bought by weight at separate counters, and పూజా సామాగ్రి — camphor, incense, turmeric, kumkum, lamp wicks — is a real and busy department in every general store here, with no European counterpart.
locale: te
