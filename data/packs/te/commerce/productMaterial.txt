description: Product material (Telugu). The textile entries are more finely divided than a European list because the distinction matters in the market here: పట్టు (silk), కంచి పట్టు, ఇక్కత్, జంధాని and కలంకారీ are separate goods at separate prices, several with a Geographical Indication tag. మట్టి (terracotta) and ఇత్తడి / కంచు (brass and bell-metal) are live household materials, not craft curiosities.
