description: Colour as it appears on a Telugu product label. Several are named after the thing itself rather than by an abstract term, which is how Telugu colour vocabulary works: పసుపు is literally turmeric, కాషాయం is the ochre of a monk's robe, ఆకుపచ్చ is "leaf-green" and నీలం is both the indigo plant and the colour.
locale: te
