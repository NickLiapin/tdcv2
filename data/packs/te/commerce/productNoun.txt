description: Product noun (Telugu) — the goods on a Telugu shop's shelves. Several have no single-word English equivalent and are ordinary here: గంగాళం and చెంబు are specific brass vessels, తాంబూలం is the betel-leaf set given to guests, గొబ్బెమ్మ and ముగ్గు పిండి belong to the Sankranti and daily-threshold rituals, and నులక మంచం is the woven rope cot that is still the default bed in much of rural Andhra and Telangana.
locale: te
