description: Leave type (Telugu). The Indian leave categories are statutory and distinct in a way the European ones are not: సాధారణ సెలవు (casual leave) is a small annual allowance taken a day or two at a time, ఆర్జిత సెలవు (earned leave) accumulates and can be encashed on retirement, and ఐచ్ఛిక సెలవు (optional holiday) is a fixed number of days an employee chooses from a published list of festivals — which is how a workplace serving Hindu, Muslim and Christian staff handles a calendar with far more festivals than working days can absorb. That last category has no European counterpart at all.
locale: te
