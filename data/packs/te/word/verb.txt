description: Common Telugu verb, given in the infinitive. Telugu verbs agglutinate: tense, person, gender and number are all suffixed onto the stem, so వెళ్ళు becomes వెళ్ళాడు (he went), వెళ్ళింది (she went), వెళ్ళారు (they went) — one word where English needs three.
locale: te
