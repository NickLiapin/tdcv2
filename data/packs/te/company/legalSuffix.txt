description: Company legal or trading suffix as it appears on a Telugu signboard or letterhead. The legal forms themselves are English and stay English even in fully Telugu text — ప్రైవేట్ లిమిటెడ్ is the transliteration of "Private Limited", which is the form Indian company law actually registers, and no Telugu word replaces it. The genuinely Telugu ones are the trading and institutional words: భవన్, నిలయం and సదన్ name a shop-house, సంఘం and సహకార సంఘం name a society and a co-operative (the co-operative is a very live business form in rural Andhra, not a historical curiosity), and అండ్ సన్స్ / అండ్ బ్రదర్స్ are what a family firm still writes above its door.
