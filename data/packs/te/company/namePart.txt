The pattern this list encodes is real and immediately recognisable: an overwhelming share of Telugu firms are named for a temple deity or a pilgrimage town (శ్రీ వెంకటేశ్వర, బాలాజీ, తిరుమల, కనకదుర్గ, అన్నపూర్ణ, సాయి), because a family business is opened under the family deity's name, and the next largest share is named for a river or a region (గోదావరి, కృష్ణా, ఆంధ్ర, అమరావతి, నాగార్జున). The abstract virtue words (ప్రగతి, సమృద్ధి, విజయ, చైతన్య) are the third pattern and belong mostly to co-operatives, schools and banks. A generated combination is not a real company.
