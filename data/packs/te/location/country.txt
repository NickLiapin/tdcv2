description: Country and territory names in Telugu. Telugu handles foreign place names in two ways and both are represented here. A handful are genuinely TRANSLATED — భారతదేశం for India (literally "the land of Bharata"), అమెరికా సంయుక్త రాష్ట్రాలు for the United States, దక్షిణాఫ్రికా, మధ్య ఆఫ్రికా గణరాజ్యం, చెక్ గణరాజ్యం, and every "Islands" as దీవులు — and the rest are adapted into Telugu phonology and written in Telugu script, which is what Telugu media and Telugu Wikipedia do. That is not laziness or transliteration for its own sake: Telugu has no inherited name for most of the world, and inventing one would be worse than adapting the name the country uses. Duplicate English entries (Ivory Coast / Côte d'Ivoire, East Timor / Timor-Leste, Holy See / Vatican City) are merged into one Telugu name each.
locale: te
