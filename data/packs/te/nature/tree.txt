description: Tree (Telugu). వేప, రావి and మర్రి lead because they are the three trees a Telugu village is organised around — the neem at the house, the peepal and the banyan at the temple and the meeting place — and all three carry religious protection, which is why they survive in fields that have been cleared of everything else. ఎర్ర చందనం (red sanders) grows wild ONLY in the Seshachalam hills of Rayalaseema and nowhere else on earth, which is why it is both a protected species and a smuggling problem.
locale: te
