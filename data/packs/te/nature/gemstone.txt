---
description: Gemstone (Telugu). The nine at the head are the నవరత్నాలు, the set that Indian astrology assigns to the nine planets — a Telugu jeweller sells them as a set and a stone is chosen by birth chart as often as by looks, which is why వజ్రం, కెంపు and నీలం carry meanings beyond their colour here. Golconda, near Hyderabad, was the source of the world's named diamonds for centuries, so the diamond vocabulary in Telugu is old and precise.
locale: te
