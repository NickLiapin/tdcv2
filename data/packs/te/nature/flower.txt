description: Flower (Telugu). The order is not botanical but ritual: మల్లె (jasmine), కనకాంబరం (firecracker flower), బంతి (marigold), చామంతి (chrysanthemum) and పారిజాతం lead because they are the flowers actually bought — strung into a మాల for the hair or the deity — and a Telugu flower market sells by the మూర (arm-length of strung flowers), not by the stem. తామర (lotus) and మందారం (hibiscus) carry temple associations that a config generating decorative data may want to know about.
locale: te
