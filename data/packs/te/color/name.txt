description: Human-readable colour name (Telugu). Telugu colour vocabulary is largely built on the thing itself, and that is visible in the list: పసుపు is turmeric, నీలం is the indigo plant, కాషాయం the ochre of a renunciate's robe, ఆకుపచ్చ literally "leaf-green" and గోధుమ "wheat". Note that Telugu marks light and dark with లేత and ముదురు placed BEFORE the colour word (లేత గులాబీ, ముదురు నీలం), and that the three ritual colours — పసుపు, కుంకుమ and ఆకుపచ్చ — carry meaning beyond the hue in any Telugu context involving a wedding or a temple.
locale: te
