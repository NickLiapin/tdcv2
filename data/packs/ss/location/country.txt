description: Country name as written in Eswatini. Only a dozen have established siSwati forms and they are the ones this country has had to name for itself: eSwatini, its own, where the locative e- means "at the place of" and is the reason the name carries no article in English either; iNingizimu Afrika, South Africa, "the south of Africa", which surrounds Eswatini on three sides; Mozambiki on the fourth; Gibhithe for Egypt, an old borrowing through the Bible; and the colonial and trading powers — iBhrithani, iFulentji, iJelimane, iRashiya, iShayina, iNdiya, iJaphani, iMelika. The rest stand in the international form, because siSwati has not coined names for them and a pack that invented some would be putting words in the language. The initial i- on the siSwati forms is a class 9 prefix and part of the word, so it cannot be trimmed to make a shorter label.
