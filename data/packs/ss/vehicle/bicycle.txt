description: Bicycle type in siSwati. Every value is one noun followed by a relative clause, because siSwati does not stack adjectives in front of a noun the way English does: there is no way to say "mountain bike" as two words, only "the bicycle that is of the mountain", libhayisikili lasentsabeni. The la- and le- opening each qualifier are relative concords agreeing with libhayisikili, a class 5 noun, and they would change to a different letter for a noun of another class — the same qualifier attached to imoto would begin le- or ye-. That is why these values cannot be reused as a generic adjective column. Bicycles in Eswatini are working transport on the flat lowveld and around the sugar estates rather than sport; the highveld around Mbabane is too steep for them to have caught on.
locale: ss
