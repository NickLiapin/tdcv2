description: Department within an Eswatini organisation. Litiko is the head word for every value and it is worth knowing why: litiko is the word for a government MINISTRY — Litiko leTemphilo is the Ministry of Health, Litiko leTemfundvo neKucecesha the Ministry of Education and Training — and it has been carried across into company structure so that a firm's divisions are named with the same noun the state uses. What follows litiko is always a possessive phrase in le- plus a te- sector noun, so the construction stacks two possessives: "the ministry of the matters of money". That is why none of these values can be shortened to a single word, and why the te- form on its own, which is what work/industryCoherent holds, names the sector rather than the department.
locale: ss
