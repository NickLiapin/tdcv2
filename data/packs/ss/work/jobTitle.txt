description: Job title in Eswatini. siSwati builds the name of a worker from the verb for the work, using the class 1 prefix um- and the class 2 plural ba-: kufundzisa, to teach, gives umfundzisi; kwakha, to build, gives umakhi; kulima, to plough, gives umlimi; kushayela, to drive, gives umshayeli; kwelapha, to heal, gives umelaphi. That single rule generates most of this list and it is the most useful thing in the file, because it means a Swazi speaker can name an occupation the language has never had a word for. Where a job arrived with an institution and no verb, the loan is taken honestly instead: dokotela, nesi, thishela, sokhemisi. The occupations themselves are Eswatini's — sugar at Big Bend, Mhlume and Simunye is the largest private employer in the country, forestry at Bhunya and Piggs Peak the next, textiles at Matsapha after that, and behind all three the civil service and the Tinkhundla administration with its indvuna and its bucopho. Inyanga the herbalist and sangoma the diviner are on this list as occupations because that is what they are: registered, paid and consulted alongside the clinic rather than instead of it.
locale: ss
