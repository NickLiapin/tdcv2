description: Building type in siSwati. Two entries carry facts a European list has no slot for. Umuti is not a house but a HOMESTEAD — a cluster of dwellings, a cattle byre and a yard belonging to one extended family under one head — and it is the unit most of Eswatini actually lives in and is addressed by; the same word also means a tree and a medicine, which is one of the language's most quietly useful ambiguities. Indlu yetjani is the grass-thatched beehive dwelling, still built for ritual use at Ludzidzini and Lobamba long after concrete block took over daily housing. Sibaya, the cattle byre, is on a building list rather than a farm list because the national Sibaya at Lobamba is where the whole country assembles to be addressed by the King, so the word names both a kraal and a parliament.
locale: ss
