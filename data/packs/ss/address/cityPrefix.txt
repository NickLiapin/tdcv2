description: Leading element of an Eswatini place name. This file differs in KIND from its counterpart in a European pack, and the difference is the point: siSwati has no free-standing leading adjective the way Serbian has Novi or Stari, because the qualifier that opens a place name is a BOUND prefix written joined to the stem. eZulwini is "at the heavens", eManzini "at the waters", kaPhunga "at Phunga's place", kwaLuseni "at Luseni's" — the e-, ka- and kwa- are locative morphemes, not words, and this is the same e- that makes the country's own name eSwatini, "at the place of the Swazi", which is why Eswatini needs no article in English either. The rest of the list is the recurring syllabic openings of real Swazi toponyms — Mhla- in Mhlambanyatsi, Nhla- in Nhlangano, Ngwe- in Ngwenya, Dvo- in Dvokolwako. Join one to a stem with no space; separating them produces something that is not a name.
locale: ss
