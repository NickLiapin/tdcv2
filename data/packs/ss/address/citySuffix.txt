description: Trailing element of an Eswatini place name. Nearly every value ends in -ini or -eni, and that ending is the other half of the locative circumfix whose front half lives in cityPrefix: siSwati wraps a noun in e- ... -ini to turn it into a place, so umfula, a river, becomes emfuleni, "at the river", and litje, a stone, becomes etjeni. Which of -ini or -eni you get is decided by the last vowel of the stem, not by choice. The stems themselves are a map of what Swazi settlements are named after — water, stone, forest, gate, cattle byre, the reed, the marula and the knobthorn, the chief's place — and Manzini, the country's commercial centre, is simply "at the waters". Combine with a prefix element; a suffix alone does not name a real town.
locale: ss
