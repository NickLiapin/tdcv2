description: Employment benefit in Eswatini. Umhlomelo is the head word for most of these and it means an addition or a supplement, from kuhloma, to attach — so an allowance is literally something fastened onto the wage. The transport and housing allowances at the top of the list are not fringe items here: most formal jobs are in Mbabane, Manzini or Matsapha while most workers live in a rural homestead under a chief, so the daily kombi fare and a room near work are the difference between a job being worth taking and not. Imali yekuncedza umndeni ekufeni, funeral assistance, is offered by nearly every serious employer in this country, because a funeral is a large, unavoidable and frequent family expense and the burial societies that carry it are one of the most widespread financial institutions in Eswatini.
