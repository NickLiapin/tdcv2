description: Payment status in siSwati. Every value is a verb in a completed tense rather than an adjective, which means these describe an EVENT and not a state of the record: kukhokhelwe is "it has been paid", not "paid". The distinction matters for anyone building a status column, because siSwati has no participle that can sit beside a noun the way English "paid invoice" does — that has to be rebuilt as a relative clause. Two entries split what English would call refunded: kubuyiselwe imali is the whole amount returned and kubuyiselwe imali ingcosana a part of it, and there is no single word covering both.
locale: ss
