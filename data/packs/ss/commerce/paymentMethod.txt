description: Payment method in Eswatini. Two names on this list are the country's own and they matter more than the cards above them: MoMo is MTN Eswatini's mobile money service and e-Mali is Eswatini Mobile's, and between them they carry a very large share of person-to-person transfers and small retail payment in a country where formal bank accounts are far from universal. Sending money to a relative in a rural homestead means a phone rather than a branch. Note the two credit entries at the end: sikweleti sasesitolo, store credit, is the account a rural general dealer keeps in a book for regular customers and settles at month end, an institution older than any card and still running.
locale: ss
