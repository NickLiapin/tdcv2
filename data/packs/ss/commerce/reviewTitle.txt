description: Customer review headline in siSwati. Notice how few of these are noun phrases: a siSwati review title is a statement with a verb in it, because the language builds emphasis through the verb rather than through a stacked adjective. Ngiyayincoma is one word carrying subject, object and verb together — ngi- I, -ya- present, -yi- it, -ncoma praise — and that agglutination is why a siSwati headline can be shorter than its English translation while saying more. The two customer-service entries are worth reading as a pair: lusito is help or assistance, from kusita, and the same word covers a warranty department and a neighbour lending a hand, which is a fair description of how service works in a country this small.
locale: ss
