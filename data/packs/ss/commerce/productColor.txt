description: Product colour in Eswatini, and the list divides sharply in two. The first nine are the colours siSwati actually has words for, written as relative forms with the lo- concord because they are clauses rather than adjectives: lomnyama black, lomhlophe white, lobovu red, loluhlata green, lomtfubi yellow, lonsundvu brown, lomphunga grey. Loluhlata does double duty for green and blue, and a speaker who needs to distinguish them says loluhlata sasibhakabhaka, "green like the sky" — the classic Nguni green-blue merger, and the reason this pack cannot offer a single word for blue. Everything after that is a loan taken whole for a commercial colour chart, and those are class 9 nouns with i- rather than relative forms, so they do not agree with anything and cannot be used the same way in a sentence.
locale: ss
