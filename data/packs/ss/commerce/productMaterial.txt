description: Product material in Eswatini. Six of these are the country's own crafts rather than industrial inputs. Sisali, sisal, and tjani, grass, are what Swazi basketry is coiled from and it is a real export, sold through cooperatives at Malkerns and Mantenga. Luhlanga, the reed, is cut in thousands every August for Umhlanga, the reed dance, and used for screens and fencing the rest of the year. Sikhumba, hide, is what the sidvwaba, a married woman's ceremonial skirt, and the emajobo, the fur worn by regiments, are made of. Lubumba is potter's clay and ingodvo is wood, both worked here for a thousand years. The remainder are loans, and the class 9 i- prefix on every one of them marks how recently they arrived.
