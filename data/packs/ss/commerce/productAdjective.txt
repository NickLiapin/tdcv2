description: Product adjective in siSwati, and the leading lo- is the part that must not be dropped. siSwati has almost no true adjectives; what looks like one is a RELATIVE CLAUSE, and lo- is the concord agreeing with the noun it describes. Every value here agrees with a class 1 or class 3 noun, so lomusha describes umbhedze, a new bed. Attach the same qualifier to a class 9 noun and the concord changes — imoto lensha, not lomusha — and to a class 5 noun it changes again. A config that joins any of these to a random product noun will produce agreement errors in most of its rows, which a siSwati reader notices instantly. Three of the values carry a marketing claim that a shopper in Eswatini reads as meaningful: lowentiwe eSwatini, made in Eswatini, is a real preference in a market otherwise filled by South African goods.
locale: ss
