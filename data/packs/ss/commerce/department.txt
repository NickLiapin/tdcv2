description: Store department in Eswatini. Almost every value is a plural, because a department holds many of a thing rather than one: timphahla is the class 10 plural of imphahla, goods, and it heads a dozen of these on its own with a possessive saying what kind. That is why none of these can be made singular without changing what they mean. The list is shaped by a small retail market of South African chain stores in the Manzini and Ezulwini malls and the department names they use, and by the general dealer in a rural trading store, which carries food, paraffin, blankets, farm inputs and school shoes under one roof and is the reason the categories here are broader than a European list would draw them.
locale: ss
