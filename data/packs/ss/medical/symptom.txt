description: Presenting symptom in siSwati, and almost every value is a VERB rather than a noun. siSwati does not have a stock of symptom nouns the way clinical English does; it says what the body is doing. Kukhwehlela is "to cough", kuhlanta "to vomit", kujuluka ebusuku "to sweat at night", kudzinwa "to be tired" — all class 15 infinitives standing in for nouns. Where a part of the body hurts, the construction is buhlungu plus a possessive: buhlungu benhloko is "the pain of the head". This has a practical consequence for anyone building forms from this list, because these values cannot be pluralised or counted; a patient does not have three kukhwehlela. Kujuluka ebusuku, night sweats, sits high on a real Eswatini list rather than low, because in a country with this much tuberculosis it is a screening question rather than a curiosity.
locale: ss
