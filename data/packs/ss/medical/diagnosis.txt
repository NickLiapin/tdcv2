description: General diagnosis list for Eswatini, and it is weighted toward this country's actual disease burden rather than a European one. The infectious block runs first and long because that is the reality: Eswatini has among the highest adult HIV prevalence in the world and has built one of the largest treatment programmes to match, tuberculosis travels with it so closely that the two are managed by a single national programme, malaria persists in the Lubombo lowveld where the country is part of a regional elimination effort, and bilharzia is endemic along the Lusutfu and Mbuluzi. Behind that sits a fast-growing non-communicable burden of hypertension and type 2 diabetes, and behind that the injuries — road traffic on the Mbabane-Manzini corridor, and the occupational lung disease Swazi men brought back from the South African mines. The list is written the way an Eswatini chart is written, siSwati for what the patient describes and English behind an i- prefix for what gets coded, because that is genuinely how a bilingual health service records disease and pretending otherwise would misdescribe the country.
