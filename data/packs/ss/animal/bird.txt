description: Bird in siSwati, and the first value is a national emblem rather than a species record. Ligwalagwala is the purple-crested turaco, and its crimson wing feathers are royal regalia in Eswatini: only the King may wear them freely, and they are conferred on others as an honour, which means the bird is protected by custom as much as by law and its name is on the national airline and half the country's institutions. Most of the birds after it carry names built from what they sound like or do — inkonjane the swallow, ihlolamvula the coucal, whose call is said to announce rain, ithekwane the hamerkop, a bird thickly surrounded by taboo across Nguni-speaking southern Africa. The block of class 9 loans near the end covers birds that do not occur here and therefore never acquired a siSwati name; the split between the two halves of this file is a map of what the language has seen.
