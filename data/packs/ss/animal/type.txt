description: Animal in siSwati. Inkhomo, cattle, heads the list rather than sitting in it, because in Eswatini cattle are not livestock in the ordinary sense: they are the medium of lobola, the bridewealth that makes a marriage; they are counted as wealth rather than as meat; and the national Sibaya at Lobamba, where the whole country assembles to be addressed by the King, is literally a cattle byre. The big game below it is not decorative either — Hlane, Mkhaya and Mlilwane hold elephant, white rhino, lion and buffalo within an hour of Manzini, and umkhombe, the rhinoceros, is guarded here under some of the strictest anti-poaching law in Africa. Note the noun classes at work: most animals are class 9 with in-, taking tin- in the plural, while a few take class 5 li- and its class 6 plural ema-, and the class is fixed by history rather than by any property of the animal.
