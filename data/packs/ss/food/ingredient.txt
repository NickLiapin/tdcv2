description: Cooking ingredient in Eswatini. Impuphu, meal, is the head word for four separate staples here and the possessive that follows it decides which — impuphu yembila is maize meal, impuphu yemabele sorghum meal, impuphu yakolo wheat flour — so the word alone is not an ingredient and a config that shortens these will produce a value with no referent. Imvubelo is worth reading twice: it is the living sour starter kept back from one batch of incwancwa to raise the next, the same relationship a sourdough culture has to its bread, and it is not interchangeable with the iyisti a few lines below it. Emasi, soured milk, is a preserved food rather than a spoiled one, made by leaving milk in a gourd until it separates, and it is eaten with tinkhobe as a meal in its own right.
locale: ss
