description: Fruit in siSwati, and the class prefixes do real work here. A fruit tree is class 3, umganu, while its fruit is class 5 or 6, emaganu — the same stem with a different prefix names the tree and the crop, and getting the prefix wrong turns one into the other. The wild fruits at the end of the list are not folklore: emaganu, the marula, is harvested by the tonne every February for buganu beer and for the oil the fruit's kernel yields, emantungulu is the Natal plum that hedges a homestead with thorns and feeds it as well, and emakhwakhwa, the monkey orange, is picked in the lowveld bush. The cultivated block is what Eswatini's lowveld irrigation schemes grow for export — citrus around Ngonini and Tshaneni, pineapple around Malkerns — so the same list holds subsistence and cash crop side by side.
