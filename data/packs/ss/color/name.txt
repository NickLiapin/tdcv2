description: Colour name in siSwati. Only the first nine are native words, and they are relative constructions rather than adjectives — lo- plus a stem — which is why none can be used bare. The gap they leave is famous: loluhlata covers green AND blue, and a speaker needing to separate them says loluhlata sasibhakabhaka, "green like the sky", which is a comparison rather than a colour word. This is not a poverty of the language but a different carving of the spectrum, and siSwati is extravagantly rich exactly where it matters to Swazi life: Nguni languages carry dozens of precise terms for the colour and pattern of CATTLE hides — inkone for a white-backed beast, impemvu for a white-faced one, ihlosi for a leopard-marked one — a vocabulary with no equivalent in any European language and none of it usable for a paint chart, which is why the rest of this file is borrowed.
locale: ss
