description: Hotel amenity in Eswatini. Ijenaretha, the generator, is advertised for the same reason it is across the region — the power supply is not always steady and a lodge that guarantees it is selling something real. Indzawo yekwosela inyama, the braai area, is a standard fitting rather than a luxury: cooking meat over an open fire is what a Swazi family does at a gathering, and a guesthouse without a place to do it is missing a facility rather than a flourish. Kutfutfwa kusuka enkhundleni yetindiza matters because King Mswati III International at Sikhuphe is an hour and a half from the Ezulwini hotels with no public transport of any kind between them, so the transfer is part of the booking rather than an extra.
