description: Common siSwati verb, given as class 15 infinitives in ku- because that is the citation form and the only form that can stand alone as a word. Notice that every stem ends in -a: that is not a coincidence but a rule, and it is the vowel that all the machinery attaches to. Change it to -e and you have the subjunctive; insert -w- before it and you have the passive; add -isa and you have the causative; add -ana and you have the reciprocal; add -ile and you have the perfect. Kutalwa, to be born, is already a passive of kutala, to give birth, which is why a Swazi says "I was born" with no active alternative available. Strip the ku- and a stem like -hamba is a fragment, not a word.
locale: ss
