description: Common siSwati descriptive word. None of these is an adjective in the English sense — siSwati has only a handful of true adjective stems, and everything else that describes is a RELATIVE CLAUSE built with a concord. The lo- at the front of every value here agrees with a class 1 or class 3 noun, and it must change for any other class: umuntfu lomkhulu is a big person, but a big house is indlu lenkhulu and a big stone is litje lelikhulu, with three different concords on the same stem. Nine of these are the colour terms, and loluhlata covers green and blue together, which is the classic Nguni merger and the reason blue needs a comparison — loluhlata sasibhakabhaka, green like the sky.
locale: ss
