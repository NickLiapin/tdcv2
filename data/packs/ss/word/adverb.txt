description: Common siSwati adverb. Two devices make most of them. The prefix ka- turns a stem into a manner adverb: kahle well, kabi badly, kancane a little, kabili twice — and it is the same ka- that appears in place names meaning "at the place of", which is a locative doing double duty. The prefix nga- makes an instrumental or manner phrase: ngekushesha quickly, ngempela truly, ngeliphutsa by mistake, ngenhloso on purpose. The time words in the middle are bare nouns used adverbially without any marker at all — namuhla today, itolo yesterday, kusasa tomorrow — and kusasa is worth a second look, because it also means "in the morning", so a siSwati sentence about tomorrow and one about the morning can be the same sentence.
locale: ss
