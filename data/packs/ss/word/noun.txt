description: Common siSwati noun, for slugs, usernames and tags — chosen to spread across the noun classes rather than to cluster in one, so that a reader can see the prefix system working. Class 1 gives umuntfu, umfati, umngani; class 3 gives umuti, umfula, umoya, umlomo; class 5 gives lilanga, liso, litje; class 7 gives sikhatsi, sihlahla, sive, sandla; class 9 gives inja, inkhomo, inyoni, indlela; class 11 gives lwandle. Every one of them carries its prefix as part of the word, and the stem alone is not a word: strip in- from inja and nothing is left that a speaker can say. That is the single most important thing to know before slicing any siSwati value in this pack for a shorter identifier.
