description: Filler sentences in siSwati, the lorem-ipsum prose of this pack. Written by hand: data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs assembles these files from public-domain books and there is no siSwati title in Project Gutenberg to draw on, so the corpus was composed rather than extracted. It is deliberately plain and domestic — the homestead, the fields, the weather, the animals, school, work, the market, the ceremonies, the road — because filler that all comes from one subject reads as a glossary rather than as language. Two structural facts are visible in almost every line. First, siSwati marks the subject on the VERB with a concord agreeing in noun class, so umfana ugijima and bafana bagijima differ in the verb as well as the noun, and inja iyakhonkotsa takes i- because inja is class 9 while lilanga liyashisa takes li- because lilanga is class 5; the pronoun is normally absent because the concord already carries it. Second, the -ya- that appears in some present-tense verbs and not others is not optional and not a tense: it is used when the verb ends the sentence and dropped when anything follows it, which is why kuyashisa stands alone but ushisa kakhulu does not.
