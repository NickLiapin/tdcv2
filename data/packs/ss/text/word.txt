description: Filler words in siSwati, the lorem-ipsum vocabulary of this pack. Written by hand: data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs assembles these three files from public-domain books and has no siSwati title to draw on, so the list was compiled rather than extracted. It is deliberately ordinary — kin, the body, the house, food, the land, animals, time, the state, abstractions, adjectives, verbs, numbers and function words — so that a random draw reads as siSwati rather than as a glossary of one subject. Every noun carries its class prefix because the prefix is part of the word: strip in- from inja and nothing pronounceable remains, and umuntfu without umu- is not a shorter form of anything. Every verb is a class 15 infinitive in ku- and every stem ends in -a, which is the vowel all the language's machinery attaches to. The descriptive words all open with lo-, the relative concord, because siSwati has almost no true adjectives and says "which is big" where English says "big" — and that concord agrees with class 1 and class 3 here and would have to change for any other noun. Two of the function words are single syllables with no independent meaning at all, na and nga, which do the work of a dozen English prepositions between them.
locale: ss
