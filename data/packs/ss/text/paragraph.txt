description: Filler paragraphs in siSwati, composed by hand for the same reason as the sentence file: data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs builds these from public-domain books and there is no siSwati text in Project Gutenberg to build from. Each paragraph runs two to four sentences on one subject, and the subjects are ordinary — the homestead at dawn, the fields through the season, the market, the clinic, the road, the ceremonies. Reading a few in succession shows the feature that most distinguishes siSwati prose from English: agreement runs right through a sentence, so that a single noun class fixes the shape of the subject concord on the verb, the relative that describes it, the possessive that follows it and the pronoun that later refers back to it. Change umuti to indlu and half the words in the sentence change with it. That is why the paragraphs cannot be reassembled from the sentence file by shuffling clauses, and why a config that stitches siSwati fragments together will produce text that is grammatical nowhere.
