description: Insurance type in Eswatini. Umshuwalense wemngcwabo, funeral cover, belongs near the top of any honest ordering for this country rather than at the bottom: it is by a wide margin the most widely held insurance product in Eswatini and in southern Africa generally, sold in small monthly premiums, and it exists because a funeral is a large obligation that falls on an extended family with little warning. Umshuwalense wetifuyo, livestock cover, is on the list for a related reason — cattle are a household's stored wealth here, the medium of lobola and the measure of standing, so losing a herd to drought or disease is a capital loss rather than an operating one. Umshuwalense is a loan from English insurance taken as a class 3 noun with um-.
