description: Garment in Eswatini, and the ceremonial block in the middle is what makes this list Eswatini's rather than anyone's. Lihiya is the printed cotton cloth worn wrapped over the shoulder or round the waist, by men and women alike, and it is the single most recognisable Swazi garment; at Umhlanga and Incwala the pattern and colour of it say something about who is wearing it. Sidvwaba is the pleated ox-hide skirt that marks a married woman and is worn at ceremony. Emajobo are the fur strips worn hanging from the waist by men in regimental dress. Ligcebesha is the beaded neckpiece. None of these is costume: they are current dress with occasions attached, and a Swazi wardrobe holds them alongside the suit and the jeans on the same list.
