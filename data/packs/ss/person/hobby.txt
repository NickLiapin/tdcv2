description: Pastime in siSwati, and every value is an INFINITIVE — the class 15 form in ku-, which is how siSwati names an activity at all. There is no bare noun for "singing"; there is only kucula, "to sing", used as the name of the thing. That is why nothing on this list can be shortened by dropping the first two letters, and why a config joining these to a verb produces a double infinitive. Several entries name crafts that are still economically real in Eswatini rather than hobbies: kwaluka tikhwama is the grass and sisal basketry that Swazi women sell through cooperatives at Malkerns and Mantenga, kwenta tigcebesha is beadwork, and kubata tigodvo is the woodcarving trade. Kusina covers the ceremonial dancing of Umhlanga and Incwala as well as social dancing, and makhweyane, the braced gourd bow at kudlala makhweyane, is Eswatini's own instrument.
