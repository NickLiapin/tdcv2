description: Female given name in Eswatini. The single most productive pattern is the no- prefix, a contraction of the possessive "mother of", which turns almost any noun into a woman's name: Nomsa is "mother of grace", Nomcebo "mother of wealth", Nokuthula "mother of peace", Nokwanda "mother of increase", Nolwazi "mother of knowledge", Nobuhle "mother of beauty", Nozipho "mother of gifts". A second pattern builds the name on a completed verb in -ile or -iwe, giving a name that reports something that has happened: Busisiwe "she has been blessed", Sibongile "we have given thanks", Thandiwe "she is loved", Lindiwe "she was waited for", Khethiwe "she was chosen", Zanele "the girls are enough", which is a name given when a family has had daughters and hoped for a son. Tandzile, Tfobile and Tsandzile show the siSwati spelling where Zulu writes Thandile and Thobile. The seven royal names — Labotsibeni, Gwamile, Dzeliwe, Lomawa, Ntfombi, Nukwase, Zihlathi — are queen mothers, and Gwamile, "the resolute one", is the praise name of the regent who paid for Eswatini's first national school out of a cattle levy. The surname a Swazi woman carries is drawn from person/lastName exactly as a man's is; Swazi surnames have no feminine form.
