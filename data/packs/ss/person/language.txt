description: Language name in siSwati. Every value carries the class 7 prefix si-, which is what marks a language in every Nguni tongue, and the prefix is written lowercase with the stem capitalised — siSwati, not SiSwati or Siswati — because it is grammatically bound rather than part of the name. That is also why the language cannot be called Swati in siSwati; strip the prefix and you are left with the noun class stem for the people, not a language. Eswatini has two official languages, siSwati and siNgisi, and the constitution names siSwati first; parliament and the courts run largely in English while Sibaya, the national assembly at Lobamba, runs entirely in siSwati. siSwati is mutually intelligible with siZulu and largely so with siXhosa and siNdebele, which is why a Swazi worker in Johannesburg does not learn a new language to get by.
