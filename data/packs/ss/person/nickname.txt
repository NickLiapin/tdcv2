description: Short form of a name in Eswatini. The commonest device is not adding a suffix but cutting the vowels out of the middle: Sibusiso becomes Sbu, Sipho becomes Spho, Mduduzi becomes Mdu, Nkosinathi becomes Nkos. The resulting cluster is unpronounceable by the language's own rules — siSwati syllables end in a vowel — which is precisely what marks the form as familiar rather than formal, and it is why these are written but rarely used in careful speech. A second device is to keep only the meaningful half of a compound name, so Vusumuzi becomes Vusi, Mandlenkhosi becomes Mandla, Nkosinhlanhla becomes Nhlanhla and Nobuhle becomes Buhle; that is why several values here look like whole names, because they ARE whole names doing duty as short ones. The kinship terms at the end — Babe, Bhuti, Sisi, Gogo, Mkhulu, Malume — are used as names in their own right for a person whose role in the household outranks their name.
