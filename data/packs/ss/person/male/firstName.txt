description: Male given name in Eswatini. Three stocks are mixed here in roughly the proportions a school register carries. The largest is siSwati meaning-names, and the meaning is usually a sentence about the circumstances of the birth or a message to the family: Sipho is "a gift", Themba "trust", Bongani "give thanks", Musa "grace", Nkosinathi "the Lord is with us", Sibusiso "a blessing", Mduduzi "the comforter", Njabulo "joy", Mandlenkhosi "the strength of the king", Nkululeko "freedom", Mfanawenkhosi "the king's boy". Second, the same names in siSwati rather than Zulu spelling — Sitsembiso against Sithembiso, Vusumuti against Vusumuzi, Bhekumuti against Bhekumuzi, Mnotfo against Mnotho — both of which appear on Eswatini rolls and neither of which is a misspelling of the other. Third, royal and historic names still given to children: Mswati, Sobhuza, Ngwane, Makhosetive, Somhlolo, Mbandzeni, Ludvonga, Malunge. The English block at the end is not decoration either; mission schooling left a large stock of biblical and virtue names in ordinary use, and Gift, Justice, Welcome, Wonderboy, Comfort and Doctor are given names in Eswatini exactly as written.
