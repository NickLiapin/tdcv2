description: Case type in Eswatini. Licala is the head word and it carries a usefully wide meaning: a court case, an offence, and also simply a fault or blame, so to say a person has a licala is to say they are at fault before any court is involved. Licala lemhlaba, the land case, is the commonest civil matter to reach a Swazi National Court and it is rarely about ownership, because Swazi Nation Land is not owned — it is about allocation, boundaries between homesteads, and which chief a household belongs to, and those are exactly the questions the customary system exists to answer. Licala lekondla bantfwana, maintenance, is the commonest matter in the magistrates' courts.
