description: Weather condition in siSwati. Eswatini fits four sharply different climates into 200 kilometres: the cool wet Highveld around Mbabane, which catches mist and can frost; the Middleveld; the hot dry Lowveld at Big Bend and Simunye, where summer runs past 40 degrees; and the Lubombo plateau along the Mozambique border. A single national forecast is therefore always wrong somewhere, and the same day is honestly described by several of these lines at once. Note the two grammatical types mixed here: nouns like imvula and inkhungu, and infinitives like kubalela and kushisa, which name a condition as an activity the weather is doing rather than a thing it is.
