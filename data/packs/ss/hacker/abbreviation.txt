description: Technical abbreviation, used unchanged and in Latin letters inside otherwise siSwati text — which is normal Swazi technical writing rather than a lapse. siSwati has no reason to translate these and no mechanism to: an initialism is built from the initial letters of English words, so a siSwati rendering would need a siSwati phrase behind it first, and none exists for most of them. Two are worth flagging as more present in Eswatini than the rest. SIM and OTP belong to a country where mobile money is the main payment rail: the one-time PIN sent by SMS is how a MoMo or e-Mali transfer is authorised, and a great many Swazi adults use one weekly without ever having touched a bank card.
locale: ss
