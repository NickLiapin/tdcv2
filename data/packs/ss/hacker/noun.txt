description: Technical noun in siSwati, and the file shows the two strategies the language uses side by side. Where an old word can stretch, it does: sikhiya is a key for a door and a key in a database, inkhumbulo is memory in a person and in a machine, indlela is a path on the ground and a path in a filesystem, sikhala is a gap and a field. Where nothing stretches, siSwati borrows and prefixes: iseva, ikheshi, ifayela, ikhode. The most telling entry is ligciwane lekhompiyutha, a computer virus — ligciwane is the ordinary word for a pathogen, the same one used of HIV in this pack's medical files, and English made exactly the same metaphor from exactly the same source, independently.
