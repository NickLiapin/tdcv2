description: Gemstone and ornamental mineral in siSwati. The last two entries are the ones that belong to Eswatini rather than to a jeweller's window. Ihemathayithi and isipekhulariti, haematite and its glittering variety specularite, were mined at Lion Cavern on Ngwenya mountain about 43,000 years ago — not for iron, which nobody could yet smelt, but for red and silver pigment used on the body and on the dead. That makes Ngwenya one of the oldest known mines on earth, and it makes these two minerals the reason Eswatini has a claim in the history of mining that no diamond mine can match. The Ngwenya deposit was worked again industrially for iron ore from 1964, and the mountain now carries both a 43,000-year-old pit and a twentieth-century open cast within sight of each other.
