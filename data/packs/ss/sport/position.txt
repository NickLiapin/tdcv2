description: Playing position in siSwati, drawn from across the sports this pack covers. Every value is an agent noun in um- built from a verb, which is how the language names a role: kuvikela, to defend, gives umvikeli; kuhlasela, to attack, gives umhlaseli; kugcina, to keep, gives umgcini. That derivation is why a goalkeeper is umgcini welisango, "the keeper of the gate", and why the same word serves for a shopkeeper elsewhere in this pack. The left and right qualifiers are worth noting: wangasencele is "of the left" and wangasekudla "of the right", and the second is built on kudla, to eat, because the right hand is the eating hand — a body-based direction system rather than an abstract one. Kaputeni and unompempe, the captain and the referee, are loans, the second from the sound of the whistle.
