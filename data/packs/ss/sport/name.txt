description: Sport in Eswatini. Libhola alone, with no qualifier, means football, and the national side is Sihlangu Semnikati, "the King's Shield"; netibhola, netball, is the leading women's sport and is played at every high school in the country. Ragbhi has a genuine following here, and Eswatini has fielded a national side since the 1990s. Kwekhwela intsaba is on the list for a specific reason: the annual Sibebe Survivor walk up the granite dome above Mbabane draws thousands and is one of the largest participatory sporting events in the country. Note the -bhola family — libhola, netibhola, volibhola, softibhola, bhasikhidi — where an English ball game keeps the word ball and takes a siSwati class prefix, and note also that ice sports and winter sports are entirely absent, which is honest for a country that does not freeze.
