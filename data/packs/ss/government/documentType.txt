description: Official document in Eswatini. Two at the end have no counterpart in a European list and both follow from the same fact — that most of the country is Swazi Nation Land administered by chiefs. Incwadzi yesikhulu, the chief's letter, is a written confirmation from a chief that a person belongs to his chiefdom, and it is asked for in situations where a European system would ask for proof of address: opening an account, applying for a plot, registering for a programme. Imvume yekuhamba nemkhawulweni is the border pass used for local crossings into South Africa and Mozambique by people who live along a frontier that cuts through families. Sitifiketi and ilayisensi are loans; incwadzi and imvume are not, and the older words are the ones attached to the older institutions.
