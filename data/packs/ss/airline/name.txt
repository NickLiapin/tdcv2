description: Airline known in Eswatini. Two are the country's own and they bracket fifty years. Royal Swazi National Airways flew from 1978 and collapsed in the 1990s, and its Fokker and BAe aircraft out of Matsapha are still remembered. Eswatini Air launched in 2023 from King Mswati III International Airport at Sikhuphe, the airport built in the bush near Big Bend that stood almost unused for a decade after opening. Between them Airlink from Johannesburg carried nearly all of the country's air traffic, and still carries most of it: Eswatini's practical connection to the world is a 45-minute hop to OR Tambo and a connection there, which is why the list below is dominated by carriers a Swazi traveller meets in a South African transit lounge rather than on a Swazi apron.
