description: Character class for a game, rendered into siSwati agent nouns — and the exercise shows what the language does and does not have a slot for. Libutfo and umlwi cover the warrior easily, because Eswatini's emabutfo, the age regiments, are a live institution and the vocabulary is current. Umelaphi, the healer, is straightforward. But the fantasy caster splits into two words that are not interchangeable and neither of which is a game term: sangoma is a diviner who is called by the ancestors and works with them, inyanga is a herbalist who works with plants, and umtsakatsi is a sorcerer working for harm — an accusation rather than a profession, and a serious one. A config that treats all three as flavours of "mage" is flattening a distinction that matters a great deal in Eswatini.
locale: ss
