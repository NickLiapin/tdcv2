description: Industry in siSwati — the FULL list, of which work/industryCoherent is the ten-value subset this pack ships job titles for. Every value is the te- possessive, "matters of", attached to an infinitive or a noun, which is the construction Eswatini uses for a government ministry and has carried into every sector name. The composition of the list is the country's: tekulima kwemoba, sugar cane growing, is separated from general agriculture because sugar is Eswatini's largest export, largest private employer and the reason three company towns exist; tekwenta shukela is the milling that follows it; tekugawula tihlahla is the Usutu pine and eucalyptus plantations; and tekumba covers coal at Maloma, iron at Ngwenya and the quarries, which is what remains of a mining industry that once ran an asbestos mine at Bulembu among the largest in the world.
