description: Evocative adjective for an Eswatini company name or slogan, and every one is a relative clause rather than a true adjective: the lo- at the front is a concord agreeing with a class 1 or class 3 noun, and it changes shape for every other class. That is the single fact a config using this file must know, because a slogan pairing one of these with a class 9 noun will disagree in every row. Note how many end in -ko or -ako: that is the relative form of a verb rather than of an adjective, so lokhulako is literally "which grows" and lophumelelako "which succeeds" — siSwati prefers to say what a thing DOES over saying what it is, which is why a Swazi company slogan tends to read as a verb where an English one reads as a boast.
locale: ss
