description: Evocative element for an Eswatini company name, to be combined with a legalSuffix. The proportions are drawn from what Swazi firms are actually called, and the answer is overwhelmingly the land: rivers first — Lusutfu, Komati, Mbuluzi, Ngwavuma — then the mountains and reserves, Sibebe the granite dome above Mbabane, Emlembe the highest peak, Lubombo the escarpment on the Mozambique border, Mdzimba the range where kings are buried. Then the towns, then the royal names, which carry real weight on a signboard: Sobhuza, Somhlolo, Ngwane, Gwamile. Then trees, with umganu the marula at the head, and animals led by ligwalagwala, the purple-crested turaco whose feathers are royal regalia and whose name is on the national airline. The abstractions come last because they come last in practice too, and the ceremonies — Umhlanga, Incwala, Buganu — are used for anything with a national claim in its name.
locale: ss
