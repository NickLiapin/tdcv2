description: Noun for an Eswatini company name, slogan or mission statement. The first half is abstract and most of it takes the class 14 prefix bu- or the class 15 infinitive ku-, which are siSwati's two ways of making an abstraction: bumbano unity, bungcweti expertise, buhle beauty on one side, and kubambisana partnership, kutfutfuka development, kunakekela care on the other. The second half is concrete on purpose, because that is where Swazi naming actually goes — a firm here is far more likely to be called after a river, a mountain, the sun or a pillar than after a virtue, which is why insika, umtfombo, lilanga and inkhanyeti belong on a buzzword list in this country. Umtfombo is the best of them: it means a spring of water and, by extension, a source or an origin.
locale: ss
