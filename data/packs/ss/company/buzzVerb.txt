description: Verb for an Eswatini company slogan, all in the class 15 infinitive with ku-, which is the only form in which siSwati can name an action. Look for the -isa endings, because they are the language's engine: kukhula is to grow and kukhulisa is to make grow, kutfutfuka is to progress and kutfutfukisa is to develop, kucina is to be firm and kucinisekisa is to make certain. That single suffix turns any intransitive verb into a transitive one and gives siSwati most of its corporate vocabulary for free — which is why a Swazi slogan can say what a company does TO its customers with a word derived from what those customers do on their own. Kwabelana, to share with one another, shows the reciprocal -ana doing the same job in the other direction.
locale: ss
