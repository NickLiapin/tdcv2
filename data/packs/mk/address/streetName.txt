description: Street names found in towns across North Macedonia — the revolutionaries of 1893-1903, the writers of the standard language, the mountains and rivers, and the dates. THE NAME GOES AFTER THE THOROUGHFARE WORD (ул. Гоце Делчев), which is why address/streetSuffix.txt must be composed first. Ѓорче Петров, Ѓорѓи Абаџиев and Караџица carry the Macedonian-only ѓ and џ; Васил Иљоски carries љ; Пчиња carries њ. Кирил и Методиј and Климент Охридски are the alphabet's own creators and are on a street in every town in the country. Streets belonging to one particular city belong in a country pack, not here — this list is the shared national stock that repeats from Skopje to Strumica.
