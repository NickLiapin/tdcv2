description: KIND of court, as Macedonian names it — not the roster of actual courts. THE NAMED COURTS OF NORTH MACEDONIA ARE THE COUNTRY PACK'S BUSINESS and live at north_macedonia.government.court, which lists every basic, appellate and supreme court by seat. This file holds the generic words a Macedonian legal text uses for a class of court, so it stays usable for a case in any jurisdiction. Меѓународен carries ѓ. Note that Уставен суд is not a court of the judiciary at all in the Macedonian arrangement: it sits under its own chapter of the constitution and reviews laws rather than cases.
