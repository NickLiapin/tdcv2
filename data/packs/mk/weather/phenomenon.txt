THE LAST TWO ARE NAMED LOCAL WINDS AND THEY MATTER: вардарец is the cold, dry, sometimes violent north-westerly that funnels down the Vardar valley from Skopje to Gevgelija and on into Greece — it is the reason the valley is a wind-energy corridor — and југо is the warm, damp southerly that brings the change in weather and, in local belief, headaches with it. Neither has an English name; both are ordinary weather-forecast vocabulary in Macedonian.
