TWO GRAMMATICAL THINGS ARE DELIBERATE HERE AND SHOULD SURVIVE ANY EDIT. First, several sentences use the л-form past (рекол, дојдоа … паднал, бил затворен, работел, кодифицирале), which is the REPORTED or non-witnessed past — Macedonian marks whether the speaker saw the event, and news writing and retold stories take this form while a first-hand account takes the -ше past (доцнеше, стоеја, седна). Mixing the two at random in generated prose reads as though the writer keeps changing whether they were present. Second, the definite article appears as a SUFFIX and in more than one of its three forms (утрото, ѕидот, планината, ќерка си), which is what makes the text Macedonian rather than Serbian or Russian at a glance.
