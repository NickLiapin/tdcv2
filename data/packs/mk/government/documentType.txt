description: Kind of official document (Macedonian). ТАПИЈА IS THE ONE WITHOUT AN ENGLISH WORD: a Turkish loan for the title deed, still the everyday name for what the cadastre issues. The three извод entries are not three documents but one register read three ways — Macedonian keeps separate civil registers for births, marriages and deaths, and an извод is a certified extract from whichever one applies, so a Macedonian record asks which book rather than which certificate. Уверение за неосудуваност — the certificate of no criminal record — is demanded for most jobs and for many licences.
