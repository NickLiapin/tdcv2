description: Technical action as a VERBAL NOUN IN -ње. This file is where the English -ing has no Macedonian counterpart: Macedonian has no gerund and no participle that can stand as a noun, so an ongoing action is named with the -ње noun (заобиколување, "bypassing") or with a fully conjugated да-clause. Every value here therefore carries њ, one of the six letters Macedonian has and Russian does not, and that is grammar showing through rather than the list being stuffed.
