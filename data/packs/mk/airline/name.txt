THE COUNTRY HAS NO NATIONAL CARRIER AND HAS NOT HAD ONE SINCE 2009, when МАТ — Македонски авиотранспорт — went bankrupt; a later attempt, Air Macedonia, never flew commercially. Skopje and Ohrid are effectively low-cost airports today, with Wizz Air by far the largest operator, so a Macedonian aviation dataset looks nothing like a Bulgarian or Croatian one where a flag carrier still anchors the schedule. МАТ is kept in the list because it is what older Macedonian records name.
