description: Kind of event (Macedonian). THE LAST THREE HAVE NO ENGLISH EQUIVALENT. Слава is the household patron-saint day, a fixed annual feast a family keeps and to which neighbours come without invitation. Собор is the village fair on the parish saint's day, which is when emigrants come home. Панаѓур — carrying ѓ, from the Greek panegyris — is the market fair that grew out of the same thing and is now a trade event in its own right. A Macedonian event calendar without these three is missing most of its year.
