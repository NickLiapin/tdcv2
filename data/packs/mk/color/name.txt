description: Colour name (Macedonian), FEMININE SINGULAR — because the word боја ("colour") is feminine, so "red" as a colour is црвена and not the masculine црвен. A config gluing one of these to a masculine noun will produce disagreement; adjectives in Macedonian agree in gender and number, and although the language lost every noun case it kept that agreement completely. Two words worth noting: кафеава is "brown" and comes from кафе, coffee, and темносина / светлосина are single words, so Macedonian writes "dark blue" as one compound and not as two.
