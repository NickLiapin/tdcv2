description: Diagnosis (Macedonian). Macedonian clinical vocabulary transcribes the international term into Cyrillic rather than translating it, so these are recognisable to a doctor anywhere; where a native word exists it tends to be the older one (мозочен удар beside "исхемичен мозочен удар"). Ноќна апнеја carries ќ.
