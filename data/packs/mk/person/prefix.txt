description: Macedonian honorific or title, in the abbreviated form written BEFORE a name. The commonest one carries a Macedonian-only letter: г-ѓа is госпоѓа, "Mrs", and its ѓ is the reflex of Proto-Slavic *dj where Bulgarian has жд (госпожа) and Serbian has ђ — one abbreviation that tells the three languages apart. ѓакон is the same letter in a church title. Macedonian academic titles PRECEDE the name and stack (проф. д-р Марија Стојановска), which is why person/suffix.txt holds almost nothing academic. хаџи is a fossilised pilgrimage title that also froze into a whole family of surnames (Хаџиевски, Хаџиев).
