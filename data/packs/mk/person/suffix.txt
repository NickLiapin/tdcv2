description: Name suffix (Macedonian). MACEDONIAN HAS NO Jr / Sr CONVENTION AT ALL, so the roman numerals here number MONARCHS and never a son who shares his father's name — Филип II Македонски, Александар III Македонски, Самоил. A Macedonian son with his father's name is told apart by the patronymic middle name instead. Academic titles come BEFORE the name in Macedonian (проф. д-р Иван Петровски), so what genuinely trails a name is only a doctoral abbreviation or an international degree carried over untranslated.
