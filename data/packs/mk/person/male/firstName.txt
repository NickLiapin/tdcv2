description: Macedonian men's given names, commonest first. Written in the 31-letter MACEDONIAN Cyrillic alphabet, which is neither the Russian nor the Serbian one: Ѓорѓи, Ѓоко, Ѓорче, Ѓурѓе and Ванѓел carry ѓ where Serbian writes ђ and Russian has no such letter, and Љупчо, Љубе, Љубен, Љубомир and Вељан carry љ. There is no ё, ы, э, ъ, ь, й, щ, ю or я anywhere in Macedonian, and a list containing them is Russian text under a Macedonian label. TWO SHAPES OF NAME SIT HERE SIDE BY SIDE and both are ordinary legal names: the full church form (Димитар, Александар, Костадин, Методија) and the short native form (Диме, Ацо, Коле, Тале, Пеце, Трпе, Боне), which goes on an identity card in its own right and is not a nickname — person/nickname.txt is a different thing. Names ending in -е and -о are that second type. THE WEIGHTS ARE A FITTED rank-decay curve (rank^-0.45), NOT measured bearer counts: no public register of Macedonian given-name frequencies is published. The exponent puts the leading name near its estimated real share of about 3%. Ферид, Џевдет and Шабан are Macedonian citizens' names from the Turkish and Albanian communities, in the Cyrillic spelling the Macedonian civil register uses, because this pack is in Macedonian only.
