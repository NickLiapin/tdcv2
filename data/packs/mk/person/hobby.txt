description: Hobby or pastime (Macedonian). MOST OF THESE ARE VERBAL NOUNS IN -ње, which is how Macedonian names an activity — читање, готвење, пливање, планинарење, сликање, танцување, пеење, набљудување — and the suffix carries the letter њ, one of the six Macedonian has and Russian does not. That single ending is why њ is common in this pack while ѕ is rare: it is productive, so any verb can produce one. Note that Macedonian has NO INFINITIVE, so this -ње noun is the only way to name an activity as a thing; a language with an infinitive would use that instead, which is why the English list reads "Reading, Cooking" and this one cannot be a word-for-word map of it.
