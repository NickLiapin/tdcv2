description: Nationality / demonym (Macedonian), masculine singular NOUN. MACEDONIAN BUILDS DEMONYMS WITH -ец, -анец, -ѓанец, -јанец AND -ќанин, and the last two are where the language shows itself: Канаѓанец, Холанѓанец, Швеѓанец, Шкотланѓанец and Новозеланѓанец all carry ѓ, and Египќанин carries ќ, both from the same Proto-Slavic sounds (*dj and *tj) that Bulgarian turned into жд and шт and Serbian into ђ and ћ. Азербејџанец carries џ and Полјак carries љ. These are NOUNS, not adjectives — a Macedonian is еден Македонец — and the feminine is a separate word in -ка or -анка (Македонка, Србинка, Гркинка), so this list cannot double as an adjective list; person/language.txt holds the adjectives.
