description: Macedonian women's given names, commonest first. The Macedonian-only letters are here inside real names rather than as decoration: Снежана, Соња, Дуња and Тања carry њ; Љубица, Љупка, Љубинка, Лилјана and Смиља carry љ; Ѓурѓица, Ѓурѓа, Ѓурѓина, Анѓела, Ванѓа, Ванѓелија and Ѓоргина carry ѓ. Macedonian has no ё, ы, э, ъ, ь, й, щ, ю or я. TWO FAMILIES OF NAME sit here together: the international and church stock (Марија, Елена, Софија, Катерина, Ана) and the native Slavic stock built on transparent everyday words, which a speaker hears as words and not as names — Цвета is 'blossom', Искра is 'spark', Снежана is 'snow', Убавка is 'beauty', Ружа and Трендафилка are both 'rose', Дивна is 'wonderful', Македонка is the country itself. Names in -ка and -ица have a diminutive SHAPE but are full legal names and not pet forms. THE WEIGHTS ARE A FITTED rank-decay curve (rank^-0.45), NOT measured bearer counts; no public register of Macedonian given-name frequencies is published. Џенета and Шефика are Macedonian citizens' names from the Turkish and Bosniak communities, in the Cyrillic spelling the civil register uses.
