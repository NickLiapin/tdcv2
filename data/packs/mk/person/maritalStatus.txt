description: Marital status (Macedonian). THE WORD FOR "MARRIED" IS DIFFERENT FOR A MAN AND FOR A WOMAN, and not by an ending but by a different root: a man is женет (from жена, "he has taken a wife") and a woman is мажена (from маж, "she has been given a husband"). There is no single word covering both, so a Macedonian form either asks the gender first or writes both. Same for the negative — неженет and немажена — and for widowhood, вдовец and вдовица. вонбрачна заедница is the registered unmarried partnership.
