description: Common Macedonian adjectives in the MASCULINE SINGULAR INDEFINITE form. An adjective agrees with its noun in gender and number (добар / добра / добро / добри) and takes the same three definite articles the noun does (добриот / добриов / добрион), so a config joining an adjective to a noun must match the gender itself — the pack cannot do it, because these lists are drawn independently. Macedonian has NO CASES, so agreement stops at gender and number and there is nothing further to decline. Note the -ол and -ов stems: топол becomes топла, светол becomes светла — the vowel drops in every form except this one, so the citation form here is the odd one out and not the stem. среќен carries ќ, туѓ carries ѓ, љубезен carries љ.
locale: mk
