description: Common Macedonian verbs — cited in the THIRD PERSON SINGULAR PRESENT, because MACEDONIAN HAS NO INFINITIVE AT ALL. It lost it, as Bulgarian and Greek did, and replaced it with a да-clause: English "he wants to work" is тој сака да работи, literally "he wants that he works", with the second verb fully conjugated. So a Macedonian dictionary headword is работи, not a form corresponding to "to work", and this list is what a dictionary would print. A CONFIG CANNOT TREAT THESE AS INFINITIVES: gluing работи after сака produces nonsense; the да must go between. THE OTHER THING A CONFIG CANNOT SEE is that Macedonian marks EVIDENTIALITY in the past. There are two past tenses and choosing between them is not stylistic: работеше is the witnessed past, "he worked and I saw it", while работел is the reported or inferred past, "he worked, so I am told or so it seems". News writing, retold stories and anything second-hand take the л-form; a first-hand account takes the other. A generated Macedonian narrative that mixes them at random reads as though the writer keeps changing whether they were there. Macedonian-only letters live in very ordinary verbs: доаѓа, наоѓа, поаѓа and раѓа carry ѓ, and плаќа, враќа, испраќа, сеќава and среќава carry ќ.
