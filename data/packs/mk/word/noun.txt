description: Common Macedonian nouns, in the BARE FORM WITH NO DEFINITE ARTICLE — and that choice is the single most important thing to know about every noun list in this pack. MACEDONIAN PUTS ITS DEFINITE ARTICLE ON THE END OF THE WORD, and it has THREE of them, not one: -от is neutral (градот, "the city"), -ов is proximal, the city here by me (градов), and -он is distal, the city over there, out of sight (градон). Feminine and neuter and plural have the same three-way split (-та/-ва/-на, -то/-во/-но, -те/-ве/-не). No other standard Slavic language has the three-way set — Bulgarian has one article and Serbian has none — and it is the plainest single test of whether a text is Macedonian. THE CONSEQUENCE FOR A CONFIG: a value here is indefinite, so joining it to anything is safe, but a config that needs "the city" must add the suffix itself and must choose which of the three it means. Macedonian also has NO NOUN CASES AT ALL, so there is no genitive, dative or instrumental to worry about — a noun has exactly two forms, singular and plural, times the three articles. Macedonian-only letters appear here in ordinary words: луѓе (people) and ѓубре carry ѓ, куќа and ноќ and моќ carry ќ, ѕид and ѕвезда carry ѕ, коњ and прашање carry њ, љубов carries љ, џеб carries џ. Note also the apostrophe in 'рж (rye): it marks a syllabic р at the start of a word, an orthographic device Macedonian has and its neighbours do not.
locale: mk
