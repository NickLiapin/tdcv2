description: Cooking ingredient (Macedonian). Шеќер (sugar) and спанаќ (spinach) carry ќ and are as everyday as a word gets. Кашкавал is the hard yellow sheep or cow cheese of the whole region and is not interchangeable with сирење, the white brined one; a Macedonian recipe naming one will not accept the other. Ајвар appears as an ingredient and not only as a dish, because it is used the way a Frenchman uses tomato purée.
