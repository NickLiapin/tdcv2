description: Vegetable (Macedonian). Модар патлиџан (aubergine) carries џ, кељ (kale) carries љ, and спанаќ and леќа carry ќ — four Macedonian-only letters in one short vegetable list, and none of them forced. Пиперка is the single most important vegetable in the country: North Macedonia grows peppers on an industrial scale for ajvar, and a Macedonian recipe that says "пиперка" without qualification means the long red ротунда or куртовска капија, not a bell pepper.
