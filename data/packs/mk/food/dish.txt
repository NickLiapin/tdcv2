description: Dish (Macedonian). THE FIRST DOZEN ARE THE MACEDONIAN TABLE and they are not translations of anything. Тавче гравче — beans baked in an earthenware pan with red pepper — is the national dish. Ајвар is the roasted-pepper relish every household still makes by the sackful in September, and пинџур is its rougher cousin with tomato and aubergine. Пастрмајлија is the boat-shaped flatbread with cubed pork from Štip, and it has its own annual festival. Ѓомлезе, from the Mavrovo villages, is a layered pastry baked in a šač. Ѓомлезе and ѓувеч carry ѓ; ќебапи, ќофтиња and ќофте carry ќ; пинџур carries џ.
