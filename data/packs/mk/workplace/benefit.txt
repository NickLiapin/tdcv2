description: Employment benefit (Macedonian). К-15 HAS NO WESTERN EQUIVALENT: it is the annual holiday allowance, a lump sum paid before the summer break, and the name is the number of the old Yugoslav pay-slip line it sat on — a Macedonian employee asks whether an offer includes К-15 the way an American asks about the 401(k) match. Топол оброк is the meal allowance, also a separate payroll line rather than a canteen. Осигурување carries њ.
