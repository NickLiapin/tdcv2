description: Job title (Macedonian). ЅИДАР — the mason — CARRIES Ѕ, the letter no other living Slavic Cyrillic alphabet uses, and it is one of the very few everyday words that does; лифтџија and оџачар carry џ. Six trades on this list are Macedonian in a way an English list cannot be: резбар is the woodcarver of the Mijak school, whose iconostases at Sveti Jovan Bigorski and Sveti Spas took years each; иконописец is the icon painter; лозар and тутунопроизводител are the vine-grower of Tikveš and the tobacco grower of Prilep, two of the country's defining occupations; шивач is the garment worker, of whom there are tens of thousands; and печалбар — not listed here because it is a status and not a trade — is the emigrant labourer whose remittances are a tenth of the economy.
