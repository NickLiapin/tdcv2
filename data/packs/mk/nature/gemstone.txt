description: Gemstone (Macedonian). Охридски бисер is a Macedonian product rather than a mineral: it is made in Ohrid from an emulsion of the scales of the endemic плашица fish over a mother-of-pearl bead, by two families who have kept the method secret since the nineteenth century, and it is what a Macedonian means by "pearl".
