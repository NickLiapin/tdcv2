description: Musical instrument (Macedonian). THE SECOND HALF IS THE MACEDONIAN FOLK SET and it is not a museum list: гајда (bagpipe), зурла (a piercing double-reed shawm), тапан (the double-headed drum slung from the shoulder), кавал and шупелка (end-blown flutes), тамбура (long-necked lute) and дајре (frame drum) are played at weddings and at every village собор. Зурла and тапан always come as a pair, two zurlas against one drum, and that pair is the sound of a Macedonian wedding procession. Банџо carries џ.
