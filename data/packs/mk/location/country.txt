description: Country and territory names in Macedonian. TRANSLATED, not transliterated — Германија, Унгарија, Грција, Романија, Холандија and Соединети Американски Држави are Macedonian words with their own history, not English ones respelled. Северна Македонија is the country's constitutional name since 12 February 2019. Note the neighbours: Србија, Бугарија, Грција, Албанија and Косово are all directly adjacent, which is why their Macedonian names are old and short rather than borrowed. Абуџа, Џибути and Азербејџан carry џ; Нов Зеланд and Њу Делхи show that Macedonian renders "New" sometimes as Нов and sometimes as Њу depending on whether the name was translated or borrowed whole.
