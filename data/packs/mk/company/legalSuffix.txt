description: Company legal form used in North Macedonia, written AFTER the name (Алкалоид АД, Пекара Жито ДООЕЛ), plus the trade-name elements that go in the same slot. ДООЕЛ HAS NO ENGLISH EQUIVALENT and is the commonest form for a small Macedonian business: друштво со ограничена одговорност основано од едно лице, a limited company founded by ONE person, distinguished in law from ДОО, which has several. ТП is the sole trader, whose registered name legally contains the owner's own name. North Macedonia is the only country where Macedonian is official, so these are the only forms the language needs — unlike the Spanish or Portuguese packs, which must stay country-neutral.
locale: mk
