THE TURKISH LAYER IS WHERE MACEDONIAN'S OWN LETTERS CONCENTRATE IN A KITCHEN, and it is a genuine structural fact rather than a curiosity: ѓезве (the long-handled coffee pot), филџан (the cup it is poured into), ќебе (blanket), тенџере (stew pot), фотелја (armchair), роштиљ (grill) and чекмеџе (drawer) are all fully naturalised Turkish loans carrying ѓ, џ, ќ or љ, and they are the ordinary words — there is no Slavic synonym in use for most of them. Ѓубриво (fertiliser) and свеќа (candle) are native words with the same letters. глушец is the computer mouse and also the animal.
