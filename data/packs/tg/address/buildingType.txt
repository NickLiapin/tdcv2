ҲАВЛӢ IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT TRANSLATE: it is a walled family compound with an interior courtyard, the normal form of a Tajik house outside the Soviet-built blocks, and it is a building type rather than a garden — rendering it as 'yard' loses what it is. Similarly чойхона, the teahouse, is a civic building and not a restaurant, and мадраса is a religious college, not a school.
