description: Trailing element of a Tajik place name (Файзобод, Панҷакент, Қалъаихумб, Сангвор). The dominant one by far is -ОБОД, Persian for 'made prosperous, settled', which ends more Tajik towns than every other element together and runs across the whole Persian world from Islamabad to Hyderabad. The rest are landscape and settlement words: -дара a valley, -рӯд a river, -чашма a spring, -кӯҳ a mountain, -боғ a garden, -зор 'a place full of', -қалъа a fort, -деҳ a village. TWO ARE NOT PERSIAN AND MARK THE OLDER SOGDIAN LAYER: -кент and -канд, town, which is what Панҷакент and Самарқанд are built from, and which predate the Persian of the region.
locale: tg
