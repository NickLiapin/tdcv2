description: Leading element of a Tajik place name (Нави Ҳисор, Сарихосор, Сафедобод). Almost all are Persian adjectives or numerals that stand in front of the noun WITHOUT the izofat — this is the one position where a Tajik modifier precedes its head, and it is why Сафедобод is one word while 'the white town' as a phrase would be шаҳри сафед with the izofat and the reverse order. The colour words carry landscape senses: сафед, white, is clear water or snow; сиёҳ and қаро, black, are bare rock or a spring-fed source; сурх, red, is iron-stained earth. Combine with a citySuffix; on its own an entry here names nothing.
