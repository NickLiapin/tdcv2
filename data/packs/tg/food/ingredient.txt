description: Cooking ingredient as Tajik names it. TWO ENTRIES CARRY THE CUISINE. ДУНБА is the fat from a fat-tailed sheep's tail and is the traditional cooking fat — rendered in the pot before anything else goes in — so a Tajik recipe that starts with vegetable oil has already been modernised. БИРИНҶИ ДЕВЗИРА is the short reddish rice grown in the Zarafshon valley that a proper plov is made with; ordinary биринҷ is not a substitute. Note that равғани пахта, cottonseed oil, is the everyday frying fat across Central Asia and is not a health-food curiosity here. ЧАККА, ҶУРҒОТ and ҚУРУТ are three different dairy products along one line: yogurt, strained yogurt and dried yogurt.
