description: Dish as Tajik names it. THE NATIONAL DISH IS ОШИ ПАЛАВ and the word order is the point: ош, the meal, takes the izofat and палав follows, so it is 'the meal of pilaf'. ҚУРУТОБ HAS NO TRANSLATION — flaky фатир bread torn into a wooden тавақ, drowned in қурут (reconstituted dried yogurt) and topped with fried onion, eaten with the hands from a shared bowl; it is the dish Tajikistan is best known for and 'yogurt bread salad' is not it. Note also that САМБӮСА is baked on the wall of a танӯр, not fried, and that СУМАЛАК is made once a year at Наврӯз from sprouted wheat, stirred overnight by a group of women, and is a ritual before it is a food.
