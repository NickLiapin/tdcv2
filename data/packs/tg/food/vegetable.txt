description: Vegetable as Tajik names it. Several entries have no single English word: КАБУДӢ is the bundle of mixed fresh herbs that comes with every meal and is treated as a vegetable, not a garnish; МОШ is the mung bean and is a staple grain-legume here rather than a health-food import. Note that картошка and помидор are Russian loans that entirely displaced any older word, while the older Persian names survive for everything that grew here first — пиёз, сабзӣ, шалғам, каду, лӯбиё, нахӯд.
