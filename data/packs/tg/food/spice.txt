ЗИРА COMES FIRST BECAUSE IT DEFINES THE FOOD: black cumin is what makes a Tajik plov smell like a Tajik plov, and no substitution reads as authentic. ЗИРК, dried barberry, goes into the same pot for sourness. ҚУРУТ is the hardest one for a translator — dried salted yogurt balls, dissolved in water to make a sauce, at once a dairy product and a seasoning. ЗАЪФАРОН and ҲИЛ arrived along the same routes as the language itself and are Persian words English also borrowed, as saffron and cardamom.
