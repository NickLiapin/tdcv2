description: Nationality or ethnicity as Tajik names it — the миллат line of a Soviet-era internal passport, which Tajikistan kept as a census category. The word is a BARE NOUN here (тоҷик, ӯзбек, рус), not the -ӣ adjective used for a language, so тоҷик is a Tajik person and тоҷикӣ is the Tajik language. The Pamiri peoples of Badakhshan — помирӣ, шуғнонӣ, рушонӣ, вахонӣ, язгуломӣ — are counted as Tajiks in the national census and are listed here because they name themselves separately; the Яғнобӣ speak the last living descendant of Sogdian.
