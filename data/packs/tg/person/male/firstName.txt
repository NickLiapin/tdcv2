THE STOCK IS PERSIAN AND ISLAMIC, the same stock an Iranian or an Afghan draws on, written in Cyrillic: Фарҳод, Рустам, Ҷамшед and Сӯҳроб come straight out of the Шоҳнома, and Муҳаммад, Абдулло, Юсуф and Ҳасан out of Islam. A large minority are Arabic compounds with Абд- (servant of) or -улло / -уллоҳ (of God) — Абдураҳмон, Файзулло, Насрулло — and another set are -иддин (of the faith) compounds: Нуриддин, Сироҷиддин, Шамсиддин. TAJIK GIVEN NAMES ARE STRICTLY GENDERED and this list has essentially NO overlap with the female one, so a config must pick the file by sex rather than draw from a shared pool. Weights are rank-derived Zipf, not measured: Tajikistan's civil registry publishes no name-frequency table, so no count here is a real count.
