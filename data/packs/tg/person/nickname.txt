description: Tajik nickname or familiar short form. Tajik shortens a name by CLIPPING IT AND ADDING A DIMINUTIVE, most often -ҷон (dear soul), -бой, -хон or -ак: Муҳаммад becomes Маҳмадҷон, Карим becomes Каримҷон, Гулнора becomes Гулҷон. -ҷон is by far the commonest and attaches to almost any name, male or female, which is why so many entries here end in it. -ак is affectionate on a child and can be rude on an adult. The bare clipped stems (Мурод, Рустам) are also used alone among friends.
locale: tg
