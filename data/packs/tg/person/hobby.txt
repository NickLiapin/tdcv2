description: Pastime as Tajik names it. MOST ENTRIES ARE VERBAL NOUNS ENDING IN -Ӣ, which is how Tajik turns an activity into a noun: шоҳмотбозӣ is chess-playing, моҳигирӣ is fish-taking, кӯҳнавардӣ is mountain-walking, дуторнавозӣ is dutar-playing. A config cannot build these by putting a Tajik verb next to an English gerund. Several are specific to the region rather than translated in: бузкашӣ, the mounted goat-carcass game, and гӯштингирӣ, the belt wrestling, are the two big traditional sports; кабӯтарбозӣ, keeping and flying pigeons, is a real urban hobby; and дуторнавозӣ, рубобнавозӣ and доиранавозӣ name the three instruments an amateur is most likely to own.
locale: tg
