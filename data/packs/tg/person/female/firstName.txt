description: Tajik female given name. Persian and Islamic like the male stock, but the productive elements differ: Гул- (flower) heads a whole family — Гулнора, Гулбаҳор, Гулрухсор, Гулчеҳра — and -нисо (woman), -бону and -мо / -хон are female-only endings. Дил- (heart) is the other big head: Дилбар, Дилором, Дилафрӯз. Nature words stand alone as names in a way they do not for men: Ситора a star, Лола a tulip, Наргис a narcissus, Шабнам dew. NO OVERLAP WITH THE MALE LIST — Tajik given names are strictly gendered. Weights are rank-derived Zipf, not measured; Tajikistan publishes no frequency table.
