description: Marketing verb in Tajik. THESE ARE INFINITIVES ENDING IN -ИДАН or -АН and many are COMPOUND VERBS — a noun plus кардан 'to do' or намудан — which is how Tajik makes most of its verbs: беҳтар кардан 'to improve', ҷорӣ намудан 'to introduce'. A config cannot conjugate these by changing the ending alone; the light verb at the end is the part that inflects.
locale: tg
