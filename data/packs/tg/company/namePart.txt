description: Element a Tajik company name is built from. Most Tajik trade names are a single evocative Persian noun or a Persian-Arabic abstract — Ориён, Сомон, Баракат, Файз, Умед, Ваҳдат — often the name of a dynasty, a river, a mountain or a virtue. The geographic ones double as company names because they carry the country's history: Сомон and Сомониён for the Samanid dynasty Tajikistan takes as its founding state, Ориён for the Aryan heritage claimed with it, Мовароуннаҳр for the classical name of the land between the two rivers. Combine one of these with a legalSuffix and the guillemets.
