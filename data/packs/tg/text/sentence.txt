Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Tajik book at all — a query for language code tg returns "No records found", so no public-domain corpus exists and data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs has nothing to read. The sentences are ordinary declarative Tajik in SUBJECT-OBJECT-VERB order, which is the neutral Tajik order and NOT the subject-verb-object of the European locales: the verb is last in every sentence here, so a config splicing a Tajik sentence from parts cannot use an English template. The direct object is marked with the postposed -ро (китобро хондам) and the IZOFAT links every noun to its modifier, so the grammar is carried by endings that follow the word rather than by prepositions in front of it.
