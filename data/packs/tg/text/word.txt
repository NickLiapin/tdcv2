description: Filler word list in Tajik. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Tajik book at all — a query for language code tg returns "No records found", so no public-domain corpus exists and data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs has nothing to read. The words are ordinary Tajik vocabulary spread across the everyday semantic fields, and ALL SIX letters that Tajik has and Russian does not — ғ ӣ қ ӯ ҳ ҷ — occur throughout, which is what makes this filler Tajik rather than transliterated Russian. Note the converse too: ы, ц, щ and ь do not appear anywhere, because the 1998 reform removed them from the alphabet.
