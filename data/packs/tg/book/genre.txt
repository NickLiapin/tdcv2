ДЕВОН and МАСНАВӢ are not decorative additions: a девон is the collected lyric verse of one poet, arranged by rhyme letter, and a маснавӣ is a long narrative poem in rhyming couplets — the two dominant book forms of a thousand years of Persian literature, and both are still ordinary shelf categories in a Tajik bookshop. РУБОӢ, the quatrain, is the third.
