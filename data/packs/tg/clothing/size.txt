The numeric run 42-58 is the post-Soviet ГОСТ sizing inherited from the USSR and still what a market trader and a domestic factory quote; the letter run arrived with imported clothing and is what a mall or an online shop shows. They are NOT interchangeable — a ГОСТ 48 is roughly an M, not a European 48 — so a config must not convert between them arithmetically.
