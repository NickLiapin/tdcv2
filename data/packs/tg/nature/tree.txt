description: Tree as Tajik names it. ЧИНОР, the oriental plane, is the emblematic tree — planted in every village square and beside every shrine, and old enough in some places to be a landmark in its own right. АРЧА, the Zarafshon juniper, is the tree of the high mountains and its slow-growing groves are protected. ТУТ, the mulberry, is planted for the silkworm as much as for the fruit, and its dried berries are a winter food. САФЕДОР, the poplar, lines every irrigation canal and is the standard building timber.
