description: Gemstone as Tajik names it. TWO OF THESE ARE BADAKHSHAN'S OWN AND ARE NOT ORDINARY LOANWORDS. ЛОҶУВАРД, lapis lazuli, was mined at Sar-i Sang in the Badakhshan mountains for six thousand years and supplied Egypt, Mesopotamia and the Renaissance with ultramarine; the English azure and the Russian лазурь both descend from this word. ЛАЪЛ is the Badakhshan spinel that Europe called the Balas ruby and set in crowns, believing it a ruby. ФИРӮЗА, turquoise, is the Persian word behind the French pierre turquoise and so behind the English name of the colour.
