description: Flower as Tajik names it. THE TULIP IS NATIVE HERE, not Dutch: лола grows wild on the Tajik foothills, the garden tulip was bred from Central Asian species, and the word is used for a red flower generally as well as for the tulip. САДБАРГ, literally 'hundred-petal', is the rose; the Persian rose-and-nightingale pairing behind so much poetry is садбарг and булбул. Several entries are the blossom of a fruit tree named as a flower in its own right — гули бодом, гули себ, гули анор — which is how a Tajik spring is described.
