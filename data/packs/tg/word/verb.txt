description: Common Tajik verb, in the infinitive. TAJIK VERBS END IN -ДАН OR -ТАН and the verb comes LAST in the sentence — Tajik is subject-object-verb, not the subject-verb-object of the European locales — so a config splicing a Tajik clause together from parts cannot use an English word-order template. Many everyday meanings are expressed by a COMPOUND of noun plus кардан or шудан rather than by a simple verb.
