description: Sport as Tajik names it. THE TWO AT THE HEAD ARE THE COUNTRY'S OWN. ГӮШТИНГИРӢ is Tajik belt wrestling, contested in a cotton jacket with a sash, and is the national sport with its own federation and televised championships — not a folk pastime. БУЗКАШӢ is the mounted game in which riders fight for a headless goat carcass; it is played across the Khatlon and Rasht valleys, particularly around Наврӯз, and there is no English word for it. Weightlifting and judo follow because they are where Tajikistan wins international medals.
