description: Colour as Tajik names it. MOST TAJIK COLOUR WORDS BEYOND THE BASIC SEVEN ARE BUILT FROM AN OBJECT PLUS -Ӣ, 'the colour of that thing': анорӣ is pomegranate-red, гулобӣ rose-pink, пистагӣ pistachio-green, шафтолуӣ peach, лимӯӣ lemon, заъфаронӣ saffron, қаҳваранг coffee-coloured. TWO OF THESE ARE THE SOURCE OF ENGLISH WORDS: хокӣ, from хок 'dust', is where khaki comes from, and фирӯзӣ, from the turquoise stone, is behind the English colour name. So a config CANNOT translate an English colour list into Tajik word for word and expect the reverse to hold — the borrowing already went the other way. Basic colours are the seven at the head: сафед, сиёҳ, сурх, кабуд, сабз, зард, хокистарӣ.
