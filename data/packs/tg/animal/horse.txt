description: Horse breed. ЛОҚАЙ IS TAJIKISTAN'S OWN BREED, named for the Loqai people of the Khatlon valleys — a small tough mountain horse, the one бузкашӣ is played on. АХАЛТАКА, the Akhal-Teke, is the neighbouring Turkmen breed and the most famous horse of Central Asia; ҚАРАБОИР comes from the Uzbek side. The Soviet breeds (Донӣ, Будённӣ, Орлов) arrived with the studs of the 1930s.
