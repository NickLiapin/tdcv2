description: Dog breed. TWO OF THESE ARE THE REGION'S OWN AND HEAD THE LIST. САГИ ГУРГГИР, known everywhere as the алабай, is the Central Asian shepherd — a livestock guardian bred for centuries to fight wolves, not a pet, and the largest dog anyone here keeps. ТОЗӢ is the Central Asian sighthound, coursed after hare and gazelle. The rest are international breeds under adapted names.
