description: Bird as Tajik names it. БУЛБУЛ, the nightingale, is not an ordinary bird name in this language — it is the central image of a thousand years of Persian poetry, paired with the rose, and a Tajik reader meets the word in a verse before meeting it in a field. КАБК, the chukar partridge, is kept in cages and matched in singing contests. Birds of prey (уқоб, боз, шоҳин, қаргас) are mountain species and are well distinguished in the vocabulary.
