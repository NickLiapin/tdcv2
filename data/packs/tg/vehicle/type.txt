description: Kind of vehicle in Tajik. МОШИНИ МАРШРУТӢ, the shared minibus running a fixed route and leaving when full, is the backbone of urban and intercity transport here and is a vehicle category in its own right, not a bus. Note also that ХАР and АСП are still working transport in the mountain districts where the roads do not reach, which is why they are on a vehicle list at all.
