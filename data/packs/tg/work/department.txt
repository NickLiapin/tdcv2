description: Department inside a Tajik organisation. ШӮЪБА is the ordinary word for a department and takes the IZOFAT before what it is a department of — шӯъбаи кадрҳо, 'the department of personnel' — so every multiword entry here is one phrase and must not be split. Note that the personnel department is шӯъбаи кадрҳо, from the Russian кадры, and that this project's category for such lists is `workplace` and never `hr`, which collides with the Croatian locale code.
locale: tg
