description: Seniority level. The Tajik ladder is the Soviet one and its rungs are not the English ones: КОРОМӮЗ is a trainee, then the plain title, then КАЛОН ('senior', placed AFTER the noun — муҳандиси калон), then ПЕШБАР and САРВАР. The academic and research track runs separately through ходими илмӣ and its own grades. МУОВИН is a deputy and always precedes what it deputises for.
