description: Job title in Tajik. HOW THE WORDS ARE BUILT IS THE USEFUL PART: Tajik makes an agent noun with a handful of productive endings and a config that knows them can read any title it has never seen. -ГАР is one who works a material (заргар a jeweller, кулолгар a potter, оҳангар a smith); -ЧӢ one who operates a thing (тракторчӣ, кранчӣ, гумрукчӣ); -КОР one who works a crop or field (пахтакор a cotton grower, бинокор a builder, зироаткор a crop farmer); -ДОР one who holds or keeps (боғдор, анбордор, чорводор); -ШИНОС one who knows a subject (забоншинос a linguist, ҳуқуқшинос a lawyer, таърихшинос a historian); -СОЗ one who makes (барномасоз a programmer, соатсоз a watchmaker). English -er covers all six. Technology titles are the exception and arrive as Russian or English loans, so менеҷер and дизайнер sit beside дуредгар without embarrassment. Several occupations here are specific to the country's economy: ПАХТАКОР and ПИЛЛАПАРВАР, cotton growing and silkworm rearing, are major rural employments, and ЧӮПОН, a shepherd in the high pastures, is a real full-time job rather than a rustic image.
