description: Kind of state body as Tajik names it. THESE ARE TAJIKISTAN'S, named as such, because Tajik is spoken as a state language in one country and the words mean that country's institutions. The hierarchy of the words is worth knowing: ВАЗОРАТ is a ministry, КУМИТА a state committee ranking just below one, АГЕНТӢ and ХАДАМОТ are agencies and services under a ministry, and МАҚОМОТИ ИҶРОИЯИ ҲОКИМИЯТИ ДАВЛАТӢ is the formal name for a provincial or district administration, headed by a раис appointed from above.
locale: tg
