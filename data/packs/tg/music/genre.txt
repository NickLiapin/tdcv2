description: Music genre in Tajik. TWO ENTRIES ARE THE REAL TAJIK CLASSICAL TRADITION and must not be read as folk labels. ШАШМАҚОМ is the six-cycle suite repertoire of Bukhara and Samarkand, a courtly art music with a fixed sequence of modes and classical Persian poetry as its text, inscribed on the UNESCO intangible heritage list; performing one мақом in full takes hours. ФАЛАК is the vocal genre of the mountain districts — a high, open, lamenting solo line over sparse accompaniment — and is the sound most Tajiks would name as their own. The international genres keep their loan names.
