description: Generic drug name as a Tajik prescription writes it. THE SPELLING IS THE INTERNATIONAL NONPROPRIETARY NAME TRANSLITERATED THROUGH RUSSIAN, which is what a Tajik pharmacy label and a doctor's script both show; there is no separate Persian pharmaceutical nomenclature in use. The anti-tuberculosis block (изониазид, рифампитсин, пиразинамид, этамбутол) is at the head because TB treatment is a major and centrally supplied part of Tajik prescribing. NOTE THE ТС: Tajik has no ц — the 1998 reform struck it out — so every Russian -цин comes out -тсин and цефтриаксон is written сефтриаксон.
locale: tg
