description: Blood group. THE POST-SOVIET NOTATION IS THE FIRST EIGHT and is what a Tajik clinic writes: the group is given as a Roman numeral with the antigens in brackets, I(0), II(A), III(B), IV(AB), followed by Rh+ or Rh−. The plain international forms are also here because imported forms and laboratory printouts use them. A config must not treat II(A) and A+ as different groups: they are the same blood in two notations.
