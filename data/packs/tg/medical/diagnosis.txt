description: Diagnosis in Tajik clinical wording. THE HEAD OF THIS LIST IS TAJIKISTAN'S EPIDEMIOLOGY, NOT EUROPE'S. СИЛ, tuberculosis, is a leading notifiable disease with a serious drug-resistant share and belongs at the top of any Tajik clinical dataset. ЭХИНОКОККОЗ and БРУСЕЛЛЁЗ come from close daily contact with sheep and cattle and are ordinary rural diagnoses here, not tropical curiosities. ЛЕЙШМАНИОЗ is endemic in the southern lowlands. НОРАСОИИ ЙОД and РАХИТ are public-health conditions of the mountain districts, not historical ones. ВАРАҶА, malaria, is on the list because it was endemic within living memory and Tajikistan was certified malaria-free only in 2017, so a historical series will contain it and a current one should not. Vocabulary note: the everyday disease names are Persian (сил, вабо, домана, зуком, камхунӣ, нафастангӣ) while the technical ones arrive through Russian in their international form, and both appear on the same chart.
