description: Female given names, carrying the same double system as the male list — Marit and Máret, Ellen and Elle, Berit and Biret, Kirsten and Risten, Ragnhild and Rávdná — where the Nordic form is the register spelling and the Sami form is what the woman is called. Note that Sami puts no gender into its grammar at all, so unlike Polish or Czech there is no feminine surname ending to derive and this pack ships no separate female surname list: a woman draws from person/lastName exactly as a man does. The compound names are again the largest single group. Elle-Máret, Ánne-Máret, Inger-Máret and Máret-Ánne are ordinary rather than elaborate, and the elements are recycled deliberately, since naming a daughter after a grandmother is the normal expectation and not a gesture. Sunná is the one modern entry worth pointing at: it is a Sami word rather than a christened name, part of a deliberate turn since the 1970s towards names that are Sami in origin instead of Sami in spelling. THE WEIGHTS ARE AN ORDERING, NOT MEASURED SHARES; no census counts Sami given names, and the ranks record observed frequency in Sami-language sources.
