Russia holds the fourth part of Sápmi, the Kola Peninsula, where Kildin Sami is spoken and Ter and Akkala Sami have effectively died out. The border at Sør-Varanger is the only land border Norway has with Russia and it has been crossed for trade for centuries — the Pomor trade brought Russian flour and grain north in exchange for fish, and left a pidgin, Russenorsk, behind it. The Skolt Sami, whose surnames appear in this pack's surname list, are Orthodox and were baptised in the Russian church, which is why a Russian entry belongs in a Sami cuisine list at all rather than only as a foreign restaurant.
