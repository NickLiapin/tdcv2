description: Fruit, and the first six entries are the only ones that grow here. luomi is the cloudberry, the amber-coloured bog berry that is the great gathered crop of the north — picked in August, worth real money, and surrounded by more rules than any other wild food: in parts of Finnmark the picking grounds attach to households, and in Finland foreigners were long barred from picking them in the northernmost municipalities. sarrit is the bilberry, jokŋa the lingonberry that keeps all winter without preservation because it is acid enough to keep itself, čáhppesmuorji the crowberry that carpets the plateau. These matter nutritionally in a place with no vegetable season worth the name: berries were the vitamin C, and scurvy was a real risk in the households that stopped eating them. Everything after the berries is imported, arrives by road or plane, and is written in Sami spelling — eaple an apple, peara a pear, appelsiidna an orange — with the ones that arrived too recently to be worn into shape left in Norwegian.
