description: Jobs in tourism, the child of work.industryCoherent value Mátkeealáhus. The fastest-growing industry in the Sami north and the most argued over. Winter tourism sells the northern lights, the snow, the dogs and, unavoidably, the Sami themselves — and the difference between a Sami family running its own operation and a southern company hiring actors in rented gákti to sell a joik to a bus is the whole of the argument. Sámi Duodji's certification mark and the Sami tourism association's guidelines exist for that reason. The practical friction is land: snowmobile safari routes and hotel construction cross calving grounds and winter pasture, and a herd disturbed at the wrong week loses calves.
