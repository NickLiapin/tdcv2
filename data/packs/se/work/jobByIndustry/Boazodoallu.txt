Almost none of these is a job in the sense of a wage: a boazodoalli is a licence holder in a siida, paid by what the autumn slaughter brings and by predator compensation, and the household works together without anyone being anyone's employee. siidaisit, the head of a siida, and orohathoavda, the elected chair of a grazing district, are positions with real authority over pasture, migration timing and who may join, and the second one has legal standing in Norwegian law. miessemerkejeaddji names the July work: every calf is caught and its owner's mark cut into its ears, a mark registered to a person and often inherited, so a herd of thousands is in fact many individuals' animals grazing together. skohterdivvár, snowmobile mechanic, is on this list because a broken machine forty kilometres out on the plateau in February is not an inconvenience.
