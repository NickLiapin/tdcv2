description: Abbreviated weekday names for timetables, rosters and column headings. Sami cuts these to the first syllable of the distinguishing element and adds a full stop, which is why Monday and Tuesday come out vuos. and maŋŋ. rather than both collapsing onto the shared -árga they are built from. maŋŋ. keeps the ŋ, the velar nasal that is a full letter of the Sami alphabet and not an n with a decoration; the sound it writes is the ng of English sing, and it can stand at the start of a syllable in Sami as it never does in English.
locale: se
