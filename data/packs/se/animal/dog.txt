description: Dogs, and this is a working list before it is a pet list. A herding siida runs on dogs: a boazobeana turns a herd, holds it against a corral fence and finds animals a rider on a snowmobile cannot see, and the herding breeds here are the Sami dog proper — the Lapponian herder of Finland, the Finnish and Swedish Lapphunds, all descended from the spitz-type dogs Sami herders bred for the job long before any kennel club wrote a standard. bivdobeana is a hunting dog and the elkhounds beside it are the northern hunting stock. The sled breeds are a separate tradition again and largely a modern sport one: a Sami herding family before the snowmobile moved with reindeer pulling the sleds, not dogs, and the racing teams that cross Finnmark every March in the Finnmarksløpet are a twentieth-century arrival. Note that a good herding dog is genuinely valuable property and is talked about in the same terms as a good heargi.
