description: The part of an address below the street: which flat, which floor, which entrance. Sami builds most of these as compounds on latnja, a room, or on sadji, a place — biilasadji is a car space and skohtersadji is a snowmobile space, and the second one is not a joke in a municipality where the snowmobile is the winter vehicle and a household may own several. The Nordic loans in the list are genuine and unavoidable: etaša, kealláris, loptta, blohkka, kantuvra and poastaboksa all came in with the housing and the post office that use them, and a Sami address form prints them. What no unit designator can express is the address a herding family actually lives at for part of the year, which is a camp on a named piece of ground with no number of any kind.
locale: se
