description: The first element of a settlement name. Sami place names are compounds and almost always describe the place rather than commemorate anyone, so the modifier here is a size, a direction, a colour, a plant or an animal: stuorra big and uhca little, bajit upper and vuolit lower, davvi north and lulli south, ođđa new and boares old. bassi, holy, marks a place that was one — a bassivárri is a mountain where offerings were made, and there are many of them. sieidi is more specific still: a sieidi is the offering stone itself, a natural rock formation treated as a being that was fed fat and blood in exchange for fishing and hunting luck, and a name built on it records where one stood. The plant elements are diagnostic of terrain rather than decorative, because a Sami name tells you what you will find: soahki birch, beahci pine, sieđga willow, skierri the ankle-high dwarf birch of the high plateau, jeagil the reindeer lichen that decides where a herd can winter.
