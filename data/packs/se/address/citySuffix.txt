description: The second element of a settlement name, which in Sami is the terrain word, and the precision of these is the point. Sami does not have one word for valley: leahki is an open valley, ávži a narrow steep one, gorsa a gorge, vággi a valley between mountains, and each names a different problem for a herd crossing it. It does not have one word for water either: johka is a river, eatnu a great river, jávri a lake, luoppal a widening of a river into a lake, sávvu a stretch of slow deep water, guoika a rapid, vuotna a fjord. muotki is the neck of land between two waters where a boat is carried across, and a name built on it marks a route that has been used for a very long time. duottar is the treeless high plateau, and it is not a mountain — Guovdageaidnu sits on it. The place name Guovdageaidnu itself is built exactly this way and means the road half-way, the point at the middle of a migration route; Kárášjohka is the Káráš river, and Deatnu is simply the great river, the one the border between Norway and Finland follows for hundreds of kilometres without asking whose fishing places it cuts.
