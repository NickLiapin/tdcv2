description: The generic element of a street or place name, and the thing to understand before joining it to anything is that Sami welds it on rather than setting it beside: the road is Ávjovárgeaidnu in one word, not Ávjovár geaidnu in two, and the first element usually shifts shape as it goes in — jávri becomes jávre-, meahcci becomes meahcce-, bákti becomes bávte-. That is consonant gradation and compound stem formation working together, and it is why the values in address/streetName here are already complete words rather than name-plus-blank. Most of this list is landscape rather than infrastructure, because Sami place naming describes terrain with great precision: guolbba is specifically a dry pine heath, mielli a river cut-bank of sand, ávži a narrow steep-sided valley, gorsa a gorge, guoika a rapid, gáisá a high bare peak that holds snow, vuopmi a wide forested valley, duottar the treeless upland plateau. Two entries are administrative: siida is the herding community, and orohat is what Norwegian law calls a reindeer grazing district, the mapped area a siida holds.
