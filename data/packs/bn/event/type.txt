description: Event type (Bengali). গায়ে হলুদ is the turmeric ceremony held before a Bengali wedding and is a separate, separately catered event from the আকদ (the Muslim contract) and the বৌভাত (the groom's family reception) — a config that models a Bengali wedding as one event will be wrong about the count, the guest list and the spend. মিলাদ, ইফতার পার্টি, কুলখানি and শ্রাদ্ধ are religious gatherings of record with dates attached. একুশে বইমেলা and বৈশাখী মেলা are annual public events of national scale.
locale: bn
