description: Sport (Bengali). Ordered by what Bangladesh plays and watches rather than by the Olympic programme: cricket first by a very long way, then football, then the indigenous games — হা-ডু-ডু (kabaddi, the national sport), দাঁড়িয়াবান্ধা, গোল্লাছুট, লাঠি খেলা, নৌকা বাইচ (the monsoon boat race, which draws crowds in the tens of thousands) and বলি খেলা (the Chittagong wrestling bout held every April). Those six have no European equivalent and are not translations of anything.
locale: bn
