description: Property type (Bengali, Bangladesh). ফ্ল্যাট leads because apartment living is what urban Bangladesh means by a home. Several have no European equivalent: a টিনশেড বাড়ি is the corrugated-iron house that most of the countryside lives in, a সাবলেট is a room let out of an occupied flat and is a large part of the Dhaka rental market, a মেস is shared bachelor lodging let by the bed, and a দোকানঘর is a shop unit with living space above. ভিটাবাড়ি is the homestead plot with the house on it, which is how rural property is described and inherited.
locale: bn
