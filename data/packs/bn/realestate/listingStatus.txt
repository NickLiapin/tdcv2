description: Listing status (Bengali). বায়না হয়েছে is the state after an advance payment has changed hands and before the deed is registered — a distinct and legally meaningful stage in a Bangladeshi property sale with no clean English equivalent. মামলাধীন (under litigation) is listed because land disputes are common enough that a listing genuinely carries the status.
locale: bn
