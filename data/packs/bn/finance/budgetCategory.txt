description: Household budget line (Bengali). বাসা ভাড়া heads the list because rent is the largest single item for most urban Bangladeshi households. Three have no European counterpart: গৃহকর্মীর বেতন — domestic help is normal well down the income scale here — কোচিং ও প্রাইভেট টিউশন, which is an unavoidable expense because private tutoring is effectively part of the school system, and আত্মীয়স্বজনকে সাহায্য, remittances and support sent to relatives, which is a standing monthly line in a large share of households.
