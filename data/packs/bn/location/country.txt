description: Country and territory names in Bengali, in the standard forms used by Bangladeshi media and Bengali Wikipedia. Several major countries are known by a Bengali name rather than a transliteration and a config must not romanise them back: মিশর is Egypt, তুরস্ক is Turkey, গ্রিস is Greece, যুক্তরাজ্য is the United Kingdom and যুক্তরাষ্ট্র is the United States — both of the last two are ordinary words meaning 'united kingdom' and 'united states' and neither contains any part of the English name. The list mirrors the en pack's entries, so a few places appear twice under both of their current names (আইভরি কোস্ট and কোত দিভোয়ার, পূর্ব তিমুর and তিমুর-লেস্তে, হোলি সি and ভ্যাটিকান সিটি); that is inherited from the reference list, not a Bengali duplication. তিব্বত is likewise carried over from the en list and is not a sovereign state.
