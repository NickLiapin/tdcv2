description: Fish name (Bengali). This list matters more in Bengali than in any European locale: মাছে ভাতে বাঙালি — 'the Bengali is made of fish and rice' — is the proverb Bengalis describe themselves with. ইলিশ, the hilsa, comes first because it is the national fish, the centre of the Pohela Boishakh meal and the subject of an annual government fishing ban. Most of these are freshwater or estuarine, which is what a delta eats; the marine species are at the end. চিংড়ি (shrimp) is included because Bengali counts it with the fish and it is Bangladesh's largest seafood export.
