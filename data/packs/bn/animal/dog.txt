description: Dog breed. দেশি কুকুর — the free-ranging pariah dog — is listed first and is by far the commonest dog in Bangladesh; the Bangladeshi variety of it is the same landrace as the Indian pariah. Pet dog ownership is much rarer in Bangladesh than in Europe, partly for religious reasons, so a config generating Bangladeshi pet records should not assume dogs at European rates. Breed names are international loanwords in Bengali script.
