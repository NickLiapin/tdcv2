description: Telecom operator in Bangladesh. Deliberately short at the top, and nothing has been invented to pad it: mobile telecom here is FOUR operators — গ্রামীণফোন, রবি, বাংলালিংক and the state-owned টেলিটক — after Airtel merged into Robi in 2016. The rest are fixed broadband ISPs and the state fixed-line operator BTCL; the ISP market is genuinely fragmented, with hundreds of small local providers that no list could enumerate, so these are only the larger national ones.
