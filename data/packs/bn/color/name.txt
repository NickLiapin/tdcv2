description: Colour name (Bengali). Four have no clean English equivalent and are the ones a config translating from English will lose: গেরুয়া is the ochre-saffron of monastic robes, মেটে is the dull earth-brown of unglazed pottery, ধূসর is grey but কাঁচা হলুদ is specifically the yellow of raw turmeric, and মেহেদি is the red-brown of henna. লাল-সবুজ is listed as one value because red-and-green is read as a single colour in Bangladesh — it is the flag, and every Victory Day and cricket match is described in it.
locale: bn
