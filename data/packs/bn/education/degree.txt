description: Educational qualification (Bengali, Bangladesh). Two things a config cannot infer. First, the ladder is এসএসসি at about sixteen, এইচএসসি at about eighteen, then স্নাতক and স্নাতকোত্তর; there is no 'high school diploma' and no associate degree. Second, Bangladesh runs a PARALLEL madrasa system whose certificates are legally equivalent at each rung — দাখিল equals SSC, আলিম equals HSC, ফাজিল equals a bachelor's and কামিল a master's — and several million students are in it, so a Bangladeshi education dataset that offers only the general stream is missing a whole track. জেডিসি and জেএসসি were junior-level public examinations that Bangladesh has since discontinued but which appear in older records.
