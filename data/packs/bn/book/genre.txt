description: Book genre (Bengali). Three are specifically Bengali categories rather than translations: মুক্তিযুদ্ধভিত্তিক (writing about the 1971 Liberation War) is its own shelf in every Bangladeshi bookshop, ভ্রমণকাহিনি (travel writing) is a far larger genre in Bengali than in English, and প্রবন্ধ is the discursive essay, a form Bengali readers buy in volume.
locale: bn
