description: Musical instrument (Bengali). একতারা comes first: the one-stringed drone lute of the Baul singers is close to a national symbol. দোতারা, ঢোল, ঢাক, খোল, মন্দিরা, খমক and বাঁশি are the instruments of Bengali folk and devotional music; তবলা, হারমোনিয়াম, সেতার, সরোদ and এসরাজ come from the Hindustani classical tradition. The Western instruments at the end are played here too but are not what the tradition is built on.
