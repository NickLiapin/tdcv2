description: Common Bengali adverb. Note that Bengali forms most manner adverbs by adding -ভাবে or -ে to an adjective (দ্রুতভাবে, ধীরে, সহজে) rather than by a single suffix like English -ly; several here are reduplicated forms (ধীরে ধীরে, আস্তে আস্তে), which is a productive Bengali pattern that intensifies or extends the action and has no English equivalent.
locale: bn
