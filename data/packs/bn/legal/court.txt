description: Court or tribunal (Bengali). Bangladesh's Supreme Court has two divisions — the আপিল বিভাগ and the হাইকোর্ট বিভাগ — and they are not separate courts. গ্রাম আদালত is the village court run by a Union Parishad and has no European equivalent: it hears small civil and criminal matters locally, with elected representatives sitting alongside the parties' nominees. নারী ও শিশু নির্যাতন দমন ট্রাইব্যুনাল and শ্রম আদালত are specialised tribunals with their own procedure.
locale: bn
