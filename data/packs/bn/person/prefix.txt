description: Bengali honorific or form of address, written before a name. The first two are the ones no European locale has an equivalent for and no config can guess: মোঃ (short for মোহাম্মদ) and মোছাঃ (short for মোসাম্মৎ) stand in front of a very large share of Bangladeshi Muslim names of record — a National ID, a school certificate and a payroll line read মোঃ শফিকুল ইসলাম and মোছাঃ রোকেয়া বেগম, not the bare name. They are gendered: মোঃ for men, মোছাঃ for women. জনাব / জনাবা are the formal Mr / Mrs of Bangladeshi officialese, শ্রী / শ্রীমতী the Hindu equivalents, and আলহাজ্ব marks someone who has performed the Hajj — it is in ordinary administrative use on signboards and nameplates, not an archaism.
locale: bn
