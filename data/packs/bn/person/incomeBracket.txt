description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Bengali. Amounts are in TAKA (৳, BDT), the currency of Bangladesh — NOT the Indian rupee, which is what a West Bengal pack would carry. The bands are written in the South Asian numbering system, which groups by লাখ (1 lakh = 100,000) and কোটি (1 crore = 10,000,000 = 100 lakh) rather than by thousands and millions; a Bangladeshi salary is quoted as ৳১২ লাখ, never as ৳1.2 million. Digits are Bengali (০-৯), which is what Bangladeshi banknotes, forms and newspapers actually print. A config formatting a raw number for a Bangladeshi reader must also use the 2,2,3 digit grouping (৳১২,০০,০০০), not the Western 3,3,3. The bands are set to Bangladeshi income levels, which are far below Indian urban ones — the top band here would be an ordinary professional salary in a European pack.
