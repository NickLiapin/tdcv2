description: Language name in Bengali. বাংলা comes first: it is the sole official language of Bangladesh and the only one whose status was won by people shot for demanding it — the 21 February 1952 Language Movement, now UNESCO's International Mother Language Day. The next block is the languages actually spoken inside Bangladesh: the Chittagong Hill Tracts languages (চাকমা, মারমা, ত্রিপুরা/ককবরক, ম্রো, বম), সাঁওতালি and গারো in the north, রোহিঙ্গা in the Cox's Bazar camps, and উর্দু among the Bihari community. চাটগাঁইয়া and সিলেটি are listed as languages, not dialects, because they are not mutually intelligible with standard Bengali.
