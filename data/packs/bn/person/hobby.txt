description: Pastime or hobby (Bengali, Bangladesh). আড্ডা comes first because it is the defining Bengali pastime and has no English equivalent — the long, unhurried, purposeless conversation among friends over tea, treated seriously enough that Bengalis name it as a hobby on a form. পাখি দেখা, নৌকা ভ্রমণ and মাছ ধরা reflect a delta country of rivers, and ঘুড়ি ওড়ানো is a seasonal event (সাকরাইন in old Dhaka) rather than a children's game.
locale: bn
