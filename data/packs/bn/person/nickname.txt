description: Bengali ডাকনাম — the call-name a family actually uses at home, as opposed to the ভালো নাম (the 'good name') on the birth certificate. This two-name system is universal in Bengali culture and it is the single thing about Bengali names a config is most likely to get wrong: the ডাকনাম is NOT a shortening of the given name and cannot be derived from it. A man registered as মোঃ শফিকুল ইসলাম is টুটুল to everyone who knows him; a woman registered as সানজিদা আক্তার is মিতু. So draw this list independently of person/male/firstName.txt and person/female/firstName.txt rather than deriving it from either. Most of these are used for either sex; a few (খোকা, খুকি) are explicitly the boy and girl words.
locale: bn
