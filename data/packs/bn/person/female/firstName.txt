description: Bangladeshi female given names in Bengali script, frequency-ordered, with the Muslim stock at the head and the Hindu and Christian names at roughly their real share of the country. Three things a config cannot infer. First, the female counterpart of মোঃ is মোছাঃ (the abbreviation of মোসাম্মৎ), and it prefixes a large share of women's names of record on a Bangladeshi National ID exactly as মোঃ does for men. It is not a given name; it lives in person/prefix.txt. Second, this list deliberately does NOT overlap person/male/firstName.txt: Bengali given names are sex-marked, with the feminine carrying the Sanskritic -আ / -ঈ ending (সুমন male / সুমনা female, অমল male / অমলা female, শুভ্র male / শুভ্রা female), so a shared name would be an error rather than the intentional unisex overlap the Hindi pack has. Third, আক্তার, বেগম and খাতুন are missing here on purpose — they are extremely common in Bangladeshi women's names but sit in the SURNAME slot, not the given-name slot, and person/lastName.txt explains why they are excluded there too.
