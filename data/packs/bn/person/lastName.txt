description: Bangladeshi surnames in Bengali script, frequency-ordered. THE PACK SPEAKS BANGLADESH, not West Bengal (the full note on that choice is in person/gender.txt), and the surname distribution is the single place where that choice shows most. Bangladesh is about 91% Muslim and 8% Hindu, so the head of this list is Islamic (ইসলাম, রহমান, আহমেদ, হোসেন, খান) where a West Bengal list would be led by দাস, ঘোষ, মুখোপাধ্যায় and চট্টোপাধ্যায়. The Kulin Brahmin names (চট্টোপাধ্যায় / চ্যাটার্জি, বন্দ্যোপাধ্যায় / ব্যানার্জি, মুখোপাধ্যায় / মুখার্জি, গঙ্গোপাধ্যায় / গাঙ্গুলি) ARE here, in both their Sanskritic and anglicised spellings, because Bangladesh has a real Hindu minority — but they sit far down the list, which is where the population actually puts them. Reuse this pack for West Bengal and the names will be right while the proportions will be wrong. Three further things a config cannot infer. First, Bengali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this one list serves male and female rows alike and there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt — its absence is the design. Second, the sex-marked surname-slot words are EXCLUDED: আক্তার, বেগম, খাতুন, বিবি and নেছা for women, দেবী and রানী in the Hindu convention. They are among the commonest words in the surname field of a Bangladeshi NID, but a shared list containing বেগম would hand that name to male rows, so a config that wants them must supply them per gender itself. Third, a large share of these are not inherited family names at all but occupational or title words (মৃধা, হাওলাদার, তালুকদার, সরদার, প্রামাণিক, কর্মকার, সূত্রধর) that became fixed surnames; siblings sharing one is normal, cousins sharing one is not to be assumed. The weights: the top thirty approximate real Bangladeshi shares, and below that they are a tiered long tail rather than a measured figure — Bangladesh has no Nguyen, and no single surname approaches a fifth of the country.
