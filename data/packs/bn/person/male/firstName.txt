description: Bangladeshi male given names in Bengali script, frequency-ordered. The pack speaks BANGLADESH, so the head is the Muslim name stock (~91% of the country) and the Hindu, Buddhist and Christian names follow at weights near their real share. Four things a config cannot infer. First, মোঃ — the abbreviation of মোহাম্মদ — stands in front of a very large share of Bangladeshi male names of record: a National ID reads মোঃ শফিকুল ইসলাম, not শফিকুল ইসলাম. It is not a given name, so it is NOT composed from here; it lives in person/prefix.txt and a config that wants realistic Bangladeshi records should prepend it to roughly half of its male rows. মোহাম্মদ appears in this list in full because it is also used unabbreviated in the given-name slot. Second, many of these are the FIRST half of a two-word given name and are rarely used alone: শফিকুল, আশরাফুল, রফিকুল, মিজানুর, আনিসুর and every other -উল / -উর form expects a second element (শফিকুল ইসলাম, মিজানুর রহমান), and that second element is drawn from person/lastName.txt. Third, Bengali given names are clearly sex-marked and this list deliberately does NOT overlap person/female/firstName.txt — unlike Hindi, where the Punjabi -প্রীত names are genuinely unisex, Bengali marks the feminine with the Sanskritic -আ / -ঈ ending (সুমন male, সুমনা female; অমল male, অমলা female), so a name in both lists would be an error, not a fact. Fourth, none of these is what a Bengali family actually calls the person at home — that is the ডাকনাম, and it lives in person/nickname.txt.
locale: bn
weighted: true
