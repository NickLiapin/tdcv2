description: Diagnosis (Bengali, patient-facing register). Ordered by what a Bangladeshi clinic actually sees, which is why the list opens with conditions a European pack barely carries: ডেঙ্গু is an annual national epidemic, টাইফয়েড and আমাশয় and কৃমি সংক্রমণ are routine, যক্ষ্মা (tuberculosis) is among the highest burdens in the world, কালাজ্বর is endemic to the northern districts, and আর্সেনিকজনিত বিষক্রিয়া — arsenic poisoning from shallow tube wells — affects tens of millions of people and is the largest mass poisoning in recorded history. Alongside them sit the non-communicable diseases that now kill most Bangladeshis: hypertension, diabetes, stroke and chronic kidney disease. A caution a config cannot infer: Bangladeshi clinical records are written in ENGLISH even on Bengali-language forms, so a real prescription or discharge summary will say 'Hypertension', not উচ্চ রক্তচাপ. The Bengali terms here are the register of patient counselling, health education and Bengali medical writing, and that is the honest description of what this list is.
