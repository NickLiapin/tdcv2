description: Generic drug name in Bengali script. Bangladesh follows the BRITISH INN spellings, not the American ones, so it is প্যারাসিটামল and never acetaminophen, and অ্যাড্রেনালিন rather than epinephrine — a config translating a US drug list will get this wrong. ওরস্যালাইন heads the list for a reason no other locale shares: oral rehydration solution was developed in Dhaka at what is now icddr,b, is credited with saving tens of millions of lives, and is sold in every village shop in Bangladesh as an everyday item rather than a pharmacy product. Bangladesh also manufactures about 98% of the medicine it consumes, so these are locally made generics rather than imports.
locale: bn
