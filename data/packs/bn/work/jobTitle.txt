description: Job title (Bengali, Bangladesh) — a flat list across sectors. Ordered to reflect the real Bangladeshi labour market rather than a European one: the garment-factory roles, agriculture, fishing, rickshaw and CNG driving, and the informal trades come high, because that is where most of the country works. Several have no European equivalent — a সিএনজি চালক drives the three-wheeled auto-rickshaw, a মাঝি poles or rows a river boat, a সারেং is a launch skipper, a আনসার is a member of the auxiliary paramilitary force posted to guard public buildings, and a ইমাম, a মুয়াজ্জিন and a পুরোহিত are salaried positions of record. মুক্তিযোদ্ধা is not an occupation but appears on Bangladeshi records as a status with a state pension attached. Note also that a very large share of Bangladeshi employment is informal and has no title at all: a config generating employment data should be prepared for a blank in that field rather than assuming everyone has one of these.
