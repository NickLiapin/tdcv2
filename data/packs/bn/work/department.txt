description: Company department (Bengali). Bangladeshi corporate life runs on a mix of Bengali and English department names and both appear on real org charts; the Bengali forms are given here because that is what the pack is for. মার্চেন্ডাইজিং and কমপ্লায়েন্স are listed because they are standard departments in the garment sector, which is where a large share of Bangladeshi white-collar jobs sit — কমপ্লায়েন্স in particular exists because of buyer audits after the 2013 Rana Plaza collapse, and it has no counterpart in a European org chart of the same size.
