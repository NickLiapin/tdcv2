description: Technical abbreviation. International ones are used unchanged in Bengali. Four are specifically Bangladeshi everyday tech acronyms: NID (the national identity number, which gates SIM registration and every online government service), MFS (mobile financial services — the bKash and Nagad category), BEFTN (the electronic funds transfer network) and RTGS. OTP is included because one-time passwords are how almost every Bangladeshi transaction is confirmed.
