description: Employment benefit (Bengali). Two are legally mandated in Bangladesh and have no European equivalent: উৎসব বোনাস is the festival bonus, paid twice a year (at the two Eids for Muslim staff, at their own festivals for others) and normally equal to a month's basic pay — every Bangladeshi employee expects it and its late payment is a standard cause of garment-sector strikes. বৈশাখী ভাতা is the separate Pohela Boishakh allowance paid to government employees. ভবিষ্য তহবিল is the provident fund and গ্র্যাচুইটি the end-of-service gratuity, both far more central to a Bangladeshi compensation package than a pension scheme is.
