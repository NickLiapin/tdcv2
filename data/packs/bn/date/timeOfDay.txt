description: Time of day (Bengali). শেষরাত is the last watch of the night before dawn and is a live time marker in Bangladesh rather than a poetic one — it is when সেহরি is eaten in Ramadan and when the ফজর prayer falls. গোধূলি is the dusk half-hour when cattle come home, and Bengali distinguishes it from সন্ধ্যা proper.
