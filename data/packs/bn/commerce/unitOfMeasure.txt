description: Unit of measure (Bengali). Bangladesh is metric on paper and traditional in the market. মণ (about 37.32 kg), সের and ছটাক are still quoted for grain, fish and jaggery; কাঠা, শতাংশ, বিঘা and একর measure land and appear on every deed — a কাঠা is about 1.65 decimals and there are 20 to a বিঘা, but the exact value varies by district, which is a real hazard for anyone computing with it. হালি is a count of four, used for eggs and bananas and nothing else. A config must not assume kilograms.
