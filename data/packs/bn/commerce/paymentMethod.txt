description: Payment method (Bengali, Bangladesh). This list is where Bangladesh diverges most sharply from a European one. বিকাশ, নগদ, রকেট and উপায় are mobile financial services, not wallets attached to a bank account, and between them they carry a very large share of all retail and person-to-person payment in the country — বিকাশ alone is used as a common noun for sending money. ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি is the default for online shopping. Credit cards are a minority instrument held mostly by urban salaried professionals, so a config that models Bangladeshi payments on card rails will describe almost none of it.
