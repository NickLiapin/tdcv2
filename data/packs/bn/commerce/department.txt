description: Shop department or product category (Bengali), as a Bangladeshi marketplace or department store organises it. কাঁচাবাজার — the wet market for fresh vegetables, fish and meat — is a category of its own and is where most household food is still bought; মুদি is the dry-goods grocery. Both belong here rather than being folded into a single 'grocery'.
locale: bn
