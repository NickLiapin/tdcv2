description: Legal or trading suffix in a Bangladeshi company name. লিমিটেড and প্রাইভেট লিমিটেড are the registered forms under the Companies Act. এন্টারপ্রাইজ, ট্রেডার্স and স্টোর are not legal forms at all but are the overwhelmingly common trading names of Bangladeshi sole proprietorships — 'করিম এন্টারপ্রাইজ' is what a small business is actually called, and a config generating Bangladeshi company names should use them far more often than the corporate forms. সমিতি is a registered cooperative society, a real and very common legal entity here.
