description: Official document or record (Bengali, Bangladesh). জাতীয় পরিচয়পত্র (the NID) is the master identity document and its number gates almost everything else — a SIM card, a bank account, a passport, a vote. Several have no European counterpart: কাবিননামা is the Muslim marriage contract and the legal proof of marriage, ওয়ারিশ সনদ is the certificate of heirs issued by a Union Parishad chairman and required to inherit anything, চারিত্রিক সনদ is a certificate of good character demanded for jobs and admissions, নামজারি is the mutation of a land record, and দলিল is the registered deed itself. মুক্তিযোদ্ধা সনদ certifies status as a freedom fighter of 1971 and carries a pension and quota rights.
locale: bn
