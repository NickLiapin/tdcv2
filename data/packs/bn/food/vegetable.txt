description: Vegetable (Bengali). Most of these have no English name in general use — লাউ, কচু, ঝিঙা, চিচিঙ্গা, পটল, ধুন্দল, কাঁকরোল, শজনে, থোড় and মোচা are ordinary market items in Bangladesh and untranslatable one for one. শাক covers the leafy greens as a class and Bengali names each one separately, which is why several appear individually.
