description: Spice or seasoning (Bengali). পাঁচফোড়ন is the five-spice mix that is the signature of Bengali cooking and exists nowhere else — whole cumin, nigella, fenugreek, fennel and radhuni, used unground. রাঁধুনি (radhuni, wild celery seed) is the fifth of them and is a specifically Bengali spice that no other South Asian cuisine uses. সরিষার তেল is listed among the spices rather than the oils because in Bengali cooking mustard oil is a flavour, not a medium.
locale: bn
