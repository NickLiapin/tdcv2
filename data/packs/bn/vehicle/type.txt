description: Vehicle type (Bengali, Bangladesh). The first eight are what actually moves people and goods here and none of them has a European counterpart: a রিকশা is the cycle rickshaw, an ইজিবাইক its battery-powered replacement, a সিএনজি অটোরিকশা the three-wheeled taxi named after its fuel, a লেগুনা a converted pickup running a fixed urban route, and নসিমন and করিমন are unregistered rural vehicles built around irrigation-pump engines. The river craft — লঞ্চ, ট্রলার, নৌকা, ফেরি — are transport, not leisure: a large part of the country is reached by water. Cars come well down the list because private car ownership is a small urban minority.
