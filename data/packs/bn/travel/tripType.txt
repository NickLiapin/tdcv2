description: Purpose of a trip (Bengali). Four are specific to Bangladesh and are among the commonest reasons anyone travels. হজ্জ and ওমরাহ are pilgrimages to Mecca — Bangladesh sends well over a hundred thousand pilgrims a year on a state-administered quota, and the trip is planned and saved for over decades. ঈদের ছুটিতে বাড়ি যাওয়া is the mass return to the village at Eid, when something like ten million people leave Dhaka in a few days; it is the largest recurring movement of people in the country. প্রবাসে কাজে যাওয়া is departure for overseas work.
