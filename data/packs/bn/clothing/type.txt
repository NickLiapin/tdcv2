description: Garment (Bengali, Bangladesh). The first ten have no European equivalent and are what most people actually wear: শাড়ি and সালোয়ার কামিজ with an ওড়না for women, পাঞ্জাবি with পায়জামা for men on formal and religious occasions, and লুঙ্গি — a tube of cloth worn at the waist — as ordinary everyday and household dress for men across the country. গামছা is the thin checked cotton towel that doubles as a headwrap and a shoulder cloth and is close to a national object. Note that Bangladesh makes clothing for the world and wears something else.
locale: bn
