description: Bengali thoroughfare type. Unlike the European locales this element goes AFTER the name, not before it — বঙ্গবন্ধু এভিনিউ, স্টেশন রোড, মিরপুর সড়ক — which is the reverse of Polish ulica Długa. Two facts a config cannot infer. First, a Bangladeshi address locates a building by AREA at least as often as by street, which is why পাড়া, মহল্লা, নগর, আবাসিক এলাকা, হাউজিং, সেক্টর and ব্লক are here alongside the road words; a Dhaka address is routinely written as house-road-block-area with no street name that a map would recognise. Second, রোড and এভিনিউ are English loans in everyday Bangladeshi use and are written in Bengali script, not translated to সড়ক — both forms are correct and both are in real addresses.
locale: bn
