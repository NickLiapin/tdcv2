description: Directional qualifier used with a Bangladeshi locality, block or sector name (উত্তর বাড্ডা, পশ্চিম রামপুরা). কিবলা is the one no European locale has: it is the direction of prayer, roughly west-north-west from Bangladesh, and it is genuinely used to orient a room or a plot in property descriptions and in mosque construction. উজান and ভাটি are the upstream and downstream halves of a river country and name whole regions, not just directions — ভাটি অঞ্চল is the seasonally flooded lowland of the northeast.
