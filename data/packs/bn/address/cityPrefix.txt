description: Leading element of a Bangladeshi place name (নতুন বাজার, পুরান ঢাকা, বড় মগবাজার). The Persian and Arabic elements — শাহ, মীর, খাজা, ফতে, দৌলত, নওয়াব, আজিম — are the mark of the Bengal Sultanate and Mughal period and are what most distinguishes a Bangladeshi toponym from a West Bengal one, where Sanskritic elements are commoner. Combine with a real toponym and a citySuffix; on its own this does not name a real place and a generated combination must never be presented as one.
locale: bn
