description: Street names found across Bangladesh rather than in one city — the national figures whose names are on a road in virtually every town (বঙ্গবন্ধু শেখ মুজিব, শেরে বাংলা, মাওলানা ভাসানী, কাজী নজরুল ইসলাম, জিয়াউর রহমান), the Dhaka roads well enough known to read as generic, the landmarks that generate a road name everywhere (স্টেশন, কলেজ, হাসপাতাল, বাজার, ঘাট), and the ordinary descriptive and flower-and-tree names. Compose BEFORE an address/streetSuffix, because Bengali puts the thoroughfare word after the name: বঙ্গবন্ধু এভিনিউ, স্টেশন রোড, শহীদ মিনার সড়ক. Note the river words — ঘাট, লঞ্চঘাট, ফেরিঘাট, খেয়াঘাট — generate street names in Bangladesh the way a rail station does in Europe, because much of the country still moves by boat.
locale: bn
