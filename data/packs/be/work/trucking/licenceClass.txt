CATEGORY F HAS NO EU EQUIVALENT: it is for tractors and self-propelled agricultural machinery, issued by the agriculture inspectorate rather than the traffic police, and in a country with this much farmland it is a common licence. Category I covers other self-propelled machines. A Belarusian licence prints the categories as Latin letters even though everything else on it is Cyrillic.
