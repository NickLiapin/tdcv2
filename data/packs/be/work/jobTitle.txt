description: Job title in Belarusian, in the MASCULINE form the Belarusian staff list uses. BELARUSIAN JOB TITLES ARE GRAMMATICALLY GENDERED but the official штатны расклад prints the masculine for both sexes, so a woman is entered as настаўнік and only spoken of as настаўніца; the feminine forms are therefore deliberately NOT in this list, because a personnel record would not contain them. The exceptions are the few jobs whose feminine form IS the official title — Даярка, Швачка, Медыцынская сястра — and those are listed as they stand. ПВТ (Парк высокіх тэхналогій) is why the software titles are so numerous: it is a special legal regime with its own tax rules, and it made IT one of the largest white-collar employers in the country.
