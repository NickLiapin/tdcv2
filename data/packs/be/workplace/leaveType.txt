BELARUS HAS ONE OF THE LONGEST CHILDCARE LEAVES IN THE WORLD: адпачынак па догляду за дзіцем runs until the child is THREE, the job is held open for the whole period and a state allowance is paid throughout, so a Belarusian HR record routinely shows a three-year gap that is not unemployment. Донарскі дзень is a paid day off for giving blood, a statutory entitlement. Адгул is time off in lieu.
