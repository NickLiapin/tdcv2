description: Blood group (Belarusian). WRITTEN WITH THE ROMAN NUMERAL BESIDE THE LETTER, which is the post-Soviet convention and is what a Belarusian medical card, a donor card and a military ID all print: 0 is I, A is II, B is III and AB is IV. A dataset that gives a Belarusian patient a bare "A+" is not wrong about the biology but is wrong about the document. The Rh sign is written with the Latin letters Rh in an otherwise Cyrillic record.
locale: be
