description: Colour name (Belarusian). БЛАКІТНЫ AND СІНІ ARE TWO DIFFERENT COLOURS in Belarusian, exactly as in Russian and Ukrainian and unlike English: блакітны is light blue and сіні is dark blue, and no Belarusian would call them shades of one word. Чырвоны, Белы and Зялёны are the three colours of the country's two rival flags and are the first three most Belarusians name. Akanne shows in the colour words themselves: Залаты not Золотой, Карычневы not Коричневый, Аранжавы not Оранжевый.
