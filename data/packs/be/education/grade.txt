BELARUS GRADES ON A TEN-POINT SCALE, introduced in 2002 and used from primary school to university: 1 is the lowest passing entry and 10 the highest, with "залічана" for pass/fail subjects. This is NOT the Russian five-point scale and a dataset that scores Belarusian pupils out of five is wrong. Anything below 3 is a fail in practice.
