BELARUS RUNS THE TWO-TIER SOVIET RESEARCH DEGREE SYSTEM: кандыдат навук comes first and доктар навук is a second, higher doctorate earned years later, so "doctor" in a Belarusian CV does not mean PhD. Дацэнт and прафесар are academic RANKS conferred separately from the degrees. Сярэдняя спецыяльная адукацыя (a technical college) is a distinct level between school and university with no exact English equivalent.
