description: Belarusian honorific or title written BEFORE a name. TWO REGISTERS COEXIST AND THEY ARE POLITICALLY LOADED: спадар / спадарыня / спадарычна is the native Belarusian address, revived from the interwar period and standard in Belarusian-language publishing and among Belarusian speakers; пан / пані is the older Grand-Duchy form, still ordinary in the west. Both are here because a Belarusian text uses one or the other and never таварыш, which belongs to a closed Soviet register. Academic titles PRECEDE the name in Belarusian and stack (праф. д-р Марыя Багдановіч), which is why person/suffix.txt holds almost nothing academic. к.т.н. and д.т.н. are the two-tier Soviet-inherited degree system Belarus still runs: кандыдат навук below, доктар навук above.
