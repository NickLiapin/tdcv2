description: Pastime, in Belarusian. Several are specific to the country rather than generic: збор грыбоў and збор ягад are not a curiosity but a mass autumn activity in a country that is two-fifths forest and where the wild-mushroom harvest is regulated by law; бортніцтва is tree beekeeping in a hollowed living pine, a Belarusian and Polesian craft on the UNESCO intangible-heritage list and a completely different thing from пчалярства in hives; лазня is the bathhouse as a weekend social occasion. Wi-Fi-era loanwords (падкасты, стрымінг, геакэшынг) keep their international shape.
