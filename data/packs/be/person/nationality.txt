description: Nationality as Belarusian names it, MASCULINE singular — the form a Belarusian document prints in the нацыянальнасць field, which in Belarus is an ethnicity field distinct from citizenship and is filled in by declaration. Беларус heads the list; Рускі, Паляк, Украінец, Яўрэй and Татарын are the historic minorities of the country, and Татарын is not a recent arrival — Lipka Tatars have lived in Belarus since the fourteenth century and still have mosques there. Літвін is deliberately absent from this list even though it is the historic self-name, because on a modern form it would be read as "Lithuanian".
