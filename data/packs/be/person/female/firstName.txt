description: Belarusian women's given names, commonest first. AKANNE IS THE PROOF: an unstressed o is WRITTEN a, so Мікалай is not Николай respelled, Аляксандр is not Александр, Кацярына is not Екатерина and Наталля is not Наталья — the Belarusian spelling follows the Belarusian pronunciation, and this is the sharpest single test against Russian. DZEKANNE AND TSEKANNE are in the names too: Дзмітрый, Дзяніс, Уладзімір, Вадзім, Генадзь, Аркадзь carry дз where Russian has soft д, and Цімафей, Валянцін, Настасся carry ц where Russian has soft т. Ганна is the Belarusian form and Анна is not — the initial г is written and pronounced, which is why the Belarusian and Russian versions of the commonest woman's name in the country are different strings. Настасся, Соф’я, Дар’я and Аўдоцця show the Belarusian doubling of a consonant before -я where Russian writes a soft sign or ъ. Алеся, Зося, Марыля, Данута, Ядвіга and Яніна are the native and Catholic-western stratum, ordinary legal names rather than diminutives. THE WEIGHTS ARE A FITTED rank-decay curve (rank^-0.45), NOT measured counts. Note that the given-name lists do NOT overlap between the sexes in Belarusian: there are no unisex given names in the civil register.
