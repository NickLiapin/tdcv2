description: Language as Belarusian names it. Беларуская and Руская are BOTH state languages of Belarus under the 1995 referendum, and the two are not interchangeable in practice — most everyday urban speech is Russian while Belarusian is the language of schooling in part, of literature and of official symbolism, so a dataset about Belarus that offers only one of them is wrong about the country. Ідыш and Татарская are here because they are historic languages of Belarus, not exotic additions: Yiddish was one of the four official languages of the BSSR from 1927 to 1937, alongside Belarusian, Russian and Polish, and its name is on the republic's coat of arms of that period. Трасянка, the mixed Belarusian-Russian everyday speech, is deliberately not listed: it is a register, not a language, and no form offers it.
