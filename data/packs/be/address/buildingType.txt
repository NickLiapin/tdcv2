description: Building type (Belarusian). FOUR ARE SPECIFIC TO THE COUNTRY and a Western European listing has no single word for them: хрушчоўка is the five-storey walk-up block of 1957-1985 that still houses a large share of urban Belarus and is a real property category in every advertisement; сталінка is its heavier pre-1956 predecessor with higher ceilings and costs more; лецішча is the summer cottage on a garden plot that a great many town families also own and go to at weekends; свіран is the timber granary standing separate from the house on a Belarusian farmstead. хата is the traditional village house and is not a synonym for дом — a дом is any building, a хата is that building.
