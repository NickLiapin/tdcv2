description: Belarusian thoroughfare type. IT GOES BEFORE THE NAME (вул. Янкі Купалы, пр-т Незалежнасці), the opposite of the English order, and the abbreviated form is what is written on an envelope. Two entries have no English equivalent. завулак is a short side street hung off a longer one and keeps its parent's name with a number (1-ы Савецкі завулак), so завулак names are never independent. аграгарадок is a Belarusian administrative invention of 2005: a village upgraded by decree with mains services and a school, and it is a postal address type in its own right. гасцінец is the old word for a highway between towns and survives in road names.
locale: be
