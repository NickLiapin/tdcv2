description: Trailing element of a Belarusian place name — the noun a qualifier attaches to (Новы Двор, Старое Сяло, Вялікая Слабада). THREE OF THESE ARE SETTLEMENT TYPES WITH NO ENGLISH WORD and they carry real history: слабада was a settlement freed from dues for a term in order to attract people; засценак was a smallholding of the petty gentry beyond the boundary of the common field, and its inhabitants were legally noble and often poorer than their peasant neighbours — a whole social class of the Grand Duchy lived in them; фальварак was the manor farm the estate was worked from. Гарадзішча and Селішча are archaeology in the name: they mean the site where a fort or a village USED to be. Each of these is the second word of real Belarusian settlements.
