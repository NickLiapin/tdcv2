IT AGREES IN GENDER WITH THE NOUN THAT FOLLOWS, which is why Новы / Новая / Новае are three entries and not one — Belarusian has full gender agreement and this element is an adjective, not a fixed prefix. Combine with address/citySuffix.txt; a prefix alone does not name a real settlement, and the actual Belarusian settlements live in the country pack at belarus.geo.
