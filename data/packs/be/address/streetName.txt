description: Street names that repeat in towns across Belarus — the writers, the Grand Duchy, the Soviet layer and the plain descriptive names. THE NAME GOES AFTER THE THOROUGHFARE WORD (вул. Янкі Купалы), which is why address/streetSuffix.txt must be composed first, and IT IS ALREADY IN THE RIGHT CASE: a person's name stands in the GENITIVE (Янкі Купалы, Максіма Багдановіча, Васіля Быкава) while a descriptive name is a FEMININE ADJECTIVE agreeing with вуліца (Савецкая, Лясная, Кветкавая), so the two shapes in this file are not inconsistent — they are two different grammatical constructions that both follow вул. THREE HISTORICAL LAYERS SIT SIDE BY SIDE IN EVERY BELARUSIAN TOWN and all three are here: the Belarusian literary canon (Купала, Колас, Багдановіч, Скарына, Быкаў, Караткевіч), the Grand Duchy and the national revival (Каліноўскага, Вітаўта, Рагнеды, Еўфрасінні Полацкай, Касцюшкі, Сапегі), and the Soviet stratum (Леніна, Кірава, Савецкая, Камсамольская), which is still the commonest layer on the ground. 3 Ліпеня is Belarus's independence day and is a street name in its own right. Streets belonging to one particular city are the country pack's business; this is the shared national stock.
