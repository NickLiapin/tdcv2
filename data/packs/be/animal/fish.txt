description: Fish (Belarusian). THE FIRST TWO THIRDS ARE FRESHWATER because Belarus is landlocked and every fish a Belarusian catches comes out of a river, a lake or a fish farm — Шчупак, Лешч, Карась and Плотка are the everyday catch of the Prypiac and the Braslaw lakes. The sea fish at the end are all things bought in a shop, which is why Селядзец (salted herring) is culturally the most important of them and Траска is second.
