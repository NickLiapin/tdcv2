description: Musical instrument (Belarusian). THE FIRST FOUR ARE BELARUSIAN FOLK INSTRUMENTS and are not decoration: цымбалы, the hammered dulcimer, is the national instrument and is taught as a principal study in Belarusian music schools right up to conservatory level, which is true nowhere else; дуда is the Belarusian bagpipe; жалейка is a single-reed pipe and ліра the hurdy-gurdy the wandering singers carried. Баян is the Russian-type chromatic button accordion and is far commoner in Belarus than the piano accordion.
