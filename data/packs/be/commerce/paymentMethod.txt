ЕРІП (Адзіная разліковая і інфармацыйная прастора, branded «Разлік») is a single state clearing hub through which almost every bill in the country is paid — utilities, fines, tuition, online orders — from any bank's app by looking the payee up in one shared tree, and no other country has an equivalent single point. БЕЛКАРТ is the Belarusian national card scheme; state salaries and pensions are commonly paid onto BELKART cards, which do not work abroad. Халва is an interest-free instalment card and растэрміноўка by card is an ordinary way to buy an appliance here.
