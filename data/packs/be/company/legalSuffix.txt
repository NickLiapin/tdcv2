description: Legal form of a Belarusian company, in the abbreviation that goes with the name. THESE ARE BELARUS'S OWN FORMS, because Belarusian is the language of one country and the abbreviations are what a Belarusian register prints. THE UNITARY ENTERPRISE (УП) HAS NO WESTERN EQUIVALENT: its property is not owned by the enterprise but stays with its single founder, so ПУП, ДУП, РУП and КУП are four different owners of the same legal shape — private, state, republican and municipal — and the letter in front says which. ААТ and ЗАТ are the open and closed joint-stock companies; ТАА is the ordinary limited company; СВК is the agricultural production cooperative, which is what most collective farms were converted into. The forms are written in the abbreviated Belarusian form; the Russian-language register writes the same entities ОАО, ЗАО, ООО.
