description: Dish, named in Belarusian, Belarusian cooking first. ДРАНІКІ ARE THE NATIONAL DISH and the word is worth knowing: they are grated raw potato fried as a pancake, and the potato is not incidental — Belarus eats among the most potatoes per head in the world and бульба is a national nickname. FOUR ITEMS HAVE NO ENGLISH NAME AT ALL: мачанка is a thick pork gravy that pancakes are dipped into and is a whole meal; верашчака is its older, sourer version; бабка is grated potato baked in the oven with pork; кулага is a berry-and-flour dessert cooked slowly, and it is what Belarusian folklore means by a sweet. Крамбамбуля is a spiced honey spirit, historically Belarusian and now made again commercially.
