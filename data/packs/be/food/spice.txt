description: Spice or seasoning (Belarusian). Belarusian cooking is a herb cuisine rather than a spice cuisine: кроп, часнок, хрэн, кмен and лаўровы ліст do most of the work, and кмен in particular goes into bread, cheese and sauerkraut here in a way it does not further west. The tropical spices at the end are all imports and all arrived through the same trade the rest of Europe used.
locale: be
