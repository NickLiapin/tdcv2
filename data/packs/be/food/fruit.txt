description: Fruit and berry (Belarusian). THE BERRIES ARE NOT A GARNISH HERE: журавіны grow on the Polesian bogs, чарніцы and брусніцы in the pine forests, and picking them is a household activity with a season and a law regulating it. Belarus is one of Europe's largest wild-cranberry producers. Антонаўка is a named apple variety so common in Belarusian gardens that it functions as a fruit word of its own.
