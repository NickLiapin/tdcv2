description: Religion or confession as Belarusian names it. BELARUS IS THE SLAVIC CONFESSIONAL BORDER and the list has to show it: Праваслаўе is the largest confession, but Каталіцтва is around a sixth of the population and is the majority in the Hrodna region, which is why Belarusian keeps a full Catholic vocabulary (касцёл for a Catholic church against царква for an Orthodox one). УНІЯЦТВА — the Greek Catholic church of the 1596 Union of Brest — WAS THE FAITH OF THE MAJORITY OF BELARUSIANS from the seventeenth century until it was abolished by the Russian empire in 1839, so it is not a fringe entry here but the country's largest former church. Іслам belongs to the Lipka Tatars, settled since the fourteenth century; Іудаізм to a community that was a third of the urban population before 1941.
