description: Compass direction (Belarusian), the sixteen points. THE INTERCARDINALS ARE TWO WORDS in Belarusian — паўночны ўсход, not a single compound as in Macedonian or German — because the first half is an adjective agreeing with the second. EVERY ONE OF THE EIGHT CONTAINING "ўсход" SHOWS THE Ў RULE: у becomes ў after a vowel, so it is паўночны ўсход with ў but Усход with У when the word starts the phrase. That alternation is automatic and obligatory in Belarusian and exists in no other Slavic language.
