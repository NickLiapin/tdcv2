Even so the old pink пашпарт gramadzianina is still in circulation and still valid. ПРАЦОЎНАЯ КНІЖКА has no Western equivalent: a paper booklet in which every employer records the dates and reason for leaving, kept by the employer while you work there, and it decides the pension. Даведка is the general word for an official certificate and a Belarusian life needs a great many of them.
