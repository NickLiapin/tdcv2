Brittany's own connection to the medium is industrial rather than linguistic: Rennes has hosted Stunfest since 2005, one of the oldest video game festivals in France, and the region's digital industry grew out of state telecommunications research planted at Lannion in the 1960s, which is how a small town inside the Breton-speaking Trégor ended up with a fibre-optics laboratory in it.
