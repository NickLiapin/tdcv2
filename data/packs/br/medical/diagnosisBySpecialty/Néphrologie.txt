Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease is the entry that behaves unlike the rest: it is the commonest inherited kidney disease anywhere and a leading genetic cause of dialysis, it runs visibly down a family tree, and it is very often found by an ultrasound ordered for something else entirely. Nephrology is also the one French specialty with a complete national count behind it, the REIN register, which has recorded every dialysis and transplant patient in the country region by region since the mid-2000s, so regional figures here are measured rather than modelled. Its entry forms, like these labels, are French.
