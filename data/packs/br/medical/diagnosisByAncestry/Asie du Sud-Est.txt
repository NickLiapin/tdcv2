description: Conditions more frequent in people of Southeast Asian descent. Syndrome de Brugada earns a place in an ancestry file rather than a cardiology one: it is an inherited sodium-channel disorder that kills young men in their sleep, described in the region under local names for a nocturnal death long before an electrocardiogram explained it, and it remains a leading cause of sudden death under forty in Thailand, Laos and the Philippines. Haemoglobin E is the other regional marker, harmless on its own but producing a severe transfusion-dependent anaemia when it meets a beta-thalassaemia allele, which is why carrier status is worth establishing before a couple has children. The haemoglobin electrophoresis report that settles it is a French-language laboratory document, like every label in this folder.
