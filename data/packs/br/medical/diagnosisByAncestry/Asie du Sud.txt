description: Conditions more frequent in people of South Asian descent, and the file where the numeric thresholds in a config will betray you. Cardiometabolic risk arrives at a lower body-mass index in this population, which is why the World Health Organization set an overweight cut-off near 23 rather than 25 for it, and why Obésité centrale à indice de masse corporelle normal is a real presentation and not a contradiction in terms: filter these patients on a BMI above 30 and most of them disappear from the extract. Diabetes and coronary disease also appear a decade earlier than European averages predict, early enough that several of these qualify for ALD status, the affection de longue durée that makes French care for a chronic illness fully reimbursed. The labels follow that same administrative paperwork and are French.
