description: Chinese dishes as a Breton traiteur or restaurant lists them, and the list contains its own explanation: nems and rouleaux de printemps are Vietnamese and not Chinese, and they are here because the Chinese restaurant trade in France was built in large part by ethnic-Chinese refugees from Vietnam, Cambodia and Laos who arrived after 1975. That is why a French chinois menu is Cantonese and Teochew with a South-East Asian seam running through it, and why riz cantonais — a menu name used in France and not in Guangzhou — appears on almost every one of them.
