description: Technical abbreviations, used unchanged in Breton technical writing exactly as they are in French and English. Nothing here is translated and nothing should be: an abbreviation is a shape on a screen before it is a phrase, and the Breton Wikipedia, the Breton Firefox and the Breton LibreOffice all leave these alone while translating the prose around them. That is worth stating because the rest of this folder does the opposite — Breton has a real, deliberately built computing vocabulary, and the contrast between a fully calqued noun like urzhiataer and an untouched CPU is the actual shape of the register. Note that Breton has no letter names of its own for reading these aloud in the way French does: a speaker will spell out C-P-U with French letter names in one breath and finish the sentence in Breton, which is normal Breton technical speech and not code-switching in any interesting sense.
locale: br
