LAHUTA, the single-string bowed lute, is the instrument the northern epic is sung to and it has no orchestral equivalent; CIFTELIA has two strings, one for melody and one drone, and is the emblematic Albanian folk instrument; the GAJDE is the bagpipe of the south and the SHARKIA the larger long-necked lute.
