description: Country, as ALBANIAN names it, weighted by population in millions. DEFINITE FORM. Albanian marks definiteness with a suffix, and a country standing alone as a label takes it: the country is Shqiperia, Gjermania, Italia, not the indefinite Shqiperi, Gjermani, Itali that appear after a preposition (ne Shqiperi, in Albania). This differs from albania.geo.county in the Albania COUNTRY pack, which ships the indefinite form the domestic administration uses — the two conventions are both correct and they are not interchangeable. Names are TRANSLATED, not transliterated: Germany is Gjermania and the United Kingdom is Mbreteria e Bashkuar. The four Albanian-speaking or Albanian-neighbouring states are all here — Shqiperia, Kosova, Maqedonia e Veriut and Mali i Zi — because a pack in this language that cannot name Kosovo is not usable.
