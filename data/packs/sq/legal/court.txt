description: The WORDS Albanian uses for kinds of court — generic legal vocabulary, not one country's judiciary. Albanian is the official language of two states and of a third country's second language, so a language pack cannot name a real bench: Albania's actual courts, reorganised by the judicial map of 1 February 2023, live in albania.government.court, and Kosovo's are different again (Kosovo has Gjykata Themelore where Albania has Gjykata e Shkalles se Pare). GJYKATA E KASACIONIT is here because it is the Kosovo-side term a reader may meet; the Albanian supreme court is Gjykata e Larte.
