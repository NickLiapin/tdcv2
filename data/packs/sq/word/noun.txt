description: Ordinary Albanian noun, INDEFINITE SINGULAR — and this file is where the pack records the decision, because it affects almost every list here.
  ALBANIAN NOUNS HAVE TWO FORMS AND THE DEFINITE ARTICLE IS A SUFFIX, not a separate word. mal is 'a mountain' and mali is 'the mountain'; vajze is 'a girl' and vajza 'the girl'; liber / libri, koke / koka, qytet / qyteti, uje / uji. THIS PACK SHIPS THE INDEFINITE FORM throughout, because that is the citation form, the form a dictionary lists and the form that follows a number or the article nje.
  What that means for a config: joining a bare value from this pack into a sentence that needs a definite noun produces the wrong Albanian, and the fix is NOT to append a letter. The suffix depends on gender and on the final sound — masculine nouns take -i (qytet -> qyteti) or -u after a velar or a vowel (mik -> miku, zog -> zogu), feminine take -a (vajze -> vajza, dropping the final -e), and a fair number are irregular (djale -> djali, uje -> uji, dimer -> dimri, which loses a vowel). There is no rule a template can apply blindly.
  Where a category is conventionally cited definite instead, the pack follows the convention and says so in that file: sq.geo.country and sq.location.country (Gjermania, not Gjermani), sq.person.zodiac (Dashi), and sq.address.streetSuffix (Rruga).
locale: sq
