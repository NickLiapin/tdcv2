description: Kind of official document, in Albanian. CERTIFIKATE PERSONALE is the extract from the civil register that an Albanian is asked for at almost every counter — it is issued on demand and expires in ninety days, so it is a document people obtain repeatedly rather than hold. LETERNJOFTIM is the identity card; the word for passport is pasaporte.
