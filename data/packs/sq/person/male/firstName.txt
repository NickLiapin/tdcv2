description: Albanian male given names.
  Three strata sit in this list and they are not interchangeable. The Muslim-origin stock (Bajram, Halil, Ismail, Rexhep) is the largest, because Albania and Kosovo are majority Muslim. The Christian stock (Gjergj, Gjon, Kole, Pjeter, Zef, Marash) is northern Catholic and southern Orthodox and is a real minority, not decoration. The third stratum is the one no neighbour has: names MADE from ordinary Albanian words during the national revival and again under communism, when religious names were discouraged — Agim is 'dawn', Genc, Agron, Teuta and Bardhyl are Illyrian rulers, Fatmir is 'good fortune', Shpetim is 'salvation', Ylber is 'rainbow', Kushtrim is the war-cry. These are transparent to any Albanian speaker in a way that Peter and John are not to an English one.
  WEIGHTS. There is no published frequency table of given names for Albania, so the counts here are the project's default gentle decay curve and not measurements — and unusually, that curve is close to right for this language. The commonest name in Kosovo, Bajram, is borne by 4,485 people out of about 1.6 million (census ReKos 2024), roughly four tenths of one per cent; Albanian given names really are that evenly spread, so a sharp curve would be the lie here rather than the flat one. The ORDER, which is what the curve turns into a weight, is evidence-based: the names the Kosovo census reports as commonest first, then the stock that is verifiable across public life, then names attested in Newmark's Albanian-English Dictionary (OUP 1998) and in the Fjalor me emra njerezish (Tirane 1982).
  A generational break runs straight through this list and a config drawing a whole population from it will mix two eras. The names at the top belong to people born before about 1990; children registered in Albania in the last decade are overwhelmingly given short international names — Noel, Aron, Andrea, Sara, Amelia — which the Civil Status office reports as the most-registered names of the last five years. Both kinds are in the list. If you need one era, filter.
