description: Albanian honorific, in the abbreviated form written before a name. Z. is zoti (Mr), ZNJ. is zonja (Mrs) and ZJ. is zonjusha (Miss). The religious titles are the interesting part of this list and they are four traditions in one small country: DOM before a Catholic priest, AT before a monk or an Orthodox father, HOXHE before a Sunni imam, and BABA before a Bektashi cleric — the Bektashi order has its world headquarters in Tirana, so BABA is a live Albanian title and not a borrowing. Academic titles in Albanian go BEFORE the name, never after it: Prof. Dr. Emri Mbiemri.
