description: Albanian short form or pet name. These are not made-up diminutives: the northern Catholic short forms are so established that they function as full names in their own right — NDUE and NDOC come from Antonio, PRENG and PRELE from Prendush, LEKE from Aleksander, ZEF from Jozef, KOLE from Nikolle, LUSH from Luigj (Luigj Gurakuqi was Lush to everyone). The commonest live suffixes are -i, which is simply the definite article doing duty as a pet form (Agim -> Gimi, Ardit -> Ardi), and -ush / -ushe, an affectionate ending (Verushe, Merushe). CUNI, literally 'the boy', and LALI, 'the elder brother', are used to a man of any age.
locale: sq
