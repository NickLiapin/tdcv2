description: Academic qualification (Albanian). Albania joined the Bologna process, so the ladder runs Bachelor (three years), Master Profesional or Master i Shkencave (two), Doktoraturë. MASTER I SHKENCAVE and MASTER PROFESIONAL are NOT interchangeable: only the first admits its holder to a doctorate. Diploma e Maturës Shtetërore is the school-leaving certificate, awarded on the state matura examination that also decides university admission.
