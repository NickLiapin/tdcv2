description: Colour name (Albanian). THE FORM MATTERS HERE. Albanian adjectives agree with their noun, and a colour named on its own agrees with NGJYRA, colour, which is feminine — so the native colours carry the feminine article e and the feminine ending: e kuqe, e bardhe, e zeze. Attached to a masculine noun they change: nje fustan i kuq, not e kuqe. The borrowed names (blu, roze, bezhe, gri, portokalli, vjollce, bordo) never inflect at all, which is why they look bare beside the native ones. A config that puts these straight after a masculine noun will produce agreement errors on every native colour and none of the borrowed ones.
locale: sq
