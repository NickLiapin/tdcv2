description: Job title (Albanian, masculine form). The feminine exists and differs — mesues / mesuese, infermier / infermiere, shites / shitese — so a record that pairs one of these with a woman's name is grammatically wrong unless the config swaps the ending. That swap is not mechanical: most take -e, but avokat and mjek are used unchanged for women in official Albanian, and a few are lexically different.
