description: The world's countries as Albanian names them, alphabetical by the Albanian name. DEFINITE FORM throughout, matching sq.geo.country — Gjermania not Gjermani. Names are translated, never transliterated: the Ivory Coast is Bregu i Fildishte, Cape Verde is Kepi i Gjelber, Montenegro is Mali i Zi. Two pairs catch people out and both are in this list: NIGERI is Niger and NIGERIA is Nigeria, one letter apart, and MALI is the west African republic while the identical Albanian common noun mali means 'the mountain'.
