description: Dish (Albanian). The list opens with what an Albanian household actually cooks. TAVE KOSI is the national dish — lamb baked under a set custard of yoghurt and egg — and it is the one to know: yoghurt is not a side in Albanian cooking, it is a baking medium. BYREK is the filo pie that is also breakfast, and FLIA, the layered pancake cooked under a lid heaped with embers, is the dish of Kosovo and the northern highlands rather than of Tirana.
