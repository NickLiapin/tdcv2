description: Sport (Albanian). The order is Albanian rather than international: football first by a very long way, then basketball and volleyball, then the individual sports Albania and Kosovo actually win medals in — PESHENGRITJE, weightlifting, gave Albania its Olympic weightlifters, and XHUDO gave Kosovo its first Olympic gold through Majlinda Kelmendi. American football, baseball and cricket are at the bottom because essentially nobody here plays them.
