description: Legal form appended to an Albanian company name. SH.P.K. (shoqeri me pergjegjesi te kufizuar) is the limited company and by far the commonest; SH.A. is the joint-stock company. Kosovo uses the same two forms, and D.O.O. appears where a company registered under the Serbian-language form. NIPT-holding sole traders are registered as PERSON FIZIK, which is a status rather than a suffix but is written the same way on an invoice.
locale: sq
