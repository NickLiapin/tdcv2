description: Weekday (Albanian). Every weekday name carries the article E and it is part of the name, not a preposition: the day is E HENE, never henë alone. These are the indefinite forms used after nje or in a list; the definite forms used as a subject are e hena, e marta, e merkura, e enjtja, e premtja, e shtuna, e diela. Monday first, as the Albanian week runs.
