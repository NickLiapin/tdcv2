description: Leading element of an Albanian place name (Fushe-Kruje, Vau i Dejes, Shengjin, Zall-Herr). It does not by itself name a real settlement — compose it with a toponym. Shen- is the reduced form of shenjt, saint, and it fuses with the following name rather than standing apart: Shen + Kolle gives Shenkoll, not Shen Kolle.
locale: sq
