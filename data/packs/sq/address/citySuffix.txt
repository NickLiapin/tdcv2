description: Trailing qualifier of an Albanian place name (Malesi e Madhe, Berzhite e Siperme, Kukes Qender). The qualifier is an ADJECTIVE and it agrees with the gender of the settlement noun, which is why both members of each pair are here: i Madh for a masculine name, e Madhe for a feminine one. A config that always picks one of the pair will produce agreement errors on half the rows.
