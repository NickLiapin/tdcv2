description: Albanian thoroughfare word — it goes BEFORE the name, as in Polish and unlike English: Rruga Myslym Shyri, Bulevardi Deshmoret e Kombit, Sheshi Skenderbej. Note these are the DEFINITE forms (rruga, bulevardi, sheshi), not the indefinite rruge, bulevard, shesh, because an Albanian address header is always definite; a config that joins the indefinite noun to a street name produces something no Albanian would write. Where the name that follows is a place rather than a person the thoroughfare takes an ablative with e: Rruga e Kavajes, Rruga e Durresit.
locale: sq
