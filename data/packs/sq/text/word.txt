Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Albanian book at all — a search of the catalogue for language sq returns 'No records found', and gutenberg.org/browse/languages/sq is a 404, so there was no public-domain corpus to build this from and it was composed by hand. Every other locale whose text comes from real books says so in its own description; this one says the opposite, and the difference is the point. The vocabulary is ordinary modern standard (Tosk-based) Albanian, chosen so that the list exercises the alphabet rather than avoiding it: Ë, the schwa, which rivals plain e for the commonest letter in the language, and Ç, and every one of the nine digraphs dh, gj, ll, nj, rr, sh, th, xh, zh. A word list for this language that carries almost no ë is not Albanian, and that is the single easiest way to catch a pack that was transliterated instead of written.
