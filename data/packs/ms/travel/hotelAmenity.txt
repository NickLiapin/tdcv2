description: Hotel amenity (Malay). PERCUMA IS THE WORD TO NOTICE: in Malaysia it means free of charge and is what 'free Wi-Fi' is written as, while in Indonesian percuma means futile or worthless — so 'Wi-Fi percuma' reads as complimentary internet on one side of the Straits and useless internet on the other. The last four are standard in Malaysian hotels and absent from a European list: a SURAU is a small prayer room, every room carries a PENUNJUK ARAH KIBLAT marking the direction of Mecca, a SEJADAH is a prayer mat available on request, and halal certification of the kitchen is a booking criterion rather than a footnote.
