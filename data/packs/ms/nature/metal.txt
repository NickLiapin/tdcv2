description: Metal (Malay). THE FALSE FRIEND HERE IS DANGEROUS: Malaysian KELULI is steel and Malaysian BAJA is fertiliser, while Indonesian BAJA is steel. A word-for-word translation between the two languages turns a steel mill into a fertiliser plant. GANGSA is bronze where Indonesian says perunggu, and SUASA is a red gold-and-copper alloy with a Malay name and no English one. TIMAH, tin, is on this list for a reason: tin mining is what drew the Chinese and British to the peninsula and what founded Kuala Lumpur and Ipoh.
