description: Property type (Malay). RUMAH TERES, the terraced link house, is what most of urban Malaysia lives in and is quoted as setingkat or dua tingkat — one storey or two. RUMAH KEDAI is the shophouse, shop below and dwelling above, the basic building of every old town centre. SOHO — small office home office — is a Malaysian developer's category with its own licensing. RUMAH PANJANG is the Iban and Bidayuh longhouse of Sarawak, a single structure holding dozens of family units, and it is a real residential property type there and nowhere else.
