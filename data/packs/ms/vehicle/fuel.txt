Malaysian petrol is sold by RESEARCH OCTANE NUMBER and everyone says the number rather than the word: RON95 is the subsidised grade, capped by the government and available to citizens, and RON97 is unsubsidised and floats — so the grade a Malaysian buys is an economic fact about them as well as a technical one about the car. NGV is compressed natural gas, mostly used by taxis.
