description: Dish (Malay), as eaten in Malaysia. Nasi lemak is the national dish and is eaten at breakfast. Note the Chinese-Malaysian names are romanised from HOKKIEN, CANTONESE and TEOCHEW, not from Mandarin — char kway teow, bak kut teh, chee cheong fun, kolo mee — and are written that way on every menu in the country, so they are not misspellings of pinyin. Several look Indonesian and are not: Malaysian laksa splits into asam (sour, tamarind and fish) and lemak (coconut curry) and neither is Indonesia's laksa, and Malaysian rojak is a fruit salad in prawn paste, not Indonesia's rujak.
