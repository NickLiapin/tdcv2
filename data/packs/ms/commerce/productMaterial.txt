description: Product material (Malay), as a noun — compose with 'daripada': 'meja daripada kayu jati'. The timber block is the Malaysian hardwood trade's own vocabulary, sold under these Malay names worldwide. KELULI is steel and KELULI TAHAN KARAT stainless — remember that Malaysian BAJA means fertiliser, so the Indonesian word for steel is a different substance here. Rattan, mengkuang and pandan are woven materials with no English one-word equivalents.
