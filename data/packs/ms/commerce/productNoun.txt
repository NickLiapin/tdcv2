description: Product noun (Malay). Several of the commonest household items are named differently from Indonesian and this list is where a translated pack shows: KERETA is a car in Malaysia and a TRAIN in Indonesia, which is the single most consequential false friend between the two languages; PETI SEJUK is the fridge (Indonesian kulkas, from Dutch koelkast), MESIN BASUH the washing machine (mesin cuci), TUALA the towel (handuk, from Dutch handdoek), CADAR the bedsheet (sprei), KATIL the bed (tempat tidur), CAWAN the cup (cangkir), SUDU the spoon (sendok), BEG the bag (tas), KASUT the shoes (sepatu), SELUAR the trousers (celana) and BASIKAL the bicycle (sepeda). Almost every one of those pairs is English-route Malay against Dutch-route Indonesian.
locale: ms
