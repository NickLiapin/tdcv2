description: Payment method (Malay). DUITNOW and DUITNOW QR are Malaysia's national real-time transfer and QR rails and are now the default way one Malaysian pays another; FPX is the online banking gateway that nearly every Malaysian merchant site offers, and it debits a bank account directly rather than a card. TOUCH 'N GO started as the toll and transit card and became the largest e-wallet in the country. 'Bayar semasa terima' is cash on delivery and is still very common outside the cities.
