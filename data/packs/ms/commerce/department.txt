description: Store department or category (Malay). Note BARANGAN RUNCIT for groceries and PASAR RAYA for the supermarket itself. The halal and non-halal division at the end is not a nicety: Malaysian supermarkets physically separate the pork section behind its own entrance and give it separate trolleys and separate checkouts, so 'daging' and 'bahagian tidak halal' are different departments in the same shop.
