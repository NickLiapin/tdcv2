description: Malaysian job title in Malay, across sectors. THIS IS THE FILE WHERE MALAY AND INDONESIAN PART COMPANY MOST VISIBLY, because Malaysia named its professions off English and Indonesia off Dutch: JURUTERA is the engineer (Indonesian insinyur, from ingenieur), PEGUAM the lawyer (pengacara), JURURAWAT the nurse (perawat), PENSYARAH the university lecturer (dosen), SETIAUSAHA the secretary (sekretaris), AKAUNTAN the accountant (akuntan), JURUWANG the cashier (kasir) and PEMANDU the driver — and that last one is a trap, because Indonesian PEMANDU is a tour guide and its driver is a sopir. Kerani is the clerk. JURUUKUR BAHAN, the quantity surveyor, is a whole profession that exists in Commonwealth construction and nowhere in the Dutch-descended world. PENOREH GETAH is a rubber tapper and PEKEBUN KECIL a smallholder — two of the largest occupations in rural Malaysia. Cikgu is what a schoolteacher is called to their face; guru is the word on the payroll. One more false friend, and it is a job title in both languages: Malaysian PENGACARA is a compere — a news presenter or a master of ceremonies, which is why 'pengacara berita' and 'pengacara majlis' are on this list — while Indonesian PENGACARA is a lawyer. Neither language would read the other's job advertisement correctly.
