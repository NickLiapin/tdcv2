description: Mining method (Malay). Two of these are the vocabulary of Malaysian tin and exist nowhere else: PAM KELIKIR, the gravel-pump mine, was the small-operator method that most Malaysian tin came out of, and KAPAL KOREK, the bucket-ladder dredge, was the industrial one — the surviving dredge at Tanjung Tualang is a national monument. Lombong lampan is the older sluice-and-palong washing method.
