description: Company department (Malay). JABATAN is a department in Malaysia — and that is a FALSE FRIEND worth knowing, because Indonesian jabatan means a post or position, while Indonesian's department is departemen or dinas. Malay's word for the position is JAWATAN, one vowel away. Sumber Manusia is human resources; note the category holding those files here is 'workplace' and not 'hr', because hr is also the language code for Croatian.
