description: Technology noun (Malay). DEWAN BAHASA DAN PUSTAKA COINED MOST OF THESE RATHER THAN BORROWING THEM, and the results differ from Indonesian at nearly every term: PERISIAN is software (Indonesian perangkat lunak, a calque), PERKAKASAN hardware (perangkat keras), PANGKALAN DATA a database (basis data), RANGKAIAN a network (jaringan), FAIL a file (berkas), CAKERA a disk (cakram), SKRIN a screen (layar), KATA LALUAN a password (kata sandi), PELAYAR a browser (peramban) and LAMAN WEB a website (situs web). Malay generally kept the English root and wrapped native morphology round it; Indonesian more often translated the meaning.
