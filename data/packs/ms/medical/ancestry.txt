These are ancestry populations as clinical genetics defines them — geographic descent, not social race categories. Note EROPAH, not the Indonesian Eropa, and PERIBUMI for indigenous. Asia Tenggara is the one that matters most in a Malaysian dataset: it is the ancestry of the majority of the population and it carries alpha-thalassaemia, haemoglobin E and G6PD deficiency at rates that make all three routine newborn-screening targets in Malaysia rather than rare findings.
