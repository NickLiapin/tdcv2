description: Common generic drug name (Malay). Malaysia prescribes under BRITISH APPROVED NAMES, not the American ones, because its pharmacopoeia and its doctors' training both came through the United Kingdom: FRUSEMIDE not furosemide, salbutamol not albuterol, paracetamol not acetaminophen, adrenaline not epinephrine. Indonesian took its drug names through Dutch and lands on a third set of spellings again. The Malay spellings below follow DBP transliteration — k for c, s for c before e and i, -il for -yl.
