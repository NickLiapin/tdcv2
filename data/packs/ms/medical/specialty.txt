description: Medical specialty (Malay). SAKIT PUAN is the one no dictionary prepares you for: it is literally 'women's illness' and it is the standard Malaysian name for gynaecology, printed on hospital signboards and on referral letters. PERGIGIAN is dentistry, from gigi, tooth — Indonesian says kedokteran gigi. Note that Malaysian clinical practice runs largely in English despite all of this; these are the Malay forms used on signage, in patient-facing material and in government documents.
