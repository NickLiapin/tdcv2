MALAYSIA RUNS TWO PARALLEL INSURANCE SYSTEMS and a config that models only one is modelling half the market: conventional INSURANS and TAKAFUL, the Syariah-compliant form, in which participants contribute to a mutual fund rather than buy a transfer of risk, because conventional insurance involves elements Islamic law does not permit. Every major Malaysian insurer offers both through separate licensed entities, and the products pair up — insurans hayat with takaful keluarga, insurans motor with takaful motor. PERKESO is the statutory social security scheme and is not optional.
