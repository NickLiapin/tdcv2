description: Budget or spending category (Malay). ZAKAT is on this list because it is a real recurring line in a Malaysian Muslim household budget: it is a religious obligation of two and a half per cent of qualifying wealth, it is collected by a state religious authority, and what is paid is deductible against federal income tax ringgit for ringgit — so it is simultaneously a charity, a tax and a budget item. DUIT RAYA is the cash given to children and elders at Hari Raya and is a genuine annual expense. TOL is a category of its own because the Malaysian expressway network is tolled end to end.
