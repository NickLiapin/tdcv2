description: Loan type (Malay). The -I suffix is the marker of a Syariah-compliant product — pembiayaan peribadi-i, pembiayaan perumahan-i — and those are not loans at all in the legal sense: Islamic finance forbids interest, so the bank buys the asset and sells it on at a marked-up deferred price, or leases it. That is why the Malay word shifts from PINJAMAN, a loan, to PEMBIAYAAN, a financing. AR-RAHNU is Islamic pawnbroking, where the pawnbroker charges a safekeeping fee rather than interest, and it is widely used for small short-term credit against gold.
