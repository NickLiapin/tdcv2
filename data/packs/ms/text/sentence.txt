description: Malay filler sentences. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Malay book at all, so unlike fr/de/it/es/pt/pl these are not extracted from a public-domain corpus. Written in Malaysian standard Malay, not Indonesian — kereta is a car, tingkap a window, bilik a room, petang the afternoon, kerana rather than karena. Notice that no verb in any of these carries a tense: 'membuka' is the same form whether the window was opened yesterday or will be tomorrow, and where time matters a separate word does the work — sudah, sedang, akan, masih.
