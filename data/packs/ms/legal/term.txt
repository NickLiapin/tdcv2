description: Legal term (Malay). RAYUAN is an appeal where Indonesian says banding; PEGUAM is an advocate and solicitor — in Malaysia one profession, not two as in England — and PENDAKWA RAYA is the public prosecutor, the office Indonesian calls jaksa. Note IKAT JAMIN for bail and SABIT KESALAHAN for a conviction. A Malaysian judgment is written in either Malay or English: the subordinate courts work mainly in Malay and the higher courts largely in English, so a Malaysian legal dataset is genuinely bilingual and a config that assumes one language will mislabel half of it.
