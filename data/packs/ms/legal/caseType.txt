description: Legal case type (Malay). GUAMAN is a civil suit where Indonesian says gugatan; SAMAN is a summons, and in everyday Malaysian speech a saman is specifically the traffic ticket. KEBANKRAPAN is bankruptcy (Indonesian kepailitan). The Syariah entries at the end can only arise between Muslims and only in the Syariah courts: FARAID is the fixed-share Islamic inheritance calculation, NAFKAH is maintenance, HADANAH is custody of a child, and talak, fasakh and khuluk are three legally distinct routes out of a marriage rather than three words for divorce.
locale: ms
