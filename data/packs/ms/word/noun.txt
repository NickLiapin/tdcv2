description: Common noun (Malay). Two things a config cannot infer. PLURALS ARE MADE BY REDUPLICATION, not by a suffix: buku becomes buku-buku, kanak-kanak is already plural in shape, and appending -s or -an produces something that is not Malay. Most of the time the plural is not marked at all — 'tiga buku' is three books with the noun untouched — so reduplication is emphatic rather than obligatory. And AFFIXATION IS THE WHOLE WORD-BUILDING MACHINE: one root yields a dozen nouns through pe-, pen-, per-, -an, -kan and the circumfix ke-...-an. From 'ajar' (teach) Malay builds pengajar (teacher), pelajar (pupil), pengajaran (the teaching), pelajaran (the lesson), pembelajaran (learning) and ajaran (a doctrine) — six nouns, one root, and a stemmer that strips affixes will collapse all six. This list holds the finished words, in Malaysian rather than Indonesian forms where the two differ.
