description: Common adverb (Malay). This list carries the words that do the work a European verb ending would do: sudah and telah mark a completed action, sedang marks one in progress, akan marks the future, belum marks not-yet and pernah marks ever-having. Malay puts them BESIDE the verb and leaves the verb alone, so 'dia sudah pergi' and 'dia akan pergi' differ by one free-standing word. Without one of these a Malay sentence is simply untensed, and context supplies the time.
locale: ms
