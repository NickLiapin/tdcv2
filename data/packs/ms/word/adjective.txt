description: Common adjective (Malay). Malay adjectives FOLLOW the noun — 'rumah besar' is the big house, never 'besar rumah' — and they do not agree with it in gender or number, because Malay has neither. Comparison is analytic and made with separate words: lebih besar (bigger), paling besar or terbesar (biggest), sebesar (as big as). Several here are Malaysian where Indonesian says something else: sejuk is COLD in Malaysia and merely cool in Indonesia, senang is EASY in Malaysia and content in Indonesia, sulit is CONFIDENTIAL in Malaysia and difficult in Indonesia, and comel is cute in Malaysia and naggingly talkative in Indonesia. Those four are false friends, not synonyms.
