description: Leave type (Malay). CUTI HAJI is on this list because it is real: Malaysian employers commonly grant unpaid or special leave for the pilgrimage to Mecca, which for most Malaysians is a once-in-a-lifetime absence of several weeks, and the state pilgrims' fund exists to save for it. Cuti bersalin is maternity leave — 98 days in the private sector since 2023 — and cuti paterniti is the seven-day paternity entitlement introduced at the same time.
