description: Language name (Malay). Malay names a language with the word 'bahasa' in front, and the country adjective that follows is the Malaysian spelling, which differs from Indonesian more often than it agrees: Bahasa Inggeris not Inggris, Bahasa Jepun not Jepang, Bahasa Perancis not Prancis, Bahasa Sepanyol not Spanyol, Bahasa Cina not Tionghoa. The top of the list is Malaysia's real linguistic situation: Malay is the national language, English is the second language of business and the courts, and Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Hakka, Teochew and Tamil are spoken as first languages by millions. Iban, Kadazandusun, Bidayuh, Melanau, Bajau and Murut are the indigenous languages of Sabah and Sarawak. Kristang is the Portuguese creole still spoken in Melaka after five centuries, and Baba Malay is the Peranakan Malay of the Straits Chinese — both are Malaysian and endangered.
