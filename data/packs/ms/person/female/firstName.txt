description: Malay female given name, in the same three census-weighted blocks as the male list. TWO PREFIX ELEMENTS DOMINATE AND THEY DATE THE BEARER: Siti opens a very large share of Malay women born before about 1990, Nur and Nurul a very large share born after, and both are followed by a second name which is what the woman is actually called — Siti Nurhaliza is Nurhaliza to everyone. They are weighted heavily for that reason, not on the default curve. There is NO person/female/lastName.txt in this pack and its absence is deliberate: Malay names do not inflect for gender, so the surname slot is identical for both sexes. What differs is the CONNECTOR, not the name — binti for a daughter, bin for a son — and that belongs in the config, not in a name list. The -ah ending on Aminah, Azizah, Hafizah and the rest is Arabic feminine morphology carried in with the Arabic names; Malay itself has no gender. The Chinese Malaysian block is given names only and the family name comes FIRST (Lim Mei Ling), so render these lastName-then-firstName. Iman and Raihan also appear in the male list: they are unisex.
locale: ms
weighted: true
