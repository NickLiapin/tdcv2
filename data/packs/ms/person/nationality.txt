description: Nationality (Malay). Malay has no demonym suffix at all — no -ish, -ian or -ese. A nationality is the word 'orang' or 'warganegara' followed by the UNCHANGED country name: orang Malaysia, warganegara Jepun. This list holds the bare country form, which is what a form field carries, and the country spellings are Malaysian: Jepun not Jepang, Perancis not Prancis, Sepanyol not Spanyol, New Zealand not Selandia Baru, Emiriah Arab Bersatu not Uni Emirat Arab.
