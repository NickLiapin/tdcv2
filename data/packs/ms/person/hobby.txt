description: Hobby or interest (Malay). The traditional Malay pastimes near the top have no European counterpart and are named here rather than translated: wau is the giant Kelantanese kite, gasing the spinning top thrown by adults in competition, congkak the seed-and-hollow board game, sepak takraw the kick-volley game, silat the Malay martial art, and dikir barat, kompang and rebana are choral and percussion forms. Mengaji is Quranic recitation and is a daily activity for a large share of the population. Note badminton, not bulu tangkis — Malaysia uses the English name for the sport it is best at.
