description: Malay honorific and form of address. Encik, Puan and Cik are the everyday three (Mr, Mrs, Miss); Tuan is the formal Sir. The long middle block is MALAYSIA'S HONOURS SYSTEM and has no European equivalent: Tun, Tan Sri, Datuk Seri and Datuk are federal or state awards conferred by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong or a Ruler, they PRECEDE the name, they are used in every context including bank forms and news copy, and there are tens of thousands of holders, so a Malaysian dataset that never emits one is unrealistic. Datin is the wife of a Datuk, Puan Sri of a Tan Sri, Toh Puan of a Tun — the female form marks the husband's rank, not the woman's, and a woman who earns the award herself is Datuk or Datuk Seri in her own right. Yang Berhormat (YB) is for a sitting legislator. The last block — Tengku, Tunku, Raja, Nik, Wan, Che, Megat, Syed and Sharifah — are NOT honorifics but INHERITED NAME ELEMENTS that sit in front of the given name and pass down the family: Syed and Sharifah claim descent from the Prophet, Tengku and Raja mark royal descent, and Nik, Wan and Che are north-eastern family markers. They must not be stripped as titles. Haji and Hajjah are carried by anyone who has made the pilgrimage.
