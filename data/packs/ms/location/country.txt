description: Country and territory names (Malay), alphabetical. This is the same naming as geo/country.txt without the population weights, and it is where the English-versus-Dutch split shows up in bulk: Malaysia writes Poland, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Denmark, Iceland, Ireland, Greece, Hungary, Croatia, Cyprus, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Belgium, New Zealand, Mexico, Colombia, Brazil, Ecuador, Jamaica, Madagascar, Mozambique, Maldives and Ukraine essentially as English does, where Indonesian has Polandia, Swedia, Norwegia, Finlandia, Eslandia, Irlandia, Yunani, Hongaria, Kroasia, Siprus, Luksemburg, Swiss, Belgia, Selandia Baru, Meksiko, Kolombia, Brasil, Ekuador, Jamaika, Madagaskar, Mozambik, Maladewa and Ukraina. The Arabic-route names go the other way: Mesir, Maghribi, Lubnan. Names of Muslim-majority countries generally follow the Arabic form because that is how Malaysia has known them longest.
