description: Human-readable colour name (Malay). Three everyday colours differ from Indonesian: KELABU is grey (Indonesian abu-abu), PERANG is brown (Indonesian cokelat) and MERAH JAMBU is pink — literally rose-apple red — where Indonesian says merah muda. Malay also has coklat for brown, but it is the chocolate-coloured brown; perang is the brown of hair and skin. KUNING LANGSAT, the pale yellow of the langsat fruit, is a skin-tone word before it is a colour word, and sawo matang, ripe sapodilla, is its darker counterpart — both are complexion descriptions in ordinary Malay.
