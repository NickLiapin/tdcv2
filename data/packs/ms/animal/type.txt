description: Animal, general (Malay). Harimau Malaya, the Malayan tiger, is the national animal and stands on the coat of arms. KANCIL, the mousedeer, is not just a species but the trickster of Malay folk tales, the small clever one who outwits the tiger, and Malaysians name a car after it. Two everyday words differ from Indonesian by borrowing route: ARNAB is the rabbit, from Arabic, where Indonesian took kelinci from Dutch konijn, and BIRI-BIRI is the sheep where Indonesian says domba.
