description: Fish (Malay). Malay puts the word IKAN in front of most fish names, and the name alone is often meaningless without it. IKAN BILIS is the dried anchovy that goes into every nasi lemak — Indonesian calls the same fish teri. Siakap is the barramundi, keli the catfish (Indonesian lele) and haruan the snakehead (Indonesian gabus), a freshwater fish Malaysians eat after surgery because it is believed to help wounds close.
