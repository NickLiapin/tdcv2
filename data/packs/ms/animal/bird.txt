description: Bird (Malay). ENGGANG, the hornbill, is the emblem of Sarawak — Iban calls it kenyalang and the state is nicknamed Bumi Kenyalang, the land of the hornbill. Helang is the eagle, spelt without the initial e that Indonesian gives it. Tempua is the weaver bird whose hanging nest is a Malay figure of speech, and burung hantu, literally 'ghost bird', is the owl.
