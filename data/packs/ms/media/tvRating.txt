description: Film and television content rating in Malaysia. The three letter codes are the real classifications issued by the Lembaga Penapisan Filem, the national censorship board: U is general, P13 admits under-thirteens only with a parent, and 18 is adults only. That is the whole scale — Malaysia has no PG-13-and-R-and-NC-17 ladder — and the board also cuts films rather than only rating them, so a title released here may be shorter than the same title elsewhere. The descriptive Malay phrases after the codes are what appears on a broadcast caption.
