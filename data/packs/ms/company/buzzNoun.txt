description: Business buzz noun (Malay). THIS IS WHERE THE -ITI ENDING LIVES and it is the most systematic difference between written Malay and written Indonesian: an abstract noun that English ends in -ity takes -ITI in Malay and -ITAS in Indonesian, every time. Kualiti / kualitas, aktiviti / aktivitas, produktiviti / produktivitas, komuniti / komunitas, realiti / realitas, identiti / identitas, universiti / universitas. Malaysia took the English word and Indonesia the Dutch one, and the rule is regular enough that a reader can date a document to one country from a single noun.
