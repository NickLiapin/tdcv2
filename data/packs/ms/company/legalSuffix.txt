description: Company legal or name suffix used in Malaysia. SDN. BHD. — Sendirian Berhad — is THE Malaysian company form and appears after most business names in the country; BHD. alone, Berhad, is the public limited company listed on Bursa Malaysia; PLT is Perkongsian Liabiliti Terhad, the limited liability partnership. None of the three exists in Indonesia, which uses PT, CV and Tbk, so the suffix alone tells a reader which side of the Straits a company is registered on. The English words after them are not decoration: Malaysian company names are routinely half Malay and half English in the same registered name — Setia Jaya Resources Sdn Bhd — because English is a working language of business here.
