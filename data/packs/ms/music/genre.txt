description: Music genre (Malay), including the Malaysian ones. NASYID is Islamic vocal music, usually a cappella or lightly accompanied, and is a mainstream commercial genre here with chart acts and its own awards — not a niche. ASLI, GHAZAL, ZAPIN, JOGET, INANG and MASRI are the traditional Malay forms, and ghazal in particular is Johor's own, a Malayised Persian-Indian form played on harmonium and tabla. DIKIR BARAT is the Kelantanese call-and-response choral form and BORIA is Penang's. IRAMA MALAYSIA was a deliberate 1990s fusion of pop with those traditional rhythms. Dangdut and keroncong appear here as Indonesian imports that Malaysians listen to, not as Malaysian genres.
