description: Musical instrument (Malay). The first block has no European counterpart and is the actual instrument list of Malaysian traditional music: REBANA and KOMPANG are frame drums, kompang played in rows at weddings; SERUNAI is the double-reed shawm of the wayang kulit ensemble; GAMBUS is the Arab lute that came with the traders; SAPE is the boat lute of the Orang Ulu of Sarawak and is the sound most outsiders now associate with Borneo; SUNDATANG and KULINTANGAN are Sabahan; CAKLEMPONG is the Minangkabau-descended gong-chime set of Negeri Sembilan. Gamelan in Malaysia is the Terengganu and Pahang court version and is tuned differently from the Javanese one.
