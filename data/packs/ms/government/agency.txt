description: Government body (Malay) — Malaysian federal ministries, departments and statutory bodies. This names MALAYSIA's institutions because Malay has one country large enough to have a full apparatus; Brunei and Singapore use the same language for entirely different bodies, and a config for either should not draw from this list. JABATAN is a department and SURUHANJAYA a commission. Note the Diraja in Polis Diraja Malaysia and Jabatan Kastam Diraja Malaysia — 'royal', conferred by the King, and part of the legal name rather than an epithet. KWSP and PERKESO are the retirement and social security funds every employed Malaysian pays into.
