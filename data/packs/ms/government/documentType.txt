description: Official document type (Malay). The kinds of document, not one country's numbering scheme — the identity-card and passport NUMBER formats are country data and live in the Malaysia country pack. SIJIL is a certificate where Indonesian says sertifikat; SURAT AKUAN SUMPAH is a statutory declaration, which in Malaysia is a Commissioner-for-Oaths document used far more widely than an affidavit is in Europe. GERAN TANAH is the land title. Note that a Muslim marriage produces a Syariah court certificate and a non-Muslim marriage a National Registration Department one, so 'sijil perkahwinan' covers two different documents issued by two different authorities.
