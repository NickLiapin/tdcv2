description: Building type (Malay). Two Malaysian house types have no one-word English name and are given here as Malaysians use them: rumah teres is the terraced link house that most of urban Malaysia lives in, and rumah kampung is the traditional timber house on stilts. Kedai rumah is the shophouse — a shop on the ground floor with dwellings above — which is the basic unit of every old Malaysian town centre. Note the Malay word for room is BILIK, not the Indonesian kamar, and floor level is ARAS, not lantai.
