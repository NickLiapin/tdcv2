description: Malay thoroughfare type. Malay puts it BEFORE the name — Jalan Ampang, Lorong Melati, Persiaran Perdana — the opposite of English and the same as Indonesian. Beyond Jalan and Lorong the Malaysian set is its own: Persiaran is a boulevard, Lebuh a broad street, Lebuhraya a motorway, Susur a slip road, Changkat a street up a slope, Solok and Lengkok short curved streets in Penang and Kuala Lumpur, Medan a square. Taman, Bandar and Seksyen are not streets at all but the housing estate, township and numbered sector a street sits in, and a Malaysian address stacks them: 'No. 12, Jalan SS 15/4B, Taman Subang Jaya'. Malaysian street names are very often NUMBERED rather than named — Jalan 3/12A, Jalan PJU 8/5 — which is why a config joining a suffix to a name only models half the country's addresses.
