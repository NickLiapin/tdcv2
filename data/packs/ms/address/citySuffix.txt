description: Trailing qualifier of a Malaysian place name (Johor Bahru, Petaling Jaya, Shah Alam, Subang Jaya). Note BAHRU, not baru — Malaysian toponyms keep the older spelling with the h, which Indonesian dropped, so Johor Bahru and not Johor Baru. The estate-name block at the end — Jaya, Perdana, Utama, Indah, Permai, Impian, Idaman, Bestari, Gemilang — is what Malaysian property developers actually name housing estates, and reading one off an address tells a Malaysian roughly when it was built.
