description: Malay address unit designator. ARAS is the Malaysian word for a floor or storey where Indonesian says lantai, and Tingkat serves as well; BILIK is a room where Indonesian says kamar. Peti Surat is a post office box and BEG BERKUNCI — literally 'locked bag' — is the Malaysian bulk-mail equivalent, addressed exactly like a box and unique to Commonwealth postal systems. Malaysian addresses put the unit number first with 'No.' and write the poskod BEFORE the town.
