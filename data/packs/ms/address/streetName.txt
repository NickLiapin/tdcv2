description: Street name element used across Malaysia. These are the words Malaysian streets are named FROM — flowers, trees, gems, virtues, heroes — not one town's street list, which belongs in a country pack. Note bunga raya, the hibiscus, is the national flower and names a street in nearly every town, and delima, nilam, baiduri and kencana are the classical Malay gem words that Malaysian housing estates run through in blocks. Hang Tuah, Hang Jebat, Hang Kasturi, Hang Lekir and Hang Lekiu are the five warriors of the Melaka sultanate and appear together in many towns. A large share of real Malaysian streets carry no name at all, only a number — Jalan SS 2/24, Jalan USJ 11/3D — and that pattern is a template job, not a list one.
