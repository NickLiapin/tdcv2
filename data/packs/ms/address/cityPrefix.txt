description: Leading element of a Malaysian place name. Several are geography words that became name particles and map onto the peninsula's rivers and coasts: Kuala is a river mouth or confluence, which is what Kuala Lumpur and Kuala Terengganu are named for, Alor is a stream channel (Alor Setar, Alor Gajah), Parit an irrigation ditch (Parit Buntar, Parit Raja), Permatang a low ridge, Bagan a landing place, Batu a milestone from the old British road-marking (Batu Pahat, Batu Caves). Combine with a real toponym; on its own none of these names a real town.
locale: ms
