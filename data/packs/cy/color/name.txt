description: Colour name (Welsh). Welsh does not cut the spectrum where English does, and GLAS is the proof: it covers blue, but it is also the green of living grass (glaswellt, "grass", is literally blue-straw) and the grey of a horse (caseg las). LLWYD runs from grey through brown to the pale of a monk's habit. GWYRDD, the tidy word for green, is a borrowing from Latin viridis and arrived to fill a gap the native system did not have. Two colour adjectives also have separate FEMININE forms, so the same colour has two spellings: gwyn/gwen and melyn/melen — ci gwyn but cath wen.
locale: cy
