description: Fruit (Welsh). LLUS are bilberries — a small dark upland whin-berry picked on the Welsh hills in August, and a different fruit from the imported blueberry, which Welsh has to call llus gleision to tell them apart. CRIAFOL, rowan berries, come from the tree traditionally planted by a farmhouse door.
