description: Weather condition (Welsh). Wales is one of the wettest countries in Europe and the vocabulary shows it: GLAW is rain, GLAW MÂN drizzle, PISTYLLIO the rain coming down like a spout, and the ordinary idiom for a downpour is BWRW HEN WRAGEDD A FFYN — "throwing down old women and sticks". HINDDA is settled fair weather and has no one-word English equivalent.
