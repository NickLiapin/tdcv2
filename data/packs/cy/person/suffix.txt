description: Post-nominal or generational suffix (Welsh). YR IEUAF and YR HYNAF are junior and senior. AS is the abbreviation for Aelod o'r Senedd, a Member of the Welsh Parliament, and also for Aelod Seneddol, a Member of the UK Parliament — the same two letters for two different bodies. The honours themselves (OBE, MBE) are United Kingdom awards and their registers are UK data.
