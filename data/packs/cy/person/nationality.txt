description: Nationality (Welsh). Welsh nationality nouns INFLECT FOR GENDER and the two forms are listed as separate values, masculine then feminine: CYMRO is a Welshman and CYMRAES a Welshwoman, SAIS an Englishman and SAESNES an Englishwoman, GWYDDEL and GWYDDELES, ALBANWR and ALBANES, FFRANCWR and FFRANCES. The feminine is usually -es but the stem often changes with it (Cymro to Cymraes is not a suffix you can bolt on), so the pair cannot be derived from one member — which is exactly why both are in the file. A config drawing a nationality for a female row and getting Cymro has produced something wrong in a way no length or character check will catch.
