description: Language name (Welsh). Note the difference between CYMRAEG and CYMREIG, which English cannot make with one word: Cymraeg is the LANGUAGE (and "Welsh-language" — ysgol Gymraeg is a Welsh-medium school), while Cymreig means Welsh in the general sense (caws Cymreig is Welsh cheese, and it is not made of language). Getting them the wrong way round is the standard learner's mistake. Welsh also has native names for all six Celtic languages — Cymraeg, Gwyddeleg, Gaeleg yr Alban, Manaweg, Llydaweg, Cernyweg — because three of them are its own close relatives: Breton and Cornish are Brythonic like Welsh, and a Welsh speaker can read a fair amount of Breton.
