description: Title placed before a name (Welsh). Y PARCHEDIG, "the Reverend", is disproportionately common in Welsh records because Nonconformist chapels had a minister in every village and the minister was often the most-recorded person in it. BONWR and BONESIG are the native Welsh equivalents of Mr and Mrs, used in formal Welsh-language correspondence where English would use the borrowed forms.
