description: Zodiac sign (Welsh). Welsh translates the signs rather than keeping the Latin, and each carries the definite article as part of the name: Y LLEW is "the lion", Y FORWYN "the virgin" (morwyn soft-mutated after the article because it is feminine), YR AFR "the goat", Y CARIWR DŴR "the water carrier".
