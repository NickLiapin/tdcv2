description: Time of day (Welsh). Welsh counts the parts of the day differently at the edges: BORE BACH is "little morning", the small hours before dawn, and MIN NOS is "the edge of night", the last light. NOS and NOSWAITH are not interchangeable — noswaith is the evening as an occasion (noswaith dda, "good evening"), nos the night itself.
