description: Product adjective (Welsh), in the MASCULINE radical form. Welsh puts the adjective AFTER the noun, which is the opposite of English: "a small chair" is cadair fach, not bach cadair. And because cadair is feminine, bach soft-mutates to fach — so a config that joins a productAdjective to a productNoun gets both the order and the initial letter wrong if it copies English. Some adjectives also have a distinct feminine form (gwyn/gwen, trwm/trom, byr/ber, crwn/cron, melyn/melen) which this list does not carry.
