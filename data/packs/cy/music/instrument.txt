description: Musical instrument (Welsh). Three of these are Welsh instruments. The TELYN, the harp, is the national instrument, and the TELYN DEIRES — the triple harp, with three parallel rows of strings and no pedals — is played nowhere else. The CRWTH is a bowed lyre that died out around 1800 and has been revived. The PIBGORN is a hornpipe with cow-horn bell and reed, also Welsh and also revived.
