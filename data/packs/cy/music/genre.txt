description: Music genre (Welsh). CANU PENILLION, also called cerdd dant, has no equivalent anywhere else: a singer improvises a counter-melody in Welsh over a harpist playing a different tune, the two lines meeting at the end. CYMANFA GANU is congregational hymn-singing as a public event, and PLYGAIN the pre-dawn Christmas carol service sung unaccompanied in parts.
