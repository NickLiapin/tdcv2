description: Evocative noun or name element for a Welsh company name, to be composed with a legalSuffix. Compounds are the Welsh way of forming these, and the second element soft-mutates: Cwm + Gwyrdd gives Cwm Wyrdd. Many are landscape words because Welsh business names lean on landscape the way English ones lean on surnames.
