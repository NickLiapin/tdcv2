description: Property type (Welsh). TYDDYN has no neat English equivalent: it is a smallholding — a house with a few acres, too small to be a farm and too agricultural to be a cottage — and it is an ordinary category on a Welsh estate agent's books. HAFOD and HENDRE appear here too, as the upland and lowland dwellings of the old transhumance year.
