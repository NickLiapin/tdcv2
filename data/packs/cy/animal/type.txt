description: Animal, general (Welsh). Three compounds name an animal by where it lives rather than what it is: DYFRGI the otter is dŵr + ci, "water dog"; MOCHYN DAEAR the badger is "earth pig"; TWRCH DAEAR the mole is "earth boar". LLWYNOG and CADNO are both the fox and both standard — the first is the northern word, the second the southern, and a Welsh dataset that uses only one has quietly picked a region.
