description: Insect (Welsh). BUWCH GOCH GOTA, the ladybird, is a three-word phrase in which both adjectives mutate after the feminine buwch — coch to goch, cwta to gota — so "little red cow" surfaces with two changed initials and no config could rebuild it from the dictionary forms. GWAS Y NEIDR, the dragonfly, is "the adder's servant"; PILI-PALA and GLÖYN BYW are both ordinary words for a butterfly, the first southern and the second northern.
