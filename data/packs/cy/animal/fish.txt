description: Fish (Welsh). Two are found almost nowhere else and have no ordinary English name: the GWYNIAD is a whitefish marooned in Llyn Tegid (Bala) since the ice age and lives in that one lake, and the TORGOCH (arctic charr, "red belly") survives in a handful of deep Snowdonia lakes. SEWIN, the sea trout, is the rare traffic in the other direction — a Welsh word English borrowed rather than translated.
