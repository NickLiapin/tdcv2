description: Bird (Welsh). The BARCUD COCH, the red kite, is the bird Wales is known for: it was down to a handful of pairs in the mid-Wales hills and every British red kite alive descends from that remnant. Several names show mutation inside the compound — TYLLUAN WEN ("barn owl") is gwyn feminised and mutated after the feminine tylluan, GLAS Y DORLAN ("kingfisher") is literally "the blue of the riverbank", CNOCELL Y COED "the knocker of the wood".
locale: cy
