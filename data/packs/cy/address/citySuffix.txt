description: Trailing element of a Welsh place name, shipped in the SOFT-MUTATED form the compound imposes (fawr, fach, wen, ddu, goch, las) rather than the dictionary form (mawr, bach, gwen, du, coch, glas). That is a decision, not an accident: nearly every Welsh place-name head is a feminine noun (pont, tref, llan, craig, ffynnon, melin) and a feminine singular noun soft-mutates the adjective that follows it — Pont + mawr = Pontfawr, Tre + gwyn = Trewyn. After a MASCULINE head (bryn, maes, cwm, llyn, rhos) the radical is required instead: Bryn Mawr, not Bryn Fawr. The list cannot carry both, so it carries the commoner one and this description carries the warning.
locale: cy
