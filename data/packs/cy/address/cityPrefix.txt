description: Leading element of a Welsh place name. This is where initial mutation bites hardest, because the second element of the compound is soft-mutated by the first: Llan + Mair = LlanFAIR, Llan + Dewi = LlanDDEWI, Llan + Padarn = LlanBADARN, Aber + Tawe = AberTAWE. LLAN (an enclosed churchyard) is the single most productive element in Welsh toponymy and is normally followed by a saint. Values here are the RADICAL form of the prefix; a config that concatenates a prefix and a bare second element gets the spelling of the join wrong unless it mutates, and nothing in the two strings tells it to.
locale: cy
