description: The name part of a Welsh street, to be placed AFTER a streetSuffix (Stryd + y Bont = Stryd y Bont). Most entries are genitive phrases with the definite article, and they are deliberately the safest shape to generate: the article y/yr already fixes the mutation inside the phrase (y Bont from pont, y Farchnad from marchnad, yr Orsaf from gorsaf), so the value is stable whichever generic precedes it. The bare adjectives at the end of the list are NOT stable — they are given in the feminine soft-mutated form that Stryd/Heol/Ffordd require (Fawr, Newydd, Hir), and after a masculine generic such as Maes they would need the radical. Personal names do not mutate in this position. Streets belonging to one particular Welsh town, and the English-language names on the same bilingual signs, are country data and live in the united_kingdom pack.
locale: cy
