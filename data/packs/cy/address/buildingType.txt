description: Building type (Welsh). HAFOD and HENDRE are a pair no other European default has: the hafod is the upland summer dwelling a family moved to with the stock in May, the hendre the lowland winter home they came back to — transhumance written into the housing stock, and into hundreds of place names. A BEUDY is a cowshed, an EFAIL a smithy. Note NEUADD BENTREF ("village hall"): neuadd is a feminine noun, so the following pentref soft-mutates to bentref. Values are given in the radical (dictionary) form except where the compound itself fixes the mutation.
