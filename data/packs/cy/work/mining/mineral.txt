description: Mined mineral or commodity (Welsh). The first four are Welsh industrial history rather than a general list. GLO CARREG is anthracite, the hardest and cleanest-burning coal, and the south-west Wales field was the largest anthracite field in the world. LLECHI is slate: the quarries of Gwynedd roofed the nineteenth century and are now a World Heritage Site. COPR made Swansea the world's copper-smelting centre, and AUR from the Dolgellau belt is still mined in small quantities for wedding rings.
