description: Job title (Welsh). Note the pair ATHRO and ATHRAWES: Welsh job titles OFTEN inflect for the sex of the holder — athro is a male teacher, athrawes a female one; cogydd/cogyddes, ysgrifennydd/ysgrifenyddes, rheolwr/rheolwraig, gweithiwr/gweithwraig, actor/actores. The masculine is used generically when the sex is unknown, but a Welsh dataset that pairs a female given name with athro has produced a real error, and no length or character check will find it. A handful of titles here exist because Wales does: CYFIEITHYDD and SWYDDOG Y GYMRAEG are translator and Welsh-language officer, posts that public bodies in Wales are required to fill, and TIWTOR CYMRAEG teaches the language to adults.
