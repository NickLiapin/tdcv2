description: Verb (Welsh), in the VERB-NOUN form, which is the dictionary form. Welsh has no infinitive: the citation form of a verb is a noun, so canu is both "to sing" and "singing", and every progressive is built from it with yn (mae hi'n canu, "she is singing"). Welsh is also a VSO language — the verb comes FIRST, before its subject — so a sentence assembled in subject-verb order is not Welsh word order. Note the pair GWYBOD and ADNABOD: Welsh, like French and Spanish and unlike English, has two verbs for "know" — gwybod for knowing a fact, adnabod for knowing a person or a place.
