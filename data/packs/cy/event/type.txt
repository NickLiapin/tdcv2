description: Event type (Welsh). Four of these are Welsh institutions with no English equivalent, and translating them loses the thing itself. An EISTEDDFOD is a competitive festival of poetry, music and prose that has run in some form since 1176; the National Eisteddfod is conducted entirely in Welsh and moves to a different town each year. A CYMANFA GANU is a mass hymn-singing meeting where the congregation is the choir. A NOSON LAWEN is an evening of songs, recitation and comic turns. A TWMPATH is a folk dance. The GORSEDD is the order of bards that presides over the eisteddfod.
