description: Flower (Welsh). CENNIN PEDR, the daffodil, is literally "Peter's leeks" — which is exactly why Wales has two national emblems that keep being confused: the leek (cenhinen) and the daffodil (cennin Pedr) share a word, and both are worn on Saint David's Day. Several other names are descriptions rather than labels: LLYGAD Y DYDD is "the eye of the day" (daisy), DANT Y LLEW "the lion's tooth" (dandelion), CLYCHAU'R GOG "the cuckoo's bells" (bluebells), BYSEDD Y CŴN "the dogs' fingers" (foxglove), LILI WEN FACH "little white lily" (snowdrop).
