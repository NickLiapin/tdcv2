description: Compass direction (Turkmen). Three of the four are transparent sun words: gundogar is 'the sun rises', gunbatar 'the sun sets', gunorta 'sun-middle', that is midday. North is the odd one — demirgazyk, 'the iron peg', the Turkic name for the Pole Star that the tether of the sky was thought to be driven into.
