Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. Turkmen clinical vocabulary is a deliberate mixture: native names survive for the long-known diseases (suyji keseli 'the sweet disease' for diabetes, demgysma for asthma, incekesel for tuberculosis) while everything named after the nineteenth century arrives through Russian.
