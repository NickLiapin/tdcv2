description: Job title (Turkmen), across sectors. Turkmen forms most occupational names with the suffix -cy / -ci 'the one who does X' — halycy 'carpet weaver' from haly 'carpet', daýhan-adjacent balykcy 'fisherman' from balyk 'fish' — so the trade names are transparent in a way the Russian-borrowed technical titles are not.
