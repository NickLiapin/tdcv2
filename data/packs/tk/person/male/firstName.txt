description: Turkmen male given names. Two things a config cannot infer. FIRST, most are COMPOUNDS built from a small closed set of second elements — -geldi 'came', -myrat 'wish', -guly 'servant of', -berdi 'gave', -muhammet — so Amangeldi, Nurberdi and Orazguly are one name each, not a first name plus something. SECOND, a large share are CALENDRICAL and record when the child was born: Anna- is Friday (the Turkmen weekday name), Juma- is Friday again through Arabic, Oraz- is the Ramadan fast, Gurban- is Eid al-Adha, Bayram- a festival, Sapar- and Rejep- are Islamic months, Yaz- is spring. Annageldi therefore means 'he came on a Friday'. Weights are the project's default decay curve, NOT measured shares: Turkmenistan publishes no name frequency statistics, so no real distribution was available to encode.
locale: tk
