description: Informal Turkmen name. Two devices, both live. FIRST, a compound name is cut back to its first element — Annageldi becomes Anna, Gurbanguly becomes Gurban, Berdimuhammet becomes Berdi — which is why so many short Turkmen names look like the beginnings of longer ones. SECOND, the affectionate particle -jan (from Persian 'soul') is glued on: Amanjan, Aynajan. It is added to a name of either sex and to kin terms, and it is not a diminutive of size.
locale: tk
