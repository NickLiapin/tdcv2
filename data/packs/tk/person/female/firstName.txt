description: Turkmen female given names. The striking pattern is the OGUL- prefix, which literally means 'son': Ogulgerek is 'a son is needed', and Ogulbagt, Ogulnabat, Ogulsoltan and Ogultac are the same wish. They were given to daughters in the hope that a boy would follow, and they are still in use, so a reader must not take them for male names. The other productive elements are -gul 'flower' and Ay- 'moon'. 'Dursun' means 'let her stay' and was given after infant deaths in the family. Weights are the project's default decay curve, NOT measured shares — Turkmenistan publishes no name frequency statistics.
