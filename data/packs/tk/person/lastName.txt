description: Turkmen surnames. Turkmenistan's civil registry inherited the Russian pattern and kept it: a surname is a father's given name plus -ow (after a consonant) or -ýew (after a vowel), which is why every entry here ends that way. They are written with W, NOT V — the Turkmen Latin alphabet has no letter v, so the president's name is Berdimuhamedow at home and 'Berdimuhamedov' only in foreign transliteration. Native surname forms and the patronymic markers ogly / gyzy exist but are not what a passport shows. The list is built from the given names in person/male/firstName.txt by that rule, which is how the registry itself forms them; weights are the project's default decay curve, not measured shares.
locale: tk
