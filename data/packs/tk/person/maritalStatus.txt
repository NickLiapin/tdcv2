description: Marital status (Turkmen). There is NO single word for 'married': a man 'oylenen' (literally 'he has taken a house') and a woman 'durmusa cykan' ('she has gone out into life'), and the same split runs through the unmarried forms. A config that wants a status consistent with a person's sex must choose the matching phrase; the neutral 'nikaly' (in wedlock) is the way round it.
locale: tk
