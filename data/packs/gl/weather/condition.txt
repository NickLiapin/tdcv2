description: Weather condition, in Galician. ORBALLAR - to fall as an extremely fine, near-weightless drizzle that soaks you without seeming to rain - is the single most Galician word on this list and has no one-word English equivalent. Galician distinguishes at least four grades of rain, and using 'chuvia' for all of them loses the distinction a Galician forecast actually makes.
