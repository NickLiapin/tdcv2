description: Weather phenomenon, in Galician. GALERNA is the sudden violent north-wester that can arrive on a calm summer afternoon and has drowned fishing crews within living memory; MARUSIA is the heavy swell that runs before a storm; RESACA the dangerous backwash on the beaches of the Costa da Morte. These are hazard words in Galicia, not poetry.
