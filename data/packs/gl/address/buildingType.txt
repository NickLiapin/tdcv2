description: Building type in Galician. Several have no equivalent outside Galicia and the north of Portugal. A PAZO is the granite manor house of the Galician gentry, with its own chapel and dovecote. An HORREO is the raised stone granary standing on staddle-stones that keeps maize dry and out of reach of rats - there are tens of thousands still standing and it is the single most recognisable Galician structure. A CETAREA is a seawater tank cut into the rock to hold live shellfish, and a CONSERVEIRA the cannery that made the rias industrial. A LONXA is the auction hall where the catch is sold the hour it lands. Reitoral is the parish priest's house, now very often a rural hotel, and casa de aldea a village house let to visitors - both are ordinary property types in Galician listings.
