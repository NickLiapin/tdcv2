description: Leading element of a Galician place name (Vilanova, Castrelo, Pontenova, San Cibrao, O Grove, A Pobra). THE ARTICLE IS PART OF THE NAME and Galician capitalises it: A Coruna, O Carballino, As Pontes, Os Blancos. That matters far more than it looks, because the article then CONTRACTS with any preposition put in front of it - de + O Grove = do Grove, a + A Coruna = a Coruna with an accent, en + As Pontes = nas Pontes. So a template that writes 'de' plus a place name drawn from this list produces 'de O Grove', which no Galician text contains. San and Santa are far and away the commonest first element, because the parroquia system named settlements after their church.
locale: gl
