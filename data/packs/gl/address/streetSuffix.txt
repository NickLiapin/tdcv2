description: Galician thoroughfare type. It goes BEFORE the name (rua Nova, avenida de Fisterra, praza do Obradoiro), the opposite of English, and it stays LOWER CASE inside running text. Rua is the ordinary Galician word for a street; Galician does not use calle. Corredoira and congostra are sunken lanes between field walls and are genuine address types in rural Galicia, not metaphors. LUGAR is the one that surprises people: Galicia's settlement is dispersed across roughly 30000 named places inside about 3700 parroquias, and a very large share of rural addresses have no street at all - they read 'Lugar de Vilar, 12' or give the parroquia instead. Aldea and parroquia are therefore address units here, not descriptions.
