description: The NAME half of a Galician street, composed AFTER a streetSuffix: rua Nova, praza do Obradoiro, avenida de Rosalia de Castro. THIS FILE IS WHERE THE ARTICLE CONTRACTION BITES. Most entries begin with an already-contracted preposition plus article - do, da, dos, das - because de + o = do and de + a = da obligatorily in Galician. The contraction is baked into the value on purpose: a config that stored 'Horreo' and joined 'rua' + 'de' + 'Horreo' would emit 'rua de o Horreo', which is not Galician. Three stocks are mixed, as the real street map mixes them: common nouns with the article (do Horreo, da Ponte, do Muino), saints and Marian titles, and pan-Galician cultural figures who have a street in essentially every Galician town - Rosalia de Castro, Curros Enriquez, Castelao, Valle-Inclan, Otero Pedrayo, Cunqueiro, Manuel Murguia, Eduardo Pondal, Concepcion Arenal. Those names are LANGUAGE-level rather than country-level: they are the writers of the Rexurdimento and of the Galician twentieth century, and they are why two Galician towns' street maps rhyme. No real municipality's actual street list is here; that belongs to the Spain country pack.
