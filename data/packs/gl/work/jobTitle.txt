description: Job title in Galician. Many are listed in BOTH grammatical genders (cocineiro / cocineira, mestre / mestra, avogado / avogada) because Galician marks the gender of a job title and a record whose gender column says Muller but whose job says Cocineiro is ungrammatical, not merely unusual. A number of these trades exist nowhere else and are not translations: a PERCEBEIRO gathers goose barnacles off wave-hit rock, a MARISCADORA works the intertidal flats under licence, a REDEIRA mends nets on the quay, a BATEEIRO farms mussels on a raft, a PULPEIRA cooks and serves octopus at fairs, a PALILLEIRA makes bobbin lace at Camarinas, an AZABACHEIRO carves jet for Santiago pilgrims, a ZOQUEIRO makes wooden clogs, and the AFIADOR - the itinerant knife grinder of Ourense - is proverbial enough that Galicians abroad are still called by the name.
