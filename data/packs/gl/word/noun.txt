description: Galician noun. Note what the definite article does to anything joined in front of it, because it affects every template that composes a phrase: o, a, os, as MERGE with the preceding preposition and the merge is obligatory in writing, not optional. de + o = do, de + a = da, en + o = no, en + a = na, a + o = ao (also written o), a + a = a with a grave-less accent, con + o = co, con + a = coa, por + o = polo, por + a = pola, and the indefinites do it too: de + un = dun, en + unha = nunha. So a config that emits 'de' and 'o hórreo' separately writes 'de o horreo', which is wrong in Galician the way 'of the the' is wrong in English. Galician also keeps the article in front of possessives (o meu can, 'the my dog') and in front of many place names, so the merge fires there too.
locale: gl
