description: Galician adjective, MASCULINE singular. Galician inflects adjectives for gender and number - bo / boa / bos / boas, pequeno / pequena - so joining one of these to a feminine noun without changing the ending is ungrammatical, and this list carries only one of the four forms. Adjectives ending in -e, -l, -z and -ar (grande, azul, feliz, familiar) are invariable in gender and are safe to join either way.
