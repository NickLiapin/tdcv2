description: Galician adverb. The productive suffix is -mente added to the FEMININE form of the adjective (rapida > rapidamente), and when two -mente adverbs are coordinated Galician drops the suffix from all but the last: 'clara e brevemente'. Aqui / ai / ali is a THREE-way distance system - here by me, there by you, over there by neither - matching este / ese / aquel, where English has only here and there.
locale: gl
