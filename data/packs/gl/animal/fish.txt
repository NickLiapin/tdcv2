description: Fish, in Galician. This list is longer and more specific than most locales' because fish is the centre of Galician life: XOUBA is a small sardine and a different item on a menu from SARDINA, RODABALLO is turbot and is farmed here on an industrial scale, and the LAMPREA - a jawless parasitic fish caught in the Mino with medieval stone-weir traps - is a delicacy with its own season and festival.
