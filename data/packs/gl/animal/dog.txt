description: Dog breed. Breed names are international, but the first is a GALICIAN breed found essentially nowhere else: the CAN DE PALLEIRO, the yard-and-herding dog of the Galician smallholding, on the Xunta's register of native breeds and pulled back from near-extinction in the 1990s. PODENGO, GALGO ESPANOL and MASTIN are Iberian rather than specifically Galician.
