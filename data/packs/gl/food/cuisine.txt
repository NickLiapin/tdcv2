description: Cuisine, named in Galician. GALEGA heads the list because it is the home cuisine and because it is genuinely distinct from Spanish cooking rather than a region of it: it is Atlantic rather than Mediterranean, built on shellfish, octopus, pork, greens and maize bread, and it uses almost no olive oil, no saffron and no rice as a staple. Portuguesa and Espanola are both here as separate cuisines, which is the honest arrangement for a kitchen that sits between the two.
