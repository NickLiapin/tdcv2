description: Cooking ingredient, named in Galician. UNTO is the one a translator gets wrong: it is cured pork fat, kept hung and rancid on purpose, and a spoonful of it is what makes caldo galego taste of Galicia rather than of vegetable soup. Pemento doce and pemento picante are the two paprikas that dress polbo a feira. Fariña de millo is maize flour, the basis of broa, and is not wheat flour.
