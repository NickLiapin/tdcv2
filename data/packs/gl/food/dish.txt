description: Prepared dish as a Galician menu names it. The list is Galician-weighted rather than international: polbo a feira is octopus boiled in a copper pot, cut with scissors and dressed with paprika and oil, and it is served by a pulpeira at every fair; empanada is a flat two-crust pie, NOT the Argentine turnover of the same name; caldo galego is the everyday soup of greens, potato and white beans with unto; grelos are turnip tops and have no English name; a filloa is a crepe traditionally made with the blood of the winter pig; tarta de Santiago carries the cross of the Order of Santiago in icing sugar; and queimada is spirit burned with sugar and coffee beans while a conxuro is recited over it.
locale: gl
