description: Vegetable, named in Galician. GRELOS lead because they define the Galician kitchen and have no English name at all - they are the flowering tops of the turnip, eaten as a green, and they carry a protected designation of origin. Nabiza is the same plant at an earlier stage and is a different vegetable to a Galician cook. Repolo, berza and couve are three distinct cabbages here, not synonyms. Faba de Lourenza and pemento de Padron, Herbon, Arnoia, Couto and Mougan are Galician protected varieties.
