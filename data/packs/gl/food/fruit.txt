description: Fruit, named in Galician. The head of the list is what actually grows in Galicia: the chestnut was the staple carbohydrate before the potato arrived and the souto that produced it is still a landscape unit and a surname; mazá, pera and cireixa come from the orchards of the interior valleys; and uva is here because Galicia makes albarino, godello, mencia and ribeiro. Amora, arando, sanguino and abelan are the hedgerow gathering. Tropical fruit is at the end and is imported.
