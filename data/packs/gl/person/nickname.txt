description: Galician hypocoristics - the everyday short form of a given name. Galician forms these differently from Castilian and the suffixes are the tell: -iño/-iña (Manuelino, Carmiña), -uxa/-uxo (Maruxa from Maria, Catuxa from Catarina), -eco, and truncation from the FRONT rather than the back (Sabela from Isabel, Tareixa from Teresa, Lucho from Luis). Pepe, Chelo and Lola are shared with Castilian; Moncho, Suso, Xurxo, Fuco, Farruco, Sito, Xela, Lelo and Xoco are not. Farruco and Fuco are both from Francisco, by two different routes.
