description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Galician. Amounts are in EUR, Spain's currency and therefore Galicia's. Galician writes a decimal COMMA and groups thousands with a full stop, so 30.000 EUR here is thirty thousand, not thirty. The bands are set low relative to the en pack on purpose: Galician household income sits below the Spanish average, and a pack that copied US brackets would describe a different country.
locale: gl
