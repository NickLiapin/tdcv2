description: Nationality adjective, in Galician, in the MASCULINE singular. Galician inflects these for gender and number - un home espanol, unha muller espanola, uns homes espanois - so a config joining a nationality to a feminine noun needs the feminine form, which this list does not carry. Galego is included as a nationality label because Galicia is a nacionalidade historica under the Spanish constitution and people do give it in that slot; it is not a state.
