description: Galician male given names with REAL MEASURED bearer counts - the number of living men in Galicia carrying the name, Instituto Galego de Estatistica, January 2023. These are not the pack's default rank-decay curve: Manuel really does have 65083 bearers against Antonio's 29867, and the distribution really is that top-heavy, so a dataset built on this file inherits Galicia's actual name concentration. The list is NOT the Castilian one respelled. Xoan is not Juan respelled, Xurxo is not Jorge, Xosé is not Jose, and Brais, Iago, Anxo, Roi, Breogan, Xian, Uxio, Cibran, Aldan, Fiz, Xacobe and Martiño have no Castilian equivalent at all. The letter X does the work Castilian gives to J and soft G and Portuguese gives to J: Xurxo, Xoan, Xabier, Xacobe. Breogan and Brais are the Celtic-revival layer that Galician nationalism put back into use in the twentieth century. Manuel at the head, and its average bearer age of 62, is itself a demographic fact: the head of this list is an old population and the Galician-form names near the middle (Brais, Iago, Xian) are young ones.
