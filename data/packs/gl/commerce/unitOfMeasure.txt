description: Unit of measure in Galician. METRIC, because Spain is metric: quilogramo, litro, metro. No pounds, ounces or gallons. FERRADO is included and is genuinely used in Galicia for land: it is the traditional area unit, still quoted in rural property sales, and it is NOT a fixed size - it runs from roughly 400 to 640 square metres depending on the parish, which is exactly the sort of thing a data pack should say out loud rather than silently round.
