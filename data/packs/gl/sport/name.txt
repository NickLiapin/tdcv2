description: Sport, in Galician. The tail of this list is Galicia's own: TRAINEIRAS are the thirteen-oar sea rowing boats raced between coastal towns; the XOGO DOS BIRLOS is the Galician nine-pin bowling game; CHAVE is a throwing game at an iron key on a post; LOITA GALEGA is the traditional wrestling, revived from the medieval texts; and the RAPA DAS BESTAS, in which unbroken wild horses are wrestled and shorn bare-handed in a stone curro, is a contest rather than a festival and has no equivalent anywhere.
