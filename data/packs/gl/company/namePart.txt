description: Element a Galician company name is built from. Two stocks are mixed, as the real register mixes them: international abstract nouns (Nexo, Vector, Horizonte) and GALICIAN LANDSCAPE words, which is what a genuinely local firm is usually named after - a ria, a cape, a river, a tree, a stone. Fisterra, Ortegal, Ons, Sálvora, Cíes, Sil, Miño, Ulla, Tambre, Lérez and Eume are Galician places and waters; carballo, castiñeiro, toxo, xesta and érbedo are its plants; and Breogán, Nabia, Lug and Ártabro come from the Celtic and pre-Roman layer that Galician branding leans on heavily.
