description: Compass direction in Galician, the sixteen points. Galician says Leste and Oeste for east and west, and forms the compounds without a hyphen (Nordeste, Sursueste). A second and older set is in daily use beside these and is listed in address/direction: the wind rose of the Galician coast, which names each bearing after the wind that blows from it - nordes, vendaval, travesia, terral, galerna.
