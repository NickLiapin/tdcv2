description: Tree, in Galician. CARBALLO (the pedunculate oak) is the national tree, gives its name to towns, surnames and the carballeira where a romaria is held, and is the single most loaded word on this list. EUCALIPTO is here because it has to be: Galicia has the largest eucalyptus plantation area in Europe, it is the backbone of the pulp industry and simultaneously the country's bitterest environmental argument, and a Galician tree list that omitted it would be describing the landscape people wish for rather than the one that exists.
