description: Musical instrument, in Galician. The head is the Galician traditional set: the GAITA GALEGA is the mouth-blown bagpipe with a conical chanter and one to three drones, the PANDEIREITA the square-frame tambourine played by women in threes, the ZANFONA the hurdy-gurdy, the REQUINTA a small transverse flute, and the CUNCHAS scallop shells scraped as a rhythm instrument - which is exactly what it sounds like.
