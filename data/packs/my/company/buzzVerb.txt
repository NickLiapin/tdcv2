description: Corporate buzz verb, in the plain Burmese dictionary form. Burmese verbs take no person or tense ending, so these are complete words as they stand: တိုးတက်စေ is "to cause to progress" and needs nothing added. The နိုင် and စေ suffixes visible here are the causative and potential markers and are part of the verb, not conjugation. Note that Burmese is verb-final, so a slogan built from these puts the verb at the END of the phrase, never in the middle as English does.
locale: my
