description: Corporate buzz adjective, the kind that fills a Myanmar company profile or a government project name. ရွှေ (golden), မြ (emerald) and ရတနာ (jewel) are the three most overused openers in Myanmar business naming and are adjectives here for that reason. ခေတ်မီ ("modern"), ဖွံ့ဖြိုးတိုးတက်သော ("developed") and ရေရှည်တည်တံ့သော ("sustainable") are the vocabulary of five-decades of development planning and read to a Burmese ear the way "synergistic" reads in English.
locale: my
