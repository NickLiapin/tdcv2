description: Legal form appended to a Myanmar company name. THIS LIST IS DELIBERATELY SHORT — Myanmar's Companies Law 2017 recognises only a handful of forms and inventing forty would have produced strings that no registration document contains. The workhorse is ကုမ္ပဏီလီမိတက်, which is what almost every private company is; a public company adds အများနှင့်သက်ဆိုင်သော in front of it. Note that a Myanmar company name is routinely written in BOTH scripts and that the English "Co., Ltd." is at least as common on a signboard as the Burmese, so a config generating Myanmar company names should expect a mixed-script string rather than a purely Burmese one. သမဝါယမအသင်း (cooperative society) is a distinct legal person under a separate law and is not a company at all.
