description: The name element itself, before the legal form. Myanmar company naming draws on a small and very recognisable set of ideas. GOLD AND JEWELS DOMINATE: ရွှေ, မြ, စိန်, ပတ္တမြား, နီလာ, ရတနာ and their compounds open an enormous share of Myanmar company names, because gold is both the national savings instrument and the national metaphor for quality. Next come the auspicious Pali abstractions — မင်္ဂလာ, သီရိ, ဇေယျာ, သီဟ, အောင် — the same words that name streets and children. Then the country's own landmarks and creatures: ဧရာဝတီ and သံလွင် (the two great rivers), ဟင်္သာ and ဒေါင်း and နဂါး and ကျား, ပုဂံ and ရွှေတိဂုံ. Two or three of these are simply concatenated to make a name: ရွှေမင်္ဂလာ, မြရတနာ, ဧရာအောင်.
