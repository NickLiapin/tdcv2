description: Weather condition. Myanmar's year has THREE seasons, not four (see date/season.txt), and the words here follow the monsoon: မိုးအုံ့, မိုးဖွဲ, မိုးသည်း and မိုးသည်းထန် are four distinct grades of rain because the rainy season needs them. နွေပူပြင်း is the hot-season condition, when the dry zone regularly exceeds 40 °C. နှင်းကျ (frost) happens only in the Shan and Chin hills.
locale: my
