description: Hotel room type. The English category words are used in Myanmar hotels alongside the Burmese ones; both are here because both appear on the same tariff card. ရိုးရာစတိုင်အခန်း (traditional-style room) is a real product in Bagan and Inle, meaning teak, lacquer and a low bed rather than a themed one.
locale: my
