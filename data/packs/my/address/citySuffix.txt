description: The last element of a Myanmar town name, to be joined to address/cityPrefix WITH NO SPACE AND NO HYPHEN — Burmese writes compounds solid, so ရွှေ + ဘို is ရွှေဘို, never "ရွှေ ဘို". Most of these are landscape words, which is what Burmese place names mostly are: ကုန်း a mound, ချောင်း a creek, ဆည် a weir, ကျွန်း an island, အင်း a lake, တောင်း a basin, ဆုံ a confluence. ကြီး ("great") and လေး ("small") are the ordinary way a pair of neighbouring places is distinguished. မြို့ is the generic word for "town" and is appended to almost any place name in running text — ရန်ကုန်မြို့ means "the town of Yangon" — so a config that appends it to a name that already ends in မြို့ will produce a doubled word.
