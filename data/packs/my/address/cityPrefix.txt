description: The first element of a Myanmar town name. These are real morphemes that open real towns — ရွှေ opens ရွှေဘို and ရွှေတောင်, ကျောက် opens ကျောက်ဆည် and ကျောက်ဖြူ, မင်း opens မင်းဘူး and မင်းလှ — and they combine with address/citySuffix to build a plausible Burmese place name. They are NOT the towns themselves: the real Myanmar gazetteer is country data and belongs in the Myanmar country pack. Two of these carry meanings a translator will trip on. တောင် is both "mountain" and "south", so တောင်ကြီး is readable as either "great mountain" or "great south" and only local knowledge settles it. အထက် and အောက် mean "upper" and "lower" in the sense of UPRIVER and DOWNRIVER on the Ayeyarwady, not north and south on a compass. Joined to a suffix these run together with no space and no hyphen.
locale: my
