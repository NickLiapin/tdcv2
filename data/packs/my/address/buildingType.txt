description: Building type in Burmese. The list is ordered by what Myanmar actually has rather than by the reference pack: ပျဉ်ထောင်အိမ် (plank house on posts), ဝါးအိမ် (bamboo house) and ဓနိမိုးအိမ် (nipa-thatch house) are ordinary dwellings for most of the country, while တိုက်ခန်း — a walk-up flat in a brick block — is the Yangon norm. လက်ဖက်ရည်ဆိုင်, the tea shop, is a building type in Myanmar in the way a pub is in Britain: it is where business, news and politics happen, and a dataset of Myanmar premises that has no tea shop in it is wrong. ဘုန်းကြီးကျောင်း (monastery) is listed separately from ဘုရား (pagoda) because they are different institutions — the pagoda is a solid stupa nobody enters and the monastery is where monks live and where village schooling has traditionally happened. NOTE THAT BURMESE HAS NO SPACES BETWEEN WORDS, so every one of these is a single unbroken run of characters however many words it translates to.
locale: my
