description: Street-type word, appended AFTER the name and joined to it with no space: အနော်ရထာ + လမ်း is အနော်ရထာလမ်း. THIS LIST IS DELIBERATELY SHORT AND WAS NOT PADDED TO THE REFERENCE LENGTH. English has around forty street types (Street, Avenue, Boulevard, Drive, Court, Terrace, Place, Way…) and Burmese has about a dozen: လမ်း covers street, road, avenue and drive all at once, and the distinctions English draws are simply not drawn. Inventing thirty more would have produced words no Burmese address contains. Note also that a very large share of Myanmar streets have NO name at all — Mandalay and downtown Yangon are numbered grids, so the address reads ၃၅လမ်း or ၄၆လမ်း in Burmese digits, with the number where a name would be.
