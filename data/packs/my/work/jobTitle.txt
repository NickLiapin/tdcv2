description: Job title. THE SHAPE OF THIS LIST IS THE SHAPE OF MYANMAR EMPLOYMENT, not of a European one. Roughly half the workforce is in agriculture, so လယ်သမား and တောင်သူ are not folkloric entries. The largest formal-sector employer is garment manufacture, so အထည်ချုပ်လုပ်သား and အပ်ချုပ်သမား carry real weight. AND MOST WORK IN MYANMAR IS INFORMAL — an estimated four in five jobs — so ဈေးသည် (market seller), လက်ဖက်ရည်ဆိုင်စားပွဲထိုး (tea-shop waiter), ဆိုင်ကယ်ကယ်ရီ (motorbike taxi), ဆိုက်ကားနင်းသူ (trishaw rider) and နေ့စားလုပ်သား (day labourer) describe how a great many people actually earn. ရဟန်း and သီလရှင် are listed because ordination is a life state a large share of the population passes through, though it is a vocation rather than employment. A Myanmar occupation dataset built from office job titles has described the small formal minority and called it the country.
