description: Kind of official document. THE FIRST ENTRY IS THE ONE MYANMAR LIFE TURNS ON: နိုင်ငံသားစိစစ်ရေးကတ်ပြား, the NRC card, which is the identity document, and whose colour encodes citizenship class — pink for full citizens, blue and green for associate and naturalised — so possession alone does not settle status. အိမ်ထောင်စုစာရင်း, the household list, records who lives at an address and is required for school enrolment, a SIM card and a hospital admission; a household that has not updated it cannot do those things. ထောက်ခံစာ, the recommendation letter from a ward or village administrator, is required to obtain almost anything else on this list and has no European counterpart. THE ACTUAL NUMBER FORMATS OF THESE DOCUMENTS ARE NOT IN THIS PACK — they belong to the Myanmar country pack, because a document number is a country fact and not a language one.
