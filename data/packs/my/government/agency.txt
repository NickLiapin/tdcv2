THE LOWEST THREE ARE THE ONES ORDINARY LIFE RUNS THROUGH: ရပ်ကွက်အုပ်ချုပ်ရေးမှူးရုံး (ward administrator's office) and ကျေးရွာအုပ်ချုပ်ရေးမှူးရုံး (village tract office) issue the recommendation letters without which nothing else can be done, and မြို့နယ်အထွေထွေအုပ်ချုပ်ရေးဦးစီးဌာန (township General Administration Department) is the spine of the Myanmar state. Myanmar's ministries are named descriptively and are reorganised often, so these are the standing functions rather than a snapshot of one government's cabinet — which is a country-pack question, not a language one.
