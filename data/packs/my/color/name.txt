description: Colour. Burmese builds a bare colour word with the prefix အ- (အနီ red, အပြာ blue, အဝါ yellow, အစိမ်း green) and a derived shade with the suffix -ရောင်, literally "light" or "hue", appended to whatever the colour is being compared to. That is why most of this list is a noun plus ရောင်: ခရမ်းရောင် is "brinjal-hue", လိမ္မော်ရောင် is "orange-fruit-hue", ကြက်သွေးရောင် is "chicken-blood-hue". Two entries name colours by objects that only Myanmar sees daily and they are the reason this list is not a translation of the English one: သနပ်ခါးရောင် is the pale creamy yellow of thanaka, the ground bark paste worn on the face by most women and many children in the country, and သင်္ကန်းရောင် is the dark ochre-maroon of a monk's robe, a colour so specific that no other word will do. ရင့် ("deep") and နု ("tender") are the general modifiers for dark and light and can be appended to almost any of these.
locale: my
