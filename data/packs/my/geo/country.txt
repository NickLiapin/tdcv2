description: Country, weighted by population in millions, AS BURMESE NAMES IT. Every name is taken verbatim from CLDR (locale my, ICU 76.1) rather than transliterated by hand, so this pack and the Unicode Consortium agree character for character. Burmese has three layers of country name and all three are visible here. THE OLDEST ARE NATIVE WORDS WITH NO PHONETIC RELATION TO THE ENGLISH AT ALL: တရုတ် is China, ပြင်သစ် is France, ကုလား-derived အိန္ဒိယ is India, and ဂျာမနီ aside, a config that tries to romanise တရုတ် back will get "Tayoke", not "China". The second layer is Pali and Sanskrit, reaching Burma with Buddhism: သီရိလင်္ကာ, ကမ္ဘောဒီးယား. The third and largest is English respelled to Burmese sound: ဘရာဇီး, ပါကစ္စတန်, ဩစတြေးလျ. မြန်မာ is Myanmar's own name for itself and is not a loan of anything. These strings are the KEYS of capitalByCountry, currencyByCountry and currencyCodeByCountry and must match those filenames character for character.
locale: my
weighted: true
