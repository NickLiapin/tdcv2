description: Academic award. Myanmar's system is British in shape and the abbreviations are written in Latin letters on the certificate itself, which is why they appear that way in person/suffix.txt. တက္ကသိုလ်ဝင်တန်း (matriculation) is the single most consequential exam in Myanmar life and its result decides which faculty a student may enter, so it is an award in practice. ဓမ္မာစရိယ is the senior Pali scriptural degree of the monastic system, an award of real standing that has no place in a Western degree ladder.
locale: my
