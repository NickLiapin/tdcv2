---
description: Adverb. Burmese adverbs mostly PRECEDE the verb they modify, which is itself at the end of the clause, so the order is subject, object, adverb, verb. Several of these are reduplications of an adjective (ဖြည်းဖြည်း from ဖြည်း "slow", ကောင်းကောင်း from ကောင်း "good"), which is the ordinary Burmese way of turning a stative verb into a manner adverb. အရမ်း and အလွန် both mean "very" but sit at different registers: အလွန် is written and formal, အရမ်း is spoken.
