description: Adjective. IN BURMESE AN ADJECTIVE IS A VERB. ကောင်း is both "good" and "to be good" and behaves grammatically like any other verb: it can end a sentence on its own (ကောင်းတယ် = "it is good") and it needs the particle သော or တဲ့ to modify a noun (ကောင်းသောလူ = "a good person"). There is no separate class of adjectives at all, and a config that places one of these bare in front of a noun the English way produces something ungrammatical. ရင့် ("deep") and နု ("tender") are the general intensity modifiers, used for colours as well as flavours.
