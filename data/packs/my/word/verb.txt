description: Common verb, in the plain dictionary form. Burmese verbs are INVARIANT: no person, no number, no tense ending. Time is shown by a sentence-final particle (တယ် for realis, မယ် for irrealis) or by an adverb, so စား is "eat", "eats", "ate" and "will eat" alike. Burmese is VERB-FINAL, so in any real sentence the verb comes last, after both subject and object. Adjectives are also verbs — see word/adjective.txt — so ကောင်း belongs to the same class as စား.
locale: my
