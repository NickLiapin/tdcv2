description: Common noun. Burmese nouns take NO ARTICLE, NO PLURAL ENDING AND NO CASE: အိမ် is "house", "a house", "the house" and "houses" alike, and plurality is marked only when it matters, with the separate particle တွေ or များ. Counting requires a CLASSIFIER between the number and the noun — လူ ၃ ယောက် ("person 3 human-classifier"), စာအုပ် ၂ အုပ် ("book 2 volume-classifier") — and the classifier depends on the shape and kind of the thing, so a config that joins a number to a noun without one has produced ungrammatical Burmese.
locale: my
