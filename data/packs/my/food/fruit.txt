description: Fruit. The tropical fruit of Myanmar comes first and much of it has no English name at all: ဇီးဖြူသီး, မာလကာသီး, သစ်တော်သီး, ဒူးရင်းသီး (durian), မင်္ဂုတ်သီး (mangosteen), ကျွဲကောသီး (custard apple). သီး is the classifier for fruit and is part of the word — မန်ကျည်း is the tamarind TREE and မန်ကျည်းသီး the tamarind fruit, and dropping the classifier changes what you have said. သရက်သီး, the mango, has hundreds of named Myanmar varieties and စိန်တလုံး is the famous one.
