description: Cooking ingredient. ဆန် (uncooked rice) and ထမင်း (cooked rice) are different words and both are here, because in Burmese "to eat" is ထမင်းစား, literally "to eat rice" — a meal without rice is not a meal. ပဲမျိုးစုံ covers the pulses Myanmar grows and exports in enormous quantity. မြေပဲဆီ (groundnut oil) and နှမ်းဆီ (sesame oil) are the two traditional cooking fats; palm oil has displaced them by volume but not in the words. ကွမ်းရွက်, ဆန်မှုန့် and မုန့်ဖတ် are the bases of the noodle and snack kitchen.
locale: my
