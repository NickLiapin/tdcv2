description: Vegetable. ကြက်သွန်နီ and ကြက်သွန်ဖြူ (shallot and garlic) head the list because a Myanmar curry starts with both crushed in oil, every time. ဒန့်သလွန်ရွက် (moringa leaf), ကန်စွန်းရွက် (water spinach), ရုံးပတီသီး (okra) and ဗူးသီး (bottle gourd) are the daily vegetables; ရွက် means "leaf" and သီး "fruit", and both are part of the noun. မြေပဲ (groundnut) is here as well as in food/ingredient because Myanmar grows and presses it for the cooking oil that defines the cuisine.
locale: my
