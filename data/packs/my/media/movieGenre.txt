description: Film genre. ဇာတ်ကား is the Burmese word for a feature film and these are the categories a Myanmar video shop or streaming menu uses. ဟာသ has an outsized share of Myanmar cinema. ရိုးရာဇာတ်ပွဲ is the all-night open-air theatre performance — song, dance, comedy and myth in one — which is a genre of its own and predates film.
