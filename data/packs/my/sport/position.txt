description: Playing position, general. Burmese names positions by where they stand — နောက်ခံလူ is "the person behind", ရှေ့တန်းလူ "the person in the front line" — so many of these are the same word used across sports, and the English loanwords (ပွိုင့်ဂတ်, လီဘီရို, ဆက်တာ) are used where Burmese has no term. ဒိုင်လူကြီး (referee) and နည်းပြ (coach) are not playing positions but appear on a Myanmar team sheet where an English one would separate them.
locale: my
