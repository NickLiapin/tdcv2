description: Sport. မြန်မာ့လက်ဝှေ့ (lethwei) is the one no other country has: bare-knuckle boxing in which headbutts are legal and a knocked-out fighter may be revived and continue, fought at pagoda festivals for centuries. ခြင်းလုံး is the national game and is cooperative rather than competitive — see sport/sportCoherent.txt. ဘောလုံး is by far the most watched sport, mostly English Premier League on a tea-shop television. Note that Myanmar's national teams, clubs and stadiums are country data and are not in this pack.
