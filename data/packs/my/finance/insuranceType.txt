MYANMAR IS ONE OF THE LEAST INSURED COUNTRIES ON EARTH — penetration is well under one per cent of GDP — and until 2013 the state insurer was the only one permitted to sell anything at all, so several of these products are barely a decade old in the private market. ကြိုတင်စုဆောင်း-style life products and third-party motor cover are what actually sells. သီးနှံအာမခံ (crop insurance) exists as a pilot rather than as a product a farmer can walk in and buy. In practice a Myanmar household's insurance is gold, land and relatives.
