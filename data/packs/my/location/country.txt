description: Country name in Burmese — 195 sovereign states, TAKEN VERBATIM FROM CLDR (locale my, ICU 76.1) rather than transliterated by hand. Three things a config must not do with this list. Do not romanise these back: တရုတ် (China), ပြင်သစ် (France), အိန္ဒိယ (India), ဂရိ (Greece) and ဘုရင့်နိုင်ငံ-style names are Burmese words rather than respellings, and a phonetic reverse-transliteration will produce nonsense. Do not assume one word per country: several CLDR names contain an ASCII SPACE (အမေရိကန် ပြည်ထောင်စု, တောင် ဆူဒန်) even though Burmese does not otherwise separate words with spaces, so a value here may be two space-separated runs. And do not sort them with a byte comparison — Burmese collation is not codepoint order, and this list is sorted with the Unicode collator for my. Dependencies, overseas territories and disbanded states are deliberately excluded; only current sovereign states are here, which is why the count is short of the reference pack's 233.
