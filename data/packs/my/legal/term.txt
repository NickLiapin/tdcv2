description: Legal term. Burmese legal vocabulary is a translation layer over English-Indian statute, so most of these are calques: တရားလို is "the one who wants justice" (plaintiff) and တရားခံ "the one who bears it" (defendant). ဓလေ့ထုံးတမ်း is customary law, which in Myanmar is applied by religion rather than nationally — see legal/caseType.txt. စာချုပ်စာတမ်း covers both a contract and the stamped deed that proves it, and in Myanmar practice the stamped, registered document is the thing that matters: an unstamped agreement is very hard to enforce.
locale: my
