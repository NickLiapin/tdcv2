description: Stage of a hiring process. ထောက်ခံစာတောင်းခံခြင်း (asking for a recommendation letter) is a real stage in Myanmar hiring and often means a letter from a ward administrator rather than a former employer — see government/documentType.txt. Personal introduction still carries enormous weight, so a great many Myanmar hires never pass through a formal stage at all.
