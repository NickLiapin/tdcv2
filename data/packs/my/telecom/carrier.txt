description: Mobile operator and telecom service word. THE MYANMAR MOBILE MARKET IS UNUSUALLY YOUNG: until 2013 a SIM card cost the equivalent of thousands of dollars and almost nobody had a phone; licences were opened, prices collapsed to a couple of dollars, and penetration went from a few per cent to near-universal in about five years. The country therefore skipped fixed-line telephony almost entirely and went straight to mobile data. The named operators are the real ones; the words after them are the service categories a Myanmar top-up shop sells.
