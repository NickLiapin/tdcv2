description: Cat breed, as named in Burmese. ဗမာကြောင် — the Burmese cat — is a genuine internationally recognised breed whose founding female, Wong Mau, was brought out of Burma in 1930, and Myanmar also has the ဗမာဟိမဝန္တာကြောင် (Birman, the "sacred cat of Burma"). Every other name here is a foreign breed transliterated, because Myanmar has no cat-breeding tradition and the ordinary Myanmar cat is unpedigreed: အိမ်ကြောင် (house cat) and လမ်းကြောင် (street cat) are the two categories most Burmese would actually use.
