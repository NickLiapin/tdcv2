description: Bird. ဒေါင်း, the green peafowl, is the national bird and the emblem of Burmese nationalism — the fighting peacock is on the flag of the independence movement and on the one-kyat note of the colonial period — so it is not merely one bird among many here. ဟင်္သာ (hamsa, a mythical golden goose) and ကိန္နရီ (kinnari, a half-bird half-woman of Buddhist cosmology) are birds a Burmese speaker names without hesitation but no ornithologist will find; they are in the list because they are in the language, on temple walls and in personal names. ဂဠုန် is the garuda, the Sunday birth-sign.
