description: Shipping status. ဂိတ်တွင်ရောက်ရှိ ("arrived at the bus terminal") is a delivery state, not a transit one: for a great deal of Myanmar freight the journey ends at a bus-station counter and the recipient goes to collect it. ရေလမ်းခရီးတွင် (in transit by river) is a real status on Ayeyarwady consignments and can mean days.
