description: Colour as it appears on a product listing, where Burmese habitually keeps the -ရောင် suffix that plain speech may drop. Three of these are colour words a Myanmar shopper reads and a translator will not find in a dictionary of colour terms: သနပ်ခါးရောင် is the pale yellow of thanaka bark paste, ကြေးရောင် the brass of a monastery bell, and သင်္ကန်းရောင် the dark ochre of a monk's robe. English brand colours are used untranslated by Myanmar sellers too, which is why a handful of loans sit at the end.
