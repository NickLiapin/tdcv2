description: Adjective for a product listing. Burmese adjectives are stative verbs — ကောင်း is both "good" and "to be good" — so these attach to a noun with the particle သော or တဲ့ rather than sitting bare in front of it: ကောင်းသောပစ္စည်း, not "ကောင်း ပစ္စည်း". နိုင်ငံခြားဖြစ် ("foreign-made") and ပြည်တွင်းဖြစ် ("domestically made") are among the strongest selling adjectives in Myanmar retail and carry a weight no English equivalent has: imported goods are widely assumed better, and a listing says so.
locale: my
