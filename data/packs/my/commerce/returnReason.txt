description: Reason for returning goods. ငွေလွှဲမှားယွင်း (wrong transfer) is a return reason in Myanmar because so much is paid by manual bank or wallet transfer that mismatched payments are a routine cause of a cancelled order. အတုဖြစ်နေ (counterfeit) is high on the list for the same reason it would be in any market where a large share of consumer goods arrives through informal border trade.
