The first block is retail floor sections as a Yangon shopping centre signs them, the second is the office departments a company org chart lists. ဆန်ရောင်းဝယ်ရေး (rice trading) and ဆီ (edible oil) get their own departments in Myanmar wholesale in a way they do not in Europe, because those two commodities dominate the domestic trade. ရွှေဆိုင် — the gold counter — is a normal department in a Myanmar market, since gold jewellery is how a great many households hold savings.
