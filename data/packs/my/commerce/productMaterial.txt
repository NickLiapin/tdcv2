description: Material. The head of the list is what Myanmar makes things from: ဝါး (bamboo), ကြိမ် (rattan), ကျွန်း (teak — Myanmar holds most of the world's natural teak), ထင်းရှူး (pine), မြေအိုး (fired clay). ယွန်း is lacquerware, the Bagan craft of layered lacquer over a woven bamboo core, and is a material word in Burmese where English needs a phrase. ပိုးထည် is silk and ရှမ်းရိုးရာလက်ရက်ကန့် is Shan handloom cotton. ရွှေချ ("gilded") is a material in Myanmar because gold leaf applied to wood, stone and skin is an everyday finish, not a luxury one.
