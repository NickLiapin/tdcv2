description: One-line review headline. Burmese review language leans on ordinary superlatives — ကောင်းတယ်, အရမ်းကောင်း — and on တန်တယ် ("worth it"), which is the single most common verdict in Myanmar online-shop comments. ရောက်တာမြန်တယ် (fast delivery) is praised far more often than the product itself, because delivery is the unreliable part.
