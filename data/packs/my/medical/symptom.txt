description: Symptom as a Myanmar patient reports it. Several of these are Burmese complaints that do not map onto a single English word. လေထိုးလေအောင့် — "wind stabbing and blocking" — covers colic, bloating, chest tightness and a good deal else, and it is one of the commonest presenting complaints in the country. အအေးမိ is "having caught cold" as an explanatory category rather than a symptom. ကိုယ်ပူ is feeling hot and is not the same statement as ဖျားခြင်း, a measured fever. ဓာတ်ကွဲ ("elements out of balance") comes from traditional medicine and a patient will use it to a Western-trained doctor without translating.
locale: my
