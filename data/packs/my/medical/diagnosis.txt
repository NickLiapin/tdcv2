description: General medical diagnosis in Burmese. THE HEAD OF THIS LIST IS MYANMAR'S ACTUAL DISEASE BURDEN AND IT IS NOT A EUROPEAN ONE. Myanmar is one of the world's thirty high-burden TUBERCULOSIS countries, so အဆုတ်တီဘီ is near the top rather than a historical curiosity. ငှက်ဖျား (malaria) and သွေးလွန်တုပ်ကွေး (dengue haemorrhagic fever) are seasonal mass diagnoses. အသည်းရောင်အသားဝါဘီ (hepatitis B) is carried by a large share of the population and drives the liver-cancer rate. နူနာ (leprosy) is still diagnosed. ခွေးရူးပြန်ရောဂါ (rabies) kills people every year. Alongside them sits the ordinary non-communicable load — ဆီးချို, သွေးတိုး, လေဖြတ် — which is rising fast. A dataset that gives Myanmar patients a European diagnosis mix will be wrong at the top of the distribution, which is where it matters.
