THE DISTRIBUTION IS NOT THE EUROPEAN ONE — group B is markedly more common across mainland Southeast Asia than in Europe, and Rh-NEGATIVE IS RARE, under one per cent of the Myanmar population against roughly fifteen per cent in Europe. A config that generates Myanmar blood groups on a European distribution will produce far too many Rh-negative patients, which is exactly the population a blood bank dataset would be used to model.
