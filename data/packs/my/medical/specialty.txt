description: Medical specialty, full list. Burmese names a specialty by what it treats rather than with a Greek stem, so the words are long and transparent: အရိုးအဆစ်ခွဲစိတ်ကုပညာ is "the science of bone and joint surgery". ရိုးရာဆေးပညာ (traditional Myanmar medicine) is a REGISTERED MEDICAL PROFESSION in Myanmar with its own government university, its own licensing and its own hospitals, not a folk practice — a Myanmar clinician dataset that omits it is missing a real branch of the system. ရွာသူမီးယပ်ဆရာမ, the auxiliary midwife, is the health worker most rural Myanmar people actually see.
locale: my
