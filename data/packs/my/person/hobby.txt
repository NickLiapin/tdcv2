description: Hobby and pastime. Several of these have no European counterpart and are ordinary leisure in Myanmar. လက်ဖက်ရည်ဆိုင်ထိုင် — sitting in a tea shop — is a genuine pastime and arguably the national one, and it is not the same activity as "drinking tea". တရားထိုင် (meditation) is practised by ordinary lay people on a schedule, not only by monks, and ဘုရားဖူး (going to pagodas) is both devotion and the commonest form of domestic tourism. ခြင်းလုံး is chinlone, the cane-ball circle game that is Myanmar's national sport and is played cooperatively with no opposing side. စစ်တုရင် is Burmese chess, which has its own opening rules and is not international chess.
