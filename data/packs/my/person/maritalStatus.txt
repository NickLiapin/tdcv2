description: Marital status. Burmese states it as a matter of HAVING A HOUSEHOLD rather than of a ceremony: အိမ်ထောင်ရှိ is literally "has a household" and အိမ်ထောင်မရှိ "has no household", and those are the two words on a Myanmar form. That is not merely idiom — a Myanmar marriage under Buddhist customary law is constituted by living together publicly as husband and wife, with no registration and no certificate required, so the state's question really is whether a household exists. Burmese distinguishes မုဆိုးမ (widow) from မုဆိုးဖို (widower) with separate words. ရဟန်းဘောင်ဝင် (entered the monastic order) is a real status on a Myanmar record and has no European equivalent: a monk or nun is outside the marital categories altogether, and a married man who ordains later in life is neither married nor divorced.
