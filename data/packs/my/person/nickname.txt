description: Nickname and pet name. Burmese nicknaming works by three devices, all visible here. REDUPLICATION of one syllable of the name (ဇော် becomes ဇော်ဇော်, နီ becomes နီနီ) — note that this is also how many formal names are built, so a reduplicated string may be a nickname or a legal name and nothing in the string distinguishes them. BIRTH ORDER as a name: အကြီး, အလတ်, အငယ်, ညီလေး, ညီမလေး — a Burmese family may address its children this way for life. And the ဖိုး / မ + word pattern (ဖိုးလှ, ဖိုးသား, မငယ်), which is village-informal and slightly affectionate. Foreign nicknames borrowed from film and pop are ordinary among young people in Yangon and a handful sit at the end.
locale: my
