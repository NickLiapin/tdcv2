---
description: Nationality adjective. Burmese does not have a separate adjectival form: the nationality is the country name plus လူမျိုး ("people/race") or နိုင်ငံသား ("national/citizen"), and the two are NOT interchangeable. မြန်မာနိုင်ငံသား means a citizen of Myanmar whatever their ethnicity; မြန်မာလူမျိုး means ethnically Bamar and excludes a Shan, Kachin or Karen citizen. That distinction is politically live in Myanmar and a config translating "Burmese" without choosing between them is picking a side by accident. This list uses နိုင်ငံသား throughout, the citizenship reading. For the ethnic categories see person/language.txt.
locale: my
