description: Common verb in the infinitive, in Nynorsk. This is the single most divergent list in the pack: twenty-five of the forty-five differ from Bokmål, and they are the verbs a language cannot do without. vere for være, gjere for gjøre, seie for si, sjå for se, kome for komme, gje for gi, tenkje for tenke, fortelje for fortelle, spørje for spørre, byggje for bygge, spele for spille, byrje for begynne, halde for holde, bere for bære, opne for åpne, vekse for vokse, hugse for huske, selje for selge, ete for spise and springe for løpe. The last pair is not a spelling at all but a different word: Nynorsk springe against Bokmål løpe.
