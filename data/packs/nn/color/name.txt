This is the LOWEST-overlap list in the pack and the reason is one rule applied over and over: three of the basic colours are spelled differently — raud for rød, kvit for hvit, grøn for grønn — and every compound built on them changes too. rustraud, vinraud, mørkeraud, karmosinraud, teglraud; kremkvit, perlekvit; limegrøn, olivengrøn, mosegrøn, flaskegrøn, smaragdgrøn. elfenbein carries the diphthong Bokmål lost in elfenben, and gammalrosa the Nynorsk gammal for gammel.
