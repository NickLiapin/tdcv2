READ THIS BEFORE COMPARING PACKS: this list is identical to the nb pack's, and that is correct rather than a copy. A surname is a legal name in one national population register; it is spelled the way the register spells it whichever written standard the bearer writes, so there is no Nynorsk form of Hansen and inventing one would be a fabrication. Surnames DO NOT inflect for gender in Norwegian, so there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack and its absence is the design. Two thirds of Norwegian surnames are farm names (gardsnamn, Bokmål gårdsnavn) — Berg, Haugen, Moen, Strand, Nygård, Bakke, Vik, Myklebust — and those are the names that dominate the Nynorsk-writing west, while the frozen -sen patronymics at the head of this list are an eastern and urban pattern. SSB publishes bearer counts nationally and not by county, so the western skew is described here and NOT encoded in the weights: guessing a regional distribution would have been an invented number.
