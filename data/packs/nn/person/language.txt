description: Language as Nynorsk names it. The first three entries are the point of this file: norsk is the language, and nynorsk and bokmål are its two written standards — not two languages and not dialects. Roughly one Norwegian in ten has nynorsk as their registered school standard, concentrated in the western counties. The three Sámi languages and kvensk are the recognised minority languages; Nynorsk writes nordsamisk, lulesamisk and sørsamisk exactly as Bokmål does. teiknspråk carries the Nynorsk diphthong against Bokmål tegnspråk.
