description: Common allergen in Nynorsk. Five differ from Bokmål: mjølk for melk, kveite for hvete (the same Nynorsk word that means halibut — a real homograph), graspollen for gresspollen, kvefsestikk for vepsestikk and skaldyr for skalldyr. Birch, grass and mugwort pollen are the three that matter in Norway, in that order through the season.
