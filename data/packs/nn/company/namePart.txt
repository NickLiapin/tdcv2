description: Element to build a Norwegian company name from, in Nynorsk. Aust for Øst, Rein for Ren, Segl for Seil, Kjelde for Kilde, Straum for Strøm, Framtid for Fremtid and Bryggje for Brygge are the divergences. The list is mostly landscape and seafaring because that is what Norwegian firms name themselves after — Straum in particular does double duty as current and as electricity, which is why so many Norwegian power companies use it.
