description: What follows a Norwegian company name. The first fifteen are the legal forms and are fixed abbreviations set by statute — AS is aksjeselskap, ASA the public equivalent, ENK a sole proprietorship, IKS an inter-municipal company, BRL a housing cooperative — and they are identical in both written standards because they are codes. The descriptive tail is where Nynorsk shows: Stiftinga for Stiftelsen, Foreininga for Foreningen, Sameige for Sameie, Gruppa for Gruppen, Eigedom for Eiendom, Verkstad for Verksted, Løysingar for Løsninger, Tenester for Tjenester, Rådgjeving for Rådgivning, & Søner for & Sønner — and Noreg for Norge, which is what a Nynorsk company actually puts in its name.
