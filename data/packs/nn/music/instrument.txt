description: Musical instrument in Nynorsk. trekkspel and munnspel replace trekkspill and munnspill. The last five are Norwegian folk instruments and the first of them is a Nynorsk-country instrument specifically: the hardingfele, with four or five sympathetic strings under the fingerboard, comes from Hardanger in Vestland — the same region that carries the written standard. langeleik, lur, bukkehorn and seljefløyte are the others, and none has an English name.
