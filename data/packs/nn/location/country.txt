description: Country names in Nynorsk, sorted in NORWEGIAN alphabetical order — the 26 Latin letters, then æ, ø and å in that order AFTER z. Compare the tail with the Bokmål pack to see the whole point of this locale in one place: nb ends Zambia, Zimbabwe, Øst-Timor, Østerrike, Ålandsøyene, while nn ends Zambia, Zimbabwe, Ålandsøyane — because Nynorsk keeps the Old Norse diphthong in Aust-Timor and Austerrike, which moves both countries from the very end of the alphabet to near the very front. The other systematic difference is the definite plural: Nynorsk island groups end -øyane where Bokmål ends -øyene (Færøyane, Filippinane, Salomonøyane, Cookøyane, Maldivane, Seychellane). And Norway itself is Noreg. Sorted by an English or plain-Unicode collator, Ålandsøyane jumps to the front, which is the commonest way a Norwegian list comes out visibly wrong.
