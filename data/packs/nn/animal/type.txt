description: Kind of animal in Nynorsk — farm animals first, then the wild ones of Norway, then a few animals a Norwegian knows only from a zoo. kval and kvalross are the divergence and they are the clearest example of the hv-/kv- rule in the whole pack: Nynorsk writes kv where Bokmål writes hv, so hval and hvalross become kval and kvalross. spekkhoggar takes the -ar agent ending. villrein is the wild reindeer and rein the herded one, and Norwegian keeps them apart.
