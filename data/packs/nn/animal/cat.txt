description: Cat breeds recognised in Norway, in Nynorsk. Norsk skogkatt is the national breed. Breed names built on a place take the Nynorsk origin suffix -ar where Bokmål takes -er — persar, siamesar, abyssinar, burmesar, orientalar — and heilag birma carries the Nynorsk heilag for hellig. The English and made-up breed names (ragdoll, sphynx, scottish fold, russian blue) keep their real spelling in any locale.
