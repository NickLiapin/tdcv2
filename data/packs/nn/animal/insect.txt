description: Insects and other small creatures in Nynorsk. kvefs is the western word for the wasp that Bokmål calls veps; sommarfugl, augestikkar, grashoppe and nattsvermar are the Nynorsk forms of sommerfugl, øyenstikker, gresshoppe and nattsvermer. knott and klegg have no English single word — the first is the biting midge of a Norwegian summer evening and the second the horsefly.
