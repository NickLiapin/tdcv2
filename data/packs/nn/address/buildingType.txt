description: Kind of building in Nynorsk. Nine of these are different words from Bokmål and they show three separate Nynorsk rules at once: bustad for bolig and verkstad for verksted (the -stad compounds), leilegheit for leilighet and rekkjehus for rekkehus (the -leg-/-kje- forms), and sjukehus, sjukeheim, skule and kyrkje for sykehus, sykehjem, skole and kirke. støl is the western word for the summer mountain farm that eastern Norwegian calls seter, and it is what a Nynorsk municipality writes. naust, stabbur and fjøs are shared and untranslatable — a naust is a boathouse on the shore, a stabbur a raised storehouse on pillars.
