description: The sixteen-point compass in Nynorsk. aust is the divergence: Nynorsk kept the Old Norse diphthong austr where Bokmål took Danish øst, so every compound containing it differs too — nordaust, søraust, aust-nordaust. nord, sør and vest are shared. This is the clearest place in the pack to see the diphthong rule at work, because the four cardinal points are a closed set and exactly one of them moved.
