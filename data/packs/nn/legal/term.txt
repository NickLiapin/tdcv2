description: Term of art from Norwegian procedure, in Nynorsk. Two entries are genuinely different WORDS and not spellings: orskurd is the Nynorsk term for the court order that Bokmål calls a kjennelse, and påtalemakt replaces påtalemyndighet — both are the forms used in Nynorsk judgments. saksøkjar, forsvarar and meddommar carry the -ar agent ending, forelding replaces foreldelse and mellombels forføying replaces midlertidig forføyning. A meddommar is a lay judge who sits on the bench with equal vote, which Norwegian courts use as standard.
