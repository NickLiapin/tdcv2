description: Job titles in Nynorsk. This is the LOWEST-overlap list in the pack after color/name, and the reason is one rule that happens to land on job titles harder than anywhere else: Nynorsk forms agent nouns in -ar where Bokmål forms them in -er. lærar, utviklar, forskar, rådgjevar, leiar, arbeidar, seljar, dommar, meklar, bakar, målar, sveisar, dykkar, fiskar. Nouns in -iker become -ikar: mekanikar, elektrikar, analytikar, automatikar. On top of that come the word-level differences — sjukepleiar for sykepleier, røyrleggjar for rørlegger, sakshandsamar for saksbehandler, rekneskapsførar for regnskapsfører, marknadsførar for markedsfører, eigedomsmeklar for eiendomsmegler, kyrkjetenar for kirketjener, reinhaldar for renholder, vaktmeister for vaktmester, glasmeister for glassmester, hamnearbeidar for havnearbeider, kabintilsett for kabinansatt and salssjef for salgssjef. Loanwords do NOT take -ar: ingeniør, direktør, revisor, konsulent, jurist, lege and professor are identical in both standards, and that boundary is the thing a config author most often gets wrong.
locale: nn
