Seven of the twenty-two differ from Bokmål and each shows a different rule: sal for salg and marknad for marked (native word forms), rekneskap for regnskap and forsking for forskning (Nynorsk -ing where Bokmål has -ning), eigedom for eiendom (diphthong), tryggleik for sikkerhet (Nynorsk -leik where Bokmål borrowed the Low German -het) and etterleving for etterlevelse (-ing for -else). HMS is helse, miljø og tryggleik — occupational health and safety — and is a legally required function in every Norwegian workplace.
