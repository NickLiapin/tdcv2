description: Norwegian dishes as Nynorsk names them. Food is where the two standards separate hardest, because the words are old and native: kjøt for kjøtt (pinnekjøt, kjøtkaker), graut for grøt (rømmegraut, risgraut), steik for stek, omn for ovn, kvit for hvit, skule for skole (skulebrød) and bollar/vaflar for boller/vafler. komle is the west-coast name for the potato dumpling that eastern Norway calls raspeball and Trøndelag klubb — the same dish under three regional names, and komle is the one a Nynorsk municipality prints on a canteen menu. smalahove is a salted, smoked half sheep's head and is eaten in exactly the counties that write Nynorsk.
