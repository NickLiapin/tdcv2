description: Court and dispute body (Estonian), as the court system of ESTONIA is built. Three tiers, but two separate first-instance branches: a maakohus hears civil and criminal cases and a halduskohus hears cases against the state, both appeal to a ringkonnakohus, and the Riigikohus at the top is at once the court of cassation and the constitutional court — Estonia has no separate constitutional tribunal of the German or Polish kind. Töövaidluskomisjon and üürikomisjon settle employment and tenancy disputes out of court and most never reach a judge.
