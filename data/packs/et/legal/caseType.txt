description: Legal case type (Estonian). Estonian law splits punishable acts in two and the distinction runs through the whole system: a kuritegu is a crime and goes to a criminal court, a väärtegu is a misdemeanour and is usually settled by the police or an inspectorate without a judge at all. Haldusasi, an administrative case against the state, goes to a separate court branch.
