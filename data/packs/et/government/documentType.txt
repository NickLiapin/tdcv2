description: Official document type (Estonian). The digital documents are not a novelty item on this list, they are the ordinary case: every Estonian resident holds an ID-kaart with a chip that gives a legally binding signature, and Mobiil-ID and Smart-ID do the same from a phone. An e-residendi digi-ID is issued to non-residents who run an Estonian company from abroad and grants no residence or citizenship at all — it is a signing key, and it is regularly misread as an immigration document.
