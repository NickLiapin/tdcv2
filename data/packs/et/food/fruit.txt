The wild berries are not a garnish in this list, they are the point: mustikas, pohl, jõhvikas and murakas are picked in quantity by ordinary families every August under the right of public access, which lets anyone gather berries and mushrooms on land they do not own. Astelpaju, sea buckthorn, is grown commercially here and pressed for juice.
