description: Spice or herb (Estonian). Kadakamarjad and mädarõigas are the two an Estonian kitchen has that a Mediterranean one does not: juniper berries flavour the smoked meat and the Christmas pork, and grated horseradish is what goes beside sült. Till is used at a volume that surprises visitors — it goes on the potatoes, in the soup and over the fish.
