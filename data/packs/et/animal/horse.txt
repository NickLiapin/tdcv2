description: Horse breed (Estonian). Three of these are native: eesti hobune is the old Estonian Native, one of the few surviving north European forest horses; tori hobune was bred at the Tori stud from 1856; eesti raskeveohobune is the Estonian Draft. All three are on the national list of endangered farm-animal breeds.
