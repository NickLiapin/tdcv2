description: Dog breed (Estonian). Eesti hagijas, the Estonian Hound, is the only dog breed native to Estonia — bred in the 1940s when Moscow ordered every Soviet republic to develop a hound too short to hunt elk — and it was made a national symbol in 2005. Everything else is an international breed under its Estonian name.
