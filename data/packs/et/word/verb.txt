description: Common verb (Estonian), given in the -ma infinitive, which is the citation form. Estonian has NO FUTURE TENSE: the present carries it, so ma lähen means both I am going and I will go, and a config writing about tomorrow needs no separate form — only a time word. Estonian also has two infinitives, -ma and -da, which are not interchangeable: ma hakkan lugema but ma tahan lugeda.
locale: et
