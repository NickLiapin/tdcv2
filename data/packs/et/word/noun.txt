description: Common noun (Estonian). Estonian has no articles and no gender, so a bare noun is complete as it stands; what it has instead is FOURTEEN CASES, and every relation English puts in a preposition Estonian puts on the end of the word — maja is a house, majas in the house, majast out of the house, majja into the house, majaga with the house, majani as far as the house. The other thing this file records is compounding, which is unlimited and ordinary: raudteejaam is raud+tee+jaam, iron-road-station, and Estonian will happily build sünnipäevanädalalõpupidu, a birthday-weekend-party, as a single legal word.
