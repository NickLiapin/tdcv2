description: Common adjective (Estonian, nominative singular). Estonian adjectives agree with their noun in case and number but NEVER in gender, because the language has none — so one form serves any referent and the only thing that ever changes is the case. Note also the length contrast the spelling only half shows: koli, kolli and kolli are three different words distinguished by short, long and overlong quantity, and Estonian writes only two of the three degrees.
locale: et
