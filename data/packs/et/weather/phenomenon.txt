description: Weather or sky phenomenon (Estonian). Two are specific to the latitude. Valged ööd are the white nights of June, when at 59° north the sky never fully darkens and Estonians simply stop going to bed; and virmalised, the aurora, are visible from Estonia several times a winter. Jäide, glaze ice, is the freezing rain that coats every surface and closes the roads.
