What an Estonian lender checks is the maksehäireregister, the register of payment defaults kept by Creditinfo Eesti: a default is either recorded against you or it is not, and the bands below are how lenders describe the result rather than a computed number. A dataset that needs a three-digit score for an Estonian person is modelling something that does not exist here.
