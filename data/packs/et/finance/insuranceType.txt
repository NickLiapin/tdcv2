description: Insurance type (Estonian). Liikluskindlustus and kasko are two different things and Estonians never confuse them: liikluskindlustus is the compulsory third-party motor cover without which a car may not legally be on the road, kasko is the voluntary comprehensive cover on top. Health cover is missing from the top of this list on purpose — Estonian residents are covered by the state Tervisekassa through their social tax, so private tervisekindlustus is a top-up rather than the main event.
