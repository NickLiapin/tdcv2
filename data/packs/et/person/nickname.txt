description: Common informal or folk short name (Estonian). Two things are worth knowing. First, these are rahvapärased nimed — the short forms that came out of the Lutheran church registers when German and biblical names were reshaped by Estonian mouths, so Johannes became Jaan and Juku, Katariina became Kadri and Kati, Magdaleena became Mall. Second, several are literary: Toots, Kiir and Teele are the schoolchildren of Oskar Luts’s Kevade, the best-loved book in the language, and an Estonian hearing Toots hears the character before the name. Nothing on this list is marked for sex by its ending, only by usage.
locale: et
