description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Estonian. Amounts are in EURO: Estonia adopted the euro on 1 January 2011, replacing the kroon, and was the first of the post-Soviet states to do so. Estonian writes thousands with a space and the decimal with a comma, so 45 000,50 €, and the symbol stands after the number with a space between. The brackets are set against Estonian earnings rather than western European ones — the average gross wage is a little under 2 000 euros a month, so the top band here is genuinely high pay in this country.
