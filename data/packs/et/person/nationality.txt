description: Nationality / demonym (Estonian). Estonian forms almost all of them with the suffix -lane, added to the country stem — eestlane, soomlane, rootslane, sakslane — and the same suffix works for any new country name, which is why the list is so regular. Note that the word for a nationality is never marked for the sex of the person: eestlane is an Estonian man and an Estonian woman alike.
