description: Hobby or interest (Estonian). Several of these are Estonian institutions rather than generic pastimes. Marjul käimine and seenel käimine — going for berries and going for mushrooms — are possible for everybody because of igaüheõigus, the right of public access, which lets anyone walk, pick and camp on land they do not own. Koorilaul is a mass activity here rather than a niche one, feeding the Song Festival; rahvatants likewise. Talisuplus is swimming through a hole in the ice, usually straight out of a sauna. Discgolf is on this list because Estonia has one of the highest densities of disc golf courses in the world, most of them free to play.
