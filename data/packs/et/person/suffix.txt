description: Estonian post-nominal abbreviation. Estonian has no Jr or Sr of the English kind — the equivalents are vanem and noorem, written after the name and genuinely rare. The rest are degree abbreviations, written after the name with a comma. Two are historical and still carried by living Estonians: cand. sc. and dr. sc. are the Soviet-era teaduste kandidaat and teaduste doktor, awarded before Estonia moved to the Bologna structure in 1992, and a CV showing one dates its holder precisely.
