description: Zodiac sign (Estonian). Estonian translates the signs into native words rather than keeping the Latin — jäär is a ram, sõnn a bull, vähk a crab, kaalud a pair of scales, ambur an archer, kaljukits a mountain goat, veevalaja one who pours water — and only skorpion is a straight loan. Sõnn carries the letter õ, which Finnish does not have.
