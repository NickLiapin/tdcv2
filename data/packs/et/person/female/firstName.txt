description: Estonian female given names, frequency-ordered; the leading figures are bearer counts on the scale the population register publishes and the tail is a smooth decay anchored to the same scale. The generational split is sharper than in the male list: Anne, Tiiu, Malle, Sirje, Ene, Ülle and Aime are overwhelmingly women born in the 1950s and 1960s, Nele, Sandra, Mirtel and Pilleriin are the school registers of today, and the block from Alide to Vilhelmine is the German-derived layer of women born before the Second World War, still alive in Estonian records. Several names are ordinary Estonian words used as names, which Estonian allows freely because a name needs no gender ending to work: Leelo is a folk song, Tuuli wind, Õie flower-of, Õnne luck, Salme a word for a strait. The pair Õie and Õnne is the quickest proof that this file is Estonian and not Finnish, because the letter õ does not exist in Finnish at all. Russian given names are not here — see person/lastName for the reasoning.
