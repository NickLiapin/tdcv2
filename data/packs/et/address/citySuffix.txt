description: Trailing element of an Estonian place name. Two families sit here. The landscape words -mäe, -järve, -jõe, -saare, -ranna, -nurme, -metsa describe the terrain and behave like their Finnish counterparts. The other family has no Finnish twin: -vere, -la, -ste and -küla are inherited settlement suffixes whose first parts are usually a person long forgotten, which is why Rakvere, Rapla and Kohila mean nothing at all to a modern Estonian ear. Compose after a cityPrefix.
