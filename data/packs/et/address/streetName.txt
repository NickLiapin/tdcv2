description: Modifier part of an Estonian street name, in the GENITIVE, to be composed with a streetSuffix — Kase tänav, Jaama tee, Vabaduse puiestee. Estonian keeps the two as separate words, unlike Finnish and Swedish which compound them, so a name and a suffix can be drawn independently here. A few Estonian streets are a bare adjective with no thoroughfare word at all (Pikk, Lai, Uus in the Tallinn old town) and those are listed as they stand. The personal names are Estonian writers and folk figures with a street in most towns; city-specific streets belong in the country pack.
locale: et
