---
description: Leading element of an Estonian place name (Suure-Jaani, Väike-Maarja, Vana-Vigala, Põhja-Tallinn). Unlike Finnish, which binds the parts into a single word, Estonian hyphenates and puts the first element in the GENITIVE: suur gives Suure-, väike gives Väike-, kõrge gives Kõrge-. Compose with a citySuffix; on its own the element names no real place.
locale: et
