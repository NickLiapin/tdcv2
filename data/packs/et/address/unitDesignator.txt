description: Estonian address unit designator. An Estonian flat address is street, house number, hyphen, flat number — Pikk 12-5 — so the commonest designator of all is the bare hyphen and the word korter is normally left out; krt is its abbreviation and appears on formal documents. Estonia has no stairwell letter of the Finnish or Swedish kind. Pakiautomaat is a parcel-locker bay and is a real delivery address here, because lockers rather than doorsteps are how most Estonian parcels arrive.
