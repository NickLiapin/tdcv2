description: Directional qualifier used with an Estonian district, estate or road name (Põhja-Tallinn, Kesklinn, Ida-Viru). Like Finnish and unlike the Germanic languages, Estonian has single native words for the intermediate compass points — kirre, kagu, edel, loe — rather than compounding north with east. The hyphenated forms are the genitive stems that stand in front of a place name: kirre gives Kirde-, loe gives Loode-.
