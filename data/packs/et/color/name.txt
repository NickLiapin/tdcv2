description: Human-readable colour name (Estonian). Estonian builds most colour names by compounding a modifier onto the base — hele- for light, tume- for dark, and any noun at all for a shade, so rukkilillesinine is literally cornflower-blue. Rukkilill is Estonia’s national flower and the blue of the flag, so that shade name is not decorative here. Note that an Estonian colour word never agrees with its noun in gender, because the language has none to agree with.
