description: Company legal form or name suffix (Estonian). OÜ, osaühing, is the private limited company and is overwhelmingly the commonest form in the country — Estonia’s e-Residency programme lets a non-resident found one online in under a day without ever setting foot in the country, which is why there are more registered OÜs per head here than almost anywhere. AS is the public limited company, MTÜ a non-profit association, SA a foundation, FIE a sole trader, KÜ the apartment association that owns the common parts of a block of flats. Estonian writes the form BEFORE the name as often as after it: OÜ Tamme Kaubandus is as normal as Tamme Kaubandus OÜ.
