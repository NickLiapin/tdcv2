description: Venue type (Estonian). Lauluväljak and laululava are venue types rather than single buildings: a laululava, an open-air song stage with a shell-shaped roof, stands in almost every Estonian town and is where the local song and dance festivals happen. Rahvamaja, the parish community house, is the other one — most were built between the wars and they are still where a village holds its concerts, elections and funerals.
