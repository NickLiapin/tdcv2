description: Country and territory names (Estonian). Estonian keeps its own forms for most of them and translates rather than transliterates — Saksamaa, Rootsi, Soome, Venemaa, Prantsusmaa, Holland, Ühendkuningriik — with -maa, literally land, as the commonest ending. Estonian uses no article, but country names inflect like any other noun through fourteen cases: Eestis is in Estonia, Rootsi is to Sweden, Soomest is from Finland, so a config that drops one into a sentence must inflect it rather than paste the nominative.
