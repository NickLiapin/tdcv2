description: Compass direction (Estonian), sixteen points. The four intermediate points have SINGLE NATIVE WORDS — kirre north-east, kagu south-east, edel south-west, loe north-west — inherited from Finnic and not built by compounding north with east the way English, German and Russian do. Only the sixteenths compound, and Estonian joins them with a hyphen using the genitive stem: põhja-kirre, ida-kagu. Estonian writes compass directions in lower case.
