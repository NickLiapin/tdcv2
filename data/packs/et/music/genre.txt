description: Music genre (Estonian). Two of these are Estonian genres rather than local names for international ones. Regilaul is the pre-rhyming runic song of the Finnic peoples, sung in an eight-syllable line with a leader and a chorus, and it is still performed and recorded. Koorimuusika is a genre in the ordinary sense here — Estonia has one of the highest rates of choir membership in the world and the composers Arvo Pärt, Veljo Tormis and Cyrillus Kreek wrote for those choirs specifically.
