description: Month name (Estonian), written in LOWER CASE. Estonia uses the international Latin names, which is where it parts company with Finnish: Finnish says tammikuu where Estonian says jaanuar. Estonian does still have a full set of native folk month names, and they are alive in almanacs and in speech — näärikuu, küünlakuu, paastukuu, jürikuu, lehekuu, jaanikuu, heinakuu, lõikuskuu, mihklikuu, viinakuu, mardikuu, jõulukuu, most of them named after the saint’s day or the farm work that falls in them. They are not the values here because no Estonian writes them on a form.
