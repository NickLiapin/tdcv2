description: Academic or educational qualification (Estonian). The Estonian ladder is põhiharidus (basic, to year 9), then either gümnaasium or a vocational school, then rakenduskõrgharidus or a bakalaureus, and Estonia follows the Bologna 3+2 structure. Two Soviet-era titles are still carried by living Estonians and appear on CVs: teaduste kandidaat and teaduste doktor, awarded before the system changed in 1992.
