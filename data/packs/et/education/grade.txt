description: Grade (Estonian). Estonian schools mark on a FIVE-POINT numeric scale where 5 is the best and 1 the worst — the opposite direction from the German scale and a different range from the American letters — and the words in brackets are the official names of the marks. Universities switched to the letters A to F, with F failing, when Estonia joined the Bologna process, and both scales are in daily use. Arvestatud and mittearvestatud are the pass/fail marks for courses that are not graded.
