description: Property listing status (Norwegian). Budrunde is not a translation of "bidding" but a named stage: Norwegian homes are sold by an open bidding round in which every bid is legally binding, bids are disclosed to all parties, and the whole thing is usually over within a day of the viewing. Prisantydning is an indicated price, not an asking price — the final price routinely lands above it.
