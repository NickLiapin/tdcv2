description: Property type (Norwegian). Norway has THREE distinct ownership forms for a flat and they are not interchangeable: selveier means owning the flat outright, borettslag means owning a share in a housing cooperative that owns the building and gives you the right to live in one flat, and aksjeleilighet means the same through shares in a company. The form changes the price, the tax, the paperwork and what the buyer may do with it, so a listing always states which.
