description: Event type (Norwegian). Three of these are Norwegian institutions with no English equivalent. Julebord is the compulsory-feeling company Christmas dinner, held any time from mid-November. Dugnad is unpaid collective work the members of a housing block, a sports club or a school do together, summoned rather than hired — the word carries a social obligation that "volunteering" does not. Konfirmasjon is a rite of passage at fifteen that half the country now holds in a secular version.
