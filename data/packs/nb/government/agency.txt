description: KIND of public body, in the Norwegian words for it — not the names of individual agencies. That boundary is deliberate and it is not the one en and pl draw: the Norway country pack already ships the named Norwegian agencies at norway.government.agency, verified entry by entry against the Enhetsregisteret, so naming them again here would be a second copy of a country pack's data inside a language pack, drifting apart at the first rename. Norwegian government is directorate-shaped, and these words are how it is described: a departement sets policy, a direktorat or etat does the work with its own budget and organisation number, a tilsyn inspects, and a statsforvalter represents the state in a county. Note two live renames — fylkesmann became statsforvalter in 2021, and the pair kommune / fylkeskommune are elected local government rather than state bodies.
