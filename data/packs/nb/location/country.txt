description: Country names in Norwegian, sorted in NORWEGIAN alphabetical order. That sort is the point: the Norwegian alphabet is the 26 Latin letters followed by æ, ø and å, in that order, AFTER z — so this list ends Zambia, Zimbabwe, Øst-Timor, Østerrike, Ålandsøyene. Sorted by an English or plain-Unicode collator the same three jump to the front (Ålandsøyene first, Østerrike near the As), which is the commonest way a Norwegian list comes out visibly wrong. The names are Norwegian, not English respelled: Germany is Tyskland, Greece is Hellas, China is Kina, Switzerland is Sveits, the United Kingdom is Storbritannia.
