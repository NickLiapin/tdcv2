description: Noun (Norwegian), INDEFINITE SINGULAR — the dictionary form, which is the form this pack ships for every noun list. Two things a reader needs from this file. First, the definite article is a SUFFIX, not a word: bok becomes boka or boken, hus becomes huset, and both boka and boken are correct Bokmål — Bokmål has three genders, but the feminine is optional in conservative usage, so a feminine noun may take either the feminine -a or the masculine -en, and neither is an error. Shipping the indefinite form is what lets a config choose. Second, look at bok, mat and gate beside their Danish cognates bog, mad and gade: Norwegian kept the hard consonants that Danish softened, and that single sound shift accounts for a large share of the difference between two written languages that otherwise look almost the same on the page.
