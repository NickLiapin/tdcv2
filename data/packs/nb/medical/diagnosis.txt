description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient — no sex or ancestry restriction; sex-specific conditions are in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale and ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. Three entries are Norwegian rather than translated: kols is the ordinary Norwegian word for COPD and is written lowercase like a common noun even though it began as an acronym; grå stær and grønn stær are cataract and glaucoma, literally "grey" and "green staring". Borreliose and vinterdepresjon are on the list because tick-borne disease and seasonal affective disorder are both markedly commoner at Norwegian latitudes than the reference list implies.
