description: Company department or function (Norwegian). Two notes. HMS — helse, miljø og sikkerhet — is a statutory function every Norwegian employer must have, with an internkontroll system behind it, so it is a department and not a policy. Personal is what Norwegian calls the human-resources function; the two-letter English abbreviation is avoided here for the same reason the category itself is called workplace and not hr, since hr is the language code for Croatian.
