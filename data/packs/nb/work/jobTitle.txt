description: Norwegian job titles across sectors. Several have no clean English equivalent and are here because they are ordinary Norwegian jobs: helsefagarbeider and vernepleier are two distinct care qualifications below nursing, LIS-lege is a doctor in specialty training, barne- og ungdomsarbeider works in schools and after-school care, reindriftsutøver herds reindeer under Sami reindeer-husbandry law, and prosessoperatør runs a chemical or petroleum plant. Sivilingeniør is a protected title, not a seniority level.
