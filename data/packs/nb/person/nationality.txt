description: Nationality (Norwegian). These are NOUNS naming a person — nordmann, svenske, tysker — not adjectives; the adjective is a different word (norsk, svensk, tysk) and a config that wants "Norwegian passport" needs the adjective, not this list. Several are gendered in origin (nordmann, franskmann, engelskmann literally end in -mann) and are used for women too. Same and kven are Norway's indigenous and national-minority peoples and are nationalities in ordinary Norwegian use.
