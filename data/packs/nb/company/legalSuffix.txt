description: Company legal form or name suffix used in Norway. AS (aksjeselskap) is the ordinary limited company and covers the overwhelming majority of Norwegian firms; ASA is its listed form. NUF is the Norwegian branch of a foreign company, ENK a sole proprietorship, IKS a company owned jointly by municipalities, HF a state health trust, and BRL a housing cooperative — none of which map onto Inc/LLC/Ltd. The trailing words after the legal forms are name elements, not legal forms.
