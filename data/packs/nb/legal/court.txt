It is a three-tier system — tingrett, lagmannsrett, Høyesterett — with two additions no other country has in the same shape: forliksrådet, a lay conciliation board that most civil claims must pass through first and that can give a binding judgment, and jordskifteretten, a court that redraws property boundaries and shared rights over land.
