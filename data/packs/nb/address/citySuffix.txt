description: Trailing element of a Norwegian place name (Lillehammer, Sandvika, Fredrikstad, Hammerfest). Norwegian glues it straight onto the first element with no space, so compose without a separator: Berg + dal gives Bergdal, not "Berg dal". Many appear in both an indefinite and a definite form (vik/viken, haug/haugen, li/lia) and both are real place names.
locale: nb
