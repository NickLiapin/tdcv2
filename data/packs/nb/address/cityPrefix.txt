description: Leading element of a Norwegian place name (Nord-Odal, Nedre Eiker, Store Ringheim, Vestre Toten). Combine with a real toponym; it does not by itself name a real place. Norwegian keeps two parallel sets of compass qualifiers — the plain nord/sør/øst/vest and the older comparative nordre/søndre/østre/vestre — and both are live in municipality names.
