---
description: Complete Norwegian street names, of kinds found in nearly every municipality. These are WHOLE names, not stems to compose: Norwegian glues the thoroughfare word onto the name as one word (Storgata, Kirkeveien, Solbakken), so there is no separate suffix to append the way ulica or Street is appended in other locales — that is what address/streetSuffix.txt is for, and the two are alternatives, not a pair. Names honouring a person keep the genitive -s and a separate gate/vei (Karl Johans gate, Nansens vei). Street names specific to one city belong in a Norway country pack.
locale: nb
