description: Norwegian thoroughfare word. Unlike most European locales this goes AFTER the name and is written as part of the same word, with no space: Stor + gata is Storgata, Kirke + veien is Kirkeveien. Both the Bokmål doublets are live — gate and gata, vei and veien — and a municipality picks one; -gata dominates in Oslo and the east, -gaten in Bergen and the south-west. veg/vegen is the Nynorsk-leaning spelling and is used in official street names in many eastern and central municipalities even where the municipality writes Bokmål.
