description: Norwegian address unit designator. A Norwegian flat is identified by a bruksenhetsnummer written H0101 — H for a dwelling floor, then the storey, then the flat on that storey — which is a code rather than a word, so the words below are what appears beside it on a door, a form or a deed. gnr./bnr./snr. are the cadastral triple (holding, plot, section) that identifies any Norwegian property.
