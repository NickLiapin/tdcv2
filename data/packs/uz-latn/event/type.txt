description: Event type (Uzbek). THE FIRST BLOCK IS THE LIFE-CYCLE OF AN UZBEK FAMILY and each is a named, separately celebrated occasion: beshik toʻyi when a baby is first put in the cradle, sunnat toʻyi for a boy's circumcision, fotiha toʻy for the engagement, nikoh toʻy for the wedding itself, and ehson, the memorial meal held on fixed days after a death. Hashar is a collective volunteer work day called by the mahalla. These are not optional social extras; they organise the calendar.
locale: uz-latn
