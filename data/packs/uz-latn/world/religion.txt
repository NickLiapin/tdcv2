THE SUFI ORDERS ARE LOCAL FOUNDATIONS, not imports: Naqshbandiya was founded in Bukhara, Yassaviya in Turkistan and Kubroviya in Khorezm, and their shrines are still visited. Zardushtiylik — Zoroastrianism — was the religion of this region before Islam and the Avesta is traditionally placed in Khorezm. Islam here is overwhelmingly Sunni of the HANAFIY school, which is a specific legal tradition and not a detail: it shapes everything from inheritance to the wedding contract.
