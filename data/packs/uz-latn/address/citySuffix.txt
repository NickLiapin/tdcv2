description: Trailing element of an Uzbek place name (Yangiyoʻl, Kattaqoʻrgʻon, Mingbuloq, Uchquduq, Chorbogʻ). THREE LANGUAGES ARE STACKED IN THIS LIST and the layering is the history of the region: -kent and -obod are Iranian — kent is Sogdian for town, which is why Toshkent means stone town, and -obod is Persian for a settled, watered place; -qoʻrgʻon, -tepa, -quduq, -buloq and -koʻl are Turkic; and qalʼa, mozor and langar are Arabic-Islamic. Water dominates the Turkic layer — buloq spring, quduq well, ariq canal, soy mountain stream — because in Central Asia a settlement is named for its water.
