description: Leading element of an Uzbek place name (Yangiyoʻl, Eskishahar, Kattaqoʻrgʻon, Uchquduq, Beshariq). NUMBERS ARE A LIVE TOPONYMIC ELEMENT here in a way they are not in Europe: Uch- (three), Besh- (five), Yetti- (seven) and Ming- (thousand) open real settlement names and usually count wells, canals or springs. Colours do the same work — Oq- white, Qora- black, Qizil- red, Koʻk- blue. Combine with a real toponym; on its own it does not name a settlement.
locale: uz-latn
