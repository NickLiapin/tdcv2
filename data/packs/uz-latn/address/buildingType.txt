description: Building type (Uzbek). HOVLI IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT TRANSLATE: the traditional Uzbek house faces INWARD onto a walled courtyard, so hovli names the whole property — yard, rooms and gate together — and is the commonest kind of dwelling outside the apartment blocks. Choyxona, the teahouse, is a building type in its own right and is where a mahalla meets. The Russian layer is plainly visible in kottej, dacha, sex, angar and obshchejitiye, all of which arrived in the Soviet period.
locale: uz-latn
