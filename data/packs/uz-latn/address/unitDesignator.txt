description: Unit designator inside an Uzbek address. The order runs from the largest part inward — massiv, kvartal, mahalla, then uy, podyezd, qavat, xonadon — which is the Soviet addressing scheme and is still what a postal form asks for. MAHALLA IS NOT AN ORDINARY WORD FOR DISTRICT: it is a legally constituted neighbourhood body with an elected chairman, and it appears in an Uzbek address as a real administrative level.
