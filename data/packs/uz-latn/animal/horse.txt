description: Horse breed (Uzbek). QORABAYIR IS THE NATIONAL BREED — a compact, hard-hoofed mountain horse bred in the Zarafshon valley and the one ridden in koʻpkari, the mounted goat-carcass game that fills a winter Sunday. Lokay comes from the Tajik south, Axaltekin from Turkmenistan, and argʻumoq is the old literary word for a fine riding horse rather than a registered breed.
