description: Uzbek honorific / form of address. THREE OF THESE GO AFTER THE GIVEN NAME, not before it, and they are the ones an Uzbek actually uses every day: aka for an older man (Sardor aka), opa for an older woman (Dilnoza opa), and domla for a teacher or scholar (Rustam domla). Janob and xonim are the formal written Mr / Ms and do precede the name. Hoji marks someone who has made the pilgrimage; qori someone who has memorised the Quran; ustoz a master or mentor. The academic, medical and military titles at the end precede the name and are Russian-mediated.
locale: uz-latn
