description: Nationality / ethnonym (Uzbek). The first block is the peoples of Uzbekistan itself and several of them exist essentially nowhere else: the qoraqalpoq of the Aral basin, whose language is co-official in their republic; the buxoro yahudiysi, the Bukharan Jews, a Persian-speaking Jewish community of the Silk Road cities; the eroniy, Shia Persians resettled around Samarqand; the Central Asian arab of Qashqadaryo, who have kept the ethnonym for three centuries; the lyuli, Central Asian Roma; and the koreys, the Koryo-saram deported from the Soviet Far East in 1937 and now a settled Uzbek minority. Ethnonyms are written lower case in Uzbek.
