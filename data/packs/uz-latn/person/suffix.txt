description: Name / academic suffix (Uzbek). Uzbek has NO native Jr./Sr. convention — a son is told apart by his patronymic (Sardor Anvar oʻgʻli) rather than by a generational tag — so the first two are imported and sit oddly. The degree abbreviations are the Soviet-inherited ones still printed on Uzbek diplomas: f.f.n. is candidate of philological sciences, i.f.d. doctor of economic sciences, t.f.n. candidate of technical sciences.
