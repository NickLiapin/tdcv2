description: Hobby / interest (Uzbek). Several of these are national crafts with no one-word English equivalent: zardoʻzlik is gold embroidery, ganchkorlik is carved alabaster relief, kandakorlik is chased metalwork, doʻppi tikish is making the square skullcap every Uzbek man owns, and atlas toʻqish is weaving the ikat silk of Margʻilon. Kurash is the national jacket wrestling. Mahalla ishlari — neighbourhood-committee work — is a real and widespread use of free time in Uzbekistan and has no European counterpart.
locale: uz-latn
