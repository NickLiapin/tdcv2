description: Government body — the ministries, committees and agencies of UZBEKISTAN, named for one country deliberately, because Uzbek is the state language of a single state and these words simply are the names. Two have no European counterpart: the Mahalla va oilani qoʻllab-quvvatlash vazirligi is a ministry for the neighbourhood committees, which are a real tier of administration here, and hokimlik is the office of the hokim, the appointed governor of a province or district. A config needing a generic public body should use company/industry or work/department instead.
