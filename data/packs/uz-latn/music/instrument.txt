description: Musical instrument (Uzbek). THE FIRST BLOCK IS THE UZBEK ENSEMBLE and none of it translates: the dutor is a two-stringed long lute played in the home, the tanbur is its concert relative and carries the maqom melody, the gʻijjak is a spike fiddle, the doira is the frame drum whose rhythm names the dance, and karnay and surnay are the long trumpet and shawm that announce a wedding from the street and can be heard a kilometre away.
locale: uz-latn
