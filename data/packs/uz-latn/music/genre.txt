description: Music genre (Uzbek). SHASHMAQOM IS THE CLASSICAL TRADITION — six modal cycles of Bukhara and Samarqand, sung in Uzbek and Tajik, listed by UNESCO — and katta ashula is the open-air song of the Fergana valley, sung by two or three men holding a plate to the mouth to project the voice. Estrada is the post-Soviet pop mainstream and is what most contemporary Uzbek music is filed under.
