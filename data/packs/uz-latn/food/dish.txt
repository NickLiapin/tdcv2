description: Dish (Uzbek). PALOV IS THE CENTRE OF THE CUISINE and is counted in dozens of regional versions — Toshkent, Samarqand, Fargʻona and Buxoro each cook it differently — and toʻy oshi, the wedding pilaf, is cooked before dawn in a cauldron for hundreds of guests. Non is not a side dish: obi non and patir are baked on the wall of a tandir, stamped with a chekich, and never cut with a knife. Sumalak is made once a year, from sprouted wheat stirred overnight by the women of a mahalla, for Navroʻz.
locale: uz-latn
