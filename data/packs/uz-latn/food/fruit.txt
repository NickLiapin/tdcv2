description: Fruit (Uzbek). QOVUN — the melon — deserves its place at the top: Uzbekistan grows scores of named varieties, they are sold by variety rather than by weight, and a good one is a gift worth carrying across a border. Oʻrik (apricot) is the second national fruit and is dried into the pitted turshak that fills a winter dastarxan. Note that xurmo names BOTH the date and the persimmon, which is a genuine ambiguity in the language and not a mistake in this list.
locale: uz-latn
