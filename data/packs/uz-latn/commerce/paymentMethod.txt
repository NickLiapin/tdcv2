BOʻLIB TOʻLASH — paying in interest-free instalments arranged at the till — is the single most common way large purchases are made in Uzbekistan and has no direct European counterpart; nasiya is its older informal cousin, a running tab kept by a neighbourhood shopkeeper on trust.
