description: Unit of measure (Uzbek). SOTIX IS THE ONE A FOREIGN READER WILL NOT KNOW: it is one hundredth of a hectare, a hundred square metres, and it is the unit land is bought, sold and argued about in across Uzbekistan — a house plot is six sotix, not 600 square metres. Tanob is the older unit it replaced. Everything else is metric; Uzbekistan has never used imperial measures.
