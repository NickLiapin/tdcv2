description: Colour name (Uzbek). THE BLUE-GREEN BOUNDARY IS NOT WHERE ENGLISH PUTS IT. Koʻk covers both: koʻk osmon is the blue sky and koʻk choy is green tea, koʻkat is the green herbs on a table, and koʻkarish is what a plant does in spring. Yashil, the separate word for green, is a Persian loan and is the one used for a green traffic light or a green shirt, but koʻk is still what a speaker reaches for about growing things. Moviy and zangori are both blue and not interchangeable — moviy is the deeper sea blue, zangori the pale sky blue. Compound colour names are built with -rang, colour: jigarrang is liver-coloured, qaymoqrang the colour of clotted cream.
locale: uz-latn
