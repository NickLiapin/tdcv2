description: Company legal form / name element used in Uzbekistan. MChJ (masʼuliyati cheklangan jamiyat) is the limited company and by far the commonest; AJ is a joint-stock company; XK a private enterprise; DUK a state unitary enterprise; and YaTT a registered individual entrepreneur. TWO OF THESE HAVE NO EUROPEAN EQUIVALENT: QK, the qoʻshma korxona or joint venture with a foreign partner, is a distinct registered form dating from the 1990s, and OK, the oilaviy korxona or family enterprise, is a legal form in which the members must be relatives. The rest are the trade words a company name is built from.
locale: uz-latn
