description: Employment benefit (Javanese). THR — Tunjangan Hari Raya — is the compulsory religious-holiday bonus every Indonesian employer must pay before Idul Fitri, worth a month's salary, and it is the single most important item on this list to a Javanese employee; the whole country's spending calendar turns on it. UANG MAKAN and UANG TRANSPORT are separate daily allowances rather than parts of salary.
