description: Material a product is made of (Javanese). The natural block leads because it is what Javanese craft actually uses: JATI teak, PRING bamboo, ROTAN cane, LULANG rawhide (the material a wayang puppet is cut from), MENDHONG a sedge woven into mats, ENCENG GONDOK water hyacinth now woven into bags, and GERABAH fired earthenware. KAIN LURIK is the striped hand-woven cotton of Central Java and BATIK TULIS the hand-drawn cloth, as against BATIK CAP which is stamped and BATIK PRINT which is merely printed and is not batik at all — a distinction that decides the price by a factor of fifty.
