description: Film genre (Javanese). The horror block is disproportionate on purpose: Indonesian cinema's dominant commercial genre is horror built on Javanese folk belief, and KUNTILANAK, GENDERUWO, POCONG, SUNDEL BOLONG and BABI NGEPET are specific named beings rather than generic ghosts. A Javanese audience reads them as a genre the way an English one reads slasher or ghost story.
