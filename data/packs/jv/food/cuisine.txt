The first four are the archipelago's own regional traditions, and a Javanese distinguishes them as sharply as a European distinguishes French from Italian. WALANDA — Dutch — is on this list and would look wrong in any European locale: three and a half centuries of colonial kitchens left a whole naturalised Dutch-Javanese repertoire that is now ordinary Solo and Semarang food, not foreign food, and selat solo, bistik jawa, klappertaart, kroket and semur are eaten without anyone thinking of the Netherlands.
