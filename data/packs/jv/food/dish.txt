description: Dish (Javanese). Note SEGA, not nasi: Javanese calls cooked rice sega in ngoko and sekul in krama, and every rice dish in this list is built on it — sega liwet, sega kucing, sega megono, sega tiwul. SEGA KUCING is literally "cat rice", a fist-sized banana-leaf parcel sold from an angkringan cart, and SEGA TIWUL is made from dried cassava rather than rice at all, a famine food from the limestone south that is now sold as a delicacy. GUDHEG, TEMPE and SATE KLATHAK carry the retroflex dh and th that Indonesian does not have. Names of dishes are register-free — you order gudheg in ngoko or krama alike — but the verb you order it with is not.
