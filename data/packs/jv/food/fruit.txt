description: Fruit (Javanese). Three of these are among the sharpest single-word tests of Javanese against Indonesian, because the languages use unrelated roots for the commonest fruits of all: GEDHANG is a banana where Indonesian says pisang, PELEM is a mango where Indonesian says mangga, and KATES is a papaya where Indonesian says pepaya. A list that said pisang and mangga would be an Indonesian list in Javanese clothing. DUWET, KEPEL, GOWOK, MUNDU and CEREMAI are Javanese fruits with no English name; kepel was a palace fruit, planted in kraton gardens and eaten by court women because it perfumes the breath.
