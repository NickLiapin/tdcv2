description: Cooking ingredient (Javanese). SANTEN is coconut milk (Indonesian santan), LENGA is oil (minyak), GLEPUNG is flour (tepung), ENDHOG is an egg (telur), BANYU is water (air), URANG is a prawn (udang), IWAK covers both fish and any meat side dish, and PITIK is a chicken (ayam). Eight words, eight differences from Indonesian, in the most ordinary vocabulary a language has — which is the point of the list. TEMPE and ONCOM are fermented soy and peanut cakes; tempe is a Javanese invention and the word is Javanese.
