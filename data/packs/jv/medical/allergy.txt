description: Allergen (Javanese). The food block leads with URANG, YUYU and IWAK LAUT — prawn, crab and sea fish — because in coastal Java these are the commonest reported food allergies by a wide margin, well ahead of the peanut and dairy allergies that lead a European list. DEBU OMAH is house dust and SERBUK SARI pollen.
