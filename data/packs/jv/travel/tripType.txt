description: Purpose of a journey (Javanese). MUDIK — the annual return to one's home village for Idul Fitri — is the largest human migration on earth and is a trip type in Indonesia the way commuting is elsewhere; ZIARAH is pilgrimage to graves, especially those of the Wali Sanga, the nine saints credited with bringing Islam to Java, and is a mass tourism category in its own right. NYADRAN is the pre-Ramadan grave visit and TIRAKATAN an overnight vigil journey.
