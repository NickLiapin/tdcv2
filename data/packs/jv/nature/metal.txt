description: Metal (Javanese). WESI is iron, TEMBAGA copper, SALAKA silver, MAS gold, WAJA steel. Two entries are Javanese and untranslatable. WESI AJI is heirloom iron — the metal of a keris or a spear, believed to hold power, and valued for its history rather than its purity. PAMOR is the contrasting light metal folded into a keris blade to make its pattern, classically meteoric nickel from the Prambanan fall, and the word now means the pattern itself. SWASA is a gold-copper alloy used for court ornament and has no English name.
