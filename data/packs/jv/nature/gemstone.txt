description: Gemstone (Javanese). INTEN is a diamond and SESOTYA the krama word for a jewel in general. AKIK — agate and chalcedony generally — leads because Java has an enormous ring-stone culture: men wear them daily, they are graded and named by locality, and a boom in the 2010s made kalimaya opal, bacan chrysocolla and pancawarna household words. MUSTIKA is a stone believed to carry power, which is a category rather than a mineral and may be an agate, a pearl or something found inside an animal.
