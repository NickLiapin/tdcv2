description: Tree (Javanese). JATI is teak, the tree Java is built and carved from, and a teak plantation is the classic Javanese managed forest. RINGIN, the banyan, is sacred: one stands in the centre of every alun-alun and a pair stand in the south square at Yogyakarta, and it is not felled casually. PRING is bamboo, which Javanese treats as a tree and which supplies scaffolding, walls, cages, furniture and the flute. KLAPA is the coconut palm and GLUGU its trunk used as a beam.
