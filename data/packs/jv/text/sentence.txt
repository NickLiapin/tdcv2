description: Javanese filler sentences. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Javanese book at all — the site's language search for jv returns "No records found", verified against a Polish control query that returns books, so unlike fr/de/it/es/pt/pl these are not extracted from a public-domain corpus and were composed for this pack. Written in NGOKO throughout except two rows kept deliberately in KRAMA ("Panjenengan badhe tindak dhateng pundi?" and "Kula badhe kondur rumiyin") so that the register difference is visible inside the data and not only described in a header. Two grammar facts a config must not fight. Javanese verbs carry NO TENSE — "mangan" is eat, ate and will eat alike, and time is carried by a separate word: wis (already), lagi (in progress), arep (about to), durung (not yet), mau (earlier), mengko (later), sesuk (tomorrow), wingi (yesterday). And Javanese marks the PLURAL by reduplication rather than by a suffix, so bocah-bocah is children and wong-wong is people; a generator must never build a Javanese plural by appending letters.
locale: jv
