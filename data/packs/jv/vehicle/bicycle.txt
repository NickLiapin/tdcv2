description: Bicycle type (Javanese). PIT ONTHEL is the upright steel roadster left behind by the Dutch — the name is Javanese, from the sound or the action of pedalling — and it has an enormous nostalgic following in Java, with clubs, rallies and period costume. PIT KEBO is the heavy-framed carrier bicycle.
