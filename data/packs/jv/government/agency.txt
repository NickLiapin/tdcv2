description: Government body. Javanese is spoken natively in essentially one state, Indonesia, so the institutions named here are Indonesian ones — that is what the words mean, and there is no generic alternative to put instead. Recorded so the next reader is not guessing; if the Indonesia COUNTRY pack ever claims this address, the country pack should win. The village-level block is the one that matters most in Javanese daily life and is genuinely Javanese in vocabulary: KALURAHAN or DESA is the village, PADUKUHAN the hamlet, and RT and RW the neighbourhood associations that issue the letters you need before any other office will talk to you. Yogyakarta uses KALURAHAN and KAPANEWON where the rest of Indonesia uses Kelurahan and Kecamatan, a deliberate revival of the Javanese court terms.
