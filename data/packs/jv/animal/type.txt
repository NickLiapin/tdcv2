description: Kind of animal (Javanese). KEWAN is the general word. Note ASU for dog (Indonesian anjing), PITIK for chicken (ayam), WEDHUS for goat (kambing), JARAN for horse (kuda), IWAK for fish, KETHEK for monkey (monyet), ULA for snake (ular), KODHOK for frog (katak), YUYU for freshwater crab and URANG for prawn — the everyday animal vocabulary is almost entirely different from Indonesian. KANCIL, the mouse-deer, is the trickster hero of Javanese folktales and is the animal a Javanese child hears about first.
