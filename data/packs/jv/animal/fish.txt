description: Fish (Javanese). The freshwater block is what is farmed and eaten inland — LELE catfish, WADER a small river fish eaten whole, GURAMEH, TOMBRO carp, NILA, BANDENG milkfish from brackish ponds — and the sea block is what the north coast lands. IWAK is the general word and also means any meat side dish, so "iwak" on a menu is not necessarily fish.
