description: Horse type (Javanese). JARAN KEPANG is not a horse at all but the flat woven-bamboo horse ridden in the trance dance of the same name, and it is what most Javanese think of first on hearing the word — it is listed because a config drawing "horse" in a Javanese context will meet it. Kuda Sandel and Kuda Sumba are the archipelago's own small hardy breeds.
