description: Bird (Javanese). PERKUTUT, the zebra dove, heads the list for a reason: keeping one for its voice is a Javanese institution, and the proverb "curiga, kukila, wanita, wisma, turangga" — keris, bird, wife, house, horse — names the five things a Javanese man is traditionally meant to possess, with the BIRD second. A good perkutut sells for more than a motorcycle. GLATHIK is the Java sparrow, endemic to the island and now endangered largely because it was trapped as a cage bird. JALAK SUREN was traditionally kept as a house guard because it calls at strangers.
