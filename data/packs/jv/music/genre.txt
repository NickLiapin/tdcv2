description: Musical genre (Javanese). KARAWITAN is the whole art of gamelan music and GENDHING an individual composition in it; MACAPAT is sung classical verse (see book/genre.txt); LANGGAM JAWA is Javanese song in keroncong style and CAMPURSARI the hugely popular fusion of gamelan with keroboard and dangdut that dominates Javanese weddings; TEMBANG DOLANAN are children's play songs; GEROK and SINDHENAN are the choral and solo vocal styles that sit over a gendhing. JARANAN, KETOPRAK, LUDRUK and WAYANG KULIT are theatre forms with their own music and are listed because Javanese does not separate the music from the performance it belongs to.
