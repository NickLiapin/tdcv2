description: Trailing element of a Javanese place name, and the other half of the system described in address/cityPrefix.txt. Most are auspicious rather than descriptive: -REJO and -ARJO and -HARJO all mean prosperous, -MULYO noble, -SARI essence, -ASRI lovely, -MAKMUR abundant, -SEJAHTERA well-off, -RAHARJA safe and prosperous — so Sidomulyo, Purworejo, Sukoharjo and Wonosari are wishes made into addresses. -KERTO and -KERTA mean well-ordered (Purwokerto, Mojokerto, and Surakarta itself). The compass block — -LOR, -KIDUL, -WETAN, -KULON, -TENGAH — uses the Javanese compass words set out in geo/direction.txt and splits a settlement that has outgrown one name, which is why Gunungkidul and Jakarta Selatan are named on two different systems.
