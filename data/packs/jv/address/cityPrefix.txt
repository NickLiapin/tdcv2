description: Leading element of a Javanese place name — and Javanese toponyms are BUILT rather than inherited, from a small set of productive elements that combine almost freely with the suffixes in address/citySuffix.txt. The commonest are geography words: BANYU is water (Banyuwangi, Banyumas), KALI a river (Kaliurang, Kalibawang), WONO or WANA a forest (Wonosobo, Wonogiri, Wonosari), GIRI a mountain, TEGAL a dry field, SUMBER a spring, JATI teak, KARANG a yard or rock, GUNUNG a mountain, TANJUNG a headland. SIDO — "may it become" — and SUKO — joy — are wishes rather than places, and PURWO means beginning or east; these three generate an enormous number of village names because they can be prefixed to almost any auspicious noun. Combine with a suffix; on its own none of these names a real town, and real towns belong to the Indonesia country pack.
