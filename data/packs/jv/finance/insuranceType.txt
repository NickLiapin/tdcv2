BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan are Indonesia's compulsory national health and employment schemes and are what most Javanese actually hold; commercial cover sits on top of them. ASURANSI SYARIAH is takaful, structured to comply with Islamic law, and is a distinct product with its own regulator.
