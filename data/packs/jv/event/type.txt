description: Kind of gathering (Javanese) — and the first block has no European equivalent. SLAMETAN is the communal ritual meal that marks every passage of Javanese life; TINGKEBAN is the seventh-month pregnancy ceremony, BROKOHAN the birth one, TEDHAK SITEN the ceremony where a baby first touches the ground, SUNATAN a circumcision, MITONI another name for the seventh-month rite. RUWATAN is a cleansing performed to release a person from misfortune, usually with a wayang play. NYADRAN is the pre-Ramadan grave-cleaning visit and SEKATEN the week-long Yogyakarta and Solo fair around the Prophet's birthday. WETONAN is the 35-day birthday of date/pasaran.txt. Note SLAMETAN and KENDURI are the same event named by two words, the second Arabic-derived.
