description: Where an event is held (Javanese). PENDHAPA is the open pavilion of a Javanese house or public building and is the default venue for anything formal; LATAR is the front yard, and a wedding or a wayang performance in Java is very often held in the street outside the house under a hired tent (TARUB), with the road closed for two days.
