description: Player character archetype (Javanese). The first block draws on Javanese and wayang categories rather than on the European fantasy set: SATRIYA is the noble warrior hero, PANDHITA the ascetic sage, BEGAWAN a hermit-teacher, PUNAKAWAN the clown-servant who is also the wisest character on the screen, RAKSASA and BUTA are ogres, DHUKUN a shaman, EMPU a smith. Games sold in Indonesia mostly use the English class names, which is the second block.
