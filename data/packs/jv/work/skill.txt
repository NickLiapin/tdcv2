description: Workplace skill (Javanese). The soft-skill block at the front is Javanese and culturally loaded: TEPA SLIRA is measuring another person by yourself before you act, EMPAN PAPAN is judging what a particular place and moment allow, and both are named virtues a Javanese employer will say out loud about a good colleague. NGEMONG is looking after junior staff in the way one looks after a child. The technical block is Indonesian and English, because that is the language it is taught and advertised in.
locale: jv
