description: Seniority level (Javanese). The first block is the Javanese ladder of standing in a workplace, which is expressed with age and kinship words rather than with rank words — SEPUH is "elder" and does real work in an office, and being called MBAH or SESEPUH at work is a compliment about experience, not about age alone. The second block is the borrowed corporate ladder used in job advertisements.
