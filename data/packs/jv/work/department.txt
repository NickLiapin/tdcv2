description: Company department (Javanese). Corporate vocabulary in Java is Indonesian — a Javanese-speaking office still calls the personnel department Sumber Daya Manusia — so most of these are Indonesian terms used in Javanese sentences. Note the category is workplace/ and work/, never hr/: hr is the ISO code for Croatian and an address beginning hr. resolves to the Croatian pack instead of to a department list.
