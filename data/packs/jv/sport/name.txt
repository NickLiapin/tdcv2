description: Sport (Javanese). The traditional Javanese block at the end is play rather than athletics and has no European equivalent: EGRANG is stilt-walking, DHAKON the seed-sowing board game (congklak in Indonesian), BENTHIK a stick-and-peg batting game, ENGKLEK hopscotch, JAMURAN and CUBLAK-CUBLAK SUWENG singing circle games, and LAYANGAN kite-flying, which in Java is competitive and involves cutting the other kite's line. JARANAN — hobby-horse trance dance — is included with a caveat: Javanese classes it as a performance, not a sport, and it is here only because a config asking for "things people do in a field on a Sunday" will otherwise miss it.
locale: jv
