description: Company legal form. These are INDONESIA'S, since that is the jurisdiction a Javanese-language business is registered in: PT is a limited company, PT Tbk a listed one, CV a limited partnership, UD a sole trader, Firma a general partnership, Koperasi a cooperative, Yayasan a foundation, Perum and Persero state enterprises, and BUMDes a village-owned enterprise, which is a Javanese village institution as much as a corporate form. Javanese is spoken natively in essentially one state, Indonesia, so the institutions named here are Indonesian ones — that is what the words mean, and there is no generic alternative to put instead. Recorded so the next reader is not guessing; if the Indonesia COUNTRY pack ever claims this address, the country pack should win.
