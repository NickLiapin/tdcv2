description: Colour (Javanese). The core terms are ngoko and every one of them differs from Indonesian: ABANG red (merah), IRENG black (hitam), PUTIH white (same), KUNING yellow (same), IJO green (hijau), BIRU blue (same), WUNGU purple (ungu), KLAWU grey (abu-abu), JAMBON pink (merah muda). The KRAMA colours are separate words again — abrit for red, cemeng for black, pethak for white, jene for yellow, ijem for green — and cemeng is the word inside Giri Cemeng, the Javanese name for Montenegro. The last block is BATIK AND CLASSICAL vocabulary that has no everyday use and no Indonesian equivalent: SOGAN is the deep red-brown of Solo batik got from soga bark, WEDEL the indigo blue, GADHUNG a pale young-leaf green, WILIS a dark green, WULUNG the blue-black of an old keris blade or a mourning cloth, DADU a pale pink, and BANGKA the grey of dead ash. Core terms verified against the Javanese Wikipedia article on warna.
