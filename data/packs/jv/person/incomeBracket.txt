description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Javanese. Amounts are in RUPIAH (Rp), the currency of Indonesia, which is the only state where Javanese is spoken natively — the bracket boundaries are a country fact and a config comparing across locales must not read them as comparable to a euro or dollar band. YUTA is Javanese for million and MILIYAR for a thousand million; note Javanese and Indonesian both count in yuta/juta and miliar rather than in lakh or crore, despite the Sanskrit layer elsewhere in the vocabulary.
