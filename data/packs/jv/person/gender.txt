description: Gender label. Javanese marks it lexically, not grammatically — there is no gendered pronoun, no gendered agreement and no gendered surname anywhere in the language — so these words appear on a form and almost nowhere else. lanang and wadon are NGOKO; kakung and estri are the KRAMA pair and are what an official Javanese-language document or a wedding invitation uses. priya and wanita are the Indonesian-derived formal pair, common on modern paperwork.
