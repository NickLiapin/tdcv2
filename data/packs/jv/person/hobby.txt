description: Pastime (Javanese, ngoko). A block of these are specifically Javanese and have no one-word translation. NGOPI is not simply "drinking coffee" but sitting out at a warung half the night talking. MACAPAT is chanting classical verse in its eleven metres. NGGAMEL and NYINDHEN are playing gamelan and singing over it. NGURI-URI BUDAYA — literally "keeping the culture alive" — is a named leisure category in Java, not an abstraction. NGINGU PERKUTUT, keeping a turtledove for its song, is the second of the five things a Javanese man is proverbially meant to possess (curiga, kukila, wanita, wisma, turangga — keris, bird, wife, house, horse), which is why bird-keeping sits in the hobby list and not the pet list. TIRAKAT and LELAKU are ascetic practices — fasting, night vigils, pilgrimage to graves — undertaken as a discipline rather than as worship.
locale: jv
