description: THE SECOND-NAME SLOT — and for Javanese it is not a surname. JAVANESE HAS NO INHERITED FAMILY NAME. A very large share of Javanese carry ONE name and nothing else: Sukarno, Suharto, Sudirman, Sutrisno, Megawati. Where a person has two names, the second is a second GIVEN name chosen at birth, not something received from a parent — which is why this list deliberately OVERLAPS person/male/firstName.txt, and why Joko Widodo's children are not called Widodo. Consequences a config must not get wrong: grouping a Javanese dataset by 'last name' groups nothing, siblings need not share this field, a wife does not take her husband's, and the field should be EMPTY for a large minority of rows — a generator that always joins a first and a last name is modelling a population that does not exist. There is no person/female/lastName.txt: nothing here inflects for gender, and its absence is the design. Three blocks. The plain one (Widodo, Purwanto, Hartono) is the ordinary modern second name. The -O ending is the same [ɔ] spelling convention described under male given names. The long polysyllabic block at the end (Wignyosaputro, Prawirodirjo, Reksohadiprodjo, Tjokroaminoto, Mangkunegara) is PRIYAYI — the Javanese administrative and court gentry — whose names WERE handed down and often granted with an office rather than born into; Tjokroaminoto keeps its pre-1972 Dutch spelling because personal names were exempted from the spelling reform, which is why oe, dj, tj and j-for-y still appear on Javanese identity documents. Weights are rank-derived. NOT INCLUDED: the Chinese-Indonesian and Arab-Indonesian surnames carried by Javanese-speaking minorities, which are real but belong to a name system this list does not model.
