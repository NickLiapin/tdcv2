description: Javanese female given name. Weights are rank-derived, not registry counts. SRI heads the list because it is a Sanskrit honorific prefix ('radiant', the goddess of rice) that Javanese uses as a free-standing woman's name and as the first element of thousands of compound ones. The feminine markers are -i, -ni, -ti, -sih, -ah and -em. Two blocks are specifically Javanese: the -NINGSIH / -NINGRUM / -NINGTYAS compounds (Wahyuningsih, Kusumaningrum, Wahyuningtyas — 'ning' is the Javanese linker, and sih is love, rum fragrance, tyas the heart), and the -EM / -INEM block (Painem, Wagiyem, Ponikem, Legiyem, Tukiyem) which is the FEMALE half of the pasaran-birthday naming described in person/male/firstName.txt. Wayang names — Srikandi, Sembadra, Arimbi, Banowati, Drupadi, Larasati, Kunthi — are given from the shadow-puppet repertoire and carry the character's reputation with them. Javanese given names are NOT largely unisex: this list and the male one are almost disjoint, and the ending usually decides. Javanese has SPEECH LEVELS (unggah-ungguh): ngoko for equals and intimates, madya in between, and krama for a respected addressee, with a krama inggil vocabulary layer on top for the addressee's own body, acts and possessions. THIS PACK SHIPS NGOKO, the base register — it is the citation form in every Javanese dictionary, the form Javanese Wikipedia writes its running prose in, and the only register in which a general word list is register-neutral enough to be safe.
