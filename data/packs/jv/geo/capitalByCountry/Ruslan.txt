description: Ibukutha Ruslan — the capital of Ruslan, as Javanese names it. Note the Dutch-derived city names that follow the country names: Moskou for Moscow, Warschau for Warsaw, Wenen for Vienna, Kopenhagen for Copenhagen, Atena for Athens, Kairo for Cairo, Lisabon for Lisbon. Kutha is the Javanese word for a city, which is why Mexico City is Kutha Meksiko.
