description: Common Maltese nouns, SINGULAR AND WITHOUT THE DEFINITE ARTICLE. Two things a config cannot infer. (1) BROKEN PLURALS: Maltese does not form the plural by adding a suffix, it changes the vowels INSIDE the word — ktieb/kotba, tifel/tfal, raġel/rġiel, mara/nisa, dar/djar, belt/bliet, triq/toroq, ras/irjus, kelma/kliem — and there is no rule that derives the plural from the singular, so a pluralising config will be wrong on most of this list. Some nouns do take a sound plural (omm/ommijiet, xogħol/xogħlijiet), and a few are COLLECTIVES where the singular is derived from the plural instead (ġebel is stone, ġebla is one stone). (2) Maltese also keeps a DUAL, a third number between singular and plural, for body parts and time units: id/idejn, għajn/għajnejn, sena/sentejn, jum/jumejn. (3) The DEFINITE ARTICLE is not carried here and must not be glued on blindly: it assimilates to the first consonant — id-dar, it-triq, ix-xemx, is-sena, ir-raġel, in-nar, iż-żmien, iċ-ċavetta, iġ-ġebla, l-omm before a vowel, il- everywhere else.
locale: mt
