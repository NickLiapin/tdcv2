description: Common Maltese adverbs. MALTESE HAS NO ADVERBIAL SUFFIX — no equivalent of English -ly or Italian -mente — so most adverbs are PREPOSITIONAL PHRASES built on bi- plus the definite article plus a noun: bil-mod is 'with slowness', bir-reqqa 'with care', bil-ferħ 'with joy'. That is why so many values here are two words, and why an adverb cannot be derived from an adjective by suffixing anything.
