description: Common Maltese verbs, cited in the THIRD PERSON MASCULINE SINGULAR PERFECT, because MALTESE HAS NO INFINITIVE — there is no form corresponding to 'to write', and the dictionary headword is kiteb, literally 'he wrote'. Under each of these sits a consonantal ROOT of usually three letters, and the whole word family is made by threading vowels through it: from K-T-B come kiteb (he wrote), jikteb (he writes), ktieb (book), kitba (writing), miktub (written), kittieb (writer). That is Semitic morphology, and it is why a Maltese verb cannot be built by adding endings to a stem.
