description: Title before a name (Maltese). Two are Maltese habits a European list would miss: PERIT, the title of an architect and civil engineer, is used before the name exactly as Dr is, and DUN is the title of a secular priest — Malta's best-known poet is Dun Karm. Onor. marks a member of Parliament, and Avukat and Nutar are used as address titles rather than as job descriptions.
