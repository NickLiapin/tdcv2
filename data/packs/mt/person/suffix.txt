description: Post-nominal (Maltese usage). LL.D. is the one to know: the Maltese law degree was the Doctor of Laws, so a Maltese lawyer signs LL.D. and is addressed as Dr where an English lawyer would be Mr. B.E.&A. is the Bachelor of Engineering and Architecture behind the title Perit. The British-era honours at the tail are on older records; K.U.O.M. is the Maltese National Order of Merit.
