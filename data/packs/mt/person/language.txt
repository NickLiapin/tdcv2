IL-MALTI IS THE ONLY SEMITIC LANGUAGE WRITTEN IN THE LATIN ALPHABET AND THE ONLY SEMITIC LANGUAGE THAT IS AN OFFICIAL LANGUAGE OF THE EUROPEAN UNION. Its grammar is Arabic — a three-consonant root system, broken plurals, no infinitive, pronoun objects suffixed to the verb — while roughly half of its vocabulary is Sicilian, Italian and English, so a sentence can be Semitic in structure and Romance in words at the same time. It descends from the Siculo-Arabic of the ninth-century conquest, and it is the national language of a state that is also officially English-speaking: both Malti and Ingliż are official, and Maltese is the language of the courts and of Parliament.
