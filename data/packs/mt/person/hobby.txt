description: Pastime (Maltese). Il-kaċċa and l-insib — hunting and bird trapping — are on this list because they are genuinely widespread Maltese pastimes and a standing political argument with the EU, not because they are quaint; il-qdif, rowing, is the Grand Harbour regatta; and għawm and għadis, swimming and diving, are what an island of clear water does. Note the GĦ opening three of them.
