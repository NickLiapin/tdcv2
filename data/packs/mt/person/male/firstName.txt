description: Maltese male given names, commonest first. TWO LAYERS, and both are live: the Catholic saints' names in their English and Italian civil-register spellings (Joseph, Carmel, Emanuel, Salvatore) that dominate every generation born before about 1980, and the English names that took over afterwards (Kurt, Clint, Jake, Liam). The Maltese forms at the tail — Ġużeppi, Karmenu, Ċensu, Wenzu, Salvu — are what the same men are actually CALLED: the register says Joseph and the village says Ġużi, and both are correct. Carmel as a man's name will look wrong to a European reader and is not an error: it is Our Lady of Mount Carmel, and it is one of the commonest male names on the islands. Counts are the pack's default decay curve, not measured frequencies.
