description: Marital status (Maltese), both gendered forms where Maltese has them — għażeb is an unmarried man and għażba an unmarried woman, armel a widower and armla a widow, and no single form covers both. Two dates matter for any Maltese dataset: DIVORCE WAS ONLY INTRODUCED IN 2011, by referendum, Malta being the last state in the EU to allow it, so divorzjat cannot legitimately appear in a Maltese record dated before then; and same-sex marriage has been available since 2017.
