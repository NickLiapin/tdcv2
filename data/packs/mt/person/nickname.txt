description: Familiar short name (Maltese). Maltese hypocoristics are not the first syllable of the register name — they are worn-down Sicilian forms, so Ġużeppi becomes Żeppi or Peppi or Ġużi, Vincenzo becomes Ċensu, Lorenzo becomes Wenzu, and Salvatore becomes Salvu. A config that truncates a given name will not produce any of these. The English short names at the tail belong to the generation named in English.
