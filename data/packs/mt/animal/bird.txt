description: Birds (Maltese). Malta's bird names come out of its trapping and hunting tradition, which is why finches carry distinct native names — gardell the goldfinch, verdun the greenfinch, sponsun the chaffinch — where an English list would need two words. The merill, the blue rock thrush, is the national bird; the falkun is the bird the island gave its name to in English. Għasfur is the generic word for bird.
