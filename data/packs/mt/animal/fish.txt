description: Fish (Maltese). The lampuka, the dorado, is the fish of the Maltese year — it runs from late August to December and the lampuki season and its pie are a national fixture — and Maltese has one-word names for the Mediterranean catch where English needs two. These are market names as a fishmonger in Marsaxlokk writes them; the last three are the shellfish and cephalopods a Maltese fish stall sells beside the fish.
