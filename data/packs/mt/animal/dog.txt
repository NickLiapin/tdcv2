description: Dog breeds. Two are Maltese and keep their Maltese names: the Kelb tal-Fenek, the rabbit dog, is the national breed and the island's hunting hound, and the Kelb tal-But, the pocket dog, is the small white breed the rest of the world simply calls the Maltese. The remaining names are international and are not translated.
