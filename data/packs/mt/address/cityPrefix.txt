description: Leading element of a Maltese place name. Ħal and Ħaż are worn-down forms of raħal, village, and are written joined to the name (Ħal Lija, Ħaż-Żebbuġ, Ħ'Attard); Ta' and Tal- are the genitive 'of'; Marsa is Arabic for harbour and Bir for well, which is why so many Maltese toponyms open with them. Combine with a real toponym — a prefix plus a suffix does not by itself name a real place.
locale: mt
