description: The name part of a Maltese street, to follow a streetSuffix (Triq il-Kbira, Vjal l-Indipendenza). These values CARRY the definite article, and that is deliberate: a Maltese street name is a genitive phrase in which the article is obligatory — Triq ix-Xemx, never Triq Xemx — and its form is dictated by the first consonant of the name. Maltese fuses the definite article onto the following word and ASSIMILATES it to that word's first consonant: il- by default, but ix-Xemx, is-Sajf, id-Dar, it-Torri, in-Nar, ir-Repubblika, iz-Zija, ic-Cavetta (ic with a dot), ig-Gnien (ig with a dot), iz-Zebbug (z with a dot), and l- before a vowel. A config that glues a bare il- onto every value will be wrong on most of them. Saints, clergy and named people take NO article at all (Triq San Pawl, Triq Dun Karm). Real Maltese street names, not invented ones; the streets of one named locality belong in a country pack.
locale: mt
