description: Trailing qualifier of a Maltese place name (San Pawl il-Baħar, Wied il-Għajn, Ħal Għargħur ta' Fuq). Maltese does NOT build place names with a suffix in the Germanic sense: the name is a prefix plus a noun, and what trails is a second noun in the genitive or a direction word — so these values carry their own article. Combine with a real toponym; the pieces do not by themselves name a real place.
