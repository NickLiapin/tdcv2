description: Unit designator inside a Maltese address. Read this before parsing one: a Maltese house usually has a NAME rather than a number (Dar il-Ħolm, Triq il-Kbira, Ħal Lija), so a numeric house field is often empty. Flats carry English abbreviations by convention even in an otherwise Maltese address, because the property market runs in English.
