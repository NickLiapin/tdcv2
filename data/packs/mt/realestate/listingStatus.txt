description: Listing status (Maltese). TAĦT KONVENJU is the stage a foreign reader will not have: the konvenju is the binding promise-of-sale agreement signed months before the final deed, and a Maltese property spends most of its sale under it. SHELL and FINISHED are the other Maltese habit — property is routinely sold as a bare concrete shell for the buyer to finish, and the listing says so in English even in a Maltese advertisement.
