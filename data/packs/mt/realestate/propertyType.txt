Maisonette and penthouse are used untranslated and mean something specific in Malta — a maisonette is a self-contained house-level flat with its own street door — and a RAZZETT is a converted stone farmhouse, the most sought-after property in Gozo. Dar tal-karattru is a house of character, an old townhouse kept with its original stonework.
