description: Government body — MALTA's, named because Maltese is the language of one state and these are the names its speakers actually use. Identità is the identity and residency agency, Jobsplus the employment service, and the Kunsill Lokali is the ONLY elected body below national level, since Malta has no province or county tier. The identity-card and tax number formats these bodies issue live in the Malta country pack.
