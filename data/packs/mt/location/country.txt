description: Countries as MALTESE names them. This list is deliberately SHORTER than the 233 of the en pack: a country is here only where Maltese has an established name for it, because inventing a Maltese spelling for a state that Maltese writing has never had to name would be inventing data. Note the shape of the adaptations — /sh/ is written x (Bangladexx), /k/ before a front vowel is k not c (Marokk), and several names take the definite article in running text (l-Italja, ir-Renju Unit) which is stripped here as a list value.
locale: mt
