description: Mineral (Maltese). The first four are what Malta actually extracts — ĠEBLA TAL-FRANKA is the soft globigerina limestone the whole country is built from and the reason Maltese towns are the colour they are, ĠEBLA TAL-QAWWI the hard coralline limestone, tafal the blue clay, and sea salt is still panned on the Gozo coast. The rest are the international mineral names in Maltese spelling.
