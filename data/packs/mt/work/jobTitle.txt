description: Job title (Maltese), masculine singular — Maltese job titles agree with the holder, so an għalliema is a woman teacher and an infermiera a woman nurse, and a config that prints these unchanged beside a female given name is writing the masculine form. PERIT is the protected title of an architect and civil engineer and is used before the name like a doctorate. Several titles stay in English because that is what the Maltese labour market calls them (accountant, welder, chef).
