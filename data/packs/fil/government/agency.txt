description: Government body, in Filipino. THE COUNTRY IS THE PHILIPPINES — Filipino is official in one state and nowhere else, so naming its institutions is naming what the words mean. Note the administrative vocabulary itself, which is the language part: Kagawaran is a department, Kawanihan a bureau, Komisyon a commission, Tanggapan an office, and those four words classify the whole executive branch. Filipinos in practice say the English acronym — DepEd, DOH, DSWD, BIR — and the Filipino names below are what appears on the letterhead and in Filipino-language proclamations. Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino is the body that governs the language this pack is written in: created by the 1987 Constitution, it standardises Filipino orthography and vocabulary and issues the spelling rules the rest of these files follow.
