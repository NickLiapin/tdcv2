description: Mobile and internet carrier. Brand names, untranslated. Globe and Smart are the two that matter and between them cover almost the whole country; TM, TNT and Sun are their prepaid sub-brands, which exist as separate brands because the Philippine market is overwhelmingly PREPAID rather than contract — a Filipino buys load in small amounts from a sari-sari store rather than paying a monthly bill. DITO is the third network, licensed in 2019 to break the duopoly. The international carriers are here because Philippine records routinely contain them: several million Filipinos work abroad and keep a foreign number.
