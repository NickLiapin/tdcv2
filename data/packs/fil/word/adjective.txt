description: Common adjective. NEARLY ALL OF THEM BEGIN WITH ma-, WHICH IS THE POINT: Filipino builds an adjective by prefixing ma- to a noun root, so ganda (beauty) becomes maganda, lakas (strength) becomes malakas, and bilis (speed) becomes mabilis. The adjective is derived and the root is the noun. A handful — bago, luma, pangit, tamad — are basic and take no prefix, and they are the exceptions. There is no comparative or superlative ending: mas maganda is 'more beautiful' and pinakamaganda is 'most beautiful', both built with separate words, so nothing can be suffixed to make a comparison. Adjectives also link to their noun with -ng or na, giving magandang bahay but malaki na bahay is wrong and malaking bahay is right.
locale: fil
