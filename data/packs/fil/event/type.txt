description: Kind of event. FOUR OF THESE ARE PHILIPPINE INSTITUTIONS AND NOT TRANSLATABLE. The pista is the town's patronal fiesta: one day a year when a municipality shuts, cooks for anyone who walks in, and holds a procession — attendance is a social obligation, not an outing. The debut is the eighteenth birthday of a Filipina, staged with eighteen roses, eighteen candles and eighteen treasures, and it is a formal event with printed invitations. Simbang Gabi is the cycle of nine dawn masses before Christmas, begun at four in the morning; completing all nine is said to earn a wish. The lamay is the wake, which in the Philippines runs for several days and nights in the family's own house with food, cards and visitors sleeping over, so a Philippine funeral is a week-long event and not an afternoon. Binyag and kumpil, baptism and confirmation, are family events on the scale of a wedding.
