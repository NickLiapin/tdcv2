description: Symptom, in Filipino, in the words a patient uses at the counter. Note the ma- adjectives again: masakit ang ulo is literally 'the head is painful', so Filipino states a symptom as a property of the body part rather than as something the person has. Hilo covers dizziness and nausea together and a doctor has to ask which. Kabag is trapped wind, a complaint Filipinos report constantly and which English has no comfortable word for.
locale: fil
