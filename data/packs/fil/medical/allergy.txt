description: Allergen, in Filipino. Hipon and alimango lead because shellfish allergy is the one Filipinos most often report and seafood is eaten daily; peanut allergy, which dominates a North American list, is markedly less common here. Alikabok and amag — dust and mould — matter more in a hot wet climate than pollen does, and there is no hay-fever season because there is no pollen season.
