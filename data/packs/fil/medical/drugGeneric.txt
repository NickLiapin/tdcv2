description: Generic drug name. NOT TRANSLATED, AND THAT IS THE LAW: the Generics Act of 1988 requires every Philippine prescription to be written in the generic name, with any brand name only in parentheses beneath it, so the international non-proprietary name is what appears on a Philippine chart and on the pharmacist's label. Paracetamol rather than acetaminophen, because the Philippines follows the British-international name for that one. Anti-tuberculosis drugs are more prominent in Philippine dispensing than in a European list, and the state supplies them free.
