description: Kind of leave. THREE OF THESE ARE PHILIPPINE STATUTORY LEAVES WITH NO EUROPEAN COUNTERPART. Solo parent leave gives seven days a year to a parent raising a child alone, under the Solo Parents' Welfare Act. VAWC leave gives ten days to a woman who is a victim of violence, under Republic Act 9262, so she can attend to legal and medical matters. Special leave for women, the so-called gynaecological leave, gives up to two months of paid leave after surgery for a gynaecological disorder, under the Magna Carta of Women. Maternity leave was extended to 105 days in 2019, which is long by regional standards; paternity leave is seven days.
