description: Tail of a registered company name. Trading, Enterprises, Marketing and General Merchandise are the four that make a Philippine business name recognisable at a glance — a family firm registered with the DTI is far likelier to be 'Reyes Trading' or 'Santos General Merchandise' than to carry a Latin abbreviation. OPC is the One Person Corporation, a company form created by the Revised Corporation Code in 2019 that lets a single natural person incorporate; before it, a Philippine corporation needed five incorporators. Kooperatiba is a large sector here, with its own regulator and its own law. The English forms dominate because Philippine company registration is done in English.
