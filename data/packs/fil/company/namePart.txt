description: Word a Philippine company builds its name out of. These are Filipino words with the meanings companies actually reach for — light, dawn, strength, harvest, the sea, the hardwoods, the birds — and several are the real names of real firms, which is the point: Liwayway is a Manila food manufacturer, Haribon a conservation foundation, Sarimanok a broadcaster's emblem. Balangay is the plank-built boat the first Filipinos arrived in and the root of barangay, the unit of Philippine government; a company called Balangay is claiming both meanings at once. Bayanihan, the custom of a village carrying a whole house to a new site, is the most-used word in Philippine corporate naming and the hardest to translate: it is collective effort as an obligation rather than a virtue.
locale: fil
