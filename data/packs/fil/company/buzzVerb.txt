description: Corporate verb, in Filipino. Every one of these is an actor-focus form built with mag-, mang-, pag- or the -um- infix, which is the citation shape a Filipino verb is listed in. IT IS NOT AN INFINITIVE. Filipino has no infinitive and no subject-verb agreement; what the affix marks is which participant the sentence is about. Change magpalawak (to expand, actor in focus) to palawakin (to be expanded, thing in focus) and the same root now demands a different sentence shape. See word/verb.txt for the full worked example.
