description: Corporate noun, in Filipino. Two strata again: the Spanish-Latin abstract nouns (estratehiya, imprastraktura, integrasyon) which Filipino borrowed whole and which are what a written Filipino report uses, and the native pang-/ka- formations (pagbabago, pakikipag-ugnayan, kahusayan) coined for the same ideas. Note the shape of the native ones: pag- turns a verb into an action noun and ka- -an turns a root into an abstract quality, so bago (new) gives pagbabago (change) and husay (skill) gives kahusayan (excellence). That is how Filipino makes abstractions, and it is productive, not a fixed list.
locale: fil
