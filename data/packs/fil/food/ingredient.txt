description: Cooking ingredient, in Filipino. FIVE OF THESE ARE THE WHOLE FOUNDATION AND A EUROPEAN PANTRY HAS NONE OF THEM: suka (cane or coconut vinegar), toyo (soy sauce), patis (fish sauce), bagoong (fermented shrimp or fish paste) and gata (coconut milk). Adobo is suka plus toyo; sinigang is soured broth plus patis; kare-kare is unthinkable without bagoong on the side; and everything from Bicol is gata. Achuete, annatto seed, is a colouring rather than a flavouring and is what makes kare-kare orange. Tuyo, daing and tinapa are three different preservations of fish — sun-dried, split-and-dried, and smoked — and Filipino keeps them apart where English says only 'dried fish'.
