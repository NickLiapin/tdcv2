description: Fruit, in Filipino. Mangga heads it: the Philippine carabao mango is the country's best-known agricultural export and mango is what a Filipino means by fruit unless another is named. Kalamansi is the small green citrus used the way a Mediterranean cook uses lemon, squeezed into everything from soy sauce to tea, and it has no English name — 'calamansi' in English is simply the Filipino word. Balimbing, the starfruit, doubles as a political insult: it has many sides, so a politician who changes party is a balimbing. Sampalok, tamarind, is a souring agent before it is a fruit. Durian, marang and mangosteen are Mindanao fruits and Baguio strawberries are grown in the only part of the country cool enough for them.
locale: fil
