The list leans Asian and Middle Eastern rather than North Atlantic because that is where Filipinos fly: the Gulf carriers move the overseas Filipino workforce, and the Manila-Hong Kong, Manila-Singapore and Manila-Dubai corridors carry more Philippine traffic than any European route.
