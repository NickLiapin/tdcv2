THESE ARE FILIPINO WORDS, NOT PHILIPPINE STREETS: the actual streets of the country are named after the heroes of the revolution and after presidents, they repeat identically in town after town, and they live in philippines.geo.streetName. What belongs to the LANGUAGE is the other Philippine naming habit, which is real and enormous: subdivision developers name their streets after flowers, trees, gemstones, birds and Filipino virtue words, so a Philippine village is a themed grid of Sampaguita, Narra, Gumamela and Kalayaan. The four blocks below are those four themes. Several are reduplicated compounds (Ilang-Ilang, Ipil-Ipil, Waling-Waling) because reduplication is ordinary Filipino word formation, not emphasis.
