description: Thoroughfare type. THE ORDER FLIPS DEPENDING ON WHICH LANGUAGE YOU ARE IN, and a config has to choose: native Filipino puts the generic FIRST and links it to the name (Kalye Rizal, Daang Bakal, Liwasang Bonifacio), while Philippine street signage, which is in English, puts it LAST (Rizal Street, Ayala Avenue). Both orders are correct Philippine data and the English one is far commoner on paper, which is why the English types are here rather than translated away. Daan takes the linker and becomes Daang before a name; Kalye and Kalsada are Spanish loans and Lansangan is the native word for a public road. Eskinita is a lane too narrow for a car, and looban is the interior of a block reached only on foot — neither has an English equivalent, and both are ordinary Philippine addresses.
locale: fil
