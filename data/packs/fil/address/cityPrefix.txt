description: Leading element of a Philippine place name. Combine with a toponym; it does not by itself name a real place. Two systems are mixed because the country's naming is: the Spanish saint prefixes San, Santa and Santo, which the 1849 parish map left on hundreds of towns and which are abbreviated Sto./Sta. on every road sign, and the native Filipino qualifiers Bagong, Lumang, Malaking, Munting, Mataas na and the compass words Hilagang, Timog, Silangang, Kanlurang. The native qualifiers carry the linker -ng or na and cannot be used bare: it is Bagong Silang, never Bago Silang.
locale: fil
