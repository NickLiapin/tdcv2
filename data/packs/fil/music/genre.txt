description: Music genre, in Filipino. OPM stands for Original Pilipino Music and is a genre label rather than a description: it names Filipino-language popular song as a category, it is used on charts and in radio formats, and the P is the older spelling of Pilipino. KUNDIMAN is the classical Filipino art song, in three-four time, addressed to a beloved who is also the country — the form carried nationalist feeling through the Spanish and American periods and is studied as serious repertoire. HARANA is the serenade sung beneath a window, a courtship practice with its own etiquette, now largely gone. KULINTANG is the gong-row music of the Maranao and Maguindanao of Mindanao, an ensemble tradition with no European relation at all. RONDALLA is the Spanish-descended plucked-string ensemble of bandurria, laud and octavina, taught in Philippine schools. Balagtasan is a debate in verse, performed.
