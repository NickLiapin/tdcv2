description: Gemstone, in Filipino, mostly Spanish loans respelled. PERLAS deserves its place at the top rather than in the middle: the Philippines is the world's leading source of the golden South Sea pearl, grown in Palawan, and the largest natural pearl ever recorded — the Pearl of Lao Tzu, about six kilograms — was taken from a giant clam in Philippine waters. Kabibe and sigay, shell and cowrie, are worked as ornament here and are listed beside the stones because Filipino treats them as the same trade.
