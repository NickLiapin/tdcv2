description: Tree, in Filipino. NARRA IS THE NATIONAL TREE, a hardwood whose timber is now protected from cutting. MOLAVE, YAKAL, KAMAGONG, IPIL and the lauan group are the Philippine hardwoods that built the galleons and the churches; kamagong, Philippine ebony, is dense enough to sink in water and is the traditional wood for arnis sticks. BALETE is the strangler fig and carries folklore rather than timber: it is where the spirits are said to live, and Filipinos do not cut one down casually. KAWAYAN, bamboo, is on the list although it is a grass, because it is the country's most-used building material. ABAKA is likewise a banana relative rather than a tree, listed for the same reason: it produces Manila hemp. PILI is a Bicol nut tree with no relation to anything European.
