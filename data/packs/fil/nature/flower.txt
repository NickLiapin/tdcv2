description: Flower, in Filipino. SAMPAGUITA IS THE NATIONAL FLOWER, a small white jasmine strung into garlands and sold at church doors and traffic lights; its name comes from sumpa kita, 'I promise you'. WALING-WALING is the great orchid of Mindanao, once nearly collected to extinction and the subject of a law that briefly made it a second national flower. ILANG-ILANG gives the oil the perfume industry buys. DAMA DE NOCHE opens and releases its scent only after dark. The Philippine flower names are mostly Spanish loans — rosal, adelfa, camia, santan — because the ornamental garden arrived with the friars, while the reduplicated ones (ilang-ilang, waling-waling) are native.
