description: Kind of cycle. Padyak and trisikad are the same idea with two names — a sidecar bolted to a bicycle, pedalled for hire over short distances — and they are public transport in Philippine towns rather than recreation, which is why they head a list that otherwise holds sports bicycles. Padyak literally means 'to pedal hard'. E-bike, here, usually means a small electric tricycle rather than an assisted bicycle.
