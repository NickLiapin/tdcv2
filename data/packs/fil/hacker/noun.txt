description: Technical noun. TWO VOCABULARIES EXIST FOR THE SAME THINGS AND ONLY ONE IS SPOKEN. The Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino and the University of the Philippines have coined native terms — salansan for file, talaan for record, imbakan for storage, hudyat for signal, panuntunan for protocol — and they appear in Filipino-language computing textbooks and in government documents. In an actual Philippine office nobody says them; the borrowed forms below, respelled or unchanged, are what is said. Both are here because a pack that shipped only the coinages would be describing a language nobody writes code in, and one that shipped only the loans would hide that the coinages exist.
