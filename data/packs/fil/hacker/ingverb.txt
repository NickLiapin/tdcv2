description: Technical verb in the progressive. FILIPINO HAS NO -ING SUFFIX. The ongoing action is built two ways at once: the infix -in- goes inside the root, and the first consonant-and-vowel of the root is REDUPLICATED. So suri (examine) becomes sinusuri, 'is being examined' — s-in-u-suri, with -in- infixed and su- doubled. Reduplication here is grammar, not emphasis, exactly as it is in araw-araw (daily) and bahay-bahay (house to house). Notice also that these are all OBJECT-FOCUS forms: sinusuri says the thing is being examined and never says by whom, while nagsusuri would put the examiner in focus instead. English gets one -ing form; Filipino gets a different verb shape for each participant.
