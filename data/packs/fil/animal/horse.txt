description: Horse breed. The list is short because it honestly is: the Philippines is not a horse country and the animal that does the work in a Philippine field is the kalabaw, the water buffalo, not the horse. The kabayong Pilipino is a real recognised landrace, small and hardy, descended from the horses Spain brought in the sixteenth century; the Baguio horses that carry tourists around Wright Park are its best-known survivors. The rest are international breed names.
