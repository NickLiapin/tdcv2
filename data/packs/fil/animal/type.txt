description: Animal, general. Kalabaw, the water buffalo, is the national animal and the working animal of Philippine agriculture; the tamaraw is its wild cousin, found only on Mindoro and down to a few hundred individuals. Pawikan is the sea turtle and is protected by name in Philippine law. Musang, the palm civet, is the animal that eats and passes the coffee cherries sold as kape alamid. Note that Filipino does not mark number on the noun: aso is dog and dogs alike, and plurality is carried by the separate particle mga before the noun.
locale: fil
