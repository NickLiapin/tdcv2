description: Pastime, in Filipino. Videoke belongs at the top and is not a joke: singing to a machine is the default Philippine social activity, machines are rented for birthdays and sit permanently in bars, sari-sari stores and living rooms, and neighbourhood disputes about volume are a recognised category of barangay complaint. Sabong, cockfighting, is legal, licensed, televised and enormous. The street-game block is what Philippine children play and none of it has an English name: patintero is a running game across chalked lines, tumbang preso knocks over a tin with a slipper, piko is hopscotch, luksong tinik is a jumping game over stacked hands, sipa keeps a weighted washer in the air with the feet, holen is marbles, and sungka is the Philippine mancala, played on a carved wooden boat with cowrie shells.
locale: fil
