description: Nationality adjective, in Filipino. PILIPINO AND FILIPINO ARE BOTH HERE ON PURPOSE. In Filipino the person is a Pilipino or Pilipina and the country is Pilipinas, with a P, because the native alphabet had no F; the language and the modern national identity are spelled Filipino, with an F, restored in 1987 when the alphabet was expanded. So a Filipino speaks Filipino and is a Pilipino, and the two spellings are not interchangeable in careful writing. Most of the rest are Spanish adjectives respelled — Amerikano, Hapones, Tsino, Aleman, Pranses.
