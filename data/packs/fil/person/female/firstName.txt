description: Female given name, roughly by frequency. MARIA IS NOT ONE NAME BUT A SYSTEM. Formally a very large share of Filipinas are christened Maria plus a second name — Maria Cristina, Maria Teresa — and the compound is abbreviated Ma. on every form, so 'Ma. Luisa Santos' is an ordinary way to write a Philippine name and the Ma. is part of the legal name, not a title. The virtue block is Spanish and Catholic — Fe, Esperanza and Caridad are Faith, Hope and Charity and are real women's names, often on three sisters — as are Concepcion, Asuncion, Purificacion and Natividad, each naming a feast of the Virgin. Precious, Princess and Queenie are the other Philippine habit: an English abstract noun given as a legal first name, which reads as a nickname to a foreigner and is not one. The tail is the Tagalog revival: Ligaya, Liwayway, Luningning, Tala, Amihan, several of which — Kalayaan, Malaya, Dalisay, Hiraya, and Angel — are unisex and appear in the male list too, which is intended rather than a leak. The weights are a gentle decay lifted at the top for Maria; no published Philippine frequency table exists at this granularity, so the curve is a shape and not a measurement.
