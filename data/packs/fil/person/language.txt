description: Language, as Filipino names it. THE FIRST TWO ARE NOT THE SAME ENTRY AND THE DISTINCTION IS LEGAL. Filipino is the national language, declared by the 1987 Constitution and developed by the Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino; Tagalog is one of the country's languages, spoken natively around Manila and southern Luzon, and Filipino is its standardised register opened to borrowing from the others. A census asks a Filipino which language they speak at home and Tagalog, Sebwano, Ilokano and Hiligaynon are separate answers. THE COUNTRY HAS ABOUT 180 LIVING LANGUAGES and nineteen are recognised as regional auxiliary official languages, so 'the Philippine language' does not exist. Chavacano is the only Spanish-based creole in Asia, spoken in Zamboanga. The foreign names are Filipino respellings: Ingles, Espanyol, Hapones, Aleman, Pranses.
