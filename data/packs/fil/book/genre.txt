description: Book genre, in Filipino. Several of these are categories of Philippine literature with no English counterpart and are not translations of the English list: alamat is the origin-legend, the story of how the pineapple got its eyes; awit and korido are the metrical romances of the Spanish period, of which Florante at Laura is the monument; sanaysay is the essay as a recognised literary form taught in every Filipino class; dula is the stage play; balagtasan is the improvised poetic debate in verse, named after Francisco Balagtas, performed live and judged. Hugot, at the end, is the modern one: the genre of short pieces built around an ache, usually romantic, that gave Philippine publishing a whole shelf after 2014.
