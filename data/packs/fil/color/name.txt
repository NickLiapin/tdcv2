description: Colour name. FILIPINO'S NATIVE COLOUR VOCABULARY IS SMALL AND THE REST IS BORROWED, which is the fact a config cannot infer. The unquestionably native basic terms are about six — puti, itim, pula, dilaw, berde or luntian, and asul or bughaw — and beyond them Filipino reaches for Spanish (rosas, lila, kahel, biyoleta, karmesi) or builds a compound with kulay, 'colour of' (kulay-langit, kulay-dagat, kulay-lupa), the way English says sky-blue. Kayumanggi is the exception and matters: it names brown, and it is also the word Filipinos use for their own skin, appearing in patriotic writing as the colour of the race. Ube is the purple yam and, because of the yam, the colour purple — a colour term that started as a vegetable and is now on paint charts.
