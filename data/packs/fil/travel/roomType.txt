THE BASIC PHILIPPINE DISTINCTION IS FAN ROOM VERSUS AIRCON ROOM, and it is a price tier rather than a preference: a budget Philippine hotel or lodge lists both, the fan room costs a fraction of the other, and a booking record without that split is missing the commonest choice a Filipino traveller makes. Barkada room sleeps a group of friends — barkada is the Filipino word for one's regular circle, with no English single word — and is priced per head.
