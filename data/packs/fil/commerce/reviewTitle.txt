description: Headline of a customer review, in Filipino. Sulit is the one that carries the most weight and has no tidy English equivalent: it means the thing was worth what was paid for it, and 'sulit' alone is a complete and very common Philippine review. Legit and lodi are internet Filipino — lodi is idol spelled backwards, part of a productive slang system that reverses syllables.
locale: fil
