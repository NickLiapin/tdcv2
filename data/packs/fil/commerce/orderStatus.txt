description: Order status, in Filipino. Note the shape of the words: most are the ma-/na- affixed forms that mark a completed action on the thing rather than by an actor — naihatid is 'it has been delivered', with the delivery in focus and the courier unmentioned. Filipino chooses the affix by which participant the sentence is about, so an order-status vocabulary naturally comes out in the object focus while an English one comes out as a past participle.
