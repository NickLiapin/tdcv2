description: Shop department, in Filipino. Palengke and supermarket are both here and are not the same shop: the palengke is the wet market where meat, fish and vegetables are bought loose and haggled over, the supermarket is the air-conditioned one with barcodes, and most Philippine households use both in the same week. Ukay-ukay is the imported second-hand clothing trade, a retail category of its own with its own shops. Pasalubong is a department in real Philippine stores: the souvenir food you are obliged to bring home to everyone from a trip.
