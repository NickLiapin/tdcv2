description: Marketing adjective for a product. ALMOST EVERY ONE OF THESE IS BUILT WITH ma-, and that is the grammar point: Filipino makes adjectives by prefixing ma- to a noun root, so ganda (beauty) gives maganda (beautiful) and tibay (durability) gives matibay (durable). The adjective is derived, not basic. There is no comparative or superlative suffix either — 'more beautiful' is mas maganda and 'most beautiful' is pinakamaganda, both built with separate words, so a config cannot make a comparative by appending anything.
