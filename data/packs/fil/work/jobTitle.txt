description: Job title. THE PHILIPPINES EXPORTS LABOUR ON A SCALE NOTHING IN EUROPE MATCHES and this list is shaped by it: roughly two million Filipinos work abroad at any time, Filipino seafarers crew something like a quarter of the world's merchant fleet, and nursing, caregiving, seafaring and domestic work are studied and licensed here for employment somewhere else. KASAMBAHAY is a legally defined occupation with its own statute, the Batas Kasambahay of 2013, which set a minimum wage, social insurance and a written contract for household workers. YAYA, the child's nurse, is a Filipino word that has travelled with the workers who hold the job. The other shaping fact is the business-process industry: over a million Filipinos answer calls, process claims and do back-office work for foreign companies through the night. Titles are given in Filipino where the trade has a living Filipino name and in English where the job arrived already named, which is exactly how a Philippine CV reads.
