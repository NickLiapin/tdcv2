description: Nationality / demonym (Hungarian). A Hungarian demonym is ONE lowercase word that serves as both the adjective and the noun and never inflects for gender or number in that role: magyar means Hungarian, a Hungarian man, a Hungarian woman and Hungarian-made alike. There is no masculine/feminine pair to choose between, as there is in every neighbouring language.
locale: hu
