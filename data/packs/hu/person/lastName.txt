description: Hungarian family name, roughly frequency-ordered. Hungarian puts the FAMILY NAME FIRST — Nagy Peter is Mr Nagy, not Mr Peter — which puts it with Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and Chinese rather than with any of its European neighbours; a config that renders given-name-then-surname produces an order no Hungarian document uses. The surname does NOT inflect for gender, so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName here: a woman and her brother carry the identical string. A married woman may instead take the traditional -ne form built on her HUSBAND'S full name (Nagy Peter -> Nagy Peterne, "Mrs Peter Nagy"), a pattern with no European parallel; that form is derived from the husband's name, not from this list. Weights are a rank-decay curve, not measured shares: Hungarian surnames are gently spread, with Nagy at roughly 2.4 per cent of the population, so no single name dominates.
weighted: true
locale: hu
