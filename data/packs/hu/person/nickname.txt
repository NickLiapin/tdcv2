description: Common Hungarian hypocoristic / informal name (Istvan -> Pista, Erzsebet -> Erzsi, Katalin -> Kati). Hungarian builds these with the diminutive endings -i, -csi, -ka/-ke and -us, and the vowel of the ending harmonises with the name: KatiKA but ErzsiKE. Several are far from the full name they stand for — Pista from Istvan, Jancsi from Janos, Bandi from Andras.
locale: hu
