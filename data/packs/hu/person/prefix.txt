description: Hungarian honorific / form of address. The academic and generational ones (Dr., Prof., Ifj., Id., Ozv.) genuinely PRECEDE the name. The courtesy words at the end of the list do not: ur, holgy, asszony and urno FOLLOW the full name in Hungarian — Nagy Peter ur, Kovacs Anna asszony — so a config that puts them in front produces the wrong thing. Ifj. and id. (younger / elder) are how a Hungarian father and son of the same name are told apart, in place of Jr. and Sr.
