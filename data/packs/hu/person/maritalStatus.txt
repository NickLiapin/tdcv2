description: Marital status in Hungarian. This is the one corner of the language where the vocabulary itself is sexed even though the grammar is not: notlen (literally "wifeless") is said only of a man and hajadon only of a woman, and likewise nos of a man against ferjezett of a woman. Hazas and egyedulallo are the sex-neutral alternatives that official forms increasingly prefer.
