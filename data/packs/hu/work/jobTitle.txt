description: Hungarian job titles across sectors (tech, healthcare, trades, transport, business, service, public). Hungarian job titles have NO gendered forms: apolo, tanar, szinesz and igazgato name a woman and a man alike, and there is no feminine ending to add — unlike German, Polish or Czech, where a feminine title is expected. The few -no titles that do exist (tanarno, szineszno) are optional and increasingly avoided.
locale: hu
