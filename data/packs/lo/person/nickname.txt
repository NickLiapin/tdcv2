description: Lao nickname (ຊື່ຫຼິ້ນ). EVERY LAO PERSON HAS ONE and it is not a diminutive of the given name — it is a separate, usually one-syllable word given in infancy and used by family, friends, classmates and colleagues for life. It is what a Lao person is actually called day to day, while the given name is for documents. The stock is ordinary vocabulary: animals (ກົບ frog, ໝູ pig, ໜູ mouse, ນົກ bird, ປູ crab, ແມວ cat), colours (ດຳ black, ແດງ red, ຂາວ white), body and size (ນ້ອຍ small, ໃຫຍ່ big, ອ້ວນ fat), and weather and sky (ຝົນ rain, ຟ້າ sky, ດາວ star, ເດືອນ moon). Many are frankly unflattering and that is affectionate, not rude. A nickname does not correlate with gender, and it does not appear on any identity document, so a dataset must never treat it as a name field.
locale: lo
