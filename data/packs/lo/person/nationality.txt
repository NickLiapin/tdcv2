description: Nationality. LAO BUILDS NO SEPARATE ADJECTIVE: the country name IS the nationality, and a person of that country is ຄົນ 'person' plus the country — ຄົນລາວ, ຄົນຝຣັ່ງ, ຄົນຫວຽດນາມ. Nothing is inflected and there is no Lao equivalent of the France/French pair, so a config that expects to derive one form from the other has nothing to derive. The bare forms are given because that is what a Lao form prints in a nationality box. Every name here is the Lao Wikipedia spelling of that country, not a transliteration composed for this pack; where Lao writes ປະເທດ 'country of' in front, it has been dropped, since a form prints the bare name.
locale: lo
