description: Language, as Lao names it. The pattern is transparent: ພາສາ 'language' plus the people or country. ພາສາລາວ is the sole official language of Laos and is the first entry for that reason, but the country is genuinely multilingual — ພາສາມົ້ງ (Hmong), ພາສາຂະມຸ (Khmu) and several dozen more are first languages for roughly a third of the population, and ພາສາໄທ is understood by most lowland Lao because Thai television and the closeness of the two languages make it half-intelligible without study.
locale: lo
