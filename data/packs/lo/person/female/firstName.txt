description: Lao woman's given name. The given name is the name of address and the sort key in Lao (see person/lastName.txt), so this column, not the surname, is what a Lao salutation or directory uses. The female stock leans on flower, moon, light and gem words — ບົວ- 'lotus', ດວງ- / ເດືອນ 'moon', ແສງ- 'light', ຄຳ- 'gold', ມະລິ / ຈຳປາ jasmine and frangipani, ດາວ 'star', ແກ້ວ 'gem' — but this is a tendency, not a rule: ວົງເດືອນ, ສີສະຫວາດ, ຄຳຜຸຍ and ດວງໃຈ are all borne by men too, so the OVERLAP with person/male/firstName.txt is deliberate. A MARRIED WOMAN'S SURNAME: a Lao woman commonly takes her husband's family name, but she is not required to and many keep their own; either way she continues to be called by her GIVEN name, so a married-name model that changes how she is addressed is modelling a European convention Laos does not have. The names here are drawn from real Lao women in public life and sport; nothing was composed by analogy. THE COUNTS ARE A NOMINAL RANK CURVE: Laos publishes no given-name frequency table.
