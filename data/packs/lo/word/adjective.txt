---
description: Common adjective. In Lao an adjective follows its noun and works as a verb in its own right — ເຮືອນໃຫຍ່ is 'house big', and ເຮືອນໃຫຍ່ on its own is already the sentence 'the house is big'. There is no copula to insert.
locale: lo
