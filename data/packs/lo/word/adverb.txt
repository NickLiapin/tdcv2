description: Common adverb. Note that ໆ is not a letter but the Lao repetition mark: it doubles the word before it, so ໄວໆ is ໄວ ໄວ, 'quickly'. A tokeniser must not strip it and a character filter must not drop U+0EC6.
locale: lo
