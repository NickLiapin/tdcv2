description: Kind of event. ບຸນ is the Lao word for a merit-making festival and heads the calendar: ບຸນປີໃໝ່ (Lao New Year, April), ບຸນບັ້ງໄຟ (the rocket festival that calls the rains), ບຸນຊ່ວງເຮືອ (boat racing at the end of the rains), ບຸນທາດຫຼວງ (the national stupa festival). A ບາສີ is the string-tying ceremony held for a welcome, a departure, a wedding or a recovery — there is no European category for it.
locale: lo
