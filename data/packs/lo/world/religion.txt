THERAVADA BUDDHISM IS THE RELIGION OF ABOUT TWO THIRDS OF LAOS and the ordering here reflects that. ສາສະໜາຜີ — the veneration of ຜີ, the spirits of place, house and ancestor — is not a footnote and not a survival: it is practised by the highland peoples as their own religion and simultaneously by most lowland Lao Buddhists, so a person can honestly answer both, and a single-valued religion column misrepresents the country. The ບາສີ / ສູ່ຂວັນ ceremony that marks every Lao wedding, welcome and departure is a spirit rite, not a Buddhist one. Christianity is a small minority, mostly Protestant among Hmong and Khmu communities and Catholic in the towns along the Mekong.
