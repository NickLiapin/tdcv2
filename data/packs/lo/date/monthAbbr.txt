description: Abbreviated month. Lao abbreviates by taking the first letter of each of the first two syllables and putting a full stop after each — ມັງກອນ becomes ມ.ກ. and ພຶດສະພາ becomes ພ.ພ. Note that two pairs collide on their initials in careless writing (ມີນາ / ມິຖຸນາ and ມີນາ / ເມສາ), which is why Lao text often keeps a whole syllable instead: ມິ.ຖ. rather than ມ.ຖ. The trailing full stop is part of the abbreviation and should not be stripped.
locale: lo
