description: Country, as Lao names it, weighted by population in millions. THE NAMES ARE TRANSLATIONS, NOT TRANSLITERATIONS OF ENGLISH: Lao took most of them through French, which is why Germany is ເຢຍລະມັນ (Allemagne), the Netherlands is ໂຮນລັງ (Hollande), Spain is ແອັດສະປາຍ (Espagne) and Greece is ເກຣັກ (Grèce) — a pack that respelled the English forms would get every one of those wrong. In running Lao the name is normally preceded by ປະເທດ 'country of' (ປະເທດຝຣັ່ງ); the bare form is given here because that is what fills a country field. THIS LIST IS DELIBERATELY SHORTER THAN THE WORLD: it holds only the countries for which a Lao capital name and a Lao currency name could both be verified, because geo/capitalByCountry and geo/currencyByCountry are keyed on it and a key with no verified child would be a silent empty draw. See location/country.txt for the full 160-country list of Lao country names, and geo/currencyByCountry for exactly which currencies had to be left out and why.
