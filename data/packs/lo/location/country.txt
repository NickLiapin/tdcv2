description: Country name in Lao — the full list, as against the shorter population-weighted geo/country.txt. Every name is the Lao Wikipedia spelling; ປະເທດ 'country of', which Lao writes in front of a country name in running prose, has been stripped so the value fits a form field. MOST OF THESE CAME THROUGH FRENCH rather than English, because French was the administrative language of Laos until 1953 and Lao took its world geography from it: Germany is ເຢຍລະມັນ from Allemagne, the Netherlands ໂຮນລັງ from Hollande, Spain ແອັດສະປາຍ from Espagne, Sweden ຊູແອດ from Suède, Switzerland ສະວິດ from Suisse, New Zealand ນູແວນເຊລັງ from Nouvelle-Zélande and Egypt ເອຢິບ from Égypte. Respelling the English names would produce a list no Lao reader recognises.
