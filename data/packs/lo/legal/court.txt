THIS IS THE LAO JUDICIAL VOCABULARY AND LAOS IS THE ONLY COUNTRY THAT USES IT, since Lao has essentially one state — so unlike the Spanish or Portuguese packs there is no need to hedge about which country's judiciary is meant. The Lao system runs from a district people's court up through provincial and regional courts to the People's Supreme Court, with a separate military court; ສານປະຊາຊົນ, 'people's court', is the standard term at every level.
