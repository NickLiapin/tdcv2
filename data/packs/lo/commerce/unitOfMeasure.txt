description: Unit of measure. LAOS IS METRIC, but three Lao market units matter. ກີໂລ is what a market actually quotes; ຂີດ is a hundred grams and is how small quantities of meat and chilli are bought; ຈອກ, ບ່ວງ and ຖ້ວຍ are the cooking measures. ຕິບ, the sticky-rice basket, is a real unit of cooked rice.
locale: lo
