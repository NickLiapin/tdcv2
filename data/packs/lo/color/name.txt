description: Colour name. ສີ 'colour' prefixes every one of them and is not separable. TWO LAO/ENGLISH MISMATCHES MATTER HERE. Lao splits English 'blue' into two unrelated words — ສີຟ້າ is sky blue and ສີນ້ຳເງິນ is deep blue — and treats them as different colours, not shades, so a single 'blue' cell cannot round-trip. And Lao pink is ສີບົວ, 'lotus colour', not a modified red. Note also that ສີຄຳ and ສີເງິນ are the same words as gold and silver the metals.
locale: lo
