description: Kind of official document. THE ຫົວໜ່ວຍ ONE IS ບັດປະຈຳຕົວ, the Lao identity card, and the one with no foreign equivalent is ປຶ້ມສຳມະໂນຄົວ — the household book, held by the head of the household, listing every registered member, and required for school enrolment, land transactions and marriage. A Lao person's legal existence is anchored to a household, not only to a personal document. NO NUMBERS OR FORMATS ARE GIVEN HERE: the structure of a Lao ID number, passport number or vehicle registration is country data and belongs in a Laos country pack, which does not yet exist.
locale: lo
