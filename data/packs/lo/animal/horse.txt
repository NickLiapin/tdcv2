description: Horse. LAOS IS NOT A HORSE COUNTRY and this is the shortest list in the pack for that reason. The buffalo, not the horse, is the Lao draught animal; horses appear mainly as small hill ponies in the north, and a Lao speaker has no everyday breed vocabulary at all. Rather than transliterate a page of European studbook names that no Lao document contains, this file gives the descriptive terms Lao actually uses.
