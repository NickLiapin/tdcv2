description: Job title. THE SHAPE OF LAO EMPLOYMENT IS NOT THE SHAPE OF THIS LIST: agriculture is the occupation of most Lao adults, and much of that work is unwaged family farming that no job title describes, so a uniform draw from this file overstates the professional and urban share enormously. Note ພະນັກງານ, which prefixes a large family of titles and means roughly 'staff member of' — a Lao job title is more often 'staff of X' than a distinct noun. ຊ່າງ heads the skilled trades and ນັກ the professions and practitioners.
locale: lo
