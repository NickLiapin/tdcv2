description: The qualifying word that follows ຖະໜົນ or heads a Lao village name. TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE USING IT. First, many Lao urban roads are NUMBERED rather than named — route 13 is the country's spine and Vientiane has whole grids of T-numbered roads — so a purely lexical street name misses a large share of real Lao addresses. Second, the named ones are almost all TOPONYM COMPOUNDS built on a landscape head: ໂພນ 'hillock', ດອນ 'island or rise', ນາ 'paddy', ຫ້ວຍ 'stream', ໜອງ 'pond', ທົ່ງ 'plain', ຫາດ 'sandbank', ດົງ 'thicket', ວັງ 'deep pool', ຜາ 'cliff', ຖ້ຳ 'cave', ຕາດ 'waterfall', ປາກ 'river mouth' — plus a merit or prosperity word. Compose one from each half and you get a Lao-shaped name; the specific streets of Vientiane, Luang Prabang and Pakse are real places and belong in a country pack, not here.
locale: lo
