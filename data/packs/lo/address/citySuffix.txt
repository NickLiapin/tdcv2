description: The trailing qualifier of a Lao place name — the half that follows the landscape head in address/cityPrefix. These are ordinary Lao adjectives and merit words, so ໂພນ + ສະຫວ່າງ reads as 'bright hillock' and ໜອງ + ບົວ as 'lotus pond'. Lao writes the compound with no space, which is why a Lao place name is one token however many words it contains.
locale: lo
