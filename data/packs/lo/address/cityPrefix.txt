description: The leading element of a Lao place name. Lao toponyms are transparent compounds and this is the head: ວຽງ 'walled town' (as in ວຽງຈັນ), ຫຼວງ 'royal' (ຫຼວງພະບາງ), ຊຽງ 'town' in the northern Tai form (ຊຽງຂວາງ), ປາກ 'river mouth' (ປາກເຊ), ທ່າ 'landing place' (ທ່າແຂກ), ຫາດ 'sandbank' (ຫາດຊາຍຟອງ). Combine one with a qualifier from address/citySuffix; on its own it does not name a real place, and the actual towns and provinces of Laos belong in a country pack.
locale: lo
