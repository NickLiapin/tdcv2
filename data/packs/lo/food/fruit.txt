description: Fruit. NOTE THE ໝາກ PREFIX: nearly every Lao fruit name begins with ໝາກ 'fruit of', so ໝາກມ່ວງ is 'mango-fruit' and the bare ມ່ວງ is the tree. It is not separable and it is not an article — stripping it does not give a shorter form of the name, it gives a different word. This is one of the clearest everyday markers of Lao against Thai, which prefixes its fruit names with ma- or phon- instead (written in Thai letters, deliberately not quoted here so that this pack contains no Thai codepoint anywhere), and the ໝ in ໝາກ is the single Lao character U+0EDD that Thai does not have at all.
locale: lo
