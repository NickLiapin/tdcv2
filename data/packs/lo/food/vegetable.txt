description: Vegetable. ຜັກ 'leafy vegetable' and ໝາກ 'fruit-vegetable' are both live prefixes and the choice between them follows the plant, not the kitchen: a tomato is ໝາກເລັ່ນ because it is botanically a fruit, morning glory is ຜັກບົ້ງ because it is a leaf. ຜັກບົ້ງ, ໜໍ່ໄມ້ (bamboo shoot) and ໃບຍໍ are the everyday three that a Lao market always has and a European greengrocer never does.
