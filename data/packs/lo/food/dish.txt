STICKY RICE IS THE POINT: ເຂົ້າໜຽວ is not a side dish in Laos, it is the meal, eaten with the fingers from a woven ຕິບເຂົ້າ basket, and Laos eats more of it per head than any country on earth. ລາບ, the minced-meat salad, is the national dish and its name is a homophone of the word for good fortune, which is why it is served at every celebration. ປາແດກ, fermented fish, is the base seasoning of Lao cooking and is what distinguishes a Lao ຕຳໝາກຫຸ່ງ from the Thai som tam beside it on the same menu.
