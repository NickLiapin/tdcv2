---
description: Common Chinese words for slugs, usernames and tags. Handwritten, and it has to be: Chinese writes NO spaces between words, so a frequency list cannot be extracted from a corpus by splitting on whitespace — that would yield whole clauses that merely look like words. Segmenting Chinese needs a morphological analyser, which build-text-corpus.mjs does not have and does not pretend to, exactly as for Japanese. The sentence and paragraph files beside this one are NOT written by hand and are not shipped yet; Project Gutenberg does hold Chinese prose, so they are the corpus builder's job, not a hand-writer's.
locale: zh-cn
