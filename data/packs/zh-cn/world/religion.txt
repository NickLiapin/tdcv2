description: Religion (Chinese). China recognises five official religions — 佛教, 道教, 伊斯兰教, 天主教, 基督教 (Protestantism, counted separately from Catholicism) — and the largest actual category is 无宗教信仰. 民间信仰 (local folk religion, including 妈祖信仰) is practised far more widely than any of the five and is not officially a religion at all.
