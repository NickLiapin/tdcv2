description: Chinese nickname (小名 / 昵称). Three patterns account for nearly all of them: 老 or 小 in front of the family name (老王, 小李), 阿 in front of one character of the given name (阿强, 阿珍 — southern), and a doubled character (乐乐, 圆圆), which is what parents call small children.
locale: zh-cn
