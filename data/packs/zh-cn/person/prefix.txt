description: Chinese form of address. Chinese puts these AFTER the family name — 王先生, 李老师, 张医生 — which is the opposite of every European locale in this pack. The two that go in FRONT are 老 and 小 (老王, 小李), which mark relative age between people who know each other and are not titles at all.
locale: zh-cn
