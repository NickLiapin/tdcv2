description: The 56 officially recognised ethnic groups (民族) of China. This is a field on every household register and was printed on the identity card, so it is ordinary furniture of a Chinese record and has no counterpart in the European locales. 汉族 is about 91% of the population; the other 55 are the 少数民族. The list is unweighted on purpose — a config that wants the real mix should weight 汉族 itself rather than have the pack decide.
