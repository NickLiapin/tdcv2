description: Chinese address element. A Chinese address runs LARGEST to SMALLEST — province, city, district, street, building, room — the exact reverse of the European order, and it is written with no commas and no spaces: 北京市朝阳区建国路88号. There is no postal-code-then-city block either; the postcode goes on its own line. 路 街 大道 巷 are the street types; 号 is the building number and always FOLLOWS the number (88号, never 号88).
locale: zh-cn
