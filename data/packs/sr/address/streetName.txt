description: Street names that recur across Serbian towns (rulers and voivodes, writers, scientists, regions, rivers, trades, dates). Most are in the genitive or in a possessive-adjective form because that is how a Serbian street name is built — Улица Николе Тесле is "the street of Nikola Tesla", not "Nikola Tesla street" — so these are already inflected and must not be re-inflected by a config. Compose after a streetSuffix; streets unique to one city belong in a country pack.
