description: Leading element of a Serbian place name (Нови Сад, Стара Пазова, Велико Градиште). Serbian normally puts the qualifier FIRST and inflects it for the gender of the settlement noun, which is why the list comes in masculine/feminine/neuter triples — Нови Сад but Стара Пазова but Ново Милошево. Combine with a real toponym; it does not by itself name a real city.
locale: sr
