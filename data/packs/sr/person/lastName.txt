description: Serbian surname, ONE list for both sexes. Serbian surnames do NOT inflect for gender: a woman in the Петровић family is Ана Петровић exactly as her brother is Марко Петровић, and the same holds for the minority types — the Vojvodina -ов/-ев names (Попов, Тот) and the adjectival -ски names (Дунђерски) are identical for both. That is why there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack: its absence is the design, not an omission. The archaic -ка / -ева / -ова forms (Јовановићка, Јовановићева) are derived possessive adjectives used when a woman's surname stands alone in an oblique case, not a second nominative surname, so they are deliberately not here. What DOES change is declension: a man's surname declines (видео сам Петровића) while a woman's does not (видео сам Ану Петровић) — a fact about cases, not about the form in this list. Weights are REAL bearer counts, not the pack's default decay curve. They come from a Serbia surname incidence table whose totals run somewhat above the 2022 census (it puts the top ten at about 844.000 where the census reports over 700.000), so treat them as proportions rather than as headcounts. The proportions are the point: Serbian surnames are heavily concentrated in -ић patronymics, and Јовановић, Николић, Петровић and Марковић together are on the order of 6% of the country. On the pack's default rank-decay curve the top name would come out near 0,3%, which would misrepresent Serbia badly. 200 names rather than the Polish pack's 443: these are the ones with a real measured count behind them, and the brief says ship it shorter rather than pad.
weighted: true
locale: sr
