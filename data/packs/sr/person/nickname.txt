---
description: Common Serbian hypocoristic. Serbian shortens a name and then adds -а, -е or -и, which is why so many of these end in a vowel and why a man's nickname can look grammatically feminine — Аца from Александар, Мића from Милош, Пеђа from Предраг. Цеца, from Светлана, is the best-known of the female ones.
locale: sr
