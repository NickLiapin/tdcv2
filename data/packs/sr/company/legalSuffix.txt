description: Company legal form and name tail used in Serbia. The genuinely legal forms are the first block — д.о.о., а.д., о.д., к.д., ПР, задруга, ЈП and their spelled-out variants, as the Business Registers Agency records them. The rest are name tails that trade alongside the form (Група, Инжењеринг, Промет), because Serbian company names carry those far more often than they carry a second legal marker. Fewer strictly legal forms than the Polish list because Serbian company law simply has fewer.
