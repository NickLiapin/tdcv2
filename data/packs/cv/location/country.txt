description: Country and territory names in Chuvash, sorted in Chuvash alphabetical order. That order is not the Latin one and not the Russian one either: Chuvash inserts four extra letters into the Cyrillic sequence, ӑ directly after а, ӗ after ё, ҫ after с and ӳ after у, so Ҫурҫӗр Корея files under ҫ between Судан and Тайвань rather than anywhere a default collator would put it, and a config that re-sorts this list with a stock comparator will scramble it. The shapes are worth explaining. Chuvash builds most country names on the Russian ones and then applies its own endings, which is why the Russian -ия comes out as a bare -и throughout: Итали, Испани, Германи, Австрали, Румыни, Эфиопи. Раҫҫей for Russia is the important exception and the genuine Chuvash word, not Россия respelled. Native vocabulary does the compounding work where a name has grammar in it: утравӗсем is "its islands" and ends every archipelago on the list, тата is "and" and joins Антигуа тата Барбуда, Республики is the possessive that makes Тӗп Африка Республики, and the compass words give Кӑнтӑр Корея, Ҫурҫӗр Македони, Тухӑҫ Тимор and Анӑҫ Сахара. Раштав утравӗ for Christmas Island is a small pleasure: Раштав is the Chuvash word for Christmas, borrowed early and thoroughly naturalised. Being Turkic buys Chuvash nothing here, and that is worth saying plainly. Chuvash is the only surviving Oghur Turkic language, the sole living relative of the Bulgar of the Volga Bulgars, and it split from the rest of Turkic so early that it is mutually unintelligible with all of it — where Common Turkic has z and š, Chuvash has r and l, Turkish kız against Chuvash хӗр "girl", Turkish taş against Chuvash чул "stone" — so a Chuvash speaker gets no free ride from Turkish or Azerbaijani place names and the world map arrived through Russian instead. Where a small territory has no distinct attested Chuvash form, the Russian one stands unchanged, which accounts for Гибралтар, Монтсеррат, Питкэрн, Реюньон and most of the rest of the minor entries. That is not a gap being papered over: Chuvash-language geography writing follows Russian usage for territories nobody has had cause to name in Chuvash, and inventing forms for them would be inventing the language.
