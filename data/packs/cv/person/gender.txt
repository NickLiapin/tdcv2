Chuvash has no grammatical gender at all: one pronoun вӑл covers he, she and it, verbs and adjectives never agree for sex, and nothing in the language forces a speaker to say which one a person is. These two labels are therefore lexical nouns, арҫын "man" and хӗрарӑм "woman", literally "male person" and "female person", not a grammatical category the way Serbian Мушки / Женски is.
