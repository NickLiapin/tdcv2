description: Diagnosis for a male patient — the sex-specific conditions first, then the ordinary chronic disease of a middle-aged man in the Volga region. The wording is Russian, and deliberately so: medicine in the Chuvash Republic is conducted, coded and archived in Russian, a discharge summary from the Republican Clinical Hospital in Cheboksary is a Russian document, and the ICD-10 line under it is a Russian translation of an international standard. Chuvash-language medical vocabulary exists — the health column of a Chuvash newspaper manages perfectly well — but it is a register for talking to patients, not for writing a chart, and a list of Chuvash diagnosis names would be a fiction. What is regional here is the selection rather than the language: cardiovascular disease and its companions run heavy across the middle Volga, which is why hypertension, ischaemic heart disease, hyperlipidaemia and stroke crowd the second half of a list that in another country might be dominated by something else.
