description: Surname of the Chuvash Republic, and the headline fact is that it is RUSSIAN. Orthodox baptism from the eighteenth century onwards assigned Russian patronymic surnames wholesale across the middle Volga: a village was christened, the priest wrote the father's Christian name plus -ов or -ев into the register, and that string became the family's name for good. So Иванов, Петров, Васильев, Николаев, Фёдоров, Григорьев and Яковлев dominate the registers of Chuvashia today exactly as they dominate a Russian oblast's, and the Chuvash names — Нарспи, Сетнер, Илемпи, Атнер — survived on the other side of the person, as GIVEN names and in literature. This is the single most interesting thing about Chuvash onomastics, and it means a Chuvash surname list that looked Chuvash would be a forgery. What IS local is the mix. Chuvashia is unusually dense in the saint's-name patronymics that a mass baptism produces, so Ефимов, Никифоров, Тимофеев, Порфирьев, Илларионов, Спиридонов, Прокопьев, Терентьев, Гаврилов, Данилов, Игнатьев, Афанасьев and Емельянов sit far higher here than they would in a national table, while the occupational and nickname surnames that fill a Russian list — Кузнецов, Гончаров, Мельников — sit lower, because the republic did not acquire its names by trade. EVERY value in this file ends in -ов, -ёв, -ев, -ин or -ын, the masculine citation form, which is how a register writes it and how the pack needs it: the feminine full name is derived by appending а to a value from this list, Иванов to Иванова, and that rule is correct for exactly these endings and wrong for anything else. That is why there is no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack — the feminine set is this list plus -а, and holding it twice would only let the two drift apart. It is also why the indeclinable Chuvash literary surnames are deliberately absent: Айхи, Ухсай, Митта, Юхма and Ҫеҫпӗл are real and famous, but they do not inflect, a woman named Айхи is Айхи, and letting them into this list would make the generator produce a nonexistent Айхиа. THE WEIGHTS ARE AN ORDERING, NOT MEASURED SHARES. Russia publishes no surname frequency table for Chuvashia, so the ranks here encode observed commonness and nothing else. The curve is steep at the head, because the top handful genuinely are enormous in the republic, and flattens out along the tail, where the differences between one patronymic and the next stop meaning anything.
