Товаровед is the entry with no English equal — a merchandise specialist responsible for the quality, documentation and assortment of goods received, a post inherited from Soviet trade organisation and still staffed and still examined for. Продавец-консультант rather than plain продавец is what a chain store advertises, because the job description now includes advising, and the two titles carry different pay. All Russian: shop signage in Cheboksary is sometimes bilingual, but the employment contract behind the counter is not.
