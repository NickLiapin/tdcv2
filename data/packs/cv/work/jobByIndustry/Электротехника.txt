description: Jobs in electrical engineering, listed separately from machine building because in Cheboksary it genuinely is a separate industry with its own institutes and its own vocabulary. Low-voltage switchgear, relay protection and control cabinets are the local speciality, so the list carries posts that would look oddly narrow anywhere else — a relay-protection engineer, a winder of electrical machines, an assembler of distribution boards — and they are ordinary vacancies here. Метролог is on the list because a plant that ships tested equipment needs a calibration service by law. Russian throughout: the standards, the drawings and the vacancy texts are all Russian.
