description: Jobs in transport and warehousing. The M7 «Волга» federal highway runs the length of the republic between Moscow and Kazan and passes Cheboksary, which makes long-haul road freight a real local trade rather than a borrowed category; the Volga itself still carries bulk cargo in the open season, and the Kanash railway junction is a working depot town. Кладовщик is the storekeeper who signs for goods and is materially liable for them under a separate contract, which is why the post exists as its own title and not as a shade of "warehouse worker". Russian throughout — waybills, licences and dispatch are federal paperwork.
