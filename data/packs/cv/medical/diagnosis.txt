description: Diagnoses in Russian, which is not an oversight but the fact of the matter: medicine in the Chuvash Republic is conducted in Russian from end to end. Doctors train in Russian, the polyclinic card and the discharge summary are written in Russian, the diagnosis is coded against the Russian ICD-10 edition and the ministry reports in Russian — while the patient across the desk may well have described the illness in Chuvash, which is why medical/symptom.txt is the one file in this folder that is not. The caseload is the region's own and not a European average. Cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease dominate: the Volga federal district has among the highest stroke incidence in Europe and hypertension is the entry point to most of the rest. Alcohol-attributable disease is written out here as its own group — liver, pancreas, nerve, heart, withdrawal and Korsakov's syndrome — because it is a leading cause of premature male death in the region and a Russian chart names it plainly. Tuberculosis is an ordinary polyclinic entry, not an exotic one, since the whole adult population is screened annually by fluorography and phthisiology is a standalone specialty with its own dispensary. Three infections here are specifically local: tick-borne encephalitis and Lyme borreliosis from the oak and linden forests, and haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome — мышиная лихорадка — carried by bank voles, for which the middle Volga is the largest focus in the world. Endemic goitre reflects genuinely iodine-poor soils. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale and diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry.
