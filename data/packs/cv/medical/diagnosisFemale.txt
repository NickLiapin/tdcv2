Two of the names carry Russian medical history rather than the international eponym: Turner syndrome is синдром Шерешевского — Тернера here, because Nikolai Shereshevsky described the condition in Moscow in 1925, seven years before Henry Turner, and Russian textbooks have credited both ever since. The obstetric entries are the ones a district hospital sees most: gestational diabetes screening is universal in the Russian antenatal schedule, and preeclampsia and placental complications are what a Chuvash central district hospital transfers to the perinatal centre in Cheboksary.
