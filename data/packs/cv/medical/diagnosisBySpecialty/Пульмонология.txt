description: Diagnoses seen in pulmonology. Туберкулез легких appears on an ordinary outpatient list because tuberculosis in Russia is neither rare nor exotic: the whole adult population is invited to annual fluorography, and a positive film from a Chuvash district polyclinic goes first to a pulmonologist and then to the phthisiology dispensary, which holds the case thereafter. Chronic obstructive disease and chronic bronchitis carry the rest of the department, on a smoking prevalence among men that remains among the highest in Europe.
