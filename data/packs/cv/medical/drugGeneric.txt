description: Generic names as a pharmacy in Cheboksary prints them: the INN transliterated into Russian, which is how the Russian state medicines register writes it and how a prescription is legally required to be written. The formulary is Russia's rather than a European one, and four entries show it. Метамизол натрия is анальгин, withdrawn or restricted across much of the world for agranulocytosis and still one of the most-taken analgesics in Russian households. Дротаверин is но-шпа, the near-universal post-Soviet antispasmodic. Изониазид and рифампицин are ordinary stock rather than specialist items, because tuberculosis treatment is dispensed free through a standing dispensary network. Тиамина хлорид is here for the alcohol-related neuropathy and Korsakov cases that the region records in quantity, and левотироксин натрия for a population living on iodine-poor Volga soils.
