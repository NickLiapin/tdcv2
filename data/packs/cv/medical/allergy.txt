description: Allergens as a Chuvash Republic polyclinic records them, in Russian, because the allergy history goes onto a Russian form even when it was taken out loud in Chuvash. The seasonal calendar is the Middle Volga's rather than the Balkans': ragweed, which heads every Serbian allergy list, barely reaches this latitude, so the spring peak here is birch pollen and the late-summer one is wormwood and goosefoot. Hop dust is on the list for a reason no other Russian region can claim — Chuvashia grows the great majority of Russia's hops and has done for centuries, and hop-garden work is a real occupational sensitiser here. Bee and wasp stings and honey belong to the same rural picture, village beekeeping being ordinary. Novocaine appears where a Western list would not have it: procaine is still the routine local anaesthetic in Russian dentistry and minor surgery, so it is the drug a post-Soviet chart most often carries an allergy note against.
