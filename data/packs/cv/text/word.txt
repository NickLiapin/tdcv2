description: Filler words in Chuvash, the lorem-ipsum vocabulary of this pack, gathered by hand: the corpus builder at data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs cuts these lists out of public-domain books and it has no Chuvash title to cut, so the words were collected rather than extracted. The spread is deliberately ordinary — kin, the body, the house and the yard, food and cooking, farming, animals, the village and the town, water and weather, trade, the state and the school, abstractions, colours, adjectives, verbs, numbers and function words — so that a random draw reads as Chuvash and not as the glossary of one subject. Verbs stand in their bare stem, which in Chuvash is at once the dictionary headword and the second-person singular imperative: вула read, ҫыр write, кай go. The infinitive is that stem plus -ма or -ме, and vowel harmony alone decides which of the two you get: a back-vowel stem gives вулама, a front-vowel stem gives ӗҫлеме, and the same back-against-front split runs through every suffix a Chuvash word can take. The rhotacism that puts this language on its own branch of Turkic is visible in the list itself — ҫӗр "hundred" answers Turkish yüz, and чул "stone" answers taş. A word that carries two senses appears once and once only, and the list does not repeat it to make up a count: ҫӗр is the earth, the night and the hundred; ҫул is the road and the year; уйӑх is the moon and the month; кил is the home and the verb come; ӗҫ is the work and the verb drink; чӗлхе is the tongue and the language. The postpositions are listed as words in their own right, because Chuvash puts them behind the noun and has no prepositions at all. The settled Russian loans belong here too and are not a lapse — шкул, больница, магазин, аптека, вокзал, машина, автобус, телефон, банк, налог, министр, паспорт — because school, medicine, administration and commerce are conducted in Russian in the Chuvash Republic, where Russian is co-official, and the loan is what a speaker actually says.
