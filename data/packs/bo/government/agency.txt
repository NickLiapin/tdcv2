description: Kind of public body (Tibetan). THESE ARE CATEGORIES, NOT INSTITUTIONS, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. There is no Tibetan state and no Tibetan civil service; Tibetan speakers deal with Chinese, Indian, Nepalese and Bhutanese agencies. What a language pack can hold is the Tibetan WORDS for kinds of office - the education bureau, the health bureau, the tax office - and that is what this is. Named ministries belong in a country pack.
