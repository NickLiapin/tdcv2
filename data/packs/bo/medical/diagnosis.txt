description: Diagnosis (Tibetan), in the modern biomedical register a hospital record uses. TIBETAN HAS TWO MEDICINES AND TWO NOSOLOGIES, AND THIS FILE IS ONLY ONE OF THEM. Alongside biomedicine, gso-ba rig-pa (Sowa Rigpa) is a living clinical system whose diagnoses are disorders of the three nyes-pa - rlung (wind), mkhris-pa (bile) and bad-kan (phlegm) - and of chu-ser (serum). Those four are at the head of this list because a Tibetan clinic really does write them on a chart; the rest are biomedical entities under their Tibetan names. A dataset mixing the two systems in one column is mixing two different theories of what a disease is.
