description: Medicine (Tibetan). THIS IS THE TIBETAN PHARMACOPOEIA, NOT A LIST OF INTERNATIONAL GENERICS, and that is a deliberate refusal: paracetamol, amoxicillin and metformin have no Tibetan names - a Tibetan prescription writes them in Latin or transliterates them ad hoc with no agreed spelling - so a biomedical drug column for this locale should carry the international generic name unchanged rather than a Tibetan invention. What Tibetan does have is its own materia medica, and its formula names follow a rule a config cannot guess: CHIEF INGREDIENT PLUS THE NUMBER OF INGREDIENTS. Gur-gum 13 is the thirteen-ingredient saffron formula, A-ru 18 the eighteen-ingredient myrobalan one. The numerals are Tibetan digits (U+0F20-U+0F29).
