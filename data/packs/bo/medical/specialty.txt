---
description: Medical specialty (Tibetan). The productive pattern is <disease> + rig-pa, 'the science of', so snying-nad rig-pa is cardiology, literally 'the science of heart disease'. bod-sman, Tibetan medicine, is a specialty in its own right and staffs its own departments in hospitals across the Tibetan areas; it is not folk practice and belongs in this list beside the others.
locale: bo
