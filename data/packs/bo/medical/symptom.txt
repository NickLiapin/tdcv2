description: Symptom (Tibetan). THIS IS THE FILE WHERE THE HONORIFIC LEXICON BITES. Tibetan keeps a second vocabulary, zhe-sa, for anyone senior, and the body is where it is densest: mgo/dbu head, lag-pa/phyag hand, mig/spyan eye, rna-ba/snyan ear, kha/zhal mouth, snying/thugs heart, rkang-pa/zhabs foot, lus/sku body. A doctor writing a chart uses the ORDINARY word, which is what this list holds throughout; a doctor SPEAKING to a lama, an elder or a patient he wishes to honour must use the honorific one for the same body part, and 'dbu na-gi 'dug' and 'mgo na-gi 'dug' are the same headache in two registers. No config can pick between them from a gender or age column - it is a social relation, not an attribute of the patient - so the pack ships one register and says which.
locale: bo
