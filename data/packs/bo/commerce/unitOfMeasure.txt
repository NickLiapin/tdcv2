description: Unit of measure (Tibetan). THE TRADITIONAL UNITS ARE STILL IN USE AND THEY ARE NOT METRIC. bre is a volume measure of grain, khal is twenty bre, rgya-ma is the catty of about 500 g, srang a weight of about 37 g that was also the old silver currency unit, and zho and skar-ma are its tenth and hundredth. The body measures khru (cubit), mtho (span) and sor (finger width) are used for cloth and building. dpag-tshad is the yojana, a day's march, and turns up in religious and geographical texts rather than in a shop. The metric units at the end are the loans.
locale: bo
