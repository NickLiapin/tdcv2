---
description: Animal (Tibetan). Weighted to the high plateau, which is what these words are for: g.yag is the male yak and 'bri the female - Tibetan has no single word covering both, and calling a female yak a yak is the standard English mistake. mdzo is the yak-cattle hybrid, rkyang the wild ass and gtsod the chiru antelope; none of the three has an everyday English name.
locale: bo
