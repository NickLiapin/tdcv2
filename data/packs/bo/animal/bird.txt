description: Bird (Tibetan). khrung-khrung is the black-necked crane, the bird of the Tibetan plateau and of a famous poem attributed to the sixth Dalai Lama; bya-rgod, the vulture, is the bird of the sky burial and is not a bird of ill omen in Tibetan the way it is in English.
locale: bo
