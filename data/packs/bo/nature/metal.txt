description: Metal (Tibetan). The traditional metallurgical set, which is what Tibetan actually has words for. dngul-chu, literally 'silver water', is mercury, and it is a medicine in the Tibetan tradition rather than only a poison. Modern elements - aluminium, titanium, uranium - have no established Tibetan name and enter as loanwords; they are deliberately not listed.
