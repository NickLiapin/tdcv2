description: Flower and flowering plant (Tibetan). SHORT ON PURPOSE, and the reason is worth knowing: Tibetan botanical vocabulary comes almost entirely from the medical tradition gso-ba rig-pa, so a Tibetan plant name is usually a materia-medica entry - spang-rgyan is the gentian used against fever, bong-dkar the aconite, gur-gum the saffron. Ornamental-garden flower names of the European kind largely do not exist in Tibetan.
