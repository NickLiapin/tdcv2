description: Book format (Tibetan). THE TIBETAN BOOK IS NOT A CODEX. A dpe-cha is a stack of loose oblong leaves, printed or written on both sides, wrapped in cloth and pressed between two wooden boards - it has no spine, no binding and no page order enforced by anything but the reader's care, and it is still the format of every liturgical text in use. shing-par means the book was printed from carved wooden blocks, which is how Tibet printed for six hundred years. A config that assumes 'hardback or paperback' has no slot for either.
