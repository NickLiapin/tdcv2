description: Literary genre (Tibetan). THESE ARE TIBETAN GENRES, NOT TRANSLATED WESTERN ONES. rnam-thar is the life of a realised master and is a genre with its own conventions, not simply 'biography'; mgur is spiritual song; gter-ma is a text held to have been concealed and later rediscovered, which is a claim about the book's own history that no Western genre makes; sgrung is the epic, above all the Gesar epic, the longest poem on earth and still sung by bards. The modern genres at the end are twentieth-century imports.
locale: bo
