description: Month of the Gregorian year (Tibetan). Tibetan month names are ORDINAL, not proper nouns: zla-ba dang-po is literally 'first month'. That has a consequence a config cannot infer - the same twelve words also name the months of the TIBETAN LUNISOLAR CALENDAR, which does not line up with this one, so 'zla-ba dang-po' means January in a Gregorian date and Losar month in a traditional one. See DATE_LOCALE.json.
