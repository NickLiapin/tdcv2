description: Birth-year animal of the Tibetan calendar (lo-rtags). NOT the Greek zodiac: Tibetan reckons a birth sign from the YEAR, not the month, on a twelve-animal cycle that combines with five elements to make the sixty-year rab-byung cycle. Aries and Scorpio have no Tibetan equivalent and are deliberately absent.
