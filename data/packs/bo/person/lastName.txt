description: Tibetan house, estate and clan names. READ THIS BEFORE USING IT AS A SURNAME COLUMN: most Tibetans have NO family name at all. The ordinary personal name is two given-name elements and nothing else, and the second element is NOT a surname - it is not inherited, siblings need not share it, and a woman does not take her husband's. What is listed here is the minority pattern: the house names of the Lhasa aristocratic and estate families (Ngapo, Lhalu, Surkhang, Yuthok, Shatra, Phala, Tsarong, Doring), which prefix rather than follow the given name, plus the ancient clan names (Nanam, Dro, Gar, Chim, Nub, Ba, Chokro, Nyak) and the six proto-clans Se, Mu, Dong, Tong, Dra and Dru. A realistic Tibetan person record leaves the surname column EMPTY for most rows and draws from this list for a small minority. Deliberately UNWEIGHTED: no frequency data exists for Tibetan house names and a decay curve here would be invented precision. There is no person/female/lastName.txt because Tibetan house names do not inflect for gender - that absence is the design.
locale: bo
