description: Third-person pronoun (Tibetan). Tibetan pronouns do NOT distinguish he from she - khong and kho are selected by RESPECT, not by gender: khong is the honorific used of anyone one speaks of politely, kho / mo is the plain form, and mo is the only gendered one in the set. A config that maps a gender column onto a pronoun column will be wrong for this locale; it should map a politeness level instead.
locale: bo
