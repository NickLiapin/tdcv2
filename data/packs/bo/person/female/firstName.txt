Deliberately OVERLAPS person/male/firstName.txt: a large share of Tibetan given names are unisex, so Tsering, Tenzin, Tashi, Pema and the seven weekday names appear in both lists and that is not duplication. The unambiguously female elements are those built on -sgrol-ma (Dolma), -lha-mo, -mo, -skyid, -sgron, -mtsho, -dbyangs and -'dzoms; a name ending in -mo or -sgrol-ma is female, which is the only gender signal a Tibetan name carries. There is no feminine form of a family name - see person/lastName.txt. Weights are the project's default decay curve over a qualitative ranking, not a census; no name census exists for Tibetan.
