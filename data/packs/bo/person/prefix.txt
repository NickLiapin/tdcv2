description: Name title / prefix (Tibetan). These come from the HONORIFIC register (zhe-sa): sku-zhabs (Mr) and lcam / lcam-sku-zhabs (Mrs) are the modern civil titles, while dge-bshes, mkhan-po, rin-po-che, bla-ma, a-ni and dge-slong are religious ranks that outrank any civil title and are used in place of it, never stacked with it. lags is not a title but a respect particle appended to a name (bsTan-'dzin lags) and is listed here because that is the slot a config puts it in.
