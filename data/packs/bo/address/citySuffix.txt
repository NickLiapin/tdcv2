description: Second element of a Tibetan place name, and each one is a LANDFORM: -thang a plain, -sgang a ridge, -lung and -rong a valley, -mdo a river confluence, -mtsho a lake, -phug a cave, -brag a rock face. Tibetan toponymy describes terrain, which is why so many of these are geographical rather than commemorative. -gling ('isle', by extension 'place') and -dgon ('monastery') are the two commonest.
