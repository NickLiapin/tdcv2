description: Name element for a street (Tibetan). These are the WORDS Tibetan street names are made of - auspicious abstracts (bde-skyid happiness, zhi-bde peace, mthun-sgril unity), the sun and moon, the precious substances - to be composed before an address/streetSuffix value. Actual named streets (Barkhor, Lingkhor, Yuthok Lam) are Lhasa city data and belong in a country pack, not here.
locale: bo
