description: Kind of building (Tibetan). The suffix -khang, literally 'house', is FULLY PRODUCTIVE and is how Tibetan makes almost every building word: ja-khang teahouse, za-khang restaurant, sman-khang hospital, dpe-mdzod-khang library. A config can therefore compose new ones the same way. rdzong is a fort that is also a district seat, and gzhis-ka is a landed estate - neither has an English one-word equivalent.
locale: bo
