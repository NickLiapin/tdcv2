---
description: Academic or monastic degree (Tibetan). THE MONASTIC LADDER IS THE REAL TIBETAN DEGREE SYSTEM and it comes first: dge-bshes lha-rams-pa is the highest degree of the dGe-lugs monastic universities and takes about twenty years of debate; mkhan-po is its counterpart in the rNying-ma and Sa-skya schools. The modern university degrees at the end are recent, are studied in Chinese, Hindi, Nepali or English rather than in Tibetan, and their Tibetan names are translations.
locale: bo
