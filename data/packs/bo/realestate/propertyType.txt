description: Property type (Tibetan). rtswa-thang (pasture) and phyugs-ra (stock pen) are property types on the plateau, and for a pastoral household grazing land is the asset that matters - not a building. 'brog-gur, the black yak-hair tent, is a dwelling and belongs in a housing column.
