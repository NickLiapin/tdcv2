description: Vehicle (Tibetan). rta (horse) and g.yag (yak) are in this list on purpose: across the pastoral plateau they are transport, not livestock, and a Tibetan travel record that cannot record them is missing rows. Note that 'khor-lo 'wheel' is also the Dharma wheel, so a bare 'khor-lo in a religious text is not a vehicle.
locale: bo
