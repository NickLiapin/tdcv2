---
description: Fuel (Tibetan). lci-ba, dried yak dung, is listed as a fuel because on the treeless plateau it IS the household fuel - it heats and cooks for most pastoral households and is stacked on the walls of every nomad camp. Leaving it out to keep the list looking like a European one would misrepresent the place.
locale: bo
