description: Music genre (Tibetan). THESE ARE TIBETAN GENRES. nang-ma and stod-gzhas are the classical and dance repertoires of old Lhasa; lha-mo is Tibetan opera, sung outdoors over a whole day; dbyangs is the liturgical chant of the monasteries, notated in its own curving script; mgur is the spiritual song of a realised master; and Ge-sar sgrung is the sung epic. Rock and pop are at the end because they arrived last, not because they matter least to young listeners.
