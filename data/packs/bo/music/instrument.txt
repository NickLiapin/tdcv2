description: Musical instrument (Tibetan). dung-chen is the long telescopic horn of the monastery roof, sometimes four metres long; rkang-gling is a trumpet made from a human thighbone and is a ritual instrument, not a curiosity; sgra-snyan is the six-stringed Tibetan lute that accompanies almost all secular song.
