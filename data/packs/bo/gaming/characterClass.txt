description: Playable character class (Tibetan). Tibetan has no game-industry vocabulary of its own, so rather than invent one this list uses the REAL Tibetan words for the roles a game would need: sngags-pa the tantric practitioner, sgom-chen the hermit meditator, gter-ston the treasure revealer, mkha'-'gro the dakini, dpa'-bo the hero. They are the words a Tibetan speaker would reach for, which is a different thing from a translation of 'paladin'.
locale: bo
