GUTENBERG DOES HOLD A TIBETAN BOOK - the query l.bo returns ebook 67451, 'Minor Tibetan Texts: 1. The Song of the Eastern Snow-mountain' (Johan van Manen, Calcutta 1919), while l.tib and l.bod return nothing - so 'no Tibetan book at all' would be false. It is unusable for two measured reasons. IT IS NOT PROSE: its 16341 Tibetan codepoints sit in 1878 separate runs whose median length is TWO SYLLABLES, 76 per cent are one or two syllables and only two runs reach fifteen, because the Tibetan is a word-by-word glossary wrapped around a song inside an English scholarly edition; the song's own lines close with the verse mark U+0F0E NYIS SHAD (68 of them) against 17 ordinary shad in the entire book. AND ITS ORTHOGRAPHY IS CONTESTED IN THE SOURCE ITSELF: van Manen prints two manuscript recensions that disagree on spelling and records his informants' colloquial forms against the dictionaries, so a corpus built from it would emit 1919 variant spellings rather than standard written Tibetan. Hence these lines were written by hand. NOTE FOR ANY TOKENISER READING THIS FILE: a Tibetan word is one to four SYLLABLES, and the syllables are separated by U+0F0B TSHEG - so splitting these values on the tsheg gives you syllables, not words, and splitting them on whitespace gives you nothing at all, because there is no whitespace in Tibetan. Word boundaries are UNMARKED and can only be found with a dictionary. Every value in this file already carries its final tsheg, as a Tibetan dictionary headword does.
