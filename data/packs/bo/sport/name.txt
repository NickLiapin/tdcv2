---
description: Sport (Tibetan). The traditional plateau contests come first - archery, horse racing, wrestling, stone lifting - and dpyid-'gyed, the summer festival at which they are held, is the frame they belong to. Modern sports are named descriptively in Tibetan (rkang-rtsed spo-lo, 'foot-play ball') or not translated at all.
locale: bo
