description: Colour name (Tibetan). Two things a config cannot infer. Colour terms are built from a root plus -po / -pa (dkar-po white) or -kha / -khu (ljang-khu green), and the compound X-mdog 'the colour of X' is fully productive - gser-mdog gold-coloured, g.yu-mdog turquoise-coloured - so the Tibetan colour vocabulary is open by construction and this list is a sample, not an inventory. smug-po is the specific dark maroon of monastic robes and is a far more salient colour word in Tibetan than 'purple' is in English.
locale: bo
