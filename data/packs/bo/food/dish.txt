description: Prepared dish (Tibetan). HONORIFIC REGISTER: everything here is the ORDINARY word. Tibetan keeps a whole second vocabulary, zhe-sa, for speaking to or about someone senior, and food is where it bites hardest - kha-lag 'food' becomes zhal-lag, ja 'tea' becomes gsol-ja, za 'to eat' becomes bzhes or mchod. A menu in a monastery guest hall and the same menu in a roadside teahouse are lexically different documents. This pack ships the ordinary register throughout and does not mix the two, because a list with both in it is a register error no config can repair.
locale: bo
