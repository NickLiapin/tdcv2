description: Spice and seasoning (Tibetan). These names come from the Tibetan materia medica, where taste is a clinical category: gso-ba rig-pa sorts every substance by the six tastes ro-drug (sweet, sour, salty, bitter, hot, astringent) and by whether it warms or cools. na-le-sham is black pepper, pi-pi-ling long pepper, sug-smel cardamom.
locale: bo
