description: Vegetable (Tibetan). SHORT BY GEOGRAPHY. Much of the Tibetan plateau is above 4000 m and above the limit of vegetable cultivation, so the traditional diet is barley, dairy and meat and the native vegetable vocabulary is correspondingly thin - la-phug, the turnip-radish, is the one that grows everywhere. Most other vegetable names in modern Tibetan are Chinese or Indian loans, and pe-tshe (cabbage) is one of them.
locale: bo
