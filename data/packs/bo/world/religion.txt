description: Religion and religious school (Tibetan). Bon is the indigenous pre-Buddhist religion of Tibet and is still practised; it is not a sect of Buddhism and does not belong under one. The four Buddhist schools rNying-ma, bKa'-brgyud, Sa-skya and dGe-lugs plus Jo-nang and the non-sectarian Ris-med movement are the divisions that actually organise Tibetan religious life, which is why they sit in the same list as the world religions rather than under a sub-category.
locale: bo
