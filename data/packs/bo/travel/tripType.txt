description: Kind of journey (Tibetan). gnas-skor, pilgrimage to a holy place, is the journey type Tibetan culture is organised around and it is first here for that reason; skor-ra is walking a holy circuit, and 'brog-'grim is going out with the herds, which is a seasonal migration rather than a holiday.
