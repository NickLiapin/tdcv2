---
description: Computing noun (Tibetan). Tibetan DOES have a modern computing vocabulary, built by translators in Dharamsala and Lhasa from native roots rather than borrowed: glog-klad is a computer, literally 'electric brain'; dra-rgya the internet, 'net web'; mnyen-chas software, 'soft tool'; sra-chas hardware, 'hard tool'. The words are transparent in a way English's are not.
locale: bo
---
