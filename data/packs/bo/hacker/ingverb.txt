description: Computing verb in the progressive (Tibetan). Tibetan makes the progressive with the auxiliary bzhin-pa after the verb - phab-len byed-bzhin-pa, 'downloading' - so the English -ing has an exact structural counterpart here, unusually.
locale: bo
