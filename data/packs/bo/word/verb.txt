description: Common verb (Tibetan), cited in the present stem. TWO THINGS A CONFIG CANNOT INFER. (1) A TIBETAN VERB HAS UP TO FOUR STEMS - present, past, future and imperative - and they are not formed by adding an ending but by changing the letters of the root: byed / byas / bya / byos 'do', gtong / btang / gtang / thongs 'send', lta / bltas / blta / ltos 'look'. Any config that treats a verb as a single string and appends a tense marker will produce something no Tibetan wrote. (2) THE HONORIFIC LEXICON REPLACES THE VERB OUTRIGHT: za 'eat' becomes bzhes, 'gro 'go' becomes phebs, lab 'say' becomes gsung, sdod 'stay' becomes bzhugs, nyal 'sleep' becomes gzim, byed 'do' becomes mdzad. These are DIFFERENT WORDS, not politer forms of the same one, and which one is correct depends on who is being spoken to or about - a social relation the row does not contain. THIS FILE SHIPS THE ORDINARY REGISTER THROUGHOUT.
locale: bo
