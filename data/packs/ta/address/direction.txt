description: Directional qualifier used with a Tamil locality or street name. The four மாட வீதி values are specifically Tamil and are not a stylistic flourish: a Tamil temple town is laid out as concentric rectangles of streets around the sanctum, and வடக்கு / தெற்கு / கிழக்கு / மேற்கு மாட வீதி is the literal postal address of a large number of real houses in Madurai, Kumbakonam, Chidambaram and every other temple town. கீழ் and மேல் (lower and upper) also work as fixed halves of a settlement name rather than as slopes.
locale: ta
