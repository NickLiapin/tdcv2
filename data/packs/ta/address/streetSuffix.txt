description: Tamil thoroughfare type. Two things a config cannot infer. First, this element goes AFTER the name, not before it — காமராஜர் சாலை, அண்ணா நகர் மூன்றாம் தெரு — which is the reverse of the European pattern. Second, a Tamil address locates a building by NEIGHBOURHOOD at least as often as by street, which is why நகர், காலனி, குடியிருப்பு, தோட்டம் and பேட்டை (settlement and quarter units, not roads) belong in the same list as சாலை and தெரு. ஒழுங்கை is the Sri Lankan Tamil word for a lane and is what Jaffna addresses use where Tamil Nadu writes சந்து; a config generating Sri Lankan addresses should prefer it. மாட வீதி is the temple-circuit street (see address/direction.txt).
locale: ta
