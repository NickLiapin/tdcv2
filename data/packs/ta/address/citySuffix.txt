description: Trailing element of a Tamil place name. These are the real toponym endings of the Tamil country and they are nothing like the north Indian set — there is no -pur, no -abad, no -garh here. They are also regionally readable: -பட்டி and -பாளையம் cluster in the western Kongu districts, -குடி and -குறிச்சி in the deep south, -பாக்கம் and -வாக்கம் around Chennai and the old Tondaimandalam, -பட்டினம் and -துறை on the coast because both words mean a port, and -குளம் and -ஏரி wherever the landscape is tank-irrigated, which is most of it. The last group (-முனை, -களப்பு, -பாணம், -தீவு) is the Sri Lankan Tamil set. Combine with a real toponym; a generated combination does not name a real place.
