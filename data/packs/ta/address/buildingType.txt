description: Building type (Tamil). Several have no European equivalent worth translating to. ஓட்டு வீடு (tile-roofed) and ஓலைக் குடிசை (palm-thatch) are the housing categories an Indian census schedule really uses, and they are the answer for a large share of rural Tamil Nadu. A மெஸ் is the small Tamil eating house that serves one fixed meal a day — the word is an English loan that has meant something purely Tamil for a century, and a "military hotel" is the non-vegetarian kind. A மடம் is a monastic house attached to a temple, a சத்திரம் a free rest house for pilgrims, and a தொழுவம் the cattle shed that in a village is a building in its own right.
locale: ta
