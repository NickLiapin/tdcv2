description: Leading element of a Tamil place name. திரு is the one to notice: it means sacred or venerable and it prefixes an enormous number of Tamil temple towns — திருநெல்வேலி, திருச்சிராப்பள்ளி, திருவண்ணாமலை, திருச்செந்தூர், திருப்பூர், திருவாரூர் — so it is a live productive element and not decoration. Combine with a real toponym and a citySuffix; on its own this does not name a real place, and a generated combination must never be presented as one.
locale: ta
