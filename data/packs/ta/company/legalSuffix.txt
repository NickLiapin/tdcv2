description: Company legal or name suffix. These are the real legal forms of the four Tamil-speaking jurisdictions and they are written in Latin abbreviations on a company's own letterhead even in Tamil documents: Pvt Ltd and LLP in India, PLC and (Pvt) Ltd in Sri Lanka, Pte Ltd in Singapore, Sdn Bhd and Berhad in Malaysia. நிறுவனம், கழகம், சங்கம் and கூட்டுறவுச் சங்கம் are the Tamil words, and கூட்டுறவுச் சங்கம் matters because the cooperative is a very common business form in the Tamil countryside.
