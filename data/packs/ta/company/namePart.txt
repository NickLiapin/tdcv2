description: Evocative noun for a company name in Tamil (compose with a legalSuffix or an adjective). Tamil business naming leans on three wells that a European list has no counterpart for: the ancient dynasties and their emblems (சேரன், சோழன், பாண்டியன், புலிக்கொடி, வில், மீனம்), the temple and family deities that a founder names the firm after (முருகன், மீனாட்சி, லட்சுமி, விநாயகா, அய்யப்பா — an enormous share of real Tamil firm names begin this way), and the pure-Tamil coinages of the Dravidian era (தமிழ், முத்தமிழ், அறிவு, செம்மொழி, கலைஞர்). Combining any of these with a legal suffix produces a name that reads as genuinely Tamil rather than translated.
locale: ta
