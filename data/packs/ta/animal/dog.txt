description: Dog breed (Tamil). The first four are Tamil breeds and the reason this list is not a translation: the ராஜபாளையம் is a white sighthound from the town of that name and is on a Tamil Nadu postage stamp, the கன்னி and the செம்பியன் are its relatives, and the சிப்பிப்பாறை is a hunting dog of the southern hills. நாட்டு நாய் — the ordinary Indian pariah dog — is what most dogs actually are.
locale: ta
