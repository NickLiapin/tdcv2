description: Fish (Tamil). These are the fish of the Coromandel and Palk Bay markets under the names the fish market uses — வஞ்சரம் (seer fish), சங்கரா (red snapper), வவ்வால் (pomfret), நெத்திலி (anchovy), கெண்டை (carp), விறால் (snakehead) — not translations of an Atlantic list. கருவாடு, salted dried fish, is a preparation rather than a species and lives in food/ingredient.
locale: ta
