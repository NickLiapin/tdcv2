description: Bird (Tamil). மயில் the peacock heads the list because it is India's national bird and a constant in Tamil poetry and temple sculpture; வண்ணாத்திக் குருவி and நாரை are the herons of the paddy field; பனங்காடை and செங்கால் நாரை are the migratory birds of the Tamil wetlands. All are birds actually seen in the Tamil country rather than a translated northern-hemisphere set.
