Two of these are TRANSLATIONS rather than respellings and a config must not romanise them back: இலங்கை is Sri Lanka and is the ordinary Tamil word for the island, older than the English name and used unchanged in Tamil for two thousand years; மாலைத்தீவு is the Maldives and means literally "the garland of islands", from which the English name descends rather than the other way round. ஐக்கிய இராச்சியம் and ஐக்கிய அமெரிக்கா likewise contain no part of the English words — they mean "united kingdom" and "united America". Note also that Tamil renders Greece as கிரேக்கம் and Turkey as துருக்கி, both borrowed centuries ago through trade rather than through English. The five countries where Tamil is spoken at home in numbers — India, Sri Lanka, Singapore, Malaysia and Mauritius — are all present, and Fiji and Mauritius are carried despite tiny populations because they are where the nineteenth-century Tamil diaspora went.
