description: Compass direction (Tamil). Tamil builds the intercardinals by compounding the two cardinals with the northern one first, exactly as English does, and the sixteen-point names simply repeat the nearer cardinal (வட வடகிழக்கு = north-northeast). Worth knowing separately: the Tamil words for the directions are also the names of the classical LANDSCAPES of Sangam poetry only by coincidence — those are named for their terrain (குறிஞ்சி hill, முல்லை forest, மருதம் farmland, நெய்தல் coast, பாலை wasteland), not for compass bearings, and a config must not use one set for the other.
locale: ta
