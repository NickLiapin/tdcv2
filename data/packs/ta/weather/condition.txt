---
description: Weather condition (Tamil). வெயில் — bright hot sun — is the default Tamil weather word and is not the same as "sunny": it carries heat rather than pleasantness. தூறல் is the fine drizzle of the northeast monsoon and சாரல் the wind-blown spray of it, two words English collapses into one.
locale: ta
