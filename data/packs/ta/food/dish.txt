description: Everyday dish eaten across the Tamil-speaking world, named in Tamil — the pan-Tamil repertoire that is not tied to one region. Regional specialities live in food/dishByCuisine. Two things a reader from elsewhere in India should not assume. Tamil food is built on FERMENTED RICE-AND-LENTIL BATTER (இட்லி, தோசை, ஊத்தப்பம், ஆப்பம், இடியாப்பம்) rather than on wheat bread, and the batter is ground and left overnight, so these are not quick dishes. And the everyday meal is a sequence, not a plate: சாம்பார் then ரசம் then மோர், each poured over rice in that order, which is why they are separate entries rather than one "curry".
locale: ta
