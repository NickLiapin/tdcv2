description: Spice or herb (Tamil). மிளகு — black pepper — heads the list for a reason a config cannot infer: it is native to this coast, it is what the Roman and Arab traders came for, and Tamil cooking used it as the hot spice for centuries before மிளகாய், the chilli, arrived from the Americas with the Portuguese; the two words are near-identical and are not the same plant. கல்பாசி (black stone flower, a lichen) and மராட்டி மொக்கு are the two spices that define Chettinad cooking and have no place in a European kitchen or an English spice rack at all. பெருங்காயம் is asafoetida, used in almost every Tamil lentil dish and in no Western one.
locale: ta
