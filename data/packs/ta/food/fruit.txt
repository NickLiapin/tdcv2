description: Fruit (Tamil). The head of the list is what actually grows and is eaten in the Tamil country, not a translated European set: mango, banana and jackfruit are the முக்கனி, the "three fruits" named together as a set in Tamil literature since the Sangam period. நுங்கு and பனம்பழம் are the two stages of the palmyra fruit — the tender jelly seed eaten in summer and the ripe pulp — and the palmyra is Tamil Nadu's and northern Sri Lanka's state tree; neither has an English name in ordinary use.
locale: ta
