description: Vegetable (Tamil). Three things a translated list would miss. கீரை — leafy greens — is a category in its own right in Tamil cooking with a dozen separately named members (முளைக்கீரை, அரைக்கீரை, பசலைக்கீரை, முருங்கைக்கீரை, வெந்தயக்கீரை, பொன்னாங்கண்ணிக் கீரை), each with its own cooking method and its own place in the week, and lumping them as "spinach" loses the whole system. வாழைத்தண்டு and வாழைப்பூ, the banana stem and the banana flower, are ordinary vegetables here and are sold as such. And சின்ன வெங்காயம், the small shallot, is not interchangeable with the big onion in Tamil cooking — sambar and most குழம்பு call for the shallot specifically.
locale: ta
