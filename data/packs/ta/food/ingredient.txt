description: Cooking ingredient (Tamil). Two staples have no English kitchen equivalent worth translating to. புழுங்கல் அரிசி is parboiled rice — steamed in the husk before milling — and it is the Tamil household rice; பச்சரிசி, raw rice, is used for different dishes and the two are not interchangeable. நல்லெண்ணெய், cold-pressed sesame oil, is the traditional Tamil cooking fat and the word means literally "the good oil". வத்தல் and வடகம் are vegetables sun-dried in brine during the hot months to be fried through the rest of the year, a preserving practice with no Western counterpart. வெல்லம் (unrefined palm or cane jaggery) is the sweetener, not white sugar.
locale: ta
