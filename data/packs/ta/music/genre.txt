description: Music genre (Tamil). கர்நாடக சங்கீதம் — Carnatic music — is the classical system of the Tamil south and is not a regional variant of the Hindustani music of the north: it has its own repertoire, its own composers, a different concert form and a different set of ragas, and the December Chennai season is the largest classical music festival in the world. The forms below it (வர்ணம், கீர்த்தனை, ராகம் தானம் பல்லவி, தில்லானா) are Carnatic concert items, not genres of song. தேவாரம் and திருவாசகம் are the Saiva hymn corpus, sung in temples for over a thousand years. கானா is the urban working-class genre of north Chennai, ஒப்பாரி the improvised funeral lament, and வில்லுப்பாட்டு a narrative form performed to a bow-shaped instrument. திரையிசை — film music — is by a wide margin the largest category anyone actually listens to.
locale: ta
