description: Musical instrument (Tamil). The head of the list is the Carnatic and Tamil temple ensemble, and three entries deserve a note. நாதஸ்வரம் with தவில் is the outdoor temple-and-wedding pair, the loudest non-brass wind instrument in the world, and no Tamil wedding happens without it. பறை is the frame drum whose players were historically the most stigmatised of Tamil castes and which is now deliberately reclaimed as a performance instrument by young Tamils. And யாழ் is the ancient Tamil harp, described in Sangam literature, extinct as a playing tradition and being reconstructed — the city of யாழ்ப்பாணம், Jaffna, is named after it.
locale: ta
