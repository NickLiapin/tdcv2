description: Noun (Tamil). THE FACT A CONFIG CANNOT INFER: Tamil is agglutinative, and a noun in running text is almost never the bare form given here. Case, number and postposition stack as suffixes on one word — வீடு (house) → வீட்டில் (in the house) → வீடுகள் (houses) → வீடுகளில் (in the houses) → வீடுகளிலிருந்து (from the houses) — and there are eight cases plus a productive set of postpositions, so a single noun has dozens of surface forms. The stem often changes when a suffix attaches (வீடு → வீட்ட-, மரம் → மரத்த-, ஆறு → ஆற்ற-), which means a config CANNOT build a correct inflected form by concatenating a suffix to the value in this file. Use these as bare citation forms — for labels, tags, slugs and lists — and do not try to put them into a Tamil sentence without real morphology.
