description: Verb (Tamil), given in the ROOT form, which is how a Tamil dictionary cites a verb. Like the nouns, these are not usable as they stand in a sentence: a finite Tamil verb agglutinates tense, person, number and negation onto the root in one word — செய் (do) → செய்கிறேன் (I do) → செய்தான் (he did) → செய்யவில்லை (did not do) → செய்யப்படும் (will be done) — and Tamil verbs fall into seven conjugation classes whose tense markers differ, so the correct form cannot be built by appending a fixed suffix. Use these for labels and lists, not for generated prose.
