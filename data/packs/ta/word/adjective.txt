description: Adjective (Tamil). Note that Tamil adjectives do not inflect at all — they take no gender, number or case, and they are simply placed before the noun (பெரிய வீடு, பெரிய வீடுகள், பெரிய வீட்டில்). This is the opposite of Polish or Russian and means a config can concatenate an adjective to any noun without agreement work.
locale: ta
