description: Court or tribunal, named in Tamil. This is the WORDS a language uses for kinds of court. The hierarchy named is India's, because that is where most Tamil speakers are, and it is stated here so the next reader is not guessing: Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia have their own structures and those belong in their country packs. Two entries have no European counterpart at all — the லோக் அதாலத் is a statutory conciliation forum whose settlements are binding, and the கிராம நீதிமன்றம் is a mobile village court.
locale: ta
