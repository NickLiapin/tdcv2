description: Country and territory names in Tamil, in the standard forms used by Tamil media and Tamil Wikipedia. Several are Tamil WORDS rather than transliterations and a config must not romanise them back: இலங்கை is Sri Lanka, மாலைத்தீவு the Maldives, வங்காளதேசம் Bangladesh, எகிப்து Egypt, கிரேக்கம் Greece, துருக்கி Turkey, ஐக்கிய இராச்சியம் the United Kingdom and ஐக்கிய அமெரிக்கா the United States — the last two are ordinary phrases meaning "united kingdom" and "united America" and contain no part of the English name. Where the en reference list carries a country twice under two English names (Côte d'Ivoire / Ivory Coast, East Timor / Timor-Leste, Holy See / Vatican City) the entries are merged into one Tamil name each. திபெத் is carried over from the en list and is not a sovereign state.
locale: ta
