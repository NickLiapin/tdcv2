description: Human-readable colour name (Tamil). Classical Tamil built its colour vocabulary on five roots — செம்மை red, கருமை black, வெண்மை white, பசுமை green and பொன்மை gold — and the modern words are still visibly built from them, so a Tamil colour name often tells you its family in a way an English one does not. Note also that பச்சை means green AND raw or unripe, and பசலை is the pale green of new leaves; neither maps cleanly onto one English word. காவி is the ochre of ascetics' robes and is a colour word with religious and political weight, not a neutral one.
locale: ta
