description: Official identity, civil-registration and revenue document, named in Tamil. ஆதார் and பான் are the two documents almost every Indian record keys on; the ration card is the household document that a very large number of Tamil families use as proof of address. பட்டா and சிட்டா are the land-title and land-extract documents of the Tamil Nadu revenue system and have no equivalent outside it. The list names kinds of document, not one country's issuing formats — the actual number layouts belong in the country packs.
locale: ta
