description: Government body, regulator or public office named in Tamil. Every entry is a real, currently existing body. The list is deliberately GENERIC-plus-Indian rather than one country's: Tamil is an official language of four jurisdictions, so வட்டாட்சியர் அலுவலகம், மாவட்ட ஆட்சியர் அலுவலகம், ஊராட்சி and நகராட்சி (the revenue and local-government tiers) are the offices a Tamil citizen deals with, and the named national bodies are India's because that is where most Tamil speakers live. Sri Lankan, Singaporean and Malaysian agencies belong in those country packs.
