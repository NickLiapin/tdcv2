description: Garment type (Tamil). The first block is Tamil dress and has no European equivalent worth translating to: the வேட்டி is the men's unstitched lower cloth (a முண்டு in Kerala Tamil, a சாரம் in Sri Lanka), the சேலை is the six-yard drape and the நவ்வாரி the nine-yard one worn differently, the தாவணி is the half-saree a girl wears between childhood and adulthood, and the துண்டு is the shoulder cloth that completes a man's formal dress. The rest is the everyday international wardrobe, which is what most people are actually wearing.
locale: ta
