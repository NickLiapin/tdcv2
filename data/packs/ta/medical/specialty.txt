description: Medical specialty in Tamil. Five entries at the tail are the AYUSH systems, and they are not folklore: சித்த மருத்துவம் is the Tamil traditional medical system, it has government colleges awarding a five-and-a-half-year degree, a state department, its own hospitals and its own pharmacopoeia in Tamil, and a Tamil patient may well see a Siddha physician and an allopathic one for the same complaint. A dataset of Tamil health providers that offers only the biomedical specialties will be wrong about a real share of them.
locale: ta
