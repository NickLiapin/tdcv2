Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale / diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. TWO THINGS A CONFIG CANNOT INFER. First, on the language: medicine in Tamil Nadu, Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia is taught, practised and RECORDED IN ENGLISH, so a real hospital chart says "Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus", not வகை 2 நீரிழிவு. The Tamil terms in this list are the ones used in public-health material, patient leaflets, the state health department's Tamil communications and the school and Siddha-college textbooks — real, standard, in daily use, and NOT what a discharge summary looks like. A config generating synthetic hospital records for a Tamil hospital should probably use the English list and reach for this one for patient-facing text. Second, on the contents: the tail of this list carries the infections that a Tamil clinic actually sees and a European list does not — டெங்கு காய்ச்சல், சிக்குன்குனியா, டைபாய்டு காய்ச்சல், எலிக்காய்ச்சல் (leptospirosis, which follows every northeast-monsoon flood), காசநோய் and மலேரியா are ordinary diagnoses here, not tropical curiosities.
