description: Common Tamil content words, for slugs, usernames, tags and short filler. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Tamil book at all — the language search returns no records — so this list was written by hand rather than mined from a corpus by data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs the way the en, pl and ru filler is. That is worth knowing when comparing locales: some have a real corpus behind their filler and this one does not. These are in WRITTEN Tamil (செந்தமிழ்), the literary standard, and that choice matters more in Tamil than in most languages: Tamil diglossia is extreme, and written and spoken Tamil differ enough in verb endings, pronouns and vocabulary to work as two nearly separate registers. Nobody speaks the sentences in this file, and nobody writes the way they speak — a Tamil novel narrates in written Tamil and puts spoken Tamil (பேச்சுத்தமிழ்) inside quotation marks. So this filler reads correctly as prose, as a document, as an article; a config generating chat messages, subtitles or dialogue with it will produce something no Tamil speaker would ever say. Two grammatical notes. Nouns are given in the bare citation form and Tamil is agglutinative, so a noun in a real sentence carries case and number as stacked suffixes and its stem often changes (வீடு → வீட்டில், மரம் → மரத்தில்) — see word/noun.txt. Verbs are given as the verbal noun in -தல் / -த்தல், the form a Tamil dictionary and a Tamil headline both use.
locale: ta
