description: Tree (Tamil). ஆல் and அரசு (banyan and peepal) are the temple trees planted at village boundaries; பனை, the palmyra, is Tamil Nadu's state tree and northern Sri Lanka's economic backbone, giving sap, sugar, fruit, timber, thatch and the ஓலை leaf that Tamil literature was written on for two thousand years; வேம்பு, the neem, is planted at almost every Tamil house. கடம்பு and புன்னை are the sacred trees of specific temples.
locale: ta
