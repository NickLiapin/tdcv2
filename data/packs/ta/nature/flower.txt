description: Flower (Tamil). மல்லிகை heads the list because jasmine is not decorative in Tamil life: Madurai jasmine has a geographical indication, women wear it daily in the hair, and it is sold by weight at dawn in every market. குறிஞ்சி, the Strobilanthes of the Nilgiris, flowers once every twelve years and gives its name to one of the five classical Tamil landscapes.
locale: ta
