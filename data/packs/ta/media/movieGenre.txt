description: Film genre (Tamil). Four are Tamil cinema's own shelf labels and are used by audiences and reviewers alike: கிராமத்துப் படம் (the village film, a genre in its own right since the 1980s), குடும்பப் படம் (the family drama), மசாலா படம் (the deliberately mixed-mode commercial film) and பக்திப் படம் (the devotional). Tamil cinema is one of the largest film industries in the world by annual output and its genre vocabulary is not a translation of Hollywood's.
locale: ta
