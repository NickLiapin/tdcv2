---
description: Tamil honorific or form of address, written before a name. திரு / திருமதி / செல்வி are the everyday Mr / Mrs / Miss, and செல்வன் is the boys' counterpart of செல்வி — Tamil has a distinct title for an unmarried male, which English lost with "Master". முனைவர் is the Tamil word for a doctorate holder and is used in preference to டாக்டர் in Tamil academic writing, where டாக்டர் tends to mean a physician. அடிகள், குருக்கள் and தேசிகர் are Saiva religious titles, அருட்தந்தை and சகோதரி the Catholic Father and Sister, and ஜனாப் and ஹாஜி the Muslim ones; all are in ordinary current use, not archaisms.
locale: ta
