description: Language name (Tamil). தமிழ் comes first, and it is the one entry in this pack that carries a fact worth stating once: Tamil is one of the world's longest continuously documented living languages, with an unbroken literary record of more than two thousand years — the Tolkāppiyam grammar and the Sangam anthologies are read in the same language, with effort, by literate speakers today, and India designated it a classical language in 2004, the first so recognised. It has official status in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, is one of the two official languages of Sri Lanka, is one of Singapore's four, and is the medium of a state-funded school system in Malaysia. The next block is the languages actually spoken alongside Tamil rather than the world's biggest: the Nilgiri languages (படகா, தோடா, கோத்தா, இருளா, குரும்பா), சௌராஷ்டிரம் — a Gujarati-descended language whose speakers have lived in Madurai for four hundred years and which is written in Tamil script — then Sinhala and Malay and Mandarin, which are what Tamil sits beside in Sri Lanka, Malaysia and Singapore. The world languages follow.
