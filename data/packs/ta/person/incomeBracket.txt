description: Annual household income bracket, labelled in Tamil. Amounts are in INR (₹), because Tamil Nadu holds the great majority of the language's speakers — the other three Tamil-speaking countries use the Sri Lankan rupee, the Singapore dollar and the ringgit, and their bands are country data and belong in the country packs, not here. The bands use the INDIAN NUMBERING SYSTEM, which groups by லட்சம் (1 lakh = 100,000) and கோடி (1 crore = 10,000,000), and a Tamil salary is quoted as ₹12 லட்சம், never as ₹1.2 million. A config that formats a raw number for a Tamil reader must also use the 2,2,3 digit grouping (₹12,00,000), not the Western 3,3,3.
locale: ta
