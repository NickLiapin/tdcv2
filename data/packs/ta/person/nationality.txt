description: Nationality or demonym (Tamil, noun form). Led by the four countries where Tamil is a language of daily public life — India, Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia — and then by the countries of the older Tamil diaspora, which is why மொரிசியஸ், ஃபிஜி, தென்னாப்பிரிக்கா, ரீயூனியன் and கயானா sit far above their populations: Tamil indentured labour went to all of them in the nineteenth century and their Tamil communities are still there. Note that Tamil forms most demonyms with the suffix -ர் on the country name (இந்தியர், இலங்கையர், சிங்கப்பூரர்), so the demonym and the country name are not independent words the way "Netherlands / Dutch" are in English.
locale: ta
