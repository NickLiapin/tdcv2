Worth recording separately: Tamil Nadu was the first Indian state to formally recognise the transgender community, and the word the state adopted, திருநங்கை, has since displaced the older அரவாணி in official Tamil; a config that models identity rather than the male/female name directories should carry it in a list of its own.
