description: Marital status (Tamil). An Indian, Sri Lankan or Singaporean form written in Tamil usually offers only the first two; the rest are the words a longer register uses. Tamil distinguishes the widowed by the spouse lost — கணவரை இழந்தவர் is a woman who lost her husband, மனைவியை இழந்தவர் a man who lost his wife — so a single "widowed" value cannot be produced without knowing the row's gender. விதவை, the old blunt word for a widow, carries a weight of social stigma that the neutral phrases do not, and a config generating administrative data should prefer the phrases.
locale: ta
