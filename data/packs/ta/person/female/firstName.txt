description: Tamil female given names, roughly frequency-ordered. Three things a config cannot infer. First, this list deliberately does NOT overlap person/male/firstName.txt: Tamil marks gender morphologically, feminine in -இ / -ஆ against masculine -அன் / -ம் (செல்வி / செல்வன், தமிழரசி / தமிழரசன், இளவரசி / இளவரசன்), so a shared name would be a mistake rather than a fact. Second, the -அம்மாள் and -அம்மா names (பொன்னம்மாள், முத்தம்மாள், வள்ளியம்மாள், அங்கம்மாள்) are a strong AGE marker, not a style: they belong to women born before roughly 1960 and a config generating twenty-year-olds should not draw them. Third, the same Dravidian-era split as in the male list runs through this one — the devotional Sanskritic band (லட்சுமி, சரஸ்வதி, பத்மாவதி, ராஜேஸ்வரி) against the pure-Tamil band the movement created (தமிழ்ச்செல்வி, கனிமொழி, கயல்விழி, வெண்ணிலா, தென்றல், இனியா, யாழினி), and drawing across both gives a cohort that no real Tamil family looks like. Note also that a woman's name of record is written with an initial in front of it exactly as a man's is; the initial is her father's given name before marriage and, by convention in Tamil Nadu, her husband's after it — see person/lastName.txt.
