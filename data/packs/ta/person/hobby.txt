description: Hobby or interest (Tamil). Four entries are specifically Tamil rather than translated: கோலமிடுதல் is the daily rice-flour drawing made on the threshold before dawn, a practice with its own competitive and geometric literature; சிலம்பம் is the Tamil staff-fighting martial art; கர்நாடக இசை and பரதநாட்டியம் are the classical music and dance systems of the Tamil south, taught to an enormous number of Tamil children as a formal discipline rather than as a pastime. கிரிக்கெட் and கபடி are here because they are what is actually played.
locale: ta
