description: Tamil male given names, roughly frequency-ordered. Four things a config cannot infer. First, Tamil given names are morphologically sex-marked — the masculine ends in -அன் / -ன் / -ம் and the feminine in -இ / -ஆ (செல்வன்/செல்வி, தமிழரசன்/தமிழரசி, இளவரசன்/இளவரசி) — so this list deliberately does NOT overlap person/female/firstName.txt; a name in both would be an error, not a fact about unisex naming the way the Punjabi -preet names are in Hindi. Second, the list mixes two name STOCKS that a Tamil reader dates instantly: the older Sanskritic-devotional stock (சுப்பிரமணியன், ரங்கநாதன், கோபாலகிருஷ்ணன், சீனிவாசன்) and the deliberately Sanskrit-free stock the Dravidian movement produced from the 1930s onward (தமிழ்ச்செல்வன், நெடுமாறன், வளவன், செழியன், அதியமான், சேரன் — several of them the names of Sangam-era kings and poets, revived on purpose). Choosing between them is a political and generational statement, and a config that wants an internally consistent cohort should draw from one band rather than the whole file. Third, the -சாமி and -முத்து names (ராமசாமி, கந்தசாமி, பழனிசாமி, மாரிமுத்து) are the ones that most often turn up as an INITIAL in front of somebody else's name, because they are the generation that is now the fathers of record; see person/lastName.txt. Fourth, the Muslim and Christian Tamil names at the tail are Tamil names, not imports: சவரிமுத்து and இருதயராஜ் are Tamil words, and மைதீன் and ஹனீஃபா have been Tamil names on the Coromandel coast for centuries.
