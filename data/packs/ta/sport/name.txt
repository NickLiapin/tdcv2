description: Sport (Tamil). The head of the list is what is actually played and watched in the Tamil country. Four entries have no equivalent anywhere else: சிலம்பம் is the Tamil staff-fighting martial art, now a recognised sport with graded competition; ஜல்லிக்கட்டு is the bull-embracing contest of the Pongal festival, which in 2017 produced the largest civil protest in Tamil Nadu's recent history when it was banned; ஏறுதழுவல் is its older Tamil name; and உறியடி and கிட்டிப்புள் are village games still organised at temple festivals. வள்ளம் ஓட்டம் is the Onam-season snake-boat racing of the far south and Kerala border.
locale: ta
