description: Event type (Tamil). Six entries are Tamil life-cycle and temple events with no European counterpart: the திருமணம் is preceded by a நிச்சயதார்த்தம், the வளைகாப்பு is the bangle ceremony in the seventh month of pregnancy, the காதுகுத்து is the ear-piercing, the பூப்புனித நீராட்டு marks a girl's coming of age and is a large catered public event in Tamil families, and the தேர்த்திருவிழா is the temple chariot procession that a whole town turns out for.
locale: ta
