description: School subject as taught in secondary education in Fryslân, and the first line is the whole point of the file. Frisian is a compulsory subject in the province's primary schools and a recognised examination subject in secondary school, which makes it the only regional language in the Netherlands with a slot of its own on the timetable rather than an after-hours club; schools may apply to the province for a partial ûntheffing, an exemption, and enough of them do that the amount of Frisian actually taught varies a great deal from school to school. A number of primary schools go much further and run as trijetalige skoallen, working across three languages - Frisian, Dutch and English - each used as the language of instruction for part of the week instead of being merely a subject. Because that first slot had to be found somewhere, this list drops the Dutch arts-appreciation block, culturele en kunstzinnige vorming, rather than one of the sciences. The rest are the ordinary havo and vwo subjects under Frisian names: ierdrykskunde is geography, from ierde, earth; skiednis is history; maatskippijlear is civics, from maatskippij plus lear, a body of teaching; and keunst shows the eu that Frisian writes where Dutch has a bare u.
