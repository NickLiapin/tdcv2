description: Hotel room type (West Frisian). The counting words are the file's own lesson, because a room list is mostly numbers: ien, twa, trije and fjouwer give ienpersoans-, twapersoans-, trijepersoans- and fjouwerpersoanskeamer, and trije and fjouwer stand beside English three and four where Dutch has drie and vier. A keamer is a room and a bêd a bed, the same word as English bed. Gesinskeamer is the family room, on gesin, a household with children, which Frisian distinguishes from famylje, the wider relations - a distinction English collapses into family and a booking form usually gets wrong.
locale: fy
