description: Human-readable colour name (West Frisian, lowercase). The basic terms are the clearest short proof in this pack that Frisian and English are cousins: read, grien, giel, wyt and swart stand beside red, green, yellow, white and black where Dutch has rood, groen, geel, wit and zwart, and giel against yellow is a straight cognate that Dutch lost. Pears is purple, and it is a Frisian word rather than a respelling of Dutch paars. Oranje carries a weight here it does not carry in any other locale in this project, but not the one a Dutch list gives it: orange is the House of Orange and the national team, while Fryslân's own colours are the blue and white diagonals of the provincial flag with seven red pompeblêden - water-lily leaves - on it, so read and blau are the colours a Frisian sees on a flagpole. Lowercase throughout, as in Dutch and unlike German, where colour nouns are capitalised.
locale: fy
