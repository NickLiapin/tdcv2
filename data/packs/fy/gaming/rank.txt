description: Competitive rank tier (West Frisian). The ladder is the usual metals-then-gems progression, and the Frisian forms are the ones from nature/metal and nature/gemstone: izer for iron, with a single i and no Dutch IJ digraph, brûns, sulver. Master and grutmaster sit above the gems, and útdager, the challenger, tops it - útdaagje is to challenge, built on út and daagje, the same daagje that means to dawn, so the word carries the sense of calling someone out into the open.
locale: fy
