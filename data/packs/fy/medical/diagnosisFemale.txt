description: Diagnoses in West Frisian that require female anatomy and cannot occur in a male patient; general conditions live in medical/diagnosis, and breast cancer is deliberately absent because it occurs in men too. The Frisian for the uterus is liifmoer, body-mother, assembled from different halves than Dutch baarmoeder and the root of both Liifmoerkanker and Liifmoerhalskanker. Fleesbeammen is fibroids read literally as meat-trees, and its second half shows brekking, the vowel change that turns beam into beammen in the plural. Swangerskip ends in -skip, the suffix English writes -ship where Dutch writes -schap, and Aaistokkanker keeps aai, the Frisian egg, where Dutch has ei.
