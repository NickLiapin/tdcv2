description: Medical specialties in West Frisian, following the register the Dutch specialist system actually recognises rather than the English names. Three compounds repay a close reading. Hielkunde is general surgery, from hiel-, to heal, so Eachhielkunde is ophthalmology with each, the Frisian eye, and Kiel-noas-earhielkunde is ENT built from three plain body words — throat, nose and ear, the last spelt exactly as in English. Bernegenêskunde is paediatrics from bern, the child, the word English keeps in Scots bairn. Âldereingenêskunde is elderly-care medicine, from âlderein, the old folk, and it is a separately registered specialty that exists in the Netherlands and almost nowhere else. Fryslân has no academic hospital of its own: its general hospitals stand in Ljouwert, Snits, Drachten and It Hearrenfean, and the nearest university clinic is over the provincial border in Grins.
