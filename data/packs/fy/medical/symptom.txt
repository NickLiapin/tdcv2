description: Complaints in West Frisian, worded the way a patient states one at the húsdokter's desk. Frisian does not fetch air, it fetches breath: sykhelje is to breathe, and the syk inside it is the breath, not sickness — that is siik, a different word with a different vowel, and a Dutch reader gets the pair wrong on sight. Pine is pain, so Hollepine, Búkpine, Kielpine and Lege rêchpine are built the way Dutch builds them but out of different words for head, belly, throat and back. Jokte is an itch, Wurgens is tiredness from wurch, Betizing is confusion and Prûsten is sneezing. Two entries end in -leas, the Frisian suffix that is English -less and Dutch -loos: Gefoelleazens is numbness and Sliepeleazens is sleeplessness.
locale: fy
