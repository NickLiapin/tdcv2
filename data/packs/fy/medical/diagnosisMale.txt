description: Diagnoses in West Frisian that require male anatomy and cannot occur in a female patient; general conditions live in medical/diagnosis. Two Frisian habits run through the list. Sied is seed, the direct cognate of the English word where Dutch has zaad, and it builds Foartidige siedlozing and Siedblaaskesûntstekking; the middle of that second compound is a diminutive, blaas becoming blaaske and its plural blaaskes, one of the three endings -ke, -tsje and -je that Frisian uses where Dutch has only -je. The other habit is the disappearance of c: it becomes s before e and i and k elsewhere, so cele turns into -sèle in Farikosèle and Hydrosèle and cryptorchisme into Kryptorkisme.
locale: fy
