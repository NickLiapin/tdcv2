description: Diagnoses in West Frisian that can occur in any patient — sex-specific ones live in diagnosisMale and diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. The list is deliberately written in two registers at once, because that is how medicine is spoken in Fryslân: the everyday name is Frisian and the clinical name is Latin, and both are in use across the same desk. So Grize stear and Griene stear stand for cataract and glaucoma, grey and green cataract, where the colour is the entire distinction; Mûzels is measles, Wetterpokken is chickenpox, Mageswolm is a stomach ulcer and Wûnroas is erysipelas, while every -itis is left in the Latin shape it arrived in. Note sykte where Dutch has ziekte and fan where Dutch has van, and that a Dutch -ie ending turns into Frisian -y throughout: Epilepsy, Leukemy, Anemy.
locale: fy
