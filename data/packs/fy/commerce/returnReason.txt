description: Order return reason (West Frisian). Under the EU and Dutch herroepingsrjocht a buyer may send an online purchase back within fourteen days without giving any reason at all, so Fan gedachten feroare is a legally complete entry here rather than the thin excuse it would read as elsewhere. Three lines carry Frisian words a Dutch reader will misjudge. Flater is the ordinary, neutral word for a fault or defect where Dutch would say fout, so fabrikaazjeflater is a manufacturing fault; Dutch does have flater, but it means a public blunder, which makes it a false friend rather than a shared word. Earne oars is somewhere else, built on earne, a word for somewhere that Dutch lost and replaced with ergens. And let is late - the English word, against Dutch laat - so Te let levere is a delivery running behind and has nothing to do with the Dutch laten it looks like.
locale: fy
