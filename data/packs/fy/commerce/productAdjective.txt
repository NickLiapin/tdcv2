description: Product adjective (West Frisian), given in the uninflected base form. As in Dutch, an attributive adjective takes an -e ending in nearly every position (de tûke lampe, in tûke lampe) and drops it only before a singular indefinite neuter noun (in tûk apparaat), so a config that glues these straight onto a noun has to supply the ending itself. Two suffixes here break if a Dutch spelling rule is applied to them. Dutch -baar is Frisian -ber: draachber, ferstelber, oplaadber, stapelber, útwreidber. And Dutch -loos is Frisian -leas, which is the English -less; triedleas is literally thread-less and is the word for wireless, because a wire is a tried in Frisian where Dutch has draad, and lûdleas and merkleas are built the same way. Watch licht as well: on this list it means light in weight and nothing else, because the light you see by is ljocht, a separate word with a separate vowel, and Frisian keeps the pair apart where Dutch lets licht cover both senses.
locale: fy
