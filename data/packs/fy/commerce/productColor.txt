description: Colour as written on a Frisian product listing - capitalised the way a listing capitalises it, which is why this is a separate file from color/name, where the basic terms are lowercase. Two things break here that would not break in English. The diacritics are letters and not decoration: Brún, Sûkeladebrún, Sânkleurich and Trochsichtich carry ú, û and â, and a pipeline that strips accents to build an ASCII slug produces brun and sankleurich, which are not words in this language and will not match anything. And every shade name is a solid compound with the colour itself at the end - Mosterdgiel, Leistiengriis, Ivoarwyt, Marineblau, Salmrôze - so the distinguishing half is the first half, and truncating a listing colour to a fixed width leaves the least informative part standing. Twakleurich shows where the language sits: Frisian counts twa, the same word as English two, where Dutch counts twee.
locale: fy
