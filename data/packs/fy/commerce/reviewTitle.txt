description: Product review headline (West Frisian), as a buyer writes one. Frisian intensifies differently from Dutch and both of its everyday devices are on this list. Tige is the general intensifier - tige tefreden, tige brûkerfreonlik - and it is the same word as in tige tank, which is how a Frisian says thank you very much. Poer is not a separate adverb but a prefix welded onto the adjective: poerbêst is the ordinary superlative a satisfied customer reaches for, and the same prefix gives poerlilk, furious, and poertsjuster, pitch dark. The third thing to read closely is the comparative: Frisian says better as ferwachte and flugger levere as tasein, using as where Dutch requires dan - the construction a Dutch schoolteacher corrects is the only correct one here. As in Dutch, the register runs flat, and Docht wat it dwaan moat is genuine approval rather than faint praise.
locale: fy
