description: Store department or shopping category (West Frisian). Three of these are words Dutch has no form of. Ark is the ordinary Frisian word for tools - the whole hardware trade in three letters - and it is not the Dutch ark, which is a boat, so it is a false friend rather than a loan. Boartersguod is toys, built on boartsje, to play, a verb with no Dutch cognate at all; Dutch spelen is a different root entirely. Skjintme is beauty, from skjin, clean, and it carries the sk- that Frisian shares with English against the Dutch sch-. Wettersport sits low in this list and should not: Fryslân holds the Snitser Mar and the rest of the Frisian lakes, the Waadsee and the skûtsje racing fleet, and a chandlery in Snits or Grou stocks a department that a shop in a landlocked province would not have. Doch-it-sels is do-it-yourself with the Frisian imperative doch, from dwaan, which is the English do. Note also that Drogisterij and Apoteek are separate trades and not synonyms: the first sells toiletries and remedies over the counter, the second dispenses prescriptions.
locale: fy
