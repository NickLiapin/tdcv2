What the lines are here to show is the grammar: the finite verb stands second in a main clause and last in a subordinate one opened by om't, dat, as, wylst or doe't; separable verbs come apart in the main clause and only rejoin at the end of the subordinate one, as in hy docht de doar iepen; the perfect takes hawwe or wêze according to the verb, so sy hat it boek lêzen but hy is nei hûs gien; the progressive is oan it plus a verbal noun, hja is oan it kôkjen; and negation is net for a verb but gjin for a noun. The apostrophe in yn 'e, op 'e and om't is not stray punctuation but the standard written shape of a reduced article and of a clitic t.
