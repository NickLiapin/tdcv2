description: Animal, general (West Frisian). Read down the first fifteen and the North Sea Germanic family tree is visible without a philologist: hûn is hound, ko is cow, skiep is sheep, mûs in flearmûs is mouse, foks is fox, and every one of them stands nearer its English cousin than to Dutch hond, koe, schaap, muis and vos. Two entries are Frisian compounds that describe the animal outright: a hedgehog is a stikelbaarch, a prickle-pig, and a hippopotamus is a nylhynder, a Nile-horse, built on hynder rather than on the Dutch paard. Ko is not an ordinary entry in this locale either - the Frysk-Hollânske dairy cow, the black-and-white animal exported from these fields as the Holstein-Friesian, is the single thing this province is best known for worldwide. Bever and das are here because both were locally extinct in the Netherlands and have been reintroduced, so they are ordinary nature vocabulary again rather than history.
