description: Technical abbreviation, and the one list in this pack where Frisian and Dutch legitimately come out identical line for line. Abbreviations are not words but runs of Latin letters standing in for English or Dutch phrases, and nobody re-cuts them for a smaller language: the Fryske Akademy in Ljouwert sets Frisian spelling and vocabulary and has never had a mandate to mint acronyms, so a Frisian engineer writes TCP, JSON and SSD exactly as everyone else does while every ordinary word around them changes. AVG is the interesting one and the only entry that is not international. It is the Dutch short form of the European data protection regulation, from Algemene verordening gegevensbescherming, and it turns up constantly in systems work in the Netherlands; the Frisian name of the same law is Algemiene Feroardering Gegevensbeskerming, whose own initials would come out AFG, but the short form is fixed by Dutch statute and Frisian keeps AVG. Treat every value as an opaque token - never case-fold one, never split one, and never expect a Frisian expansion to sit behind the letters.
locale: fy
