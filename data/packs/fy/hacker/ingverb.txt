description: Technical verb as a present participle. Frisian builds it by adding -end to the infinitive, so the language's two live conjugation classes show through the whole list: class I verbs in -e give -end (ferbine becomes ferbinend, oerskriuwe becomes oerskriuwend) while class II verbs in -je give -jend (berekkenje becomes berekkenjend), which is why so many lines here end in -jend and not one of their Dutch counterparts does. Do not build a progressive tense out of these. Frisian has a real progressive of its own and it uses a different construction entirely - hy is oan it programmearjen, literally he is on the programming, or the posture form hy sit te wurkjen, he sits to work - so the participle is attributive or headline material only, which is exactly what this filler list is for. Two spellings will be misjudged by a Dutch reader: Dutch over is Frisian oer, giving oerskriuwend, and the circumflex and acute in útrôljend are letters rather than decoration, since ô and û are separate vowels that change the word.
