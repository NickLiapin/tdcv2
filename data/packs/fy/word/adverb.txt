description: Common adverb (West Frisian), lowercase. Frisian has no -ly suffix and no separate manner form: for manner it simply reuses the bare adjective, so a manner-adverb list would be a copy of word/adjective.txt, and what is collected here is time, place, degree and frequency instead. The place words run as a matched set - hjir, dêr, oeral, earne, nearne - and earne and nearne, somewhere and nowhere, are built on a root that Dutch ergens and nergens do not share. Nea for never carries the ea diphthong that marks the older Frisian layer of the vocabulary, as meastal and leaver do. Aanst is soon or in a moment and is one of the words a Dutch speaker in Fryslân has to be taught. Tige is the intensifier a learner meets on day one, because tige tank is thank you very much. Fansels, of course, is literally of-self, and spitigernôch, unfortunately, is spitich plus enough - both are one word in Frisian and a phrase in English.
locale: fy
