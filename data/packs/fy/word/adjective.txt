description: Common adjective (West Frisian), lowercase, in the uninflected base form. Frisian adds -e before most nouns exactly as Dutch does - in grut hûs but it grutte hûs - so a config that glues these straight onto a noun will sometimes produce the wrong form. Several entries are the reason Frisian is grouped with English rather than with Dutch: siik is sick against Dutch ziek, sêft is soft against zacht, swiet is sweet against zoet, hjit is hot against heet, and in every one of them the vowel is the English vowel and not the Dutch one. Min is the everyday word for bad and has nothing to do with Dutch slecht. Dreech, wurch and moai carry difficult, tired and beautiful with no Dutch cognate standing behind them at all, and goedkeap shows the ea diphthong that Dutch spells oo. One trap this list cannot show by itself: leech means both low and empty, so it appears here once, and a config that wants the other sense gets no help from this file.
locale: fy
