description: Property amenity as a Frisian listing writes it. Berging - a lockable private store, usually on the ground floor - and fytsestalling are near-universal features and are always named in an advertisement; sinnepanielen and dûbel glês are selling points rather than extras, because a listing must state an energy label and insulation is what moves it. Three words are worth the file on their own. Iepen hurd is the open fireplace, and hurd is a hearth, the same word as English hearth where Dutch has haard. Flier is a floor, standing beside English floor against Dutch vloer, so flierferwaarming is underfloor heating. And glês is glass, with the circumflex, one of the pack's clean Anglo-Frisian pairs.
locale: fy
