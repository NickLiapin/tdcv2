description: Business buzz verb (West Frisian, infinitive). Frisian runs every borrowed Latinate management verb through -earje and never through the Dutch -eren: optimalisearje, yntegrearje, digitalisearje, sertifisearje, konsolidearje. The native verbs beside them show what the language does with prefixes - gear- is the Frisian for together, so gearbringe is to bring together and gearwurkje is to cooperate, where Dutch uses samen-; wer- is the repeat prefix, giving werdefiniearje and werwurdearje; and ûnt- opens or undoes, as in ûntslute, ûntwerpe and ûntregelje. Oandriuwe and bine show the consonants moving: Dutch drijven and binden lose the v to a w and the nd to an n. Polderje and boargje come straight out of the working culture rather than out of English - to polder is to negotiate everyone present into a compromise, and to boargje a change is to make it stick in the organisation permanently, which English can only render as a phrase.
locale: fy
