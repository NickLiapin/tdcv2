description: Business buzz noun (West Frisian, lowercase). Frisian builds these the way it builds any abstract noun and none of the endings match Dutch: -aasje where Dutch has -atie (yntegraasje, transformaasje, ynnovaasje), -y where Dutch has -ie (strategy, telemetry, synergy), -int where Dutch has -ent (segmint, rendemint), and -ens where Dutch has -heid, which is how skaalberens, waarnimberens and doelmjittigens are made. Nouns in -ing take their plural in -s and never in -en. Three entries are the vocabulary of working life in this corner of Europe rather than management jargon: oerlis is the standing consultation a decision has to pass through, draachflak is the broad backing a proposal needs before it may proceed at all however good it is, and poldermodel names the consensus-by-negotiation habit after the committees that governed the drained polders. Hefbeam, leverage, is built on beam, which is Frisian for both tree and beam where Dutch has boom; and merk is market with no t in it, the same word as in Ljouwerter merk.
locale: fy
