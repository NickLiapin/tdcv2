description: Evocative noun for a West Frisian company name (compose with a legalSuffix or an adjective). None of these is a real firm. Place names in Fryslân carry two official forms, Frisian and Dutch, and a company trading in Frisian takes the Frisian one - Ljouwert not Leeuwarden, Snits not Sneek, Harns not Harlingen, Boalsert not Bolsward - so a name built from this list will not match a Dutch gazetteer even though every town in it is on the map. The land block is the part no imported list would hold: a terp is the artificial dwelling mound villages were raised on before there were dykes, which is why a province without hills has churches standing above the fields; a miede is hay meadow as against greide, grazing pasture, and Frisian keeps the two apart; a dobbe is the pond dug on a mound to water cattle; klaai and wâld are the sea-clay north and the wooded southeast, the two halves the province still splits itself into. Pompeblêd is the water-lily leaf, seven of which make the provincial flag, and a skûtsje is the flat-bottomed freight barge built shallow enough for the canals, which now races every August.
locale: fy
