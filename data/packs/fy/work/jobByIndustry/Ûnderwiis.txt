description: Jobs in education. Frysk-dosint stands on this list because Frisian is a compulsory subject in every primary and secondary school in the province, and teaching it needs a qualification of its own; beyond that there are the trijetalige skoallen, the three-language schools that run their teaching in Frisian, Dutch and English by turns, which is a staffing model that exists nowhere else in the country. Yntern begelieder is the primary-school learning-support coordinator, a named Dutch post with a caseload rather than a class, and dekaan here means the secondary-school careers and study-choice adviser, not a university dean - a false friend that catches English readers every time.
