VCA is the site-safety certificate demanded of most industrial and construction work in the Netherlands and Belgium, and bedriuwshelpferliening is the in-house emergency response training Dutch law obliges every employer to arrange. The trade entries carry the Frisian roots plainly: laskjen for welding, leadjitterswurk for plumbing from lead and jitte, to pour, tekeninglêzen from lêze, to read, and gearwurkjen for teamwork, where gear- means together and turns up again in gearkomste, a meeting.
