description: Job titles across every sector, in West Frisian, with the province's own labour market left visible in the list. Fryslân is dairy country - the Holstein-Friesian cow is named after it - so feehâlder, molkfeehâlder, feedokter, agrarysk adviseur and meiwurker suvelfabryk are ordinary entries here rather than colour. So is water: a dykwacht walks the sea dyke, the Wetterskip Fryslân is a separately elected water authority that sets the water table and employs people to do it, a sylwachter keeps a sluice, and a wadgids is a licensed qualification rather than a hobby, since guiding walkers over the tidal flats of the Waadsee needs a certificate. Skûtsjeskipper is a paid job because the racing barges are the original freight boats, one per town, and they carry a crew. Two Frisian habits run through the whole list: agent nouns take -er or -der wherever the trade word is native (metselder, makelder, skilder, fisker, oersetter), and compounds are built on Frisian roots rather than Dutch ones - toskedokter for a dentist, from tosk, the same word as English tusk; leadjitter for a plumber, literally a lead-pourer; jiskeman for a bin man, from jiske, ashes; ynkeaper from keapje, to buy; skjinmakker from skjin, clean; and fleantúchmonteur from fleane, to fly. Frysk-dosint is a real post because Frisian is a compulsory school subject in the province, and meiwurker Tresoar names a job at the provincial archive and library in Ljouwert.
locale: fy
