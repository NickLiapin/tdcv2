description: Mobile and broadband plan type (West Frisian). Sim only is the dominant contract form in this market and the reason is legal rather than commercial: since 2016 a phone bundled into a subscription counts as consumer credit under Dutch law and has to be registered with the credit bureau, which pushed almost everyone into buying the handset separately. Alles-yn-1 is the standard bundle of internet, television and telephony. The Frisian forms to note are ûnbeheind for unlimited, on the ûn- prefix that does the work of Dutch on- and English un-, gesinsbondel for a family plan, and opwurdearje, to top up - literally to up-value, the same verb Frisian uses for revaluing anything.
