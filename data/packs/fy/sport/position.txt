description: Playing position drawn from several sports at once (West Frisian). Because it mixes sports, the same word means different things depending on the game: middenfjilder is a midfielder in football and hockey but the centre fielder in honkbal, and Center is the middle man of an ice-hockey line and also the tallest player in basketball. For a coherent single-sport draw use sport/positionBySport instead. Two keatsen roles close the list, Opslagger and Efterynse, because a Frisian position list that left out the province's own sport would be a Dutch list with the spelling changed; the third keatsen role, Foarynse, is in the Keatsen child file. Frisian basketball keeps the English position names unchanged while football rebuilt every one of its own on Frisian roots - ferdigener, middenfjilder, bûten - and both facts are visible in a single draw from here.
