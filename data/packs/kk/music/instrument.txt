description: Musical instrument. THE FIRST BLOCK IS KAZAKH AND IS PLAYED, TAUGHT AND SOLD TODAY. ДОМБЫРА, the two-string long-necked lute, is the national instrument and is close to universal; ҚОБЫЗ is the bowed horsehair fiddle of the baqsy, the shaman, and its sound is deliberately rough; СЫБЫЗҒЫ is the end-blown flute played with a simultaneous sung drone; ЖЕТІГЕН is the seven-string zither; ШАҢҚОБЫЗ the jaw harp; САЗСЫРНАЙ a clay ocarina; АСАТАЯҚ a rattling staff; ДАҢҒЫРА a frame drum; КЕРНЕЙ a long horn. A Kazakh music school teaches домбыра alongside piano as a main instrument, not as folklore.
