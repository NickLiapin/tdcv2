description: Music genre. THE KAZAKH GENRES AT THE END ARE GENRES, NOT SONGS, and none has a European equivalent. КҮЙ is an instrumental piece for dombyra or kobyz that tells a story without words and is named after the story; a күйші is judged on which күй they play, the way a pianist is on repertoire. ТЕРМЕ is a fast sung recitation of moral instruction over a repeated dombyra figure. ЖЫР is the heroic epic, sung by a жырау for hours. ТОЛҒАУ is the reflective poem. АЙТЫС is the improvised sung contest between two poets, broadcast live on national television with a jury and a scoreboard, and it is the most watched of them all.
locale: kk
