description: Property type as a Kazakhstani listing states it. THE LISTING CONVENTION IS DIFFERENT FROM THE ANGLOPHONE ONE: a flat is advertised by its ROOM COUNT, not by bedrooms or floor area — 1-бөлмелі, 2-бөлмелі, 3-бөлмелі — where the room count includes the living room, so a 2-бөлмелі пәтер is a one-bedroom flat by British reckoning. САЯЖАЙ, the dacha, is a distinct legal and practical category: a plot with a summer house outside the city, very widely owned, often without winter heating, and priced and taxed differently from a dwelling.
