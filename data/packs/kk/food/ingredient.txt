description: Cooking ingredient. THE MEAT ORDER IS THE FACT WORTH RECORDING: қой еті (mutton) and жылқы еті (horse) come before beef and chicken, and PORK IS ABSENT — Kazakhstan is majority Muslim and a Kazakh recipe does not reach for it. Horse is not a novelty meat here but a staple with its own butchery vocabulary (қазы from the rib, жая from the rump, жал from the crest). The dairy block is equally specific: қымыз is fermented mare's milk, шұбат fermented camel's milk, айран a sour drink, құрт hard dried sour-milk balls that keep for a year without refrigeration, and талқан toasted milled grain — between them they are how a mobile herding society stored food, and all five are still on sale.
