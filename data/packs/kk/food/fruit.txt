АЛМА, THE APPLE, IS FIRST AND IT IS NOT ARBITRARY: the wild apple Malus sieversii, the ancestor of every cultivated apple on earth, is native to the Tien Shan foothills above Almaty, and the city is named for it — Алматы is "the apple place". Өрік, the apricot, and жүзім come from the same mountain belt. Қарбыз and қауын, watermelon and melon, are the summer fruit of the south and the melons of Qyzylorda are a national reference point. The berries at the end — қарақат, бүлдірген, таңқурай — are northern and forest, so the list spans the whole climatic range of the country.
