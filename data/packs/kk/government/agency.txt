description: Government body. This names KAZAKHSTAN'S institutions, which is what the words mean for a language spoken in essentially one country, and the reader should not mistake it for a generic list. Two are worth flagging: the ХАЛЫҚҚА ҚЫЗМЕТ КӨРСЕТУ ОРТАЛЫҒЫ (the "Government for Citizens" one-stop centre) is where nearly every civil document is issued in person, and the Мемлекеттік кірістер комитеті is the tax and customs authority rolled into one body.
