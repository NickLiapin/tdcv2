THE PRIMARY IDENTITY DOCUMENT IS THE ЖЕКЕ КУӘЛІК, the internal ID card, which every citizen over sixteen must hold and which is what is shown at a bank, a polling station or a police stop; THE PASSPORT IS FOR FOREIGN TRAVEL ONLY and most people's daily life never involves one. Every document here is keyed to the ЖСН, the twelve-digit individual identification number, which is the spine of the whole civil register — the generator for it lives in the Kazakhstan country pack, not in this language pack, because a number format belongs to a state and not to a language.
