description: School subject in Kazakhstan. Two entries have no Western counterpart: ҚАЗАҚСТАН ТАРИХЫ is a separate compulsory subject from world history and is examined on its own, and ӨЗІН-ӨЗІ ТАНУ, "self-knowledge", was a distinct compulsory ethics-and-values subject in the Kazakhstani curriculum. Note also that Kazakh language and Kazakh literature are two subjects, not one, and the same is true of Russian.
locale: kk
