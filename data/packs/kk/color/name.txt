description: Colour name in Kazakh. TWO THINGS DO NOT MAP ONTO ENGLISH. FIRST, КӨК covers blue AND the green of living things — көк аспан is the blue sky, көк шөп is green grass, көктем is spring, "the greening" — and жасыл is a later, narrower word for green that does not reach the grass. So a config translating "green" gets жасыл for a painted object and көк for a plant, and the two are not interchangeable. SECOND, Kazakh has a whole separate colour vocabulary FOR HORSES which has no English equivalent and which the last block of this list holds: торы is bay, жирен chestnut, күрең dark chestnut, боз grey-white, ақбоз white, құла dun, шабдар palomino, ала piebald, шұбар dappled. These words are used of horses first and of anything else only by extension, so a Kazakh hearing торы assumes a horse is meant — which makes them wrong for a paint chart and right for livestock data.
locale: kk
