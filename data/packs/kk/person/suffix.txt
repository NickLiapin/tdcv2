description: What follows a Kazakh name. The first two entries are the important ones and they are not really suffixes in the English sense: ұлы and қызы attach to the FATHER'S given name to build the patronymic that occupies the middle slot of every official Kazakh record — Ерланұлы, Ерланқызы. The rest are academic degrees and titles, written after the name in a document. THE GENERATIONAL NUMERALS ARE DELIBERATELY ABSENT: Kazakh has no Jr / Sr / II / III convention, because a son is never given his father's name — the patronymic already carries the father, so repeating it would be incoherent. Кіші and үлкен below mean junior and senior in rank or age, not in a naming line.
