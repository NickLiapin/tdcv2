description: Monthly gross income band in TENGE (KZT, symbol ₸), which is the unit any Kazakhstani salary is quoted in. The bands are set against real magnitudes rather than converted from a dollar list: the statutory minimum wage is in the low tens of thousands of tenge a month, the national median sits in the low hundreds of thousands, and a band above a million tenge is genuinely high earning. The tenge sign follows the number with a space, and the thousands separator is a space (350 000 ₸), never a comma.
locale: kk
