description: Kazakh female given name, roughly frequency-ordered, with rank-derived Zipf weights rather than registry counts, for the same reason as the male list. Two elements dominate and are worth naming because they make the list instantly readable: гүл, Persian for flower, opens or closes a very large share of Kazakh female names (Айгүл, Гүлнар, Гүлжан, Нұргүл, Гүлназ, Бибігүл), and ай, moon, does the same (Айгүл, Айнұр, Айжан, Айсұлу, Айым, Айдана). Казах female names are formed from visible words — Жұлдыз is star, Мөлдір is clear, Салтанат is splendour, Меруерт is pearl, Ботагөз is camel-calf-eye, an old compliment for large dark eyes — so a Kazakh reader parses them as vocabulary rather than as opaque labels. GIVEN NAMES ARE NOT UNISEX in Kazakh: this list and the male list are disjoint, and the two are kept apart by their final element rather than by a grammatical ending, because Kazakh has no grammatical gender at all.
