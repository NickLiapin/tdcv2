description: Kazakh female surname — the feminine form parallel to person/lastName.txt, LINE FOR LINE, and DERIVED BY SCRIPT from it rather than maintained by hand, so the two files cannot drift apart. Two rules and one non-rule. RULE ONE, the Soviet pattern: a surname ending in -ов, -ев or -ин takes a final -а — Ахметов becomes Ахметова, Әлиев becomes Әлиева, Сейфуллин becomes Сейфуллина. This is borrowed Russian morphology, not Kazakh agreement; Kazakh itself has no grammatical gender, and the language would not inflect a name at all if left to its own devices. RULE TWO, the native pattern: ұлы, "his son", is REPLACED by қызы, "his daughter" — Момышұлы becomes Момышқызы, Байтұрсынұлы becomes Байтұрсынқызы. This is a real swap of one word for another rather than an ending, so the stem is untouched. THE NON-RULE: the bare ancestral surnames — Бөкейхан, Шоқай, Мұртаза, Тілеуберді — are identical to their masculine entries, because there is nothing there to inflect. Of the 192 surnames, 153 take the Russian -а, 30 swap ұлы for қызы, and 9 are unchanged. SEPARATELY, and not a surname: the middle slot of an official Kazakh record is the PATRONYMIC, father's given name plus ұлы or қызы — Айгүл Ерланқызы, Асхат Ерланұлы — so a woman with the surname Ахметова may carry the patronymic Ерланқызы beside it. That patronymic is always sex-marked even when the family name is not.
