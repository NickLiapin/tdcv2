description: Kazakh short or familiar name. THIS IS NOT THE ENGLISH "Bob for Robert" SLOT AND A CONFIG SHOULD NOT TREAT IT AS ONE. The dominant Kazakh pattern is the -еке / -ке honorific: the first syllable of a name plus еке, used TO someone older or senior and never by a peer or downward — Нұрсұлтан becomes Нұреке, Мұхтар becomes Мұке, Сәкен becomes Сәке, Ыбырай becomes Ыбеке. It is a mark of respect, so it can be applied to a man of seventy but not to a child, which is the exact reverse of an English diminutive. The -тай forms are kinship endearments (ататай, әжетай, апатай) and belong to family speech. Only the last few entries, the Russian-style -ик forms, work the way an English nickname does, and they came in with Russian.
