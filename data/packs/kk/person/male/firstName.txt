description: Kazakh male given name, roughly frequency-ordered, with rank-derived Zipf weights rather than registry counts — the Bureau of National Statistics publishes birth totals but not a name frequency table, and inventing one would be worse than saying so. Four layers are visible and all four are current. The native Turkic layer is built on transparent words: Нұрлан and Нұржан on нұр (light), Ерлан and Ержан on ер (man, hero), Бақыт (happiness), Болат (steel), Арыстан (lion), Жақсылық (goodness). The Arabic-Islamic layer arrived with Islam: Мұхтар, Қасым, Рамазан, Хамит, Мансұр. The Persian layer gives Дәулет, Рүстем, Дидар. And the -жан ending on Бауыржан, Нұржан, Бақытжан is Persian jan, soul, glued onto a Kazakh stem — one of the most productive name-forming elements in the language. Nine letters here exist in Kazakh and not in Russian: Ә Ғ Қ Ң Ө Ұ Ү Һ І, and they are dense in this list (Нұрлан, Қайрат, Мұрат, Әлихан, Бақыт, Өмірзақ, Ұлан, Ілияс). VOWEL HARMONY governs which suffix a name takes when it is inflected — Нұрланға but Серікке — so a config that appends a case ending must not use one fixed suffix.
