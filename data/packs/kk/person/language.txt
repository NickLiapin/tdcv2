description: Language as Kazakh names it. Казақ тілі is the state language and орыс тілі has the constitutional status of a language of interethnic communication, so both head the list; the languages after them that carry no тілі — хинди, урду, суахили — are the ones Kazakh borrows as a bare noun. Note that Kazakh names a language with the noun plus тілі, "its language", where English uses a bare adjective, so "he speaks German" is "ол неміс тілінде сөйлейді" and the possessive suffix is not optional.
locale: kk
