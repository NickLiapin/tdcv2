description: Garment. The second half of this list is Kazakh national dress and it is not costume-museum vocabulary: ШАПАН, the long quilted coat, is still given as a ceremonial honour to a guest or an elder and a Kazakhstani wedding will hand out several; ТАҚИЯ is the skullcap; БӨРІК and ТЫМАҚ are the fur hats, тымақ being the deep winter one with ear and neck flaps; САУКЕЛЕ is the tall conical bridal headdress, worn once; КАМЗОЛ the fitted sleeveless overcoat; МӘСІ the soft leather boot worn indoors with КЕБІС, an overshoe, pulled on over it at the door. Note also that КӨЙЛЕК means both a dress and a shirt — one word, two garments, and only context separates them.
locale: kk
