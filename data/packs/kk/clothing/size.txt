description: Clothing size as a label in Kazakhstan shows it. BOTH SYSTEMS ARE LIVE AND ON THE SAME RAIL: imported clothing carries the letter sizes, while anything from a post-Soviet manufacturer carries the numeric size inherited from the Soviet standard, which is roughly the chest or bust measurement in centimetres divided by two — so a women's 46 is not a European 46 and not a UK 46 either. A shopper reads whichever is printed, which is why both are here; a config that needs one scale must filter.
