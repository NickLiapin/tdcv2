description: Unit of measure. KAZAKHSTAN IS FULLY METRIC AND THE IMPERIAL UNITS ARE DELIBERATELY ABSENT: there is no pound, ounce or gallon here, because none of them appears on a Kazakhstani price label, invoice or delivery note. Центнер, a hundred kilograms, is included because it is the standard unit of the grain harvest and is quoted in every agricultural report. Пұт, the pre-revolutionary sixteen-kilogram unit, is NOT included: it is historical and would be wrong on a modern document.
locale: kk
