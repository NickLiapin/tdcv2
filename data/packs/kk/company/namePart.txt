description: Element of a Kazakhstani company name. These are the words real firms are actually built from and most of them are transparent Kazakh nouns with a positive charge: ҚАЗЫНА is treasure, БЕРЕКЕ abundance, ЫРЫС fortune, ӨРКЕН growth, ТАБЫС success, СЕНІМ trust, АМАНАТ a trust placed in someone's keeping. A second group is national symbols — БӘЙТЕРЕК the world-tree and the Astana monument, ТҰЛПАР the winged horse, БАРЫС the snow leopard, ҚЫРАН and БҮРКІТ the eagles, ШАҢЫРАҚ the crown of the yurt, which is on the flag. A third is geography used as a brand: АЛАТАУ, САРЫАРҚА, ТҰРАН, ЖЕТІСУ. The Kazakh business-name field draws on the steppe rather than on Latin roots, which is why an Apex / Vertex / Nexus list would be wrong here.
locale: kk
