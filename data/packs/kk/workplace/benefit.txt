description: Employment benefit as a Kazakhstani vacancy advertises it. Two are local: САНАТОРИЙ ЖОЛДАМАСЫ, a paid stay at a spa sanatorium, is a surviving Soviet benefit that large employers and unions still fund; and ЖОЛ ШЫҒЫНЫН ӨТЕУ covers the flights home for rotational workers, which for a Tengiz crew is a benefit worth more than a gym card. Note that pension contributions are compulsory and paid into the state ЕНПФ fund, so "зейнетақы жарнасы" as a benefit means an employer contribution above the statutory one.
