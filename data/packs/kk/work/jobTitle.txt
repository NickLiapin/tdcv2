description: Job title in Kazakh. The list follows Kazakhstan's actual employment rather than a European template, so mining, oil and gas, and herding occupy far more of it than they would in a Western locale, and a block near the end has no European equivalent at all: ШОПАН is a shepherd who lives with the flock, ЖЫЛҚЫШЫ a horse herder, ТҮЙЕШІ a camel herder, САУЫНШЫ a milker, and all four are current paid occupations with entries in the state occupational classifier, not folklore. КҮЙШІ and ДОМБЫРАШЫ, the composer-performer and player of the two-string dombyra, and БҮРКІТШІ, the eagle hunter, are likewise real occupations that appear on real Kazakhstani payrolls in philharmonics and tourism. Note that many titles are formed with the agentive suffix -шы / -ші, whose vowel is chosen by VOWEL HARMONY: жылқы gives жылқышы (back), ел gives елші (front), and a config must not append one fixed form.
