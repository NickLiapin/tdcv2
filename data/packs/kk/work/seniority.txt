KAZAKHSTAN INHERITED A FIVE-STEP LADDER FROM THE SOVIET CIVIL-SERVICE GRADES AND IT IS STILL WHAT AN OFFICIAL JOB TITLE USES: кіші маман, маман, аға маман, жетекші маман, бас маман — junior, plain, senior, leading, chief. It is a different shape from the Western junior / mid / senior / staff / principal ladder because the fourth and fifth rungs are about scope of responsibility rather than technical depth, and бас is a real rank rather than a flourish. The English-derived management titles at the end of the list are used by private and international companies, so both ladders appear in one labour market and a CV may mix them.
