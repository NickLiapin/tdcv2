THE LAST BLOCK IS THE KAZAKH NATIONAL SPORTS AND THEY ARE COMPETED, NOT COMMEMORATED: ҚАЗАҚ КҮРЕСІ is belt wrestling with its own world championship; КӨКПАР is the mounted game played over a goat carcass; БӘЙГЕ is the long-distance horse race, run over distances up to fifty kilometres; ҚЫЗ ҚУУ is the mounted chase between a man and a woman; АУДАРЫСПАҚ is wrestling on horseback; ТЕҢГЕ ІЛУ is snatching a coin from the ground at a gallop; ЖАМБЫ АТУ is shooting a suspended target from horseback; ТОҒЫЗҚҰМАЛАҚ is the nine-pit board game with a national federation and rated players; and АСЫҚ АТУ is the knucklebone game. All of them appear at the World Nomad Games. Bоксing is high in the list because Kazakhstan is a genuine boxing power with multiple Olympic golds.
