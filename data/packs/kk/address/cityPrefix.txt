description: Leading element of a Kazakh place name (Жаңа Іле, Үлкен Алматы, Қызылжар). The colour words carry a fixed geographic sense on the steppe rather than a literal one: ақ, white, means clear or fresh water; қара, black, means either a spring-fed source or bare ground with no snow; қызыл, red, is iron-stained earth. Combine with a real toponym; on its own an entry here does not name a settlement.
