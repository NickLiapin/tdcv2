description: Trailing element of a Kazakh place name (Ақтөбе, Қарағанды, Жаңақала, Қызылорда). Almost every one is a landscape word, which is what steppe toponymy is made of: -тау a mountain, -көл a lake, -бұлақ a spring, -сай a gully, -төбе a hill, -құм sand, -жар a cut bank. Two are borrowed and mark trade routes rather than terrain: -кент is Sogdian for town and runs along the old Silk Road in the south (Шымкент, Түркістан's neighbours), and -орда is the Mongol word for a khan's camp (Қызылорда).
locale: kk
