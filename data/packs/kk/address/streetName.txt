description: Street names found across Kazakhstan — writers, composers, khans and batyrs, civic values, geography. Compose before a streetSuffix (Абай даңғылы, Төле би көшесі); the street list of one particular city belongs in a country pack, not here. Two things to know. First, a great many Kazakh streets are named for a person with a TITLE attached that is part of the name and cannot be dropped: Төле би, Қазыбек би, Әйтеке би (би, a judge of the steppe), Қабанбай батыр, Бөгенбай батыр, Наурызбай батыр (батыр, a warrior), Абылай хан, Бұқар жырау (жырау, an epic singer). Second, the post-1991 renamings are visible in the list — Тәуелсіздік (independence), Мәңгілік Ел (the eternal nation), Алаш (the 1917 national movement), Желтоқсан (the December 1986 uprising) — sitting beside Soviet-era survivals like Гоголь and Пушкин, which were largely left alone.
locale: kk
