description: Country as KAZAKH names it, weighted by population in millions. Translated rather than transliterated, and several of the translations are the point: Мысыр is Egypt, a Turkic word older than the Russian Египет and unrelated to it; Ұлыбритания is "great Britain" built from Kazakh ұлы, great; Америка Құрама Штаттары is the United States rendered word for word with құрама, assembled. The Central Asian neighbours all carry the Persian -стан, land of — Өзбекстан, Қырғызстан, Тәжікстан, Түрікменстан, Ауғанстан, and Қазақстан itself. KAZAKHSTAN IS AT ITS REAL WEIGHT OF 20 AND IS NOT BOOSTED, so a plain draw will rarely produce it and a config wanting a Kazakhstan-centred dataset must filter rather than expect the weighting to do it. Country names take case suffixes like any other Kazakh noun and the suffix is chosen by vowel harmony — Түркияда, Ресейден, Қытайға — so a config dropping one into a sentence must inflect it and cannot use a single fixed ending.
