description: Blood group, IN BOTH NOTATIONS, because a Kazakhstani medical card uses the post-Soviet one and an international form uses the other. The local notation writes the ABO group as a ROMAN NUMERAL with the letter in brackets — І (0), ІІ (А), ІІІ (В), IV (АВ) — followed by Rh+ or Rh−, so a real Kazakhstani record says "ІІ (А) Rh+" where a Western one says "A+". A config that parses only the letter form will fail on half the documents in this country, which is why both are shipped.
