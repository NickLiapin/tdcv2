description: Common Kazakh noun. WHERE VOWEL HARMONY BITES: the plural suffix has SIX shapes and the noun chooses one by its last vowel and last consonant — -лар / -лер after a vowel or a sonorant (бала-лар, үй-лер), -дар / -дер after a voiced consonant (жыл-дар, ел-дер), -тар / -тер after a voiceless one (кітап-тар, мектеп-тер). So a config cannot pluralise a Kazakh noun by appending one fixed string; it must either store the plural or implement the rule.
