description: Common Kazakh verb, IN THE -У DICTIONARY FORM. Kazakh has no infinitive in the English sense; the form a dictionary lists is the verbal noun in -у, which is simultaneously "to go" and "going". The vowel of the ending is fixed at -у, but every OTHER suffix the verb takes — tense, person, negation — is chosen by vowel harmony, so бару gives барамын but келу gives келемін, and a config building a sentence cannot append one fixed ending.
