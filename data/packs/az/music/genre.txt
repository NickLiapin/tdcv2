description: Music genre (Azerbaijani). MUĞAM comes first because it is the classical art music of Azerbaijan and a UNESCO intangible heritage item: a modal improvisation cycle sung over tar and kamança, and nothing in a Western genre list corresponds to it. Aşıq is the bardic tradition, meyxana a competitive sung repartee, and the Azerbaijani jazz-mugham fusion of Vaqif Mustafazadə is a genre with a name of its own.
