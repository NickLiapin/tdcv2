description: Dish (Azerbaijani). Plov is the centre of the cuisine and is counted in dozens of named versions, of which şah plov, baked inside a lavash crust, is the ceremonial one; dolma means anything stuffed, from vine leaves to aubergines; qutab is a thin folded flatbread filled with greens, meat or pumpkin. Paxlava, şəkərbura and şorqoğal are the fixed Novruz table.
