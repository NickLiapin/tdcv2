description: Azerbaijani thoroughfare type — like Turkish, Azerbaijani puts it AFTER the name (Nizami küçəsi, Azadlıq prospekti, Səməd Vurğun meydanı). WHERE VOWEL HARMONY BITES: most of these are the bare noun plus the third-person possessive suffix, whose vowel is chosen by the noun's own last vowel — küçə + si, yol + u, meydan + ı, prospekt + i. A config that concatenates a name and a suffix must take the whole surface form from this list, never build one by gluing a fixed ending onto a noun. The prospekt / massiv / bulvar block came in through Russian and marks the sharpest visible break from Turkish, which has cadde and bulvar but no prospekt at all.
