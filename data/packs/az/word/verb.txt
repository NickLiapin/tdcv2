description: Common verb (Azerbaijani, infinitive). THE VOWEL HARMONY IS VISIBLE IN EVERY LINE: the infinitive ending is -maq after a stem whose last vowel is back (a, ı, o, u) and -mək after a front one (e, ə, i, ö, ü) — almaq but bilmək, oxumaq but görmək. Every other suffix in the language behaves the same way, with two forms or four; the plural is -lar / -lər, the locative -da / -də, the accusative -ı / -i / -u / -ü. This is why an Azerbaijani suffix cannot be concatenated blindly onto a stem, and why a config that needs an inflected form must draw the whole surface form from a list rather than build it.
