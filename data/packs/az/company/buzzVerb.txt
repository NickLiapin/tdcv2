description: Business buzz verb (Azerbaijani, infinitive). The infinitive suffix has TWO forms — -maq after a back vowel, -mək after a front one — so optimallaşdırmaq but gücləndirmək. This is vowel harmony at its plainest, and it is why an infinitive cannot be built by appending a fixed ending to a stem.
