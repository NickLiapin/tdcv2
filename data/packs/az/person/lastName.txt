description: Azerbaijani surname, roughly frequency-ordered, with rank-derived Zipf weights rather than registry counts. TWO SYSTEMS SIT SIDE BY SIDE HERE AND THEY CARRY DIFFERENT HISTORY. The great majority of the list ends in -ov / -yev, a Russian genitive ending fixed onto an Azerbaijani given name during the imperial and Soviet periods — Əli became Əliyev, Məmməd became Məmmədov — and this is what most Azerbaijanis carry today. The rest are native: -lı / -li / -lu, usually from a place or a title (Hacıbəyli, Qarabağlı, Naxçıvanlı); -zadə, Persian for born of (Rəsulzadə, Vahabzadə, Mustafazadə); and -oğlu, Turkic for son of, which is rare as a family name because its main job is elsewhere. Both systems are real and current, and a wave of families reverted from -ov to a native form after 1991 without it becoming universal. THE FEMININE FORM IS SHIPPED, in person/female/lastName.txt, and is derived from this file BY SCRIPT line for line: -ov becomes -ova and -yev becomes -yeva, while the -lı, -li, -lu, -zadə and -oğlu names do not change at all, because Azerbaijani itself has no grammatical gender and the inflection is borrowed Russian morphology rather than native agreement. SEPARATELY, the patronymic: an Azerbaijani full name is given name + father's name + oğlu (son of) or qızı (daughter of) — Rəşad Elçin oğlu, Aysel Elçin qızı — which occupies the middle slot of an official record and is not a surname. That patronymic IS sex-marked even though the family name usually is not.
