description: Azerbaijani male given name. Ordered by a plausible frequency, with rank-derived Zipf weights rather than counts from a registry — the state statistics office does not publish a name frequency table, and inventing one would be worse than saying so. Three layers are visible: the Arabic-Islamic core (Məhəmməd, Hüseyn, Ömər, İbrahim), the Persian layer (Bəhram, Firuz, Cahangir, Pərviz, Şəhriyar) and a Turkic revival that gathered pace after 1991 (Altay, Toğrul, Uğur, Turan, Alpay). The El- block — Elçin, Elnur, Elşən, Elmar, Elgün — is a genuinely Azerbaijani twentieth-century coinage on el, meaning the people, and has no Turkish equivalent. The letter Ə appears in a large share of these names and is the single fastest proof this is not a Turkish list.
