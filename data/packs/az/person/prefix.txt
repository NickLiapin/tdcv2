description: Azerbaijani honorific / form of address. Two of these go AFTER the given name, not before it: bəy for a man and xanım for a woman (Elçin bəy, Aysel xanım). A third, MÜƏLLİM — literally teacher — is the everyday respectful address for any educated or senior man and also follows the name (Rəşad müəllim); its feminine is müəllimə. This is a distinctly Azerbaijani usage with no Turkish counterpart. The academic, medical and military titles below all precede the name.
