description: Common Azerbaijani short form of a given name. DELIBERATELY SHORT: Azerbaijani hypocoristics are formed productively rather than drawn from a fixed inventory, so only forms in general use are listed rather than a longer list of plausible-looking constructions. Three patterns account for nearly all of them — a final -o on the first syllable or two (Rəşid to Rəşo, Kamran to Kamo, Hüseyn to Hüso), the diminutive -ş (Məmməd to Məmiş), and the Russian -ik that came in during the Soviet period (Əli to Əlik).
