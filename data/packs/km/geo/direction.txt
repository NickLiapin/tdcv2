description: Compass direction. Khmer names the four intercardinal points with SINGLE inherited Sanskrit words — ឥសាន, អាគ្នេយ៍, និរតី, ពាយ័ព្យ — rather than by compounding the cardinals as English does with north-east, and those four words are not analysable into their neighbours. Khmer has no established sixteen-point rose: there is no Khmer equivalent of "north-northeast", so this list stops at the eight points plus the centre and the vertical pair, and the sixteen-point set is deliberately absent rather than invented.
locale: km
