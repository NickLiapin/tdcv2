description: Fish. Almost every name begins with ត្រី, the word for fish, so the noun and its class marker are inseparable. ត្រីរ៉ស់ (snakehead) is the staple of the Tonle Sap fishery and the fish in ត្រីអាំង; ត្រីម្រឹទ្ធ, the giant barb, is the national fish; the tiny ត្រីរៀល is the fish the currency is popularly said to be named after. Note that Khmer classes whales and dolphins as ត្រី too, so this list is a folk category rather than a biological one.
