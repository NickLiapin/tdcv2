description: Writing system, as Khmer names it. Three entries are Khmer's own styles rather than separate scripts: អក្សរមូល is the heavy rounded ceremonial hand used on temple signs and book titles, អក្សរជ្រៀង the slanted everyday hand, and អក្សរខម the Khmer-derived script used to write Pali in Thai and Cambodian temples. The Khmer script itself is an abugida with the largest alphabet in the world by Guinness' count — 74 letters — and it stacks consonants into clusters with the invisible COENG mark U+17D2.
