description: Colour. Two Khmer facts a config cannot infer. First, ខៀវ is the blue term but still covers green in fixed expressions — ស្មៅខៀវ is green grass, not blue grass — so a Khmer colour column is not a clean partition of the spectrum the way an English one is. Second, Khmer has no separate light/dark colour words: it compounds the base colour with ខ្ចី (light) or ចាស់ (dark), and that pattern is productive for every colour, which is why so much of this list is built that way. Each weekday also has a traditional colour worn on it.
locale: km
