description: Filler word. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Khmer book at all, so there is no public-domain corpus to build this from and the list was written by hand. Khmer is written WITHOUT spaces between words — a space in Khmer marks the end of a phrase or a clause, roughly where English would put a comma. Every value here follows that rule, so a tokeniser that splits on whitespace will return phrases, not words, and a word count taken that way will be wrong by a factor of several. Word segmentation for Khmer needs a dictionary or a model, not a regex. Each entry here is therefore ONE word and contains no space at all.
