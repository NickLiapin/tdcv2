description: Medical diagnosis in the clinical Khmer of Cambodian hospital records. Khmer coins medical vocabulary from Pali and Sanskrit rather than borrowing the English — ជំងឺរបេង for tuberculosis, ទឹកនោមផ្អែម literally "sweet urine" for diabetes — and every name begins with ជំងឺ ("disease"), រលាក ("inflammation of") or មហារីក ("cancer of"), which act as class prefixes. The list leans towards what a Cambodian clinic actually sees: dengue, tuberculosis, malaria and liver fluke sit beside the hypertension and diabetes of an ageing middle class.
locale: km
