description: Generic drug name. These are deliberately written in the Latin INN rather than in Khmer script, and the reason is practical: a Cambodian prescription, a pharmacy shelf label and the national essential-medicines list all print the International Nonproprietary Name in Latin letters, because a wrong drug name kills people and the INN is the one spelling every pharmacist in the country reads the same way. Transliterating them into Khmer here would put a string in the pack that no Cambodian pharmacy would recognise.
locale: km
