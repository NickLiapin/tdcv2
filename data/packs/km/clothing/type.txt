description: Garment. ក្រម៉ា is the one item on this list every Cambodian owns: a checked cotton cloth used as a scarf, a towel, a sun hood, a baby sling and a bag, and it is the national garment. សំពត់ is the wrapped lower cloth and សំពត់ចងក្បិន is the version drawn between the legs to make trousers. សំពត់ហូល is the ikat silk woven for ceremony. None of these has a European equivalent and none should be translated as "skirt".
locale: km
