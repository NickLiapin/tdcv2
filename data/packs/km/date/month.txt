description: Month name. The Khmer solar calendar months are the Gregorian twelve and carry these Khmer names, all of them Pali loans. In a written date they are preceded by ខែ ("month"): ថ្ងៃទី១ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ២០២៦. Khmer ALSO keeps a separate lunar month cycle (មិគសិរ, បុស្ស, មាឃ …) used for religious dates and for Choul Chnam Thmey; those are not these months and are not interchangeable with them.
locale: km
