description: Weekday. Each is the word ថ្ងៃ ("day") plus a planet name of Indian origin, so the Khmer week is the Sanskrit planetary week: ចន្ទ is the moon, អង្គារ Mars, ពុធ Mercury. The week starts on SUNDAY. Every day also carries a traditional colour worn on it — Monday orange, Tuesday purple, and so on.
locale: km
