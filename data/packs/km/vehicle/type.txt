description: Vehicle. Three entries are ordinary in Cambodia and untranslatable elsewhere. រ៉ឺម៉កកង់បី is the Cambodian tuk-tuk: a passenger trailer towed behind an ordinary motorbike, not a three-wheeler like the Thai one. ម៉ូតូឌុប is a motorbike ridden as a taxi. គោយន្ត, the "iron ox", is a two-wheeled hand tractor hitched to a cart and is the commonest farm vehicle in the country.
