description: Government body. Khmer is essentially the language of ONE country, so these are the real ministries and agencies of CAMBODIA rather than generic labels — a Khmer speaker reading "Department of Health" would not recognise it as a name. Every ministry name begins with ក្រសួង, and the pattern is productive. The identifier formats these bodies issue are Cambodia country data and are not in this pack.
locale: km
