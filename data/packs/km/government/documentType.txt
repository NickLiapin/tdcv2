description: Official document type. Two entries have no European equivalent and are central to Cambodian administrative life: សៀវភៅគ្រួសារ, the family book, records a household and is demanded for school enrolment, land dealings and voting; សៀវភៅស្នាក់នៅ, the residence book, records who lives at an address. The number formats these documents carry are Cambodia country data and are not in this pack.
locale: km
