description: Trailing element of a Khmer place name. Half of these are Sanskrit and Pali dignifiers borrowed for towns and provinces — បុរី "city" (from puri), នគរ "capital", សិរី "splendour", មានជ័យ "victorious" — and half are plain Khmer qualifiers of size, colour and direction. NO space joins the parts of a Khmer toponym: this element is written flush against the stem in front of it.
locale: km
