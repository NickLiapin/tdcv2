description: Direction word used with Cambodian district, road and estate names. Khmer has SINGLE inherited Sanskrit words for the four intercardinal points where English has compounds — ឥសាន northeast, អាគ្នេយ៍ southeast, និរតី southwest, ពាយ័ព្យ northwest — and they are not built from the cardinal words at all. អាគ្នេយ៍ is also the everyday Khmer name for South-East Asia. The ខាង- forms are the ordinary spoken ones.
