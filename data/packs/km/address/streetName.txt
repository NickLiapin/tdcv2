description: Real Cambodian road name written bare — the thoroughfare word from address/streetSuffix.txt goes IN FRONT of it, giving ផ្លូវព្រះមុនីវង្ស. Two facts a config needs. First, most Cambodian streets have NO name: Phnom Penh is a numbered grid and an ordinary address reads ផ្លូវលេខ ២៧១, so a realistic generator should emit ផ្លូវលេខ + a number far more often than it draws from this list. Second, the named roads are almost all commemorative — kings (ព្រះមុនីវង្ស, ព្រះនរោត្តម, ព្រះស៊ីសុវត្ថិ), foreign countries and their leaders (សហព័ន្ធរុស្ស៊ី, ម៉ៅសេទុង, ជ្វាហារឡាល់នេរូ), and national figures. The full per-city street inventory, postcodes and province list are Cambodia country data and belong in a Cambodia country pack, not here.
locale: km
