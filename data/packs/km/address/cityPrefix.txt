description: Leading element of a Khmer place name. Cambodian toponyms are transparent compounds built from ordinary words — ភ្នំពេញ is "Penh's hill", កំពង់ចាម is "the Cham landing", ព្រៃវែង is "long forest" — and this list holds the head elements that start them: ភ្នំ hill, ព្រៃ forest, កំពង់ river landing, បន្ទាយ fortress, កោះ island, ស្ទឹង river, ត្រពាំង pond, ទួល mound. NO space joins the parts of a Khmer place name, so this element is written flush against what follows and never names a real place on its own.
locale: km
