description: Khmer female given name. The given name comes SECOND, after the family name, and the family name is drawn from the SHARED km.person.lastName list — Khmer family names do not inflect for gender, and a Cambodian woman keeps her own family name for life; she does not take her husband's. A woman is conventionally given a name meaning beauty, a flower or a light — បុប្ផា "flower", រស្មី "ray", ធីតា "daughter", រតនា "gem". The prefix ស្រី- ("girl") heads a whole family of female names and has no male counterpart. Khmer marks no gender grammatically, so several entries here are also in the male list on purpose. Weights are a nominal decay curve; Cambodia publishes no given-name frequency table.
