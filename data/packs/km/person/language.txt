description: Language, as Khmer names it. The list begins with Cambodia's own languages: ខ្មែរ is the sole official language, and ចាម, ព្នង, ទំពួន, គ្រឹង, ចារ៉ាយ, គួយ and ស្ទៀង are indigenous minority languages spoken inside the country. Khmer names several European languages through French rather than English — បារាំង for French, អាល្លឺម៉ង់ from Allemagne, អេស្ប៉ាញ from Espagne.
