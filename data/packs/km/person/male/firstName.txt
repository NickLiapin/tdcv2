description: Khmer male given name. The given name comes SECOND, after the family name. Khmer given names are overwhelmingly Sanskrit and Pali loans, and a man is conventionally given a name meaning a virtue or a strength — សំណាង "fortune", បញ្ញា "wisdom", ឧត្តម "supreme", រតនៈ "jewel". Khmer has no grammatical gender, so nothing in the WORD marks the name as male; the split is conventional, and a real overlap with the female list is deliberate — សុខា, សុវណ្ណា, ធារ៉ា, ចន្ទ្រា and ពៅ are worn by both. Because a Cambodian family name is normally the father's given name, many of these words also appear in person/lastName. Weights are a nominal decay curve; Cambodia publishes no given-name frequency table.
