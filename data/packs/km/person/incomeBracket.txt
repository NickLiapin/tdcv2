description: Household income bracket, monthly, in US dollars. The dollar is deliberate and not an Americanism: Cambodia runs a dual-currency economy in which the riel (រៀល, KHR) circulates for small change while salaries, rent and prices above a few dollars are quoted and paid in USD. The bands are set at Cambodian levels, not Western ones. Arabic digits are used because that is what a Cambodian payslip prints, though Khmer has its own digits ០១២៣៤៥៦៧៨៩.
locale: km
