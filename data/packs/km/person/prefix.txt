description: Khmer honorific placed before the whole name. Khmer has NO capital letters, so a title is the only visual marker a name carries. លោក / លោកស្រី / កញ្ញា are the everyday Mr / Mrs / Miss. ឯកឧត្តម and លោកជំទាវ are the Excellency pair for a senior official and his wife; សម្ដេច is the highest civil honorific and ព្រះតេជព្រះគុណ addresses a Buddhist monk. អ្នកឧកញ៉ា is a royally conferred title held by business figures and has no European equivalent. Academic and medical titles go BEFORE the name in Khmer, never after it.
locale: km
