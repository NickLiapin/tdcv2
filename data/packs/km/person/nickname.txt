description: Khmer nickname (ឈ្មោះហៅក្រៅ). Nearly every Cambodian carries one and it is used far more than the given name — in the family, at school and often at work. It is characteristically ONE syllable and frankly descriptive: តូច "small", ធំ "big", ធាត់ "fat", ស្គម "thin", ខ្មៅ "dark", ពៅ "youngest". Fruit, gems and animals are the other common source. The ស្រី- forms are given to girls. A nickname is not a shortening of the given name the way Bob is of Robert; it is an independent name.
locale: km
