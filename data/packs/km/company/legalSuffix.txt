description: Cambodian legal form of a company. These come from the Law on Commercial Enterprises and are written AFTER the name, as ក្រុមហ៊ុន សុវណ្ណ ឯ.ក. The abbreviations ឯ.ក and ម.ក are what appears on a signboard and on an invoice. Note that the English "Co., Ltd" and "Plc" are used unchanged on Cambodian bilingual paperwork and are kept here for that reason.
locale: km
