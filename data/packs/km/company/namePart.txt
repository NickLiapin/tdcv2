description: Element of a Cambodian company name. Cambodian firms name themselves after the temples, the rivers and the auspicious Pali words a customer will trust — អង្គរ, បាយ័ន, មេគង្គ, សុវណ្ណ "golden", រុងរឿង "flourishing", ជោគជ័យ "success" — and a name is usually two or three of these run together. NO space joins them in Khmer.
locale: km
