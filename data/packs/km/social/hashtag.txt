description: Hashtag. Khmer hashtags run the words together without spaces, which is not a stylistic choice — Khmer is written without spaces between words anyway, so a Khmer hashtag looks exactly like ordinary Khmer text with a # in front. A tokeniser that splits hashtags on word boundaries will find none here.
locale: km
