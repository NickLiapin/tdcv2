description: Unit of measure. Khmer counts with a CLASSIFIER between the number and the noun, so these words are grammatical requirements rather than optional packaging labels: you cannot say "three shirts" in Khmer without one. Cambodia is metric, but the market still weighs in ជី and តំលឹង — traditional Chinese-derived units of about 3.75 g and 37.5 g — when selling gold, and rice is sold by the ថូ (a tin can of roughly 1.4 kg).
locale: km
