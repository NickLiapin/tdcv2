description: Tree. ដើមត្នោត, the sugar palm, is the national tree and the silhouette on the horizon of every Cambodian rice field: its sap becomes ស្ករត្នោត (palm sugar), its leaves become thatch and its trunk becomes house posts. ដើមពោធិ៍ and ដើមជ្រៃ are temple trees. ក្រញូង, បេង, ធ្នង់ and ស្រឡៅ are Cambodian hardwoods.
