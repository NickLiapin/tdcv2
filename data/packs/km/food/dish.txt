description: Dish. Khmer dishes lead. សម្ល is the word for a soup-stew and heads a whole family (សម្លម្ជូរ sour, សម្លករ coconut-and-kroeung); ខ is a caramel-braise; អាម៉ុក is fish steamed in banana leaf with coconut and kroeung and is the national dish. Note that Khmer writes a dish name with NO internal spaces even when it is four words long.
locale: km
