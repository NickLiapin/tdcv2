description: Music genre. The first block is Cambodian and has no equivalent elsewhere. ភ្លេងពិណពាទ្យ is the court and temple ensemble; ភ្លេងមហោរី the string ensemble; ភ្លេងការ the wedding suite played from dawn; ភ្លេងអារក្ស the music of spirit-possession ceremonies; and រាំវង់, រាំក្បាច់, សារ៉ាវ៉ាន់ and ឡាំលាវ are the four social dances every Cambodian wedding runs through in that order.
