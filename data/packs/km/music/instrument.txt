description: Musical instrument. The Cambodian block leads. ចាប៉ីដងវែង, the long-necked lute of the storyteller-singers, is on UNESCO's urgent-safeguarding list — the Khmer Rouge killed almost every master and the tradition was rebuilt from a handful of survivors. រនាតឯក is the lead xylophone of the pinpeat ensemble, គងវង់ the circle of tuned gongs, ស្រឡៃ the quadruple-reed oboe and ខ្សែដៀវ a one-string bow resonated against the player's chest.
