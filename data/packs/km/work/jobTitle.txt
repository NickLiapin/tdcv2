description: Job title. Khmer builds most occupation names with the agent prefix អ្នក- ("one who"), which is invariant: it marks no gender and no number, so អ្នកបើកបរ is driver, drivers, a man driver and a woman driver alike. A handful of occupations keep older Pali-derived words instead — វេជ្ជបណ្ឌិត, មេធាវី, ចៅក្រម, ចុងភៅ. Several entries here have no European counterpart: អ្នកបើកម៉ូតូឌុប (motorbike taxi) and អ្នកបើកតុកតុក are ordinary declared occupations in Cambodia.
