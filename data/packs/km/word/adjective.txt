---
description: Common adjective. A Khmer adjective FOLLOWS its noun — ផ្ទះធំ is "house big" — and it never agrees with anything, because Khmer marks no gender, number or case. Khmer adjectives also behave as verbs: ផ្ទះធំ can mean "the big house" or "the house is big", and no copula appears in either.
locale: km
