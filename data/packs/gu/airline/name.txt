description: Airline name as written in Gujarati. Airline branding is a proper name and Gujarati prose writes the international carriers in Gujarati script rather than translating them, which is what this list does; the Latin-alphabet form is equally correct in a Gujarati newspaper. The head of the list is the set a Gujarati traveller actually meets, and that ordering reflects the diaspora more than it reflects global market share: the London, Newark, Nairobi and Gulf routes carry a very large Gujarati traffic, so British Airways, Emirates, Etihad, Qatar Airways, Kenya Airways and Air India appear where a European list would put other names.
locale: gu
