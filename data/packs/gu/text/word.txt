description: Common Gujarati content words, for slugs, usernames, tags and short filler. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Gujarati book at all — the language search returns "No records found" and gutenberg.org has no Gujarati language page — so this list was written by hand rather than mined from a corpus by data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs the way the en, pl and ru filler is. That is worth knowing when comparing locales: some have a real corpus behind their filler and this one does not. TWO GRAMMATICAL WARNINGS. Nouns are in the bare citation form, and Gujarati nouns carry THREE genders — masculine, feminine and a fully live neuter, which distinguishes Gujarati from Hindi — plus number and a stack of case postpositions, so ઘર in a real sentence appears as ઘરમાં, ઘરનું, ઘરે or ઘરો. Verbs are given as the verbal noun in -વું, the form a Gujarati dictionary and a Gujarati headline both use; a finite Gujarati verb agrees in person, number AND gender, and past-tense transitive clauses are ergative (છોકરાએ પુસ્તક વાંચ્યું — the verb agrees with the neuter book, not with the boy). A config that concatenates these words will produce readable but frequently ungrammatical Gujarati, and that is a limit of word lists, not of the data.
