description: Role-playing character class in Gujarati. These are translations into Gujarati rather than transliterations of English, because Gujarati has a perfectly good vocabulary for warriors, archers, sages and thieves and a fantasy game localised into Gujarati uses it. યોદ્ધા, ધનુર્ધર, તપસ્વી, માયાવી and રાક્ષસ all carry epic-literature weight, since the Mahabharata and Ramayana are the reference frame a Gujarati reader brings to a fantasy setting.
