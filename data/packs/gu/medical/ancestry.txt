description: Genetic ancestry group, used as the parent of diagnosisByAncestry. These are ancestry populations as CLINICAL GENETICS defines them — geographic descent, which is what carrier frequencies actually track — and not social race or caste categories. The distinction matters especially in an Indian locale: caste endogamy over many generations has produced real founder effects and real differences in carrier frequency between Indian communities, but caste is not what this list encodes and a config must not treat દક્ષિણ એશિયાઈ as a caste field.
