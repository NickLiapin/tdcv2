description: ABO and Rh blood group. Note the Indian distribution for anyone generating realistic proportions: B positive is much commoner in India than in Europe — around a third of Indians — and O positive is the single largest group, while Rh-negative blood of any type is rare, roughly five to six per cent of the population, which is why negative donors are chased so hard by Indian blood banks. A config using European frequencies for an Indian dataset will get this visibly wrong.
