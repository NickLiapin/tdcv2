description: General medical diagnosis in Gujarati. The disease mix is Indian rather than European and the difference is visible: infectious disease is far more prominent — મેલેરિયા, ડેન્ગ્યુ, ચિકનગુનિયા, ટાઇફોઇડ, ક્ષય, કમળો and લેપ્ટોસ્પાયરોસિસ are all ordinary monsoon-season diagnoses in Gujarat, not tropical curiosities — alongside a very heavy non-communicable burden of diabetes, hypertension and early coronary disease. મુખનું કેન્સર is unusually high for the reason given in medical/diagnosisByAncestry. સિલિકોસિસ is here because Gujarat's agate-grinding and stone-quarrying trades, and Surat's diamond-polishing units, produce it. Written in Gujarati, but note that a real Indian case sheet is as likely to carry the English name — Gujarati medical prose is bilingual — so a config modelling clinical records rather than patient-facing text may want the English form instead.
