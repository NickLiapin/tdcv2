Written as transliterations rather than as Gujarati coinages, and that is the honest register: Indian medicine is taught and practised in English, a Gujarati hospital's departmental signboard says કાર્ડિયોલોજી, and a Gujarati patient asks for the કાર્ડિયોલોજી વિભાગ. Gujarati translations exist for some of these (હૃદયરોગ, ચેતારોગ, ત્વચારોગ) and appear in health journalism and government pamphlets, but nobody navigates a hospital by them.
