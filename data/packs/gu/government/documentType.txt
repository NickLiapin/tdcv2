description: Kind of official document, in Gujarati. These are the NAMES OF KINDS of document, not particular Indian identity schemes — this pack deliberately writes no Aadhaar, PAN, voter-ID or passport-number data, and any named Indian document scheme along with its number format belongs in the India country pack. Two entries are Indian categories with no European counterpart and are worth knowing about: રેશન કાર્ડ is the household food-subsidy card and doubles as the commonest proof of address for poor households, and ૭/૧૨ ઉતારો is the land record extract — the single most important document in rural Gujarat, showing who holds a field and what is grown on it, and the thing every land dispute in the state turns on. જાતિનું પ્રમાણપત્ર is a caste certificate, required for reserved-category admissions and jobs.
