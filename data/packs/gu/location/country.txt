description: Country names as Gujarati writes them. Most are respellings of the international name in Gujarati script, but several are TRANSLATIONS or independent Indic names and a config must not romanise them back into English: ભારત is India's own name and is what Gujaratis actually say; અમેરિકા, યુનાઇટેડ કિંગડમ and રશિયા sit beside the older forms; શ્રીલંકા, નેપાળ, ભૂતાન, બાંગ્લાદેશ, મ્યાનમાર and માલદીવ are Indic names, not English ones; ચીન comes through Sanskrit and not through English "China"; અફઘાનિસ્તાન, ઈરાન, તુર્કી and મિસ્ર arrived through Persian and Arabic centuries before any English contact — મિસ્ર is Egypt and shares nothing with the English word. યવન and other archaic names are deliberately not used.
