description: Gujarati family names. UNLIKE Tamil and Telugu, these are REAL INHERITED SURNAMES: a Gujarati child takes the father's family name, it passes down unchanged through the generations, and two rows sharing પટેલ can be read as kin. So a config may inherit this field down a family tree, which the Tamil and Telugu packs explicitly forbid. THE CONVENTION FOLLOWED HERE is the ordinary Gujarati name of record, which has THREE parts: given name + father's given name + family surname, as in નરેન્દ્ર દામોદરદાસ મોદી or મોહનદાસ કરમચંદ ગાંધી. This file holds only the THIRD part; the middle part is drawn from person/male/firstName.txt, because it is literally the father's given name. A married woman conventionally replaces the middle part with her HUSBAND's given name while keeping or taking his surname, so the middle element moves on marriage and the surname does not. THERE IS DELIBERATELY NO person/female/lastName.txt: Gujarati surnames DO NOT INFLECT FOR GENDER. પટેલ, શાહ and દેસાઈ are identical for a man and a woman, unlike Polish Kowalski/Kowalska or Russian Ivanov/Ivanova, because these names are nouns of community, occupation or place rather than adjectives agreeing with their bearer. Its absence is the design, not an oversight. WHAT THE NAMES ENCODE: most are community names, and Gujaratis read them that way instantly. પટેલ and અમીન are the Patidar landholding community and are the state's commonest surnames by a wide margin. શાહ, ઝવેરી (jeweller), દોશી (cloth merchant), સંઘવી (one who has led a pilgrimage), કોઠારી (storekeeper), વોરા, ચોકસી (assayer) and કાપડિયા (cloth) are the Vania trading castes and overwhelmingly Jain. જોશી (astrologer), ત્રિવેદી (knower of three Vedas), ઉપાધ્યાય, પુરોહિત, દવે, વ્યાસ, ભટ્ટ and પંડ્યા are Brahmin. ચૌહાણ, સોલંકી, વાઘેલા, ઝાલા, જાડેજા, ગોહિલ, ચાવડા and પરમાર are the Rajput clan names of Saurashtra and Kutch, and are ALSO carried in large numbers by Koli and Dalit families who took them. સુથાર (carpenter), લુહાર (blacksmith), સોની (goldsmith), દરજી (tailor), કુંભાર (potter), મોચી (cobbler) and ઘાંચી (oil presser) are occupational. The -વાળા / -વાલા group (નડિયાદવાળા, મીઠાઈવાલા, દારૂવાલા) means "the one of/who deals in" and attaches to a town or a trade; it is a live formation and produced most Parsi surnames — ઈરાની, વાડિયા, દસ્તૂર, એન્જિનિયર, કોન્ટ્રાક્ટર, ભરૂચા and કાત્રક are Gujarati-speaking Parsi names, not English ones. વસાવા, ગામીત, રાઠવા, ડામોર and તડવી are Adivasi names of eastern Gujarat, about fifteen per cent of the state. ગડા, છેડા, દેઢિયા, સાવલા and નાગડા are Kutchi Visa Oswal Jain. વોહરા, મેમણ, મન્સૂરી, મલેક and ઘાંચી are Gujarati Muslim. The -િયા Saurashtra cluster (સાવલિયા, ધામેલિયા, કાકડિયા, વેકરિયા) is the Patidar stock that built Surat's diamond trade. THE DIASPORA carries these names unchanged: Leicester and Wembley in Britain, the American motel trade, and East Africa before the 1972 Uganda expulsion, so a dataset of Gujaratis outside India uses this same list. THE WEIGHTS ARE AN APPROXIMATE ORDERING, NOT MEASURED FREQUENCIES — India publishes no surname census, so nothing here is a real count. The one share that is solid is the shape of the head: પટેલ is far and away the commonest Gujarati surname and is set well above the second, and the rest decay gently because Gujarat genuinely has no single dominant lineage after it.
