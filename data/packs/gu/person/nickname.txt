description: Gujarati household nicknames — the name a person is actually called at home, which in Gujarat is very often not the registered name at all. This is a real and separate naming layer: a child gets a હુલામણું નામ within the family and may be known by it lifelong to relatives and neighbours, while the school register carries something else entirely. Three patterns are visible here. The -ઉ / -ઓ / -ઈ diminutives (બકો, ટપુ, ચીકુ, મુન્નો, ગુડ્ડી, પિન્કી) are the ordinary ones. Descriptive nicknames are common and blunt in a way English no longer is — ભૂરો is "the fair one", કાળુ "the dark one", નાનો "the small one", મોટો "the big one", ગોટી "round". And clipped forms of a real given name (રાજુ from Rajendra, જીતુ from Jitendra, દીપુ from Dipak, મનુ from Manilal) are how most adult nicknames arise. Note that -ભાઈ and -બહેન attach to nicknames too: રાજુભાઈ is entirely normal.
locale: gu
