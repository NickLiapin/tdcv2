description: Marital status in Gujarati, in the words an Indian form uses. પરિણીત / અપરિણીત is the standard pair. Two entries are specific and a config should not translate them loosely: સગાઈ થયેલ is engaged, and in Gujarat the engagement (સગાઈ) is a formal, publicly announced status that can last a year or more, so it appears on forms as a real category rather than as a private understanding; નાતરું is the Gujarati term for the customary remarriage of a widow or a separated woman, historically practised among several Gujarati communities and still a live word. છૂટાછેડા લીધેલ is divorced.
