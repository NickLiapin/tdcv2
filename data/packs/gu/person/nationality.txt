description: Nationality or demonym, as Gujarati writes it. The order is not the world's — it is the GUJARATI DIASPORA's, which is one of the largest and oldest of any Indian language group and is the reason this list is worth reading. After ભારતીય come Britain (Leicester and north-west London are heavily Gujarati), the United States (where Gujaratis, and Patidars in particular, came to own a large share of the independent motel trade), Canada and Australia. Then the East African countries — Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania — because Gujarati traders settled the Swahili coast generations before the British arrived; Uganda expelled its Asians in 1972 and most of that community went to Britain and Canada, which is why the British and East African entries belong together. Then Oman, the UAE and the Gulf, where Kutchi and Gujarati merchants have traded for centuries; South Africa, where Gandhi practised for twenty-one years; and Portugal and Mozambique, which received Gujaratis from Daman, Diu and the former Portuguese territories. Fiji, Mauritius, Suriname and Trinidad follow the indenture routes.
