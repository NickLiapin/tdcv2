description: Gujarati female given names across four living generations. THE HONORIFIC: the female counterpart of -ભાઈ is -બહેન (often written -બેન and pronounced "-ben"), and it attaches to the given name exactly the same way — હીરાબહેન, ગીતાબેન, કોકિલાબહેન. As with men, the bare given name is the record form and the -બહેન form is the ordinary spoken and written one; a Gujarati newspaper naming a woman in a local story will almost always use it. A married or older woman may also take -બા (કસ્તૂરબા, હીરાબા), a more respectful form used for mothers and grandmothers. Both are Gujarati-specific and a config that never appends them will produce names that read as clipped. NOTE ON THE MIDDLE NAME: a woman's name of record also has three parts, but the middle one moves — it is her FATHER's given name before marriage and her HUSBAND's given name after, while the given name itself never changes. See person/lastName.txt; there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt because Gujarati surnames do not inflect for gender. THE GENERATIONS, and this list is more stratified by age than the male one. કસ્તૂર, પુતળી, ઝવેર, મણિ, દિવાળી, સંતોક, મોંઘી, જડાવ, સમજુ, ઉજમ and ચંચળ are names of women born before about 1940 — short, often meaning a thing or a quality (દિવાળી is the festival, ઝવેર is a jewel, સંતોક is contentment, મોંઘી is "precious"), always heard with -બા or -બહેન; કસ્તૂરબા was Gandhi's wife and પુતળીબાઈ his mother. The -આ and Sanskritic layer (કોકિલા, શારદા, સવિતા, ચંદ્રિકા, મંજુલા, પુષ્પા) belongs to women born 1940-1975. હેતલ, શીતલ, સોનલ, રૂપલ, કિંજલ, પાયલ and હિરલ — the -લ ending — are a distinctly Gujarati female shape and peak for women born 1970-1995. ધ્વનિ, ધ્રુવી, અવની, હેત્વી, ક્રિષા, જિનલ and દેવાંશી are the current generation. ટ્વિંકલ and ડિમ્પલ are genuine registered Gujarati names, not nicknames. RELIGIOUS AND COMMUNITY STOCK: અંજના, રાજુલ, ચંદનબાળા, મૃગાવતી, સુલસા, પદ્માવતી and પ્રિયદર્શના are Jain and come from the Jain narrative tradition rather than from Sanskrit poetry; ગંગા, જમના and નર્મદા are rivers, and નર્મદા in particular is Gujarat's own; પિરોજા, શિરીન, ધુન, ફ્રેની, ખોરશેદ and હુતોક્ષી are Parsi, and Parsi women of Gujarat carry a Persian given name with a Gujarati surname; the closing set is Gujarati Muslim. WEIGHTS are a plausible ordering, not a count — India publishes no given-name register. The decay is gentle for the same reason as the male list: Gujarati has no dominant female name.
