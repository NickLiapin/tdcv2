description: Spice name in Gujarati. હિંગ heads the list for a reason: asafoetida is the pivot of Gujarati cooking, and in Jain and many Vaishnav kitchens it does the job that onion and garlic do elsewhere — it is what makes a dal without alliums taste finished. The standard Gujarati વઘાર (tempering) is mustard seed, cumin, asafoetida and curry leaf in hot oil, poured over the dish at the end. ધાણાજીરું is a pre-mixed coriander-cumin powder sold as one item in Gujarat and used by the spoonful. Two are not spices at all but belong with them in a Gujarati kitchen: ગોળ (jaggery) and લીંબુનાં ફૂલ (citric acid crystals), because the sweet-sour balance is the defining move of the cuisine.
