THIS IS A LARGELY VEGETARIAN LIST AND THAT IS NOT SQUEAMISHNESS — Gujarat has the highest rate of vegetarianism of any Indian state, roughly six in ten people, and among Jains, Vaishnav Vanias and much of the Patidar community it is near universal, so meat dishes are genuinely rare on a Gujarati table. THE SWEET-SALT SIGNATURE: Gujarati savoury cooking puts jaggery or sugar into dal, into shaak and into kadhi, which is the single thing other Indians notice first about it and which a config describing a Gujarati dish should not treat as an error.
