description: Trailing element of a Gujarati place name. These are the real toponym endings of Gujarat and they are readable as history rather than as decoration. -પુર and -નગર are the everyday Sanskritic "town" endings. -આબાદ is Persian and arrived with the Gujarat Sultanate — અમદાવાદ is Ahmad's abad — and marks a Muslim-era foundation. -ગઢ is a fort (જૂનાગઢ) and -કોટ its Gujarati cognate (રાજકોટ), both clustering in Saurashtra where every small state had one. -બંદર means port and marks the coast (પોરબંદર); Gujarat has India's longest coastline and the ending is commoner here than anywhere else in the country. -પાટણ is the old capital word (પ્રભાસ પાટણ, અણહિલપાટણ). -વાડા, -પુરા, -પરા and -વાસ are quarters within a settlement rather than settlements themselves, and are what a neighbourhood is called. Combine with a real toponym; a generated combination does not name a real place, and the actual towns of Gujarat belong in the India country pack.
