description: Leading element of a Gujarati place name. Combine with a real toponym; on its own it does not name a real city. The pairs of opposites — નવા/જૂના, મોટા/નાના, ઉપલા/નીચલા — are the commonest and produce genuine Gujarati town names (જૂનાગઢ is "old fort", નવસારી, મોટા ફોફળિયા). વડ is a productive Gujarati element meaning "great" or "banyan" and gives વડોદરા, વડનગર and વડતાલ. જામ marks a town founded under the Jadeja rulers of Halar (જામનગર, જામખંભાળિયા). Note that Gujarati place-name prefixes inflect: the form here is the one used before a noun.
locale: gu
