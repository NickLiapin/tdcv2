They are used, not archaic: a Gujarati builder or estate agent will describe a plot as ઈશાન ખૂણો and expect to be understood, because Vastu rules assign a function to each corner (the kitchen belongs in અગ્નિ, the water tank in ઈશાન). The hyphenated compounds are also in use in plain modern prose, so both sets are here.
