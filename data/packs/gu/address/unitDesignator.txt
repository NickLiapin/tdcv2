description: Secondary address unit designator in Gujarati. Note how a Gujarati address is actually built, because it is not the European order: it runs SMALLEST FIRST — flat number, then wing, then building, then society, then road, then area, then city — and the society name usually does more work than the road name. પાંખ is the "wing" of a housing block (A પાંખ, B પાંખ) and is used constantly. સર્વે નં. and બ્લોક નં. are land-record numbers that appear in rural and peri-urban addresses. મુકામ and પોસ્ટ are the village-address pair: a rural Gujarati address gives મુકામ (the village you are at) and પોસ્ટ (the post village that serves it), and the two are often different places.
