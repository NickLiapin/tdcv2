description: Legal form suffix on a company name, in Gujarati usage. These are the forms Indian company law recognises and a Gujarati firm writes them in Latin abbreviations even in Gujarati prose — "ગુજરાત ટેક્સટાઇલ્સ પ્રા. લિ." is the ordinary written shape. Two are worth flagging: સહકારી મંડળી (cooperative society) is a heavyweight form in Gujarat rather than a marginal one — the state's dairy sector, Amul included, is built on village cooperatives, and so is most of its urban housing — and હિન્દુ અવિભક્ત કુટુંબ (HUF) is a distinctively Indian legal person, a joint Hindu family taxed as a single entity, with no equivalent anywhere in Europe.
