description: Colour name in Gujarati. Several of these are named for a substance rather than for a hue and a config translating them literally will lose the sense: કંકુ is the vermilion powder of the tilak, હળદરિયો is turmeric-yellow, મહેંદી is the green of henna paste, ગેરુ is the ochre of a monk's robe, કેસરી is saffron, ચંદની is moonlight-pale, and અબીલ and ગુલાલ are the powders thrown at Holi. કેસૂડી is the orange of the flame-of-the-forest flower, which is what Holi colour was made from in Gujarat before synthetic dye. મોરપીંછ is peacock-feather blue-green and is a Krishna word before it is a colour word. Note that Gujarati colour adjectives INFLECT for the gender and number of the noun they describe — લાલ does not, but પીળો becomes પીળી and પીળું — so a config joining a colour to a noun must agree them.
locale: gu
