description: Hotel amenity, in Gujarati. Two are locally decisive and a Gujarati traveller checks for them first. શુદ્ધ શાકાહારી રસોડું — a purely vegetarian kitchen, with no meat cooked anywhere on the premises — is the single most advertised feature of hotels catering to Gujarati families, and જૈન ભોજન (Jain food, no roots or alliums) is offered as standard by many. Note also that Gujarat has been a DRY STATE since 1960: the sale and consumption of alcohol is prohibited, so a Gujarati hotel has no bar and a minibar is not a normal amenity here, which is why neither appears on this list.
