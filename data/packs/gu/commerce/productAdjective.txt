description: Adjective used on a product listing, in Gujarati. NOTE THE INFLECTION, because it will break naive string joining: Gujarati adjectives ending in -ઓ agree with the noun and become -ી for feminine and -ું for neuter — નવો ફોન, નવી ઘડિયાળ, નવું ઘર. The forms here are the masculine singular, which is the citation form. Adjectives ending in a consonant (સુંદર, મજબૂત, હલકું is neuter) do not change. A config that concatenates one of these onto commerce/productNoun.txt will produce Gujarati that is understandable but visibly ungrammatical about half the time; that is a real limitation of a word-list approach to an inflecting language, not a defect in the data.
