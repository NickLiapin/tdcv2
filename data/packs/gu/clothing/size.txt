Indian ready-made clothing is sold on the S/M/L scale in Latin letters, exactly as printed on the label, and a Gujarati shop assistant says "એલ" and "એક્સ એલ" out loud. Note that most traditional Gujarati women's wear is not sized at all — a sari is a single uncut length of cloth and a ચણિયાચોળી is tailored to the wearer — so a config dressing a Gujarati row in traditional clothing should not attach a size to it.
