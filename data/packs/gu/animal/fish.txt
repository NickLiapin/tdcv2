description: Fish name in Gujarati. Gujarat has India's longest coastline and its largest marine catch, so this list is a working one rather than an ornamental one. બુમલા is Bombay duck, dried in the sun along the Valsad coast; ઘોલ is the protonibea croaker whose swim bladder makes it the most valuable fish landed in India; પાપલેટ is pomfret and સુરમઈ is seer fish, the two premium table fish of the coast. Note the cultural counterweight: a very large share of Gujaratis — most Jains, most Vaishnav Vanias and many Patidars — are strictly vegetarian and never eat any of these, so the coastal fishing economy and the inland diet sit side by side.
