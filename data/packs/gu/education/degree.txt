description: Academic degree, in Indian Gujarati usage. Indian degree abbreviations are written in Latin letters even in Gujarati prose — a Gujarati CV says B.Com., not a transliteration — which is why they appear that way here. B.Com. leads the list deliberately: commerce is by a wide margin the most-taken undergraduate stream in Gujarat, in a state whose economy is trade and where a family business is the normal destination. B.A. (Hons) and the Western "bachelor/master" wording are not used; the Indian forms are.
