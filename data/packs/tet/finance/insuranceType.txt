description: Insurance types, and two of them show Tetum keeping its own word where the surrounding term is borrowed: dental cover is seguru nehan, from nehan a tooth, and vision cover is seguru matan, from matan an eye — the same matan that appears in matan dook, the seer, literally the far-seeing one. The pattern is general in this pack: body parts, kinship, food and land stay Austronesian while the administrative frame around them is Portuguese. What the list overstates is take-up. Insurance of any kind is rare outside Dili and outside the formal employment sector, health care is provided free at state clinics, and the customary safety net that actually absorbs a household's losses is the obligation between houses rather than a policy.
