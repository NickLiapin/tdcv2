description: Weekday abbreviations. Tetum cuts to three letters and, as with the months, writes no closing full stop. Kua and Kin carry the k that replaced the Portuguese qu of quarta and quinta, so the substitution survives the clipping, while Ter would hide the s of Tersa and is therefore written Ters here. Sáb keeps its acute because the stress it marks falls inside the clipped form.
