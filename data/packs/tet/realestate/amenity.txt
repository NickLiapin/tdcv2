description: Property features, and the two most Timorese entries replace the fireplace and the central heating a Portuguese list carries, which no house in this climate has ever needed. Jerador is a generator and Tanke be'e a water tank, and both are advertised as selling points because both public utilities are intermittent: mains water reaches a minority of households and reaches them for part of the day, so a tank and a pump are what make a house liveable rather than what make it luxurious. Fatin tunu is the barbecue area, built on tunu, the Tetum verb for roasting over a fire, which is how a pig is cooked at every ceremony. Xaun ai is a wooden floor, from Portuguese chão with ch written x, plus ai, the Tetum for wood or tree.
locale: tet
