description: Hotel amenities, and two of them are advertised in Timor-Leste for reasons a European list would not produce. Jerador reserva, a standby generator, is a headline feature rather than a footnote: the national grid runs on heavy-fuel-oil plants at Hera and Betano and outages are ordinary, so a hotel that can keep the lights and the air conditioning on says so first. Ekipamentu mergullu is here because diving is the country's real tourism product — the reefs off Atauro island were measured to hold the highest average reef-fish diversity of any site surveyed anywhere, and the dive operators along the Dili waterfront are what brings most leisure visitors in. Matabixu is worth its own note: it is breakfast, and it comes from Portuguese mata-bicho, kill-the-beast, an old colloquialism for the first thing eaten or drunk in the morning that Tetum kept long after Portugal let it fade.
