description: Adjectives for a product listing. Two structural facts govern the whole file. The modifier follows the noun, as it does in Portuguese but unlike English, so the composition is kadeira dobrável and never dobrável kadeira; the same order produces uma boot, a big house, and uma lulik, a sacred house. And the adjective is invariable — no gender agreement, no plural agreement, no participle to match — so the single form here fits any noun in the productNoun file, which is exactly the caveat the pt version of this file has to make and this one does not. The native block leads: boot and ki'ik for large and small, naruk and badak for long and short, foun and tuan for new and old, di'ak for good, kmanek for handsome or fine, todan and mamar for heavy and soft, metin for firm. Nonook is native too and means quiet, doing the work of silencioso. Everything after that is Portuguese and the -ável and -ível endings come across untouched because they were already invariable in the source. Note that many of these describe things a config would not think to advertise elsewhere and that matter here: rezistente, impermeável and durável are selling points in a monsoon climate where the wet season runs half the year.
locale: tet
