description: Fantasy character classes, a vocabulary that reached Tetum entirely through screens and therefore shows the borrowing rules working on words no colonial administrator ever used. Guerreiro becomes gerreiru because Tetum writes the hard g of gue- as plain g and drops the silent u; caçador becomes kasadór with c to k, ç to s and an acute on the stressed final syllable; feiticeiro becomes feitiseiru; engenheiro becomes enjeñeiru with the g before e going to j and nh written ñ. Xamán keeps its x for the sh sound, which is the same x that turns chave into xave. Kavaleiru Mate is the death knight, and mate is Tetum's own word for death, so the compound is half borrowed and half not — a pattern this pack shows again and again.
locale: tet
