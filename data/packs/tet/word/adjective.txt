description: Everyday adjectives, and the balance of the file is the point: where a Tetum word exists it stands here instead of the Portuguese one, so cold is malirin and not friu, dark is nakukun and not eskuru, heavy is todan, light in weight is kmaan, sharp is kroat, quiet is nonook, wild is fuik, and big and small are boot and ki'ik. Only where the concept is abstract or came in with the administration does the Portuguese loan hold the slot — kapás, onestu, modernu, simples. Two entries show Tetum building meaning by compounding rather than by borrowing: laran-di'ak is kind, literally inside-good, from laran the interior of a thing, and foin-sa'e is young, literally newly-risen, the same sa'e that gives Timór Lorosa'e its rising sun. A Tetum adjective never agrees with its noun, because the language has no grammatical gender and marks plural separately with sira, so every form here is invariable and follows the noun rather than preceding it.
locale: tet
