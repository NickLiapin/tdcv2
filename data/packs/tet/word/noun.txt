description: The commonest nouns, and the split between the native and the borrowed half of the vocabulary is visible at a glance. The body, the household, the land and the things a person handles are Austronesian and old — ema person, uma house, be'e water, matan eye, liman hand, loron day, tinan year, mota river, osan money, fatin place, dalan road, labarik child, naran name. The abstractions that arrived with an administration are Portuguese — sistema, rezultadu, razaun, nivel, valór, nasaun. One entry deserves its own look: liafuan, the word for a word, is a compound of lia, speech or a matter spoken, and fuan, a fruit or a seed, so a word in Tetum is literally a seed of speech, and the same lia opens lisan, custom, and lian, language or voice. None of these nouns inflects for number; plurality is added, when it needs adding at all, by putting sira after the noun.
