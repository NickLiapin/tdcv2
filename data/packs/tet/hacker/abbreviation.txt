description: Technical abbreviations, and this is the one file in the pack where Tetum borrows nothing at all — it keeps the Latin-letter acronyms whole, because the English source phrases arrived here through the UN administration and the aid sector rather than through Portuguese. Only the last three would expand into Tetum: IA is Inteligénsia Artifisiál, BD is Baze Dadus and SO is Sistema Operativu, and each of those expansions shows the pack's usual respellings, with c before e and i going to s in inteligénsia and artifisiál, and the final -o of operativo going to -u. Anyone building Timorese IT records should know that the third loanword layer is thickest exactly here: an office in Dili discusses a network in English nouns held together by Tetum grammar.
