description: Technical adjectives. Two entries here are built out of Tetum rather than borrowed and they are the ones worth noticing. Wireless is la iha kabu, literally does-not-have cable, because Tetum negates with the particle la placed before the verb and has no prefix answering to Portuguese sem- or English un-. Open source is kódigu nakloke, from nakloke, open — a native word carrying a foreign idea. High-speed and high-performance both use a'as, the Tetum for high or tall, placed after the noun, because a Tetum modifier follows what it modifies: velosidade a'as is speed-high, not high-speed. The rest are Portuguese loans on the standard rules, digital to dijitál with an acute on the stressed final syllable, óptico to ótiku, encriptado to enkriptadu.
locale: tet
