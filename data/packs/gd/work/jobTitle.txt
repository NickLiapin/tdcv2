description: Job title in Gaelic. The productive pattern in modern Gaelic is NEACH- plus a verbal noun in the genitive — neach-lagha "person of law", neach-teagaisg "person of teaching", neach-naidheachd "person of news" — and it is used wherever no older single word exists; NEACH is grammatically neuter in effect, so the same title serves a man or a woman, which the older BEAN- and FEAR- compounds did not. The older single words are kept where they are alive: GOBHA the smith, SAOR the joiner, TÀILLEAR the tailor, GREUSAICHE the shoemaker, CÌOBAIR the shepherd, MUILLEAR the miller, BÀRD the poet — a job Gaelic society formally recognised. CROITEAR is a crofter, a legal status rather than an occupation.
