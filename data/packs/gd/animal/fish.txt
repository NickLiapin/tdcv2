description: Fish. Gaelic fish vocabulary is a fishing vocabulary and separates ages and runs that English lumps together: BRADAN is the salmon and BÀNAG the grilse returning after one winter at sea, SAIDHEAN the full-grown saithe and CUDAIG the young one, which are different fish to anybody who has caught them. SGADAN, the herring, was the economic centre of the Hebrides for a century.
