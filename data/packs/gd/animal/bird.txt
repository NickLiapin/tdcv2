description: Bird. The list is weighted towards seabirds and moorland birds because that is what the Gaelic-speaking coast has and what the vocabulary is exact about: SÙLAIRE the gannet, SGARBH the cormorant, BUTHAID the puffin, CROTACH the curlew, NAOSG the snipe, TRAON the corncrake. CAILLEACH-OIDHCHE, the owl, is the "old woman of the night"; BRÙ-DHEARG, the robin, the "red belly"; GOBHLAN-GAOITHE, the swallow, the "fork of the wind".
