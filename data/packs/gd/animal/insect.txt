description: Insect. MEANBH-CHUILEAG is the Highland midge and is the single most consequential insect in the Gaelic-speaking world; there is no reason for it to be buried in the middle of a list, so it is named here. CREITHLEAG is the cleg or horsefly, TARBH-NATHRACH the dragonfly ("serpent's bull"), GOBHLACHAN the earwig ("little forked one").
