MADADH-RUADH and SIONNACH are both the fox — the first is "red dog", and the doubling is normal in Gaelic, where the animal often has a descriptive name beside a plain one. MUC-MHARA, the whale, is the "sea pig"; DAMHAN-ALLAIDH, the spider, the "wild little ox"; DEALAN-DÈ, the butterfly, the "fire of God".
