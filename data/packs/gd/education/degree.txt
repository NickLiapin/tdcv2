THESE ARE THE SCOTTISH QUALIFICATIONS, which are not England's: a school leaver here sits NATIONAL 5 and then HIGHERS (ÀRD-ÌREAN) and ADVANCED HIGHERS, not GCSEs and A-levels, and a Scottish honours degree takes four years rather than three. The MA at ancient Scottish universities is a first degree and not a postgraduate one, which is a trap for anyone reading a Scottish CV. Gaelic names the degrees with BÀIDSEALAIR, MAIGHSTIR and DOTAIR.
