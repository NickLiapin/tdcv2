description: Kind of property. TAIGH-CROITE is a croft house and TALAMH CROITE croft land, and neither is a house or a field in the ordinary sense: a croft is a tenancy regulated by the Crofting Acts, it is bought and sold as a tenancy, and a config that treats it as freehold property has the law wrong. TAIGH-DUBH, the blackhouse, is the old drystone thatched dwelling with the byre under the same roof.
