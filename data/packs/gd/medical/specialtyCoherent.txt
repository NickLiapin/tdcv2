description: Medical specialty, parent of medical/diagnosisBySpecialty. Gaelic names a field of study with EÒLAS, "knowledge", plus the organ in the genitive — eòlas-cridhe is heart-knowledge, eòlas-àirnean kidney-knowledge — the same pattern that gives bith-eòlas for biology and speur-eòlas for astronomy. Psychiatry is the one that breaks it, being LEIGHEAS-INNTINN, "the healing of the mind", because it treats rather than studies. These are transparent Gaelic compounds and not internationally registered names: Gaelic has no body that standardises clinical terminology, so where a Gaelic word exists this pack uses it and where none exists it says so rather than coining one.
