description: Musical instrument. PÌOB-MHÒR is the great highland bagpipe and CLÀRSACH the wire-strung Gaelic harp; both are older in this language than in any other and neither is a translation of anything. FEADAN is the practice chanter, the instrument a piper actually learns on, and FEADAG the whistle. BOGSA-CIÙIL, the "music box", is the accordion, which is the backbone of a modern cèilidh band. The orchestral and popular instruments are spelt as Gaelic borrows them.
