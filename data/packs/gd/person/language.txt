description: Language a person speaks, named in Gaelic. Worth reading for what it holds that a larger language's list does not: Gaelic names ALL SIX Celtic languages separately and does not confuse them — GÀIDHLIG is this language, GAEILGE its Irish cousin, GAELG Manx, CUIMRIS Welsh, CÒRNAIS Cornish, BREATNAIS Breton — and it distinguishes ALBAIS (Scots, the Germanic language of lowland Scotland) from BEURLA (English), which English itself usually does not. Most names are formed with the -IS suffix on the country adjective: Fraingis from An Fhraing, Gearmailtis from A' Ghearmailt.
