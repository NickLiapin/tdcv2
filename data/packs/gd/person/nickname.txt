description: Byname or pet form. Two kinds are mixed here because Gaelic mixes them. The first is the hypocoristic, made with -AG, -IDH or -AIDH on a shortened stem (Iain gives Iagan and Seonaidh, Murchadh gives Murdaidh, Gilleasbaig gives Eairdsidh, Màiri gives Màili). The second is the DESCRIPTIVE BYNAME, which is not a joke name but the working identifier in a township where a dozen men share a name: Iain Bàn, Dòmhnall Ruadh, Calum Beag. Bynames of the second kind are adjectives and follow the given name directly.
