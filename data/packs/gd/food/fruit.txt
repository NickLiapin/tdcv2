description: Fruit. The first eight grow here and have old native names — UBHAL, PEUR, SÙBH-LÀIR, SÙBH-CRAOBH, BRAOILEAG (the blaeberry of the hill), SMEURAN (brambles), DEARCAG-DHUBH, DEARCAN-CAORAINN (rowan berries). The rest are imported and their names are borrowed with them; ÒR-MHEAS, "gold fruit", for the orange is the one borrowing Gaelic made into a compound of its own instead.
