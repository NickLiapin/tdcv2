MIN-CHOIRCE (oatmeal) and MIN-EÒRNA (barley meal) come before wheat flour here because that is the order they mattered in: oats and barley grew in the Highlands and wheat did not. GRUTH is crowdie, the fresh soft cheese made in every croft house, and BLÀTHACH the buttermilk left from churning.
