ALBANNACH and GÀIDHEALACH are kept apart on purpose and are not the same list: Albannach is Scottish cooking as a whole, while Gàidhealach is specifically Highland and Hebridean and holds things a Lowland kitchen has never had — GUGA, the salted young gannet taken once a year from Sula Sgeir by the men of Ness; DUILEASG, the dulse seaweed eaten as food; CEANN-CROPAIG, a fish head stuffed with liver and oatmeal.
