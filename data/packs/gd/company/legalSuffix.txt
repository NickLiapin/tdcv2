description: Legal form of a business, as a company registered in the Gaelic-speaking area carries it. These are UNITED KINGDOM and SCOTTISH forms and not international ones: there is no Inc, no GmbH and no SA here, and SCIO (Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation) and the community-benefit society exist only under Scottish law. EARRANTA is the Gaelic for "limited", and CO-CHOMANN a co-operative — the Hebridean community land trusts are constituted this way, which is why the mutual forms are not a footnote on this list.
