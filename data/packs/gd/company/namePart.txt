description: An element to build a company name from. Gaelic firms are overwhelmingly named for a PLACE or a NATURAL FEATURE — the loch, the headland, the island, the hill behind the township — which is why so much of this list is landscape vocabulary rather than abstract nouns. Compose two or three with the genitive and an article and you get a name shaped like a real one.
