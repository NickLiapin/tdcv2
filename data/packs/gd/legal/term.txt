description: Legal term. NEO-DHEARBHTE is the Scots verdict of NOT PROVEN — a third verdict open to a Scottish jury and to no English one, acquitting the accused while declining to say they are innocent. It is the clearest single sign that this list belongs to a different legal system rather than being an English one translated.
