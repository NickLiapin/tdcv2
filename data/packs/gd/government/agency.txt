description: Public body, named as Gaelic names it. Scottish Gaelic is effectively a one-country language, so these are SCOTTISH institutions rather than generic labels, and several exist nowhere else: BÒRD NA GÀIDHLIG is the statutory Gaelic language body created by the Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005, MG ALBA the Gaelic media service that funds BBC ALBA, STÒRLANN the Gaelic educational resource agency, and COIMISEAN NA CROITEARACHD regulates crofting tenure, which exists only in the seven crofting counties. The bodies that are NOT devolved — the tax office, the passport office, the driver agency, the benefits agency — are United Kingdom institutions and belong in the united_kingdom country pack, not here.
