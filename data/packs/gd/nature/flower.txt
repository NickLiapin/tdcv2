description: Flowering plant. CLUARAN and FÒGHNAN are both the thistle, the national emblem. Several carry a story rather than a description: LUS NAM BAN-SÌTH, the foxglove, is the "plant of the fairy women"; BRÒG NA CUTHAIG, the bluebell, the "cuckoo's shoe"; LUS-A'-CHROM-CHINN, the daffodil, the "plant of the bent head". CANACH is bog cotton and MURAN marram grass — both are moor and machair plants with no ordinary English name.
