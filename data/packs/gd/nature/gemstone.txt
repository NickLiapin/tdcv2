description: Gemstone. Only three of these are Gaelic words in their own right: CAIRNGORM is the smoky quartz of the Cairngorm mountains and the Gaelic CÀRN GORM, "blue cairn", is where the English mineral name came from rather than the other way round; NEAMHNAID is the pearl; FINICHE is jet. The rest are the international mineral names adapted to Gaelic spelling, which is what a Gaelic geology text does — a language with no diamond mine does not have a native word for a diamond.
