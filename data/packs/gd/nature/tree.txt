description: Tree and shrub. Every one of the first fifteen is also a LETTER: the old Gaelic alphabet named its eighteen letters after trees — Ailm the elm, Beith the birch, Coll the hazel, Dair the oak, and so on — which is why the tree words are the oldest stratum of the language and why there are exactly as many native trees with one-word names as there are letters. FRAOCH, CONASG and BEALAIDH are moorland shrubs rather than trees and are here because they are what actually grows.
