description: Kind of building. TAIGH is the general house-word and forms most of the compounds by hyphen plus a genitive noun (taigh-òsta, an inn; taigh-tasgaidh, a treasure-house or museum; taigh-solais, a light-house), which is Gaelic's ordinary way of naming an institution. TAIGH-DUBH, the blackhouse, and TAIGH-CROITE, the croft house, are specific Highland building types with no English one-word equivalent.
