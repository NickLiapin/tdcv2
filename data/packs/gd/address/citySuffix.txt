description: The qualifier that closes a Gaelic place name, to follow address/cityPrefix.txt: Baile Mòr, Achadh nan Craobh, Cnoc an Fhraoich. Two shapes are mixed, and they behave differently. The ADJECTIVES (Mòr, Beag, Bàn, Dubh...) must AGREE with the generic in front: after a masculine one they stand as written (Baile Mòr, Dùn Dubh), after a feminine one they lenite (Cill Mhòr, Beinn Bheag, Creag Bhàn). The GENITIVE PHRASES beginning nan/nam/an/na do not change at all and are safe after any generic. A config that ignores the distinction produces half-correct place names, so the split is recorded here rather than left to be discovered.
locale: gd
