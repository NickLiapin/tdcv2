They are aristocratic house endings — ދީދީ, މަނިކު, ފުޅު, ތަކުރުފާނު — carried by a minority of families descended from the old ranked classes, and they sit after the father's name (އަޙްމަދު ޢަލީ ދީދީ). Most Maldivians have none. No academic post-nominals: Maldivian degrees are written as a prefix (ޑރ.) instead.
