description: Colour name (Dhivehi). Dhivehi marks a colour word with the suffix -ކުލަ ('colour') for the borrowed and derived shades but leaves the four oldest — ރަތް, ނޫ, ކަޅު, ހުދު — bare, which is the fastest way to tell an inherited Indo-Aryan colour term from a later one. ފެއްސާ / ފެހި is green, and ރީނދޫ (yellow) is literally the turmeric root.
locale: dv
