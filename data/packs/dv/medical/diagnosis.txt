description: Diagnosis (Dhivehi), taken from the Dhivehi medical vocabulary used by the Maldives health service. Dhivehi builds most disease names transparently — ދުޅަވުން is inflammation, ރިހުން pain, ބަލި illness — so a Dhivehi diagnosis reads like its own definition. ޑެންގީ ހުން, ޗިކުންގުންޔާ and ސްކްރަބް ޓައިފަސް are the tropical ones a Maldivian record actually shows.
