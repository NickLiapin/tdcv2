description: Job title (Dhivehi). Maldivian job titles are mostly English loans written in Thaana, because the modern professions arrived with English-medium schooling; the exceptions are the old ones — ކެޔޮޅު (master fisherman), މާލިމީ (navigator), ވަޑިޔާ (boat carpenter), ފޫޅުމާމަ (midwife), ކަތީބު (island chief) — which are pure Dhivehi and have no loanword competitor.
locale: dv
