description: Fish (Dhivehi). This is the strongest list in the pack and the reason is economic: tuna is the Maldives' export and the reef its larder, so Dhivehi names fish the way other languages name trees. ކަޅުބިލަމަސް is skipjack, the national fish; ފާނަ is grouper, ހަނދި trevally, ރައިވެރިމަސް snapper, ބިބީ butterflyfish and ފެހުރިހި the whale shark.
