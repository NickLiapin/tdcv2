description: Kind of court (Dhivehi). Dhivehi is the state language of one country, so these name the courts of the Maldives: a Supreme Court, a High Court, four superior courts in Malé and a magistrate court on each inhabited island. There is no separate constitutional court — the Supreme Court holds that jurisdiction.
