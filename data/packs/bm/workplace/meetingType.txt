description: Kinds of meeting, in Bambara, on one root: `ɲɔgɔnye`, from `ɲɔgɔn` one another plus `ye` to see — a meeting is a seeing-of-each-other, and the word covers everything from two colleagues to a village assembly. `Ɲɔgɔnye ba` is the big one, the general assembly. `Kunnafoni` is information, so `Baara kunnafoni` is the briefing. `Atelier` is the one value left in French: it is the development sector's word for a workshop, it is said in French inside otherwise Bambara sentences all over Bamako, and it carries a specific format — per diems, a hotel room, a restitution at the end — that the Bambara word would not convey.
locale: bm
