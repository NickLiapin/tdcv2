description: What a Malian lodging advertises. Most of it is Bambara because most of it is ordinary life — `ji kalan` hot water, `nakɔ` a garden, `kɔlɔsibaga` a night guard, `fini koli` laundry, `sɔgɔmafana` breakfast, `sosofini` the mosquito net in a country where malaria is the leading cause of death in children. `Kuran masin` is the generator and `tilekuran` solar, and they matter more than anything else on the list because the grid load-sheds; the air conditioner is worthless during a coupure. `Sunɔgɔyɔrɔ san fɛ` is a place to sleep on the roof, which is a real and unembarrassed line on a Malian price list from March to May. Six values stay French: Climatisation, Wi-Fi, Télévision satellite, Piscine, Terrasse, Salle de sport and Navette aéroport name imported machinery and hotel fittings that arrived under those names and have no Bambara word, and putting a coined one here would invent vocabulary rather than record it.
locale: bm
