description: Teaching jobs, and `karamɔgɔ` heads the list because it is the older and wider word: it means a master of learning, and it covers the teacher of a Qur'anic school as naturally as a schoolteacher — the medersa network teaches a very large share of Malian children and its masters are karamɔgɔw, not instituteurs. `Lakɔlikaramɔgɔ` is the state-school version, built on `lakɔli` from l'école. The French titles are the grades of the national system, defined by a diploma and a decree: an `Instituteur` teaches the école fondamentale, a `Professeur de lycée` the secondary, and the distinction is a pay scale rather than a description, which is why it stays in the language the pay scale is written in.
locale: bm
