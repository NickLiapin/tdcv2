description: Hashtag words a Malian post actually carries, given without the # so a template can add it. They are written in the French spelling of Malian names rather than in the official Bambara orthography, and that is deliberate and true to practice: a hashtag has to be typeable, and ɛ ɔ ɲ ŋ are not on a default phone keyboard, so #Segou reaches people and #Segu does not. The same pressure has kept Malian written Bambara online in an improvised French-based spelling for twenty years, quite separate from the 1967 standard taught in literacy classes. `MaliKura` — the new Mali — is a real and heavily contested political slogan rather than a tourism tag.
