description: Senegalese food, which reaches Mali by two routes that have run for a very long time: the Dakar-Bamako railway, built by 1904 and the main artery of the colony, and the enormous Malian community in Senegal. `Thiéboudienne` — rice, fish and vegetables cooked in one pot — is Senegal's national dish and a Bamako restaurant fixture. `Mafé` is the same groundnut sauce Malians call tigadɛgɛna, which is a fair illustration of how little the border means to a kitchen. `Yassa`, onions and mustard and lime, is the other export that has travelled everywhere.
