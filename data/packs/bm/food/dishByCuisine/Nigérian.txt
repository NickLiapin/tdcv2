description: Nigerian food, which reaches Mali the way Nigerian films and Nigerian music do: through trade, through Nollywood on television, and through the enormous cultural weight of a country of two hundred million next door but one. `Jollof rice` is the same one-pot rice dish Senegal calls thiéboudienne and Mali calls riz au gras, and the argument about whose version is best is a genuine and cheerfully ferocious West African internet institution. `Suya` is the spiced grilled beef skewer sold by Hausa butchers, and it has travelled up into Mali under that name.
