description: Lebanese food, and it is on this list for a reason that goes back a century. Lebanese and Syrian traders began arriving in French West Africa in the 1890s and built a lasting merchant community in Bamako, Ségou and Sikasso — in import-export, textiles, hardware and construction — and their descendants are Malian. The restaurants followed, and `chawarma` is now so thoroughly a Bamako street food that a Malian would not necessarily think of it as foreign. `Kebbé` is kibbeh, bulgur and lamb; `manakich` the thyme flatbread eaten at breakfast.
