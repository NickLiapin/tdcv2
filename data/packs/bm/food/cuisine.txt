description: Cuisines, as a Bamako restaurant or delivery listing names them — the parent of food/dishByCuisine, so every value here has a file of that exact name beside it. The list is a portrait of who came to Mali and why. `Libanais` is high because Lebanese traders have been in Bamako since the 1890s and their descendants are Malian merchants; `Chinois` because Chinese construction firms built the bridges and the crews brought cooks; `Turc` because Turkey has expanded into West Africa hard since the 2000s and flies here daily; `Marocain` and `Algérien` because the Sahara is a road and not a wall; and the West African entries because Malians migrate to Abidjan, Dakar and Lagos in enormous numbers and the cooking comes home with them. `Peul` is the odd one and the most interesting: it is not a foreign cuisine but an internal one, the milk-based diet of Mali's own herding people, genuinely distinct from the millet-and-sauce kitchen of the farmers around them.
