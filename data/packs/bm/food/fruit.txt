description: Fruit, with the Bambara names first, and several of them are gathered from wild trees rather than grown. `Sira` is the baobab and also its fruit, the dry white pods the French call pain de singe, whose powder is stirred into water and is extraordinarily high in vitamin C. `Zaban` is Saba senegalensis, a forest liana fruit sold in heaps at the roadside in the rainy season and eaten sour with salt. `Ntomi` is tamarind and `Woro` the kola nut, which is not really eaten as fruit at all: kola is a stimulant, a gift, and a formal instrument — a marriage proposal is carried by kola, and a bag of it opens negotiations. `Mangoro` is the one Mali sells: mango is a serious export crop out of Sikasso and Bougouni, and the season floods the country with fruit so cheap it is given away.
locale: bm
