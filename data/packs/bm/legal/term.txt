description: Legal vocabulary, and this file is the clearest place in the pack to see the two-register problem. The first six values are Bambara and they are the words a dispute is actually argued in: `sariya` law, `kiiri` judgement or trial, `kiitigɛla` the one who cuts the judgement, `seere` witness, `jalaki` accusation or blame, `bɛnkan` the agreement that settles it. Those words handle the customary conciliation that resolves most Malian conflicts. The moment the matter enters a state court, the register switches entirely to the French below, and the litigant needs an interpreter to follow proceedings about his own land. `Médiation coutumière` at the end is where the two systems are formally stitched together.
