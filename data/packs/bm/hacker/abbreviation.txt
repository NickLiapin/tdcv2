description: Technical abbreviations, used unchanged inside otherwise Bambara text. That is not a lapse and not laziness — it is what Malian technical writing does, exactly as the Serbian pack keeps these in Latin letters inside Cyrillic prose. Bambara has no computing register of its own and no standards body producing one, so an ICT trainer in Bamako teaching a class in Bambara says API and CPU in English letters and builds the sentence around them with Bambara postpositions. The abbreviations also survive a keyboard problem that Bambara words do not: they contain no ɛ, ɔ, ɲ or ŋ, so they can be typed on any phone in the country.
locale: bm
