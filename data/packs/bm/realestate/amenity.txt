description: What a Malian property advertises, in Bambara, and the top of the list is utilities rather than luxuries. `Ji bɛ so kɔnɔ` — water is in the house — is a feature because a great many properties have neither piped water nor a connection, and the alternative is the `Kɔlɔn`, the well in the yard, or a `Forazi`, a borehole. `Kuran masin` and `Tilekuran` are the generator and the solar array, and they are what make a house usable where the grid load-sheds. `Kogo lamininen` and `Da nɛgɛma` are near-universal: the Malian house faces inward onto its own courtyard and shows a blank wall to the street, so the wall is the building rather than an extra. `Ɲɛgɛn` is the latrine. Three values stay French — Climatisation, Terrasse, Balcon — as fittings that arrived with concrete construction and are advertised under those names.
