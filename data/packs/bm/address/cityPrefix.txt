description: The opening element of a Malian place name, and it works the opposite way round from the Serbian list. A Manding compound puts the head last, so in Torokorobougou the bugu (hamlet) is the head and everything before it modifies it — the hamlet under the fig tree. That means the value here is never the settlement word, it is the thing the settlement is named for, most often a tree, a water feature, a stone or an animal. Capitalised because it opens the name, and it joins the suffix with no space between them. Alone none of these is a real town.
locale: bm
