description: Building types as a Malian address meets them. The unit that matters is the du, the walled family compound holding several houses round one swept courtyard, and a residential address in Mali points at a du rather than at a flat. So is a house and bon a single room or hut, both ordinary Bambara; bugu is the straw shelter that gave every Bamako quarter ending in -bougou its name. The rest are French, because schools, banks, factories and hospitals arrived with the colonial administration and kept its vocabulary, reshaped to Bambara sound — lakɔli from l'école, lopitali from l'hôpital, farimasi from pharmacie, magazɛn from magasin.
