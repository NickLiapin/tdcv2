description: The closing element of a Malian place name, written lowercase because it attaches directly to the prefix with no space — Sira plus bugu is Sirabugu, one word. These are the heads of the compound and they are almost all settlement or landscape nouns, which is why the list carries no adjectival endings the way a Slavic one would. Bugu is a hamlet of straw huts and is the commonest of all, so much so that a dozen Bamako quarters end in it; kɔrɔ is old or under, kura is new, ba is big and ni is small, and the pair kɔrɔ and kura sort the older half of a settlement from the newer. On a French map these appear as -bougou, -coro and -coura, which is the same morpheme in a different spelling system. Mali has almost no equivalent of the French river qualifier that closes a European place name, so none are invented here.
locale: bm
