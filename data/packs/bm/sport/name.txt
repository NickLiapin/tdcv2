description: Sports played in Mali, headed by the Bambara word for the one that matters. `Ntolatan` is football — ntola ball, tan to kick — and its dominance is hard to overstate: the Bamako derby between Stade Malien and Djoliba fills the Stade du 26 Mars, the Aigles' matches empty the streets, and every open patch of ground in the country has a goal scratched onto a wall at one end. Basketball is the surprise second, with a women's national team that has won Africa and a Bamako academy feeding the American college system. `Lutte traditionnelle` is the old one, wrestling at village festivals with its own drumming. `Awalé` and `Dames` are here because the board games played all afternoon under a mango tree are competitive sports in Mali with federations and national championships, and Mali has been a serious force in international francophone `Scrabble` — a genuinely unlikely national speciality.
