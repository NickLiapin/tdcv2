description: Where a Malian household's money goes, in Bambara. Three lines have no European counterpart. `Denbaya ɲɛnajɛw` is family ceremonies, and it is not discretionary: a naming ceremony, a marriage or a funeral obliges contributions from a wide circle of kin and the sums can dwarf a month's income. `Bolomafara` is the contribution itself, literally what is added from the hand. `Wari cili somɔgɔw ma` is money sent to relatives, which runs both ways — to the village from the city, and to the household from a brother in Abidjan or Paris, where for many families it is the largest line in the budget. `Tontin` and `Nisɔngɔ` are the two loans on the list: the first is the rotating savings club, the second is tax, and both are said in these forms rather than in any older Bambara word.
locale: bm
