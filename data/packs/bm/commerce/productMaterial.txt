description: Materials, and the Malian ones are worth picking out of the list. `Bazin riche` is imported cotton damask, dyed and then pounded with wooden mallets until it shines like metal — the beating is a trade of its own and the sound of it is a Bamako courtyard sound. `Bogolan` is mud cloth, where the pattern is what the iron-rich river mud leaves behind. `Banco` is sun-dried mud brick, the material of the Great Mosque of Djenné, the largest mud-brick building on earth, replastered every year by the whole town in a single day. `Cauris` are cowrie shells, which came across the Indian Ocean and the Sahara and served as Mali's currency into the nineteenth century before becoming ornament and divination tokens.
