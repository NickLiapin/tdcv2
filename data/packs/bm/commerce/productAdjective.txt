description: Adjectives for a product listing, in French, the language of Malian commerce. Three of them carry weight a European reader would miss. `Occasion` and `Reconditionné` describe a huge share of the goods actually sold here — clothes, phones, cars and refrigerators arrive in Mali second-hand by container and are resold, repaired and resold again, and second-hand is a normal category rather than a discount one. `Importé` is a selling point rather than a caveat, because local industry is thin and imported usually means better made. And `Fait main` and `Artisanal` mean something specific: the leather, silver, cloth and forged iron of the artisan castes, sold in the Maison des Artisans and by the roadside.
