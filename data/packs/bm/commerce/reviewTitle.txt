description: Review headlines in Bambara, and they are sentences because that is what a review is — Bambara has no habit of nominalising a verdict into a noun phrase the way French headlines do. Several show the qualitative construction that word/adjective describes: `A ka ɲi` is it is good, with `ka` marking the qualitative, and its negative is not a different word but `A man kan a sɔngɔ ma`, it is not equal to its price. `Feerekɛla ka ɲi` — the seller is good — is the highest praise on the list and the most Malian: with no escrow, no returns policy and no consumer court worth the trouble, what is being rated is the person, and a seller's reputation is the entire guarantee.
locale: bm
