description: Departments of a Malian shop or market, in French. Two entries would not appear in a European list and matter here. `Pagnes et bazin` is a department in its own right because cloth is bought by the pagne — a fixed length, sold uncut — and then taken to a tailor, so the fabric trade and the clothing trade are separate businesses; the bazin end of it, imported damask dyed and beaten to a shine in Bamako courtyards, is a serious industry. `Agriculture et élevage` is the other, covering seed, fertiliser, veterinary products and the hand tools that four Malians in five need for a living. Note how much of the list is hardware and construction: Bamako is growing fast and a very large share of urban retail is building materials.
