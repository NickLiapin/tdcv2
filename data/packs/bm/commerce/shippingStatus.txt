description: Where a parcel has got to, in Bambara, and two values are Mali's geography written into a workflow. `Bilalen mɔbili kɔnɔ` — put inside the vehicle — is the real dispatch event here, because the intercity coach lines are the national parcel network: you hand a box to the bagagiste, telephone the recipient with the plate number, and they collect it that evening. `Sera Bamakɔ` is a milestone of its own because Bamako is the only entry point that matters and a parcel for Gao travels south-west before it starts north-east. `Duwan na` keeps the naturalised loan from French douane, the customs step that consumes the time in a landlocked country whose imports clear after a thousand-kilometre road corridor. `Cibaga` is the sender, from `ci` to send plus the agent suffix -baga.
