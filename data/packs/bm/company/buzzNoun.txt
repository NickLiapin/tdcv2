description: Institutional nouns in French. The second half of this list is a project cycle rather than a vocabulary — `diagnostic`, `étude de faisabilité`, `plan d'action`, `feuille de route`, `cadre logique`, `indicateur`, `suivi-évaluation`, `restitution`, `comité de pilotage` — and those terms are as familiar in Bamako as any commercial word, because the development sector is a genuine and large white-collar employer and because state programmes are written in the same forms. `Renforcement des capacités` is the phrase that has travelled furthest: it is capacity building, it appears in almost every funded activity in the country, and Malians use it ironically as often as sincerely. `Tontine` and `microfinance` at the financial end are not jargon at all but how most Malians actually save and borrow.
