description: Sectors of the Malian economy, in French, and the order reflects the real weight rather than a European template. Agriculture and livestock come first because they occupy roughly four Malians in five: cotton run through the CMDT out of Koutiala and Sikasso, irrigated rice in the Office du Niger scheme at Niono, millet and sorghum everywhere, and a national cattle herd that is one of Africa's largest. Gold follows, because Mali is among the continent's top three producers — industrially at Loulo-Gounkoto, Syama and Fekola, and artisanally through `Orpaillage`, which employs several hundred thousand people with pickaxes, mercury and no regulation to speak of. `Transport fluvial` is a sector here and nowhere in Europe: the Niger is a highway for four months a year. `Organisation non gouvernementale` closes the list as a genuine employment sector rather than a category error.
