description: Elements a Malian company name is actually built from — compose with a legalSuffix. The Bambara block at the head is not decoration: real Malian firms, cooperatives, radio stations and NGOs are named `Jigiya` hope, `Benkadi` harmony, `Badenya` the solidarity of children of one mother, `Ɲɛtaa` progress, `Yiriwa` development, `Lafiya` well-being — these are the country's commonest business names and a config that reaches instead for French abstractions is producing a different country. Then the trees and rivers, because `Balanzan` the Ségou tree and `Joliba` the Niger are on signboards everywhere. Then the towns. Then the history: `Sunjata`, `Mansa`, `Kirina` where the empire was won in 1235, `Kurukanfuga` where its charter was proclaimed, `Wagadu` the older Ghana empire, `Sankoré` and `Djingareyber` the Timbuktu mosques, and the caste names `Jeli`, `Numu` and `Donso`. The French block at the end is the register of banks and insurers, which is a different kind of company entirely.
