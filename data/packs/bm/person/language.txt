description: Languages, beginning with Mali's thirteen national languages under their own names rather than under French labels — bamanankan, bomu, bozo, dɔgɔsɔ, fulfulde, hasanya, mamara, maninkakan, soninke, songhoy, syenara, tamasheq and xaasongaxanŋo, the Khassonké of the Kayes region, whose spelling puts three of Bambara's four special letters on display at once. The constitutional position changed recently and sharply: the 2023 constitution made the national languages the OFFICIAL languages of Mali and demoted French to a working language, reversing sixty years in which French alone had that status. Bamanankan is the one that matters practically — it is the first language of perhaps a third of Malians and the second language of most of the rest, so two strangers from opposite ends of the country will very likely meet in Bambara rather than in French. Names are lowercase because Bambara does not capitalise language names, and the world languages that follow are given in French, which is how they are named in Malian schooling.
