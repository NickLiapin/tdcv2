description: Malian short names and terms of address, which in practice are what people are actually called. Two habits fill this list. The first is the `tɔgɔma`, the namesake: a child named after a living relative is that person's tɔgɔma, and the two address each other by the relationship rather than the name for the rest of their lives, which is why so many Malians are known by a syllable of a name they share with somebody. The second is that French kinship words have become ordinary Malian names — `Tonton` and `Tantie` for any adult man or woman of the parents' age grade, `Vieux` and `Petit` fixed on particular people from childhood, `Papa` and `Baba` for a father and then for a man who is nobody's father. `Djeli` as a nickname marks somebody from a griot family, and `Mady` is the near-universal short form of Mamadou.
locale: bm
