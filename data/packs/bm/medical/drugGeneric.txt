description: Generic names as a Malian pharmacy dispenses them: by DCI — dénomination commune internationale, the French rendering of the INN — printed in French on the box. The list follows Mali's national essential medicines list rather than a European formulary. Artéméther-luméfantrine is first-line for uncomplicated malaria and artésunate for the severe form. Sulfadoxine-pyriméthamine with amodiaquine is not an ordinary prescription at all: it is the monthly seasonal malaria chemoprevention handed to every under-five through the rainy months, one of the largest such campaigns anywhere. Praziquantel and ivermectine likewise move by mass administration round, not by prescription. Hydroxyurée is here for sickle-cell disease, managed at the CRLD in Bamako, and sels de réhydratation orale with sulfate de zinc is the standard pairing for a child with diarrhoea.
