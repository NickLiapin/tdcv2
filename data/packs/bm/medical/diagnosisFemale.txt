description: Diagnoses that require female anatomy, so a config should draw from this file only on female rows — mixed into medical/diagnosis it will hand a man an obstetric complication. The obstetric half is where Mali's burden actually sits: maternal mortality is among the highest in the world, fertility is close to six children per woman, and anaemia in pregnancy is close to universal, which makes hémorragie du post-partum, prééclampsie and éclampsie ordinary entries rather than rare ones. Fistule obstétricale is on the list because prolonged obstructed labour without a caesarean still produces it and Mali runs dedicated repair campaigns for it. The CSRéf, the district referral centre, is assessed on precisely these emergencies.
