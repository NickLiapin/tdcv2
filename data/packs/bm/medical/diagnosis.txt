description: Diagnoses in French, which is not an oversight: Mali's medical register is French. Doctors train in it, the Hôpital Gabriel Touré and the Point G write their notes in it, the CSCom and the CSRéf run on French forms and the national health information system reports in it — while the patient describes the illness in Bambara, as bana and dimi and farigan. A Malian consultation is routinely interpreted across that gap in the room, which is why this file is French and medical/symptom.txt is Bambara. The caseload is Mali's, not a European one: paludisme leads because malaria is the first cause of both consultation and death, followed by severe acute malnutrition, acute respiratory infection and diarrhoeal disease; méningite is here because Mali sits inside the African meningitis belt and has a defined epidemic season; schistosomiase tracks the Niger and the Office du Niger irrigation scheme; and drépanocytose is a national priority with a dedicated centre, the CRLD in Bamako. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale and diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry.
locale: bm
