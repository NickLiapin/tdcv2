description: The one ancestry file that describes almost every row in a Malian dataset. Sickle-cell disease is the anchor: both HbS and HbC are West African variants, and HbC in particular is concentrated in this region — Mali runs a dedicated national centre for it, the CRLD in Bamako. The Duffy-negative red-cell phenotype is here as a resistance rather than a disease; it makes Plasmodium vivax malaria almost unable to establish, which is why essentially all Malian malaria is falciparum. Read the whole list as a prevalence signal, never an exclusive one: anyone can carry any of these, and most patients of this ancestry have none of them.
