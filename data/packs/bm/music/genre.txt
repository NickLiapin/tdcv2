description: Malian music, which is one of the things the country is unambiguously first at. `Jeliya` is the art of the griot — hereditary praise-singing, genealogy and history, performed by the nyamakala families whose names are Kouyaté, Diabaté, Sissoko and Soumano — and `fasa` is the praise song itself. `Wasulu` is the music of the Wassoulou country south of Bamako, sung mostly by women over the six-string kamalen ngoni, and Oumou Sangaré made it international. `Takamba` is the Songhai music of the Niger bend and `assouf` the Tuareg guitar style that the world calls desert blues. `Balani show` is the Bamako street phenomenon, a small balafon played through a PA at deafening volume for the young at a wedding, and `sumu` is the wedding itself. `Donsoya` is the hunters' music, initiatory and half-secret. The imported genres at the end are real but they arrive into a country that exports more music than it imports.
