Common Armenian words for slugs, usernames and tags, in the bare dictionary form. Three things a config should know. Every Armenian letter is TWO bytes in UTF-8 (the block sits at U+0530-U+058F), so a byte-limited column holds about half the characters it would in English — less punishing than Georgian or Devanagari, which take three. The ligature և is ONE character but uppercases to TWO, so any word containing it changes length when cased — and Unicode's default mapping gives ԵՒ where reformed Eastern Armenian writes ԵՎ. And the definite article and the possessives are SUFFIXES, so the same word appears as գիրք, գիրքը, գիրքս, գիրքդ, գրքի, գրքից — six strings, one lemma, which is what a slug generator drawing bare stems is deliberately avoiding.
