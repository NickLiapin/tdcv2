description: Element of an Armenian company name. Armenian firms name themselves after mountains, kingdoms, rivers and fruit far more than after their founders: Արարատ, Մասիս, Արագած, Նաիրի, Ուրարտու and Անի are on shopfronts in every town. Compose with company/legalSuffix, remembering that the name goes inside «» and the form after it.
locale: hy-am
