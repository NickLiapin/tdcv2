description: Garment, in Armenian. տարազ is the Armenian national costume and is not a museum word: it is worn at weddings, by dance ensembles and on Vardavar, and appears in clothing catalogues as its own category. Note that Armenian distinguishes վերնաշապիկ (a buttoned shirt) from շապիկ (a t-shirt), where English uses 'shirt' for both.
locale: hy-am
