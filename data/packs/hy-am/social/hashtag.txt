description: Hashtag in Armenian. Hashtags work in the Armenian script exactly as in Latin. Note that the ligature և (U+0587) appears inside ordinary Armenian words and its UPPER CASE IS TWO CHARACTERS — so a platform that uppercases a hashtag for matching will change its length, and #Հայևմիշտ becomes eight characters where the original had seven. The two characters are also contested: Unicode's default mapping gives ԵՒ while reformed Eastern Armenian writes ԵՎ (see world/writingSystem.txt).
locale: hy-am
