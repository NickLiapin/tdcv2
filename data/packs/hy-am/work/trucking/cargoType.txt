description: Freight type, in Armenian. Armenia is LANDLOCKED with the Turkish and Azerbaijani borders closed, so almost all road freight goes north through the single Upper Lars pass into Georgia — a mountain road that snow closes for days at a time. That is why սառնարանային (refrigerated) and շուտ փչացող (perishable) freight are the categories Armenian hauliers talk about most.
