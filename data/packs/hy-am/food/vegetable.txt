description: Vegetable, in Armenian. ավելուկ and սիբեխ are the two that make this list Armenian: they are wild greens — mountain sorrel and a chervil relative — gathered in spring, plaited into ropes and hung to dry for winter soups, and they are sold that way in every Armenian market. There is no English word for either that a shopper would recognise.
