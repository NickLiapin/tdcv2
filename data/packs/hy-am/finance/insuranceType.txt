description: Kind of insurance, in Armenian. ԱՊՊԱ is the one every Armenian driver knows by its initials — compulsory motor third-party liability, introduced in 2011 and the only insurance most households carry. Note what is NOT compulsory: Armenia has no universal mandatory health insurance, so առողջության ապահովագրություն is an employer benefit rather than a fact of life.
