description: Colour name, in Armenian. ծիրանագույն ('apricot-coloured') is the point of this list: it is the official name of the third band of the Armenian flag, fixed by the law on the state flag, and no other national flag names a colour after a fruit. Armenian builds most colour words with -գույն ('-coloured') on a noun — վարդագույն from վարդ 'rose', երկնագույն from երկինք 'sky', ոսկեգույն from ոսկի 'gold' — so a colour word in Armenian is usually transparently the thing it is the colour of.
locale: hy-am
