Most breed names are internationalisms taken through Russian and written in Armenian; վանա կատու is not — the Van cat is the white, odd-eyed, swimming cat of Lake Van, in the historically Armenian highland now in eastern Turkey, and Armenians name it after the lake rather than after the Turkish province.
