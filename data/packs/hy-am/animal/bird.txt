description: Bird, in Armenian. կռունկ (the crane) leads deliberately: it is the bird of Armenian exile, carried by Komitas's «Կռունկ», and no other bird carries that weight in the language. Several here are the birds of the Armenian highland rather than of a European garden — անգղ and գիշանգղ (the griffon and Egyptian vultures of the gorges), կաքավ (the chukar partridge, which gives its name to the fortress Կաքավաբերդ), and հոպոպ, the hoopoe of every Armenian children's rhyme.
