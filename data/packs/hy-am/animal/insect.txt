The list follows everyday Armenian usage rather than entomology: սարդ (spider), կարիճ (scorpion) and տիզ (tick) are arachnids but sit in the same folk category as the insects, and Armenian has no single everyday word that excludes them. զատիկ, the ladybird, shares its name with Easter.
