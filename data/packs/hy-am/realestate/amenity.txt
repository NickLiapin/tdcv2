description: Property feature as an Armenian listing advertises it. Three are local and tell you what Armenian housing is like: ավտոնոմ ջեռուցում (its own boiler) matters because central heating collapsed nationally in the 1990s and never came back, so a flat's heating is its own problem; ջրի պահեստարան (a water tank) is standard because mains water was rationed by the hour for years and many buildings still keep the tank; and երկաթե դուռ, a steel front door, is advertised as a feature rather than assumed.
