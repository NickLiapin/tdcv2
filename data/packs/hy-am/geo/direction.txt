description: Compass point, in Armenian. The four cardinals are transparent compounds of the sun and the day: արևելք is 'sun-rising', արևմուտք 'sun-setting', հարավ is the noon side and հյուսիս the cold one. The sixteen-point rose is built by hyphenation exactly as in English.
locale: hy-am
