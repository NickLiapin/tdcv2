---
description: Common Armenian adverb. Armenian derives most adverbs with -որեն or -բար on an adjective (արագորեն, հանդարտորեն, բարեկամաբար), but the bare adjective also serves as an adverb without any suffix at all — արագ means both 'fast' and 'quickly' — which is why the plain forms are on this list too.
locale: hy-am
