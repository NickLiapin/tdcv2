description: Common Armenian noun, in the nominative singular. THE DEFINITE ARTICLE IS A SUFFIX, not a word: ագրավոր -ը after a consonant and -ն before a vowel, so 'the book' is գիրքը and 'the eye' աչքն. Armenian has SEVEN CASES (nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, ablative, instrumental, locative), and the genitive and dative are the same form, so a template that concatenates a bare noun where a case form is needed produces something ungrammatical. There is no gender.
