---
description: Common Armenian adjective. ARMENIAN ADJECTIVES ARE INVARIABLE — no gender (the language has none), no number agreement, no case agreement — so any of these can be placed before any noun and the phrase is correct. The comparative is built with ավելի before the word, the superlative with ամենա- fused onto it: մեծ, ավելի մեծ, ամենամեծ.
locale: hy-am
