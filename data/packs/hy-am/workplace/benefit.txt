description: Employment benefit, in Armenian. THE PENSION LINE IS DIFFERENT HERE: Armenia's funded pillar (կուտակային կենսաթոշակ) is mandatory for anyone born after 1973 and is a payroll deduction, not a perk, so an Armenian benefits list has no 401(k)-style match on it. Private health insurance is the benefit that actually distinguishes employers, because there is no universal cover.
