description: Leading element of an Armenian place name (Նոր Հաճն, Վերին Պտղնի, Ներքին Գետաշեն). The first six are separate words that stand before a real toponym; the rest are BOUND first elements that fuse to the following stem with no space at all (Այգե- + -ձոր gives Այգեձոր, not 'Այգե Ձոր'), which is how most Armenian village names are actually built. Combine with a real toponym or with address/citySuffix; on its own this list names no real place.
locale: hy-am
