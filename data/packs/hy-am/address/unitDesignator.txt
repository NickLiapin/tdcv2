description: Armenian address unit designator, in the abbreviated form actually written on an envelope: «Աբովյան փող., շ. 12, բն. 34». շ. is շենք (block) and բն. is բնակարան (flat); an Armenian address needs BOTH, because a Soviet-built quarter numbers its blocks independently of the street. մուտք is the stairwell entrance — the Russian podyezd — and is part of the address, not of the directions.
locale: hy-am
