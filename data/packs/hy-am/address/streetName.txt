description: Street name elements found in town after town across Armenia. Armenian streets are named after national cultural and military figures to an extent that has no European parallel — Աբովյան, Մաշտոց, Թումանյան, Իսահակյան, Չարենց, Տերյան, Կոմիտաս, Սարյան and Նալբանդյան have a street in essentially every town in the country — so a 'generic' Armenian street name is a surname, not a flower. The nature and trade names that fill a Polish or English list are here too but are the minority. THE GENITIVE TRAP: some of these stand in the bare nominative before the type word (Աբովյան փողոց) and some take the genitive (Մաշտոցի պողոտա, Հանրապետության հրապարակ); this file holds each name in the form that is actually written on the sign, so do NOT append or strip an -ի mechanically. City-specific streets belong in a country pack; these are the ones that repeat nationwide.
