description: Product adjective, in Armenian. Armenian adjectives DO NOT AGREE with the noun in number or case — there is no gender in the language and an attributive adjective never changes shape — so unlike Polish, Russian or French, a config may concatenate any adjective here with any noun and the result is grammatical. This is the single biggest thing a template writer needs to know about Armenian.
