description: Unit of measure, in Armenian. Armenia is fully metric and has been since the Soviet period, so there is deliberately no pound, ounce or gallon on this list — an Armenian invoice never carries one. Two survivals from the pre-metric system are still heard in a market: փութ (pood, 16.38 kg) and լիտր used loosely for a wine measure.
locale: hy-am
