description: Product noun, in Armenian. տոնիր, ջեզվե and կարաս are ordinary household goods in Armenia and are sold as such: the tonir is the sunken clay oven that lavash is baked on the wall of and is on UNESCO's intangible heritage list, the karas is the buried clay wine jar, and the jezve is the long-handled pot every Armenian coffee is made in.
locale: hy-am
