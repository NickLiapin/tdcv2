THE -ՈՒՀԻ SUFFIX is worth knowing: Զարուհի, Հայկուհի, Տիրուհի, Երանուհի, Թագուհի, Պերճուհի and Սրբուհի are all built with it, and it is the same suffix that turns ուսուցիչ (teacher) into ուսուցչուհի (woman teacher). It is a DERIVATIONAL suffix on the word, not grammatical gender — Armenian has none — so it marks the name and nothing else in the sentence. Many names are simply nouns: Ծաղիկ is 'flower', Արևիկ 'little sun', Ձյունիկ 'little snow', Ոսկեհատ 'golden grain', Նվարդ and Սիրվարդ are built on վարդ 'rose'. Հռիփսիմե, Գայանե and Շողակաթ come from the fourth-century martyrdom story that ends with Armenia's conversion, and all three have churches in Vagharshapat named for them. No overlap with the male list.
