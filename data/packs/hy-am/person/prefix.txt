description: Armenian form of address. պարոն and տիկին take EITHER the surname or the given name — «պարոն Սարգսյան» in an office and «պարոն Արմեն» to someone you know — unlike Georgian, where the honorific attaches only to the given name. օրիորդ (Miss) is now old-fashioned and slightly formal. THE CLERICAL TITLES ARE A SEPARATE SYSTEM: Տեր goes before a parish priest's name (Տեր Հովհաննես), Սրբազան AFTER a bishop's, and Վեհափառ is reserved for the Catholicos of All Armenians. The academic titles are the Soviet ladder Armenia kept, so դոցենտ and պրոֆեսոր are conferred ranks rather than job descriptions.
