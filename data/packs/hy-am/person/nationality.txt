THE ARMENIAN FOR AN ARMENIAN IS ՀԱՅ, and it is not related to the word 'Armenian' in any other language: Armenians call themselves հայ, their country Հայաստան and its classical name Հայք, while 'Armenia' comes to Europe through Old Persian and was never a self-name. Most other nationalities take -ցի (ամերիկացի, ֆրանսիացի, ճապոնացի); a short set are bare stems (հայ, ռուս, հույն, թուրք, քուրդ, լեհ, չեխ, հրեա, արաբ).
