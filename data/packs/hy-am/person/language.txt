description: Language of the world as Armenian names it. EVERY LANGUAGE NAME IS BUILT WITH -ԵՐԵՆ, without exception: հայերեն, ռուսերեն, անգլերեն, ճապոներեն. The suffix is productive, so a language Armenian has never named still takes it. Note that հայերեն covers TWO literary standards that are not mutually interchangeable in writing — արևելահայերեն (Eastern, the Republic's, this pack) and արևմտահայերեն (Western, the diaspora's) — which differ in orthography, in verb conjugation and in the voicing of the stops, so a person's 'Armenian' is not one field value. For the languages actually spoken inside Armenia, including Yezidi and Assyrian, see armenia.language in the country pack.
