description: Name suffix, in Armenian. ARMENIA HAS NO Jr./Sr. GENERATIONAL SUFFIX — a father and son with the same name are told apart with ավագ and կրտսեր, and nothing follows the surname. THE REGNAL NUMBERS ARE ARMENIAN LETTERS, NOT ROMAN NUMERALS: the Armenian alphabet doubles as a numeral system (Ա=1, Բ=2, Գ=3 … Ք=9000), so it is Տիգրան Բ, not Տիգրան II, and Աշոտ Գ, not Ashot III. A formatter that emits Roman numerals for an Armenian king is writing a foreign convention. The post-nominals are the Soviet-derived academic abbreviations Armenian CVs actually carry: գ.թ. is candidate of sciences, գ.դ. doctor of sciences, բ.գ.թ. candidate of medical sciences.
