description: Pastime, in Armenian. նարդի and շախմատ are first because they are what Armenians actually do: backgammon is played in every courtyard and outside every shop, and chess is a national sport — Armenia won the Chess Olympiad three times, the men's team has a state stipend, and chess is a compulsory school subject. Կարաոկե, նարդի and խորոված-ի գնալ ('going for a khorovats') are social events rather than hobbies and belong on any honest Armenian list.
