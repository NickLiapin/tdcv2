description: Armenian hypocoristic. Armenian shortens a name with two suffixes above all: -Ո (Արմեն becomes Արմո, Գևորգ becomes Գևո, Սամվել becomes Սամո) and -ԻԿ (Աշոտիկ, Անուշիկ, Նունիկ, Ձյունիկ), and -իկ works on almost any name at all. A third layer came through Russian and is still everywhere: Գրիշա, Սաշա, Վովա, Ռուբիկ. Several of these — Հովիկ, Կարո, Սաքո — have become full given names in their own right.
locale: hy-am
