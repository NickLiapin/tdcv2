The twenty courts that actually exist in Armenia, with their statutory names, are in the armenia country pack at armenia.government.court, and repeating them here would put one country's judiciary into a language pack. Note that Armenian has no jury court and no arbitration court in the state system: ընդհանուր իրավասության դատարան (the court of general jurisdiction) is the first instance for everything that is not administrative, bankruptcy or corruption.
