description: Progress-message verb as Icelandic software actually writes it. Icelandic has NO -ing form: the progressive is built with "verið að" plus the infinitive, which is why every value here is a phrase rather than a word. The nearest single-word form, the -andi participle, is not idiomatic in a status message.
