description: Gross monthly pay band in Icelandic krónur (ISK), the currency of Iceland. Icelandic writes the thousands separator as a full stop and the decimal comma as a comma, so 1,5 millj. kr. is one and a half million krónur. Bands are monthly, not annual, because that is how Icelandic pay is quoted.
