description: Icelandic second name for a MAN. Iceland has no surnames: this is a patronymic, the father's given name in the genitive plus -son (Einar -> Einarsson). It is not inherited and not shared by a family — a man, his father and his son all carry different second names, and his sister carries -dóttir instead (see person/female/lastName). Because it is not a family name, Iceland alphabetises people by GIVEN name: the phone book, the national register and every official list are sorted on the first name, not this one. The tail of the list holds the few inherited family names (ættarnöfn) that Icelandic law froze in 1925 and that a minority of Icelanders still carry; no new ones may be created. Generated from one table together with person/female/lastName, line for line, so index N in the two files is the same father.
