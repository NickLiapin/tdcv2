description: Icelandic second name for a WOMAN — the same patronymic in its -dóttir form (Einar -> Einarsdóttir), aligned line for line with person/lastName. The two files are NOT a masculine/feminine inflection of one surname: they are the two forms a father's name takes for a son and for a daughter, so siblings of different sex genuinely carry different second names. The genitive step is real morphology and differs per name — Sigurður gives Sigurðsson but Sigurðardóttir, Hjörtur gives Hjartarson / Hjartardóttir, Sturla gives Sturluson / Sturludóttir — so both surface forms are written out rather than derived by appending a suffix. The inherited family names at the tail (Blöndal, Thoroddsen) are identical in both files, because ættarnöfn do not vary by sex.
locale: is
