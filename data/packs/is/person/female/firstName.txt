description: Icelandic female given names, roughly frequency-ordered. Every name here is a real registered Icelandic given name. Iceland keeps a legally binding approved-names register (mannanafnaskrá), maintained by the naming committee mannanafnanefnd under Lög um mannanöfn nr. 45/1996: a name not on the register cannot be given to an Icelandic citizen without the committee first admitting it, and it must be declinable in the four Icelandic cases and written in the Icelandic alphabet. So a plausible-looking invented name here would not merely be rare, it would be illegal. Since the Gender Autonomy Act nr. 80/2019 the register is no longer split by sex — any registered name may be borne by anyone — so treat this split as the usual association, not a rule.
