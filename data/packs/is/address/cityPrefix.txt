description: Leading qualifier of an Icelandic place name. Icelandic writes it with a HYPHEN and a capital on both halves — Ytri-Njarðvík, Innri-Njarðvík, Norður-Þingeyjarsýsla, Stóra-Vatnshorn — which is the opposite of the street names, where the parts are glued into one word. It does not by itself name a real place; combine it with a real toponym.
locale: is
