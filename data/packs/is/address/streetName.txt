description: Real Icelandic street names. Icelandic street names are single compound words with the thoroughfare element welded on the end — Laugavegur, Hringbraut, Skólavörðustígur — so there is no separate word for "street" the way Polish has ulica or English has Street; see address/streetSuffix for that element on its own. The house number follows the name with a space and no comma (Laugavegur 12). Names tied to one town's postcode range belong in an Iceland country pack.
locale: is
