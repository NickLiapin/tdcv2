description: The generic element that ends an Icelandic street name. Unlike Polish "ulica X" or English "X Street" this is NOT a separate word: it is welded onto the specific element with no space and no hyphen, and the compound is written as one word — Laugavegur, Hringbraut, Skólavörðustígur. Compose it that way or the address will not read as Icelandic. Each element is a noun with its own gender, which is why the definite forms differ (-gata is feminine, -vegur masculine, -stræti neuter).
