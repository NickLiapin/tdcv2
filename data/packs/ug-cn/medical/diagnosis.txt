---
description: General diagnosis in Uyghur. THE TERMS ARE BUILT, NOT BORROWED, wherever Uyghur has the parts: organ + yallughi is an inflammation (öpke yallughi pneumonia, jiger yallughi hepatitis, börek yallughi nephritis), organ + raki is a cancer, organ + téshi a stone. Where no native compound exists the international term is transliterated into the Arabic script rather than left in Latin — migrén, ekzéma, diabét is instead the native shéker késili, sugar-disease. Two entries are specifically regional: öpke sili, tuberculosis, and qizil öngech raki, oesophageal cancer, which has one of the highest incidences in the world along the old Silk Road corridor.
locale: ug-cn
