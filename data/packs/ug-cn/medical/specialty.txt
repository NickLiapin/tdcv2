description: Medical specialty in Uyghur. Most are built natively as organ + késellikliri (its diseases) instead of borrowing a Greek stem. THE LAST TWO ROWS ARE REAL AND LOCAL: uyghur tibabiti, Uyghur traditional medicine, is a licensed practice in China with its own hospitals, pharmacopoeia and university department, and xenzu tibabiti is Traditional Chinese Medicine — a Xinjiang hospital commonly runs departments for both alongside the modern ones above them.
