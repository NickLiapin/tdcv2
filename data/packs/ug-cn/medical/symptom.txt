description: Symptom in Uyghur, as a patient would say it. NOTE THE GRAMMAR: a symptom is normally organ + aghriqi, the organ plus its pain — bash aghriqi is head-its-pain — and the possessive suffix -i / -si is what joins them, which is the same joint used all through this pack for street types and specialty names. The verbal ones are infinitives: qusush to vomit, terlesh to sweat, titresh to shiver.
