---
description: Legal form of an Uyghur-registered business, TRANSLATED FROM THE CHINESE COMPANY LAW because that is the law these businesses register under — cheklik shirket is 有限公司, payliq cheklik shirket is 股份有限公司, guruppa shirkiti is 集团. The cooperative rows are not decoration: the déhqanlar koopératipi, the farmers' cooperative, is the commonest registered legal person in rural Xinjiang. Uyghur has one country, so naming that country's legal forms is correct here; the registration numbers that go with them are a country matter and are not in this pack.
locale: ug-cn
