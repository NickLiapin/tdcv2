description: Material in Uyghur. ETLES IS THE ONE TO KNOW: the Uyghur ikat silk, warp-dyed before weaving so the pattern blurs at the edges, woven in Hotan and worn as a woman's dress — it is the single most recognisable Uyghur manufactured thing and it has no English name. qashtéshi is Hotan nephrite jade, and nemde is felt. The rest run from the Turkic (tömür iron, mis copper, taş stone) through Persian (rext cloth, ünche pearl) to transliterated modern synthetics.
