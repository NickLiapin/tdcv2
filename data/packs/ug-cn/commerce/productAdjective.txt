description: Adjective a product listing uses, in Uyghur. THE -liq FAMILY IS THE HARMONY LESSON HERE. The adjective-forming suffix has FOUR shapes and the last vowel of the stem picks one: -liq after the back unrounded a and i (bash → bashliq), -lik after the front unrounded e and é (süpet → süpetlik, ishench → ishenchlik), -luq after the back rounded o and u (uzun → uzunluq), -lük after the front rounded ö and ü (köp → köplük). All four spellings appear in this pack. THIS IS THE ROUNDING HARMONY UZBEK HAS LOST AND UYGHUR KEEPS, and it is the fastest structural test of whether a text is really Uyghur rather than a Turkic neighbour in the same script.
