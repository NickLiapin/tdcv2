description: Unit of measure in Uyghur. THE LAST THREE ROWS ARE THE LOCAL FACT and a config that ignores them will produce a market stall nobody in Xinjiang recognises: jin is the Chinese catty, exactly 500 grams, and it — not the kilogram — is how fruit, meat and vegetables are priced and sold; liang is a tenth of a jin, 50 grams, used for tea and spice; mo is the Chinese land unit, about 667 square metres, and is how a field is measured. The metric units above them are correct and official, and the catty is what the scale in the bazaar actually reads.
