description: Vegetable in Uyghur. The everyday base of the kitchen is the first four — piyaz onion, kartop potato, sewze carrot and pemidur tomato — and sewze is not optional, because a polu without carrot is not a polu. jüsey is Chinese chives, borrowed from the Han kitchen next door and now ordinary in Uyghur cooking; lengpung, the cold starch jelly, is made from mung-bean starch and appears here as its plant. Words for pepper are built on much: qizil much chilli, bulghar much sweet pepper.
