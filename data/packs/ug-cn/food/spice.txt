description: Spice and seasoning in Uyghur. ZIRE COMES FIRST AND THAT IS NOT ALPHABETICAL: cumin is the signature of the whole cuisine, and a kebab is dressed with zire and qizil much and nothing else. The Persian layer names the sweet spices — darchin cinnamon, hel cardamom, qerenpül clove, zeperan saffron — and the herb block is fresh rather than dried, because kishnish (coriander), shiwit (dill), reyhan (basil) and pudina (mint) go on the plate at the table. REFUSED HERE: turmeric, which I could not confirm a settled Uyghur name for, and which is better absent than guessed.
