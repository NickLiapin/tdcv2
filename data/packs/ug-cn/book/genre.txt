description: Literary genre in Uyghur. THE FIRST BLOCK IS THE CLASSICAL ONE and its categories are Persian-Turkic rather than European: dastan is the verse epic, of which the Uyghur canon has a dozen; qoshaq is the sung folk quatrain; and maqal-temsil, proverb-and-parable, is a published book category in its own right. chöchek is the folk tale and tépishmaq the riddle, both collected and printed as children's books.
