description: Garment in Uyghur. THE DOPPA IS THE ONE EVERY UYGHUR OWNS: a square embroidered skullcap, folded flat, in patterns that name the town it comes from — the Hotan badam pattern of almond shapes is the best known. The etles köynek is the ikat-silk dress; the chapan is the long padded coat; the kesh is a soft leather boot worn inside a galosh, which is what lets a man take his shoes off at a doorway without going barefoot.
