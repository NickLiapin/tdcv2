description: Unit designator inside an Uyghur address. THE STRUCTURE IS THE CHINESE ONE, TRANSLATED: an address in Xinjiang descends turalghu rayoni (the gated residential compound) then bina (the numbered block) then birlik (the stairwell or unit) then öy (the flat), which is 小区 / 楼 / 单元 / 室 word for word. That is a different shape from the European street-plus-number and a config that models one will not model the other. The words themselves are Uyghur; the numbering scheme is a country matter and is deliberately not in this pack.
