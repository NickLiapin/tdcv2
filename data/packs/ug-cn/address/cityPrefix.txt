description: Leading element of an Uyghur place name. FOUR FAMILIES FILL IT. Age and size open the most names — Yéngi new, Kona old, Chong big, Kichik small. Colours are next and are Turkic: Aq white, Qara black, Qizil red, Kök blue, Sériq yellow, Altun gold. NUMBERS ARE A LIVE TOPONYMIC ELEMENT here as across Central Asia — Üch three, Besh five, Yette seven, On ten, Ming thousand — and they usually count springs, wells or poplars. And the oasis vocabulary: Tash stone, Qum sand, Su water, Shor saline, Toqay riverine woodland. Combine with a real toponym; on its own it names nothing.
