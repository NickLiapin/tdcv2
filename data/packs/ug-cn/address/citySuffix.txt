description: Trailing element of an Uyghur place name. THREE LANGUAGES ARE STACKED HERE and the stack is the history of the Tarim basin. The Iranian layer is oldest — -kent is Sogdian for town, -awat is Persian abad, a watered and settled place, and -bagh is a garden. The Turkic layer is about water and ground, because in an oasis a settlement is named for its water: bulaq spring, quduq well, östeng and ériq irrigation channels, köl lake, döng rise, toqay river-forest. The Arabic-Islamic layer is small and religious: mazar a saint's shrine, lenger the hostel beside it, meschit a mosque. -awat and -bagh together account for a large share of village names in southern Xinjiang.
