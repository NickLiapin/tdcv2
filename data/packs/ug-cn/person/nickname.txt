description: Informal Uyghur name form. TWO MACHINES MAKE ALL OF THESE. The first is the suffix -jan (Persian, soul), which attaches to any name at all and is not gendered: Tursunjan, Exmetjan, Gülnarjan. The second is clipping, which is why Abdulla becomes Abla, Muhemmet becomes Memet and then Met, and Exmet becomes Exet — the clipped forms are so established that several of them are now registered names in their own right and appear in person/male/firstName.txt. THE THIRD BLOCK IS THE TRADE NICKNAME — Nanway the baker, Qassap the butcher, Temurchi the smith — and it matters more than it looks: where an Uyghur family does carry something like an inherited name, this is almost always where it came from.
