description: Uyghur female given name in the Perso-Arabic script (ug-cn). Weights are rank-derived Zipf values, not registry counts — no Uyghur name frequency table is published. THE DOMINANT ELEMENT IS gül, flower, borrowed from Persian, and it works at BOTH ends of the name: Gülnar, Gülbahar, Gülnisa, Gülchehre at the front and Aygül, Nurgül, Tursungül, Reyhangül at the back. Three more elements do the same work — ay (moon), nur (light) and dil (heart) — so Aynur, Dilnur and Dilber are built from the same small kit. THE SUFFIX -xan MARKS AN OLDER GENERATION of women: Meryemxan, Xedichexan, Ayshexan. The Arabic layer keeps its feminine -e: Amine, Xediche, Helime, Rebiye, Munire. Names ending -nisa (Arabic for women) are also female-only: Gülnisa, Hörnisa, Xeyrinsa. NOTE what does NOT happen here: the family-name slot does not change for a woman, because it is her father's given name — see person/lastName.txt, and note the deliberate absence of person/female/lastName.txt.
