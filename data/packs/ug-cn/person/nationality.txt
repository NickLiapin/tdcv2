description: Ethnonym in Uyghur. THE FIRST BLOCK IS THE PEOPLES OF XINJIANG and several of them are found essentially nowhere else: the sibe, descendants of a Manchu garrison moved to the Ili valley in 1764, who still write the Manchu script; the xui, Chinese-speaking Muslims; the tatar and the özbék, long-settled town populations; the taranchi, the historical name for the settled Uyghur farmers of Ili; and the doulan, a Uyghur group of the Yarkand and Tarim river country with a muqam tradition of its own. Ethnonyms are written lower case in Uyghur and take the plural -lar / -ler by vowel harmony: uyghurlar but xenzular.
