description: Pastime, named in Uyghur. A hobby is normally a verbal noun — the -sh / -ish ending on kitab oqush (reading a book) and naxsha éytish (singing) is the infinitive, so the values read as activities rather than as nouns. THE MUSICAL BLOCK IS NOT DECORATION: the dutar, rawap, tembur, ghejek and dap are the instruments of the Twelve Muqam, and meshrep — a gathering of music, dance, food and moral instruction — is a named Uyghur institution with no English word, listed by UNESCO. Kepter béqish, keeping pigeons, and the bazaar stroll are ordinary and widespread. NOTE THE VOWEL HARMONY working across the list: körüsh but oqush, öginish but tartish — the suffix vowel copies the front-or-back class of the stem.
locale: ug-cn
