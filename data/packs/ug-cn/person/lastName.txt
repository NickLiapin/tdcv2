description: THIS IS NOT A SURNAME LIST, AND THAT IS THE MOST IMPORTANT FACT IN THIS PACK. Uyghurs in China do not carry an inherited family name. A person is a given name plus the FATHER'S GIVEN NAME, and that second element changes every generation: Ablimit son of Qurban is Ablimit Qurban, and his own son is Ekrem Ablimit. The address is called lastName only because the engine's slot is called that; the values below are therefore male given names, ordered towards the generation now supplying fathers and grandfathers rather than towards newborns, which is why Turdi, Tohti, Rozi, Zunun and Ghopur stand near the top here and not in person/male/firstName.txt. FOUR CONSEQUENCES A CONFIG MUST HANDLE. (1) There is deliberately NO person/female/lastName.txt: the father's name does not inflect, so a woman's second element is identical in form to a man's — this pack's absence of that file is the design, not an omission. (2) Siblings share it; cousins do not; a family tree cannot be reconstructed from it. (3) In Chinese-language documents the two parts are joined by the interpunct · , which is a transcription convention of the document and not part of the Uyghur name. (4) Weights are rank-derived Zipf values, not registry counts, because no Uyghur name frequency table exists. REFUSED HERE: the -ov/-ova surnames carried by Uyghurs in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, which are Soviet registry morphology and belong to a different community than this ug-cn pack describes.
locale: ug-cn
weighted: true
