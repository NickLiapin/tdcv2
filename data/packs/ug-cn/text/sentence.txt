Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Uyghur book at all — a search for l.ug returns "No records found", checked against a control search for l.pl which returns Polish titles — so unlike fr/de/it/es/pt/pl these are not extracted from a public-domain corpus. They are short, declarative and everyday, and they are written to show the grammar as well as to fill a column: Uyghur is VERB-FINAL, has no articles and no prepositions, and marks with a suffix after the noun what English puts in a word before it — bazargha to the bazaar, hoylida in the courtyard, taghdin from the mountain. The suffix HARMONISES with the stem, which is why the same case appears as -da and -de, -gha and -ge, -din and -tin across these lines.
