description: Uyghur filler words, everyday vocabulary, for slugs, tags and usernames. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Uyghur book at all — a search for l.ug returns "No records found", checked against a control search for l.pl which returns Polish titles — so unlike fr/de/it/es/pt/pl these are not extracted from a public-domain corpus. The list is chosen to exercise the ORTHOGRAPHY as well as the vocabulary: it is deliberately full of the four vowel letters that a careless Arabic-script pipeline destroys — ۇ u, ۆ ö, ۈ ü and ې é — in words like ئۆي, ئۈزۈم, تۆگە, كۆۋرۈك, سۆگەت and يېزىق, so that any normaliser that folds them onto و or ى will break visibly rather than quietly. Every word here begins with a real letter for its first vowel, carried on the hemze ئ where the word is vowel-initial.
