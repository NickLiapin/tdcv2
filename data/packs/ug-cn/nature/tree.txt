TOGHRAQ IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS: the desert poplar, Populus euphratica, which grows along the Tarim where nothing else will and is the emblem of the oasis — a Uyghur saying gives it a thousand years standing, a thousand years dead and a thousand years fallen. saksayul is saxaul and yulghun tamarisk, both desert shrubs that hold the sand; jigde is the oleaster, whose flowers scent a village in May. The orchard block is the region's living: mulberry, walnut, apricot, pomegranate, fig.
