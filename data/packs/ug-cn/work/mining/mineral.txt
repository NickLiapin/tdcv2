THE LAST ROW IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS LOCALLY: qashtéshi is nephrite jade, dug and picked from the White Jade and Black Jade rivers at Hotan for three thousand years, and it is the single mineral with a deep native Uyghur name rather than a transliterated one. Oil and coal head the list because that is the modern order of the industry; the metal names are mostly Turkic (tömür iron, mis copper, altun gold, kümüsh silver) and the industrial minerals mostly borrowed.
