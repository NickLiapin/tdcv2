description: Job title in Uyghur, and THE BEST PLACE IN THIS PACK TO SEE VOWEL HARMONY DO REAL WORK. Uyghur makes an agent noun two ways. The first is -chi, which never changes: ish-chi worker, naxsha-chi singer, sodi-ger merchant is the Persian equivalent. The second is -GUchi, and it varies FOUR WAYS by the stem — voiced-back -ghuchi (bashqurghuchi manager, tazilighuchi cleaner, qoghdighuchi guard), voiceless-back -quchi (oqutquchi teacher, satquchi salesperson), voiced-front -güchi (heydigüchi driver, öchürgüchi extinguisher, tekshürgüchi inspector), voiceless-front -küchi. A config that appends a fixed -ghuchi will be wrong on more than half the stems. THE TRADE BLOCK IS ALSO WHERE UYGHUR SURNAMES CAME FROM, on the rare occasions a family has one: nanway baker, qassap butcher, zerger jeweller, könchi tanner, gilemchi carpet weaver.
locale: ug-cn
