description: Musical genre in Uyghur. ON MUQAM IS THE FIRST ROW AND THE CENTRAL FACT: the Twelve Muqam is a suite cycle of some twelve hours, assembled in the sixteenth century by Amannisa Xan, and UNESCO lists it as intangible heritage — it is to Uyghur music what the whole classical repertoire is to a European one. The regional muqam traditions (Dolan, Turpan, Hami, Kucha) are named separately because they are genuinely different repertoires. senem (the sung-and-danced suite) and meshrep music are functional rather than concert forms.
