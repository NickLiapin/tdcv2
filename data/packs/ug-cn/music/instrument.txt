description: Musical instrument in Uyghur. THE FIRST EIGHT ARE THE MUQAM ENSEMBLE and none of them has an English name: the dutar, a two-string long-necked lute played in every house; the rawap, plucked and skin-bellied; the tembur, the long lute that carries the melody; the ghejek, a spike fiddle; the satar, bowed and with sympathetic strings, which leads a muqam; the dap, the frame drum with rings inside the rim; the naghra, the paired kettledrums played outdoors with the sunay; and the sunay, the shawm. The transliterated European instruments follow.
