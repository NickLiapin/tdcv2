description: Country and territory name in Uyghur, alphabetical, unweighted — the long list, where geo/country.txt is the short weighted one. Translated and transliterated the way Uyghur does it, not respelled from English: China is Junggo (from Chinese), Egypt is Misir, Greece is Grétsiye, the United Kingdom is Engliye. TWO PRODUCTIVE ENDINGS carry most of the list — -iye for what English writes -ia or -y, and the Persian -istan, land of, which covers the whole Central Asian and Iranian block. A handful arrive through Russian rather than English, which is why Germany is Gérmaniye and not Doychland.
