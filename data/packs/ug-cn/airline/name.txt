description: Airline as Uyghur names it. THE FIRST BLOCK IS THE CHINESE CARRIERS, whose names are descriptive and translate cleanly — Junggo jenubiy hawa yolliri is China Southern, the airline that actually flies most routes into and out of the region. The international block is transliterated. NO REGIONAL OR PREFECTURE-BRANDED CARRIER IS LISTED: those are place-bound and belong in a country pack, not a language one.
