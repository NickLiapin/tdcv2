description: Colour in Uyghur. THE SIX BASIC TERMS ARE TURKIC AND ARE ALSO PLACE-NAME ELEMENTS — aq white, qara black, qizil red, kök blue, yéshil green, sériq yellow — which is why address/cityPrefix.txt carries the same six. kök covers blue and the green of growing things, as it does across Turkic. THE DERIVED SHADES USE TWO SUFFIXES that harmonise: -ghuch / -üch for approximate (qizghuch reddish, kökǖch bluish, sarghuch yellowish) and -mtul for darkened (qaramtul blackish). Everything else is a compound with reng, colour, borrowed from Persian.
