description: Country and territory names, in Marathi. These are TRANSLATED where Marathi has its own form and only transliterated where it does not: the United States is अमेरिकेची संयुक्त संस्थाने (literally the United States of America, a genuine Marathi noun phrase with the possessive -ची welded onto अमेरिका), Turkey is तुर्कस्तान rather than the Hindi तुर्की, and Burma keeps its Marathi name ब्रह्मदेश beside म्यानमार. Two things distinguish this list from a Hindi one written in the same script. FIRST, नेपाळ: Marathi spells it with ळ, Hindi with ल, and no script test can see the difference. SECOND, every compound name joins with आणि, the Marathi word for and — अँटिग्वा आणि बार्बुडा, त्रिनिदाद आणि टोबॅगो — where Hindi uses और. A list that reads और anywhere is not Marathi. Note finally that country names inflect: a config dropping one into a sentence must write भारतात (in India), जपानला (to Japan), फ्रान्समधून (from France), with the postposition agglutinated onto the noun rather than written as a separate word.
