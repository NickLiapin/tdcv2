description: Music genre, in Marathi. Eight of these are Marathi forms with no counterpart in any other language. भावगीत is the Marathi light song, a whole twentieth-century concert-hall repertoire. नाट्यसंगीत is the music of Marathi Sangeet Natak — theatre in which the plot stops for a classical song that the audience demands to hear again — and it exists in no other Indian theatre tradition. अभंग is the Warkari devotional verse form; गवळण its companion; भारूड a sung allegory. पोवाडा is the ballad of a warrior's deeds, sung standing, and लावणी the fast, sharply worded women's song of the tamasha stage. कोळीगीत is the fisherfolk song of the Konkan coast. All of these are performed commercially today, not preserved in an archive.
