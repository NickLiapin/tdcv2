description: Official identity, civil-registration and revenue document, in Marathi. The entry with no counterpart anywhere else is ७/१२ उतारा, the seven-twelve extract: it is the Maharashtra land record that states who holds a plot, what is grown on it and what charges sit against it, and it is the single most requested document in the state — no land is bought, no crop loan is taken and no inheritance is settled without one. ८-अ उतारा and फेरफार उतारा are its companions. शाळा सोडल्याचा दाखला, the school leaving certificate, is the document an Indian carries as proof of date of birth for the rest of their life. आधार and पॅन are the two national numbers almost every record keys on.
