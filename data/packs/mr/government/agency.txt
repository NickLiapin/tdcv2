description: Government body, regulator or public office, in Marathi. This is a LANGUAGE pack, so these are the words Marathi uses for kinds of public body — but Marathi has essentially one jurisdiction, so the entries name real bodies of MAHARASHTRA and INDIA and the reader should know that rather than guess. The three-tier rural structure (ग्रामपंचायत at village level, पंचायत समिती at block, जिल्हा परिषद at district) is the Panchayati Raj system, which Maharashtra adopted early and runs more fully than most states; a Marathi rural record is far more likely to name a जिल्हा परिषद than any national ministry. म्हाडा, सिडको, एमआयडीसी, महावितरण and एस.टी. महामंडळ are acronym-named state corporations that Marathi speech and press use as ordinary words.
