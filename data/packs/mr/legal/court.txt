description: Court or tribunal, in Marathi. As with government/agency.txt these are the WORDS Marathi uses for kinds of court, and because the language has one jurisdiction they are the real forums of Maharashtra and India — the reader should know that rather than assume a generic list. Three have no European counterpart. लोकअदालत is a statutory settlement forum whose award is final and unappealable, held on scheduled days to clear backlog. ग्राम न्यायालय is the mobile village court. सहकार न्यायालय is the co-operative court, which exists because Maharashtra's economy runs through co-operative societies and their disputes get a dedicated bench. महसूल and तहसीलदार courts are the revenue judiciary, a parallel hierarchy that decides land questions and is quite separate from the civil courts.
