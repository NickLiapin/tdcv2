description: Legal term, in Marathi. वकीलपत्र is the signed authority appointing an advocate, filed on the record; फिर्याद is the criminal complaint that starts a police case. Marathi legal vocabulary is Sanskrit-built rather than Persian-built, which is where it parts company with Urdu-influenced Hindi court language: Marathi says प्रतिवादी and अधिकारक्षेत्र where a Hindi court file may say मुद्दालेह and इख़्तियार.
locale: mr
