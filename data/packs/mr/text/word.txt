Verbs are deliberately absent from this list and live in word/verb.txt, because a Marathi verb has no citation form that is safe to paste into a sentence — the dictionary form ends in -णे and every finite form agrees in one of three genders. The nouns here carry lexical gender that is not visible in the string (वेळ is feminine, दिवस masculine, घर neuter), so they are safe as tags and slugs but not as sentence material without the gender information in word/noun.txt.
