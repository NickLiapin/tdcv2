description: Playing positions in क्रिकेट, in Marathi. Marathi has its OWN cricket vocabulary and does not borrow Hindi's: a batter is फलंदाज (Hindi बल्लेबाज), a bowler गोलंदाज (Hindi गेंदबाज), a wicketkeeper यष्टिरक्षक and a fielder क्षेत्ररक्षक. Marathi commentary on state radio and in newspapers uses these words, so a Hindi-derived list would be wrong in every row. The fielding positions keep their English names because that is what players say.
