description: Sport, in Marathi, led by what Maharashtra actually plays. Seven are Maharashtrian or Indian and appear in no European list. मल्लखांब is gymnastics performed on a fixed wooden pole or a hanging rope, Maharashtra's own discipline and now its state sport. दांडपट्टा is the Maratha sword-flail, revived and recognised as a state sport in 2022. मर्दानी खेळ are the Maratha martial games it belongs to. कुस्ती, wrestling in a clay pit at a तालीम, is the village sport of western Maharashtra. आट्यापाट्या, विटीदांडू and लगोरी are the street games every Marathi childhood contains. बैलगाडा शर्यत, bullock cart racing, is a rural sport that was banned, litigated over for a decade and legally restored — it is a live subject, not a curiosity. कबड्डी and खो खो are Indian sports with professional leagues.
