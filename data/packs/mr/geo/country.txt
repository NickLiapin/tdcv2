The names are TRANSLATED where Marathi has its own form and only transliterated where it does not: the United States is अमेरिकेची संयुक्त संस्थाने, a real Marathi noun phrase in which the possessive -ची is welded onto अमेरिका rather than written as a separate word, and Turkey is तुर्कस्तान, Marathi's own form, where Hindi says तुर्की. नेपाळ carries ळ, the retroflex L Hindi does not have (Hindi writes नेपाल) — the cheapest single-character proof that this list is Marathi and not Hindi, since both are Devanagari and no script check can separate them. भारत sits second at its real weight rather than being pushed to the top: a plain draw from this file will produce China as often as India, and a config that wants an India-centred dataset must filter rather than rely on the weighting. Country names inflect like any other Marathi noun and take their postpositions as suffixes — भारतात (in India), जपानला (to Japan), फ्रान्समधून (from France) — so a config dropping one into a sentence has to inflect it, not paste it.
