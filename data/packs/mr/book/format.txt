description: Book or publication format, in Marathi. The one that exists in no other language's pack is दिवाळी अंक, the Diwali annual: several hundred literary magazines are published in Marathi once a year for Diwali and only then, carrying new fiction, poetry and essays by the best writers in the language, and a Marathi household buys three or four of them every autumn. It is a publishing format, not a genre, and there is nothing like it in Hindi, English or any European language.
