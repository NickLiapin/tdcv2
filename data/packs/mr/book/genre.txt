description: Book genre, in Marathi. Four of these are categories of Marathi literature with no counterpart to translate from. संतसाहित्य is the devotional verse of the Warkari saints — Dnyaneshwar, Tukaram, Namdev, Janabai — which is read as living literature and not as scripture. दलित साहित्य is the movement founded in Marathi in the 1960s and later exported to every other Indian language; Marathi is where it began. ग्रामीण कादंबरी is the rural novel, a major twentieth-century Marathi form. एकांकिका is the one-act play, which has its own inter-collegiate competition circuit across the state. पोवाडा is the sung ballad of a warrior's deeds.
