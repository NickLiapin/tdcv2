description: Educational qualification, in Marathi. The first two are the Maharashtra State Board certificates and are what most Marathi records actually hold: एस.एस.सी. is the class ten certificate and एच.एस.सी. the class twelve one, and an Indian job application asks for them by these names and not as high-school diploma. Marathi writes degree abbreviations with Devanagari letter names, so B.Sc. is बी.एस्सी. — the Hindi बी.एससी. is a different spelling of the same abbreviation. आय.टी.आय. is the industrial training certificate, the commonest post-school trade qualification in the state.
