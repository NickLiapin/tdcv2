description: Business buzz adjective, in Marathi. Most corporate Marathi borrows the English word outright (स्केलेबल, मॉड्यूलर, रिअल-टाइम) because the register was imported wholesale; the ones with native forms (चपळ, लवचिक, शाश्वत, सर्वसमावेशक) are what a government or co-operative report uses instead. Both registers are here because a real Marathi business document mixes them. The native adjectives ending in -आ inflect for three genders as everything else in this language does — see word/adjective.txt.
