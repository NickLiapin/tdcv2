description: Industry or sector, in Marathi. The list is weighted towards what Maharashtra actually runs on: साखर उद्योग (the sugar industry, organised almost entirely as farmer co-operatives and the political backbone of western Maharashtra), सूतगिरणी (cotton spinning), द्राक्ष व वाइन (the Nashik vineyards), चित्रपट (Mumbai is India's film industry) and बंदर व जहाजबांधणी (the Konkan ports). सहकार, the co-operative sector, is listed as an industry in its own right because in Maharashtra it is one — sugar, dairy, banking and credit all run through co-operative societies here in a way they do in no other Indian state.
