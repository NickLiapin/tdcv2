description: Evocative noun for a Marathi company name — compose with a company/legalSuffix. These are what Maharashtrian firms are actually called: the forts (शिवनेरी, रायगड, सिंहगड), the rivers (गोदावरी, कृष्णा, पंचगंगा, इंद्रायणी), the mountain wall itself (सह्याद्री), the deities (गजानन, विठ्ठल, तुळजाभवानी), and the abstract nouns of aspiration (समृद्धी, प्रगती, स्वराज्य, वैभव). A Marathi sugar co-operative or transport firm is far more likely to be named after a local river or fort than after a Latinate abstraction, which is why the geography leads this list. Several carry ळ — वेळू, कळस, गूळ, हळद, माळरान, मळा, तुळस — and are therefore unspellable in Hindi.
locale: mr
