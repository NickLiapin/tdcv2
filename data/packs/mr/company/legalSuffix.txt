description: Company legal form or name suffix, in Marathi. Several are Maharashtrian institutional forms that a European or even a Hindi list has no slot for. सहकारी संस्था is the co-operative society, and सहकारी साखर कारखाना — the co-operative sugar mill — is the single most important economic institution in western Maharashtra; there are more than a hundred of them and each one is a company, a political base and a village employer at once. पतसंस्था is the credit society, नागरी सहकारी बँक the urban co-operative bank, and दूध संघ the dairy union. बंधू is the Marathi of Brothers and is a genuine trading name suffix (जोशी बंधू). प्रा. लि. is the abbreviation for private limited and appears on nearly every incorporated Indian firm.
