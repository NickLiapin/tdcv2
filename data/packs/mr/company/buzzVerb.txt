description: Business buzz verb, in Marathi, given in the dictionary form that ends in -णे. Two things a config cannot infer. FIRST, Marathi has no simple verb for most of this vocabulary and builds them as COMPOUND verbs — a noun plus करणे (to do): परिवर्तन करणे, अनुकूलित करणे, विश्लेषण करणे. The noun carries the meaning and करणे carries the grammar, so a config splitting on whitespace will cut a single verb in half. SECOND, and this is where Marathi's three genders bite hardest, the PAST tense of a transitive verb agrees with its OBJECT, not its subject: त्याने अहवाल तयार केला (masculine object), त्याने योजना तयार केली (feminine), त्याने प्रारूप तयार केले (neuter). One subject, three verb forms, chosen by a noun that appears earlier in the sentence. Hindi has the same ergative pattern but only two genders to resolve it into; Marathi has three, so a sentence generator ported from Hindi will be wrong on every neuter object.
