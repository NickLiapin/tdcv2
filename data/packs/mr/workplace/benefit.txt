description: Employment benefit, in Marathi. Four are Indian statutory components with no European equivalent and they appear on every Indian pay slip. भविष्य निर्वाह निधी is the Employees' Provident Fund, a compulsory retirement deduction matched by the employer. ग्रॅच्युइटी is a lump sum payable after five years' service, also statutory. महागाई भत्ता is dearness allowance, an inflation-indexed component revised twice a year, and घरभाडे भत्ता is house rent allowance, which is a separate taxable line rather than part of basic pay. दिवाळी बोनस is the annual bonus, paid before Diwali by law in covered establishments — it is a legal entitlement, not a gift.
