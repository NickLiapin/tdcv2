description: Everyday product, in Marathi. Every noun here carries a grammatical gender that a config needs but cannot see from the string: खुर्ची is feminine, पंखा masculine, पुस्तक neuter. That matters because commerce/productAdjective.txt has to agree with it in three ways, not two. Several items are specific to a Maharashtrian household — पाटा वरवंटा is the grinding stone, विळी the floor-mounted curved blade used for cutting vegetables (it carries ळ), डबा the stacked tiffin carrier, and तांब्या the copper drinking vessel.
