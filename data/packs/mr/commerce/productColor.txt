This is the same colour vocabulary as color/name.txt but read as a fixed label rather than as an adjective in a sentence — a listing prints रंग: काळा with the masculine form regardless of the product's gender, because it is a label and not agreement. That is why this file exists separately: a config that puts color/name.txt into a sentence must agree it, and one that puts this into a colour FIELD must not.
