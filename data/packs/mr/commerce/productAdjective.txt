description: Adjective for a product listing, in Marathi. Read the note in color/name.txt before using this list: the adjectives that end in -आ — मोठा, लहान, हलका, जड, कडक — inflect for THREE genders, so मोठा (masculine), मोठी (feminine) and मोठे (neuter) are three different words and the right one is decided by the noun in commerce/productNoun.txt. A template that concatenates an adjective and a noun without agreeing them produces Marathi that is grammatically wrong in roughly two cases out of three. The rest of the list is INVARIANT — टिकाऊ, सुंदर, आकर्षक, हस्तनिर्मित, जाड, बारीक and every compound ending in -योग्य or -शील never change form — so a config that has no gender information should restrict itself to those.
