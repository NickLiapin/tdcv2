description: Spice, in Marathi. Three are Maharashtrian and appear in no other locale's list. दगडफूल is the dried lichen — literally stone-flower — that gives गोडा मसाला its dark, smoky base and is used in almost no cuisine outside the Deccan. तिरफळ is the Konkan's own Sichuan-pepper relative, numbing and citrus-scented, and no Malvani fish curry is right without it. The regional blends at the end (कोल्हापुरी, मालवणी, गोडा, कांदा-लसूण) are the finished masalas a household actually reaches for, not single-spice ingredients. हळद, तिरफळ, जायफळ and तीळ all carry ळ.
