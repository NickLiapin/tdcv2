description: Everyday dish eaten across Maharashtra, named in Marathi — the pan-Maharashtrian repertoire that is not tied to one region. Regional specialities live in food/dishByCuisine. Four of these define the cuisine and exist nowhere else: पुरणपोळी is the flatbread stuffed with sweetened split-gram paste that is made for every major festival; मिसळ पाव is sprouted-bean curry under farsan with a bread roll, eaten for breakfast and graded by how much तर्री, the red oil on top, you can take; वडा पाव is the potato fritter in a bun that is Mumbai's street food; and थालीपीठ is the multigrain flatbread patted out by hand. Note the grammatical genders in one line, because a sentence about food must agree with them: वडा is masculine, पुरणपोळी is feminine, and पिठलं is neuter. Three genders, three verb forms — Hindi has two.
