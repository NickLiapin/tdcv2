description: Animal, in Marathi. The list is led by what actually lives in Maharashtra rather than by a European bestiary: शेकरू, the Indian giant squirrel, is the state animal and lives only in the Western Ghats; बिबट्या, the leopard, is common enough to be an urban problem in Pune and Nashik; गवा is the Indian bison of the Ghats; काळवीट is the blackbuck of the Deccan plateau. Note how many of these cannot be written in Hindi: काळवीट, चितळ, वटवाघूळ, साळिंदर and कावळा all carry ळ. Note also that animal names carry grammatical gender and it is not the animal's sex — मांजर (cat) is NEUTER in Marathi, so it takes ते and neuter agreement whether the cat is male or female, which is a distinction Hindi cannot make at all.
