description: Bird, in Marathi. Almost every name here is the Marathi one used by birdwatchers in the Western Ghats rather than a translation from English. दयाळ is the oriental magpie-robin, साळुंकी the common myna, खंड्या the kingfisher, भारद्वाज the greater coucal, सुगरण the baya weaver whose hanging nest is a Maharashtrian roadside sight, and टिटवी the red-wattled lapwing. Five of them — कावळा, बगळा, दयाळ, साळुंकी, कोकिळा — carry ळ and so have no Hindi spelling at all.
