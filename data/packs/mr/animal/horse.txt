description: Horse breed, in Marathi. भीमथडी is Maharashtra's own breed, raised in the Bhima river valley and the mount of the Maratha light cavalry — it is small, hardy and bred for distance rather than height, and it appears in no other locale's list. काठियावाडी and मारवाडी are the neighbouring Gujarat and Rajasthan breeds, recognisable by inward-curving ear tips.
