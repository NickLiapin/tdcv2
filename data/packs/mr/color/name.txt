description: Colour, in Marathi — and the single clearest place in the pack to see what Marathi's THREE grammatical genders cost a config. Every colour ending in -आ here is an adjective that must agree with its noun in three forms, not two: काळा शर्ट (masculine), काळी साडी (feminine), काळं पुस्तक (neuter). Hindi has काला and काली and stops there, so a generator ported from Hindi will produce काळा पुस्तक, which no Marathi speaker would write. The colours that do not end in -आ (केशरी, गुलाबी, आकाशी, चंदेरी, मोरपंखी) are invariant and safe to paste beside any noun — a config that cannot agree its genders should draw only from those. Note also how many are unspellable in Hindi: पिवळा, जांभळा, काळा, शेवाळी, गव्हाळ, हळदी, अबोली and पोवळा all carry ळ. अबोली is named after the abolim flower and has no English name; भगवा is the saffron of the Maratha flag and is a specific shade, not a synonym for orange.
