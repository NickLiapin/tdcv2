description: Medical specialty, in Marathi. The last six are the AYUSH systems, which are government-recognised medical specialities in India with their own five-year degrees, registration councils and hospitals — आयुर्वेद in particular has a large presence in Maharashtra, and a B.A.M.S. holder is a registered medical practitioner. Leaving them out would misdescribe the Indian medical workforce. Note the -शास्त्र compounding described in medical/specialtyCoherent.txt.
