description: Symptom or presenting complaint, in Marathi — and this is the list where the difference between Marathi and Hindi is most visible to anyone who can read either. Marathi's everyday medical words are Sanskrit-derived where Hindi's are Persian- or Arabic-derived: fever is ताप not बुख़ार, jaundice is कावीळ not पीलिया, diarrhoea is जुलाब not दस्त, nausea is मळमळ not मतली, confusion is गोंधळ not भ्रम, weakness is अशक्तपणा not कमज़ोरी. Six of these carry ळ — मळमळ, जुलाब, पुरळ, जळजळ, गोंधळ, कावीळ — so they cannot be spelled in Hindi at all. Since both languages use Devanagari, this vocabulary is the only test there is.
