description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient, in Marathi — no sex or ancestry restriction. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale and ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. The list is ordered by what a Maharashtrian hospital actually records rather than by a European case mix: tuberculosis, dengue, malaria, chikungunya, leptospirosis and typhoid are routine here, and leptospirosis in particular spikes in Mumbai every monsoon when people wade through flooded streets. Note that the everyday Marathi disease vocabulary is Sanskrit-built where Hindi's is Persian-built — Marathi says ताप, कावीळ and जुलाब where Hindi says बुख़ार, पीलिया and दस्त — so this list and its Hindi counterpart are different words, not different spellings of the same words.
