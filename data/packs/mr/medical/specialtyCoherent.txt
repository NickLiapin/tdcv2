description: Medical specialty that has a coherent diagnosis list. Note the Marathi word-building: Marathi forms these with -शास्त्र (हृदयरोगशास्त्र, cardiology) where Hindi uses -विज्ञान as a separate word (हृदय रोग विज्ञान). Same script, same Sanskrit roots, different compounding habit — and Marathi writes it as ONE word, which is the agglutinating instinct that runs through the whole language.
