description: Common Marathi noun. Every one of these carries a LEXICAL gender that a config cannot read off the string and that decides the form of any adjective or past-tense verb attached to it: वेळ is feminine, दिवस masculine, घर neuter, पाणी neuter, नदी feminine. Marathi has three genders, so there is no two-way guess that works. This file is also where the language's agglutination shows: a postposition is not a separate word but a suffix that fuses onto the noun and changes its stem — घर becomes घरात (in the house), घराला (to the house), घरातून (out of the house); पुणे becomes पुण्यात; शाळा becomes शाळेत. Hindi writes घर में as two words with the noun unchanged. So a config building a Marathi phrase cannot concatenate a noun and a postposition with a space — it has to inflect the noun, and that is not a string operation. वेळ, डोळा, पातळी and फूल carry ळ.
