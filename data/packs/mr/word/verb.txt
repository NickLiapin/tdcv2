description: Common Marathi verb in the dictionary form, which ends in -णे and also serves as the verbal noun (वाचणे is both to read and reading). Two warnings a config needs. FIRST, a Marathi finite verb agrees in gender, and there are THREE genders, so one dictionary entry has three past forms: वाचला, वाचली, वाचले. SECOND, in the past tense of a TRANSITIVE verb the agreement is with the object, not the subject, and the subject takes the -ने ending: त्याने पत्र लिहिले. That means a sentence generator has to know the gender of a noun it may have chosen several slots earlier. Hindi shares the ergative pattern but resolves it into two genders; Marathi needs three, so a template ported from Hindi is systematically wrong on neuter objects. मिळणे, खेळणे and ओळखणे carry ळ.
