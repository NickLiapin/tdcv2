description: Common Marathi adjective, given in the MASCULINE singular. This is the file that explains the single most consequential fact about this pack, so read it before using any adjective list here. MARATHI HAS THREE GRAMMATICAL GENDERS — masculine, feminine and neuter — where Hindi has two, and an adjective ending in -आ takes a different ending for each: मोठा मुलगा (masculine boy), मोठी मुलगी (feminine girl), मोठे घर (neuter house, colloquially मोठं घर). The rule reaches further than adjectives: it governs the verb too, and in the past tense of a transitive clause the verb agrees with the OBJECT rather than the subject (त्याने पुस्तक वाचले, neuter; त्याने कादंबरी वाचली, feminine). So a template that joins an adjective to a noun, or a subject to a verb, must know the noun's gender — which is lexical, not guessable from meaning: मुलगा is masculine, मुलगी feminine, and मूल, child, is NEUTER whatever the child's sex. Hindi cannot produce the third form at all, so any generator ported from Hindi will be wrong on every neuter noun. The adjectives in this list that do NOT end in -आ (सुंदर, स्वच्छ, शांत, प्रामाणिक, हुशार) are invariant and safe to paste anywhere; a config without gender information should restrict itself to those. काळा, जवळचा and उथळ carry ळ.
