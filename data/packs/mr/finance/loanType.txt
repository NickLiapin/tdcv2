description: Kind of loan, in Marathi. Three of these are Indian and a European list has no equivalent. सोनेतारण कर्ज is the gold loan — household gold pledged for cash, a mainstream product offered by every bank and a very large part of Indian retail credit. बचत गट कर्ज is lending to a women's self-help group rather than to an individual, the standard rural micro-credit channel in Maharashtra. सावकारी कर्ज is borrowing from a private moneylender at punitive interest; it is illegal in most forms, it is real, and it is the credit of last resort behind much of the state's farm distress. Recording it is more honest than leaving the category clean.
