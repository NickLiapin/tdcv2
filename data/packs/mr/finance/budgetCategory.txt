description: Household budget line, in Marathi. Two entries are Indian and a European budget list has no slot for them. सणसमारंभ is festival spending, which is a real and planned annual line in a Marathi household — Diwali alone means new clothes for everyone, sweets, gifts and often a repainted house. सोसायटी देखभाल शुल्क is the monthly maintenance charge every flat-owner pays their housing society, the Indian equivalent of a service charge and usually the second-largest fixed cost after the loan.
