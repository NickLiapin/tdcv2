description: Job title in Marathi, across sectors. Several are Maharashtrian public offices that exist in no other locale and that a translated list would never produce. तलाठी is the village revenue officer who keeps the 7/12 land record — the single most recognised government post in rural Maharashtra, and the exam for it draws hundreds of thousands of applicants. कोतवाल is his assistant, ग्रामसेवक the village development officer, मंडळ अधिकारी the circle officer above the talathi, and पोलीस पाटील the village police officer. अंगणवाडी सेविका runs the village child-care and nutrition centre and आशा स्वयंसेविका is the accredited village health worker; both are overwhelmingly held by women and both are among the commonest paid roles for a rural Marathi woman. ऊसतोड कामगार, the sugarcane cutter, names a seasonal migrant workforce of several hundred thousand people who move across western Maharashtra every harvest — omitting it would make the dataset describe a state that does not exist.
