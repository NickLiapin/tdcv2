Note that मराठी टंकलेखन — Marathi typing — is a genuine, tested, certificate-bearing skill in Maharashtra: state government clerical posts require a typing certificate in Marathi, and the Devanagari keyboard layouts are a learned competence rather than something a computer literate person simply has. बैलजोडी हाकणे (driving a bullock pair) and ऊसतोड are farm skills that a Maharashtrian labour record genuinely lists.
