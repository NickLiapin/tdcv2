description: Employment or contract type, in Marathi. रोजंदारी — daily wage — is the first category rather than an afterthought, because a very large share of Indian work is paid by the day with no contract at all; कंत्राटी कामगार is the contract-labour status that the Contract Labour Act regulates and that Indian industrial records distinguish sharply from permanent employment. मानधनावर is honorarium-based, the status of anganwadi and ASHA workers, who are officially volunteers rather than employees.
