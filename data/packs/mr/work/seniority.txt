description: Seniority or grade label, in Marathi. The Indian public sector — which employs a very large share of Marathi white-collar workers — grades by वर्ग: वर्ग-१ (gazetted officer) down to वर्ग-४ (attendant and support staff), and a Maharashtrian job advertisement, pay slip and pension order all use these words rather than the corporate ladder above them. Both scales are here because both are real.
locale: mr
