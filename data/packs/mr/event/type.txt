description: Kind of event, in Marathi. This is one of the most locale-specific lists in the pack and almost none of it survives translation. गणेशोत्सव is the ten-day public Ganesh festival that Tilak turned into a mass political institution in 1893 and that shuts Maharashtra down every September; गुढी पाडवा is the Marathi new year, when a decorated pole goes up outside the door. डोहाळेजेवण is the ceremony held for a woman in her seventh month and has no Hindi or English name. मुंज is the thread ceremony. षष्ट्यब्दीपूर्ती and सहस्रचंद्रदर्शन are the formal sixtieth and eighty-first birthday rituals, both of which generate a printed invitation. साहित्य संमेलन is the annual Marathi literary convention, a three-day mass event with an elected president and tens of thousands of attendees, and there is nothing comparable in any other Indian language. The theatre entries — नाटक, संगीत नाटक, एकांकिका स्पर्धा, तमाशा, लावणी, दशावतार, भारूड, कीर्तन, पोवाडा — are living commercial and folk performance forms with their own touring circuits, not heritage revivals, and Hindi has no equivalent to any of them.
