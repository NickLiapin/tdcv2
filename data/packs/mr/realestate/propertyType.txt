description: Kind of property, in Marathi. Indian flats are described by BHK — bedroom, hall, kitchen — so a two-bedroom flat is a २ बीएचके and that is the phrase every listing, advertisement and conversation uses; a config emitting two-bedroom apartment for a Marathi row is not describing the market. वाडा is the traditional courtyard mansion, still bought and sold in old town centres. चाळीतील खोली is a room in a tenement row, which is a real and transacted property type in Mumbai and Pune. गाळा is a commercial unit in a building — a shop-shaped space rather than a shop — and बिनशेती प्लॉट is land converted from agricultural use, a legal status that decides whether anything may be built on it at all.
