description: Trailing element of a Maharashtrian place name. These are the real toponym endings of Maharashtra and several exist nowhere else in India. खुर्द and बुद्रुक are the pair the state's revenue records use to distinguish the lesser and the greater village of the same name (वाघोली खुर्द / वाघोली बुद्रुक) and they appear on thousands of official gazetteer entries; a Hindi list will not have them. -गड and -दुर्ग are fort endings and Maharashtra has some three hundred and fifty forts, so the ending is far more common here than anywhere else. -वाडी, -पाडा and -वस्ती are hamlet words, -पेठ a market ward, and -वली (बोरिवली, कांदिवली) is Konkani coastal. Note that Marathi writes गाव with no nasal where Hindi writes गाँव. Combine with a real toponym; a generated combination does not name a real place.
