description: Leading element of a Maharashtrian place name (नवी मुंबई, जुना बाजार, मोठे खेड). This list is also the shortest demonstration of the fact that reshapes half this pack: Marathi has THREE grammatical genders, so each of these adjectives has three forms and the one you pick is decided by the noun that follows — नवा वाडा (masculine), नवी वाडी (feminine), नवे गाव (neuter). Hindi has two forms and no neuter, so a Hindi-derived generator will produce नवा गाव, which is simply wrong. A config combining a prefix with an address/citySuffix must therefore agree the two, or draw only from the gender-invariant entries lower down the list. Combine with a real toponym; a generated combination does not name a real city and should never be presented as one.
