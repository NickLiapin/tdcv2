description: Building type (Marathi). Four of these have no European equivalent worth translating to. A वाडा is the courtyard mansion of an old Maharashtrian town, built round an open square with rooms on all four sides — the Peshwa's Shaniwar Wada is one. A चाळ is the single-room tenement row with a shared verandah and shared toilets, the housing of industrial Mumbai. A खानावळ is the household eating house that serves a fixed thali to regulars by the month. A नाट्यगृह is a purpose-built playhouse, and Maharashtra has one in nearly every district town because Marathi theatre is a living commercial tradition with its own touring circuit. Note ळ in शाळा, धर्मशाळा, खानावळ and ग्रंथालय: none of those spellings is possible in Hindi.
locale: mr
