description: Gemstone, in Marathi. The first nine are the नवरत्न, the nine gems of Indian astrology — हिरा, माणिक, पाचू, नीलम, पुष्कराज, मोती, पोवळा, गोमेद, वैडूर्य — each matched to a planet and worn on the advice of an astrologer, which is why a Marathi jeweller stocks and names them as a set. That is a live retail category, not folklore. पोवळा (coral) and वैडूर्य carry letters Hindi does not use.
