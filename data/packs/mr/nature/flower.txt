description: Flower, in Marathi. The list leads with what is actually grown, worn and offered in Maharashtra: मोगरा, जाई and जुई are strung into the गजरा a woman wears in her hair; झेंडू is the marigold of every festival garland; सोनचाफा is the golden champa whose scent is a Konkan summer; अबोली is the flower that gives its name to a colour with no English equivalent. कारवी is the one that could not appear in any other locale's list — a Western Ghats shrub that flowers once every seven or eight years and turns whole hillsides purple when it does, drawing crowds to the Kaas plateau. Eight names here carry ळ (कमळ, अबोली, बकुळ, कर्दळ, सूर्यफूल, गुलछडी, ब्रह्मकमळ, सोनकेवडा) and so have no Hindi spelling.
