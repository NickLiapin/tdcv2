description: Tree, in Marathi. आपटा is the one no other locale can have: its leaves are exchanged as symbolic gold on Dussehra across Maharashtra — सोनं म्हणून आपट्याची पानं — and the custom exists nowhere else. वड and पिंपळ, the banyan and the peepal, are the two village trees, and वटपौर्णिमा, the day married women tie thread round a banyan, is named after the first. पळस turns the Deccan orange in February. बाभूळ, पिंपळ, आवळा, जांभूळ, नारळ and पळस all carry ळ and cannot be written in Hindi.
