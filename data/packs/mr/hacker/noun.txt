description: Technical noun, in Marathi — and a good place to see that Marathi coins where Hindi borrows. विदागार is a database (विदा, data, is a Marathi coinage that Hindi does not use), कळफलक is a keyboard, संचिका a file, आज्ञावली a program, संकेतशब्द a password and जाळे a network. All of these are in real use in Marathi textbooks, government IT circulars and Marathi Wikipedia, and none of them is Hindi. कळफलक and जाळे carry ळ, so they cannot even be spelled in Hindi.
