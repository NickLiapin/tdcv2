description: Nationality adjective, in Marathi. Marathi builds most of these on the country name with -ई or -ीय (भारतीय, जपानी, इराणी) and borrows the English adjective for the rest (अमेरिकन, कॅनेडियन). नेपाळी carries ळ and so cannot be written in Hindi, which spells it नेपाली — the same one-letter test that separates the two languages everywhere else in this pack.
locale: mr
