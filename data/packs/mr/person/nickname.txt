description: Marathi pet name — the लाडाचं नाव a family uses at home. This works differently from Hindi and the difference matters to a config. A Hindi घर का नाम is usually unrelated to the given name (a Rajesh is गोलू for life), but the commonest Marathi pet name is DERIVED from the given name by a productive rule: drop the tail and add -या for a man or -ू / -ी for a woman. गणेश becomes गण्या, सचिन becomes सच्या, प्रशांत becomes पश्या, महेश becomes मह्या; वैशाली becomes वैशू, प्राजक्ता becomes प्राजू, मनीषा becomes मनू. So a config MAY compute one from person/male/firstName.txt, which is the opposite of the advice in the Hindi pack. The -या form is masculine and slightly rough — friends and family use it, an official form never does. The rest of the list is the undeivable stock: kinship words used as names (दादा, ताई, बाळ, माई) and the free-standing pet names.
locale: mr
