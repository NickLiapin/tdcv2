description: Marathi name suffix. The Marathi respect particle is -राव and -साहेब rather than the Hindi -जी: a Maharashtrian is addressed as पाटीलसाहेब or यशवंतराव, and both fuse to the name in writing. -पंत is the older Brahmin equivalent (नानापंत), and -शेठ marks a trader. The one that a European locale has no slot for is कै., short for कैलासवासी, written before or after a dead person's name in every Marathi obituary and memorial notice — Hindi uses स्व. for the same job, so a transliterated Hindi pack would put the wrong abbreviation on the deceased. Academic degrees follow the name as they do in English; Marathi writes them with Devanagari letter-names, so B.Sc. is बी.एस्सी. and not the Hindi बी.एससी.
