description: Marathi male given names, ordered by real prominence in Maharashtra. Four things a config cannot infer. FIRST, this list is Marathi, not Hindi, and the script cannot show that because both use Devanagari — the evidence is the words: गोपाळ, तुळशीराम, नीळकंठ, काळू, बाळू, चोखामेळा and बाळासाहेब all carry ळ, the retroflex L Marathi kept from Sanskrit and Hindi does not have (Hindi writes गोपाल, तुलसीराम). SECOND, the -राव / -साहेब / -बा endings are a Marathi honorific layer welded onto the name itself: यशवंतराव, बाळासाहेब, धोंडिबा and कोंडिबा are the names people are actually registered under, not name-plus-title, so a config must not try to strip them. THIRD, the Warkari saint-names (तुकाराम, ज्ञानेश्वर, नामदेव, एकनाथ, चोखामेळा, सावता, निवृत्ती, सोपान, विठोबा, पांडुरंग) are ordinary living given names in Maharashtra rather than religious archaisms, because the Warkari tradition is a living one. FOURTH, Marathi's three grammatical genders start here: every one of these names is masculine and takes masculine agreement on its verb and adjective (तो आला, चांगला मुलगा) — see word/adjective.txt for what that costs a sentence builder. The counts are the default gentle curve; Marathi given names are far less concentrated than surnames, and the ORDER carries the information, not the numbers.
