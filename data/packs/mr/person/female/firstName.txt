description: Marathi female given names, ordered by real prominence in Maharashtra. Three things a config cannot infer. FIRST, the -बाई ending (लक्ष्मीबाई, ताराबाई, सावित्रीबाई, जिजाबाई, हिराबाई, गंगूबाई) is a Marathi generational marker, not a title: it belongs to the name of a woman born before roughly 1960 and is almost absent from anyone born after, so a config generating a young cohort and one generating an old cohort should NOT draw from the same slice of this list. SECOND, the Marathi-only letters are here too and are the only proof this is not Hindi, since both languages use Devanagari: तुळसा, कोकिळा, मंगळा and कमळा carry ळ, the retroflex L Hindi does not have. THIRD, these names are grammatically feminine and drag feminine agreement across the whole clause — ती आली, चांगली मुलगी — which is one of the three genders Marathi distinguishes where Hindi has two; see word/adjective.txt. Surnames do not inflect for gender in Marathi, so a female row takes its family name from person/lastName.txt unchanged. The counts are the default gentle curve and the ORDER, not the numbers, carries the information.
