description: Language, named in Marathi. The list is led by the languages actually spoken in Maharashtra rather than by world speaker counts. Three notes. FIRST, अहिराणी, वऱ्हाडी, मालवणी, आगरी, झाडीबोली and कोळी are DIALECTS OF MARATHI, not separate languages, and they are listed because a Maharashtrian answering the question names them; वऱ्हाडी also contains ऱ्, the eyelash-ra, a letter form Marathi uses and Hindi does not. SECOND, तमिळ and मल्याळम are spelled with ळ in Marathi and with plain ल in Hindi (तमिल, मलयालम) — that one letter is the difference between a Marathi list and a Hindi one written in the same script. THIRD, नेपाळी likewise: Hindi writes नेपाली.
