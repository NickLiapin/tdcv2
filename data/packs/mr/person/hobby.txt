description: Hobby or pastime, in Marathi. Several are specific to Maharashtra and have no equivalent to translate to: किल्ले भटकंती is fort-walking, the state's dominant weekend pastime because Maharashtra has some three hundred and fifty hill forts; वारकरी दिंडी is walking with the pilgrim procession to Pandharpur; लावणी, तमाशा and कीर्तन are live performance traditions people take part in rather than only watch; मल्लखांब is the Maharashtrian pole gymnastic. रांगोळी, the doorstep powder drawing, carries ळ and so cannot be spelled in Hindi at all.
