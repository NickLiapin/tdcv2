description: Marathi honorific placed before a name. Four of these exist in no other language's pack and a config translating from Hindi will lose them. सौ. is सौभाग्यवती, written before a married woman's name on every wedding card, letterhead and nameplate in Maharashtra; कु. is कुमारी for an unmarried woman; चि. is चिरंजीव, written before a young man's name; and चि.सौ.कां. is चिरंजीव सौभाग्यकांक्षिणी, used for a bride-to-be between the engagement and the wedding and for nothing else. ह.भ.प. is हरिभक्तपरायण, the title of a Warkari kirtankar, and it prefixes the name on the poster of every kirtan performance in the state. The kinship words used as address terms — दादा, ताई, माई, आक्का, अण्णा, नाना, आबा, तात्या, काका, मामा — are genuine prefixes in Marathi public life and attach to strangers, not only relatives. ॲड. uses ॲ, a vowel sign Marathi has for the English 'a' of 'advocate' and Hindi does not use.
