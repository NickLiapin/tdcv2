description: Garment and footwear, in Marathi. Five entries are Maharashtrian and appear in no other locale. नऊवारी साडी is the nine-yard sari draped between the legs like trousers, the traditional Maharashtrian drape and still worn at weddings and by Lavani dancers, as against the five-yard sari worn everywhere else in India. फेटा is the Maharashtrian turban, tied fresh for each occasion rather than pre-formed. गांधी टोपी, the white side cap, is Maharashtrian political dress and is still worn by farmers' movements. कोल्हापुरी चप्पल is the hand-stitched leather sandal of Kolhapur, which carries a geographical-indication registration. उपरणे is the shoulder cloth worn with a धोतर-सदरा. The imported garments keep their English names in Marathi speech.
