description: School subjects in Nepal. नेपाली and अङ्ग्रेजी are both compulsory to grade 10, and so is सामाजिक अध्ययन; नैतिक शिक्षा (moral education) and स्वास्थ्य, जनसंख्या तथा वातावरण शिक्षा are compulsory subjects with no European counterpart. संस्कृत is offered as an optional and is compulsory in the Sanskrit-stream schools. The mother tongue subject (मातृभाषा) exists on paper for Nepal's 124 languages and is taught in very few schools.
