description: Fields of study at a Nepali campus. The three most sought-after — MBBS, engineering and a bachelor of business studies — are what a Nepali family aims a child at, and the competition for the free government seats in medicine is ferocious. वन विज्ञान (forestry), कृषि, जलस्रोत इन्जिनियरिङ and पर्वतीय पर्यटन are Nepal's own specialisms; TU's forestry and agriculture campuses at Hetauda and Rampur are national institutions. Note also that a very large share of Nepali students study ABROAD — India, Australia, Japan, Korea, the United States — and the 'no objection letter' issued for it is one of the commonest government documents in the country.
