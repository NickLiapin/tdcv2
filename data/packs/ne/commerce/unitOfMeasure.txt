description: Units a Nepali market uses, and it is not the metric list. पाथी, माना and मुरी are the volume measures grain is actually sold in — a माना is about half a litre, ten माना make a पाथी, twenty पाथी make a मुरी — and they are still used in villages and in land-rent contracts. धार्नी (about 2.4 kg) and पाउ (a quarter of a kg) are weights; a Nepali butcher quotes धार्नी. Land is measured in TWO incompatible systems: रोपनी, आना, पैसा and दाम in the hills, and बिघा, कट्ठा and धुर in the Terai, and no Nepali land record makes sense without knowing which. तोला is the gold weight (11.66 g) that every jeweller prices in. The metric units are official and are what a supermarket label prints, so both live side by side.
