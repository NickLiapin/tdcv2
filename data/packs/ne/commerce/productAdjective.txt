description: Adjectives a Nepali product listing uses. सक्कली ('genuine') and नेपाली माटोको ('of Nepali soil') are the two doing the most work in real Nepali retail: counterfeit and Indian-substituted goods are common enough that 'genuine' is a selling point in itself, and 'Nepali-made' is both a nationalist appeal and a quality claim, especially for tea, honey, pashmina and dairy. हस्तनिर्मित (handmade) sells carpets and thangkas. Note that these are ADJECTIVES and Nepali adjectives ending in -ओ agree with the noun's gender and number (राम्रो साथी, राम्री साथी, राम्रा साथीहरू), so a config pasting one in front of a noun will sometimes produce the wrong ending; the -ई and -A-final ones (सक्कली, बलियो) are safer to concatenate blind.
locale: ne
