description: Store departments in Nepali. The list is written for the shop shapes Nepal actually has: a किराना पसल (the neighbourhood grocery that sells on credit in a ledger), a सटर-sized hardware or cloth shop, a डिपार्टमेन्ट स्टोर in the towns, and the तरकारी बजार. सुनचाँदी is a department in its own right because gold is how Nepali households save — a wedding transfers it, a bank loan is secured against it, and jewellers are correspondingly everywhere. पूजा सामग्री (worship goods) is a shopping category with no European counterpart and a shop on every temple approach.
locale: ne
