description: Materials in Nepali. The distinctively Nepali entries are पश्मिना (the Changthangi goat undercoat that Nepal made its name exporting, and which is routinely faked), अल्लो (Himalayan nettle fibre, woven in the eastern hills), लोक्ता (the daphne bark that Nepali handmade paper is made of, and which is the material of the country's oldest surviving documents), राडीपाखी (hand-felted wool blanket), याकको ऊन, and बाँस, which is a structural material rather than a decorative one — scaffolding, roofing, water pipes and whole houses. साल and सल्ला are the two building timbers.
