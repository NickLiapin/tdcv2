description: Things a Nepali shop sells. The list mixes the imported and the local on purpose, because a Nepali market stall does: instant noodles beside dried nettle, a solar panel beside a stone mortar. Several entries are Nepali-only objects — खुकुरी (the national knife, a tool before it is a weapon), ढाका टोपी, नाम्लो (the head-strap a porter carries a load with, and the reason Nepali loads are carried on the forehead rather than the back), डोको (the conical carrying basket), मादल and सारङ्गी (drum and fiddle), सिलौटो and लोहोरो (the stone slab and roller every kitchen grinds spices on), and गुन्द्रुक and सुकुटी, which are preserved food rather than snacks. वाइ वाइ is a brand name that has become the Nepali word for instant noodles.
locale: ne
