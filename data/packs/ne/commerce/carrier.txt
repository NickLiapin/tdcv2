The Nepali fact here is that the national post is not the default: नेपाल हुलाक सेवा exists but domestic e-commerce moves on private couriers and on the bus — a parcel handed to a night bus conductor at Gongabu with the recipient's phone number written on it is a normal and reliable way to send something to Pokhara, and no dataset models it. Cross-border parcels from India come by road through Birgunj or Biratnagar, and the customs clearance rather than the carrier is what determines the delivery date.
