description: Common Nepali content words, for slugs, usernames, tags and short filler. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Nepali book at all, so this list was written by hand rather than mined from a corpus by data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs. Finite VERBS are deliberately absent and live in word/verb.txt as -नु infinitives, because a Nepali verb agrees with the subject's person, number and honorific level — four levels in the second person — so no single form is safe to paste into a sentence. The nouns are safe as tags but still take their postpositions as suffixes (घर → घरमा, घरबाट, घरको), so sentence use needs inflection rather than concatenation. Nepali and Hindi share Devanagari and no character test separates them; the separation here is LEXICAL, and this list carries a lot of it — केटा, केटी, मान्छे, कुकुर, बिरालो, झ्याल, ढोका, कोठा, बाटो, पसल, खोला, रुख, चरा, भात, नुन, बिहान, भोलि, हिजो, राम्रो, ठूलो, सानो, धेरै and छ are Nepali where Hindi has लड़का, लड़की, आदमी, कुत्ता, बिल्ली, खिड़की, दरवाज़ा, कमरा, रास्ता, दुकान, नदी, पेड़, चिड़िया, चावल, नमक, सुबह, कल, कल, अच्छा, बड़ा, छोटा, बहुत and है. Note also the twelve Bikram Sambat month names in the middle of the list: they are the months a Nepali actually uses, and they are here as WORDS; the calendar itself belongs to the Nepal country pack. One entry is a deliberate HOMOGRAPH and will trip a naive Hindi-detector: जून is the Nepali word for the MOON (जूनको उज्यालो, moonlight), and it happens to be spelled exactly like Hindi's name for the month of June. Nepali's June is जुन, without the long vowel. Finding जून in a Nepali list is therefore not evidence of Hindi contamination, which is a small illustration of why single-token tests have to be read rather than trusted.
