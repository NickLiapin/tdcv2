description: Diagnoses recorded for men, in Nepali. Two entries are Nepal-specific rather than generic male medicine: खैनीजन्य मुख रोग and मुखको क्यान्सर are here because chewing tobacco (खैनी, सुर्ती, गुट्खा) is a predominantly male habit in Nepal and oral cancer is accordingly one of the commonest cancers in Nepali men, well ahead of its rank in Europe; and सिलिकोसिस because it is the occupational disease of the stone-crushing and mining work that Nepali men do at home and as migrant labour in the Gulf. मदिराजन्य कलेजो रोग likewise: alcoholic liver disease is a leading cause of male death in Nepal and home-brewed रक्सी is not measured in any survey.
