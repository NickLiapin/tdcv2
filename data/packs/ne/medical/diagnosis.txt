description: General diagnoses in Nepali. The head of the list is Nepal's real burden of disease rather than Europe's: क्षयरोग (tuberculosis) is still endemic and notifiable, टाइफाइड and झाडापखाला are water-borne and seasonal, डेङ्गु has become an annual Kathmandu epidemic since 2019, कालाजार and औलो persist in the Terai, and खोपजन्य रोग like दादुरा recur where coverage lapses. उच्च लाग्ने रोग (acute mountain sickness), उच्च हिमाली फोक्सो सुन्निने and मस्तिष्क सुन्निने are altitude illnesses that are ordinary Nepali diagnoses and exotic elsewhere. सर्पदंश and कुकुरको टोकाइ (snakebite and dog bite, for rabies) are common enough in the Terai to be public health programmes. Alongside them sit the non-communicable diseases that now kill most Nepalis — मधुमेह, उच्च रक्तचाप, मुटुरोग and सीओपीडी — because Nepal is in the middle of that transition rather than at either end of it.
