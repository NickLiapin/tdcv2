description: Generic drug names as a Nepali prescription writes them, in Devanagari. Nepal's pharmacies dispense largely Indian-made generics and a great deal of medicine is sold without a prescription over the counter, antibiotics included — which is why antimicrobial resistance is a stated national health emergency. जीवनजल is the Nepali oral rehydration salt sachet, a brand name that became the word for ORS and one of the most consequential public health products in the country's history. आइरन र फोलिक एसिड and भिटामिन ए are distributed as national programmes rather than sold.
