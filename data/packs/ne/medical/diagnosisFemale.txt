description: Diagnoses recorded for women, in Nepali. Three of these are not generic women's medicine but specifically Nepali, and are here because a synthetic Nepali health record without them is a European record with Nepali labels. पाठेघर खस्ने — uterine prolapse — affects an estimated one Nepali woman in ten and something like six hundred thousand women live with it, the consequence of heavy carrying resumed within days of childbirth on steep ground; it is a national health programme in its own right and it is rare enough in Europe to be a specialist referral. अब्स्ट्रेटिक फिस्टुला follows obstructed labour far from a road. चुलोको धुवाँबाट हुने फोक्सो रोग is chronic lung disease from cooking on an indoor wood or dung stove, which is a women's occupational disease in Nepal and is why Nepali COPD is not a smokers' diagnosis the way European COPD is. पाठेघरको मुखको क्यान्सर (cervical) is the commonest cancer in Nepali women, not breast cancer.
