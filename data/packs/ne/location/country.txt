description: Country and territory names in Nepali. These follow Nepali orthographic habits rather than Hindi ones, and the differences are systematic enough to be a test of which language the list is in. Nepali writes an English 'v' as भ (भियतनाम, भेनेजुएला, मोल्डोभा, स्लोभाकिया) where Hindi writes व. Nepali writes -ल्यान्ड (थाइल्यान्ड, पोल्यान्ड, फिनल्यान्ड, न्युजिल्यान्ड, आइसल्यान्ड) where Hindi writes -लैंड. Nepali does not use the ऑ vowel sign for a borrowed English 'o', so अस्ट्रेलिया and अस्ट्रिया, where Hindi and Marathi have ऑस्ट्रेलिया and ऑस्ट्रिया. And बेलायत for the United Kingdom is Nepali's own word, borrowed through Persian, with no Hindi counterpart at all — Hindi says ब्रिटेन. The list is unweighted and roughly alphabetical by Nepali order; for a population-weighted draw with capitals and currencies attached, use geo/country.txt and its coherent children instead.
