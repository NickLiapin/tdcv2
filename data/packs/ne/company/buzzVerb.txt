description: Verb a Nepali company slogan uses. These are given in the -न्छौं form (first person plural present) because that is how a Nepali company talks about itself — हामी जोड्छौं, 'we connect' — rather than in the dictionary -नु form, which cannot be pasted into a sentence. Nepali verbs agree with person, number and honorific level, so a config concatenating one of these to an arbitrary subject can still produce a mismatch; the safest use is as a slogan line on its own.
