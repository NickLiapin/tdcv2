---
description: Word used in a Nepali company name. Nepali business naming reaches first for the mountains (सगरमाथा, अन्नपूर्ण, धौलागिरि, मकालु, हिमालय, गौरीशंकर), then for the gods (पशुपति, गणेश, लक्ष्मी, सरस्वती, बुद्ध, शिव), then for the rivers and the country itself. A shop called लक्ष्मी is invoking the goddess of wealth, and there are thousands of them. एभरेस्ट and सगरमाथा are the same mountain in two languages and both are used as brand names, sometimes by the same company. The auspicious openers शुभ, श्री, जय, सिद्धि and मंगल go in front, and the modern abstractions (डिजिटल, ग्लोबल, स्मार्ट, नेक्स्ट) go on the end of anything founded after 2010.
locale: ne
