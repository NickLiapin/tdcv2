description: Legal form written after a Nepali company name. प्रा.लि. — प्राइभेट लिमिटेड — is the overwhelming default and is what almost every registered Nepali business is. The three worth knowing beyond it: सहकारी संस्था लि., the cooperative, which in Nepal is a major financial institution and not a niche form — there are over thirty thousand registered cooperatives and they hold a large share of rural savings; and उपभोक्ता समिति, the users' committee, which is the legal entity that local infrastructure is actually built through, so a road or a water scheme has a users' committee rather than a contractor. आमा समूह (mothers' group) is the same idea at village level and holds real money. The Latin forms Pvt. Ltd. and Ltd. appear on the same signboards and are equally correct.
locale: ne
