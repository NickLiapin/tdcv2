Nepali gaming vocabulary is English almost entirely, spelled in Devanagari when written at all and usually just left in Latin — PUBG Mobile and Free Fire are what Nepal plays, on phones, in internet cafes and over mobile data. The two Nepali-flavoured entries are योद्धा and तान्त्रिक, which turn up in Nepali-made mobile games and in translated fantasy.
