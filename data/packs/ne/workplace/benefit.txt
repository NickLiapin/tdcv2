description: Employment benefits in Nepal. चाडपर्व बोनस is statutory and is the one no European list has: the Labour Act requires an annual festival allowance, paid before Dashain, of one month's salary, and Nepali household spending spikes around it. सञ्चय कोष (the Employees Provident Fund) and उपदान (gratuity) are the two compulsory contributions, and सामाजिक सुरक्षा कोष is the newer social security fund every formal employer must enrol staff in. खाजा खर्च (a tea-and-snack allowance) and खाजा समय are ordinary line items. Note that the great majority of Nepali workers are informal and receive none of this.
locale: ne
