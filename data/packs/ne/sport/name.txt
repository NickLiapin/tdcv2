description: Sports in Nepali. भलिबल is the national sport, declared so in 2017. दन्डी बियो, कबड्डी, खो खो and गुच्चा are the village games children actually play; टप्पी and चङ्गा (kite fighting, at Dashain, with glass-coated string) are seasonal. पर्वतारोहण is the one Nepal leads the world in and it is an industry as much as a sport. तेक्वान्दो and कराते are where Nepal's international medals come from. एलिफेन्ट पोलो was invented in Nepal, at Meghauli in Chitwan, and the world championship was held there for thirty years until it was stopped over animal welfare — it is included because it is genuinely Nepali and genuinely over.
locale: ne
