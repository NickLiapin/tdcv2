description: Nepali familiar names. The BIRTH-ORDER set at the head is the thing a config cannot infer and the thing that makes a Nepali name list Nepali: जेठा/जेठी eldest, माइला/माइली second, साइँला/साइँली third, काइँला/काइँली fourth, and कान्छा/कान्छी youngest, with ठूलो/ठूली for a big or elder one and सानु/सानी for a small or younger one. These are not pet names in the European sense — they are what a person is called by everyone including their spouse for their whole life, they go on shop signs (कान्छाको पसल), and they end up as legal given names on citizenship certificates often enough that कान्छा appears in person/male/firstName.txt too. The -ए/-े diminutives (बिर्खे, गोरे, कालु, धने) are village names and can be affectionate or belittling depending entirely on who is speaking. नानी and बाबु are used to and by children of either sex.
locale: ne
