description: Languages as Nepali names them. The head of this list is not a world list — it is NEPAL'S own, and that is the point: the 2021 census counted 124 mother tongues in a country of thirty million, and Nepali is the first language of only about 44 per cent of the population. मैथिली, भोजपुरी, थारू, तामाङ, नेपालभाषा (the Newar language, which is Tibeto-Burman and unrelated to Nepali), बज्जिका, मगर, डोटेली, उर्दू, अवधी and लिम्बू each have over half a million speakers. So a Nepali person's mother tongue is very often NOT Nepali, and a dataset that assumes it is has erased a third of the country. Note the spellings: Nepali writes अङ्ग्रेजी with the ङ् conjunct where Hindi writes अंग्रेज़ी with anusvara and a nukta, चिनियाँ where Hindi writes चीनी, and फ्रान्सेली where Hindi writes फ़्रांसीसी.
