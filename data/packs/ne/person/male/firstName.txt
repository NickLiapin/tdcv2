description: Nepali men's given names. The counts are the project's default gentle curve over an ordering by rough prominence, not a measurement: Nepal publishes no name-frequency table. FOUR things a config cannot infer. FIRST, a Nepali man's full name is usually THREE words, not two, and the middle word is not a second given name — it is one of a small closed set of honorific elements (बहादुर, प्रसाद, कुमार, राज, नारायण, मान, लाल, बाबु) that almost every name carries; see person/male/middleName.txt and person/male/fullName.tdc. राम बहादुर थापा and कृष्ण प्रसाद भट्टराई are the ordinary shape, and a two-word Nepali name looks abbreviated to a Nepali reader. SECOND, the Sherpa and Tamang names in this list — दावा, मिङ्मार, लाक्पा, फुर्बा, पासाङ, पेम्बा, न्यीमा — are the seven days of the Tibetan week, given for the day the child was born, so they are shared with the female list and a Sherpa household of nine can hold two दावा. THIRD, कान्छा (youngest), काजी and कालु are birth-order and appearance nicknames that become legal given names on documents; see person/nickname.txt. FOURTH, the Muslim names at the tail (मोहम्मद, अब्दुल, सलिम, रफिक) belong with the मुस्लिम surname pool and not with the others, and मोहम्मद is normally a prefix to a second name rather than a name used alone.
