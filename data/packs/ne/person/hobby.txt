description: Pastimes as a Nepali would name them. Several are specific to Nepal and would be odd anywhere else: भजन कीर्तन (devotional singing in a group, the commonest evening activity in a hill village), डाँडा घुम्ने (walking up a ridge, the standard Saturday outing since Saturday is the only day off), कबड्डी and डन्डी बियो as village games, माउन्टेन बाइकिङ and ट्रेकिङ which are both leisure and a livelihood, चियाघरमा गफ (tea-shop conversation) which is a named pastime and not a joke, and तास खेल्ने — cards, played for money at Tihar as a matter of custom and technically illegal the rest of the year. The English loans (फुटबल, क्रिकेट, भलिबल) keep their Nepali spelling: भलिबल with भ, not व, is the Nepali habit of writing an English v.
locale: ne
