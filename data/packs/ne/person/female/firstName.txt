description: Nepali women's given names, default gentle curve over a prominence ordering; Nepal publishes no name-frequency table. The -माया compound is the pattern with no European counterpart: माया is 'love' and it welds onto a first element to make one word, so धनमाया, फूलमाया, चन्द्रमाया, कृष्णमाया, हरिमाया, सानुमाया and बुद्धिमाया are single given names and not two, and माया also stands alone. It is a rural and older-generation pattern; a woman named फूलमाया is far more likely to be sixty than sixteen, so a config generating a young cohort should weight the shorter Sanskritic names up. The parallel middle-name set is कुमारी, देवी, माया, कला and रानी (person/female/middleName.txt), so सीता कुमारी श्रेष्ठ is the ordinary three-word shape. दावा, पासाङ, न्यीमा, छिरिङ, लाक्पा, पेम्बा and सोनाम also appear in the male list and that is correct, not a slip: they are Tibetan weekday names given for the day of birth and carry no gender. दोल्मा, ल्हामु, याङ्जी, छोकी and दिकी are the Sherpa and Tamang names that ARE female-only. नानी, सानु and मैया are affectionate address terms that end up on citizenship certificates.
locale: ne
