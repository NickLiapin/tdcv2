description: Nationality adjective in Nepali. Nepali builds these on the country name with -ी or -ली (नेपाली, भारतीय, जापानी, कोरियाली, फ्रान्सेली) and does NOT simply borrow the English adjective the way Marathi borrows कॅनेडियन. The one to notice is बेलायती: Nepali's word for Britain is बेलायत, an old borrowing through Persian वilayat, so a British person is बेलायती and never ब्रिटिश in ordinary Nepali — Hindi has ब्रिटिश and इंग्लैंड and no बेलायत at all, which makes this a one-word test of whether a Devanagari list is Nepali. चिनियाँ likewise, where Hindi says चीनी. लाहुरे at the end is not a nationality in the passport sense and is included because it is one: it means a Nepali who has gone abroad to soldier — originally to Lahore, then to the British and Indian armies — and it is the single most common life story behind a Nepali remittance record.
locale: ne
