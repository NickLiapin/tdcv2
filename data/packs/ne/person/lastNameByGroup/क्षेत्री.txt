description: Chhetri and Thakuri surnames — the largest single group in Nepal and the stock the Shah kings and the Rana prime ministers came from. के.सी. and जि.सी. are not abbreviations of anything the bearer spells out: they are खत्री क्षेत्री and घर्ती क्षेत्री written permanently as two Devanagari initials with full stops, and they are ordinary surnames on citizenship certificates, passports and school registers. A parser that strips punctuation from a Nepali name column destroys them. थापा, पुन, आले, बुढा, घर्ती and राना are shared with the Magar (जनजाति) pool and mean different ancestries in the two — थापा alone is borne by hundreds of thousands of Chhetris and hundreds of thousands of Magars. Ordered by rough prominence inside the group; unweighted, because no per-surname frequency count exists for Nepal at all — the census counts ethnic groups, never surnames — and a fabricated curve here would be a claim rather than an ordering. Nepali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this file serves male and female rows alike.
