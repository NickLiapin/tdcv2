description: Hill Brahmin (पहाडी बाहुन) surnames, together with the Dasnami/सन्न्यासी names गिरी, पुरी and भारती that are counted separately in the census but share the same social stratum. Almost all are place-derived or lineage (गोत्र) derived: पोखरेल from Pokhara, ढकाल, लामिछाने and सापकोटा from villages of the same name. शर्मा is not really a surname at all — it is the Brahmin varna title, taken as a written surname by families whose actual clan name is one of the others, so a शर्मा in a Nepali record may be an अधिकारी or a पौडेल who writes शर्मा on documents. K.P. Sharma Oli carries both. Ordered by rough prominence inside the group; unweighted, because no per-surname frequency count exists for Nepal at all — the census counts ethnic groups, never surnames — and a fabricated curve here would be a claim rather than an ordering. Nepali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this file serves male and female rows alike.
locale: ne
