description: Newar surnames — the indigenous people of the Kathmandu valley, whose language (नेपाल भाषा) is Tibeto-Burman and not Nepali at all. Many of these are OCCUPATIONAL caste names and still transparently say the trade: ताम्राकार coppersmith, नकर्मी ironsmith, तुलाधर scale-holder (merchant), चित्रकार painter, प्रजापति potter, ब्यञ्जनकार cook, शिल्पकार and राजकर्णिकार craftsmen, महर्जन farmer, खड्गी butcher, कापाली funeral musician. बज्राचार्य and शाक्य are the Newar Buddhist priestly castes and are the only surnames in Nepal that mark a hereditary Buddhist clergy. श्रेष्ठ is the commonest Newar surname by a wide margin and is also one of the commonest surnames in the country. Ordered by rough prominence inside the group; unweighted, because no per-surname frequency count exists for Nepal at all — the census counts ethnic groups, never surnames — and a fabricated curve here would be a claim rather than an ordering. Nepali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this file serves male and female rows alike.
