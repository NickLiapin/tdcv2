description: Madhesi surnames — the Nepali-citizen population of the Terai plain, whose home languages are Maithili, Bhojpuri and Bajjika rather than Nepali and whose surname stock is shared with Bihar across the open border. It is a caste stock, not one pool: यादव (the largest single Madhesi group), ठाकुर, मिश्र and झा are at the top of the ritual hierarchy or close to it, while धानुक, केवट, मल्लाह, नुनिया and कानु are backward-caste names. Note that साह and शाह are DIFFERENT surnames that a careless transliteration merges — साह is a Madhesi trading caste name, शाह was the royal house, and they are spelled apart in Devanagari and never in Roman. सिंह appears in three pools at once (Madhesi Rajput, Chhetri and Newar) and says nothing on its own. Ordered by rough prominence inside the group; unweighted, because no per-surname frequency count exists for Nepal at all — the census counts ethnic groups, never surnames — and a fabricated curve here would be a claim rather than an ordering. Nepali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this file serves male and female rows alike.
