description: Indigenous (आदिवासी जनजाति) surnames, hill, mountain and Terai together, because the census counts sixty-odd such peoples and a config drawing them separately would need sixty files. The head of the list is the ethnonym used as a surname — तामाङ, मगर, थारू, राई, गुरुङ, लिम्बू — and behind each of them sit the clan names its members actually use instead: a Tamang may write मोक्तान, घिसिङ, योन्जन, बोम्जन, वाइबा, पाख्रिन or लामा; a Rai writes बान्तवा, चाम्लिङ, कुलुङ, खालिङ or थुलुङ, each of which is also the name of a separate language; a Limbu writes सुब्बा, नेम्वाङ, आङबुहाङ or चेम्जोङ; a Thakali writes गौचन, तुलाचन, सेर्चन or भट्टचन. चौधरी here is the Tharu one, not the Madhesi one — the same string is used by two unrelated groups at opposite ends of the country. लामा is both a Tamang surname and a Buddhist clerical title, and Sherpa families use it for both reasons. Ordered by rough prominence inside the group; unweighted, because no per-surname frequency count exists for Nepal at all — the census counts ethnic groups, never surnames — and a fabricated curve here would be a claim rather than an ordering. Nepali surnames do NOT inflect for gender, so this file serves male and female rows alike.
locale: ne
