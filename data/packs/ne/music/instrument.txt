description: Musical instruments in Nepali. मादल is the two-headed hand drum that is the backbone of Nepali folk music and the instrument a Nepali would name first. सारङ्गी is the four-string bowed fiddle carried by the गन्धर्व caste, whose hereditary job was to travel the hills carrying news in song. मुरली and बाँसुरी are flutes, टुङ्ना a Sherpa and Tamang lute, ढ्याङ्ग्रो the shaman's frame drum, and पञ्चेबाजा the five-instrument wedding ensemble. दमाहा and सहनाई are played by the दमाई caste, which is why a Nepali wedding band and a Nepali caste name are the same word.
