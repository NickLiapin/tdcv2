description: Music genres in Nepali. लोक गीत is the base: Nepal's folk music is regional and instrument-specific, and डोहोरी — the improvised, competitive call-and-response duet sung between a man and a woman until one runs out of verses — is a genre, a venue and an evening's entertainment all at once, with no European equivalent. सेलो is Tamang, and the दमाहा and पञ्चेबाजा ensembles are the wedding music of the hills. आधुनिक गीत is the Nepali light-classical song tradition, भजन the devotional, and नेपाली र्‍याप a large and genuinely popular modern scene. रत्यौली is the women's night-before-the-wedding singing, bawdy by tradition.
locale: ne
