description: Job titles in Nepali. The list is written for Nepal's own labour market, so the head of it is not office work: किसान, ज्यामी, भरिया, घरेलु कामदार, बटुवा? no — the informal jobs are here because they are the majority. भरिया (porter) is a profession with a wage rate, a union and a legal load limit. महिला स्वास्थ्य स्वयंसेविका is the volunteer community health worker in every ward, roughly fifty thousand of them, who deliver most of Nepal's primary health outreach and are unpaid. ट्रेकिङ गाइड, सर्दार and आइस डक्टर are the mountaineering trades — the Icefall Doctors are the Sherpa team that fixes the route through the Khumbu icefall each season. लाहुरे is a soldier abroad, in the British or Indian army. मन्दिरका पुजारी, ज्योतिषी and धामी झाँक्री (the shaman, who is still the first person consulted for illness in much of rural Nepal) are real occupations recorded in real surveys.
