description: Kind of building, in Nepali. The materials matter more here than the labels: about half of Nepal's houses are still माटोको घर or ढुङ्गाको घर — mud-mortared stone or adobe with a slate, thatch or corrugated-iron roof — and that is the single most useful thing about a Nepali dwelling record, because it predicts what the 2015 earthquake did to it and what the rebuilding grant paid for. टहरा is a temporary tin shed and there are still people in them. पाटी and सत्तल are open resthouses at a crossroads or a temple, built for travellers and now often the ward's public shelter; धर्मशाला is the pilgrim version. गुम्बा (Buddhist monastery), मन्दिर and बहाल (a Newar courtyard monastery-house, the building type the Kathmandu valley is actually made of) have no European equivalent. फ्ल्याट and अपार्टमेन्ट are recent and urban; a Nepali who says अपार्टमेन्ट means a flat in a developer's block, which is a Kathmandu phenomenon barely twenty years old.
