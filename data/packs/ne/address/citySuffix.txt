description: The ending of a Nepali place name, and this is a genuinely informative list rather than decoration: a Nepali toponym almost always says what the place IS. -बेसी is a valley floor, -लेक a high shelf, -डाँडा a ridge, -टार a river terrace (which is where flat farmland and therefore towns are), -भञ्ज्याङ a pass, -गाउँ a village, -कोट or -गढी a fort, -घाट a river crossing or cremation ground, -पोखरी a pond, -धाम a pilgrimage site, -चौर a meadow, -खोला a stream, -दह a lake, -बजार a market. So पोखरा, सिन्धुली गढी, नाग्ढुङ्गा, चौतारा and मुग्लिन are readable, and a generated name built from these parts will be readable too. -पुर and -नगर are the Sanskritic town endings shared with India; -टोल is a neighbourhood inside a settlement and belongs one level down.
locale: ne
