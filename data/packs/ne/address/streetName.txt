description: The name element of a Nepali road — but read the caveat first, because it is bigger than the list. NEPAL LARGELY DOES NOT ADDRESS BY STREET. A Nepali address is district, local level (municipality or rural municipality), WARD NUMBER and टोल (neighbourhood), plus a landmark; most houses have no number, most lanes have no name, and post is delivered to a ward office or collected. Only central Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar and a few other towns have named roads at all. So a config building a Nepali address should reach for nepal.geo.localLevel and a ward number, not for this file with a house number bolted on — that shape is an American address wearing Nepali words. Where roads ARE named, these are the elements they are named after: kings and national figures (पृथ्वीनारायण, त्रिभुवन, महेन्द्र, वीरेन्द्र, भानुभक्त, अरनिको), the Kathmandu valley's own old names (कान्ति, धर्म, दरबार, नारायणहिटी), mountains and rivers, and abstractions the republic favours (शान्ति, जनता, गणतन्त्र, सहिद). Combine with address/streetSuffix.txt: पृथ्वी + मार्ग, कान्ति + पथ.
