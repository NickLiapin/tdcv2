description: Word placed before a Nepali settlement name. The vertical pair माथिल्लो and तल्लो — upper and lower — is the one that does real work: Nepali villages come in pairs stacked up a slope, so माथिल्लो पिपल and तल्लो पिपल are two settlements a steep half-hour apart, and the same is true of लेक (highland) and बेसी (valley floor) in address/citySuffix.txt. वारि and पारि (this side and the far side of the river) do the same job horizontally. नयाँ and पुरानो mark a settlement that moved or was rebuilt, very often after a landslide or the 2015 earthquake.
