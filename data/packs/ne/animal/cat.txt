description: Cat breeds. Nepal has essentially no pedigree cat culture — a Nepali cat is a बिरालो and नेपाली बिरालो is the honest first entry — so the rest of this list is the imported breed names a Kathmandu vet or a pet shop would use, spelled in Devanagari as they are pronounced. Persians are the one breed genuinely present in numbers.
