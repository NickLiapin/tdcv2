Nepal's own horse is the जुम्ली घोडा of Karnali and the तट्टु, the small hill pony that carries loads where a jeep cannot go — and load-carrying, not riding, is what a Nepali horse is for. The घोडेजात्रा (horse festival) in Kathmandu is the one day of the year horses are raced. Most of the rest are breed names a Nepali would only meet in print.
