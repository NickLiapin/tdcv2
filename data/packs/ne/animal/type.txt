description: Kinds of animal in Nepali. The head of the list is livestock, because that is what an animal is in most Nepali records: the buffalo (भैंसी) rather than the cow is the country's dairy and meat animal, since killing a cow is illegal and killing a buffalo is not, and the male buffalo (राँगो) is the sacrificial animal at Dashain. चौंरी is the yak-cattle cross that carries loads above 3,000 metres and gives the milk that चुर्पी is made from — it is not a yak, and the distinction matters in a herd record. The wild list is the Chitwan and Bardiya one: एकसिङ्गे गैंडा (the one-horned rhino, whose population Nepal has more than doubled), पाटे बाघ (Bengal tiger), हिउँ चितुवा (snow leopard) and रातो पाँडा are the flagship species of a country whose conservation record is unusually good.
