description: Birds of Nepal in Nepali. डाँफे is first because it is the national bird — the Himalayan monal, whose iridescent plumage is on the tourist board's posters — and it lives between 2,500 and 4,500 metres, which no European bird list has a slot for. काग (house crow) and भँगेरा (sparrow) are the urban birds; गिद्ध, the vulture, is here because Nepal's vultures were nearly wiped out by diclofenac given to cattle and the recovery programme is a well-known Nepali conservation story. सारस is the sarus crane of the Terai, and कालिज, ल्वाँठे and च्याखुरा are the hill pheasants and partridges people actually hunt.
