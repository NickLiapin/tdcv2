description: Dog breeds in Nepali. भोटे कुकुर is the Himalayan mastiff, the thick-coated guard dog of the high villages, and it is the only genuinely Nepali breed on the list. The street dog is not a breed and is not called one, but it is by far the commonest dog in the country and is worshipped once a year: कुकुर तिहार, the second day of Tihar, garlands every dog including the strays. The rest are imported breeds, spelled as Nepali says them.
