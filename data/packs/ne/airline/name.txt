description: Airline names as they appear on a Nepali ticket. Nepali domestic carriers keep their registered English names and Nepali writes them in Devanagari on a boarding announcement, so both forms circulate; the Latin ones are used here because that is what is printed. The Nepali fact behind this list is that domestic aviation is not a luxury but the road: large parts of the country have no motorable link at all, Lukla, Simikot, Jumla and Dolpa are reached only by air, and the STOL fleet flying them is Twin Otters and Dorniers rather than jets. Nepal's air safety record is correspondingly poor and every Nepali carrier is on the EU's banned list, which is why an internationally routed Nepali itinerary changes airline in Kathmandu.
