description: Aircraft type designators. These are manufacturer codes and are the same in every language — A320 is A320 in Nepali — so they are deliberately not transliterated. The Nepali-relevant end of the list is the STOL fleet: the DHC-6 Twin Otter, the Dornier 228 and the LET L-410 are what fly into the mountain strips, and the Y-12 is the Chinese type Nepal Airlines took. The AS350 and the Airbus H125 are the helicopters that do the rescue work above the road head. A Nepali domestic fleet with 737s in it is a fleet that could not land at most of its destinations.
