description: Kinds of insurance sold in Nepal. Penetration is low and life insurance dominates what there is, sold door to door by agents on commission. Two are Nepal-specific: पशु बीमा and बाली बीमा are government-subsidised livestock and crop policies — the state pays a large share of the premium, because a buffalo dying is a household-destroying event — and भूकम्प बीमा became a live product only after 2015, when most damaged houses turned out to be uninsured. विदेश जाने कामदार बीमा is compulsory for migrant workers and is what repatriates a body.
