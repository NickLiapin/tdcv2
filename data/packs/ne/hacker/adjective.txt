Nepali IT prose is heavily calqued — डिजिटल, भर्चुअल and वायरलेस are borrowed, while डिजिटल and स्वचालित sit beside native compounds like सुरक्षित and बहुस्तरीय. Nepali adjectives in -ओ agree with the noun, so the borrowed and -ित forms here are the ones safe to concatenate.
