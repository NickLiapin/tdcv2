description: Where an event is held in Nepal. पार्टी प्यालेस is a Nepali institution and a Nepali word: a purpose-built wedding-and-banquet hall, of which Kathmandu has hundreds, and a Nepali wedding happens in one rather than in a hotel or a church. चौतारी is the stone platform under a peepal and banyan tree at the edge of a village where people rest and meet, and it is where a village discussion actually happens. डबली is the raised platform in a Newar square where masked dances are performed. सामुदायिक भवन (community building) and वडा कार्यालय are where a ward's public meetings are held.
