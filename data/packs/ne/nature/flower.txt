description: Flowers in Nepali. लालीगुराँस is first because it is the NATIONAL FLOWER — the red rhododendron, which forests whole hillsides between 1,500 and 3,500 metres and turns them scarlet in March. सयपत्री (marigold) is the flower of Tihar: the garlands that go round every brother's neck at भाइटीका and round every dog, cow and doorway are made of it, so its price spikes for a week each autumn. मखमली (globe amaranth) is the other Tihar flower, kept because it dries without wilting. सुनगाभा is the orchid, of which Nepal has several hundred species.
