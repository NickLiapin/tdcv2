description: Gemstones in Nepali. Nepali gem vocabulary comes from Vedic astrology, where nine stones (नवरत्न) are worn to strengthen nine planets, and a jeweller and an astrologer are consulted together — so माणिक for the Sun, मोती for the Moon, पन्ना for Mercury and so on. रुद्राक्ष is not a gem but a seed and is included because it is worn and traded exactly like one, priced by the number of faces on it.
locale: ne
