description: Colour names in Nepali. Two Nepali colour words have no clean English equivalent and both are in daily use: प्याजी is 'onion', a pale pink-purple, and it is one of the commonest colours named in a Nepali clothes shop; कैलो is the light brown of hair and eyes, used of people, and a कैलो-eyed Nepali is remarked on. सिन्दुरे is the vermilion of the सिन्दुर a married Hindu woman wears in her parting — a colour with a social meaning, not just a hue. रातो leads the list because red is the auspicious colour of Nepal: the flag, the tika, the bridal sari and the temple are all red, and white is the colour of mourning rather than of weddings, which inverts the European association exactly.
