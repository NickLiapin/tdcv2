description: Film genres in Nepali. The Nepali film industry is called कोलिवुड, and its dominant genres are not Hollywood's: सामाजिक (social drama, usually about family, caste or migration) is the mainstream form, लोक (folk, built around songs and village settings) sells in the hills, and द्वन्द्वकालीन — films set during the 1996-2006 Maoist conflict — is a genre in its own right that a Nepali audience recognises immediately. वैदेशिक रोजगार films about the Gulf are a recurring subject. Hindi-language cinema plays in Nepali halls alongside Nepali films and is a bigger draw.
