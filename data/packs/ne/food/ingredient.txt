description: Everyday cooking ingredients in Nepali. चामल (uncooked rice) and भात (cooked rice) are different words and a config must not swap them. घिउ is clarified butter and the everyday cooking fat is तोरीको तेल, mustard oil, whose smell is as characteristic of a Nepali kitchen as olive oil is of an Italian one. गहत is horse gram, cooked into a soup taken for kidney stones and in winter, and it is a Nepali staple with almost no presence in European cooking. चुर्पी is rock-hard dried yak or chauri cheese, chewed for hours, and is sold as a dog chew abroad.
