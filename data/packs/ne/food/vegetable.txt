description: Vegetables in Nepali. सिस्नो (stinging nettle) is a cooked green in Nepal and not a weed — it is the poor season's vegetable in the hills and is a proverb for hardship. तामा is fermented bamboo shoot, cooked with black-eyed beans and potato into आलु तामा, and it is one of the flavours that most marks Nepali food out from Indian. निगुरो is the fiddlehead fern of the monsoon. रायोको साग, the mustard green, is the everyday leaf and is what गुन्द्रुक is usually made from. फर्सी, इस्कुस (chayote, whose shoots are eaten as well as the fruit) and करेला fill the hill kitchen garden.
locale: ne
