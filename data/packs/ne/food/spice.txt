description: Spices in Nepali. तिमुर is the one to know: Sichuan pepper, which numbs the tongue, and which is the single most identifying flavour of Nepali food — तिमुरको छोप, the chilli-and-timur dip, is on every Thakali table and exists nowhere in Indian cooking. जिम्बु is a dried Himalayan allium, fried in ghee and dropped into dal, and it is likewise Nepal-only. अलैँची here means the large black cardamom of the eastern hills, which is Nepal's biggest spice export by value and a different thing from the green cardamom Indian sweets use. भाङ्गोको छोप (hemp-seed chutney) and सिल्टिमुर are regional. The rest are shared with the subcontinent.
