description: Fruit in Nepali. Nepal's altitude range means it grows both tropical and temperate fruit within a hundred kilometres: आँप, केरा, लिची and मेवा in the Terai, सुन्तला (the Nepali mandarin, a major cash crop of the mid hills) and नास्पाती higher up, and स्याउ in Mustang and Jumla above 2,500 metres where nothing else pays. काफल is the Himalayan bayberry, which cannot be cultivated and is gathered wild in June — it is the fruit Nepali songs are about. लप्सी (hog plum) is Nepal's own and is eaten as a sour candy more than as a fruit; it grows essentially only here. आमला, बयर, ऐंसेलु and चुत्रो are the hedgerow fruit children eat walking to school.
