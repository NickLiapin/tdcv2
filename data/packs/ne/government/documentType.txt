description: Kinds of official document in Nepal. नागरिकता प्रमाणपत्र is the master document — until the National ID rolls out it is what proves you are Nepali, and you cannot open a bank account, buy land, sit an exam, get a passport, take a SIM or vote without it. सिफारिस, the ward recommendation letter, is the document that unlocks the others and has no European counterpart at all: it is a letter from your ward office saying you are who you say you are and live where you say you live, and it is required before almost every other transaction. लालपुर्जा is the land ownership certificate, the single most valuable piece of paper a Nepali household owns and the usual collateral for any loan. The specific number FORMATS on these documents belong to the Nepal country pack (nepal.docs.*), not here; this list holds the NAMES of the kinds of document.
