description: Features a Nepali property listing advertises. २४ घण्टा पानी is the headline feature in Kathmandu, where the municipal supply runs for a few hours every few days and households buy tanker water; इनार and बोरिङ (well and borehole) are therefore selling points. ब्याकअप बिजुली and इन्भर्टर are legacies of the load-shedding years and are still advertised. पिच रोड सम्म पहुँच — reachable by a metalled road — is a genuine amenity in a country where a great many properties are not.
