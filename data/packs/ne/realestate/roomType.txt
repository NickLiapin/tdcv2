description: Rooms in a Nepali house. पूजा कोठा is a room in its own right in most Hindu Nepali homes and appears in floor plans. भान्सा (the kitchen) carries a purity rule in traditional households — outsiders and lower castes were kept out of it, and in some families still are. दलान is the covered porch, आँगन the beaten-earth yard where grain is dried and weddings are held, and बुइगल the loft under the roof used for storage.
