description: Common Nepali adverbs. अहिले, भोलि and हिजो are worth noting against Hindi: Nepali has separate words for yesterday (हिजो) and tomorrow (भोलि), where Hindi uses the SAME word कल for both and lets the verb tense decide. That is a real structural difference and a good marker.
locale: ne
