description: Common Nepali nouns. Nepali nouns have no grammatical gender in the Hindi or Marathi sense — the verb does not agree with the noun's gender the way it does in Hindi — which is one of the structural differences between the two languages and makes Nepali nouns safer to paste into a generated sentence than Hindi ones. They DO take their postpositions as suffixes (घरमा 'in the house', साथीलाई 'to the friend', बाटोबाट 'from the road'), so a config dropping one into a sentence still has to attach the right ending. Several of these are the Nepali word where Hindi has a completely different one, which is the vocabulary evidence that this pack is Nepali: केटा/केटी against Hindi लड़का/लड़की, मान्छे against आदमी, कुकुर against कुत्ता, बिरालो against बिल्ली, झ्याल against खिड़की, ढोका against दरवाज़ा, कोठा against कमरा, बाटो against रास्ता, पसल against दुकान, खोला against नदी, रुख against पेड़, चरा against चिड़िया, भात against चावल, नुन against नमक, बिहान against सुबह, भोलि/हिजो against कल.
