description: Common Nepali verbs, in the -नु infinitive, which is the dictionary form. NEPALI VERBS CANNOT BE PASTED INTO A SENTENCE IN THIS FORM: a finite Nepali verb agrees with the subject's person, number AND honorific level, of which there are four in the second person, so ऊ गर्छ, तिमी गर्छौ, तपाईं गर्नुहुन्छ and हजुर गर्नुहुन्छ are all 'you do' with different endings. That makes this list safe for dictionary, tag and label use and unsafe for sentence assembly, which is why word/verb.txt and text/sentence.txt are different files with different jobs. The verbs themselves carry the strongest Nepali-versus-Hindi evidence in the pack: हुनु/छ (Nepali copula, where Hindi has है), गर्नु (Hindi करना), भन्नु (Hindi कहना), जानु/आउनु shared, हेर्नु (Hindi देखना), खानु shared, दिनु shared, लिनु shared, बस्नु shared, हिँड्नु (Hindi चलना), भेट्नु (Hindi मिलना), बुझ्नु (Hindi समझना), पठाउनु (Hindi भेजना), राख्नु shared, लेख्नु shared.
