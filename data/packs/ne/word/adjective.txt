description: Common Nepali adjectives. The ones ending in -ओ AGREE with the noun in gender and number — राम्रो केटा, राम्री केटी, राम्रा केटाहरू — so a config concatenating one blind will sometimes produce the wrong ending; the adjectives not ending in -ओ (गरिब, धनी, सजिलो is -ओ, ठीक, फरक) are safe. These are also strong Nepali-versus-Hindi markers: राम्रो where Hindi says अच्छा, ठूलो where Hindi says बड़ा, सानो where Hindi says छोटा, धेरै where Hindi says बहुत, नराम्रो where Hindi says बुरा.
locale: ne
