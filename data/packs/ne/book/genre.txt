description: Book genres in Nepali. नियात्रा is the one with no English single word: it is the Nepali travel-essay, a major and much-read literary form built on walking through the country, and it is a genre heading in every Nepali bookshop. मुक्तक (a four-line epigram) and गजल (ghazal, borrowed from Urdu and hugely popular in Nepali) are likewise shelf categories rather than niche terms. लोककथा and वंशावली — folk tale and genealogy — are the traditional prose forms.
