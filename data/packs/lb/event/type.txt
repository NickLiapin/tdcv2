description: Kinds of event. SCHUEBERFOUER heads the list and is not a generic funfair: it is the annual fair of Luxembourg City, founded by John the Blind in 1340, and it is the fixed point of the country's year. OKTAV is the other one — a religious pilgrimage that fills the capital every spring. Both are language, not institutions: they are the words for these kinds of occasion.
