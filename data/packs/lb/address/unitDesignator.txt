description: The word in front of a sub-address. Note how many of these are FRENCH words used inside Luxembourgish — Appartement, Etage, Bloc, Entrée, Escalier, Boîte, Local — because the building trade and the postal system in this country operate in French, and a Luxembourgish speaker reads them without switching. Stack is the native word for a storey and coexists with Etage in the same sentence.
