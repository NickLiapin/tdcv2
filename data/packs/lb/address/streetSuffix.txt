description: The generic tail of a Luxembourgish street name. These are the words themselves, all LOD lemmas: Strooss (street), Wee (way), Gaass (lane), Plaz (square), Bierg (hill), Pad (path), Allee, Millen (mill), Baach (brook). In real names they are written JOINED to the specifier and not spaced — Haaptstrooss, not Haapt Strooss — which is the German compounding habit and the opposite of the French forms that dominate the same register (Rue de l'Eglise). Avenue, Boulevard, Route, Rue and Montée are in the list because they are LOD lemmas too and because a Luxembourgish speaker uses them without switching language.
