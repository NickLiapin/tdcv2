description: Kinds of building, in Luxembourgish. Two entries carry a local fact. HAUS is worth reading beside the Luxembourg country pack: the commonest "street name" in the whole national register is the French word Maison, because a village too small to name its streets numbers its houses instead, so "Maison 12" is a complete legal address here. And RESIDENCE — the French word, used in Luxembourgish — is what an apartment block is called on its own front, which is why so many Luxembourg addresses read "Résidence" plus a name. Forms are LOD lemmas.
