description: Garments. Read the French down the middle of this list: JUPE is a skirt, BLUS and CHEMISIER a blouse, PALTONG a jacket (from paletot), FOULARD a scarf, CEINTURE a belt, MAILLOT a swimsuit, KOSTÜM a suit. The plain everyday clothes keep Germanic names — Hiem, Box, Schong, Strëmp, Händsch, Hutt — so once again the domestic half of the wardrobe is Germanic and the tailored half is French. BOX is the Luxembourgish word for a pair of trousers and is a false friend for an English reader. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
