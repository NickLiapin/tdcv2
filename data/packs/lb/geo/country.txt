description: The countries of the world as the Luxembourgish language names them, weighted by population in millions. Every name here was resolved out of the LOD — the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire, the state dictionary published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch — and not translated by hand; a country whose Luxembourgish name the LOD does not carry was left out rather than guessed at, which is why this list is 67 countries and not 200. Read it beside a German list and two things show. Most names ARE close to German, because Luxembourgers learned to read in German and the geography came with the schoolbooks: Italien, Polen, Japan, Kanada are identical. But the near neighbours are all different — Frankräich for Frankreich, Éisträich for Österreich, Schwäiz for Schweiz, Belsch for Belgien, Griicheland for Griechenland, Däitschland for Deutschland — and Morocco is MAROC and Cape Verde is CAP VERT, taken straight from French where German says Marokko and Kapverden. Then there is the one that shows the grammar: ANTIGUA A BARBUDA. Not "an". The Eifel Rule (see word/verb.txt) drops the final -n of "an" because Barbuda begins with a b, and the country's name is therefore spelled differently in Luxembourgish than the same conjunction would be before a vowel. The same rule is why the LOD writes "Vereenegt Staate vun Amerika" with Staate and no -n. A rule of pronunciation that changes how a proper noun is spelled is not something a German-trained transliterator would ever produce. Source: LOD, extract of 27 July 2026.
