description: Insects and small crawling things. PÄIPERLEK (butterfly) and KIEWERLEK (beetle) rhyme and both end in the diminutive-looking -lek that is not a diminutive; SEEJOMES is the LOD's second word for an ant beside the transparent Ameis; BOXEKUEDER is glossed "fly" and means, literally, a trouser-scratcher. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
