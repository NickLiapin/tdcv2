description: Kinds of animal in Luxembourgish. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand. Three entries carry the language: KÉISEKER is the hedgehog and the word has no German cousin at all (German Igel), KAWEECHELCHEN is the squirrel, and PÄIPERLEK is the butterfly — three of the most-cited Luxembourgish words precisely because a German speaker cannot guess any of them. Note also the two-word plant-and-animal names the LOD writes with a space and a capital, Wëllt Schwäin and Wëll Kanéngchen: the adjective is inflected, so a config that lower-cases or splits these breaks them.
