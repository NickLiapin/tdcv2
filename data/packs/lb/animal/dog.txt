description: Dog breeds as Luxembourgish writes them. Most breed names are international and are NOT translated — Beagle, Boxer, Chihuahua are the same string everywhere and translating them would be an error. What IS Luxembourgish is the everyday word for a dog: the LOD gives Hond and MUPP, and Mupp is the word a Luxembourger actually says. The Bouvier des Flandres and the Berger belge are named in French here because that is how the kennel and the vet write them.
