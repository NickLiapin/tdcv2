description: Everyday cooking ingredients. MËLLECH, BOTTER, MIEL, BROUT, KÉIS, EE, HUNNEG and SCHMANT are the kitchen's Germanic core — Schmant is cream and is a word German lost outside the Rhineland — while CRÈME, BISCUIT and CHARCUTERIE came in with French cooking and are written the French way. GEBEESS is jam. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
