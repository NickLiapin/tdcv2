description: Fruit in Luxembourgish. The orchard fruit is Germanic and old — APEL, BIER, PROMM, KIISCHT, KÄSCHT — and the imported fruit is French-shaped: BANANN, MELOUN, ZITROUN, LIMOUN, PAMPLEMOUSSE, APRIKOS. The -OUN ending on melon and lemon is the same rule as in Statioun and Natioun: French -on and -ion come into Luxembourgish as -oun. KRÉISCHEL is a gooseberry and MOLBIER a blueberry, neither guessable from German. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
