description: Vegetables. GROMPER is the potato and is the single most Luxembourgish word in the pack — it is a worn down "Grondbir", ground-pear, it gives the national dish Gromperekichelcher its name, and no German speaker gets it from Kartoffel. Beside it the market vegetables came in from France with their names: CHOUFLEUR, COURGETTE, AUBERGINE, CONCOMBER, PORRETT, POIVRON, NAVET, LAITUE, CHAMPIGNON. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
