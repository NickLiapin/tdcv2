This file went through a correction pass and it is worth saying what it caught, because it is the exact trap this project keeps meeting: written from memory the list had Kommod, Kësseng, Rido, Sof, Bësem, Séef, Kamm, Portemonnaie and Ski in it, and the state dictionary's forms are KOMOUD, KËSSEN, RIDDO, KANNAPEE, BIESEM, SEEF, KAMP, PORTMONNI and SCHI. Every one of those is a real Luxembourgish word spelled the way a German or French speaker would guess rather than the way Luxembourgish spells it, which is precisely how a pack ends up looking like the language and not being it.
