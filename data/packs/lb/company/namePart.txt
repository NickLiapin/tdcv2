description: Elements a Luxembourgish company name is built from. Three habits show. Companies here very often carry a PLACE name (Lëtzebuerger, Musel, Éislek, Minett, Ardennen) because the market is small enough that being from somewhere is a claim. They very often carry a FRENCH abstract noun (Solutions, Services, Conseil, Gestion, Développement) because that is the register of business French. And the international ones simply carry English. All three appear on the same commercial register page.
