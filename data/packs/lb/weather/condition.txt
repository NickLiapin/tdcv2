description: Weather conditions in Luxembourgish. NIWWEL is fog, FRASCHT frost, KEELT cold, HËTZT heat, DRÉCHENT drought. Note that Luxembourgish uses WIEDER for weather and DONNERWIEDER for a thunderstorm, so the compound means literally thunder-weather, and DIMMER serves for both thunder and the storm itself. Every value is a lemma of the LOD, the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire published by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch under the loi du 20 juillet 2018; nothing here was translated by hand.
