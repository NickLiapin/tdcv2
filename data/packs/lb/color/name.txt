description: Colours in Luxembourgish, taken STRAIGHT OUT OF THE LOD's own stock of colour adjectives rather than translated from a German list — which is why it contains words a translator would never have produced: BLUTTROUT (blood-red), FEIERROUT, KNALLROUT, GËFTGRÉNG (poison-green), KUELSCHWAARZ, PECHSCHWAARZ, SCHNÉIWÄISS, HIMMELBLO, ZITROUNEGIEL, SCHASSGIEL, GROBLO, DÄISCHTERBLO, COGNACFAARWEG. The four basic terms are the four that look least like German: ROUT, BLO, GRÉNG, GIEL, and gréng carries the é that marks the language. At the end of the list sit kaki, lila, mauve, indigo, cyan, magenta, crème, ivoire and anthrazit — international colour words that a paint chart or a fabric sample in Luxembourg prints untranslated. Those nine are flagged here as the exception because they are NOT LOD headwords; everything else in this file is.
