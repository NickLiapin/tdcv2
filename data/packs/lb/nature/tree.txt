description: Trees, every value an LOD headword. The suffix to notice is -TER: HIESELTER is a hazel, ALENTER an alder, NËSSERT a walnut tree, MAASSELTER a maple — that ending forming a tree name is a Luxembourgish word-formation habit German does not share, since German simply compounds (Haselstrauch). EECH, BICH and BIERK are oak, beech and birch, all three worn down past the point where a German reader recognises them.
