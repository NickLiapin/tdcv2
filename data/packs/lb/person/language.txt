description: Languages, named in Luxembourgish. The head of this list is not decoration: Luxembourg runs on THREE languages at once by the loi du 24 février 1984 — Lëtzebuergesch is the national language, Franséisch is the language in which the law is written, and Däitsch is the language most people were taught to read in — and Portugisesch is the fourth, spoken at home by more residents than German is. A person record here routinely carries three or four of these and a form that offers one "language" column is mismodelling the country. Names are the LOD's substantive forms.
