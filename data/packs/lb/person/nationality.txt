description: Nationality adjectives in Luxembourgish, in the -esch form the language uses for "of that country". The order at the head follows the country's actual composition rather than the alphabet: after lëtzebuergesch come the three communities that make up just over half of all foreign residents — portugisesch, franséisch, italieenesch — then belsch and däitsch. Forms are the LOD's adjectives; the LOD also carries the noun for a person (Portugis, Fransous, Däitschen, Italieener) and the pejorative Preiss for a German, which is not in this file but is in the dictionary and in daily speech.
