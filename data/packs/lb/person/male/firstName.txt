description: Male given names used in Luxembourg. The head of this list is not a guess: it is the order the national register of natural persons itself publishes, released as open data by the Luxembourg government (RNPP, "Prénoms les plus donnés au Luxembourg"), and it carries the project's default weight curve floor(1000000 * rank ** -0.1) in that order. The tail is NOT ranked — every name in it carries the same weight, the weight of the last ranked name — because the register publishes no order for the older cohorts and inventing one would be a claim this pack cannot support. What the tail is FOR is the thing that makes a Luxembourgish name list unlike a German one: about 47% of residents are foreign nationals, and the three largest communities are Portuguese (about 13% of the whole population), French (about 7%) and Italian (about 4%), so a register of adults in Luxembourg is roughly half Romance. A pack that shipped only the Germanic stock would be wrong about half the country. Note also that the register's own list keeps Lea and Léa, and Sara and Sarah, as SEPARATE entries, and that Lou appears in both the male and the female list — the register does not normalise, and neither does this file. What is NOT here, and was left out on purpose: the given names of the Balkan communities whose surnames are plainly in person/lastName.txt. Their surnames are attested by a bearer count; their given names are not, and guessing at them would have been fabrication. Source: data.public.lu, Prénoms les plus donnés au Luxembourg (RNPP extract), for the head.
