description: The four seasons. FRÉIJOER, spring, is the one that is not German: German says Frühling or Frühjahr and Luxembourgish says Fréijoer, literally "early year", with the é that marks the language. Summer, Hierscht and Wanter are close to their German cousins but not identical in spelling. All four are LOD lemmas.
