description: Filler sentences in Luxembourgish. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Luxembourgish book at all, so there is no public-domain corpus for data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs to draw on and these sixty sentences were composed for this pack. They are deliberately short and ordinary, and every content word in them was checked against the LOD before it was used. READ THEM FOR THE EIFEL RULE, because it is applied throughout and this is the only place in the pack where you can see it running in connected text. The final -n survives before a vowel and before d, t, h, n, z, and disappears before everything else, IN THE SPELLING: "Mir schwätze Lëtzebuergesch" loses the -n of schwätzen before L, but "D'Kanner spillen am Gaart" keeps it before a vowel; "Mir iesse mëttes" drops it before m, "Ech ruffen dech" keeps it before d; "D'Gromperen ... si gutt" has si, not sinn, because gutt begins with g, while "D'Suen sinn op der Bank" keeps sinn before a vowel. The article does the same thing: DEN Zuch, DEN Hierscht, DEN Hond, DEN Owend — before z, h, h and a vowel — against DE Bus, DE Kaffi, DE Wäin, DE Präis. And the possessive: "Merci fir DÄI Bréif" against däin before a vowel. Feed these sentences to a tokeniser that assumes one spelling per word and it will report the same verb twice.
