description: Filler words in Luxembourgish. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Luxembourgish book at all — a search for language code lb returns "No records found", and so does a keyword search for Luxembourgish, so there is no public-domain corpus for data/scripts/build-text-corpus.mjs to be pointed at. Rather than type four hundred words out of my head, this file is an even sample of the LEMMA LIST OF THE STATE DICTIONARY: the Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire, published as open data by the Zenter fir d'Lëtzebuerger Sprooch, 33,941 entries, extract of 27 July 2026, filtered to single-word nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs of 3 to 14 characters and sampled at a fixed stride so the draw is reproducible and covers the whole alphabet. Every word here is therefore a real Luxembourgish word attested by the state's own dictionary, which is a stronger guarantee than handwriting would have given. There is a real corpus behind this pack's word list and NOT behind its sentences: the distinction matters and is why it is stated here.
