description: Punjabi pet name / nickname (ਅੱਲ). Two patterns a European list has no equivalent of: the -ੀ diminutive of a given name (ਜੱਸੀ, ਸੁੱਖੀ, ਦੀਪੀ, ਪੰਮੀ) and the -ੋ ending that marks a woman's pet name (ਨਿੰਮੋ, ਜੀਤੋ, ਪ੍ਰੀਤੋ, ਸ਼ਿੰਦੋ, ਬੀਬੋ). English nicknames (ਹੈਪੀ, ਲੱਕੀ, ਗੋਲਡੀ, ਸ਼ੈਰੀ) are genuinely used in Punjab and are kept in their real spelling.
locale: pa-in
