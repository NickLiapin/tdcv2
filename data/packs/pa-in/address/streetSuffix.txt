description: Punjabi thoroughfare type. Unlike Polish or French, it goes AFTER the name (ਮਾਲ ਰੋਡ, ਗੁਰੂ ਨਾਨਕ ਮਾਰਗ, ਹਾਲ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ), so compose name-then-suffix. ਕਟੜਾ and ਕੂਚਾ are old walled-city block types, ਅੱਡਾ is a transport stop that becomes a place name, and ਪੱਤੀ is a village quarter.
locale: pa-in
