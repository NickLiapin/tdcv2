description: Weekday in Punjabi, Sunday first. Two differ from Hindi and are the ordinary Punjabi words: Thursday is ਵੀਰਵਾਰ (not ਗੁਰੂਵਾਰ) and Saturday is ਸ਼ਨਿੱਚਰਵਾਰ (not ਸ਼ਨਿਵਾਰ). A pack that merely transliterated Hindi would get both wrong.
