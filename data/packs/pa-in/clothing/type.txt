description: Garment in Punjabi. Several have no English equivalent and are not translated: ਪੱਗ / ਦਸਤਾਰ is the Sikh turban, an article of faith rather than a hat; ਕਛਹਿਰਾ is one of the five articles a baptised Sikh wears; ਪਟਿਆਲਾ ਸਲਵਾਰ is the pleated salwar named after Patiala; ਜੁੱਤੀ is the embroidered Punjabi slipper; ਚਾਦਰਾ is the men's wrap worn in villages; ਫੁਲਕਾਰੀ is the embroidered shawl a bride is given.
locale: pa-in
