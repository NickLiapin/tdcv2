description: Musical instrument in Punjabi. ਤੂੰਬੀ (a single-string plucked instrument), ਅਲਗੋਜ਼ੇ (paired flutes played with circular breathing), ਚਿਮਟਾ (fire tongs fitted with jingles) and ਢੋਲ are the Punjabi folk set; ਰਬਾਬ, ਤਾਊਸ, ਦਿਲਰੁਬਾ and ਸਰੰਦਾ are the Sikh kirtan instruments, the rabab associated with Bhai Mardana and the taus and dilruba with the tenth Guru's court.
locale: pa-in
