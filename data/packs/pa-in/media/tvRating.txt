description: Film and television certificate as India issues it. India does NOT use the American G / PG / R scale: the Central Board of Film Certification grants U (unrestricted), U/A with an age (parental guidance), A (adults only) and S (restricted to a specialised audience such as doctors). The list is short because that is the whole scale.
