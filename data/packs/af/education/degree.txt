description: Qualification, in Afrikaans, as awarded in South Africa. Two levels have no United States equivalent and a config cannot infer them. The Honneursgraad is a separate one-year postgraduate degree taken AFTER the three-year bachelor and BEFORE the master's - it is not an "honours" distinction attached to a bachelor's. Nasionale Senior Sertifikaat, universally called matriek, is the school-leaving certificate and the thing a job advertisement asks for. Nasionale Diploma and Gevorderde Diploma come from the universities of technology rather than the traditional universities.
