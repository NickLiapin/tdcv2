description: School mark, on the seven-level achievement scale of the South African National Senior Certificate. This scale replaces the letter grades of the reference en list entirely: marks are reported as a level from 7 down to 1 with the percentage band attached, and a learner needs level 4 in the home language and level 3 in three other subjects to pass. The named descriptors below are the official Afrikaans wording.
