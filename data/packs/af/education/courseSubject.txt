description: School subject as it appears on a South African report card, in Afrikaans. Three of these exist nowhere outside this school system. Lewensoriëntering is a compulsory subject from grade 4 to matric covering health, citizenship and careers. Wiskundige Geletterdheid is a full alternative to Wiskunde that a learner may take instead of it, not a remedial version of it. And a language is taken either as Huistaal or as Eerste Addisionele Taal - the same language, two different syllabuses and two different marks - so "Afrikaans" alone is not a subject.
