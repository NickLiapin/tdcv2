description: Reason a customer gives for returning an item, written as an Afrikaans shopper would write it. Several of these show the Afrikaans double negative, where a negated clause is closed by a second nie: "Dit werk nie soos geadverteer nie". Dutch does not do this, and it is the most reliable single way to tell an Afrikaans sentence from a Dutch one. Where nie already stands at the end of the clause it is not doubled - "Dit werk nie" is complete as it stands.
locale: af
