description: Adjective used in a product title, in Afrikaans. Afrikaans adjectives take an -e ending when they stand in front of the noun they modify and drop it when they stand alone - "’n duursame sak" but "die sak is duursaam" - so these bare forms are the predicative ones and a config that puts them straight in front of a noun should add the -e itself. The rule is not mechanical: monosyllabic adjectives ending in a consonant mostly stay bare (’n groot sak), which is why no single transformation covers the list.
locale: af
