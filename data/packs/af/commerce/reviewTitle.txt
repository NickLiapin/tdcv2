description: Headline a customer puts on a product review, in Afrikaans. Two grammatical facts of the language are visible here and nowhere else in this pack. The first is the double negative: "Sal dit nie weer koop nie" needs the second nie to close the clause, and a config cannot infer it. The second is the indefinite article ’n, written with an apostrophe, ALWAYS lowercase even when it opens a sentence, and passing the capital letter on to the word after it - so the title reads "’n Bietjie te duur", never "’n bietjie" and never "’N bietjie". The apostrophe used throughout this pack is U+2019.
locale: af
