description: Payment method, in Afrikaans. Three of these are South African rather than general: EFT - an electronic funds transfer the buyer pushes from their own banking app - is the dominant way of settling an online invoice here, Onmiddellike EFT is the bank-authorised variant, and Bewaarkoop is layby, still a mainstream way to buy furniture and clothing on a low income. SnapScan and Zapper are local QR wallets and keep their brand spelling.
