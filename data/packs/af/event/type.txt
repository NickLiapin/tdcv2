description: Kind of event, in Afrikaans. Five of these are institutions of Afrikaans community life rather than translations. A kerkbasaar is a church fete and is still the main fundraising event of a country town. Boeresport is a day of traditional field games - tug of war, sack races, jukskei. A kunstefees is one of the large Afrikaans arts festivals that anchor the cultural calendar. Matriekafskeid is the school-leaving formal. And a braai is an event, not a meal.
