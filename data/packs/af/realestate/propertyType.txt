description: Kind of property, in Afrikaans. Three are legal categories of the South African property system rather than descriptions. A deeltiteleenheid is a sectional-title unit, owned with an undivided share of common property, and is a different form of ownership from a freehold house. An erf is the surveyed stand itself. A hoewe is a smallholding on the town edge, usually a hectare or more, and is a recognised zoning class. A sekuriteitskompleks - a walled estate with controlled access - is the dominant new-build form in the metros.
