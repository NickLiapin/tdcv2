description: Verb in its progressive form, in Afrikaans. This address is where Afrikaans and English part company: Afrikaans has NO -ing participle, and there is no single word that does what "backing up" does. The progressive is periphrastic - "aan die rugsteun", literally "at the backing-up" - so every value here is two or three words, and a config that expects one word will produce something ungrammatical. The alternative construction, "besig om te rugsteun", is equally correct and equally not one word.
locale: af
