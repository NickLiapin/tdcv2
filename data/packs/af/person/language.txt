description: Language, named in Afrikaans. The first twelve are the official languages of South Africa - eleven since 1996, twelve since South African Sign Language was added in 2023 - and they lead the list because they are what a South African form actually asks you to choose from. The African-language names are written the way those languages write them, with the lowercase noun-class prefix intact: isiZulu, isiXhosa, siSwati, isiNdebele. The Namibian block follows, since Afrikaans is that country's lingua franca, and Khoekhoegowab is the Nama and Damara language.
