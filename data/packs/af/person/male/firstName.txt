description: Male given names used by Afrikaans speakers, roughly frequency-ordered. Three layers are interleaved rather than segregated, because that is how they occur. The first is the classical Afrikaner stock - Johannes, Petrus, Jacobus, Hendrik, Cornelius - which is what stands on the birth certificate; the shortened form is what the man is actually called, which is why Kobus, Koos, Hannes, Fanie, Piet and Gert appear in this list beside Jacobus and Johannes rather than only in person/nickname.txt. The second is the English-origin stock - Denver, Elton, Lionel, Shaun, Wayne, Ashley, Clifford - which is very common among Afrikaans-speaking Coloured South Africans and is not a sign that the bearer speaks English. The third is Cape Malay Muslim - Moegamat, Ebrahim, Achmat, Yusuf, Riedwaan, Faried - where Moegamat and Mogamad are Afrikaans spellings of Muhammad and frequently stand as a first element before a second given name. Note the diacritics: Daniël takes a diaeresis, and Morné, Corné, Jandré, André and Handré an acute.
