description: Short form or nickname, in Afrikaans. These are real hypocoristics, not invented ones: Kobus and Koos both come from Jacobus, Hannes and Jannie from Johannes, Sannie and Sanna from Susanna, Sarie from Sara, Miemie from Maria, Hessie from Hester. Two habits show here. The diminutive -ie or -tjie does most of the work, which is why so many of these end in it. And Oom, Tannie, Oupa and Ouma - uncle, aunt, grandfather, grandmother - are used as forms of address to any older person, related or not, and frequently attach to the short name: Oom Koos, Tannie Sannie.
locale: af
