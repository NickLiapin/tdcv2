description: Pastime, in Afrikaans. Braai is first and is not a translation of barbecue - it is a fixed social occasion and people list it as a hobby without irony. Voëlkyk, Wildkyk and Jag are ordinary suburban pastimes here rather than specialist ones, Jukskei is a traditional throwing game still played in clubs, and Boeremusiek speel and Konsertina speel belong to a living amateur tradition.
