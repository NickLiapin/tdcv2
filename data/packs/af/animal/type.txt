description: Animal names in Afrikaans. From Springbok down, the list is southern African game, and these names are why Afrikaans wildlife vocabulary cannot be derived from Dutch: Koedoe, Gemsbok, Blesbok, Rooikat, Erdvark, Ratel, Dassie and Bobbejaan name animals no Dutch speaker in the Netherlands ever had to name. Several went the other way and entered English from Afrikaans unchanged - springbok, meerkat, aardvark.
