description: Bird names in Afrikaans. Almost none are translations - they are the names the language coined for the birds in front of it, and several are onomatopoeic (Hoephoep, Kwêvoël, Bokmakierie, Piet-my-vrou) or describe behaviour (Bosluisvoël, the tick bird, rides on cattle). Bloukraanvoël is the national bird of South Africa. Note the -oël spelling: the diaeresis on ë marks a separate syllable, voël being two, and dropping it gives a different word. This list has almost no overlap with Dutch at the same address, which is what a genuinely separate lexicon looks like.
locale: af
