description: Diagnosis, in Afrikaans. Four entries near the top are what makes this a southern African list rather than a translated European one. MIV-infeksie and Tuberkulose are the two largest causes of adult illness in the region and are frequently diagnosed together. Fetale alkoholsindroom is here because the Western Cape wine districts - overwhelmingly Afrikaans-speaking - record the highest prevalence measured anywhere in the world, a legacy of the dop system that paid farm labour in wine. Silikose is the occupational lung disease of the gold mines. A diagnosis list for this locale that omits them would be a translation exercise rather than a description of the population.
