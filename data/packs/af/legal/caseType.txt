description: Kind of legal matter, in Afrikaans. South African law is Roman-Dutch at its base with English procedure on top, and two entries here have no American counterpart. A grondeis is a land restitution claim brought under the Restitution of Land Rights Act by people dispossessed after 1913, heard by a dedicated court. And what English calls bankruptcy splits in two: sekwestrasie applies to a natural person, likwidasie to a company, and they are different proceedings.
