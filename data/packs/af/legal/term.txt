description: Legal term, in Afrikaans. Two of these mark the Roman-Dutch inheritance. An interdik is what English law calls an injunction, and the word is the ordinary one here. And the profession is split: a prokureur is the attorney a client walks in to see, an advokaat is counsel who argues in the higher courts, and only recently may a client brief the second directly. A landdros is a magistrate, a regter a judge, and the two are not the same office.
