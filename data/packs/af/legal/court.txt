description: Kind of court, in Afrikaans, as the South African judiciary is structured. The Konstitusionele Hof sits above everything; the Hoogste Hof van Appèl is the general appeal court; the Hooggeregshof is the high court of a division; and the Landdroshof - the magistrate's court - is where almost every ordinary matter is actually heard. Two are specific to this country: the Grondeisehof hears land restitution claims, and the Arbeidshof and its appeal court sit outside the ordinary hierarchy entirely. Note the grave accent in Appèl.
