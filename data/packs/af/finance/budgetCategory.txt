description: Household budget line, in Afrikaans, as a South African household actually keeps it. Four of these are local rather than universal. Munisipale dienste is a single monthly account covering water, electricity, refuse and property rates. Mediese fonds is a medical scheme, which is not health insurance and is regulated separately. Gewapende reaksie is an armed-response security contract, an ordinary suburban line item. Huishulp is domestic help, employed in a large share of middle-income households and covered by its own labour law.
