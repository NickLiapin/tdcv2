description: Job title, in Afrikaans. Several belong to this economy in particular. Wynmaker and Kelderbestuurder come from an industry that employs tens of thousands in the Western Cape and is conducted almost entirely in Afrikaans. Wildbewaarder, Natuurbewaarder and Toergids come from the game and tourism sector. The mining block - Mynwerker, Skagopsigter, Mynopmeter, Metallurg - reflects an industry that shaped the country. Passer en draaier is the standard trade title for a fitter and turner. And the legal titles split three ways where English has one: a prokureur is an attorney, an advokaat is counsel, and a landdros is a magistrate, which is not a regter.
