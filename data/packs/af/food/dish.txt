description: Dishes an Afrikaans speaker eats, in Afrikaans. Most of these have no name in any other language: potjiekos is cooked in a three-legged pot over coals, bredie is a slow stew named after what is in it, waterblommetjiebredie uses the flower buds of a Cape water plant, and biltong and droëwors are air-dried meat. Note the diminutives - braaibroodjies, slaphakskeentjies, pampoenkoekies, skilpadjies - which Afrikaans uses far more freely than Dutch does. The apostrophe in koe’sisters is U+2019.
