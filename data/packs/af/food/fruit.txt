description: Fruit, in Afrikaans. Nartjie is the soft citrus the rest of the world calls a satsuma or clementine, and the word went into South African English unchanged; note the -tjie diminutive ending, which is the commonest diminutive in the language. Spanspek is the sweet melon, Waatlemoen the watermelon and Suurlemoen the lemon - literally "sour orange".
