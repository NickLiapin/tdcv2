(Edms) Bpk is Eiendoms Beperk - a private company, the equivalent of Pty Ltd - and Bpk alone is a public company. Ing is Ingelyf, an incorporated firm of professionals whose directors are jointly liable, which is why attorneys and auditors use it. BK is a Beslote Korporasie, the close corporation: it is here because hundreds of thousands still trade, but no new one has been registrable since the Companies Act of 2008 took effect in 2011, so a BK in generated data implies a business older than that.
