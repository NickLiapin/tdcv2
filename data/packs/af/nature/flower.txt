description: Flower, in Afrikaans. The first block is the Cape flora, which is a floral kingdom in its own right and has no counterpart anywhere else: the Koningprotea is the national flower, Vygie the mesembs of the dry west, Disa the red orchid of Table Mountain, and Gousblom the Namaqualand daisy that turns the Northern Cape orange for three weeks a year. Katjiepiering, Kalkoentjie and Madeliefie carry the -tjie and -ie diminutives.
