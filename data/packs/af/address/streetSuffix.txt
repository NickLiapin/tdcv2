description: Afrikaans thoroughfare element. Like Dutch it is normally FUSED onto the end of the street name as one word (Kerkstraat, Protealaan, Bergsigweg) rather than written separately as in English. Rylaan, kruin, sig, hoogte, krans and rif are the suburban-developer stock of a country of ridges and koppies; gracht, kade and singel, which name half the streets of a Dutch town built on water, are absent because the landscape is not the same one.
locale: af
