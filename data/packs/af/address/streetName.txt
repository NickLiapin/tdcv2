description: Street names of the kind found in any Afrikaans-speaking town in South Africa or Namibia. Like Dutch, an Afrikaans street name is normally ONE fused word carrying its own thoroughfare element (Kerkstraat, Protealaan, Bergsigweg), so these are complete names and address/streetSuffix.txt is the element already fused on, not something to append. The number FOLLOWS the street: "Kerkstraat 12", never "12 Kerkstraat". Two naming habits belong to this language area rather than to Dutch: the old grid of every dorp is Kerk-, Mark-, Voortrekker- and Stasie-, and the post-war suburb names its streets after indigenous plants, trees and birds - Protea, Aalwyn, Kiepersol, Kremetart, Bokmakierie. Wag-’n-bietjiestraat carries the indefinite article ’n inside a street name, which is where a config is most likely to break the apostrophe. Streets named for one person or one town belong in a country pack.
locale: af
