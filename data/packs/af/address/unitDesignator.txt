description: Afrikaans address unit designator. Two of these are institutions of the southern African postal system rather than translations of anything: Posbus is a numbered post-office box and is a completely ordinary residential address in South Africa and Namibia, and Privaatsak - "private bag" - is a bulk-delivery address used by farms, schools, hospitals and government offices where no street delivery exists. Erf names the surveyed stand itself. The numbers that follow them belong in a country pack.
