description: Trailing element of an Afrikaans place name, FUSED onto the name as one word: Bloemfontein, Vredendal, Mosselbaai, Rustenburg, Kraaifontein. This is the most visible split from Dutch at the same address - Dutch appends a separate water qualifier (Alphen aan den Rijn), while Afrikaans compounds a landscape noun onto the stem. The stock of nouns is the trekking frontier itself: -fontein a spring, -drif a river ford, -kraal a stock enclosure, -poort a gap through a mountain, -vlei a seasonal pan, -kloof a ravine, -laagte a hollow, -rus a resting place.
