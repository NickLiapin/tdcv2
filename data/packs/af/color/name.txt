description: Colour names in Afrikaans. Kakie is here for a reason worth recording: the word entered the language during the South African War as the Boer nickname for a British soldier in a khaki uniform, and it is now simply the name of the colour. Note the compounds - Lemmetjiegroen, Kastaiingbruin, Houtskoolgrys - where Afrikaans fuses modifier and colour into one word instead of writing two.
locale: af
