description: Common adverb, in Afrikaans. The last three carry the fact that makes Afrikaans negation unlike any of its neighbours: nie brackets the clause, appearing once before what it negates and again at the end - "ek het dit nie gesien nie" - and nooit and niks pull the same closing nie with them: "ek gaan nooit weer nie". Dutch, from which Afrikaans otherwise descends, does none of this. A generator that emits a single nie will produce a sentence no Afrikaans speaker would write.
