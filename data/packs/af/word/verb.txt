description: Common verb, in Afrikaans, in the bare form. This is the single form the verb takes: Afrikaans dropped the Dutch conjugation almost entirely, so ek werk, jy werk, hy werk, ons werk, julle werk and hulle werk are all identical, and only wees (is / was) and hê (het) keep any irregularity at all. A config that tries to inflect these for person is solving a problem the language does not have. Note the circumflex on hê and sê.
