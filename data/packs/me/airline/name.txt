description: Airlines serving Montenegro, under their registered Latin names, which is how they appear on a Montenegrin ticket or departure board. Air Montenegro, trading as ToMontenegro, was created in 2020 after Montenegro Airlines — the flag carrier since 1994 — lost its operating licence and was wound up, leaving the country without an airline of its own for several weeks; the new carrier started with a handful of Embraers and took over the flag-carrier role without the debts. The rest of the list divides by airport, because Montenegro's two behave nothing alike. Podgorica is the year-round airport, tied to the European hubs and to Belgrade and Istanbul through the winter. Tivat is a summer airport in the sharpest sense: it is nearly silent from November to April and then takes charter and low-cost leisure traffic from Britain, Germany, Poland, Scandinavia and the former Soviet states in volumes that briefly exceed the capital's.
