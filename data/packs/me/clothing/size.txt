description: Clothing size in the international letter scale, used in Montenegro exactly as written, in Latin capitals, and never translated — a Montenegrin label reads L and a shop assistant says "el" out loud. Nothing about the letters is local, but the distribution behind them is: repeated anthropometric surveys put Montenegro and the neighbouring Dinaric highlands at the top of the world's height tables, with young men averaging around 183 cm and in some studies higher, so a Montenegrin rail carries proportionally more L, XL and XXL than the same chain's rail in southern Europe, and imported sizing runs short in the sleeve and the leg before it runs short in the chest. The alternative scale a Montenegrin also knows is the European numeric one for women's clothing, 36 to 46, which sits alongside the letters on most labels sold here.
