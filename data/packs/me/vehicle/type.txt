description: Vehicle body types in Montenegrin. Number plates are worth knowing beside this vocabulary, because in a country this size the plate tells you where a car comes from before the driver does: a Montenegrin plate opens with a one- or two-letter code for the town of registration — PG for Podgorica, NK for Nikšić, HN for Herceg Novi, KO for Kotor, BR for Bar, UL for Ulcinj, TV for Tivat, CT for Cetinje — followed by the state coat of arms, and the international distinguishing sign is MNE. The prominence of terenac and krosover reflects what actually sells: the interior is mountainous, many village roads are unsurfaced, and ground clearance is a practical purchase rather than a fashion.
