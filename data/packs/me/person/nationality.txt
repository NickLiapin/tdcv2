description: Declared nationality or ethnicity. The first eight are Montenegro's own census categories and the order is roughly the census order: Crnogorci are about 45 per cent, Srbi about 29, Bošnjaci about 9, Albanci about 5, Muslimani about 3 as a separate declared identity from Bošnjaci, then Hrvati, Romi and Egipćani. That last one confuses everyone who meets it for the first time and is not a mistake: the Balkan Egyptians are a distinct recognised community in Montenegro, Roma-adjacent but self-identifying as descended from Egypt, counted separately in the census and represented separately in local government. Note Nijemac for a German — the ijekavian reflex again, against the ekavian Nemac — and that Musliman with a capital M is an ethnicity while musliman with a small one is a believer, a distinction Yugoslav censuses created in 1971 and Montenegro still records.
