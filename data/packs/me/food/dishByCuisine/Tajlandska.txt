Montenegro takes well over two million visitors a year against a resident population of about 620 000, and that ratio is the whole reason a country this small carries a Thai restaurant at all. The pattern is strictly seasonal: the coast between Herceg Novi and Ulcinj cooks the world's cuisines from June to September and shuts most of them by November, so a menu that exists in Budva in July has no address in the same town in February.
