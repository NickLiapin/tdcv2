TLD is on the list for a reason particular to this country: Montenegro owns one of the most commercially successful national domains in the world. The .me suffix was delegated in 2007, after the old .yu space was wound up following the end of the state union, and rather than reserve it for national use Montenegro licensed it to be sold worldwide, because me reads as an English word and yields short addresses that finish a sentence instead of merely labelling a server. Because Montenegro writes in the Latin alphabet in daily practice, acronyms like these sit inside a sentence with no adjustment whatsoever — a Montenegrin technical text needs no transliteration step before an English term can be dropped into it.
