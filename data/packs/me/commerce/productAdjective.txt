description: Product adjective in Montenegrin, given in the masculine singular indefinite form. The adjective stands before the noun and agrees with it in gender, number and case, so praktičan sto but praktična fotelja and praktično ogledalo, and a config that concatenates a value from here with a value from productNoun without adjusting the ending will produce something no listing would print. Two forms in the list are the ijekavian standard rather than the Serbian one: bezbjedan against bezbedan, and neljepljivi against nelepljivi, both from short jat. Note the definite/indefinite split that Serbian and Croatian share and that most European languages lack — praktičan is the indefinite form used when the adjective is new information, praktični the definite form used when the object is already known, and the borrowed technology adjectives here (digitalni, pametni, bežični, profesionalni, univerzalni) have settled into the definite ending permanently, because they entered the language attached to specific products rather than as descriptions. Ručno rađen and organski do particular work on a Montenegrin listing, where a great deal of what sells online is food and drink from a named village.
locale: me
