description: Video game genres in Montenegrin. The vocabulary is half translated and half borrowed, and pucačina, from pucati, to shoot, is a native formation with a colloquial suffix that no committee coined — it grew on gaming forums and stuck, which is why it sits beside the stiffer strategija u realnom vremenu without looking out of place. Montenegro's own connection to games is commercial rather than creative: the country's .me domain, delegated after the old .yu space was wound up, is a favourite with studios, streamers and gaming sites worldwide, because me reads as an English word and produces short addresses that finish a sentence instead of merely labelling a server.
