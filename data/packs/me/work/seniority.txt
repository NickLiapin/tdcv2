description: Seniority label attached to a role. Montenegro runs two naming systems side by side and this list holds both. A private advertisement in Podgorica prints the English loans — junior, medior, senior — because the software and outsourcing firms that use them work for clients abroad and hire against foreign job ladders. A public body cannot: the civil service is graded by a systematisation act, and its rungs are samostalni, viši and glavni before a title, so a ministry produces a samostalni savjetnik and a glavni inspektor where a company would produce a senior anything. Pripravnik is the entry rung in both worlds and has a statutory meaning as well, since a Montenegrin trainee period runs to a defined length and ends in an exam for several regulated professions.
