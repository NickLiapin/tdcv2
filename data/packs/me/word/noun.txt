description: Common Montenegrin noun in the nominative singular. Almost half of this list is diagnostic: dijete, svijet, snijeg, rijeka, zvijezda, vrijeme, mlijeko and riječ all descend from Common Slavic words with a long jat, and Montenegro resolves that vowel as ije, where Serbia's eastern standard has dete, svet, sneg, reka, zvezda, vreme, mleko and reč. Where the same jat was short it gives je instead, which is why the moon and the month are both mjesec, the wind is vjetar and bread is hljeb against Serbian hleb. The pattern is regular enough to be a test: if a word here has ije or je and its Serbian counterpart has plain e, the vowel is jat and the spelling is not optional. ośećaj closes the file with the other Montenegrin marker, the letter ś, against Serbian and Croatian osjećaj. mjesec is worth a second look for a different reason — it names the moon and the calendar month with one word, so a config that draws from this file for an astronomy label and a date label will get the same string for both.
