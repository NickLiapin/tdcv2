description: Common Montenegrin verb in the infinitive. The infinitive is the dictionary form, but it is not how Montenegro usually builds a future or a modal clause: alongside moram raditi a speaker will just as readily say moram da radim, "I must that I work", the da-plus-present construction that runs across the whole Balkan area and is stronger here than on the Croatian coast. Four of these verbs show the jat vowel in the ending itself — vidjeti, razumjeti, voljeti, živjeti — where Serbia's eastern standard writes videti, razumeti, voleti and živeti; the je is not a suffix but the reflex of a short jat inside the stem, and it survives into the participle (vidio, vidjela). ośetiti and pośetiti carry ś, the letter the 2009 Pravopis crnogorskoga jezika added: Serbian and Croatian write osjetiti and posjetiti. Aspect, not tense, is the axis a learner trips on — most of these are one half of a pair, so uzeti is a single completed taking and uzimati the repeated or ongoing one, and no ending will convert between them.
locale: me
