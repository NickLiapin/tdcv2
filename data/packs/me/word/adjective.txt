description: Common Montenegrin adjective, given in the masculine singular INDEFINITE form. That qualification matters, because Montenegrin adjectives have two full declensions and this file holds the shorter one. The indefinite velik is what you use predicatively and for a thing being mentioned for the first time — kupio sam nov auto, "I bought a new car" — while the definite novi marks a thing already known: novi auto je pred kućom. The two have diverged in the spoken language until only the case endings still separate them (nova čovjeka against novog čovjeka), and English does the same work with a and the. Three values show sound changes that are easy to misread: debeo ends in -o because a word-final l turned into o (debel became debeo, and the feminine is still debela), lijep and svijetao carry the long jat that Montenegro writes ije where Serbia's eastern standard writes e, and ośetljiv is spelled with ś, one of the two letters the 2009 Pravopis crnogorskoga jezika added to the alphabet — Serbian and Croatian both write osjetljiv here. strm earns its place in a Montenegrin list on merit: three quarters of the country is karst and mountain, and the everyday word for a slope you cannot drive up gets more use here than in most of Europe.
locale: me
