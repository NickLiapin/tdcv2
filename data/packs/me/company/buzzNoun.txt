description: Business buzz noun in Montenegrin. Four entries show the ijekavian standard doing its work inside otherwise international vocabulary — rješenje, vrijednost, izvještaj and bezbjednost, against the Serbian rešenje, vrednost, izveštaj and bezbednost. That matters practically rather than decoratively: a Montenegrin annual report is a godišnji izvještaj, the audited figure in it is a vrijednost, and a search that assumes the ekavian spellings will miss every filing lodged in Podgorica. Note also how the abstract nouns are built. The -acija block (integracija, optimizacija, digitalizacija) is a Latinism that entered through German and Italian and now takes any English verb as a stem, while the -ost block (efikasnost, produktivnost, održivost, usklađenost) is the native machinery for turning an adjective into a quality, and the two never mix. Održivost and društvena odgovornost carry more weight here than the list length suggests: Montenegro wrote itself into its constitution in 1991 as an ekološka država, the first country to make ecological statehood a constitutional term, and every tourism, energy and construction prospectus since has had to answer to that sentence.
