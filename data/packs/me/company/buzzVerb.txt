description: Business buzz verb in Montenegrin, in the infinitive. The infinitive is the citation form but not the everyday one: across Montenegro, as across the whole southern Štokavian area, speech prefers da plus the present tense — hoćemo da unaprijedimo rather than hoćemo unaprijediti — so a slogan built from these will read as written language, which is exactly what a slogan wants to be. Five verbs here carry the ijekavian jat: unaprijediti, obezbijediti, primijeniti, predvidjeti and mjeriti, against Serbian unaprediti, obezbediti, primeniti, predvideti and meriti. Watch what the vowel does when the verb changes shape — the long ije of unaprijediti shortens to je in the present unaprijedim only in some forms and the noun is unapređenje, so these cannot be pattern-matched by a naive rule. The other half of the list is borrowed wholesale and conjugated by one suffix: any foreign verb entering the language takes -ovati or -irati and is immediately regular, which is how optimizovati, monetizovati and migrirati became ordinary Montenegrin words without any of the friction a noun borrowing causes.
