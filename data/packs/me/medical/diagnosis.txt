description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient, with no sex or ancestry restriction; sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale and diagnosisFemale, and ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. The clinical register is international, as it is everywhere, but the spelling is Montenegrin and ijekavian throughout — upala slijepog crijeva, čvor na štitnoj žlijezdi, pleuralni izljev, anemija usljed nedostatka gvožđa — and the patient who receives one of these labels moves through a system of an unusual shape. Care begins with the izabrani doktor at a dom zdravlja, is paid for by the Fond za zdravstveno osiguranje, and ends, for anything serious, at the Klinički centar Crne Gore in Podgorica, the country's only tertiary hospital: with about 620 000 people, Montenegro has whole specialties that exist in one building and are held up by a handful of doctors. Malarija is on the list for a domestic reason and not a foreign one — the Zeta plain and the Skadar basin were malarial into the twentieth century and were cleared by drainage and spraying after the Second World War — and the Institut za javno zdravlje still runs the surveillance that would find it again.
