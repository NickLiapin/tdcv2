description: Czech address unit / interior designator, abbreviated as it is written before a number. A Czech house carries TWO numbers: the cislo popisne (c. p.), a permanent registration number unique within the municipality, and the cislo orientacni (c. or.), the sequential number along the street. Both are written together as popisne/orientacni - Dlouha 123/45 - and only the second one counts up the street.
locale: cs
