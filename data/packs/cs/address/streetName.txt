A Czech address writes the street FIRST and the number after it (Dlouha 12), and the type word - ulice - is normally left out unless it is namesti or trida. Note the large group beginning with a preposition (Na Prikope, U Rybnika, Pod Lesem, K Nadrazi): these are ordinary Czech street names, not fragments. City-specific streets belong in a country pack.
