description: Employee benefit (Czech). Stravenky (employer-issued meal vouchers) and the Multisport karta (one card admitting the holder to thousands of gyms and pools) are standard Czech benefits with no English equivalent. Czech law guarantees four weeks of paid leave, so a fifth week is offered as a benefit; sick days are paid days off that need no doctor's note.
