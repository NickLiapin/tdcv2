description: Court type in the Czech judiciary. Czechia has two supreme courts - the Nejvyssi soud for civil and criminal matters and the Nejvyssi spravni soud for administrative ones - plus a separate Ustavni soud outside the ordinary hierarchy. First-instance courts are okresni, except in Prague where they are called obvodni.
