The ending is legible geography: -ძე ("son of") is western — Imereti, Guria, Adjara, Kartli; -შვილი ("child of") is eastern — Kartli and Kakheti; -ია, -უა and -ავა are Mingrelian and the Black Sea littoral; -იანი is Svan; -ური and -ული come from the mountain valleys of Khevsureti, Pshavi and Tusheti. A Georgian reads a person's home region straight off the surname. IMPORTANT: Georgian surnames do NOT inflect for gender — ბერიძე is ბერიძე for a man and for a woman — so there is deliberately no person/female/lastName.txt in this pack; its absence is the design, not an omission. The curve is the default decay because Georgian surnames really are evenly spread: the commonest, ბერიძე, is well under one percent of the population, nothing like Vietnamese Nguyen or Korean Kim.
