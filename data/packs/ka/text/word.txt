description: Common Georgian words for slugs, usernames and tags. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Georgian book at all (a search for l.ka returns "No records found"), so unlike fr/de/it/es/pt/pl this is not extracted from a public-domain corpus. Two things a config should know about Georgian words: every letter here is three bytes in UTF-8, so a byte-limited column holds about a third of the characters it would in English; and Georgian tolerates consonant clusters that look impossible — გვფრცქვნის ("he peels us") is one word with eight consonants and no vowel between them, so do not assume a syllable count from the letter count.
