description: Music genres in Malayalam. The first eight are the local ones and they are not decorative: കർണാടക സംഗീതം is the classical tradition of the whole south, സോപാന സംഗീതം is sung only in Kerala temples, and മാപ്പിളപ്പാട്ട് is the Malabar Muslim song form with its own metre and its own Arabic-Malayalam poetic tradition.
locale: ml
