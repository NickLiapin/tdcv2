description: Musical instruments in Malayalam. The first twelve are the Kerala percussion and temple instruments — ചെണ്ട, മദ്ദളം, ഇടയ്ക്ക, തിമില, ഇലത്താളം, കൊമ്പ്, കുഴൽ — and they are what a ചെണ്ടമേളം is made of. No European list contains any of them, and a Kerala dataset without them would be describing somewhere else.
