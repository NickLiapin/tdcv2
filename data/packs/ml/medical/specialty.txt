description: Hospital departments as Kerala names them: transliterated English, which is what the signage, the OP ticket and the referral slip all say. Kerala clinical Malayalam borrows the technical term and translates the everyday one — പ്രോസ്റ്റാറ്റൈറ്റിസ് stays, but "rectal cancer" becomes മലാശയ അർബുദം. That split is how a Kerala discharge summary actually reads and is not an inconsistency in this pack.
