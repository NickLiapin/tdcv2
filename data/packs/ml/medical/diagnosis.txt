The head of the list is what the state's own burden of disease looks like — Kerala has India's highest recorded prevalence of diabetes and hypertension, and the seasonal fevers (ഡെങ്കിപ്പനി, എലിപ്പനി, ചിക്കുൻഗുനിയ) arrive with the monsoon every year. Kerala clinical Malayalam borrows the technical term and translates the everyday one — പ്രോസ്റ്റാറ്റൈറ്റിസ് stays, but "rectal cancer" becomes മലാശയ അർബുദം. That split is how a Kerala discharge summary actually reads and is not an inconsistency in this pack.
