description: Writing systems. മലയാളലിപി is this pack's own script, Unicode block U+0D00-U+0D7F, a Brahmic abugida descended from Grantha. Its nearest neighbours are named right after it and they are DIFFERENT SCRIPTS with different codepoint blocks: തമിഴ് U+0B80-U+0BFF, കന്നഡ U+0C80-U+0CFF, തെലുങ്ക് U+0C00-U+0C7F, ദേവനാഗരി U+0900-U+097F. Not one character from any of those four appears anywhere in this pack; the resemblance is genealogical, not textual, and a Malayalam pack contaminated with Tamil would still look plausibly South Indian to anyone who did not count codepoints.
