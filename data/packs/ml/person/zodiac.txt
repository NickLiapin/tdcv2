description: The twelve solar signs in Malayalam. These are also the twelve months of the Kollam Era (കൊല്ലവർഷം), Kerala's own calendar — ചിങ്ങം opens the year in mid-August — so the same word names a star sign and a month depending on context. Kerala reckons a birthday by the നക്ഷത്രം (birth star) within one of these, not by the Gregorian date.
