description: The trailing name element of a Kerala Muslim. Read the first twelve as what they are — titles and lineage words (ഹാജി one who has made the hajj, തങ്ങൾ a claimed descendant of the Prophet, മുസ്ലിയാർ a scholar, മരയ്ക്കാർ and കേയി old Malabar trading lineages), not inherited surnames. Most Kerala Muslims have no family name at all: the official name is given name plus father's given name, or a house name used as an initial. Treat this list as "what falls in the last slot on a form", not as "the surname".
locale: ml
