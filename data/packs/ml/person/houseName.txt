description: The house name — തറവാട് for a Hindu lineage, വീട്ടുപേര് for a Christian or Muslim family. This is the single most Kerala-specific piece of identity data there is: it names the ancestral property, it is what an official initial expands to, and it is the address as often as it is the name (a letter reaches പുത്തൻപുരയിൽ, ചങ്ങനാശ്ശേരി with no street on it). The words are transparent geography — പുത്തൻപുരയിൽ "at the new house", വടക്കേടത്ത് "at the northern place", കുന്നേൽ "on the hill". Not community-specific: the same formations occur in all three stocks.
locale: ml
