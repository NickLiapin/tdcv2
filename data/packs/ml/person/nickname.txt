description: Malayalam pet names. The productive pattern is the -മോൻ (son) and -മോൾ (daughter) suffix — കുഞ്ഞുമോൻ, ടിന്റുമോൾ — and it is so ordinary in Kerala that plenty of adults are called it on a payslip. കുഞ്ഞു- (little) prefixes the same way. The rest are the clipped two-syllable forms every Malayali household uses; several are community-marked exactly as the given names are (ഇത്തു and മമ്മു are Muslim, കുഞ്ഞാവ is not).
locale: ml
