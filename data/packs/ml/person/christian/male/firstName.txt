---
description: Male given names of Kerala Christians. The Syrian Christian stock is Biblical by way of Syriac rather than Latin, so the Malayalam forms diverge from the English ones: ഔസേപ്പ് is Joseph, ചാക്കോ is Jacob, വർഗീസ് is George, കുര്യൻ is Cyriac, ഈപ്പൻ is Eapen. The later names (ഷിബു, ജോമോൻ, ബിനോയ്) are the twentieth-century coinages that mark a Christian household as clearly as the old ones do. Deliberately unweighted.
locale: ml
