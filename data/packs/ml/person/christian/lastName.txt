description: The family name of a Kerala Christian. Two kinds, both real: an ancestor's given name frozen into a surname (തോമസ്, വർഗീസ്, കുരുവിള), and the വീട്ടുപേര് — the house name of the family property (പുത്തൻപുരയിൽ, കളപ്പുരയ്ക്കൽ, മഞ്ഞൂരാൻ), which is what the initial in an official name expands to. The last nine (ഫെർണാണ്ടസ്, പെരേര, ഡിക്രൂസ്…) are Latin Catholic coastal families and are Portuguese in origin — keep their spelling, it is theirs.
locale: ml
