description: The trailing name element of a Kerala Hindu — a community or caste title (നായർ, മേനോൻ, പിള്ള, നമ്പൂതിരി), not an inherited surname in the European sense. It is shorter than the other two stocks because that is the truth: Kerala Hindus draw on a few dozen such titles, and many people use a house-name initial instead and carry no trailing title at all. The last four (ഷേണായ്, പ്രഭു, കമ്മത്ത്, പൈ) are Konkani-speaking Gowda Saraswat families long settled in Kochi, a real but separate community.
