description: What follows a name in Kerala paperwork. THERE IS NO Jr / Sr / II / III HERE AND THAT IS NOT AN OMISSION: Malayalam has no generational suffix, because the naming system never passed a full name down — a son inherits his father's given name as an initial, not as a repeated name, so there is no second of anything to number. What actually trails a Malayali name on a letterhead, a nameboard or a wedding invitation is a degree, and for a civil servant the cadre abbreviation (ഐ.എ.എസ്., ഐ.പി.എസ്.), which is used exactly as a post-nominal.
