description: Nationality in Malayalam. The Gulf states sit near the front of this list on purpose: roughly two million Malayalis live and work in the Gulf, and a Kerala dataset that treats ഗൾഫ് nationalities as exotic has misread the state. The forms are the loaned English adjectives — Malayalam does have native -ക്കാരൻ / -ക്കാരി forms (ഇന്ത്യക്കാരൻ, ഇന്ത്യക്കാരി) but those inflect for gender, so the invariable loanword is what a form field holds.
