description: Male given names across Kerala, weighted so that the three naming stocks carry their 2011 census shares of the state's population: Hindu 54.73%, Muslim 26.56%, Christian 18.38%. Those three masses are measured; the shape WITHIN a stock is a Zipf estimate with exponent 0.5, not a name-by-name frequency table — no such table exists publicly for Malayalam. The default gentle curve of this project is deliberately NOT used: it would have made രാജൻ and ജനാർദനൻ equally common, which is false. Kerala is not one naming pool. The Hindu, Muslim (Mappila) and Syrian/Latin Christian stocks are almost disjoint, and a row that pairs a given name from one with a family name from another describes a person who does not exist. Drawing this list independently of person/male/firstName.txt does exactly that. Use the community-pure addresses — ml.person.hindu.*, ml.person.muslim.*, ml.person.christian.* — or the fullName generators, which draw one community per row.
