---
description: Malayalam street-name words — landmarks, trades, trees and water, the stock a Kerala road is normally named after. Two things a config cannot infer. First, the type word comes AFTER the name in Malayalam (ബീച്ച് റോഡ്, not റോഡ് ബീച്ച്), the opposite of the Polish order, so compose name + address/streetSuffix. Second, a Kerala postal address usually does not use a street at all: it is house name, place, post office and PIN (പുതിയപുരയിൽ, ചങ്ങനാശ്ശേരി പി.ഒ.), and the house name is in person/houseName.txt. Roads named after a person, and the real streets of any given town, belong to the India country pack, not here.
locale: ml
