description: Spices. This is the list Kerala is named for in every other language's history books: കുരുമുളക് (black pepper) and ഏലം (cardamom) are what the Romans, the Arabs and then the Portuguese sailed for, and both still come off the same hills. കുടംപുളി (Malabar tamarind) is the souring agent in fish curry and has no substitute in the dish.
