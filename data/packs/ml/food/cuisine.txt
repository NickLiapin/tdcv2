description: Cuisines as a Kerala menu names them, and the parent of food/dishByCuisine/. കേരളീയം leads and carries the longest child file because it is the home cuisine. What a Malayali means by "അറബിക്" is Malabar-Gulf food — കുഴിമന്തി, അൽഫാം — brought back by returning migrants, not restaurant food from Beirut, and it is more common on a Kerala high street than Italian is. The four other Indian regional cuisines are listed separately because in Kerala they are foreign food.
