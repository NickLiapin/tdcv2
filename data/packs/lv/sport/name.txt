description: Sport. Novuss is the entry no other pack has: it is a Latvian board game played with a cue on a square wooden board, invented in the 1920s, with a national federation, a championship and a place in almost every school and holiday house. Bobslejs, skeletons and kamaniņu sports are on the list for a plainer reason — the Sigulda track has made Latvia a sliding-sports country out of proportion to its size — and hokejs is the national obsession.
