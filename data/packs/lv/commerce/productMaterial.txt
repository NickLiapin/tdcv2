description: Product material. Latvian names a material with the GENITIVE of the noun and not with an adjective: koka galds is literally "table of wood", ādas soma "bag of leather". So every entry here is already a genitive and a template simply puts it in front of the noun with a space — no agreement, no ending change, whatever the noun's gender. That is why a Latvian material list is easier to compose than a Latvian colour list.
