description: Common Latvian content words, for slugs, usernames and tags. Handwritten: Project Gutenberg holds no Latvian book at all, so these were not derived by frequency from a corpus the way the Polish and Russian packs derive theirs — they are a hand-picked stock of everyday nouns, adjectives and verbs, the nouns in the nominative singular and the verbs in the infinitive. Diacritics are kept. A config that needs an ASCII slug must strip them itself, and Latvian strips ā ē ī ū to a e i u and č ģ ķ ļ ņ š ž to c g k l n s z. Doing so loses meaning rather than just decoration: kazas is goats and kāzas is a wedding, pile is a drop and pīle is a duck, and both pairs collapse to the same ASCII string. Note also that no word here contains w, x, q or y — Latvian has none of the four.
