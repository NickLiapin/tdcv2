description: Property type. Sērijveida dzīvoklis is the Soviet-era system-built flat — the 103., 104., 119. and 602. series are named in Latvian adverts as ordinary shorthand for age, layout and ceiling height — and jaunais projekts is anything built after 1991. Together they are the first thing a Latvian listing states.
