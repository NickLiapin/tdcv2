---
description: Academic qualification in the Latvian system. Latvia follows the Bologna three-cycle structure, so bakalaurs / maģistrs / doktors map onto Bachelor / Master / PhD, but the field is named in the qualification itself and always in the genitive plural — humanitāro zinātņu bakalaurs is "bachelor of the humanities". Vidējā profesionālā izglītība from an arodvidusskola or a tehnikums is a full secondary qualification, not a lesser one.
locale: lv
