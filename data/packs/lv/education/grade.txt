description: School mark. Latvia grades on a TEN-point scale where 10 is the top and 4 is the pass — not the A to F letters of the English-speaking world and not the 1-to-5 of its Slavic neighbours. Each number has a fixed verbal label, given here beside it. Some subjects are assessed pass/fail instead, which is the ieskaitīts / neieskaitīts pair.
