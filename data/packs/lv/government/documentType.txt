description: Official document type. Personas apliecība is the Latvian eID card and has been compulsory for every citizen since 2023, so it and not the passport is the everyday identity document; the passport is for travel. Zemesgrāmatas apliecība is the land-register certificate, and the land register itself is the Latvian proof of ownership.
locale: lv
