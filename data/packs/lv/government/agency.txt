description: Government body. Latvian is the official language of ONE country, so these are the ministries and public offices of the Republic of Latvia — that is what the words name. VID is the state revenue service every taxpayer and business files through, PMLP the office that keeps the population register and issues passports, CSDD the road-traffic directorate that registers vehicles and issues licences, and VSAA the social-insurance agency that pays pensions and benefits. All four are known in speech by their initials rather than their full names.
