description: Compass direction. Latvian compass points are PLURAL nouns — ziemeļi, dienvidi, austrumi, rietumi are all plural in form and take a plural verb, which no config should try to singularise. The sixteen-point names compound without a hyphen and without a space, so they run long: ziemeļziemeļaustrumi is one word.
locale: lv
