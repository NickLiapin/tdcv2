Job titles are grammatically gendered in Latvian — skolotājs is a male teacher and skolotāja a female one, ārsts and ārste, pārdevējs and pārdevēja — and this list carries the MASCULINE forms, which is what a job advertisement and a classifier use. A dataset that pairs these with female rows is producing the form a Latvian job advert uses rather than the form the woman herself would write on a business card; the distinction is real and no ending in this list should be taken as sex-neutral.
