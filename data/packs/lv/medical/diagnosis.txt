description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient — no sex or ancestry restriction. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale and diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. Two entries are here because of where Latvia is rather than because of the language: ērču encefalīts (tick-borne encephalitis) and Laimas slimība (Lyme borreliosis) are endemic across the country and Latvia reports among the highest tick-borne encephalitis incidence in the European Union, so a Latvian patient record that never mentions them is not realistic. Tuberkuloze likewise remains a live clinical category in Latvia rather than a historical one.
locale: lv
