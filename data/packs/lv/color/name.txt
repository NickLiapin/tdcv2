description: Colour name. Latvian colour adjectives inflect for gender and number like every other adjective, so these are the MASCULINE singular indefinite citation forms; before a feminine noun sarkans becomes sarkana, zaļš becomes zaļa. Three entries do not inflect at all — rozā, bēšs and the borrowed tirkīza — because they are indeclinable loans. Dzintara (amber) is a colour word in Latvian in a way it is not in English: Baltic amber washes up on the Kurzeme coast and gives its name to everything from a shade to a shipping company.
locale: lv
