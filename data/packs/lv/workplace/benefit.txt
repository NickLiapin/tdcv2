description: Employee benefit. Veselības apdrošināšana is the standard Latvian perk and the one an advert names first, because the state system is thin and a private policy is what an employer competes on. There is no equivalent of a 401(k): the second pension pillar is compulsory and state-run, so a private pension contribution (III pensiju līmenis) is the optional one an employer may add.
locale: lv
