description: Leave type. Bērna kopšanas atvaļinājums is the Latvian parental leave and runs to the child's eighteen months; paternitātes atvaļinājums is the ten working days a father takes at the birth. Mācību atvaļinājums, study leave, is a statutory entitlement in Latvia rather than a discretionary one.
locale: lv
