description: Address unit designator. A Latvian address writes the house number AFTER the street (Brīvības iela 12) and the flat after that, joined by a hyphen and with no word at all — Brīvības iela 12-34, LV-1010. So the commonest "designator" in Latvia is nothing; dz. is used only when the hyphen would be ambiguous. The remaining entries are the interior labels used on plans and in commercial leases.
locale: lv
