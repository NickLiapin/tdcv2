description: Trailing element of a Latvian place name, written JOINED to the stem the way the prefixes are: Daugavpils, Ozolnieki, Salaspils, Upesgrīva, Mežaparks. Most are ordinary nouns doing duty as toponym endings — pils is a castle, ciems a village, muiža a manor, grīva a river mouth, sēta a farmyard — and -nieki is the collective "the people of", which is how Ozolnieki means "the oak people".
