description: Compass and district qualifier used in a Latvian address or neighbourhood name. The four bare plural forms (Ziemeļi, Dienvidi, Austrumi, Rietumi) are the compass points and are plural in Latvian, never singular; the genitive forms beside them (Ziemeļu, Dienvidu) are what actually attaches to a following noun — Ziemeļu rajons, not Ziemeļi rajons.
