description: Leading element of a Latvian place name. Latvian writes it JOINED to the stem, as one word and with no hyphen and no space — Jaunpils, Vecpiebalga, Lielvārde, Mazsalaca, Augšlīgatne, Mežotne — so a config must concatenate rather than join with a space, which is the opposite of the Croatian or Polish habit. The element is a bare stem, not a whole word: jauns (new) contributes jaun-, liels (big) contributes liel-. Combine with a real toponym; on its own it names no real place.
locale: lv
