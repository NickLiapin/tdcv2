description: Latvian thoroughfare type. It goes AFTER the street name — Brīvības iela, Uzvaras bulvāris, Zaļā gatve — which is the reverse of the Croatian Ulica Nikole Tesle. Written lowercase inside a full address. Iela covers nearly every urban street; līnija is the numbered-street word peculiar to Rīga's Pārdaugava and Ķīpsala; dambis is a causeway across wet ground and appears wherever a road crosses the Daugava floodplain.
locale: lv
