description: Street names that recur across Latvian towns. Two grammatical shapes are mixed and a config composes them the same way but must not inflect them again. The GENITIVE names (Brīvības, Raiņa, Skolas, Liepu, Daugavas) are already inflected and mean "of freedom", "of Rainis", "of the school"; the ADJECTIVAL ones (Zaļā, Klusā, Platā) are feminine to agree with iela. Both stand BEFORE the thoroughfare word — Brīvības iela, Zaļā iela — which is the reverse of the Croatian order and of English "Freedom Street". The town-name entries (Jelgavas, Liepājas, Cēsu) are the ordinary Latvian way of naming the road that leaves towards that town, and recur in dozens of places. Streets unique to one city belong in the Latvia country pack, not here.
locale: lv
