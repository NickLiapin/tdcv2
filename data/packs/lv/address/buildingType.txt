description: Building type as Latvian names it. Viensēta, klēts, rija and pirts have no English equivalent and are ordinary Latvian building types: a viensēta is the single isolated farmstead that is the classic Latvian rural settlement pattern (Latvia never had the nucleated village of its neighbours), a klēts is the granary that doubled as the summer sleeping house, a rija is the threshing barn, and a pirts is the bathhouse, still built beside a new house today. Muiža is the Baltic German manor estate, of which several hundred survive.
locale: lv
