Sēņošana and ogošana — mushroom picking and berry picking — are single verbs in Latvian and are genuine mass pastimes rather than curiosities: half the country goes to the forest in September. Dziedāšana korī, choir singing, belongs on any Latvian list of this kind, because the Song Celebration draws tens of thousands of amateur singers, and pirtošanās, the sauna ritual, is a scheduled social activity and not merely washing.
