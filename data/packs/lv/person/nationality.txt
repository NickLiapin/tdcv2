description: Nationality or demonym, in the MASCULINE form. Latvian demonyms inflect for sex exactly as surnames do — latvietis is a Latvian man and latviete a Latvian woman, lietuvietis and lietuviete, krievs and krieviete — so a feminine row needs the other form, and this list does not carry it. They are written lowercase. Note that latvietis is an ethnic word: the word for a citizen of Latvia regardless of ethnicity is Latvijas iedzīvotājs or Latvijas pilsonis, and the distinction matters in Latvia.
locale: lv
