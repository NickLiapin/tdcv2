description: Language name. Latvian writes these lowercase and as a GENITIVE PLURAL of the people, not as an adjective: latviešu valoda is literally "the language of the Latvians", so the bare word here always expects valoda after it. Latvian and Lithuanian are the only two surviving Baltic languages, the last of an Indo-European branch whose other members (Old Prussian, Curonian, Semigallian, Selonian) died out. They are NOT mutually intelligible: a Latvian understands no more spoken Lithuanian than an ordinary German understands Dutch, and the shared inheritance shows in grammar and old vocabulary rather than in anything a speaker can follow. Two entries here are Latvia's own minority tongues — lībiešu is Livonian, a Finnic language unrelated to Latvian whose last native speaker died in 2013 and which is now being revived from record, and latgaliešu is Latgalian, protected by law as a historic variety of Latvian and spoken in the east of the country.
locale: lv
