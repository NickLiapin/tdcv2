description: Title placed before a name. Latvian abbreviates kungs (Mr) as k-gs and kundze (Mrs/Ms) as k-dze, with a hyphen and no full stop, which is a Latvian punctuation habit rather than a typo. Latvian has no separate Miss/Mrs distinction in current use: jaunkundze is archaic and kundze covers every adult woman. Academic and clerical titles PRECEDE the name in Latvian, so this is where a degree such as Dr. or Prof. belongs and not in person/suffix.
locale: lv
