description: Marital status. Latvian marks it on the person, so the status word itself is gendered: a man is neprecējies and a woman neprecējusies, a man is šķīries and a woman šķīrusies, a widower is atraitnis and a widow atraitne. Both forms are listed as pairs, masculine first, and a config that knows the row's sex should pick the matching one rather than drawing at random.
