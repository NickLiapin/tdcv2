description: Suffix written after a name. Latvian barely uses generational suffixes — jaunākais and vecākais (younger, elder) appear only where a father and son share a name and must be told apart, and Roman numerals are reserved for monarchs and popes. Academic degrees normally PRECEDE the name in Latvian (see person/prefix), so the degrees repeated here read as trailing qualifiers in a signature block or a conference badge rather than as the default. This list is deliberately short: Latvian has no equivalent of the English Jr / III / Esq apparatus, and padding it would misrepresent the language.
