description: Latvian female given name. Given name first, surname second, one of each. Female given names are grammatically female and end in -a or -e without exception — Anna, Kristīne, Ilze, Marta, Līga — which is why there is no overlap with the male list at all: Latvian has no unisex given name, because the ending itself carries the sex. The tail of the list carries the Slavic names (Jeļena, Svetlana, Natālija, Tatjana) borne by Latvia's Russian-speaking residents, entered in Latvianised spelling like every other name in the register. Rasma, Velta, Aina, Zenta, Biruta and Mirdza are the interwar-Republic generation and are now largely names of grandmothers; Emīlija, Sofija, Marta and Alise lead among newborns. Weights are the pack default rank-decay curve rather than measured shares.
