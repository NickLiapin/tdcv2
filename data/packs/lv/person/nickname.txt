description: Informal short form of a given name. Latvian's everyday nickname is the DIMINUTIVE, formed with -iņš / -iņa for a first-declension name and -ītis / -īte for a second, and the process is fully productive: every Latvian given name has one, and adults use them between themselves without any childish colouring. A few genuinely irregular short forms sit alongside them — Jancis and Ansis for Jānis, Pēcis for Pēteris, Krišs for Kristaps — and those are not derivable by rule. The diminutive keeps the sex of the name it comes from, so the -iņš / -ītis forms are male and the -iņa / -īte forms female.
locale: lv
