description: Tree. Ozols and liepa — the oak and the lime — are the two Latvian national trees, and the pairing is gendered in folk symbolism: the oak stands for the man and the lime for the woman, which is why a Jāņi wreath of oak leaves goes to a Jānis and one of flowers to a Līga. Priede, egle and bērzs are what actually covers the country: Latvia is a bit over half forest, and the birch and Scots pine dominate it.
