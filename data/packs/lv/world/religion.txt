description: World religion. Latvia's own three are luterānisms, Roman Catholicism and Russian Orthodoxy, and they divide the country geographically rather than randomly: the Lutheran majority in Kurzeme and Vidzeme, the Catholic majority in Latgale, and the Orthodox population largely Russian-speaking. Dievturība is the reconstructed Latvian pagan faith, founded in 1925 and drawing on the dainas — small, but registered and Latvia's own.
