description: Fruit and berry. Latvian does not separate the two the way English does, and the second half of this list is what actually grows in Latvia: dzērvene and lācene come off the raised bogs, brūklene and mellene off the forest floor, and picking them is a national pastime rather than a hobby. Smiltsērkšķis (sea buckthorn) grows on the coastal dunes.
