description: Vegetable. Kartupelis heads the list because it heads the Latvian plate; skābēti kāposti (sauerkraut) is a vegetable in its own right in Latvia and not a condiment, fermented in barrels through the winter. Sēnes — mushrooms — are counted with the vegetables here because Latvian shopping and cooking treat them that way, and autumn mushroom picking is close to a national sport.
