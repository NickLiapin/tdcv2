Latvian corporate prose borrows its jargon from English but bends it to Latvian morphology: scalable becomes mērogojams, agile becomes veikls, resilient becomes noturīgs. Where no calque took hold the loan is naturalised with a Latvian ending instead (digitāls, hibrīda, agile-loving elastīgs).
