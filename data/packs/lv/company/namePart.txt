description: Evocative noun for a company name; compose with a legalSuffix, which in Latvian goes in FRONT (SIA "Dzintars"). Several entries are Latvian rather than generic: Laima is the goddess of fate and also the country's best-known chocolate brand, Auseklis is the eight-pointed morning-star sign woven into mittens and painted on barns, Pērkons is the thunder god, Jumis is the double ear of grain that means a good harvest, and Sakta is the disc brooch of the national costume. Dzintars (amber), Daugava and Gauja are the three things a Latvian company is most often named after.
locale: lv
