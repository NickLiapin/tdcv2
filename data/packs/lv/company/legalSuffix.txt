description: Company legal form or name element. Latvian puts the legal form BEFORE the name, not after it — SIA "Latvijas Finieris", AS "Latvenergo" — which is the opposite of English Inc or Ltd, so a template must prepend rather than append. SIA (sabiedrība ar ierobežotu atbildību, a limited company) covers the overwhelming majority of Latvian firms; AS is the joint-stock company, IK the sole trader, ZS the farm holding, VAS the state-owned joint-stock company. The remaining entries are descriptive words that really do trail a Latvian company name.
