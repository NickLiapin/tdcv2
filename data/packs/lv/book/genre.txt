description: Book genre. Dainas is not a translation of anything: it is the Latvian folk-song quatrain, of which Krišjānis Barons catalogued about 218 000 texts in the Cabinet of Folksongs, now on the UNESCO Memory of the World register, and it is a shelf category in a Latvian bookshop the way poetry is elsewhere.
