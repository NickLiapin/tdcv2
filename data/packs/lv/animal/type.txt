description: Animal, general. The first two thirds are Latvia's own fauna: alnis (elk), sumbrs (European bison, reintroduced), lūsis (lynx), bebrs (beaver), mežacūka (wild boar), stirna (roe deer) and vilks (wolf) all live in the country today, and the wolf and the lynx are hunted under quota rather than being a distant idea. Sesks, cauna and āpsis are the mustelids of the Latvian forest.
