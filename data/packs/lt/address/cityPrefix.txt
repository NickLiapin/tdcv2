description: Leading element of a Lithuanian place name (Naujoji Akmenė, Senoji Varėna, Didžioji Riešė). Lithuanian adjectives agree in gender, number and case with the settlement noun, so the same word appears here in several shapes — Naujoji goes with a feminine name, Naujasis with a masculine one, Naujieji with a plural one, and picking the wrong one is a grammar error, not a stylistic choice. Combine with a real toponym; it does not by itself name a city.
locale: lt
