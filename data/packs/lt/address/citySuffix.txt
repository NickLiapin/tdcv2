description: Trailing formant of a Lithuanian settlement name. This file works differently from its Polish or English counterpart and the difference is structural: Lithuanian does NOT build a place name by setting a qualifier word beside it, it builds one by SUFFIXATION into a single word — Rokiškis, Kupiškis, Vilkaviškis, Panevėžys, Kalveliai, Naujininkai. So these are joined to a stem with no space (Rok + iškis), never appended as a separate word, and alone they are not place names.
locale: lt
