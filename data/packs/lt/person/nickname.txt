description: Lithuanian hypocoristic / informal name. Lithuanian forms these with an unusually rich set of productive diminutive suffixes — -ukas, -elis, -utis, -aitis for men and -utė, -ytė, -elė for women — so almost any given name has one (Jonas -> Jonukas, Rasa -> Rasutė). Short clippings (Kazys from Kazimieras, Algis from Algirdas) are the other pattern.
locale: lt
