description: Lithuanian surnames in the MASCULINE form, which is the citation form: a male surname ends in -as, -is, -ys, -us or -ius (Kazlauskas, Adomaitis, Kairys, Butkus, Stankevičius), and Lithuanian imposes that ending even on foreign names, so George Bush is written Džordžas Bušas and Bill Clinton is Bilas Klintonas. A Lithuanian carries a single surname, so draw once. Values are nominative; Lithuanian has SEVEN cases and a surname in running text inflects (Kazlausko namas, Kazlauskui, su Kazlausku). The first ~30 lines are the genuinely commonest surnames in Lithuania; past that the order carries no frequency claim, which is why this list is not weighted. The female counterpart is NOT one form but three or four — see person/female/lastName. Lithuanian uses ą č ę ė į š ų ū ž and has no w, x or q; it has none of the Latvian letters ā ē ī ģ ķ ļ ņ.
locale: lt
