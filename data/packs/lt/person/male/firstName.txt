description: Lithuanian male given names. Every male given name ends in -as, -is, -ys, -us or -ius — the language will not leave a masculine name without one, which is why a borrowed name is naturalised on the way in (Džordžas, Tomas, Robertas) and why Lithuanian looks so different from Latvian, which uses -s. Nominative citation form; all seven cases inflect in running text (Jono, Jonui, su Jonu). Roughly frequency-ordered at the top — Jonas, Antanas and Petras really are the commonest older names, and Lukas, Matas and Nojus the commonest newborn ones — but the tail carries no frequency claim, so the list is not weighted. Uses ą č ę ė į š ų ū ž; no w, x, q, and none of the Latvian letters ā ē ī ģ ķ ļ ņ.
locale: lt
