description: Lithuanian female given names. Female given names end in -a or -ė (Rasa, Eglė) and never take the masculine -as/-is/-us, so the male and female lists have NO overlap at all — there are no unisex names in Lithuanian. The name inflects through all seven cases in use (Rasos, Rasai, su Rasa); this list is the nominative. Roughly generation-ordered at the top — Ona, Marija and Danutė are older women, Emilija, Kamilė and Austėja are newborns — with no frequency claim in the tail, so it is not weighted. Uses ą č ę ė į š ų ū ž; no w, x, q, and none of the Latvian letters ā ē ī ģ ķ ļ ņ.
locale: lt
