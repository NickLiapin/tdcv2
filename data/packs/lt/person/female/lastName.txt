description: Lithuanian female surnames, the MARRIED form in -ienė, derived by script from person/lastName line for line so the two lists stay aligned (line N here is the wife of line N there: Kazlauskas -> Kazlauskienė). This file holds ONE of the forms the language offers, and the choice is deliberate, because a Lithuanian surname marks not only sex but MARITAL STATUS — something Polish, Russian and Czech do not do. From the stem of Kazlauskas the language builds: Kazlauskienė (married), Kazlauskaitė (unmarried), and since 2003 the legally valid marital-status-neutral Kazlauskė. The unmarried suffix varies with the male ending — -as/-a take -aitė (Kazlauskaitė), -is/-ys take -ytė (Adomaitis -> Adomaitytė, Kairys -> Kairytė), -us takes -utė (Butkus -> Butkutė) and -ius takes -iūtė (Stankevičius -> Stankevičiūtė) — while -ienė and the neutral -ė attach to the bare stem regardless. The married form was chosen because it is the one most adult women in a Lithuanian population register carry; a config that wants the maiden or the neutral form should generate it from person/lastName with the rules above rather than expect it here. Nominative; all seven cases inflect in use.
locale: lt
