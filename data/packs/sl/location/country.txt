description: Country and territory names as Slovene names them. These are TRANSLATIONS, not transliterations: Germany is Nemčija, Hungary Madžarska, Austria Avstrija, Greece Grčija, Egypt Egipt, the United States Združene države Amerike. Slovene keeps its own exonyms for its neighbours and for the countries it has long dealt with, and adopts the local spelling only where it never had one. Many of them are ADJECTIVES used as nouns — Kitajska, Hrvaška, Madžarska, Švedska, Nizozemska, Portugalska, Slovaška — and decline like adjectives rather than like nouns, which a config building a prepositional phrase has to respect.
