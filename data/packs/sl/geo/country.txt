description: Country as Slovene names it, weighted by population in millions. Slovene TRANSLATES a country name rather than respelling it — Nemčija for Germany, Madžarska for Hungary, Nizozemska for the Netherlands, Združene države Amerike for the United States, Dunaj for Vienna — and only keeps the local form where Slovene never coined one (Brazilija, Kanada, Singapur). Note that many Slovene country names are ADJECTIVES used as nouns — Kitajska, Hrvaška, Madžarska, Švedska, Nizozemska — and they decline like adjectives rather than like nouns: the locative of Hrvaška is na Hrvaškem, not v Hrvaški.
