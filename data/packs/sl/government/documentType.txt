description: Official document type (Slovene). The words name kinds of document rather than one country's forms, but the set is the one a resident of Slovenia meets: the osebna izkaznica is the identity card, the izpisek iz matičnega registra the civil-register extract that replaced the separate birth and marriage certificates, and the potrdilo o stalnem prebivališču the residence certificate an administrative office issues on request. The NUMBERS these documents carry — EMŠO, davčna številka — belong to the Slovenia country pack, not here.
