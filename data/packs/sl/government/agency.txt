description: Government body. Slovene is the official language of ONE country, so these are the ministries and public offices of the Republic of Slovenia — that is what the words name. FURS is the financial administration every taxpayer files through, AJPES the public-records agency every company files its accounts with, and ZZZS, ZPIZ and ZRSZ the health, pension and employment funds. The upravna enota is the district office where a Slovene renews an ID card or registers an address, and it has no single-word English equivalent.
