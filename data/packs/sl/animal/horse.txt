description: Horse breed (Slovene). The lipicanec is named after LIPICA, the stud farm on the Slovene Karst founded in 1580 and still working — the breed's home is in Slovenia, not in Vienna or in Croatia, and the Lipica stud is Slovene state property and a UNESCO site. The slovenski hladnokrvni konj and the ljutomerski kasač are Slovenia's two other native breeds.
