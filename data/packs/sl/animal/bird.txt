description: Bird (Slovene). The bela štorklja nests on the chimneys of Prekmurje and is the bird a Slovene village counts every spring; the divji petelin and the triprsti detel are the Alpine forest birds Slovenia is watched for internationally. The names are native Slovene words rather than Latin borrowings.
