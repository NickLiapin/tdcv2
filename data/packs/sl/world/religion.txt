description: Religion or belief (Slovene). Slovenia is historically Roman Catholic, but the EVANGELIČANSKA — Lutheran — minority of Prekmurje carries a weight out of all proportion to its size, because the Slovene literary language exists because of the Reformation: Primož Trubar, a Protestant, printed the first two Slovene books in 1550 and Jurij Dalmatin translated the whole Bible into Slovene in 1584, which fixed the written language a century before any Catholic institution took it up. Slovenia also has one of Europe's larger shares of people declaring no religion.
