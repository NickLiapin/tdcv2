Weights are the pack default rank-decay curve rather than measured shares, and here the gap is the widest in the pack: the curve gives its top entry about 0,8 percent, where MARIJA really carries something over 4 percent of Slovene women — the most concentrated single name in this locale, because nearly every woman born before about 1960 was called it or carried it as a second name. The curve therefore flattens a genuinely top-heavy distribution, and a config modelling an older Slovene cohort should weight Marija far above what this file does. Note the distinctively Slovene names: Mojca, Špela, Neža, Manca, Tjaša, Urška, Zala, Nika, Ajda, Brina and Jerneja have no equivalent in the neighbouring languages. Saša and Vanja are the only two names shared with the male list, and are genuinely unisex in Slovene.
