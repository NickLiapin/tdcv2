description: Slovene male given name. Slovene writes the given name FIRST and the surname second, and a person carries one of each. The head of the list is the real order Slovenia's register reports for living men — Franc, Janez, Anton, Ivan and Marko lead, all of them saints' or emperors' names carried by the older generations, and they sit far above the short modern ones (Luka, Nejc, Žiga, Anže, Nik, Lovro, Vid) that lead among newborns and will invert the list within a generation. Weights are the pack default rank-decay curve rather than measured shares, and it UNDERSTATES the head: the curve gives its top entry about 0,8 percent, where Franc really carries around 2,5 percent of Slovene men and the ten commonest names together cover roughly a sixth of them. A config that needs realistic name-collision rates for Slovene men should weight the head more heavily than this file does. Note the names Slovene has that its neighbours do not: Jernej, Ožbej, Nejc, Aljaž, Blaž, Žiga, Anže, Matevž, Boštjan, Primož, Tilen, Domen — the -ž and -ej endings are a Slovene signature. Saša and Vanja are unisex and appear in the female list as well; they are the only overlap between the two.
