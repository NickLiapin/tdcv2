description: Common Slovene hypocoristic. Slovene clips a name and adds -e, -i, -o, -ec or -ka, which is why so many of them end in a vowel — Tone from Anton, Jože from Jožef, Lojze from Alojz, Grega from Gregor, Drejc from Andrej, Tevž from Matevž. The -ka and -ca endings are the ordinary female ones (Francka from Frančiška, Pepca from Jožefa, Metka from Marjeta). Several of these are now registered given names in their own right rather than short forms — Nejc, Jaka, Jure, Tine and Mojca are on birth certificates — which is why they also appear in the firstName lists.
locale: sl
