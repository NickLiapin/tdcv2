description: Hobby or leisure interest (Slovene). Four on this list are Slovene mass movements rather than pastimes: PLANINSTVO — the Alpine Association is the largest voluntary organisation in the country and roughly one Slovene in twenty is a member; GASILSTVO — over 1.300 volunteer fire brigades, so a village keeps one even where nothing burns; ČEBELARSTVO — beekeeping, with one beekeeper for every two hundred people, the highest density in Europe; and PETJE V ZBORU — Slovenia has one of the world's highest densities of amateur choirs. TAROK is the national card game, played with a 54-card Tarock deck and nothing like the fortune-telling one. JAMARSTVO is caving, which began as a discipline in the Slovene karst.
