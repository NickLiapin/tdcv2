description: Slovene job titles across sectors (tech, healthcare, trades, transport, business, service, public). Given in the MASCULINE, which is the citation form; Slovene has a distinct feminine title for nearly all of them — učitelj / učiteljica, zdravnik / zdravnica, natakar / natakarica, prodajalec / prodajalka — so a config writing about a woman needs the feminine, not this list. Slovene job adverts are legally required to name both forms or to state that the masculine covers both, which is why a real Slovene vacancy reads "prodajalec (m/ž)".
