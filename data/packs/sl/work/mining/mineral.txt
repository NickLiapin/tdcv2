description: Mineral or extracted material (Slovene). Slovenia's own extraction is dominated by limestone, gravel, clay and lignite rather than metal ore, and two of its historic mines are UNESCO sites: Idrija held the world's second largest ŽIVO SREBRO — mercury — deposit for five centuries, and the Škofja Loka and Mežica lead-and-zinc workings are museums now. The Velenje lignite mine still runs and still fuels the country's largest power station.
