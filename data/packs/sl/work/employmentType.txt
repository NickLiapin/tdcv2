description: Type of employment contract (Slovene). Two are Slovene institutions with no direct equivalent: ŠTUDENTSKO DELO, student work done on a napotnica issued by an authorised agency, which is how most Slovenes under 26 first work and which is taxed and insured quite differently from employment; and S. P., the samostojni podjetnik, a sole trader who is not a company but trades under a registered name — around a fifth of Slovene businesses. Podjemna pogodba and avtorska pogodba are civil-law contracts for one-off work outside any employment relationship, and a Slovene payroll treats them differently again.
