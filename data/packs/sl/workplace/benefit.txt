description: Employee benefit (Slovene). Three of these are Slovene statutory institutions rather than perks. REGRES ZA LETNI DOPUST is a holiday allowance every employer must pay every employee once a year, at least the minimum wage, by 1 July — it is a legal right, not a bonus, and there is no English equivalent. MALICA is the meal allowance paid for every day worked, also compulsory. POVRAČILO STROŠKOV PREVOZA NA DELO, the commuting allowance, is the third. Božičnica, the thirteenth salary, is the one on this list that genuinely is discretionary.
