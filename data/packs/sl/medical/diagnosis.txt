Slovene names diabetes SLADKORNA BOLEZEN, literally the sugar disease, and cataract and glaucoma SIVA MRENA and ZELENA MRENA, the grey and the green film — the everyday Slovene terms are native compounds rather than Latin, and they are what a Slovene discharge letter puts beside the Latin. Klopni meningoencefalitis is here because Slovenia has one of the highest tick-borne encephalitis rates in Europe and vaccination against it is an ordinary part of Slovene primary care.
