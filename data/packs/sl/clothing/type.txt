description: Garment type (Slovene). Note the Slovene words where the neighbours differ: hlače for trousers, majica for a T-shirt, srajca for a shirt, copati for indoor slippers — a Slovene household changes into copati at the door — and nogavice for socks. Superge is the everyday word for trainers, where Croatian says tenisice and Serbian patike.
