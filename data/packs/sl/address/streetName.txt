description: Street names that recur across Slovene towns (writers, scientists, regions, rivers, trees, trades, dates). Slovene overwhelmingly forms a street name from a POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVE in -ova/-eva built on the honoured person's surname — Prešeren gives Prešernova, Cankar Cankarjeva, Trubar Trubarjeva — and that adjective is feminine to agree with ulica or cesta, so it stands BEFORE the type (Prešernova cesta) or alone with the number (Prešernova 12). That is the dominant shape and the reverse of the Croatian balance, where the genitive phrase leads. The minority GENITIVE names here (talcev, svobode, republike) follow the type instead — Ulica talcev, Trg svobode — and are already inflected, so must not be inflected again. Streets unique to one city belong in the Slovenia country pack, not here.
locale: sl
