description: Building type (Slovene). Kozolec is the free-standing slatted hayrack of the Slovene countryside — a drying frame built as a permanent structure, found in this form nowhere else in Europe and protected as heritage. Zidanica is the vineyard cottage of Styria and Lower Carniola, where a family presses its own wine, and hram is its eastern name; both are ordinary Slovene building types with no English equivalent.
