description: Musical instrument (Slovene). The first block is Slovenia's own: the diatonična harmonika — the button accordion universally called the frajtonarica — is the national instrument and the engine of narodnozabavna glasba; the citre are the Alpine zither of Gorenjska; the oprekelj is the hammered dulcimer; the trstenke are panpipes; the žvegla is the transverse wooden flute of Prekmurje; and the drumlica is the jew's harp. All six are played, not merely remembered.
