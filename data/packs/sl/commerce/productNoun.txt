Grammatical gender varies across this list — miza is feminine, stol masculine, ogledalo neuter — so a config composing an adjective in front of one of these must inflect the adjective to match. NOTE the false friend with the neighbours: in Slovene miza is the TABLE and stol the CHAIR, while Croatian and Serbian stol is the table and stolica the chair. A pack copied from Croatian gets the furniture the wrong way round and nothing downstream notices.
