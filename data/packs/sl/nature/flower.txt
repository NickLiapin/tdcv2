description: Flower (Slovene). Blagajev volčin — Daphne blagayana — was found above Polhov Gradec in 1837, named after the Slovene count Blagaj who reported it, and is a Slovene botanical emblem; the planika, the edelweiss, is the flower of the Julian Alps and of the Alpine Association's badge; and the nagelj, the carnation, is what stands in the window boxes of a Slovene farmhouse.
