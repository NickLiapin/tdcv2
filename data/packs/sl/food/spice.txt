description: Spice or seasoning (Slovene). Bučno olje — the dark, thick roasted pumpkin-seed oil of Styria and Prekmurje — is a protected Slovene product and is used as a seasoning rather than a cooking fat, poured over salad and even over vanilla ice cream. Pehtran, tarragon, is the filling of the sweetest Slovene potica and is a dessert herb here rather than a savoury one.
