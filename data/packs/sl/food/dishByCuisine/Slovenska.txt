description: Dishes of the Slovene kitchen. Slovenia is the only country where the Alps, the Mediterranean, the Pannonian plain and the Karst meet, and each of the four cooks differently with almost no shared vocabulary: the Alpine north eats žganci, štruklji, obara and potica; the Karst and the coast eat jota, kraški pršut, frika and ocvirkovka; the Pannonian east eats prekmurska gibanica, bograč, bujta repa and prleška tünka; and Idrija, a mining town in between, has žlikrofi and nothing else does. Potica — a rolled yeast cake with a walnut, tarragon or crackling filling — is the one dish the whole country claims, and it is protected as a Slovene speciality in the EU register.
