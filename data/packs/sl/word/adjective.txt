description: Common adjective (Slovene, masculine nominative singular — the citation form). Slovene adjectives inflect for gender, number and all six cases, and Slovene has THREE numbers, so one adjective has far more forms than a French or English one: velik, velika, veliko in the singular, velika, veliki, veliki in the DUAL, and veliki, velike, velika in the plural. A config joining one of these to a noun must match all three properties.
