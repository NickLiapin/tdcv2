Slovene verbs come in ASPECT PAIRS — delati is the imperfective and narediti the perfective of roughly the same action — and this list gives one member of each pair. Slovene conjugation also marks the DUAL: delam, delaš, dela in the singular, delava, delata, delata for exactly two, delamo, delate, delajo for three or more, so a Slovene verb has nine present-tense forms where a Croatian or Russian one has six.
