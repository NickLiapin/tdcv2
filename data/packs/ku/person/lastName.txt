THREE KINDS SIT IN ONE LIST BECAUSE KURDISH REGISTERS MIX THEM: tribal and clan names (بارزانی، جاف، هەرکی، مەنگوڕ), place-of-origin nisbas (سلێمانی، مەهابادی، ئامەدی، کۆبانی), and bare patronymics — the father's or grandfather's given name recorded as the family name (ئەحمەد، محەمەد، ڕەسووڵ), which is what most Kurdish civil files actually hold, because Kurds traditionally carried no fixed inherited surname at all and the modern one was fixed by whichever state registry enrolled the family. THESE NAMES DO NOT INFLECT FOR GENDER — there is no ku/person/female/lastName.txt and its absence is the design, unlike Polish or Czech. Weights are the project default decay, NOT measured frequency; no Kurdish surname census exists.
