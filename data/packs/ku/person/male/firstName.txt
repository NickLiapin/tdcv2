description: Central Kurdish male given names. Two layers sit side by side and both are ordinary: Islamic names shared with the wider region (محەمەد، ئەحمەد، عەلی), and specifically Kurdish names built from Kurdish words — خەبات "struggle", سەرکەوت "victory", ڕزگار "liberated", زانا "learned", هیوا "hope", چیا "mountain", کاوە after the blacksmith of the Newroz legend. Weights are the project default decay, NOT measured frequency: no Kurdish civil register publishes name counts, so the order encodes only rough familiarity and no dataset should read a real distribution out of it.
