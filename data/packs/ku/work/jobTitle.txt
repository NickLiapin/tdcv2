description: Job titles in Central Kurdish, across sectors. پێشمەرگە is a genuine Kurdish occupation with no national army behind it — Kurds have no state, so the word names a fighter of the Kurdish forces rather than a rank in anyone's civil service. Trades and farming keep native Kurdish words (بۆریکێش، لەحیمکار، شوان، جووتیار) while modern professions take internationally shared terms respelled in Kurdish script.
locale: ku
