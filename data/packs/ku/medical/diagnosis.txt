description: Medical diagnosis that can occur in any patient — no sex or ancestry restriction. Sex-specific conditions live in diagnosisMale/diagnosisFemale, ancestry-linked ones in diagnosisByAncestry. Central Kurdish clinical writing mixes two registers and both are here: native descriptive names (ڕەبۆ for asthma, ئاوی سپی for cataract, نەقرەس for gout, سیل for tuberculosis) and internationally shared terms respelled in Kurdish script (پارکینسۆن، ئەلزهایمەر، لیمفۆما).
locale: ku
