description: Weekday names, listed from شەممە (Saturday) because the working week across Kurdish-speaking areas starts on Saturday and the weekend is Friday — in Iraqi Kurdistan often Friday and Saturday. Kurdish counts its weekdays off شەممە: یەکشەممە is "one after Saturday", دووشەممە "two after", and only هەینی (Friday) has an independent name.
locale: ku
