description: Gregorian month names as Central Kurdish writes them, January first, so the list lines up index for index with DATE_LOCALE.json. These are the Levantine-origin names used across Iraqi Kurdistan. Kurds ALSO keep a solar calendar whose year opens at Newroz on 21 March with the months خاکەلێوە، گوڵان، جۆزەردان، پووشپەڕ، گەلاوێژ، خەرمانان، ڕەزبەر، گەڵاڕێزان، سەرماوەز، بەفرانبار، ڕێبەندان، ڕەشەمە; no solar month boundary meets a Gregorian one, so those names are NOT interchangeable with these and are recorded in DATE_LOCALE.json notes rather than served here.
