description: Store-cupboard ingredient. JUFKA is the hand-stretched pastry sheet every pita is built from and there is no other word for it; KAJMAK is the skimmed clotted cream matured in a wooden vessel, eaten with bread and uštipci and sold by weight; PEKMEZ is the thick unsweetened fruit butter, usually plum, cooked down for a whole day. SUDŽUK is the dried spiced beef sausage. PIRINAČ and RIŽA are both current for rice — the first from Persian, the second from Italian — and a Bosnian recipe uses either.
