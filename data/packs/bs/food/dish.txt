description: A dish, without regard to cuisine. The Bosnian entries lead: this is the everyday eating of the country, and PITA in all its named forms is the centre of it. Note that burek means the MEAT one and nothing else in Bosnia — a cheese burek is a sirnica, and asking for 'sir burek' marks you as being from Serbia or Turkey. Somun is the flatbread ćevapi are served in and it is baked fresh for them.
