description: Spice or seasoning. BIBER is the Bosnian and Serbian word for pepper where Croatian says papar, and it came through Turkish; NANA is mint, also Turkish, and is what goes in tea; ALEVA PAPRIKA is the sweet ground red pepper that colours half the country's cooking. Vegeta is a brand and is written as one — it is a Croatian stock powder that is nonetheless in every Bosnian kitchen, and translating a brand would be wrong.
