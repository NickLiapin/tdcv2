description: Job title, MASCULINE singular, which is the citation form; the feminine is formed with -ica, -ka or -inja and not by one rule (ljekar/ljekarka, sudija/sutkinja, profesor/profesorica), so a config cannot derive it from this list. FIVE ENTRIES SPLIT THE STANDARDS AND THEY ARE THE MOST-USED WORDS IN THE FILE: ADVOKAT where Croatian says odvjetnik, SUDIJA where Croatian says sudac, HIRURG where Croatian says kirurg, HISTORIČAR where Croatian says povjesničar and Serbian istoričar — a genuine three-way split — and PREVODILAC where Croatian says prevoditelj. THE RELIGIOUS PROFESSIONS ARE THREE FAITHS' WORTH because Bosnian towns employ all of them: hodža and imam lead a mosque, muezin calls the prayer, mualim teaches, and beside them stand svećenik and fratar (Catholic), sveštenik and paroh (Orthodox) and rabin. Berberin and terzija are the Ottoman trade names still used beside frizer and krojač.
