description: Employment arrangement as a Bosnian contract names it. UGOVOR O DJELU is the contract-for-a-task that carries no employment rights and is the country's commonest way of paying someone who is in practice an employee; VOLONTERSKI RAD covers the unpaid post-graduation traineeship (pripravnički staž) that many professions still require before licensing. The informal economy is large and RAD NA CRNO is the word for it.
