description: Hotel room type. Bosnian counts beds, not people: jednokrevetna is a one-bed room, dvokrevetna a two-bed. A dvokrevetna soba may hold two single beds or one double, and Bosnian marks the difference with FRANCUSKI LEŽAJ — the French bed — which is the phrase a booking page uses for a double.
locale: bs
