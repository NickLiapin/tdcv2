TWO ARE BOSNIAN-SPECIFIC AND BOTH ARE PILGRIMAGES: HADŽ is the pilgrimage to Mecca, organised in Bosnia by the Islamic Community, and AJVATOVICA is the annual walk to Prusac — the largest Muslim gathering in Europe, held every June since the sixteenth century. Beside them MEĐUGORJE is the Catholic pilgrimage site in Herzegovina that draws over a million visitors a year, more than the rest of Bosnian tourism put together. GASTARBAJTERSKI POVRATAK, the diaspora's summer return, is a real travel category here: the roads fill with German and Austrian plates every July.
