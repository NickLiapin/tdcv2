description: Common adverb. LAHKO leads and is the Bosnian form of lako, the adverb from lahak — the most-quoted single word of the Bosnian standard. MEHKO follows. VJEROVATNO is the Bosnian and Serbian form where Croatian writes vjerojatno; DŽABA and TAMAN and MAHSUZ and JOK are orientalizmi doing everyday adverbial work — džaba is free of charge or in vain, taman is 'just right' or 'just as', mahsuz is 'specially, on purpose', jok is a flat no. BUJRUM is not an adverb but the Bosnian invitation — 'please, come in, help yourself' — and there is nowhere else to record it.
