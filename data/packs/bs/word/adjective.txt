description: Common adjective, MASCULINE INDEFINITE singular. Bosnian adjectives have TWO forms in the masculine — indefinite (nov, dobar) and definite (novi, dobri) — a distinction Croatian and Serbian share and most of Europe does not, and which decides the ending in five of the seven cases. THE FIRST THREE ARE THE STANDARD'S SIGNATURE: LAHAK, MEHAK and GLUH keep the h where Croatian and Serbian write lak, mek and (in Serbian) gluv. Lahak against lak is the single most cited example of the Bosnian h, and it is in every Bosnian grammar.
locale: bs
