description: Common verb, INFINITIVE. Bosnian verbs come in ASPECT PAIRS — one form for an unfinished action and one for a finished one — and this list gives mostly imperfectives; the pair is a different word (raditi/uraditi, pisati/napisati, jesti/pojesti, gledati/pogledati) and cannot be derived by any suffix rule. FOUR H-RETAINING FORMS ARE HERE ON PURPOSE: DUHATI, GRUHATI, TRUHNUTI and KIHATI keep the h that Croatian and Serbian have mostly dropped (puhati/duvati, trunuti, kijati) — this is the Bosnian standard's characteristic restoration of the sound, and it is not archaic. The last five are orientalizmi verbs with no Slavic equivalent at all: HALALITI is to forgive a debt or a wrong and release the other person from it, AŠIKOVATI is to court, MUHABETITI is to sit and talk well, SEVDISATI is to long for someone, BEĆARITI is to live unattached.
locale: bs
