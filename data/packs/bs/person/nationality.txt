description: Nationality as a Bosnian says it, MASCULINE singular. This is the form for a man; a woman is Bosanka, Njemica, Francuskinja, Talijanka, and there is no single rule — -ka, -kinja and -ica all appear — so a config cannot derive the feminine from this list. BOSANAC is a person from Bosnia and BOŠNJAK is a member of the Bosniak people: two different words for two different things, which English collapses into one 'Bosnian' and which is the commonest single error made about this country. Hercegovac is a third — someone from Herzegovina, which is half the country's name and not a region of Bosnia.
locale: bs
