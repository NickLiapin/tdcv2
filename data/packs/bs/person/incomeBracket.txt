That peg has not moved since 1998 and is why a Bosnian reads a euro price by halving the marka. The bands sit around the country's real average net wage, which passed 1,200 KM in 2023, so the middle of this list is the middle of this country and not of a European average. MONTHLY, not annual: Bosnian pay is quoted per month and the annual figure appears nowhere outside a tax return. Thousands are written with a full stop, decimals with a comma.
