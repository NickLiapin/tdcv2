description: Short forms Bosnians actually call each other by. The Bosniak hypocoristic cuts the name to one syllable and adds -o for men and -a for women: Muhamed gives Mujo, Mehmed gives Meho, Ibrahim gives Ibro, Hasan gives Haso, Fatima gives Fata, Hatidža gives Hata. The -o ending is shared with the whole region but the STEMS are Arabic-Turkish, which is what makes the set Bosnian rather than common: Sejo, Ćazo, Salko and Šabo have no Croatian or Serbian counterpart, because the full names they come from do not exist there. The Serb and Croat short forms of Bosnia (Boro, Jovo, Pero, Ivka, Jela) are in the same list because they are in the same towns.
locale: bs
