description: Pastime. Several here are Bosnian institutions rather than hobbies anywhere else. SEVDALINKA is the urban love song of Bosnia, sung to saz and accordion, and 'pjevanje sevdalinki' is a stated pastime. SIJELO is the evening gathering held in someone's house. The ĆEJF (Turkish keyf) is the deliberate slow enjoyment of a thing, and ISPIJANJE KAHVE — an hour over coffee from a fildžan — is named as a pastime here in a way it is not in Zagreb or Belgrade. Note kahva with its h: not kava (Croatian) and not kafa (Serbian).
locale: bs
