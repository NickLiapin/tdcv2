description: Bosnian male given names. THREE STOCKS IN ONE COUNTRY and this list mixes them on purpose, because a Bosnian dataset holding only one is a dataset about one community: the Bosniak layer of Turkish, Arabic and Persian origin (Mirza, Adnan, Muhamed, Dženan, Faruk, Mustafa, Sulejman) which is the part hr and sr do NOT share; the Serb stock of Bosnia (Nemanja, Milorad, Vukašin, Radovan); and the Croat stock (Ante, Ivica, Krešimir, Stipe, Zvonimir). Names in dž, đ, š, ž, ć and č are ordinary here, not decorative. Bosnia publishes no given-name frequency table, so the counts are this project's gentle default curve ordered by rough national share and NOT a measured percentage — do not quote them as one. Nominative only: a Bosnian sentence puts a given name through seven cases (Mirza, Mirze, Mirzi, Mirzu, Mirzo, Mirzi, Mirzom), so a config that drops a name into running prose gets the wrong case and no data file can fix that.
locale: bs
weighted: true
