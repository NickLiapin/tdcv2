description: Title placed before a name. The civil titles are the regional ones, abbreviated with a full stop and lower case in running text. THE RELIGIOUS TITLES ARE THREE FAITHS' WORTH and are ordinary Bosnian address, not exotic: hadži and hadžija for someone who has made the pilgrimage, hafiz for a memoriser of the Qur'an, efendija (ef.) for a religious teacher, mualim, and the Islamic Community's reis and muftija; beside them fra for a Franciscan — the order that has been in Bosnia since the fourteenth century — don and velečasni for Catholic priests, and otac, protojerej and vladika for the Serbian Orthodox clergy. No other locale in this project needs all three sets in one file.
