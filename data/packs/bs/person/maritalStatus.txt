description: Marital status as a Bosnian register writes it. THE ADJECTIVES INFLECT and both forms are listed, because a Bosnian form printed with only the masculine is wrong for half its subjects: neoženjen is an unmarried man and neudata an unmarried woman, and the two words share no root — a man 'is unwived', a woman 'is not given away'. Same split for udovac/udovica and razveden/razvedena. HUDOVICA, with the h, is the Bosnian-standard variant of udovica: one of the restored-h forms that mark this standard off from Croatian and Serbian, listed after the plain form because the plain form is commoner in print.
