description: What follows a Bosnian name. Bosnian has NO generational suffix — no Jr, Sr, II or III — and a son bearing his father's name is distinguished by the patronymic in brackets, never by a suffix; if your config needs one, it is modelling an American name and not a Bosnian one. What does follow a name is the academic or professional qualification, and Bosnia kept the pre-Bologna Yugoslav ladder alongside the Bologna one, so dipl. ing. and mr. sc. still print on cards next to bacc. and mag.
