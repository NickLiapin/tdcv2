description: Household budget line as a Bosnian family keeps it. REŽIJE is the single word for the bundle of utility bills — electricity, water, rubbish, communal charge — and it is what a Bosnian household actually budgets by; there is no per-utility budgeting in ordinary speech. Grijanje is a separate line because district heating is billed separately in the cities and firewood is bought by the cubic metre everywhere else.
locale: bs
