description: Creditworthiness band. BOSNIA HAS NO CONSUMER CREDIT SCORE in the American sense and no FICO-like number a person can be told; what exists is the Central Bank's CENTRALNI REGISTAR KREDITA (CRK), which records every loan and every late payment and which banks read as a yes or a no. These bands are the internal risk grades a Bosnian bank uses, not a score a customer sees — that distinction is the reason this file has words and not numbers.
