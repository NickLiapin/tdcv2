SEVDALINKA and the epic pjesma are named separately from lyric poetry because they are separate traditions here, and the collections of them are a shelf in every Bosnian bookshop. Note ROMAN for the novel — the French word, used across the region — and PRIPOVIJETKA, the ijekavian short story, against Serbia's pripovetka.
