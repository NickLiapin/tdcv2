description: Street names that recur across Bosnian towns — writers, medieval rulers, rivers, trades, dates. TWO GRAMMATICAL SHAPES ARE MIXED and a config must compose them differently: the GENITIVE phrases (Meše Selimovića, Kulina bana) follow the thoroughfare type and are already inflected, so must not be inflected again; the ADJECTIVAL names (Bosanska, Kolodvorska) are feminine to agree with ulica and stand before it or alone with the number. The writers here are the shared Bosnian canon of all three communities — Meša Selimović, Mak Dizdar, Ivo Andrić, Aleksa Šantić, Petar Kočić, Antun Branko Šimić, Musa Ćazim Ćatić — and Kulin ban signed the 1189 charter that is the first document of the Bosnian state. Streets unique to one city belong to the Bosnia and Herzegovina COUNTRY pack, not to this file.
