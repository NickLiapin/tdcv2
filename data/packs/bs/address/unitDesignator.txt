description: Bosnian address unit or interior designator, in the shape written before a number. A Bosnian address puts the house number after the street (Ulica Meše Selimovića 12) and the flat after that (12/3 or 12, stan 3). SPRAT is the Bosnian and Serbian word for a storey where Croatian says kat, and it is one of the plainest everyday splits between the standards. Avlija is the walled courtyard — an orientalizam, and a real part of an old-town address.
