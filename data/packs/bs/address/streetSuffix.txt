description: Bosnian thoroughfare type. It stands BEFORE a genitive street name (Ulica Meše Selimovića, Trg oslobođenja) and AFTER an adjectival one (Kolodvorska ulica, Bosanska ulica), so a config must know which shape it is composing. FOUR OF THESE ARE ORIENTALIZMI AND HAVE NO CROATIAN EQUIVALENT: sokak is the narrow lane of an old quarter, čikma (Turkish çıkmaz) is a dead-end sokak, mahala is the residential quarter itself and is still a live address element in Bosnian towns, and ćuprija is a bridge — the word in the title of Andrić's novel, and the one Bosnians use where Croatian says most. Kaldrma is the cobbled street surface, from Greek by way of Turkish, and names streets outright. Lower case: Bosnian writes the thoroughfare type in lower case unless it opens the address.
locale: bs
