Each Bosnian noun carries a fixed grammatical gender that its ending does not always reveal, so a config attaching an adjective from commerce/productAdjective.txt must agree with it — and cannot do so from a flat list. The kitchen block near the end is Ottoman: DŽEZVA is the long-handled coffee pot, FILDŽAN the small handleless cup it is poured into, TEPSIJA the round baking pan a pita is coiled in, IBRIK the water ewer, SINIJA the low round table a family eats from and SOFRA the meal laid on it. Those six are sold in Bosnian shops today.
