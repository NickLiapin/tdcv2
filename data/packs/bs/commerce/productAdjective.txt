description: Product adjective, MASCULINE singular — the citation form. Bosnian adjectives inflect for gender, number and case, so joining one of these to commerce/productNoun.txt gives the wrong ending most of the time (kvalitetan proizvod is right, kvalitetan roba is not, it must be kvalitetna roba). Bosnian additionally has DEFINITE and INDEFINITE adjective forms — nov against novi — a distinction Croatian and Serbian share but which most European languages do not have at all, and which no data pack can choose between without knowing the sentence.
