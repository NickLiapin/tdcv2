THE POSTAL OPERATOR IS THREE COMPANIES, NOT ONE: BH Pošta serves the Federation from Sarajevo, Pošte Srpske serves Republika Srpska from Banja Luka and Hrvatska pošta Mostar serves the Croat-majority cantons — three public postal operators inside one country, each with its own network and its own stamps, all delivering to the same addresses. That is the single most useful thing to know about shipping here. A2B Express and Euro Express are the large domestic couriers. Company names keep their real spelling.
