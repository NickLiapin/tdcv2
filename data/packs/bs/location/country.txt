description: Country and territory names as Bosnian names them. These are TRANSLATIONS AND NATURALISATIONS, not transliterations: Germany is Njemačka, Hungary Mađarska, Greece Grčka, Egypt Egipat, Austria Austrija. Bosnian keeps its own exonyms for its neighbours and for the countries it has long dealt with, and adopts a local spelling only where it never had one. THE BOSNIAN-AGAINST-CROATIAN SPLITS IN THIS LIST are ŠPANIJA (hr Španjolska), HOLANDIJA (hr Nizozemska), KIPAR shared, and the -ska/-ška endings; against Serbian, Bosnian keeps ŠVICARSKA (sr Švajcarska). BOSNA I HERCEGOVINA is the full name and is never shortened to Bosna in an official list — the second half of the name is a place with its own identity, not an appendix.
