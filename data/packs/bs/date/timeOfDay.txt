description: Part of the day. The first eight are the ordinary Slavic words; the last five are THE ISLAMIC PRAYER TIMES USED AS CLOCK WORDS, which is a Bosnian fact rather than a religious one. Sabah is first light, ikindija the middle of the afternoon, akšam the moment of sunset and jacija full dark, and a Sarajevo speaker says 'pred akšam' for early evening whether or not they pray. No Croatian or Serbian speaker uses them. Sabah also gives the everyday sabahzora, meaning first light.
