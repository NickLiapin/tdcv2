description: Legal term in Bosnian. TUŽBA is the claim, TUŽILAC the claimant in a civil case and the prosecutor in a criminal one — the same word for both roles, which is a genuine ambiguity in the language and not a translation error. PRESUDA is a judgment and RJEŠENJE an order; the difference decides which appeal you file. ŽALBA is the appeal and ROK the deadline, and a missed rok is final.
locale: bs
