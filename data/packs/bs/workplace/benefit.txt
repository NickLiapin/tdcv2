description: Employment benefit. TOPLI OBROK — the hot-meal allowance — and REGRES, the annual holiday allowance, are STATUTORY cash entitlements in Bosnia, not perks: they are paid monthly and yearly as separate lines on a payslip, and a Bosnian reading a salary figure will ask whether topli obrok is in it. Both entity labour laws set them. Prevoz is the commuting allowance, also a payslip line.
