description: Colour name in Bosnian. TWO VALUES SPLIT THE STANDARDS: NARANDŽASTA is the Bosnian and Serbian form and Croatian writes narančasta (dž against č); SMEĐA is Bosnian and Croatian where Serbian usually says braon. Teget is the deep navy of a uniform and comes from Turkish; ćilibarska is amber, from kehribar; and BOJA KAHVE — coffee colour — is written with the Bosnian h, not Croatian kave or Serbian kafe. Every colour here is an ADJECTIVE in the FEMININE, agreeing with boja (colour), which is itself a Turkish loan; a config attaching one to a masculine noun must change the ending (crvena haljina, crven auto).
