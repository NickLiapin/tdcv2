description: Blood group. BOSNIA WRITES ZERO, NOT THE LETTER O — the group is 0 (nula) on every Bosnian blood-donor card, lab report and health book, following the German and wider European convention, and NOT the American O. A config that renders it as the letter O has produced a document that would be read as a typo here. Rh factor is written Rh+ and Rh- after the group, so a full Bosnian entry reads '0 Rh+'.
