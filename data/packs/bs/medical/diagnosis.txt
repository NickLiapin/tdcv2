POSTTRAUMATSKI STRESNI POREMEĆAJ is high on this list and that is an epidemiological fact about this country, not a stylistic choice: population studies after 1995 put PTSD prevalence in Bosnia in the double digits — an order of magnitude above the European baseline — and it remains one of the country's largest untreated health burdens thirty years on. Note the Bosnian forms: HRONIČNI with the h against Croatian kronični, and the ijekavian PRIJELOM, POREMEĆAJ and LIJEČENJE.
