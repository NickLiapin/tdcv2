description: Kind of official document. THREE OF THESE ARE STRAIGHT BOSNIAN-AGAINST-CROATIAN SPLITS AND THEY ARE THE THREE COMMONEST DOCUMENTS A PERSON OWNS: LIČNA KARTA is the identity card (Croatian osobna iskaznica), PASOŠ the passport (Croatian putovnica) and SAOBRAĆAJNA DOZVOLA the vehicle registration (Croatian prometna dozvola). A config that renders a Bosnian ID card as 'osobna iskaznica' has produced a Croatian document. IZVOD IZ MATIČNE KNJIGE ROĐENIH is the birth certificate and is always called that in full on a counter. The identifier NUMBERS on these documents belong to the country pack, not here.
