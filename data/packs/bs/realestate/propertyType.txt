description: Kind of property as a Bosnian listing describes it. GARSONJERA is the studio flat — one room and a kitchenette — and is a fixed category on every Bosnian portal; JEDNOSOBAN, DVOSOBAN and TROSOBAN count the ROOMS OTHER THAN THE KITCHEN, so a 'dvosoban stan' has a living room and a bedroom, not two bedrooms, and that is the single commonest misreading of a Balkan property advert by a foreign buyer. VIKENDICA is the weekend cottage and IMANJE the farmstead with land.
