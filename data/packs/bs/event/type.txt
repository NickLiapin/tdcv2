description: Kind of event. TEFERIČ is the one no neighbour has: an open-air outing to a spring or a meadow, with food and singing, held through the summer — the word is Turkish teferrüç and the thing is a Bosnian institution. MEVLUD is the sung commemoration of the Prophet's birth, held in houses and mosques for births, deaths and house-warmings alike. TEVHID is the gathering after a death. SIJELO is the evening visit. SLAVA is the Orthodox family patron-saint feast, and KRSNO IME the same thing. All five are events a Bosnian calendar has and an English one does not.
