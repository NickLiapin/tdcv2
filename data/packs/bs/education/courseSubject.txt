description: School subject as a Bosnian timetable prints it. THE LANGUAGE SUBJECT IS THE CONTROVERSY OF BOSNIAN SCHOOLING: it is written 'Bosanski, hrvatski, srpski jezik' on a Federation timetable — three languages on one line, in alphabetical order, taught in one lesson — and 'Srpski jezik' on a Republika Srpska one. That line is why the phrase 'two schools under one roof' exists. TJELESNI ODGOJ is PE in Bosnian and Croatian; Serbian says fizičko vaspitanje, and Bosnian uses odgoj for upbringing where Serbian uses vaspitanje.
