THE PLACE-AND-NATURE BLOCK IS WHY YUGOSLAV-ERA NAMES LOOK THE WAY THEY DO: firms were named for the mountain, river or valley they stood in — Bosna, Una, Vrbas, Neretva, Igman, Vlašić, Romanija, Trebević — and those names survived privatisation. The second block is the Ottoman commercial vocabulary that still names shops (Čaršija, Bezistan, Han, Ćilim, Sarač). The third is the international abstract layer any modern Bosnian startup uses.
