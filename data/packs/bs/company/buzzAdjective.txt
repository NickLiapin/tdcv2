description: Marketing adjective, MASCULINE singular. Bosnian adjectives agree with the noun they modify in gender, number and case, so a config joining one of these to company/buzzNoun.txt will produce a WRONG ending roughly two thirds of the time — inovativan pristup is right, inovativan strategija is not, it must be inovativna strategija. This file cannot fix that and a serious config should not concatenate the two blindly. The English loans at the end (smart, premium, online) do not inflect at all, which is one reason Bosnian marketing reaches for them.
