description: Marketing verb, INFINITIVE. Bosnian verbs come in ASPECT PAIRS — one form for an action in progress and one for a completed one — and this list gives the imperfective, which is what a slogan uses: optimizirati, unapređivati, razvijati. The perfective partner is a different word (unaprijediti, razviti), so a config building a past tense from this list produces 'was improving' where it means 'improved'. That is a Slavic problem no wordlist can solve, and the reason it is stated here.
locale: bs
