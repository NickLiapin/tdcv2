description: Dog breed as Bosnian names it. TORNJAK IS THE NATIVE ONE: the Bosnian-Herzegovinian and Croatian shepherd dog of the Dinaric mountains, recognised by the FCI in 2007 under a joint Bosnian-Croatian standard, and it is the breed a Bosnian names first. Barak — the Bosnian coarse-haired hound — is the second, a hunting dog bred in the Bosna valley. The rest are the international breeds, with the descriptive names translated and the trade names kept.
