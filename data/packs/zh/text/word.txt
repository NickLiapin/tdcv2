description: Ordinary vocabulary for filler text, in Traditional characters. The list is generic on purpose — time, place, people, body, feeling, house, food, nature, study, work, technology, change — so that generated prose reads as Chinese without being about anything. Where Taiwan and the mainland use different words rather than different glyphs, this list takes Taiwan's: 軟體 for software against 软件, 網路 for network against 网络, 資料 for data against 数据, 資訊 for information against 信息, 晶片 for chip against 芯片, 品質 for quality against 质量, 水準 for level against 水平, 機車 for a scooter, 捷運 for the metro and 腳踏車 for a bicycle. A converted mainland corpus would carry none of these and would read to a Taiwanese eye as translated rather than written.
