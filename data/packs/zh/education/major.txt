description: University subjects as Taiwanese department names. The unit is the 學系, and an applicant applies to a named department at a named university rather than to the university alone, so 資訊工程學系 at one institution and 資訊工程學系 at another are separate applications with separate cut-off scores. The engineering names reflect what the island's economy runs on — 電機, 電子 and 光電 are three distinct departments, not one, and they feed a semiconductor industry that makes most of the world's advanced chips. 醫學系 is a six-year undergraduate programme entered straight from secondary school, which is why there is no separate 臨床醫學 entry of the kind the mainland catalogue lists.
locale: zh
