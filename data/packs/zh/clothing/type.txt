description: Garments. Several are Taiwan's own word rather than a converted mainland one: a hoodie is 帽T, built from 帽 hood and the letter T, where the mainland says 卫衣; a dress is 洋裝, literally Western clothing, where the mainland says 连衣裙. The last three — 旗袍, 唐裝, 漢服 — are historical forms worn now for weddings and festivals.
