description: Genetic ancestry group, used as the parent of diagnosisByAncestry. These are ancestry populations as clinical genetics defines them — geographic descent, the kind that predicts carrier frequencies, HLA associations and drug metabolism — and not the ethnic categories of person/ethnicity.txt, which records Taiwan's Hoklo, Hakka, mainlander and indigenous framing alongside sixteen officially recognised Austronesian peoples. That file answers a census question and this one answers a pharmacogenetic one, which is why they neither overlap nor substitute for each other: nearly every patient in a Taiwanese record falls under 東亞裔 here no matter which group the census would assign them. The clinical weight of that is real, since HLA-B*1502 and the carbamazepine screening it triggers are tied to this ancestry rather than to any census category.
