description: Symptom as a Taiwanese patient reports it, which is not how a mainland one does. Several pairs invert or replace outright: frequent urination is 頻尿 rather than 尿頻, with the two characters in the opposite order; heartburn is 火燒心 and reflux is 胃酸逆流; a runny nose is 流鼻水 rather than 流涕; and a sore throat is 喉嚨痛. Two entries turn on characters Taiwan standardises differently — constipation is written 便祕 with the 示 radical, following the Ministry of Education's dictionary, and muscle soreness takes 痠, since Taiwan reserves 酸 for the acid. The last entry is traditional medicine rather than Western medicine: the notion of internal heat blamed for mouth ulcers, sore throat and acne, which Taiwan calls 火氣大 where the mainland says 上火. It is here because a Taiwanese patient will actually say it, and because the herbal shops sell 退火 drinks on the strength of it.
locale: zh
