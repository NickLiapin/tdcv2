description: Diagnoses seen in 皮膚科 (Taiwanese Mandarin). Taiwan's subtropical heat and humidity keep fungal infection a year-round complaint rather than a seasonal one, and 足癬 is common enough that the whole island calls it 香港腳; the same climate fills summer clinics with eczema and hand dermatitis. The register is Taiwanese throughout — 異位性皮膚炎 for atopic dermatitis, 脂漏性皮膚炎 for the seborrhoeic kind, and 乾癬 for psoriasis, a name that shares no characters at all with the mainland one.
