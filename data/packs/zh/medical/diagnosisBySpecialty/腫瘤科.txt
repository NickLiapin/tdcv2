description: Diagnoses seen in 腫瘤科 (Taiwanese Mandarin). Cancer has been Taiwan's leading cause of death every year since 1982, and the island's profile is its own — chewing 檳榔 (betel quid), usually with tobacco and slaked lime, gives Taiwanese men one of the highest rates of 口腔癌 in the world, while endemic hepatitis B made 肝癌 a top killer for a generation. Taiwanese names are shorter than the mainland's in several places, giving 乳癌 for breast cancer, 食道癌 for oesophageal and 大腸直腸癌 for colorectal.
