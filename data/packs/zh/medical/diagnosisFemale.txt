description: Diagnosis that only affects women, or is overwhelmingly female, in Taiwan's clinical register. Taiwan writes 乳癌 and 子宮頸癌 where the mainland prefers 乳腺癌 and 宫颈癌, and it treats the breast as an organ, 乳房, rather than a gland, so fibrocystic change is named of the breast. A named syndrome is a 症候群 throughout — 多囊性卵巢症候群, 更年期症候群, 透納氏症候群 — against the mainland's 综合征, and this holds for every syndrome regardless of specialty. Ectopic pregnancy is the plain 子宮外孕, pre-eclampsia is 子癲前症, the pelvis is 骨盆腔 and not 盆腔, and period pain reverses its two characters to 經痛. Cervical disease is disproportionately well documented in Taiwan because National Health Insurance has funded an annual Pap smear for every woman aged 30 and over since 1995, and the coverage that followed cut cervical cancer mortality by roughly two thirds and pushed it far down the national cancer rankings.
