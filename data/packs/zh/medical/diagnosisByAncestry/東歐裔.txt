description: Conditions markedly more frequent in people of Eastern European ancestry. Slavic populations carry their own founder alleles rather than a thinner version of the Western European set. The large CFTR deletion CFTRdele2,3 accounts for a substantial share of cystic fibrosis in Russia, Poland, Belarus and Ukraine and is almost unseen further west, and the ATP7B variant H1069Q makes Wilson disease the characteristic inherited liver disorder of the region. Phenylketonuria and congenital hypothyroidism both run above the West European average, and they are the two conditions the region's long-established newborn screening programmes were built around. Acute intermittent porphyria and hereditary haemochromatosis are followed through well-documented Central and Eastern European pedigrees. A prevalence signal, not an exclusive one.
