description: Conditions markedly more frequent in people of Middle Eastern ancestry. Consanguineous marriage rates are among the highest recorded anywhere across much of the region, and the recessive burden follows — spinal muscular atrophy, congenital adrenal hyperplasia, phenylketonuria, lysosomal storage disorders and non-syndromic hearing loss all run above world averages, frequently as one founder variant shared by an extended family or a single village. Familial Mediterranean fever and Behçet disease trace the old Silk Road distribution, the second of them tied to HLA-B51. Beta thalassaemia and G6PD deficiency mark the same malarial history as the wider Mediterranean, and type 2 diabetes prevalence in the Gulf states is now among the highest in the world. A prevalence signal, not an exclusive one.
