description: Hospital department as a Taiwanese hospital signs it. The names are not the mainland's and mostly cannot be derived from them. Internal-medicine subspecialties are built on the organ written out in full — cardiology is 心臟內科, nephrology 腎臟內科, pulmonology 胸腔內科 — and two of them name something other than an organ at all: 新陳代謝科 names the metabolism it manages rather than the endocrine glands the mainland's 內分泌科 names, and 胃腸肝膽科 spells out stomach, intestine, liver and gallbladder instead of picking one. Elsewhere paediatrics is 小兒科, dentistry is 牙科, general surgery is 一般外科, urology drops the 外 to become 泌尿科, and rehabilitation is 復健科. 過敏免疫風濕科 is one board-certified specialty covering allergy, immunology and rheumatology together, where the mainland splits allergy off. Two departments have no counterpart on a mainland list. 家庭醫學科 is family medicine, boarded in Taiwan since the late 1980s and the department that runs most hospitals' screening service, which is why 健康檢查中心 follows it here. 臨床心理科 is staffed by 臨床心理師, a separately licensed profession created by the Psychologists Act of 2001 and distinct from the physicians in 精神科. The traditional side keeps its own naming too: manual therapy sits in 中醫傷科 rather than a 推拿科. 耳鼻喉科 is one of the few names both sides share unchanged.
locale: zh
