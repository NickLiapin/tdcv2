description: Booking states as a Taiwanese or Hong Kong reservation system reports them. The chain is not linear — 已改期 can follow 已確認 without passing through cancellation, and 退款處理中 sits between the cancellation and the money actually arriving, which for a Taiwanese credit card refund is typically one to two billing cycles rather than days. Taiwan writes 付款 for payment where the mainland writes 支付.
