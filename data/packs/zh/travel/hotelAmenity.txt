description: Hotel facilities as a Taiwanese or Hong Kong booking page lists them. 三溫暖 is the local word for a sauna, and it is a sound-and-sense rendering of the foreign word that happens to read as three warmths, which is a fair description of the hot-warm-cold circuit. Air conditioning is 冷氣 in ordinary speech and 中央空調 only when it is a building system; the mainland's 空調 alone sounds technical here. 溫泉 is worth its place because Taiwan sits on a volcanic arc and hot springs at Beitou, Jiaoxi and Guanziling are the reason for a large share of domestic hotel nights. Taiwan writes 盥洗用品 for toiletries and 行李寄放 for luggage storage, against the mainland's 洗漱用品 and 行李寄存.
