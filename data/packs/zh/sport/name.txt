description: Sport in Taiwanese Traditional Chinese. 棒球 leads because it does: baseball arrived with the Japanese colonial administration, the CPBL has been running since 1990, and a Taiwanese national team win is a public event in a way no other sport's is — a mainland list headed by table tennis describes a different country. 撞球 ranks high too; Taiwan has produced several world nine-ball champions. Note the vocabulary as much as the order: table tennis is 桌球 and badminton 羽球 in Taiwan, cycling is 自由車, kayaking is 輕艇, rhythmic gymnastics is 韻律體操, and figure skating is 花式滑冰 — the mainland words for all of these are different. 國術 is the Taiwanese name for traditional Chinese martial arts and sits beside the internationally codified 武術.
