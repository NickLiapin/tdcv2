---
description: Playing position in Taiwanese Traditional Chinese, across sports. Volleyball is where Taiwan's terms part company with the mainland's: the setter is the 舉球員, literally the one who lifts the ball, not 二傳, and the libero is the 自由球員 rather than 自由人. In racket sports the position is the numbered slot a player fills in a team tie, written out in full as 第一單打 or 混合雙打.
locale: zh
