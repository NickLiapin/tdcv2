description: Corporate buzzwords, adjectival, in the Taiwanese register. Two things separate this from a mainland list. 數位 is Taiwan's word for digital where the mainland says 数字, and 客製化 is bespoke where the mainland says 定制化. And the flavour differs: Taiwan's corporate language is shaped by contract manufacturing and semiconductors rather than by consumer internet, so 精緻化 and 虛實整合 do the work that 下沉 and 中台化 do on the other side.
