description: Corporate buzzwords, nominal. 護國神山 — the sacred mountain that guards the nation — is the Taiwanese press's name for TSMC, and by extension for any company whose exports are load-bearing for the whole economy; there is no equivalent term anywhere else. 良率 (yield) and 稼動率 (equipment utilisation, a term borrowed from Japanese) are ordinary boardroom vocabulary here because so much of the economy is contract manufacturing, and they would be technical jargon in most other business cultures.
locale: zh
