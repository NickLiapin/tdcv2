description: Bicycles. YouBike is Taiwan's public bike-share, run by Giant — the world's largest bicycle manufacturer, which is Taiwanese — and it is dense enough in Taipei that it functions as part of the transport network rather than as a leisure scheme. Note 腳踏車 for bicycle, where the mainland says 自行车, and 機車 for a scooter, which on the mainland means something else entirely.
