description: Street-type words in a Taiwanese address. The hierarchy is strict and is what makes Taiwanese addresses navigable: 路 is a major road, 街 a smaller street, and then 巷 is a lane off it and 弄 an alley off the lane — so 中山路一段100巷5弄 descends three levels, and the numbers are positional rather than arbitrary.
locale: zh
