description: Documents a Taiwanese person holds. The identity card is 國民身分證 and carries a ten-character number whose first letter encodes the county of first registration and whose second digit encodes sex — a design that makes the number partly self-describing. 台胞證 is the permit a Taiwanese resident needs to enter mainland China, issued by Beijing rather than Taipei, and its existence is a consequence of the two sides not recognising each other's passports.
