description: Selling points in a Taiwanese listing. Two of these have no equivalent elsewhere. 公設比 is the share of the building's floor area that is common space — lobby, corridors, plant rooms — and it is charged to the buyer at the same price per 坪 as the flat itself, so a low 公設比 is a genuine saving and is advertised as one. 頂樓加蓋 is a rooftop addition, usually built without a permit, tolerated in practice and sold openly, and it is why a listed floor area and a measured one can disagree.
