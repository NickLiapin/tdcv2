description: Room counts as a Taiwanese listing writes them. The formula is 房 for bedrooms and 廳 for reception rooms, so 三房兩廳 is three bedrooms plus a living room and a dining room. Taiwan says 房 where the mainland says 室, and 衛浴 where it says 卫生间 — the same layout described with a different set of words.
locale: zh
