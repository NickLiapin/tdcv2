description: Writing systems, and the first two entries are the point of this whole pack. Taiwan calls its script 正體字 — orthodox characters — rather than 繁體字, traditional or complex characters, because the name asserts that nothing was added: the mainland removed. Taiwan also teaches pronunciation with 注音符號 (bopomofo), a set of 37 symbols invented in 1913, rather than with 漢語拼音 in Latin letters, so a Taiwanese keyboard and a mainland one are laid out on different principles. 台灣羅馬字 is the romanisation used for Taiwanese Hokkien.
