description: Employee benefits in Taiwan, and the package is structurally different from the mainland's 五险一金. 勞健保 is labour insurance plus National Health Insurance, both compulsory and both split between employer and employee. 三節獎金 is the three-festival bonus, paid at Lunar New Year, Dragon Boat and Mid-Autumn, and is a real component of Taiwanese annual pay rather than a courtesy. 特休 is statutory paid leave and scales with years of service, from three days in the first six months to thirty at ten years.
