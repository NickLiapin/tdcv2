description: Shipping-tracking event in Taiwan. 已送達門市 is the step a mainland tracking log has no equivalent for — the parcel has reached the convenience store the buyer chose, and the clock on the seven-day collection window starts there. Only when the buyer walks in does the log move to 已取貨; an uncollected parcel goes back to the seller as 退回中.
