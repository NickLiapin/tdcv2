description: Unit of measure in Taiwan. Two entries carry traps. 台斤 is the Taiwanese catty and weighs 600 grams, divided into sixteen 兩 of 37.5 grams each — it is not the mainland 市斤, which is 500 grams, so the same word without the 台 prefix means different weights on either side of the strait and a market price per 斤 cannot be compared across them. 坪 is the area unit every Taiwanese property listing quotes, about 3.306 square metres, inherited from the Japanese tsubo. Metric units mostly take 公 as a prefix: 公尺, 公分, 公克, 公升, 公噸.
locale: zh
