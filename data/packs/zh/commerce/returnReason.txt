description: Reason a Taiwanese customer returns an order. The first entry is the statutory one: the Consumer Protection Act gives a distance-selling buyer a 七日鑑賞期, a seven-day inspection window in which the order can be sent back without giving any reason and without penalty. It is counted from the day the goods arrive, not from the order date, and because the buyer needs no grounds it is the reason code most returns are filed under. 買貴退差價 is the price-drop guarantee several Taiwanese platforms offer.
