Computing and appliance vocabulary is where Taiwan and the mainland diverge hardest, and this list uses Taiwan's words throughout: a mouse is a 滑鼠, a laptop a 筆記型電腦, a monitor a 螢幕, a hard disk a 硬碟, a USB stick a 隨身碟, a printer an 印表機. Air conditioning is 冷氣, an air purifier a 空氣清淨機, a gas hob a 瓦斯爐, an air fryer a 氣炸鍋. A bicycle is a 腳踏車 and a crash helmet an 安全帽 — Taiwanese law requires one on every scooter, which is why the item is a mass-market staple.
