description: Product material in Taiwanese Traditional Chinese. Taiwan settled its chemistry vocabulary independently of the mainland, so these words differ at the root and not merely in glyph: silicon is 矽, which makes silicone rubber 矽膠 and never 硅膠; acrylic sheet is 壓克力; polyester is 聚酯纖維 rather than 滌綸; and toughened glass is 強化玻璃 rather than 鋼化玻璃. Denim is 丹寧, borrowed by sound.
