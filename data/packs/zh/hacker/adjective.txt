description: Technical adjectives in the Taiwanese register. The split from mainland usage is wide enough that a document can be placed by these words alone. Taiwan writes 數位 for digital where the mainland writes 數字, 分散式 for distributed against 分佈式, 非同步 for asynchronous against 異步, 快取 for cached against 緩存, 相容 for compatible against 兼容, 最佳化 for optimised against 優化, and 多執行緒 for multithreaded against 多線程. 備援 is the Taiwanese word for a redundant standby system, which the mainland calls 冗餘.
