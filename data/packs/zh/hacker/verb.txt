description: Technical verbs in Taiwanese usage. 除錯 is debugging, where the mainland says 調試; 登入 is logging in, where the mainland says 登錄; 重新開機 is rebooting a machine, and 設定 rather than 配置 is what you do to it afterwards. 拷貝 and 複製 both mean copy and both are current — 拷貝 is the older loanword from the English and tends to attach to whole disks and tapes, 複製 to files and text. A loop, which appears in the compiled result rather than in this list, is a 迴圈 here and a 循環 on the mainland.
