description: Technical abbreviations, unchanged in Taiwanese and Hong Kong writing and speech. Chinese has native forms for most of these — 應用程式介面 for API, 中央處理器 for CPU — but they appear in textbooks and government procurement documents, not in an engineer's sentence, and the Latin letters are read as English letters. This is the one part of the technical vocabulary that Taiwan and the mainland share exactly. Everything around it does not, which is worth knowing when reading a record — the same API is served here by a 伺服器, not a 服務器.
