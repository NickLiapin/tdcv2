description: Technical actions written as nouns, in Taiwanese usage. Chinese verbs do not inflect, so the difference between this list and the verb list is one of framing rather than grammar — these are the labels a progress bar or a job queue shows. The vocabulary is Taiwan's own — 設定 rather than the mainland's 配置 for configuring, 連線 rather than 連接 for connecting, 移轉 rather than 遷移 for a migration, and 貼上 rather than 粘貼 for pasting. 回溯 is a rollback.
