description: Musical genres. Three of these are Taiwanese and appear on no mainland list: 歌仔戲 is Taiwanese opera, the only major Chinese opera form that was born in Taiwan rather than imported; 布袋戲 is glove puppetry, which in the 1970s drew television audiences of over ninety per cent; and 校園民歌 is the campus folk movement of the same decade, in which students began writing Mandarin songs of their own instead of covering English ones. 台語歌 — Hokkien-language pop — carries a separate industry and its own charts.
locale: zh
