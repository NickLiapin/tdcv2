description: Spice or aromatic, Taiwanese register. 九層塔 is what makes 三杯雞 the dish it is — Taiwan's name for basil, literally 'nine-storey pagoda', where the mainland uses the botanical 羅勒 and means a different variety by it. 紅蔥頭, the shallot, fried down into 油蔥酥, is the single flavour that identifies Taiwanese cooking: it is what makes 滷肉飯 taste Taiwanese rather than merely Chinese. 馬告 is a native mountain pepper whose name comes from Atayal, 沙茶醬 is the Chaozhou peanut and dried-shrimp paste Taiwan uses wherever the mainland reaches for sesame paste, and 當歸 sits in a spice list at all because Taiwanese home cooking treats medicinal herbs as seasoning rather than as medicine.
locale: zh
