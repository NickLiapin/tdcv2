description: Cooking ingredient in Traditional characters, Taiwanese register. Several names are simply different words from the mainland's for the same thing, and a substituted glyph will not fix them: 太白粉 is the potato starch the mainland calls 澱粉, 烏醋 is the dark vinegar it calls 陳醋, 沙拉油 is the everyday cooking oil, and salmon is 鮭魚 where Hong Kong and the mainland both say 三文魚. The dairy loans mark the island twice over — 起司 for cheese against the mainland's 奶酪 and Hong Kong's 芝士, and 優格 for yoghurt against the mainland's 酸奶. 醬油膏 is soy sauce thickened with starch and is not interchangeable with 醬油; it is what goes on 肉圓 and 蚵仔煎. 虱目魚, the milkfish, is farmed across the southwest coast and eaten at breakfast.
locale: zh
