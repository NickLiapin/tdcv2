description: Dishes of 泰國菜. 月亮蝦餅 is the entry worth knowing: the 'moon prawn cake' is on the menu of essentially every Thai restaurant in Taiwan, is what Taiwanese diners think of first when they think of Thai food, and does not exist in Thailand — it was invented here. Note also that pineapple is 鳳梨 in Taiwan, so the fried rice served in a hollowed fruit is 鳳梨炒飯 and never the mainland's 菠蘿飯.
