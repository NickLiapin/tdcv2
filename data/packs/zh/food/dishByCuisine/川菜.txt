description: Dishes of 川菜. Sichuan food reached Taiwan with the 1949 migration and was rebuilt here: 川味紅燒牛肉麵, the red-braised beef noodle soup now treated as Taiwan's national dish, was invented in the 眷村 — the military dependents' villages of Kaohsiung — by soldiers from Sichuan working with what Taiwan sold, and it does not exist in Sichuan. 麻辣 names two separate sensations: 麻, the numbing tingle of 花椒, and 辣, the burn of chilli.
