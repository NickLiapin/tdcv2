description: Dishes of 火鍋. Taiwan's hotpot splits into the imported 麻辣鍋 and two native winter genres that are restaurants in their own right: 薑母鴨, duck in sesame oil, old ginger and rice wine, and 羊肉爐, mutton stewed with medicinal herbs. Both are 進補 — food eaten deliberately to warm the body — and both appear on the street the week the weather turns. Taiwan dips hotpot in 沙茶醬, a Chaozhou paste of dried shrimp, garlic and peanut, where the mainland uses sesame paste; ordering sesame paste in Taipei marks you as a visitor.
