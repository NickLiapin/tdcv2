description: Dishes of 日本料理. Japan governed Taiwan for fifty years to 1945 and the food stayed behind as ordinary rather than exotic, which is why a small town with no Italian restaurant will still have three Japanese ones. The vocabulary records the same history: Taiwanese says 生魚片 for sashimi where the mainland borrows the Japanese word 刺身, and 便當 — the Japanese bentō — has become a plain Chinese word in Taiwan while the mainland says 盒飯.
