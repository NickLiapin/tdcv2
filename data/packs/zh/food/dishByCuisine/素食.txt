description: Dishes of 素食. Taiwan has one of the highest vegetarian rates in the world — roughly a tenth of the population — sustained by Buddhist practice and by 初一十五, the first and fifteenth of each lunar month, when families who otherwise eat meat go 素 for the day, which is why even convenience stores stock vegetarian lunchboxes. Taiwanese Buddhist 素食 excludes not only meat but the 五辛: garlic, onion, leek, chive and asafoetida. 蔬食 is the newer label for vegetarian food without that restriction, aimed at diners with no religious motive.
