description: Dishes of 熱炒, the Taiwanese stir-fry beer house — fast, loud, and traditionally a flat 100 元 a plate, which is why the shops are signposted 熱炒100. 蒼蠅頭 means 'fly heads' and is minced pork with flowering chives and fermented black beans; the black specks the name refers to are the beans. 金沙 in any Taiwanese dish name means salted duck-egg yolk, and 塔香 means the dish is finished with 九層塔, Taiwan's basil.
locale: zh
