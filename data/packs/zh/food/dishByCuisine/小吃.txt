description: Dishes of 小吃 — the night-market repertoire. The word translates as 'small eats' and misleads: in Taiwan this is where people have dinner, and a 小吃 stall that has sold one dish for forty years outranks most restaurants. 蚵仔麵線 is thickened enough to be eaten with a spoon and no chopsticks, 米血糕 is pig's blood set with sticky rice and served on a stick, and 藥燉排骨 is ribs simmered in Chinese medicinal herbs, sold from the same window as the fried chicken cutlet.
locale: zh
