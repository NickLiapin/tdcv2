description: Dishes of 原住民料理, the food of Taiwan's sixteen officially recognised Austronesian peoples — a cuisine with no mainland counterpart at all, since these languages are not related to Chinese and several ingredients are named by transliteration from them. 馬告 is 山胡椒, a native mountain pepper carrying a lemongrass note, borrowed from Atayal. 雨來菇, 'rain mushroom', is a ground algae that appears only after rain and is sold under the trade name 情人的眼淚. 竹筒飯 is sticky rice steamed inside a green bamboo section and split open at the table.
locale: zh
