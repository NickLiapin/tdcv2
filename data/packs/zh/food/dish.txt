description: Taiwanese dishes, in Traditional characters. Most of these are night-market food rather than restaurant food, which is the point: the 夜市 is where Taiwan eats, and 蚵仔煎, 大腸包小腸 and 臭豆腐 are stall dishes with no restaurant equivalent. 滷肉飯 — braised pork over rice — is the island's defining everyday dish, and 珍珠奶茶 was invented in Taichung in the 1980s and has since gone everywhere.
locale: zh
