description: Condition that affects only women, or overwhelmingly women, in Taiwanese medical Chinese. Note how far the terms drift across the strait: breast cancer is 乳癌 and not 乳腺癌, the cervix is 子宮頸 and not 宮頸, the pelvis is 骨盆腔 and not 盆腔, pre-eclampsia is 子癲前症 and not 子癇前期, and lupus is 全身性紅斑性狼瘡 rather than 系統性紅斑狼瘡. Period pain reverses its own two characters — 經痛 in Taiwan, 痛經 on the mainland — and depression is 憂鬱 here against 抑鬱 there, which is why postnatal depression can only be 產後憂鬱症.
