description: Single characters that Taiwanese female given names are BUILT from — the counterpart of person/male/nameChar.txt. Draw one or two to compose a name the way parents actually do. The traditional fields are plants and flowers (梅 蘭 菊 蓉 薇 蕙), jade and stones, which is why so many carry the 玉 radical (珊 珮 琪 琳 瑤 瑾 瑄), and weather and light (雪 霞 晨 曦 月 露). The generational signal is sharp enough to date a bearer: 淑, 芬, 惠, 玲, 麗 and 華 belong to women born before 1970, 雅, 婷, 怡 and 君 to the 1970s and 80s, and 涵, 妤, 蓁, 芯 and 晴 to the 2000s. Several characters here — 佳, 欣, 恩, 允, 語, 涵, 育 — are equally at home in a man's name, so a character drawn alone does not fix a gender.
