description: Informal forms of address. The 阿- prefix and the doubled syllable are the two productive patterns: 阿明 from 志明, 婷婷 from 雅婷. 小- before a surname is what colleagues use for someone younger, and 老- for someone older, so 小陳 and 老陳 can be the same person twenty years apart.
