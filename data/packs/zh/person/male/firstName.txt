description: Male given name (名) in Traditional characters, in the Taiwanese register. Most are two characters and the pairing carries meaning rather than being a fixed name from a list — 家豪 is household-and-heroic, 冠宇 is crown-and-universe — which is why a Chinese given name is composed rather than chosen from a canon. 志明 and 家豪 are so common they are the standing joke of the 菜市場名, the market-stall name: shout it across a market and several heads turn.
