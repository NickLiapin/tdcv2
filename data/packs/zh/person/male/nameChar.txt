description: Single characters that Taiwanese male given names are BUILT from. A Chinese given name is composed rather than picked off a list — parents choose one or two characters for meaning and sound — so the space of possible names is effectively open and a flat list of whole names (person/male/firstName.txt) understates it badly. Draw one character for a single-syllable name or two for the ordinary two-character name. Taiwan's preferences are legible in this file: 宇, 翔, 睿, 廷, 豪, 宸 and 昱 dominate birth registrations since 2000, while 國, 忠, 義 and 華 mark men born before 1970. 睿 and 叡 are one word in two written forms and Taiwanese parents treat the choice between them as real, because stroke count matters: 姓名學, divination from the numerology of a name's strokes, is consulted at naming far more widely in Taiwan than on the mainland, and a professional namer is a normal thing to pay.
locale: zh
