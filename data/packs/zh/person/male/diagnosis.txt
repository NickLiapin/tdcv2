description: Condition that affects only men, or overwhelmingly men, in Taiwanese medical Chinese. The vocabulary diverges from the mainland's much further than the shared characters suggest, and a glyph conversion produces terms no Taiwanese doctor writes: the prostate is 攝護腺 here and 前列腺 there, a syndrome is a 症候群 and not a 綜合症, the epididymis is 副睪 and not 附睾, and male-pattern baldness is the plain 雄性禿. Duchenne muscular dystrophy is 裘馨氏肌肉失養症 in Taiwan, a transliteration that shares not one character with the mainland's 杜氏肌營養不良. A hernia is 疝氣 in ordinary speech and on the chart.
