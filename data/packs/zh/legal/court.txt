description: Taiwan's courts, and the structure is not the mainland's. There are no 人民法院 here: the ordinary ladder is 地方法院 → 高等法院 → 最高法院, with a separate administrative chain and a separate prosecution service (檢察署) that is organisationally distinct from the courts. 智慧財產及商業法院 hears intellectual-property and corporate cases in one specialist court, an arrangement Taiwan adopted in 2008 and consolidated in 2021. Note 智慧財產 for intellectual property where the mainland writes 知识产权.
locale: zh
