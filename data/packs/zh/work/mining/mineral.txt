description: Mineral, in Traditional characters. Taiwan mines very little metal. What it extracts at scale is 石灰石 from the ranges behind Hualien, feeding a cement industry built on that one seam, together with marble and serpentine from the same rock. Coal was the island's only significant fuel and is finished: the pits around Ruifang and Pingxi in the north were closed after a run of fatal accidents in the 1980s and the last mine shut in 2000. Nearly everything else on this list arrives by ship, which is why Taiwan's metals sector is a refining and fabrication business rather than an extractive one.
locale: zh
