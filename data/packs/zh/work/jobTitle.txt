description: Job title in Taiwan. Very little of the technical vocabulary matches the mainland's: software is 軟體, an algorithm is an 演算法, a chip is a 晶片, firmware is 韌體, a network is 網路 and data is 資料. Sales staff are 業務, marketing is 行銷, any planning role is 企劃, and recruiting is 招募. Three regulated title families do most of the work in this list. Engineers licensed under the Professional Engineers Act are 技師, as in 結構技師, 大地技師 and 環境工程技師, and the word marks a licence rather than a rank. Medical roles follow the hospital ladder 住院醫師, 主治醫師, 科主任, and nurses became 護理師 when the register was upgraded, leaving 護士 as a lower grade that has nearly died out. Chief officers end in 長: 執行長, 財務長, 技術長.
locale: zh
