description: Jobs in Taiwanese finance. Lending is 授信, so a loan officer is a 授信專員 rather than the mainland's 信貸員, and an ordinary bank employee is a 行員. The 理財專員, universally shortened to 理專, is the branch salesperson who sells funds and insurance across the counter; the role is prominent enough that mis-selling by 理專 surfaces as a public scandal every few years. Insurance is sold by licensed 保險業務員, of whom Taiwan has several hundred thousand in a population of twenty-three million, among the highest densities anywhere.
locale: zh
