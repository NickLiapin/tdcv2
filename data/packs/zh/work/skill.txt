description: Professional skill as it is written on a Taiwanese 履歷. The English technology names stay in Latin script because that is exactly how they are typed into a CV. The Chinese terms carry the difference: a project is a 專案, data is 資料, visualisation is 視覺化, users are 使用者, video is 影片, and regulatory compliance is 法令遵循, abbreviated 法遵 and the standard name for the function inside every Taiwanese bank. 國語 is what Mandarin is called in Taiwan and 普通話 appears on no local CV. 水電 is a single licensed trade covering plumbing and electrical work together, which the mainland splits into two, and welding is written 銲接 under the CNS standards and the national skills certification.
