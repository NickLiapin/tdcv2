description: Common verbs. Chinese verbs never conjugate — 吃 is eat, eats, ate, eaten and eating, with time carried by adverbs and aspect particles instead. 是 (to be) is used only to link two nouns and is dropped before an adjective, which is why 我很好 has no verb in it at all.
