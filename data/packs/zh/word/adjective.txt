description: Common adjectives. Two entries carry a real Taiwan/mainland split that a mechanical character conversion would miss: quality is 品質 in Taiwan and 质量 on the mainland, and a level or standard is 水準 here and 水平 there. Chinese adjectives do not agree with anything and do not change form; 好 is good in every position a English adjective could take, including as a whole predicate — 天氣好 is a complete sentence.
locale: zh
