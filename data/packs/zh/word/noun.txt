description: Common nouns. Note 水準 and 品質 rather than the mainland's 水平 and 質量 — the same split that appears in the adjective list. Chinese nouns have no plural and no article, so 書 is book, books, a book and the book depending only on context, which is why a Chinese sentence needs a measure word (本, 個, 張) to count anything.
locale: zh
