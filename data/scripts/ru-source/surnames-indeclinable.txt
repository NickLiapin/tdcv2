# Russian surnames that do NOT decline — identical for a man and a woman.
#
# Иванов has a feminine form and Шевченко does not: Мария Шевченко and Пётр
# Шевченко carry the same string. That is why they cannot live in the gendered
# files, where every line has a masculine and a feminine twin, and why they get
# their own file here — `person.lastName`, drawn by both genders.
#
# Endings that stay put: -енко, -ко, -ук/-юк/-чук, -ич/-вич, -их/-ых, -аго/-ово,
# and the surnames of non-Slavic origin borne across Russia — Armenian -ян,
# Georgian -швили/-дзе, and the German and Jewish surnames that have been Russian
# surnames for two centuries.
#
# The builder checks this file against the declension rules and refuses anything
# that WOULD decline, because such a name belongs in surnames-masculine.txt and
# would otherwise silently lose its feminine form.
#
# Order is frequency and becomes the weight.
